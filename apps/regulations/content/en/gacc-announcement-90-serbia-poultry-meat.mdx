Effective today. GACC Announcement No. 90 of 2026, published on 30 June, allows the import of edible frozen poultry carcasses, cuts and by-products from Serbia; live poultry are excluded.

For each batch: slaughter, cutting and storage enterprises must be registered with the Chinese side, with slaughter and cutting integrated; source animals hatched, raised and slaughtered in Serbia, traceable, free of banned veterinary drugs and additives; workshops keep dirty and clean areas separate, core temperature not above minus 15 ℃, containers sealed; inner and outer packaging labelled in Chinese and English or in Serbian with registration and batch numbers, and each box accompanied by an official original veterinary health certificate.

If registration, certificate, temperature, labelling or source farm does not match, hold the shipment: unregistered enterprises or lack of a valid certificate bar entry; excessive residues, disease, nuclear contamination and the like are handled under laws, regulations and the Protocol. The registration list and certificates prevail.

<!-- original -->

Pursuant to China's relevant laws and regulations and the inspection and quarantine requirements for poultry meat exported to China agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (GACC, hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Water Management of the Republic of Serbia (hereinafter referred to as the Serbian side), as of today, the import of Serbian poultry meat that meets the relevant requirements is allowed.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) Laws and regulations.

The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations, as well as the Measures for the Safety Administration of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China, the Provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China on the Registration of Overseas Food Production Enterprises for Import, and so on.

(2) Bilateral protocol.

The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Water Management of the Republic of Serbia on Inspection and Quarantine Requirements for Poultry Meat Exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Scope of Products Allowed to Be Exported to China

Poultry meat from Serbia allowed to be exported to China refers to edible frozen poultry carcasses (the edible parts of the body of live poultry after slaughter, bleeding and removal of feathers, internal organs, head and feet) and cuts, and edible poultry by-products. Edible frozen poultry carcasses include frozen whole chickens (excluding internal organs and digestive tract organs such as the stomach and intestines), frozen chicken meat (whole or in pieces, excluding frozen chicken breasts and frozen chicken legs), frozen chicken breasts, frozen chicken legs, frozen whole chicken wings and cut parts (wing roots, wing middles, second wing sections and wing tips); edible poultry by-products include frozen chicken hearts, frozen chicken livers, frozen chicken stomachs and frozen chicken feet.

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

(1) Poultry meat production enterprises for export to China, including slaughter, cutting and storage enterprises (of which slaughter and cutting must be integrated enterprises), shall be registered with the Chinese side; unregistered poultry meat production enterprises may not export poultry meat to China.

(2) The Serbian side shall supervise poultry meat production enterprises intending to export to China to ensure compliance with the requirements of Chinese and Serbian laws and regulations on veterinary hygiene and public health.

(3) Poultry meat production enterprises for export to China have established traceability systems to ensure that exported poultry meat can be traced back to its source farm.

IV. Requirements for Source Animals of the Products

(1) The Serbian side confirms that there is no commercial poultry highly pathogenic avian influenza or Newcastle disease outbreak in its territory, in compliance with the relevant requirements of the Terrestrial Animal Health Code of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH).

(2) They were hatched, raised and slaughtered in Serbia, have never been mixed with other captive animals, and are traceable to their source farm.

(3) They come from farms or premises where, within the 12 months before slaughter, no class II infectious or parasitic diseases of entry animal quarantine specified in the List of Entry Animal Quarantine Diseases of the People's Republic of China that are related to poultry and may be transmitted through poultry meat have occurred.

(4) They come from farms or premises that, within the 6 months before slaughter, were not subject to quarantine restrictions or surveillance due to the occurrence of poultry diseases other than those in paragraph (3) above, which are related to poultry and listed in Chinese and Serbian animal health regulations and by WOAH, involving suspected infection of poultry flocks or confirmed infection with a disease or infectious disease.

(5) No veterinary drugs or feed additives banned by both sides have ever been used.

(6) They show no clinical symptoms of any infectious disease, their carcasses and organs show no pathological changes, and they pass pre- and post-slaughter quarantine.

V. Requirements for Production, Storage and Transport

(1) The process layout of processing workshops shall separate dirty and clean areas, with a rational workflow to avoid cross-contamination.

(2) Cold stores shall have a dedicated area with clear signage for storing poultry meat for export to China. Edible poultry by-products must be packaged separately by product type.

(3) The entire process from packaging and storage to transport shall comply with the relevant hygiene requirements of China and Serbia to prevent contamination by harmful substances.

(4) Poultry meat for export to China shall be stored and transported under appropriate temperature conditions, with the core temperature of the product not above minus 15 ℃.

(5) After the goods are loaded into containers, seals shall be affixed under the supervision of the Serbian side before transport, and the seal number must be stated in the veterinary health certificate. Packaging may not be opened or replaced during transport.

VI. Product Requirements

(1) Serbia's national residue monitoring plan shall be implemented to demonstrate that residues of veterinary drugs, pesticides, heavy metals, persistent organic pollutants (POPs) such as dioxin-like compounds, dioxin-like polychlorinated biphenyls and non-dioxin-like polychlorinated biphenyls, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons and other toxic and harmful substances in the products do not exceed the “maximum residue limits” (MRLs) prescribed by China and Serbia.

(2) They are not contaminated by pathogenic microorganisms and comply with the laws and regulations of China and Serbia and international standards.

(3) They are not contaminated by nuclear radiation.

(4) They are safe and hygienic and fit for human consumption.

VII. Requirements for Packaging, Labelling and Certification

(1) They must be packaged in brand-new materials that comply with China's national food safety standards and international hygiene standards.

(2) They shall have separate inner packaging, on which the product name, country of origin, registration number of the production enterprise and production batch number shall be indicated in Chinese and English, or in Chinese and Serbian.

(3) The outer packaging shall indicate, in Chinese and English or in Chinese and Serbian, the product name, specifications, place of origin (down to country/region/city), registration number of the production enterprise, production batch number, destination (indicated as the People's Republic of China), production date (including year, month and day), shelf life, storage temperature and so on, and shall bear (by affixing or printing) the official inspection and quarantine mark.

(4) Pre-packaged poultry meat products shall also comply with China's laws, regulations and standards on labels of pre-packaged food.

(5) Each container of poultry meat exported to China shall be accompanied by at least one official original veterinary health certificate.

VIII. Requirements for Entry Inspection and Quarantine

(1) Verification of documents.

(1) Verify whether the goods come from a registered enterprise;

(2) Verify whether the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit has been obtained;

(3) Verify whether the health certificate is authentic and valid.

(2) Inspection of goods.

China Customs shall conduct inspection and quarantine of poultry meat for export to China in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules and other provisions, in combination with the requirements of this Announcement. Goods that pass inspection and quarantine shall be allowed to enter.

(3) Handling of non-compliance.

Where non-compliance is found during entry inspection and quarantine, China Customs shall handle it in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, the Protocol and other provisions.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs

30 June 2026
