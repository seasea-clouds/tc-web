On 7 November 2025, the General Administration of Customs of China issued Announcement No. 219 of 2025, effective from 15 December 2025. When importing agricultural products in the catalogue, you must fill in the overseas enterprise's China registration number in the 302 certificate column under “Product Qualification” on the customs declaration form.

It covers agricultural products under the animal and plant quarantine regime, which is a separate system from the registration of overseas food manufacturers (Decree No. 280, code 519): goods in the catalogue must come from enterprises whose registration status is normal, and you can verify the list on the website of the Department of Animal and Plant Quarantine of the GACC.

Before shipping, confirm that your enterprise is on the registration list for the corresponding product in the catalogue and that its status is normal, and that the China registration number has been passed to your importer; when one declaration covers several commodity items, fill in each one.

## GACC Announcement No. 219 (2025)

To further strengthen the traceability management of imported agricultural products and improve customs clearance efficiency, in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations and other relevant provisions, the requirements for the declaration management of overseas enterprises of imported agricultural products are hereby announced as follows:

(1) In accordance with the International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures, the GACC analyses the quarantine risks of imported agricultural products and, taking international practice into account, determines and publishes the catalogue of imported agricultural products that require registration upon recommendation by the competent authority of the exporting country (hereinafter referred to as the catalogue). The catalogue can be queried at http://dzs.customs.gov.cn/dzs/2747042/6787828/index.html. Importers shall ensure that agricultural products in the catalogue come from overseas enterprises with a normal registration status as published by the GACC.

(2) Registered overseas enterprises can be queried through the List of Quarantine Registration of Overseas Enterprises of Imported Animals, Plants and Their Products at http://dzs.customs.gov.cn/dzs/2747042/6787828/index.html, or through the Animal and Plant Quarantine Information Query function under the I Want to Query column of the Internet plus Customs platform at https://online.customs.gov.cn/ociswebserver/pages/dzjxxcx/index.html.

(3) When declaring the import of agricultural products in the catalogue, importers shall, in the certificate column for Registration of Overseas Enterprises Producing, Processing and Storing Imported Animals, Plants and Their Products under the Product Qualification item of the customs declaration form or the filing list (licence category code 302), accurately fill in the overseas enterprise's China registration number (registration number in China). Where one declaration form covers several commodity items, the corresponding enterprise's China registration number shall be filled in item by item.

This announcement comes into force on 15 December 2025.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

7 November 2025
