Uzbekistan almonds are approved for export to China. Announcement No. 196 of 2025 of the General Administration of Customs takes effect on issuance. The almonds, scientifically named Prunus dulcis, must be made by selection, cleaning and drying, and the enterprises producing, processing and storing them may export only after recommendation by Uzbekistan and registration by China.

Almonds exported to China shall not contain the five quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese Side, such as the codling moth and the European grapevine moth, or live insects and impurities. They shall be dehydrated by thermal processing with moldy kernels removed, and Uzbekistan shall conduct annual risk monitoring and trapping.

Each consignment shall carry a phytosanitary certificate and the required English declaration, and its packaging shall bear Chinese or English product and traceability information. On entry, the goods undergo customs inspection and quarantine; non-compliant goods are disposed of by law and the Uzbek Side is notified.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions on inspection and quarantine requirements for almonds exported to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese Side) and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Republic of Uzbekistan (hereinafter referred to as the Uzbek Side), almonds from Uzbekistan that meet the following requirements are permitted to be imported from today on.

I. Inspection and Quarantine Basis

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations.

(2) The Measures for the Safety Management of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China and the Provisions on the Administration of the Registration of Overseas Food Production Enterprises Exporting to China.

(3) The Protocol on Inspection and Quarantine Requirements for Almonds Exported from Uzbekistan to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Republic of Uzbekistan (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Products Permitted for Import

Almonds from Uzbekistan permitted for import refer to nuts for human consumption produced in the territory of Uzbekistan from almonds (Prunus dulcis (Mill.) D. A. Webb) planted in Uzbekistan through selection, cleaning, drying or other processing techniques.

III. Food Safety Requirements

Almonds exported to China shall comply with the food safety and plant quarantine laws and regulations of China, national food safety standards and the provisions of the Protocol.

IV. Phytosanitary Requirements

(1) Almonds exported to China shall not contain the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese Side: the codling moth Cydia pomonella, the European grapevine moth Lobesia botrana, Halli's armored scale Mercetaspis halli, the almond wasp Eurytoma amygdali Enderlein, and the Verticillium wilt fungus Verticillium dahlia.

(2) Almonds exported to China shall not contain live insects or other impurities added to or mixed with them.

(3) If, in the course of quarantine supervision by the Uzbek Side, almonds exported to China are found to carry any of the above pests, the Uzbek Side shall immediately suspend the export to China of almonds from the relevant production, processing and storage enterprises, take integrated control measures and notify the Chinese Side. After assessment by the Chinese Side confirms compliance, almonds from the relevant enterprises may resume export to China.

V. Enterprise Requirements

The Uzbek Side shall inspect the units producing, processing and storing almonds exported to China in accordance with the relevant requirements of China on the registration of overseas food production enterprises, and recommend compliant enterprises to China for registration. Only after an enterprise has obtained registration in China may its products be exported to China. The list of registered Uzbekistan enterprises exporting almonds to China may be consulted on the website of the General Administration of Customs.

VI. Raw Material Requirements

The raw materials of almonds exported to China shall come from planting enterprises confirmed by the Uzbek Side as meeting the relevant phytosanitary and food safety requirements and recorded accordingly.

VII. Processing Requirements

(1) Almonds exported to China shall be dehydrated using thermal processing machinery such as ovens or dryers, and moldy kernels shall be removed during processing.

(2) The Uzbek Side shall provide guidance and supervision over the raw material planting, production, processing, storage, transportation, export and other processes of almonds exported to China, so as to ensure compliance with the relevant safety and hygiene requirements of both countries and prevent contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

(3) The Uzbek Side shall adopt internationally recognized methods to implement an annual food safety risk monitoring plan for almonds exported to China; during the raw material planting, production, processing and storage stages of almonds exported to China, it shall monitor the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese Side and carry out trapping. At the request of the Chinese Side, the Uzbek Side shall provide all the above annual monitoring results to the Chinese Side.

(4) If, in the course of inspection and supervision by the Uzbek Side, almonds intended for export to China are found not to comply with China's national food safety standards, the relevant products shall not be exported to China.

VIII. Packaging and Labeling Requirements

Almonds exported to China shall be packaged in new materials that are clean, hygienic and compliant with food safety and phytosanitary requirements, and leakage shall be prevented during transportation. Each package shall bear in Chinese or English the statement „This product is exported to the People's Republic of China“ as well as traceability information such as the product name, place of origin, name of the production and processing enterprise and its registration number in China, and the name and address of the exporter. The above information may be affixed to the packaging in the form of labels.

IX. Transportation Requirements

Prior to shipment, the Uzbek Side shall inspect the means of transport for almonds exported to China. The means of transport shall meet hygiene and epidemic prevention requirements and shall not be mixed with quarantine pests or other restricted quarantine objects, such as weed seeds, live insects, plant debris, soil and other extraneous impurities.

X. Certificate Requirements

Prior to export, the Uzbek Side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for almonds exported to China that have passed inspection and quarantine and meet the requirements of the Protocol.

In the additional declaration column of the certificate, the name of the almond production and processing enterprise, its registration number in China and the following statement shall be indicated: „The almonds certified by this phytosanitary certificate comply with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection and Quarantine Requirements for almonds Exported from Uzbekistan to China signed by Uzbekistan and China on September 2nd, 2025. The goods are free from any quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese Side, such as Cydia pomonella, Lobesia botrana, Mercetaspis halli, Eurytoma amygdali Enderlein, and Verticillium dahlia.“ (This means that the almonds certified by the phytosanitary certificate comply with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection and Quarantine Requirements for Almonds Exported from Uzbekistan to China signed by China and Uzbekistan on September 2, 2025, and that the consignment is free from the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese Side, namely the codling moth Cydia pomonella, the European grapevine moth Lobesia botrana, Halli's armored scale Mercetaspis halli, the almond wasp Eurytoma amygdali Enderlein and the Verticillium wilt fungus Verticillium dahlia.)

The content and format of the phytosanitary certificate shall be confirmed by both sides in advance.

XI. Entry Inspection and Quarantine and Handling of Non-Compliance

(1) Entry inspection and quarantine. After almonds exported to China arrive at the entry port in China, China Customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine supervision and grant entry only if they are qualified.

(2) Handling of non-compliance. If the Chinese Side finds that almonds exported to China violate China's laws and regulations on food safety and plant quarantine, national food safety standards or the provisions of the Protocol, China Customs shall dispose of them in accordance with China's relevant laws and regulations and notify the Uzbek Side of the violation and the measures taken.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

9 October 2025
