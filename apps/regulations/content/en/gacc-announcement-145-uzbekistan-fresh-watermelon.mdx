Entry is allowed, but plots and packing houses need joint approval and registration by both sides. Announcement No. 145 opens China to Uzbekistan fresh watermelons on 10 October 2026, provided the fruit comes from a Baluchistan melon fly pest-free area. If all you have is a registration number quoted verbally by the supplier, do not book space yet.

Follow one lot end to end: before the export season China approves and publishes the registration list; pest-free areas follow ISPM 10, and a fly catch inside one must be reported to China within 48 hours; each package and pallet carries the destination marking; for the first two years 2% of each lot is sampled, falling to 1% with no problems; the ISPM 12 certificate quotes the English additional declaration verbatim.

Without the joint list, with a broken seal, or with a certificate lacking the English declaration, the consignment is held at entry; quarantine pests, branches, leaves or soil mean return, destruction or disinfestation, and the plots and packing houses may be suspended for the rest of the export season.

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Food Safety Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan (hereinafter the Uzbek side) on the phytosanitary requirements for export of fresh watermelons from Uzbekistan to China, import of fresh watermelons from Uzbekistan that meet the following relevant requirements is permitted from the date of this Announcement.

I. Quarantine Basis

(i) the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; (iii) the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; (iv) the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported Fruits; (v) the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Watermelons from Uzbekistan to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Food Safety Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

II. Names of Commodities Permitted for Entry

Fresh watermelons (hereinafter watermelons), scientific name Citrullus lanatus L., English name Fresh Watermelon.

III. Permitted Places of Origin

Watermelon producing areas in the Republic of Uzbekistan.

IV. Approved Plots and Packing Houses

Plots and packing houses exporting watermelons to China shall be reviewed by the Uzbek side and approved and registered jointly by the Chinese and Uzbek sides. The registration information shall include the name, address and registration number, so as to enable accurate traceability when exported goods fail to comply with the relevant provisions of this Announcement. Before each annual export season, the Uzbek side shall provide the registration list to the Chinese side; upon review and approval by the Chinese side, it shall be published on the website of the General Administration of Customs and updated regularly.

V. List of Quarantine Pests of Concern to the Chinese Side

(1) Baluchistan melon fly Myiopardalis pardalina (2) cucumber scab fungus Cladosporium cucumerinum (3) alfalfa Verticillium wilt Verticillium albo-atrum (4) chrysanthemum foliar nematode Aphelenchoides ritzemabosi

VI. Pre-export Management

(i) Field management.

(1) Plots exporting watermelons to China shall, under the supervision of the Uzbek side, establish sound quality management and traceability systems, implement Good Agricultural Practices (GAP), maintain plot hygiene such as no sources of pollution affecting watermelon production in the surroundings and timely removal of fallen and rotten fruits, and implement Integrated Pest Management (IPM), including regular pest monitoring and surveys, physical, chemical or biological control, and control measures such as farming operations.

(2) The Uzbek side shall, in accordance with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 6 (ISPM 6), draw up a field management plan for the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side and carry out field monitoring throughout the year. If pests or their corresponding symptoms are found during monitoring, integrated management measures including chemical, physical or biological control shall be taken to control pest population density or maintain a low level of prevalence.

(3) Watermelons exported to China shall come from pest-free areas for the Baluchistan melon fly. Such pest-free areas shall be established by the Uzbek side in accordance with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 10 (ISPM 10), with pest monitoring carried out as required (see the Annex), and shall be approved by the Chinese side. If the Baluchistan melon fly is detected within a relevant pest-free area, the Uzbek side shall immediately suspend its pest-free area status, notify the Chinese side within 48 hours and activate the emergency plan. Once the outbreak has been eradicated by the Uzbek side and recognised by the Chinese side, the pest-free area status may be restored.

(4) For cucumber scab fungus and alfalfa Verticillium wilt, the Uzbek side or its authorised personnel shall carry out surveys from the watermelon seedling stage, with a field survey every 7 days; once either of the above pests or symptoms of plant damage is confirmed, integrated management measures including chemical or biological control shall be taken. The Uzbek side shall suspend all registered watermelon plots in that producing area from exporting to China for the season and notify the Chinese side of the situation.

(5) For the chrysanthemum foliar nematode, the Uzbek side or its authorised personnel shall, during the growing period of the watermelons, carry out manual monitoring and a plot survey every 15 days, focusing on whether damage by this pest occurs on leaves, fruits and fruit stalks. If this pest or symptoms of plant damage are found, integrated management measures including chemical or biological control shall be taken.

(6) The Uzbek side shall retain pest monitoring and control records for plots exporting to China and provide them at the request of the Chinese side. Pest monitoring and control records shall include at least the time of monitoring, the names of pests found and the control measures taken; chemical control records shall include detailed information such as the names, active ingredients, concentrations and application dates of all chemical agents used during the watermelon growing season.

(7) Pest monitoring and control shall be carried out under the guidance of professional technical personnel. Such personnel shall receive training from the Uzbek side or its authorised institution.

(ii) Packing house management.

(1) The processing, packaging, storage and shipment of watermelons exported to China shall be carried out under the quarantine supervision of the Uzbek side or its authorised personnel.

(2) Packing houses for watermelons exported to China shall be clean and hygienic, with hardened floors, and shall have a raw material area and a finished product warehouse.

(3) The receiving, processing, treatment and storage areas for watermelons exported to China shall be relatively independent and reasonably laid out, and shall be separated from living areas by an appropriate distance.

(4) During packaging, watermelons exported to China shall undergo manual sorting, grading and cleaning, removing diseased fruits, insect-damaged fruits, rotten fruits, malformed fruits, branches, leaves, roots or other plant residues and soil.

(5) Packaged watermelons requiring storage shall be placed in the warehouse immediately; all watermelons placed in storage shall meet the same phytosanitary requirements and shall be stored separately to avoid reinfestation by pests.

(6) Registered packing houses shall establish a traceability system to ensure that watermelons exported to China can be traced back to the registered production plots, including records of the processing and packaging date, the registered name or its registration number, the export date, the export quantity and the country of destination.

(iii) Packaging requirements.

(1) Packaging materials shall be clean, hygienic and unused, and shall comply with the relevant phytosanitary requirements of China. If wooden packaging is used, it shall comply with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15).

(2) Each package must be marked in Chinese or English with the type and variety of watermelon, the country, the place of origin, and the name or registration number of the plot and packing house. Each package and pallet must be marked in Chinese or English with “For export to the People's Republic of China” or “Exported to the People’s Republic of China”.

(3) Containers holding watermelons exported to China must be checked at the time of loading to ensure good hygienic conditions. The container shall be sealed, and the seal shall be intact and undamaged on arrival at the Chinese port of entry.

(iv) Pre-export inspection and quarantine.

(1) During the first two years of trade, the Uzbek side shall sample 2% of each lot of watermelons exported to China for inspection. If suspected pest damage or other insects are found, the fruit may be cut open for inspection. If no phytosanitary problems occur within two years, the sampling rate shall be reduced to 1%.

(2) If quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side, branches, leaves or soil are found, the lot shall not be exported to China and, depending on the circumstances, the relevant plots and packing houses shall be suspended from exporting watermelons to China for the season. The Uzbek side shall identify the cause and take corrective measures. Records of the findings shall be kept and provided to the Chinese side on request.

(v) Phytosanitary certificate requirements.

For lots that pass quarantine, the Uzbek side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate in accordance with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12 (ISPM 12), stating the names or registration numbers of the plot and packing house and the container number (if any), and shall state in the additional declaration of the phytosanitary certificate: “This consignment complies with the requirements specified in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Watermelon from Uzbekistan to China, and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China.” (The consignment complies with the phytosanitary requirements of the Protocol for export of fresh watermelons from Uzbekistan to China and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China.)

VII. Entry Inspection, Quarantine and Treatment of Non-compliance

When watermelons exported to China arrive at a port of entry in China, China Customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine in accordance with the following requirements.

(i) Verification of relevant certificates and markings.

(1) Verify whether the imported watermelons have obtained the Import Quarantine Permit for Animals and Plants. (2) Verify whether the phytosanitary certificate complies with item (v) of Chapter VI of this Announcement. (3) Verify whether the markings on the packages and pallets comply with item (iii) of Chapter VI of this Announcement.

(ii) Entry inspection and quarantine.

(1) Watermelons exported to China shall enter through ports permitted by the Chinese side for the import of fruits. (2) When the watermelons arrive at a port of entry in China, the Chinese side shall check the relevant documents and markings and carry out inspection and quarantine in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations, national standards and the requirements of this Announcement. Lots that pass inspection and quarantine shall be admitted.

(iii) Treatment of non-compliance.

(1) If the lot is found to come from plots and packing houses that have not been approved, it shall not be admitted. (2) If quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side or quarantine pests newly occurring in Uzbekistan are found, the lot shall be returned, destroyed or disinfested. (3) If the lot is found not to comply with Chinese food safety laws, regulations and national standards, it shall be returned or destroyed.

It is hereby announced.

Annex: Guidelines for Monitoring the Baluchistan Melon Fly
