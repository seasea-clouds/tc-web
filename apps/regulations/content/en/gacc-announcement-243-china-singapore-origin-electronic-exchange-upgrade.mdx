GACC Announcement No. 243 of 2025 upgrades the China-Singapore origin electronic information exchange system from 11 December 2025, adding real-time transmission of Singapore origin certificates under the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP); the paperless declaration may skip entering certificate data and transmitting the certificate.

The upgrade covers three agreements, the China-Singapore FTA, the China-ASEAN Framework Agreement and the RCEP, with certificate data compared automatically by the system; the rules on originating status and direct consignment remain unchanged.

Where the system cannot find certificate data, supplementary entry and upload are possible before 28 February 2026; from 1 March 2026 security for duty must be arranged, after which the transmission status is checked and the security released. Paper declaration still requires a paper certificate of origin.

<!-- original -->

To further facilitate compliant customs clearance of goods under free trade agreements, upgraded functions of the China-Singapore origin electronic information exchange system go live from 11 December 2025. Building on the existing real-time electronic data exchange of origin certificates and movement certificates issued by Singapore customs under the Free Trade Agreement between the Government of the People's Republic of China and the Government of the Republic of Singapore (hereinafter the China-Singapore FTA) and under the Framework Agreement on Comprehensive Economic Cooperation between China and ASEAN (hereinafter the China-ASEAN Framework Agreement), as well as certificates of non-manipulation for goods in transit through Singapore, the real-time transmission of electronic data of origin certificates issued by Singapore customs under the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP) is newly added. Matters are hereby announced as follows:

(1) Where the consignee of imported goods or its agent (hereinafter the importer), at the time of import, applies under a Singapore-issued origin certificate for agreement tariff rates under the RCEP, the China-ASEAN Framework Agreement or the China-Singapore FTA, and chooses the paperless customs declaration method under the provisions of GACC Announcement No. 34 of 2021, it need not enter origin certificate electronic data and direct consignment rule commitments through the Origin Elements Declaration System under Preferential Trade Agreements, nor upload the origin certificate electronically.

When making the paperless declaration, where the system indicates that no origin certificate electronic data can be found, from 11 December 2025 to 28 February 2026 the importer may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of GACC Announcement No. 34 of 2021, enter origin certificate electronic information and direct consignment rule commitments through the Origin Elements Declaration System under Preferential Trade Agreements and upload the origin certificate electronically; from 1 March 2026, where the system indicates that no origin certificate electronic data can be found, the importer shall apply for the corresponding security for duty procedures as required, and may later use the online origin certificate status query function of the China Customs Origin Service Platform to confirm the transmission of origin certificate electronic data, and release the security for duty as required.

(2) Where the importer chooses the paper-based declaration method, it shall submit the paper document of the origin certificate when declaring the import.

This Announcement takes effect from 11 December 2025.

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of China

10 December 2025
