Animal protein fertilizer from Spain is cleared for import. Announcement No. 63 of 2026 specifies that the raw material is limited to blood from healthy live pigs, and that producing enterprises must be approved by Spain and registered with China.

The product must be heat-treated at a core temperature of no less than 160ºC for no less than 4 hours and comply with GB 38400 limits; registration is valid for 5 years, and outer packaging must be marked fertilizer use only.

Before export Spain samples and tests it and issues a health certificate; on entry China verifies the quarantine permit, the registered enterprise and the certificate, and handles non-compliance per the laws and the Protocol.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food of the Kingdom of Spain (hereinafter the Spanish side) on quarantine and health requirements for animal protein fertilizer from Spain for export to China, imports of products that meet the following requirements are permitted with effect from today.

I. Inspection and quarantine basis

(1) The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(3) The Measures for the Supervision of Inspection and Quarantine of Non-edible Animal Products for Entry and Exit;

(4) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food of Spain on Quarantine and Health Requirements for Animal Protein Fertilizer from the Kingdom of Spain for Export to China (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Scope of products permitted for import

Animal protein fertilizer in this Announcement refers to fertilizer processed from blood collected after slaughter from healthy live pigs.

III. Raw material requirements

(1) The raw material shall not come from ruminants, and effective measures shall be taken to prevent contamination by ruminant-derived ingredients;

(2) The animals used to produce the raw material shall come from areas free of foot-and-mouth disease, classical swine fever, swine vesicular disease and other swine infectious diseases reportable under World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) requirements, and the disease-free areas shall meet WOAH standards and be recognized by the Chinese side;

(3) The animals used to produce the raw material were all born and raised in Spain, or legally imported from other EU member states, slaughtered in slaughterhouses approved by the Spanish side, and passed ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection with no clinical signs of any animal infectious disease found;

(4) The animals used to produce the raw material do not come from animals subject to transport restrictions, or slaughtered due to an outbreak of animal disease or death from unknown causes;

(5) Regarding African swine fever, if the raw material comes from pigs raised in Spain, it shall comply with the relevant requirements of the regionalization agreement on African swine fever signed by China and Spain;

(6) No blood of other animals is used.

IV. Requirements for producing enterprises

(1) Comply with China's and Spain's hygiene and veterinary hygiene regulations on animal-derived fertilizer;

(2) Approved by the Spanish side and produced under its effective supervision, with the product allowed to be sold freely on the Spanish market;

(3) Establish and implement a hazard analysis and critical control point (HACCP) quality management system, and formulate and effectively implement product traceability and recall systems;

(4) Recommended by the Spanish side to the Chinese side and registered with the Chinese side, with a registration validity period of 5 years.

V. Production and processing requirements

(1) The product shall be heat-treated at a core temperature of no less than 160ºC for no less than 4 hours, or by other equivalent processing methods agreed by the Chinese side;

(2) The product shall not contain toxic or harmful substances harmful to animal and plant health or ingredients prohibited by the laws and regulations of both sides, and the content of toxic and harmful substances in the fertilizer shall comply with China's Requirements for the Limitation of Toxic and Harmful Substances in Fertilizers (GB 38400);

(3) For fertilizer use only;

(4) Before export to China, the product shall be sampled and tested by the Spanish official authority and meet the following requirements:

(1) Salmonella: not detected in 25 g of product: n=5, c=0, m=0, M=0;

(2) Enterobacteriaceae: not more than 100 per 1 g sample: n=5, c=5, m=0, M=1000;

n - number of samples tested;

m - threshold bacterial count; if the bacterial count in all samples does not exceed m, the result is qualified;

M - maximum bacterial count; if the bacterial count in one or more samples equals or exceeds M, the result is unqualified;

c - number of samples with a bacterial count between m and M; if the bacterial count in the other samples is less than or equal to m, the result is still considered acceptable.

VI. Packaging, labelling, storage and transport requirements

(1) Packaging materials shall be brand new, clean, sealed, moisture-proof and not easily damaged;

(2) The outer packaging shall bear a Chinese-language label, which shall comply with the relevant requirements of China's Content and Requirements for Fertilizer Labels (GB18382);

(3) The outer packaging shall indicate the name of the Spanish producing enterprise and the Spanish approval number, and clearly mark “fertilizer use only”;

(4) Effective measures are taken during the production, packaging, storage and transport of animal protein fertilizer to avoid contamination.

VII. Pre-export inspection and certificate requirements

The Spanish side is responsible for inspection and quarantine of animal protein fertilizer for export to China and shall issue a health certificate certifying that the product complies with the requirements of the Protocol.

VIII. Entry inspection and quarantine requirements

(1) Document verification.

(1) Verify whether the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit has been obtained;

(2) Verify whether the product comes from a registered enterprise;

(3) Verify whether the health certificate is authentic and valid.

(2) Cargo inspection.

China Customs shall, in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and in light of the requirements of this Announcement, carry out quarantine on animal protein fertilizer from Spain for export to China. Those that pass quarantine shall be permitted entry.

(3) Handling of non-compliance.

Where non-compliance is found during entry quarantine, China Customs shall handle it in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, the Protocol and other provisions.

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs
