Omani dried dates are approved for import into China. Announcement No. 161 of 2026 of the General Administration of Customs takes effect upon publication. The product is made from the fruit of the date palm (Phoenix dactylifera L.) by cleaning, drying and sorting. Overseas production and processing enterprises may export to China only after inspection by Oman and registration by China.

Dried dates for export to China must not carry eight quarantine pests such as the date palm leafhopper, nor live insects, soil or impurities. The whole process from raw material cultivation to export is supervised by Oman. Packaging must bear Chinese and English export information, and means of transport are inspected before shipment.

Each consignment is accompanied by a phytosanitary certificate with an English additional declaration giving the scientific names of the eight pests. On entry the dates are inspected and quarantined by customs, and any violation of food safety or plant quarantine rules is handled in accordance with the law.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions on inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for dried dates exported to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Water Resources of the Sultanate of Oman (hereinafter referred to as the Omani side), the import of dried dates from the Sultanate of Oman that meet the following requirements is permitted as of today.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection and Quarantine of Entry-Exit Animals and Plants and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations;

(2) The Measures for the Safety Administration of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China, and the Provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China on the Registration Administration of Overseas Production Enterprises for Imported Food;

(3) The Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Dried Date Palms to Be Exported from Oman to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Water Resources of the Sultanate of Oman (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Products Permitted for Import

Dried dates from Oman permitted for import refer to dried dates for human consumption made from the fruit of the date palm (Phoenix dactylifera L.), a plant of the palm family (Arecaceae) and the genus Phoenix, grown within Oman and processed in Oman by cleaning, drying, sorting and other processes.

III. Food Safety Requirements

Dried dates for export to China shall comply with China's food safety and plant quarantine laws and regulations, national food safety standards and the provisions of the Protocol.

IV. Plant Quarantine Requirements

(1) Dried dates for export to China must not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: date palm leafhopper (Ommatissus lybicus), date palm scale (Parlatoria blanchardi), date moth (Batrachedra amydraula), Egyptian cotton leafworm (Spodoptera littoralis), date stone beetle (Coccotrypes dactyliperda), a mite of the genus Oligonychus (Oligonychus afrasiaticus), dried fruit moth (Ephestia calidella) and raisin moth (Cadra (Ephestia) figulilella).

(2) Dried dates for export to China must not carry live insects, insect eggs or soil, nor be mixed with impurities such as weed seeds, plant debris, metal foreign matter and gravel.

V. Requirements for Production Enterprises

The Omani side shall inspect the production, processing and storage establishments of dried dates for export to China in accordance with the relevant requirements of the Chinese side on the registration of overseas food production enterprises, and recommend qualified enterprises to the Chinese side for registration. The Chinese side shall register the qualified enterprises. An enterprise may export its products to China only after obtaining registration in China. The list of registered Omani dried date enterprises authorized to export to China may be consulted on the website of the General Administration of Customs.

VI. Requirements for Raw Materials

The raw materials of dried dates for export to China shall come from date palm growing enterprises confirmed by the Omani side as meeting the relevant plant quarantine and food safety requirements.

VII. Requirements for the Processing Process

The Omani side shall guide and supervise the processes of raw material cultivation, production, processing, storage, transport and export of dried dates for export to China, so as to ensure that they comply with the relevant food safety and phytosanitary requirements of China and Oman and are not contaminated by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

VIII. Packaging and Labelling Requirements

Dried dates for export to China shall be packaged in new, clean, sanitary and breathable materials that meet food safety and plant quarantine requirements, and leakage during transport shall be prevented. Each package shall be labelled in Chinese or English with „This product is exported to the People's Republic of China / This product is exported to the People's Republic of China“, as well as traceable information in Chinese or English such as the name of the product, place of origin, name of the production and processing enterprise and its registration number in China, and the name and address of the exporter. The above information may be affixed to the package in the form of a label.

IX. Transport Requirements

Before shipment, the Omani side shall inspect the means of transport of dried dates for export to China. The means of transport shall meet sanitary requirements and must not carry quarantine pests or other regulated quarantine objects, such as weed seeds, live insects, plant debris, soil and other impurities.

X. Certificate Requirements

Before export, the Omani side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for dried dates for export to China that have been inspected and quarantined and meet the requirements of the Protocol.

The additional declaration column of the certificate shall state the name of the dried date production and processing enterprise, its registration number in China and „The batch of dried date palms certified by this phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Dried Date Palms to Be Exported from Oman to China signed on November 2nd,2026,in Beijing by Oman and China. The batch is free from Ommatissus lybicus, Parlatoria blanchardi, Batrachedra amydraula, Spodoptera littoralis, Coccotrypes dactyliperda, Oligonychus afrasiaticus, Ephestia calidella, Cadra (Ephestia) figulilella, and other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side. “[The dried dates certified by this phytosanitary certificate comply with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Dried Date Palms to Be Exported from Oman to China signed on November 2, 2026 in Beijing by Oman and China. The consignment is free from date palm leafhopper (Ommatissus lybicus), date palm scale (Parlatoria blanchardi), date moth (Batrachedra amydraula), Egyptian cotton leafworm (Spodoptera littoralis), date stone beetle (Coccotrypes dactyliperda), a mite of the genus Oligonychus (Oligonychus afrasiaticus), dried fruit moth (Ephestia calidella) and raisin moth (Cadra (Ephestia) figulilella) and other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side.]

XI. Entry Inspection and Quarantine and Treatment of Non-Compliance

(1) Entry inspection and quarantine. Upon arrival of Omani dried dates for export to China at the entry port in China, the Chinese customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine supervision, and entry shall be permitted only if the goods qualify.

(2) Treatment of non-compliance. If the Chinese side finds that Omani dried dates for export to China violate China's laws and regulations on food safety and plant quarantine, national food safety standards or the provisions of the Protocol, the Chinese customs shall handle the matter in accordance with China's relevant laws and regulations.

This announcement is hereby made.
