Entry is allowed, but only wild-caught aquatic products for human consumption; live animals, amphibians, reptiles and aquatic mammals are out of scope. Announcement No. 98 covers African countries with diplomatic relations to China from 10 July 2026, ending country-by-country notices. If the supplier is not on the GACC registered list, hold your booking.

Walk one Namibian wild frozen fish lot through: the authority confirms legal capture in national or international waters and monitors residues and diseases; the establishment is recommended and registered with China; each lot carries a Chinese-English sanitary certificate, English mandatory, with the model and stamps filed in advance. At the port, customs checks documents and inspects.

Noncompliant products are destroyed, returned or otherwise disposed of; for serious or repeated failures, China may tighten inspection or suspend that establishment. Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Sierra Leone, Madagascar, Djibouti and Mozambique still follow their own earlier announcements.

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China, and following risk assessment, wild aquatic products from African countries having diplomatic relations with China that meet the relevant requirements set out below are allowed for import with immediate effect.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations. (ii) The Administrative Measures for the Safety of Imported and Exported Food, and the Administrative Provisions on Registration of Overseas Food Manufacturers for Import into China.

II. Scope of Imported Products

Wild aquatic products in this announcement refer to wild aquatic animal products and products thereof for human consumption, and marine plant products such as algae and products thereof; they do not include amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, live aquatic animals, or aquatic animal and plant breeding materials.

III. Requirements for Establishments

Establishments producing wild aquatic products for export to China (including production, processing and storage establishments) must be approved by the competent authority of the exporting country (hereinafter the exporting authority) and remain under its effective supervision. The food safety and hygiene management and protection systems of these establishments must comply with Chinese food safety laws and regulations.

Establishments producing wild aquatic products for export to China must be recommended for registration by the exporting authority to the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter China). Without registration, export to China is not permitted.

IV. Requirements for Imported Products

The exporting authority must ensure that wild aquatic products exported to China meet the following conditions:

(i) Captured legally within national territory or international waters. (ii) The raw materials and products are free of all of the following circumstances: 1. Wild aquatic products already exported to China or intended for export have violated Chinese food safety laws and regulations, national standards and this announcement, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China. 2. An aquatic animal disease related to the products covered by this announcement, listed in the Catalogue of Quarantine Diseases of the People's Republic of China for Animals Entering the Country (hereinafter the Catalogue) or notifiable under World Organisation for Animal Health rules, has occurred in the exporting country, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China. 3. A major food safety incident has occurred in the exporting country, or a major public health incident at the establishment, or the fishing waters have been contaminated by pollutants, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China. (iii) Drugs or additives banned by China have not been used directly or indirectly, and restricted or permitted drugs or additives are used as prescribed. (iv) Official inspection and quarantine by the exporting authority finds none of the pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or harmful substances or foreign matter listed in Chinese laws and regulations, none of the aquatic animal diseases listed in the Catalogue, and none of the aquatic animal diseases notifiable under World Organisation for Animal Health rules. (v) The entire production process, including capture, processing, packaging, storage, transport, transit and export, meets Chinese safety and hygiene requirements and traceability requirements. (vi) Packaging and labelling of the products comply with Chinese national food safety standards and the relevant provisions on imported food safety management.

V. Certificate Requirements

The exporting authority must carry out inspection and quarantine of wild aquatic products for export as required by China, and issue a sanitary certificate, confirmed by both sides, for every lot exported, certifying that the lot complies with the relevant provisions of Chinese food safety laws, regulations and national standards. Certificates are printed at least in Chinese and English, and English is a mandatory language when completing the certificate.

The sanitary certificate must state the information of the establishment producing the wild aquatic products for export in full. The exporting authority must file the certificate model and its official stamps with China in good time; any change is filed with China at least one month before it takes effect.

VI. Import Inspection, Quarantine and Disposition of Nonconforming Products

Chinese customs will inspect and quarantine the wild aquatic products in accordance with Chinese food safety laws and regulations, national standards and the provisions of this announcement.

For nonconforming products, destruction, return or other disposition is applied. For establishments where serious problems or repeated nonconformities occur, China may apply tightened inspection and quarantine or suspend imports, among other measures.

VII. Other Requirements

For wild aquatic products from Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Sierra Leone, Madagascar, Djibouti and Mozambique, the relevant import inspection and quarantine requirements continue to follow GACC Announcement No. 34 of 2022, No. 121 of 2022, No. 146 of 2024, No. 147 of 2024, No. 148 of 2024, No. 46 of 2026 and No. 58 of 2026.

It is hereby announced.
