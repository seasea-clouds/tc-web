1 December 2026, No.61: domestic consignee; Product License: reg./filing no., code 526.

No number needed: registration/filing samples, non-commercial test, R&D and promotional samples, official supplies and exhibits. The first two types must attach documents: test proof, plan and testing capacity (contract if a third party is used), and a promotion plan stating place, manner, audience and disposal. Semi-finished goods: “cosmetic semi-finished product”, “15-Non-prepacked”, and the filler's details under “Remarks”.

Before export, declare truthfully the production licence, label artwork and its Chinese translation, and compliance with the importing country standards; produce these documents when customs verifies. The importer must record the declaration number, entry date, name, brand, registration or filing number, specification, quantity and weight, batch, expiry or shelf life, country of origin, country of trade, manufacturer, overseas exporter, storage place, buyer and contact details, and sales date and quantity. The Announcement takes effect on 1 December and four earlier announcements are repealed.

To effectively implement the Measures of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Import and Export Cosmetics (Order No. 284 of the General Administration of Customs, hereinafter the “Measures”), the relevant matters are hereby announced as follows:

I. On Declaration of Imported Cosmetics

When importing cosmetics, cosmetics importers shall declare to customs the Customs Declaration Form for Import Goods of the People's Republic of China in the capacity of “domestic consignee”, and the declaration shall meet the following requirements:

(1) The special cosmetics registration certificate number or ordinary cosmetics filing number obtained from the drug regulatory authority shall be duly entered in the certificate field of “Import (Non-) Special-Use Cosmetics Administrative License” under “Product License” on the declaration form (license category code “526”).

(2) Samples for registration or filing of imported cosmetics, non-trial samples for enterprise testing, R&D and promotion, cosmetics imported as official supplies by foreign diplomatic and consular missions in China and by representative offices in China of international organizations enjoying diplomatic privileges and immunities, and imported cosmetics exhibits for non-trial or non-sale use are exempt from entering the special cosmetics registration certificate number or ordinary cosmetics filing number.

(3) Samples for registration or filing of imported cosmetics and non-trial samples for enterprise testing, R&D and promotion shall be declared in accordance with Article 13 of the Measures, and the “other materials required by customs” are specified as follows:

(1) For samples used for registration or filing of imported cosmetics, relevant supporting materials issued by cosmetics registration and filing inspection and testing institutions shall be provided.

(2) For non-trial samples imported for testing and R&D, a testing and R&D plan and a capability certification document of the testing and R&D entity shall be provided. Where a third-party testing institution is entrusted to conduct the testing, an entrusted testing agreement shall also be provided.

(3) For non-trial samples imported for promotion, a promotion plan shall be provided, specifying the location and manner of use, the target audience, and the disposal method of the samples.

(4) For imported cosmetics semi-finished products, “cosmetics semi-finished product” shall be entered in the declaration element “Other” under “Specification and Model”, “15-Non-prepacked” shall be ticked in the “Goods Attribute” field, the name, address and cosmetics production license number of the filling or subpackaging enterprise shall be stated in the “Remarks” field, and a declaration shall be made that the imported cosmetics semi-finished products meet the mandatory requirements of national technical specifications.

II. On Information Records of Imported Cosmetics

The information on imported cosmetics recorded by cosmetics importers shall include the declaration form number, entry time, name, brand, special cosmetics registration certificate number or ordinary cosmetics filing number, specifications, quantity/weight, production batch number and restricted use date or production date and shelf life, country or region of origin, country or region of trade, name of the manufacturer or processor, name of the overseas exporter (agent), storage location, name and contact details of the sales target, sales date and quantity, etc. Cosmetics importers are encouraged to use information technology means to record the above information.

III. On Applications for Pre-export Inspection of Cosmetics

When applying for inspection before the export of cosmetics, the manufacturer, consignor or their agent shall truthfully declare that the manufacturer has obtained a cosmetics production license, holds label samples and their Chinese translations, and that the exported cosmetics meet the standards or contractual requirements of the importing country (region), among other matters. Where verification is required in the course of customs supervision, the manufacturer, consignor or their agent shall submit the above relevant materials for inspection.

IV. On “Mandatory Requirements of National Technical Specifications”

The “mandatory requirements of national technical specifications” referred to in the Measures include supplementary technical requirements for cosmetics quality and safety formulated by the drug regulatory authority under the State Council in light of the needs of supervision and administration.

This Announcement takes effect on 1 December 2026. Where previously issued provisions are inconsistent with this Announcement, this Announcement shall prevail. The Announcement of the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on Matters Concerning Implementation of the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Import and Export Cosmetics (formerly General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine Announcement No. 110 of 2012), the Announcement of the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on Issuing the Provisions on the Administration of Filing of Domestic Consignees of Imported Cosmetics and of Import and Sales Records (formerly General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine Announcement No. 77 of 2016), the Notice of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on Implementing the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Import and Export Cosmetics (Guozhi Jiashi Han [2012] No. 242), and the Notice of the General Office of the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on Implementing the Provisions on the Administration of Filing of Domestic Consignees of Imported Cosmetics and of Import and Sales Records (Zhi Jian Ban Shi Han [2016] No. 1462) are hereby repealed simultaneously.

It is hereby announced.
