Announcement No. 131 [2026] of the General Administration of Customs, the Ministry of Commerce and the National Disease Control and Prevention Administration takes effect on publication on 1 September 2026: to implement the Ecological Environment Code, the three authorities repeal the 1986 Joint Notice on Banning Imports of Old Gunny Sacks [No. 77 of 1986], so the blanket ban on old gunny sack imports no longer applies.

If you handle imports of old gunny sacks and other jute packaging: import formalities no longer cite [No. 77 of 1986]; such goods are still cleared under the current rules on entry animal and plant quarantine, health quarantine and solid waste management, and the announcement is not an endorsement of old gunny sack imports.

It is signed on 1 September 2026, the publication date and the effective date being the same day; only [No. 77 of 1986] is repealed and other current rules are unaffected; the full names of the three authorities are the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China, the Ministry of Commerce of the People's Republic of China and the National Disease Control and Prevention Administration.

<!-- original -->

To implement the Ecological Environment Code, the General Administration of Customs, the Ministry of Commerce and the National Disease Control and Prevention Administration decide to repeal the Joint Notice on Banning the Import of Old Gunny Sacks [No. 77 of 1986]. This announcement takes effect on the date of publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs, Ministry of Commerce, National Disease Control and Prevention Administration

1 September 2026
