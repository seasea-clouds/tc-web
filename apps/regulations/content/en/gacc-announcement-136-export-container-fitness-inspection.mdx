GACC Announcement No. 136 takes effect October 1: containers exporting perishable and frozen foods need fitness inspection. Firms self check, carriers file a compliance declaration via the Single Window before loading, customs reviews and spot checks, loading only if passed, results valid 21 days.

Frozen exports: self check cleanliness, hygiene, refrigeration, tightness; before loading apply via China International Trade Single Window and upload the compliance declaration; customs reviews, spot checks, permits loading if passed; at export declaration checks result, rejects failures by law.

Commodity codes subject to fitness inspection are on the GACC portal website; inspection requirements and the compliance declaration form are in the two annexes. Results lapse automatically when overdue and need a fresh application; if earlier rules conflict, this announcement prevails.

<!-- original -->

To effectively prevent the risk of exported foods being contaminated by containers, and in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations and other relevant laws and regulations, the General Administration of Customs of China hereby announces the following on container fitness inspection for exporting perishable and frozen foods:

I. Scope of goods. The commodity codes of perishable and frozen foods subject to container fitness inspection are available at http://jckspj.customs.gov.cn/spj/zcfg18/index.html on the portal website of the General Administration of Customs of China.

II. Enterprise self inspection. Producers and operators of exported perishable and frozen foods shall strictly fulfil their primary responsibility for food safety, conduct self inspection of containers, and select containers that are clean, hygienic, properly refrigerated and tightly sealed to ship the relevant products. The container fitness inspection requirements are set out in Annex 1.

III. Fitness inspection. The carrier, the packing unit or its agent of exported perishable and frozen foods shall, before loading, through the outbound container fitness inspection module of the “China International Trade Single Window”, submit an inspection application to the customs at the place where the container is stored and upload the “Export Container Fitness Compliance Declaration” (hereinafter the “Compliance Declaration”, see Annex 2). Customs shall review the “Compliance Declaration” and conduct spot checks on the containers, and those found qualified shall be permitted to load perishable and frozen foods for export. The fitness inspection result is valid for 21 days and lapses automatically upon expiry.

IV. Export verification. At the time of export declaration for perishable and frozen foods, customs shall verify the container fitness inspection result; where verification fails, the declaration shall be rejected and returned in accordance with the law.

This announcement shall take effect on October 1, 2026. Where provisions previously issued on container fitness inspection for exporting perishable and frozen foods are inconsistent with this announcement, this announcement shall prevail.

It is hereby announced.
