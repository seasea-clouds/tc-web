Announcement No. 255 of 2025 took effect on 25 December 2025: China Customs issues the Certificate of Inspection and Quarantine for Inbound Goods (the Certificate) electronically and on paper, the two running in parallel; the consignee and its broker can download the electronic copy on the International Trade Single Window instead of waiting for paper by post.

At Tianjin Port an imported dairy consignment cleared inspection: the broker signed in to the Single Window with an account authorised by the consignee, downloaded the electronic certificate from the inspection and quarantine e-certificate system and filed the papers on the spot; the supermarket scanned the certificate QR code with the Handheld Single Window and matched the number, product name and issue date.

A paper copy is still available on request. The QR code is only a lookup entry: where scanning returns nothing, the electronic certificate downloaded from the Single Window prevails.

<!-- original -->

To make it easier for enterprises to obtain the electronic version of the Certificate of Inspection and Quarantine for Inbound Goods (hereinafter the Certificate), the relevant matters are announced as follows.

(1) The Certificate is issued in electronic and paper versions, which run in parallel.

(2) The consignee of the imported goods and the customs broker entrusted by it may query and download the electronic Certificate through the inspection and quarantine electronic certificate system of the China International Trade Single Window.

(3) By scanning the QR code attached to the Certificate through the Handheld Single Window and relevant mobile applications, the electronic information of the Certificate can be queried.

This Announcement takes effect on the date of its issuance.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of China

25 December 2025
