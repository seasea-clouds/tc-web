Two new declaration items are being added to the export customs declaration, so take care not to miss any when filling it out. GACC Announcement No. 40 of 2026 states that the Customs Declaration for Export Goods adds two declaration items, the Prohibitions and Restrictions Control Identification Code and the Prohibitions and Restrictions Control Declaration Elements, to regulate the declaration conduct of consignors of export goods.

Both items are conditionally mandatory and need not be filled in for every shipment. You can consult the filling instructions on the website of the China International Trade Single Window to learn under what conditions and how to fill them in.

This Announcement takes effect on the date of issuance. Before filling out the form, check the latest filling requirements on the Single Window to avoid declaration errors that may affect customs clearance.

<!-- original -->

To further regulate the declaration conduct of consignors of export goods, the General Administration of Customs decides to adjust the relevant items of the Customs Declaration for Export Goods as follows:

Add the declaration items Prohibitions and Restrictions Control Identification Code and Prohibitions and Restrictions Control Declaration Elements. These declaration items are conditionally mandatory, and the specific filling instructions can be consulted on the China International Trade Single Window at the following website address: https://www.singlewindow.cn

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

15 April 2026
