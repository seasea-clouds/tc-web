How much a China compliance engagement asks of you can be counted, six architecture items must close before the pilot starts, three of them await your call, and eight execution items settle one by one as business moves, while daily input concentrates on two fixed actions, the weekly meeting and the monthly reconciliation. Proof of capability does not rest on words, each of the three badges comes with its own way of being verified, and all four reference types are taken from public channels. Chapter seven of the brand dossier uses one page to show how capability is proved, chapter eight uses three pages for inputs, governance and reconciliation, and this article takes it page by page, putting every table in front of you line by line.

## The three columns of proof of capability

The badge on the benchmarks column is verifiable, and its five lines read, the platform combination is stores plus licensed payment plus three-order consistency plus an ERP connected to multiple border crossings, fulfillment is AEO certification plus four bonded warehouse locations plus self-operated systems, track record is several global nutrition brands that entered China through the same channel, the comparison benchmark is the public practice of comparable brands, and deliverables and filing records are checkable end to end. The badge on the evidence column is matchable, and its five lines read, system interface demos run on the spot, filing list samples and three-order messages, settlement statement templates that can be matched line by line, a reference frame taken from public regulatory processes, and all materials supplied with the proposal and open to verification. The badge on the verification column is traceable, and its five lines read, classification is judged by the ruling rationale set against test reports, every segment of the funds path leaves a voucher, exceptions show the audit trail of the correction within twenty-four hours, public comparison follows the practice of comparable brands, and conclusions are archived item by item with a memo issued afterwards.

## Three badges and four types of public reference

The way the three badges are verified is written at the bottom of the same page. Verifiable means the platform, fulfillment and track record are shown on the spot in the system interface demo inside the proposal. Matchable means the three-order messages, the filing samples and the settlement templates match line by line, and the ledger, the orders and the monthly figures come from one source. Traceable means classification, funds and exceptions all carry vouchers and audit trails, checked against public cases.

Public references fall into four types, the store type is the stores and pages of comparable brands, open to public lookup, the fulfillment type is that cross-checking of the three orders is itself a public regulatory process, the settlement type is that licensed collection and payment with periodic reconciliation is an industry standard, and the compliance type is that a quota ledger with end-to-end audit trails is a general requirement. The bottom right of the page states, the system interface demo inside the proposal can be run on the spot on request, and the references come from public sources. One industry practice, international nutrition brands entering China through the 1210 mode is a matter of public record, and this proposal takes the same channel and the same compliance structure. The checking process has three steps, first look up the public references, then cross-check item by item, and record every difference in writing. Case details and references can be requested in person.

## Reading the four governance cards one by one

The contact card sets a single window, our side names the project lead, you name your counterpart, the contact persons go into the project charter, no second channel is set up, and day-to-day communication runs along this line. The rhythm card sets the meetings, during the startup phase there is a weekly meeting plus a written progress note every two weeks, once execution begins this changes to once every two weeks, and topics, conclusions and actions are archived with the ledger, visible to both sides at the same time, with no rescheduling for ad hoc matters.

The ledger card sets four elements, deliverable, status, owner and deadline, visible to both sides, the monthly reconciliation runs on the ledger without extra material, and the ledger updates in real time in the reconciliation portal with a timestamp on every status change. The escalation card sets two channels, routine matters are resolved by the project lead, matters that cross teams are escalated to our decision level, and a compliance red line goes straight to the decision level and pauses the actions concerned, because not stopping would only let the risk grow.
