Imports banned immediately. After Niger reported foot and mouth disease, contagious bovine pleuropneumonia, peste des petits ruminants, sheep pox and goat pox, Announcement No. 137 from China's customs and agriculture rural authorities bans cloven hoofed animals and related products from Niger, covering direct imports, third country transshipment, post or carriage.

Your lot of frozen mutton transshipped via a third country arrives: verify origin first; once it hits the ban, no release. Goods are returned or destroyed under customs supervision; animal and plant waste and swill unloaded from the transport are treated and must not be discarded; illegal inbound goods seized at border checks are destroyed.

The six measures take effect on issue. Violators face the Customs Law, Biosecurity Law, Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and its implementing rules; customs offices and agriculture rural departments at all levels do quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision. Country and lot are only a walkthrough; the ban scope and origin evidence govern.

<!-- original -->

Recently, Niger reported to the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) outbreaks of foot and mouth disease, contagious bovine pleuropneumonia, peste des petits ruminants, sheep pox and goat pox. To prevent the introduction of these diseases and protect China's livestock safety and biosecurity, and in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, the following is hereby announced:

I. It is prohibited to import, directly or indirectly, cloven hoofed animals and related products from Niger (products derived from cloven hoofed animals that are unprocessed, or that have been processed but may still transmit the disease).

II. It is prohibited to send by post or carry into the country cloven hoofed animals and related products from Niger. Once found, all such goods shall be subject to return or destruction.

III. Animal and plant waste, swill and the like unloaded from inbound means of transport from Niger shall, without exception, undergo disinfection or pest-control treatment under customs supervision and shall not be discarded without authorization.

IV. Cloven hoofed animals and related products from Niger that have entered the country illegally and are seized by border inspection and other departments shall, without exception, be destroyed under customs supervision.

V. Anyone who violates the above provisions shall be dealt with by customs in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations.

VI. Customs offices at all levels and agricultural and rural affairs departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Animal Epidemic Prevention and other relevant provisions, cooperate closely and carry out quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision work well.

This Announcement shall take effect on the date of its publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of China and Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

10 September 2026
