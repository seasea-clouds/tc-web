On 18 November 2025, the General Administration of Customs of China (GACC) issued Announcement No. 223: effective immediately, fresh kiwifruit from Iran meeting the quarantine requirements is allowed for import. Orchards, packinghouses and cold treatment facilities for kiwifruit exported to China must be audited by the Iranian side and approved and registered by GACC.

The announcement lists 5 quarantine pests including the Mediterranean fruit fly and the European grapevine moth: orchard traps at 1 per hectare checked every 2 weeks, pre-export cold treatment in any of three schedules (1.11 °C/14 days to 2.22 °C/18 days), and 2% sampling with cutting inspection of at least 60 fruits.

Phytosanitary certificates must be issued under ISPM 12 and bear the English additional declaration; non-compliant consignments will be returned, destroyed or treated as applicable, and the orchards and packinghouses concerned suspended from export to China for the season.

In accordance with China's relevant laws and regulations and the provisions on the phytosanitary requirements for the export of fresh kiwifruit from Iran to China agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Islamic Republic of Iran (hereinafter referred to as the Iranian side), fresh kiwifruit from Iran meeting the following relevant requirements is allowed to be imported from the date of issuance.

I. Basis for inspection and quarantine

(1) The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Quarantine of Animals and Plants Entering and Leaving the Territory and its implementing regulations;

(3) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(4) The Measures for the Inspection, Quarantine and Supervision of Imported Fruits;

(5) The Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Kiwi Fruits from Iran to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Islamic Republic of Iran.

II. Name of the commodity allowed for import

Fresh kiwifruit (hereinafter referred to as kiwifruit), scientific name Actinidia deliciosa, English name Kiwi Fruits.

III. Places of origin allowed

Kiwifruit producing areas of Iran.

IV. Approved orchards and packinghouses

Orchards, packinghouses and cold treatment facilities for kiwifruit exported to China must be audited by the Iranian side and approved and registered by the Chinese side. The registration information includes the name, address and registration number, so that consignments not complying with this announcement can be accurately traced. Before each export season, the Iranian side shall provide the Chinese side with the list of enterprises, which will be published on the GACC website after review and approval by the Chinese side.

V. List of quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side

(1) Mediterranean fruit fly Ceratitis capitata

(2) European grapevine moth Lobesia botrana

(3) Fig wax scale Ceroplastes rusci

(4) Kiwifruit bacterial canker pathogen Pseudomonas syringae pv. actinidiae

(5) Pseudomonas viridiflava

VI. Pre-export management

(i) Orchard management.

(1) Orchards producing kiwifruit for export to China shall, under the supervision of the Iranian side, establish and improve a quality management and traceability system, implement Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) or phytosanitary management, maintain orchard hygiene, promptly remove fallen fruit and remove rotten fruit at harvest, and implement Integrated Pest Management (IPM), including regular pest monitoring and surveys, physical, chemical or biological pest control, and agronomic control measures, so as to control pests that may occur.

(2) The Iranian side shall, in accordance with the International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 6 (ISPM 6), formulate a management plan for the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side and organize the implementation of orchard monitoring. In the first year of trade, the monitoring scheme and the corresponding integrated control measures shall be carried out under the supervision of the Iranian side, and detailed measures shall be provided to the Chinese side upon request. Orchard pest monitoring and control shall be implemented under the supervision and guidance of technical personnel who have been trained by the Iranian side or its authorized institutions. Once a quarantine pest of concern to the Chinese side is detected, prevention and control measures shall be taken immediately.

(3) All orchards for export shall keep records of pest monitoring and control and provide them to the Chinese side upon request. The control records include detailed information on all chemical agents used during the growing season, such as their names, active ingredients, application dates, concentrations and dosages.

(4) For the Mediterranean fruit fly, orchards for export shall adopt comprehensive field management measures, including monitoring trapping and chemical or biological control, to reduce the population density of Ceratitis capitata. Monitoring shall start from flowering and end at harvest. Traps shall be set in orchards for export at a density of 1 per hectare (at least 3 traps for orchards of less than 3 hectares) and checked every 2 weeks. Once Ceratitis capitata is detected, effective control shall be carried out immediately.

(5) For the European grapevine moth, orchards for export shall adopt comprehensive field management measures, including monitoring trapping and chemical or biological control, to reduce the density of Lobesia botrana. Monitoring shall start from flowering and end at harvest; traps shall be set in orchards for export at a density of 1 per hectare (at least 3 traps for orchards of less than 3 hectares), checked every 2 weeks, with lures replaced every 4 weeks. Once Lobesia botrana is detected, effective control shall be carried out immediately.

(6) For the fig wax scale, orchards for export shall carry out monitoring under the supervision and guidance of technical personnel of the Iranian side, focusing from flowering to harvest on surveys of branches, stems, leaves and fruits for Ceroplastes rusci. If symptoms of this pest are found, measures such as biological, physical or chemical control shall be taken immediately to control its occurrence.

(7) For Pseudomonas syringae pv. actinidiae and Pseudomonas viridiflava, orchards for export shall carry out monitoring under the guidance of the Iranian side, from flowering to harvest, with surveys at least once every 2 weeks. If the pests or their corresponding symptoms are found during monitoring, biological, physical or chemical control shall be applied promptly. For orchards where control is incomplete, the Iranian side will suspend their eligibility for export to China and notify the Chinese side immediately. Upon request, the Iranian side shall provide the Chinese side with investigation reports or management records on these two pests.

(ii) Packinghouse management.

(1) The processing, packing, storage and loading of kiwifruit for export to China shall be carried out under the quarantine supervision of the Iranian side or its authorized officials. Packing and cold storage facilities shall be kept in good sanitary condition and shall be equipped with functions to prevent re-infestation by pests (such as insect-proof screens).

(2) During packing, kiwifruit shall be washed, brushed, disinfected, sorted and graded, with defective fruits removed, to ensure that it is free from insects, mites, rotten fruit, and twigs, leaves and soil.

(3) Packed kiwifruit to be stored shall be put into storage immediately and kept separately to avoid re-infestation by pests.

(4) Containers for transporting kiwifruit exported to China must be inspected at the time of loading to ensure good sanitary conditions.

(iii) Packing requirements.

(1) Packing materials shall be clean, hygienic and unused, and shall comply with China's relevant phytosanitary and sanitary requirements. If wooden packaging is used, it must comply with the International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15).

(2) Each package shall be marked in Chinese or English with information such as the fruit name, country, place of origin, and the name or registration number of the orchard and packinghouse. Each package and pallet shall be marked in Chinese or English with “Exported to the People's Republic of China”.

(iv) Phytosanitary treatment requirements.

Cold treatment targeting the Mediterranean fruit fly shall be applied to kiwifruit exported to China, under the supervision of the Iranian side or its authorized personnel, in accordance with the operational procedures for pre-export cold treatment (Annex 1) or the operational procedures for cold treatment in transit (Annex 2). The cold treatment parameters shall meet any one of the following conditions:

(1) 1.11 °C or lower (pulp temperature), for 14 consecutive days or more;

(2) 1.67 °C or lower (pulp temperature), for 16 consecutive days or more;

(3) 2.22 °C or lower (pulp temperature), for 18 consecutive days or more.

(v) Pre-export inspection and quarantine.

(1) The Iranian side or its authorized institutions shall sample and inspect kiwifruit for export to China at a rate of 2% per consignment. The minimum sample size is 1200 fruits, and at least 60 of them, or suspicious fruits found during the inspection, shall undergo fruit-cutting inspection or be subject to quarantine identification. If no phytosanitary problems have occurred within 2 years, the sampling rate may be reduced to 1%, but not less than 600 fruits.

(2) If a quarantine pest of concern to the Chinese side, twigs, leaves or soil is found, the consignment shall not be exported to China, and the orchards and packinghouses concerned shall not export to China during the current export season until the Iranian side has identified the cause and taken corrective measures. Meanwhile, the Iranian side shall keep records of the interceptions and provide them to the Chinese side upon request.

(vi) Phytosanitary certificate requirements.

(1) For consignments that pass quarantine inspection, the Iranian side shall issue phytosanitary certificates in accordance with the International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12 (ISPM 12), stating the name or registration number of the orchard and packinghouse, and entering the following additional declaration: “This consignment complies with requirements specified in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Kiwi Fruits from Iran to China, and is free from any quarantine pests of concern to China.” (The consignment meets the requirements of the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Kiwi Fruits from Iran to China and is free from any quarantine pests of concern to China.)

(2) For consignments subjected to pre-export phytosanitary treatment, the treatment method, treatment temperature, duration, and the name or registration number of the treatment facility shall be stated on the phytosanitary certificate. For consignments subjected to cold treatment in transit, the phytosanitary certificate shall be annotated with “Cold treatment in transit” (cold treatment during transport), and shall state the treatment temperature, duration, container number and seal number.

(3) Before the official start of trade, the Iranian side shall provide the Chinese side with samples of phytosanitary certificates.

VII. Inspection and quarantine upon entry and handling of non-compliance

When kiwifruit exported to China arrives at a Chinese port of entry, Chinese customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine in accordance with the following requirements.

(i) Verification of certificates and marks.

(1) Verifying whether the imported kiwifruit has obtained the quarantine permit for the import of animals and plants.

(2) Verifying whether the phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of item (vi) of Chapter VI of this announcement.

(3) Verifying whether the marks on packages and pallets comply with the provisions of item (iii) of Chapter VI of this announcement.

(4) For consignments subjected to pre-export cold treatment, verifying whether the phytosanitary certificate, the cold treatment result report confirmed by the signature of the Iranian side, and the pulp temperature probe calibration records meet the provisions of item (iv) of Chapter VI of this announcement.

(5) For consignments subjected to cold treatment in transit, verifying whether the phytosanitary certificate, the cold treatment report, and the pulp temperature probe calibration records meet the provisions of item (iv) of Chapter VI of this announcement.

(ii) Inspection and quarantine upon entry.

(1) Kiwifruit exported to China shall enter through ports approved by the Chinese side for fruit import.

(2) Inspection and quarantine of the imported kiwifruit shall be implemented in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and the requirements of this announcement; consignments that pass inspection and quarantine shall be allowed entry.

(iii) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) If a consignment is found to come from unapproved orchards, packinghouses or cold treatment facilities, it shall be denied entry.

(2) If the cold treatment is deemed ineffective, the consignment shall be subject to measures such as cold treatment at destination (which may be conducted within the same container), return or destruction.

(3) If live quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side or live quarantine pests newly occurring in Iran, or soil, plant residues, etc. are found, the consignment shall be returned, destroyed or subject to phytosanitary treatment.

(4) If a consignment is found not to comply with Chinese food safety laws and regulations and national standards, it shall be returned or destroyed.

It is hereby announced.

Annexes: (1) Operational procedures for pre-export cold treatment; (2) Operational procedures for cold treatment in transit.

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China

18 November 2025
