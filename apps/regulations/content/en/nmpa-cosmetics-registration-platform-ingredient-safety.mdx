This announcement governs submission timing and platform entry, not formulation technology. From 1 May 2021, special registration and ordinary filing use the new platform; the old platform only handles accepted but undecided applications. Overseas registrants must appoint a domestic responsible person in China, or accounts and materials fail.

Ingredient safety follows three steps: May 2021 source and trade name; January 2022 preservative, sunscreen, coloring, hair-dyeing and anti-spot whitening ingredients; January 2023 all ingredients, retained products completed before 1 May 2023. Anti-spot whitening and anti-hair loss also need a human efficacy test report.

A European whitening essence brand opens a new account via its domestic responsible person in China, submitting formulation, execution standard and label draft; whitening ingredients report phase-two safety, with the human efficacy test report attached at registration. An old-platform filed moisturizing cream supplements the standard and label draft before 1 May 2022 and files its annual report before 31 March each year.

NMPA Announcement on Matters Concerning the Implementation of the Provisions on the Administration of Documentation for Cosmetic Registration and Filing
No. 35 of 2021

In order to implement the relevant provisions of the Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Cosmetics and the Measures for the Administration of Cosmetic Registration and Filing, further standardize the administration of cosmetic registration and filing, and implement the Provisions on the Administration of Documentation for Cosmetic Registration and Filing (hereinafter referred to as the Provisions), the matters concerned are hereby announced as follows:

Cosmetic Registration and Filing Information Service Platform

To facilitate enterprises in preparing in advance for cosmetic registration and filing, from 1 April 2021, domestic cosmetic registrants, filers, domestic responsible persons in China and cosmetic manufacturers may, through the NMPA online service hall of the national integrated online government service platform, submit relevant materials and apply for registration and filing user accounts on the Cosmetic Registration and Filing Information Service Platform (hereinafter referred to as the new registration and filing platform) in accordance with the requirements of the Provisions. From 1 May 2021, cosmetic registrants, filers and domestic responsible persons in China shall apply for registration of special cosmetics or file ordinary cosmetics through the new registration and filing platform.

From 1 May 2021, the former administrative license and filing information management system for cosmetics (hereinafter referred to as the former registration and filing platform) no longer accepts registration applications for special cosmetics or filings of ordinary cosmetics. Special cosmetic registration applications that were submitted and accepted on the former platform before that date but had not yet received an approval decision shall continue to be reviewed and approved on the former platform.

Products Registered and Filed on the Former Registration and Filing Platform

To ensure the safe use of cosmetics and protect the lawful rights and interests of consumers, for cosmetics that have obtained registration or completed filing on the former registration and filing platform, registrants and filers shall, through the new registration and filing platform, submit the product execution standard and product label draft, fill in the product formulation of domestic ordinary cosmetics, and upload label images of the sales packaging of special cosmetics before 1 May 2022.

Submission of Safety-Related Information on Cosmetic Ingredients

From 1 May 2021, when applying for registration or filing, registrants and filers shall provide information on the source and trade names of ingredients in the product formulation; for ingredients subject to quality specification requirements under the Safety and Technical Standards for Cosmetics, they shall also submit quality specification certificates or safety-related information for those ingredients.

From 1 January 2022, when applying for registration or filing, registrants and filers shall, in accordance with the requirements of the Provisions, provide safety-related information on ingredients with preservative, sunscreen, coloring, hair-dyeing and anti-spot whitening functions.

From 1 January 2023, when applying for registration or filing, registrants and filers shall, in accordance with the requirements of the Provisions, provide safety-related information on all ingredients. For cosmetics that had obtained registration or completed filing earlier, registrants and filers shall supplement the safety-related information on all ingredients in the product formulation before 1 May 2023.

Efficacy Evaluation Test Reports for Anti-Spot Whitening and Anti-Hair Loss Cosmetics

From 1 January 2022, when applying for registration of anti-spot whitening or anti-hair loss cosmetics, the registration applicant shall submit a qualifying human efficacy test report in accordance with the provisions.

For anti-spot whitening and anti-hair loss cosmetics for which registration was applied for and obtained before 1 May 2021, the registrant shall supplement and submit a human efficacy test report before 1 May 2023.

For anti-spot whitening and anti-hair loss cosmetics for which registration was applied for and obtained between 1 May and 31 December 2021, the registrant shall supplement and submit a qualifying human efficacy test report before 1 May 2022.

Annual Reports for Ordinary Cosmetics

From 1 January 2022, ordinary cosmetics filed through either the former or the new registration and filing platform are uniformly subject to the annual report system. Filers shall, between 1 January and 31 March each year, submit through the new registration and filing platform the annual report for ordinary cosmetics that have been filed for a full year.

This announcement is hereby made.
