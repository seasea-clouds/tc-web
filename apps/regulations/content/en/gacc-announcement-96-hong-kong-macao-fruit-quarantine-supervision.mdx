China Customs Announcement No. 96 of 2026 takes effect on 1 September 2026: fresh fruit supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China must be traceable to specific orchards batch by batch, and packing plants must hold a customs registration and keep an orchard list.

One consignment of fresh citrus bound for Hong Kong, China: the packing plant must be on the registration list and keep an orchard list with names, addresses and contact details; on intake it verifies the commitment certificate of compliance with standards and records the fruit name, quantity, date and supplier; each crate states the fruit type, origin, orchard name and packing plant name or code; a cargo list is issued for Hong Kong, road vehicles are sealed and the seal number is written on the list; records are kept for at least 3 years.

If fruit cannot be traced to an orchard, if records are untrue, or if the Hong Kong, China or Macao, China authority notifies a safety problem, the local customs office investigates, tightens supervision and may require test reports; a safety problem must be handled and reported at once. Consolidated declaration still follows the applicable rules.

Announcement No. 96 of 2026 of the General Administration of Customs (on the Requirements for the Inspection, Quarantine and Supervision of Fruit Supplied to Hong Kong and Macao)

To further standardize the inspection, quarantine and supervision of fruit supplied to Hong Kong and Macao, safeguard its quality, safety and stable supply, and promote the sound development of trade, the following requirements are hereby announced in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(iii) Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(iv) Law of the People's Republic of China on Quality and Safety of Agricultural Products;

(v) Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations;

(vi) Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Exported Fruit;

(vii) Special Provisions of the State Council on Strengthening the Supervision and Administration of Food and Other Product Safety;

(viii) Opinions of the General Office of the CPC Central Committee and the General Office of the State Council on Further Strengthening Whole-Chain Food Safety Supervision.

II. Scope of Application

This Announcement applies to the inspection, quarantine and supervision of fresh fruit supplied to Hong Kong and Macao.

III. Enterprise Registration

Customs implements registration administration for packing plants of fruit supplied to Hong Kong and Macao (hereinafter referred to as packing plants); registration shall follow the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Exported Fruit.

IV. Product Quality and Safety Management by Enterprises

(i) A packing plant shall fulfill its primary responsibility for product quality and safety, conduct production and business activities in accordance with relevant laws, regulations and standards, act with integrity and self-discipline, accept social supervision and bear social responsibility.

(ii) A packing plant shall ensure that the fruit it supplies to Hong Kong and Macao meets the inspection and quarantine requirements of the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region.

(iii) A packing plant shall establish a list of source orchards, including orchard names, addresses and contact details. Fruit from the plant's own orchards, or from orchards certified as green, organic, famous-special-excellent-new or geographical indication, is encouraged.

(iv) A packing plant shall conduct intake inspection of the fruit and verify the commitment certificate of compliance with standards; it shall truthfully record the name, quantity, intake date and supplier information of the fruit, properly keep inspection records, purchase vouchers and commitment certificates, and ensure that the fruit is traceable to the orchard.

(v) A packing plant shall mark the fruit type, origin, orchard name, and packing plant name or code on the crates of fruit supplied to Hong Kong and Macao.

(vi) A packing plant shall provide a cargo list as required by the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region, seal the road vehicles carrying the fruit, and mark the seal number on the cargo list and other documents; the sample cargo list is set out in the Annex.

(vii) Where fruit supplied to Hong Kong and Macao has a safety problem that may cause or has caused harm to human health and life safety, the packing plant shall take appropriate measures in accordance with the law and immediately report to the local customs office.

(viii) Records of product quality and safety management of a packing plant shall be truthful and kept for not less than 3 years. Packing plants are encouraged to use information technology to establish such records.

V. Exit Inspection, Quarantine and Supervision

(i) Where fruit supplied to Hong Kong and Macao is notified by the competent authority of the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region for a safety problem, the customs office where the packing plant is located shall organize an investigation, take tightened supervision measures where necessary, and may require the packing plant to submit test reports.

(ii) Fruit supplied to Hong Kong and Macao declared to customs under the consolidated declaration model shall be handled in accordance with relevant provisions.

VI. Information Notification

Customs shall strengthen coordination with local government authorities and enhance collaborative governance. Where fruit supplied to Hong Kong and Macao fails inspection and quarantine, or is notified as non-compliant by the competent authority of the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region, the customs office where the packing plant is located shall provide feedback to the packing plant and notify the local government authorities in accordance with relevant provisions.

This Announcement takes effect on 1 September 2026.
