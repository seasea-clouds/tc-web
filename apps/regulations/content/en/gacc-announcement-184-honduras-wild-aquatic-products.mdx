Wild aquatic products from Honduras are approved for import into China effective immediately. Announcement No. 184 of 2025 takes effect on 11 September 2025. Wild aquatic animal products and algae and other marine plant products for human consumption are permitted; amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, species in the CITES Appendices and China's List of State Key Protected Wild Animals (LSKPWA), live aquatic animals, and aquatic breeding material are excluded.

Production enterprises must be approved and supervised by the Honduran side and recommended by it for registration with the Chinese side; without registration, no export to China. Raw materials must be legally harvested in national or international waters and free from drugs banned by China and from diseases.

Each consignment requires a health certificate in Chinese and English confirmed by both sides, with English mandatory; non-compliant products are destroyed, returned, or otherwise disposed of; in cases of serious or repeated non-compliance, inspection and quarantine may be intensified or imports suspended.

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the relevant provisions concerning the inspection and sanitary requirements for wild aquatic products exported from Honduras to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock of the Republic of Honduras (hereinafter referred to as the Honduran side), imports of wild aquatic products from Honduras that meet the following relevant requirements are permitted effective immediately.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations;

(ii) The Measures for the Safety Administration of Imported and Exported Food and the Provisions on the Administration of the Registration of Overseas Production Enterprises for Food Imported into the People's Republic of China;

(iii) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock of the Republic of Honduras on the Inspection and Sanitary Requirements for Wild Aquatic Products Exported from the Republic of Honduras to China (hereinafter referred to as the „Protocol“).

II. Scope of Import Products

Wild aquatic products refer to wild aquatic animal products and products thereof intended for human consumption, and algae and other marine plant products and products thereof, excluding amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, species listed in the Appendices to the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES) and in China's List of State Key Protected Wild Animals (LSKPWA), live aquatic animals, and breeding material of aquatic animals and plants.

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

Production enterprises that export wild aquatic products to China (including production, processing, and storage enterprises) shall be officially approved by Honduras and subject to its effective supervision. The food safety and hygiene management and protection systems of such enterprises shall comply with the relevant laws and regulations of China on food safety.

In accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, production enterprises that export wild aquatic products to China shall be recommended by the Honduran side to the Chinese side for registration. Without registration, they shall not export to China.

IV. Requirements for Import Products

The Honduran side shall ensure that wild aquatic products exported to China meet the following conditions:

(i) They are legally harvested in national or international waters.

(ii) Neither the raw materials nor the products have been affected by any of the following issues:

(1) Wild aquatic products from Honduras already exported or intended for export to China have seriously violated the relevant laws and regulations of China on food safety and the provisions of the Protocol.

(2) Aquatic animal diseases related to the products covered by the Protocol, which are listed in the List of Quarantine Diseases for Animals Imported into the People's Republic of China and are notifiable under the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH), have occurred in Honduras and have seriously affected the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China.

(3) A major food safety incident has occurred in Honduras, a major public health incident has occurred in a production enterprise, or the harvesting waters have been affected by pollutants, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China.

(iii) No drugs or additives banned by China have been used, directly or indirectly, and drugs or additives restricted or permitted by the Chinese side have been used in accordance with the relevant provisions.

(iv) Upon inspection and quarantine by the competent authorities, no pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or harmful substances, or foreign matter listed in the laws and regulations of China, and no cases of any infectious or parasitic diseases, have been found, and no aquatic animal diseases listed in the List of Quarantine Diseases for Animals Imported into the People's Republic of China or notifiable under WOAH have been found.

(v) The entire production process (including harvesting, production and processing, packaging, storage, transport, transshipment, and export) complies with the relevant safety, hygiene, and traceability requirements of China.

(vi) The packaging and labeling of the products comply with the national food safety standards of China and the relevant provisions on the safety administration of imported food.

V. Requirements for Certificates

The Honduran side is responsible for conducting inspection and quarantine of wild aquatic products exported to China in accordance with the requirements of the Chinese side, and for issuing, for each consignment of wild aquatic products exported to China, a health certificate confirmed by both sides attesting that the consignment complies with the relevant provisions of the laws and regulations of China on food safety. The certificate shall be printed in at least Chinese and English (English is mandatory when the certificate is filled out).

The health certificate shall be fully completed with information on the production enterprise of the wild aquatic products exported to China. The Honduran side shall promptly provide the Chinese side with a specimen of the certificate and the official seal for record-filing. In the event of any changes, the Honduran side shall file them with the Chinese side at least one month before they take effect.

VI. Other Requirements

The Chinese customs shall conduct inspection and quarantine of wild aquatic products exported to China.

Non-compliant products shall be destroyed, returned, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with the laws and regulations of China. With respect to production enterprises that have serious problems or repeated instances of non-compliance, the Chinese side may take measures such as intensifying inspection and quarantine or suspending imports.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China

11 September 2025
