After the repeal, importers of waste metals, waste plastics and waste paper no longer separate them by category, declare item by item, or seek customs approval for mixed loading. The announcement took effect on the day it was issued, and declarations now follow the current solid waste rules.

The repealed 2010 rules required key solid wastes to be shipped separately, separated by category inside a container and processed by pressing, baling or packing, with other categories limited to two percent of a container load; bulk shipments had to be sorted under customs supervision before declaration.

In practice, file under the current catalogue of solid wastes banned from import and the disposal rules at the port: do not apply the old classified shipment and park sorting steps to legacy contracts, returns or processing trade leftovers, and keep the original category details with the documents for traceability.
