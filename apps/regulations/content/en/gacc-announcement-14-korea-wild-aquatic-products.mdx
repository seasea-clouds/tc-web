GACC Announcement No. 14 of 29 January 2026 takes effect on publication, allowing imports of wild aquatic products from the Republic of Korea: wild aquatic animal products for human consumption and derivatives, and marine plant products such as algae, are admitted; amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, live animals, and breeding materials are excluded.

Production enterprises must be approved and supervised by the Korean side, then registered with China; without registration, no export is allowed. Raw materials must be legally harvested, with no China-banned drugs or additives. Each consignment requires a health certificate jointly confirmed by both sides, in Chinese and English, English mandatory.

On entry, products are inspected per Chinese laws, standards, and the Cooperation Arrangement. Non-compliant products are destroyed, returned, or otherwise treated. Serious or repeated problems may lead to tightened inspection or suspended imports.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the relevant provisions on inspection and sanitary requirements for wild aquatic products exported from the Republic of Korea to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries and the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety of the Republic of Korea (hereinafter referred to as the Korean side), as of today, imports of wild aquatic products from the Republic of Korea that meet the following relevant requirements are permitted:

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations.

(ii) The Administrative Measures for the Safety of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China, and the Provisions on the Registration Administration of Overseas Production Enterprises for Food Imports into the People's Republic of China.

(iii) The Cooperation Arrangement between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries and the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety of the Republic of Korea on Inspection and Sanitary Requirements for the Import and Export of Wild Aquatic Products (hereinafter referred to as the Cooperation Arrangement).

II. Scope of Imported Products

Wild aquatic products exported to China refer to wild aquatic animal products and products thereof intended for human consumption, and marine plant products such as algae and products thereof; they do not include amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, live aquatic animals, or breeding materials of aquatic animals and plants.

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

Production enterprises exporting wild aquatic products to China (including production, processing, and storage enterprises) must be officially approved by the Republic of Korea and under its effective supervision. The food safety and hygiene management and protection systems of such enterprises must comply with the relevant laws and regulations of China on food safety.

Enterprises exporting wild aquatic products to China must obtain registration in China in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China on food safety. No export may be made without registration.

IV. Requirements for Imported Products

The Korean side shall ensure that wild aquatic products exported to China meet the following conditions:

(i) The wild aquatic products are legally harvested in domestic waters or international waters.

(ii) The raw materials and products do not involve any of the following circumstances:

(1) Wild aquatic products exported from the Republic of Korea to China violate the relevant laws and regulations of China on food safety, national standards, or the provisions of the Cooperation Arrangement, thereby seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products.

(2) An aquatic animal disease related to the products covered by the Cooperation Arrangement, being one listed in the List of Animal Quarantine Diseases for Entry into the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the List) or notifiable as specified by the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH), has occurred in the Republic of Korea, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products.

(3) A major food safety incident has occurred in the Republic of Korea, or a major public health event has occurred at a production enterprise, or the harvesting waters have been affected by pollutants, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products.

(iii) No drugs or additives banned in China have been used, directly or indirectly, and drugs or additives restricted or permitted in China have been used in accordance with the relevant provisions.

(iv) Upon inspection and quarantine by the competent authorities, no pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or hazardous substances, or foreign matter listed in the laws and regulations of China have been found, and no infectious diseases, parasitic diseases, or aquatic animal diseases listed in the List or notifiable as specified by WOAH have been found.

(v) The harvesting, production and processing, packaging, storage, transportation, transshipment, and export of the wild aquatic products all comply with the relevant safety and hygiene requirements and traceability requirements of China.

(vi) The packaging and labeling of the products comply with the laws, regulations, and standards of China.

V. Certificate Requirements

The Korean side is responsible for conducting inspection and quarantine of wild aquatic products exported to China in accordance with the requirements of the Chinese side, and for issuing, for each batch of wild aquatic products exported to China, a sanitary certificate confirmed by both sides certifying that the batch complies with the relevant provisions of the laws and regulations of China on food safety and its national standards. The certificate will be printed in Chinese and English, with English being mandatory when the certificate is completed.

The certificate shall be completed in full with information on the production enterprise of the wild aquatic products exported to China. The Korean side shall provide the Chinese side with a specimen certificate and, as requested by the Chinese side, with official seals; any modification or adjustment shall be notified to the Chinese side, and until the Chinese side is informed of the relevant information, the existing format and seals remain valid.

VI. Other Requirements

China Customs conducts inspection and quarantine of wild aquatic products exported to China in accordance with the laws, regulations, and standards of China and the Cooperation Arrangement.

Non-compliant products are destroyed, returned, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with the laws and regulations of China. For production enterprises with serious problems or repeated non-compliance, the Chinese side may take measures such as intensifying inspection and quarantine or suspending imports.

This announcement is hereby made.

General Administration of Customs

29 January 2026
