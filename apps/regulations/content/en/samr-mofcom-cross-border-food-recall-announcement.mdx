SAMR and MOFCOM jointly issue Announcement No. 1 of 2026 on food recall supervision.

Cross-border e-commerce operators bear primary responsibility and must appoint a domestic food business to handle recalls and register it with the platform.

Operators must stop sales and recall on safety risks; platforms must supervise recalls, with non-compliance entered into credit management.

<!-- original -->

SAMR and MOFCOM Announcement No. 1 of 2026

To prevent food safety risks in cross-border retail imported food and protect consumer food safety, under the Notice of MOFCOM, the National Development and Reform Commission, the Ministry of Finance, GACC, the State Taxation Administration and SAMR on Improving Supervision of Cross-Border E-Commerce Retail Imports (Shang Caifa [2018] No. 486), the following is announced on further strengthening recall supervision of cross-border retail imported food.

(1) Operators of cross-border retail imported food (cross-border e-commerce enterprises) bear primary responsibility for food safety and shall appoint a domestic food production or operation enterprise (entrusted enterprise) to handle recalls, define recall procedures, and register the entrusted enterprise's information with the cross-border e-commerce third-party platform operator (platform).

(2) Upon discovering quality or safety risks or problems in relevant food, cross-border e-commerce enterprises shall immediately stop sales, notify the entrusted enterprise to recall sold food and handle it properly, and promptly report the recall and handling to market regulation and other authorities.

(3) Platforms shall urge cross-border e-commerce enterprises to strengthen quality and safety risk prevention and ensure that the enterprises and entrusted enterprises carry out recalls. For enterprises that do not take proactive recall measures, the platform shall suspend providing platform services.

(4) Cross-border e-commerce enterprises, entrusted enterprises and platforms that fail to fulfil recall obligations shall be handled strictly under laws and regulations by market regulation authorities and notified to relevant departments for inclusion in credit management.

Hereby announced.

SAMR and MOFCOM

7 January 2026
