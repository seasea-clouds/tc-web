From 1 January 2020 the Provisions for Crude Drugs Importation replace the 2005 interim measures. First importation requires approval by the provincial drug regulatory department and a subsequent filing, while repeat importation is filed directly and managed under a catalogue.

For first importation the provincial drug control institution completes sample testing within 30 days and the authority decides within 20 days. The approval holder must import within one year through the port named in the approval document, filing there and declaring to customs with the Drug Import Note.

Importers are limited to marketing authorization holders of Chinese patent medicines within China, manufacturers of traditional Chinese medicines and drug distributors licensed for Chinese crude drugs or prepared slices. Crude drugs may be sold only after passing port testing, and a false filing carries a fine of 10,000 to 30,000 yuan.

## Provisions for Crude Drugs Importation

(Promulgated by Decree No. 9 of the State Administration for Market Regulation on 16 May 2019, effective 1 January 2020)

### I. General Provisions

**Article 1**　In order to strengthen the supervision and administration of crude drugs importation and ensure their quality, these Provisions are formulated in accordance with the Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, the Regulations for Implementation of the Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws and administrative regulations.

**Article 2**　These Provisions apply to the application, approval, filing, port testing, supervision and administration of crude drugs importation.

**Article 3**　Crude drugs shall be imported through ports where drug importation is permitted by the State Council or through border ports where crude drugs importation is permitted.

**Article 4**　The National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) is responsible for the supervision and administration of crude drugs importation nationwide. The NMPA entrusts the drug regulatory departments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government (hereinafter the provincial drug regulatory departments) with the review and approval of first importation of crude drugs, and supervises and guides that entrusted work.
Provincial drug regulatory departments shall supervise crude drugs importation in accordance with law and conduct the review and approval of first importation in the name of the NMPA within the scope of the entrustment.
The drug regulatory department of the place where a port permitting drug importation or a border port permitting crude drugs importation is located (hereinafter the drug regulatory department of the port) is responsible for the filing of crude drugs importation, organizes port testing and conducts supervision and administration.

**Article 5**　A crude drugs importer under these Provisions means the applicant applying for approval of first importation of crude drugs or the unit filing the importation of crude drugs.
A crude drugs importer shall be a marketing authorization holder of Chinese patent medicines, a manufacturer of traditional Chinese medicines or a drug distributor whose business scope covers Chinese crude drugs or prepared slices, within the territory of China.

**Article 6**　For first importation of crude drugs, the approval document for crude drugs importation shall be obtained under these Provisions and filing shall then be made with the drug regulatory department of the port. First importation of crude drugs means importation of crude drugs that are not from the same country (region), not by the same applicant or not of the same biological origin.
For non-first importation of crude drugs, filing shall be made directly with the drug regulatory department of the port under these Provisions. Non-first importation is subject to catalogue management, and the catalogue is formulated and adjusted by the NMPA. Where a variety is not yet in the catalogue but the applicant, the origin and the country (region) remain unchanged, it shall be managed as non-first importation.

**Article 7**　Imported crude drugs shall comply with national drug standards. For varieties not included in the current edition of the Pharmacopoeia of the People's Republic of China, the standards for importation of crude drugs shall apply; for varieties included neither in the current Pharmacopoeia nor in the standards for importation of crude drugs, other national drug standards shall apply. In ethnic minority areas, locally customary ethnic medicinal materials without a national drug standard shall conform to the crude drug standards of the corresponding province or autonomous region.

### II. Application and Approval for First Importation of Crude Drugs

**Article 8**　For first importation of crude drugs, the applicant shall complete the Application Form for Crude Drugs Importation through the information system of the NMPA (hereinafter the information system) and submit the following materials to the provincial drug regulatory department of its location:
(1) the Application Form for Crude Drugs Importation;
(2) a copy of the applicant's Drug Manufacturing Certificate or Drug Distribution Certificate, or, where the applicant is a marketing authorization holder of a Chinese patent medicine, a copy of the relevant drug approval document;
(3) a copy of the entity registration certificate of the exporter;
(4) a copy of the purchase contract and its notarial certificate;
(5) information on the ecological environment, resource reserves, wild or cultivated status, harvesting and primary processing at the place of production;
(6) the crude drug standards and their source;
(7) the original identification certificate of the biological origin of the crude drugs, issued by an institution within China qualified to identify animal or plant origin and stating the identification basis, conclusion, sample images, identifier, institution and its official seal.
The applicant shall be responsible for the authenticity of the submitted materials.

**Article 9**　On receipt of the materials for first importation of crude drugs, the provincial drug regulatory department shall conduct a formal review of their regularity and completeness. Errors that can be corrected on the spot shall be corrected on the spot; where the materials are incomplete or not in the statutory form, the applicant shall be informed on the spot or within 5 days, in a single notice, of everything to be supplemented or corrected, and if no notice is given within that period the application shall be deemed accepted from the date the materials were received.
The provincial drug regulatory department shall issue a notice of acceptance or non-acceptance for an application for first importation of crude drugs; a non-acceptance shall be reasoned in writing.

**Article 10**　After receiving the notice of acceptance, the applicant shall promptly submit the test samples to the provincial drug control institution of its location together with the materials specified in Article 8.

**Article 11**　On receipt of the test samples and related materials, the provincial drug control institution shall complete sample testing within 30 days, issue a test report for imported crude drugs to the applicant and report it to the provincial drug regulatory department. Where testing must be extended due to the characteristics of the variety or the test items, the extended period and the reasons shall be reported in writing to the provincial drug regulatory department and notified to the applicant.

**Article 12**　Where the applicant objects to the test result, it may apply for re-testing under the Drug Administration Law. The drug control institution shall reach a re-testing conclusion within 20 days after accepting the re-testing application, report it to the provincial drug regulatory department and notify the applicant.

**Article 13**　Where the provincial drug regulatory department considers that the applicant needs to supplement materials during the approval process, it shall inform the applicant in a single notice of all required supplements.
The applicant shall submit all supplementary materials at once within 4 months after receiving the notice. Failure to submit in time shall lead to a decision of disapproval. Where force majeure or other reasons prevent timely submission, the applicant shall apply to the provincial drug regulatory department of its location for an extension and explain the reasons.

**Article 14**　The provincial drug regulatory department shall decide on approval or disapproval within 20 days after accepting the application. Where the requirements are met, a one-time approval document for crude drugs importation shall be issued. The periods for testing and for supplementary materials are not counted in the approval time limit.

**Article 15**　To change an approved item of an approval document for crude drugs importation, the applicant shall complete the Supplementary Application Form for Crude Drugs Importation through the information system and submit a supplementary application to the provincial drug regulatory department that issued the document. The applicant shall be the holder of the original approval document and shall submit the following materials:
(1) the Supplementary Application Form for Crude Drugs Importation;
(2) the original approval document for crude drugs importation;
(3) materials relating to the item to be changed.
Where the applicant changes its name, in addition to the materials in the first paragraph, it shall submit copies of its Drug Manufacturing Certificate or Drug Distribution Certificate and of the amendment page, or copies of the drug approval document and of the approval document for the supplementary application on the change of the holder's name.
Where the applicant changes the port of arrival, in addition to the materials in the first paragraph, it shall submit a copy of the purchase contract and its notarial certificate.

**Article 16**　The provincial drug regulatory department shall complete the review and approval within 20 days after accepting the supplementary application. Where the requirements are met, an approval document for the supplementary application for crude drugs importation shall be issued.

**Article 17**　Where the provincial drug regulatory department decides to approve, it shall serve the approval document for crude drugs importation or for the supplementary application on the applicant within 10 days after the decision; where it decides to disapprove, it shall serve a notice of review opinions within 10 days after the decision, state the reasons and inform the applicant of its right to apply for administrative reconsideration or to bring an administrative lawsuit.

### III. Filing

**Article 18**　The applicant of first importation of crude drugs shall, within 1 year after obtaining the approval document, organize the importation through the port of arrival stated in that document.

**Article 19**　The importer shall file with the drug regulatory department of the port, complete the Testing Application Form for Crude Drugs Importation through the information system and submit the following materials:
(1) the original Testing Application Form for Crude Drugs Importation;
(2) a copy of the certificate of the place of production;
(3) the crude drug standards and their source;
(4) copies of the packing list, bill of lading and shipping invoice;
(5) where crude drugs are re-exported through other countries (regions), copies of all purchase contracts, packing lists, bills of lading and shipping invoices from the place of production to each transit place;
(6) where the imported crude drugs involve endangered wild fauna or flora whose import and export are restricted by the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora, a copy of the import and export permit issued by the national authority for the management of endangered species.
For the filing of first importation, a copy of the approval document for crude drugs importation and, if any, of the approval document for the supplementary application shall also be submitted in addition to the materials in the first paragraph.
For the filing of non-first importation, a copy of the importer's Drug Manufacturing Certificate or Drug Distribution Certificate, a copy of the entity registration certificate of the exporter and a copy of the purchase contract and its notarial certificate shall also be submitted; where the importer is a marketing authorization holder of a Chinese patent medicine, a copy of the relevant drug approval document shall be provided.

**Article 20**　The drug regulatory department of the port shall conduct a formal review of the completeness and regularity of the filing materials. Where the requirements are met, it shall issue the Drug Import Note, take back the approval document for first importation of crude drugs, and send a notice of port testing for imported crude drugs to the drug control institution of the port together with one copy of the filing materials.

**Article 21**　The importer shall, with the Drug Import Note, complete the customs declaration, inspection and release formalities.

### IV. Port Testing

**Article 22**　Within 2 days after receiving the notice of port testing, the drug control institution of the port shall agree with the importer on a time for on-site sampling and take samples at the designated storage location on schedule. At the on-site sampling the importer shall present the original certificate of the place of production.

**Article 23**　The drug control institution of the port shall check the consistency of the original certificate of the place of production and the actual arrival of the crude drugs with the filing materials provided by the drug regulatory department of the port. Where they are consistent, it shall take samples, fill in the sampling record for imported crude drugs, mark “Sampling Completed” on the original Drug Import Note held by the importer and affix its official seal; where they are not consistent, it shall not take samples and shall report to the drug regulatory department of the port within 2 days.

**Article 24**　The drug control institution of the port shall in general complete testing within 20 days after sampling and issue a test report for imported crude drugs. Where testing cannot be completed on time for objective reasons, it shall notify the importer in writing of the extended period and the reasons and report to the drug regulatory department of the port.
The drug control institution of the port shall submit the test report to the drug regulatory department of the port and notify the importer.
Imported crude drugs may be sold and used only after passing port testing.

**Article 25**　Where the importer objects to the test result, it may apply for re-testing under the Drug Administration Law. The drug control institution shall reach a re-testing conclusion within 20 days after accepting the application, report it to the drug regulatory department of the port and notify the importer.

### V. Supervision and Administration

**Article 26**　On receiving a notice of non-sampling for imported crude drugs, the drug regulatory department of the port shall, for all crude drugs that are evidenced to possibly endanger human health and have completed customs release, take the compulsory administrative measure of seizure or detention and decide on the disposal within 7 days.

**Article 27**　For imported crude drugs that fail the testing standards and have completed customs release, the drug regulatory department of the port shall, promptly after receiving the test report, take the compulsory administrative measure of seizure or detention, decide on the disposal in accordance with law and report the disposal to the provincial drug regulatory department of its location.

**Article 28**　The NMPA may, as needed, organize overseas inspection of the production sites of imported crude drugs, including the place of production and primary processing. Crude drugs importers shall coordinate with exporters in the inspection.

**Article 29**　When purchasing imported crude drugs, marketing authorization holders of Chinese patent medicines, manufacturers of traditional Chinese medicines and drug distributors shall verify the copy of the test report issued by the drug control institution of the port and the copy of the Drug Import Note marked “Sampling Completed” and bearing the official seal, and shall strictly apply the rules on drug traceability management.

**Article 30**　Packaging of imported crude drugs must be suitable for their quality requirements and convenient for storage, transport and import testing. Each package shall state the Chinese name of the crude drugs, the approval document number (except for non-first importation), the place of production, shipping marks and numbers, the name of the importer, the name of the exporter, the port of arrival, the weight and the date of processing and packing.

**Article 31**　The acceptance of applications for crude drugs importation, the approval results, the circumstances of violations of laws and regulations and the resulting penalties shall be disclosed on the website of the NMPA.

### VI. Legal Liabilities

**Article 32**　Where an importer obtains an approval document for first importation of crude drugs by providing false certificates, documents, materials or samples or by other deceptive means, it shall be handled under the Drug Administration Law and other laws and regulations.

**Article 33**　Where an importer files for the importation of crude drugs by providing false certificates or documents or by other deceptive means, it shall be given a warning and fined not less than 10,000 yuan and not more than 30,000 yuan.

### VII. Supplementary Provisions

**Article 34**　The numbering format of approval documents for crude drugs importation is: (abbreviation of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government) + Yaocai Jinzi + 4-digit year + 4-digit serial number.

**Article 35**　These Provisions shall come into force on 1 January 2020. The Provisions for Crude Drugs Importation (Interim) promulgated by the former State Food and Drug Administration on 24 November 2005 shall be repealed simultaneously.
