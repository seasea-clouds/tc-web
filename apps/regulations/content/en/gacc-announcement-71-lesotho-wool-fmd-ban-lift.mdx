Announcement No. 71 of 2026 lifts the foot-and-mouth disease ban on Lesotho wool.

The ban imposed by Announcement No. 28 is lifted from the date of publication; imported wool must still meet Chinese laws and technical requirements.

Imported wool must meet the foot-and-mouth disease virus inactivation requirements of the WOAH Terrestrial Animal Health Code.

<!-- original -->

Based on the results of risk analysis, the ban on wool from Lesotho imposed by Announcement No. 28 of 2026 jointly issued by GACC and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs is hereby lifted from the date of publication of this Announcement.

Wool imported from Lesotho shall comply with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the technical requirements for foot-and-mouth disease virus inactivation set out in the Terrestrial Animal Health Code of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH).

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs and Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

26 May 2026
