They can enter now. GACC Announcement No. 22 permits Uruguayan pecan nuts: in-shell or shelled nuts from mature thin-shell hickory fruit grown in Uruguay, dried and sorted. Growers must be filed by the Uruguay side; production, processing and storage units recommended by it and registered by the Chinese side before shipping.

A consignment runs the whole course: filed raw material, packed in new and clean material, each package marked in Chinese or English with "This product is exported to the People's Republic of China" plus name, origin, plant, China registration number and exporter name and address; before loading the Uruguay side checks transport; before export it issues a phytosanitary certificate whose additional declaration gives the plant, registration number and the English statement.

Customs inspection: entry once the goods pass. If pests of concern to China are found, or food safety standards or the Protocol are breached, the goods are handled under Chinese law, with no return or destruction prescribed. A number given by word of mouth does not count; the GACC website list and the original certificate govern. Batch and country only illustrate the process.

<!-- original -->

Under Chinese laws and regulations and the provisions agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter "the Chinese side") and the Ministry of Livestock, Agriculture and Fisheries of the Oriental Republic of Uruguay (hereinafter "the Uruguay side") on inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for pecan nuts exported from Uruguay to China, imports of Uruguayan pecan nuts meeting the following requirements are permitted from the date of this announcement.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations;

(ii) the Measures of the People's Republic of China for the Administration of Import and Export Food Safety and the Provisions of the People's Republic of China on the Registration of Overseas Manufacturers of Imported Food;

(iii) the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Livestock, Agriculture and Fisheries of the Oriental Republic of Uruguay on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Pecan Nuts to Be Exported from Uruguay to China (hereinafter "the Protocol").

II. Products Permitted for Import

Uruguayan pecan nuts permitted for import are in-shell or shelled nuts fit for human consumption, made in Uruguay from the mature fruit of thin-shell hickory grown in Uruguay through drying, sorting and other processes.

III. Food Safety Requirements

Pecan nuts exported to China shall comply with Chinese laws and regulations on food safety and plant quarantine, national food safety standards and the Protocol.

IV. Plant Quarantine Requirements

(i) Pecan nuts exported to China shall not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: Trogoderma granarium Everts, Pestalotiopsis clavispora and Phytophthora cactorum.

(ii) Pecan nuts exported to China shall not carry live insects, insect eggs or soil, and shall not be mixed with weed seeds, plant residues, metal foreign matter, gravel or other impurities.

V. Requirements for Production Enterprises

The Uruguay side shall, in line with Chinese requirements on the registration of overseas manufacturers of imported food, inspect the units producing, processing and storing pecan nuts for export to China and recommend those that comply to the Chinese side for registration. The Chinese side registers the enterprises that comply. An enterprise may ship its products to China only after it has been registered in China. The list of registered Uruguayan enterprises exporting pecan nuts to China can be consulted on the GACC website.

VI. Requirements for Raw Material

The raw material for pecan nuts exported to China shall come from growing enterprises confirmed by the Uruguay side as meeting the relevant plant quarantine and food safety requirements and filed for the record.

VII. Requirements for the Processing Course

The Uruguay side shall guide and supervise the growing of raw material, production, processing, storage, transport, export and other processes of pecan nuts for China, so as to ensure compliance with the relevant food safety and plant health requirements of China and Uruguay and prevent contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

VIII. Packaging and Marking Requirements

Pecan nuts exported to China shall be packed in new, clean, hygienic and breathable material that meets food safety and plant quarantine requirements, and leakage during transport shall be prevented.

Each package shall be marked in Chinese or English with "This product is exported to the People's Republic of China", together with traceability information such as the product name, origin, the name of the production or processing enterprise and its China registration number, and the name and address of the exporter.

The above information may be affixed to the package in the form of a label.

IX. Transport Requirements

Before loading, the Uruguay side shall inspect the means of transport used for pecan nuts exported to China.

The means of transport shall meet hygiene requirements and shall not carry quarantine pests or other regulated quarantine articles, such as weed seeds, live insects, plant residues, soil and other impurities.

X. Certificate Requirements

Before export, the Uruguay side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for pecan nuts that have passed inspection and quarantine and comply with the Protocol.

The additional declaration column of the certificate shall state the name of the enterprise producing or processing the pecan nuts, its China registration number, and "The batch of pecan nuts certified by this phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Pecan Nuts to Be Exported from Uruguay to China signed on February 3th, 2026, by Uruguay and China. The consignment is free from Trogoderma granarium Everts, Pestalotiopsis clavispora, Phytophthora cactorum and other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side." (The pecan nuts certified by this phytosanitary certificate comply with the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Pecan Nuts to Be Exported from Uruguay to China signed by China and Uruguay on 3 February 2026. The consignment is free from khapra beetle, Pestalotiopsis clavispora, Phytophthora cactorum and other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side).

XI. Entry Inspection, Quarantine and Handling of Non-compliance

(i) Entry inspection and quarantine.

After pecan nuts exported to China arrive at a Chinese port of entry, China Customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine supervision, and entry shall be allowed once they pass.

(ii) Handling of non-compliance.

Where the Chinese side finds that pecan nuts exported to China breach Chinese laws and regulations on food safety and plant quarantine, national food safety standards or the Protocol, China Customs shall handle the case under the relevant Chinese laws and regulations.

It is hereby announced.
