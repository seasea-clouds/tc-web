A 500-yuan bonded order leaves 314.5 yuan after six layers of deductions: comprehensive tax, platform fee, fulfillment, first-leg freight, commission, and settlement and remittance. That is a net margin of about 62.9 percent, or roughly 44 US dollars. The figures are illustrative values calculated from assumed parameters, not a quotation; replace them with your order value and the rates on your quotation and the result changes. Three variables deserve attention first: order value, commission rate and freight mode.

## How a five-hundred-yuan order breaks down

The waterfall starts at 500 yuan. The first layer deducts 45.5 yuan of comprehensive tax, followed by 25 yuan of platform fee, 15 yuan of fulfillment, 20 yuan of first-leg freight, 75 yuan of commission, and 5 yuan of settlement and remittance, leaving 314.5 yuan. An order value of 500 yuan is about 69 US dollars; the six deductions total 185.5 yuan and the net margin is 62.9 percent. Every amount in the chart is an assumed value used only to demonstrate the calculation path; replace each layer with your own figure and the path stays the same.

## What each cost line is billed on

Each of the seven cost lines has its own billing basis. Change the order value or the transport mode and only the corresponding rows move.

| Cost line | Billing basis |
|---|---|
| Product cost | Landed cost, provided by you |
| Comprehensive tax | Dutiable value × 9.1% |
| Platform service fee | Percentage of GMV |
| Fulfillment | Per order or by weight |
| First-leg freight | By weight and volume; sea and air differ widely |
| Commission | On attributed GMV; rate set by you |
| Settlement and remittance | Percentage of amount, including foreign exchange |

The comprehensive tax layer is charged at 9.1 percent of dutiable value, matching the basis in Cai Guan Shui [2016] No. 18, which levies cross-border e-commerce retail imports at 70 percent of the statutory tax payable; the single-transaction and annual limits follow Cai Guan Shui [2018] No. 49. You provide the landed product cost; platform fee, commission and settlement follow percentage-of-amount rules; fulfillment and first-leg freight follow weight, volume and transport mode. All rates are subject to a formal quotation.

## The three most sensitive variables

Revisit the example: total deductions of 185.5 yuan, a net amount of 314.5 yuan, about 44 US dollars, and a net margin of 62.9 percent. For the same order, the most sensitive variables are order value, commission rate and freight mode, in that order. Raise the order value and every percentage-based line moves with it; switch first-leg freight from air to sea and the per-order freight charge changes materially; the commission rate is the dial you can turn directly.

At an order value of 500 yuan, every five-percentage-point cut in the commission rate leaves 25 yuan more per order, about 3.5 US dollars. At a fixed order value the relationship is linear; change the order value and you must recalculate. In the other direction, every five-point rise in the rate removes the same amount.

## Fixed costs and volume-driven costs

When orders grow, not every cost grows in proportion. Separate the lines that follow order volume from the one-time investments, and you can judge whether net margin improves at scale.

| Type | Items |
|---|---|
| Varies with order volume | Platform commission, fulfillment, settlement and remittance, all percentage-based |
| Fixed or one-time | System integration, amortization of bulk first-leg freight, bonded ledger maintenance |

These three volume-driven lines are spread across each order in proportion, while the three fixed lines are shared across total order volume. More orders dilute the fixed lines and net margin improves; this is where scale tiers matter most, rather than pushing down every rate.
