Yes, they can enter. GACC Announcement No. 55 opens the door for Spanish pistachios and dried figs: the Spanish side inspects production, processing and storage units under the rules on registering overseas food producers and recommends them to the Chinese side; products may be shipped to China only after the enterprise is registered in China.

Your consignment of in-shell pistachios: check the processor's registration number in China and the pre-export fumigation record; a Spanish phytosanitary certificate travels with the goods, and its additional declaration states the enterprise name, registration number and the full protocol-compliance sentence; every package is marked in Chinese or English with the destination, product name and exporter address.

At the Chinese port of entry, China Customs releases the goods after a satisfactory inspection; breaches of Chinese food safety or plant quarantine law, national standards or the Protocol are handled under Chinese law. The two products carry separate declarations tied to their own pest lists, so registration numbers and additional declarations must match line by line.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food of the Kingdom of Spain (hereinafter the Spanish side) on the inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for pistachios and dried figs exported from Spain to China, Spanish pistachios and dried figs meeting the following requirements are permitted for import with immediate effect.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations;

(2) The Measures of the People's Republic of China for the Safety Management of Import and Export Food; the Provisions of the People's Republic of China on the Registration and Administration of Overseas Producers of Imported Food;

(3) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food of the Kingdom of Spain on the Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Pistachios and Dried Figs Exported from Spain to China (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Products Permitted for Import

Spanish pistachios permitted for import are uncooked pistachios for human consumption, in shell or shelled, made from pistachios grown in Spain by sorting, hulling and drying.

Spanish dried figs permitted for import are dried figs for human consumption made from figs grown in Spain by fumigation, washing, blanching, drying, ultraviolet sterilisation and sorting.

III. Food Safety Requirements

Pistachios and dried figs exported to China shall comply with the relevant laws and regulations of China on food safety and plant quarantine, the national food safety standards and the Protocol.

IV. Plant Quarantine Requirements

(1) Pistachios exported to China shall be free from the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: carob moth Ectomyelois ceratoniae (Zeller), the dermestid Trogoderma inclusum LeConte, warehouse beetle Trogoderma variabile Ballion, and the dermestid Trogoderma versicolor (Creutzer).

(2) Dried figs exported to China shall be free from the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: Mediterranean fruit fly Ceratitis capitata Wiedemann, codling moth Cydia pomonella (L.), Sowbane masaic virus(SoMV), carob moth Ectomyelois ceratoniae (Zeller), and the dermestid Trogoderma versicolor (Creutzer).

(3) Pistachios and dried figs exported to China shall not carry live insects, eggs or soil, and shall not be mixed with weed seeds, plant residues, metal foreign matter, grit or other impurities.

(4) Pistachios and dried figs exported to China shall undergo fumigation before export so that no live insects remain.

V. Requirements for Production Enterprises

The Spanish side shall inspect the production, processing and storage units handling pistachios and dried figs exported to China against the Chinese requirements for registering overseas producers of imported food, and recommend qualified enterprises to the Chinese side for registration. The Chinese side registers those that qualify. An enterprise may export to China only after it is registered in China. The list of registered Spanish enterprises is available on the GACC website.

VI. Raw Material Requirements

The raw materials for pistachios and dried figs exported to China shall come from pistachio and fig growing and production enterprises confirmed by the Spanish side as meeting the relevant plant quarantine and food safety requirements.

VII. Processing Requirements

The Spanish side shall guide and supervise the growing of raw materials and the production, processing, storage, transport and export, and other processes of pistachios and dried figs exported to China, ensure compliance with the relevant food safety and plant health requirements of China and Spain, and prevent contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

VIII. Packaging and Marking Requirements

Pistachios and dried figs exported to China shall be packed in clean, hygienic, breathable and new materials that meet food safety and plant quarantine requirements, and leakage during transport shall be prevented. Each package shall be marked in Chinese or English with the words “This product is exported to the People's Republic of China”, together with traceable information such as the product name, place of origin, the name of the production and processing enterprise and its registration number in China, and the name and address of the exporter. The information may be affixed to the package as a label.

IX. Transport Requirements

Before shipment, the Spanish side shall inspect the means of transport for pistachios and dried figs exported to China. The means of transport shall meet hygiene requirements and shall not carry quarantine pests or other regulated articles such as weed seeds, live insects, plant residues, soil and other impurities.

X. Certificate Requirements

Before export, the Spanish side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for pistachios and dried figs exported to China that have passed inspection and quarantine under the Protocol.

The additional declaration box of the phytosanitary certificate for pistachios exported to China shall state the name of the pistachio production and processing enterprise, its registration number in China and the words “The batch of pistachios certified by this phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for the Export of Pistachios and Dried Figs from the Kingdom of Spain to the People's Republic of China Between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food of the Kingdom of Spain signed on April 14th, 2026, by Spain and China. The consignment is free from Ectomyelois ceratoniae (Zeller), Trogoderma inclusum LeConte, Trogoderma variabile Ballion, and Trogoderma versicolor (Creutzer), and other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side.” (the pistachios covered by this phytosanitary certificate comply with the Protocol on inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for pistachios exported from Spain to China signed by the Chinese and Spanish sides on 14 April 2026; the consignment is free from carob moth, the dermestid Trogoderma inclusum, warehouse beetle, the dermestid Trogoderma versicolor and other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side).

The additional declaration box of the phytosanitary certificate for dried figs exported to China shall state the name of the dried fig production and processing enterprise, its registration number in China and the words “The batch of dried figs certified by the phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for the Export of Pistachios and Dried Figs from the Kingdom of Spain to the People's Republic of China Between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food of the Kingdom of Spain signed on April 14th, 2026, by Spain and China. The consignment is free from Ceratitis capitata Wiedemann, Cydia pomonella (L.), Sowbane masaic virus(SoMV), Ectomyelois ceratoniae (Zeller), and Trogoderma versicolor (Creutzer), and other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side.” (the dried figs covered by the phytosanitary certificate comply with the Protocol on inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for dried figs exported from Spain to China signed by the Chinese and Spanish sides on 14 April 2026; the consignment is free from Mediterranean fruit fly, codling moth, Sowbane masaic virus, carob moth, the dermestid Trogoderma versicolor and other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side).

The quarantine treatment method, such as the agent, temperature, duration and other technical requirements, shall be stated in the phytosanitary certificate.

XI. Entry Inspection, Quarantine and Non-compliance Handling

(1) On arrival at a Chinese port of entry, China Customs shall carry out inspection, quarantine and supervision of pistachios and dried figs exported to China, and shall permit entry once the goods pass.

(2) Where pistachios or dried figs exported to China are found to breach Chinese food safety or plant quarantine laws and regulations, national food safety standards or the Protocol, China Customs shall handle the case under the relevant laws and regulations of China.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

28 April 2026
