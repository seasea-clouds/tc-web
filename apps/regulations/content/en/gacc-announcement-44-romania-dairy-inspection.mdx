Romanian dairy products are cleared for import. Announcement No. 44 of 2026 requires producers to be approved by Romania and registered in China, with unregistered products barred from export.

Milk-producing animals must come from free-of-disease farms and a residue monitoring plan must be implemented; products are UHT or pasteurized and accompanied by a veterinary health certificate.

Packaging must bear Chinese and English information on origin, registration number and batch; the whole chain must be hygienic, containers sealed, and packaging not opened or changed in transit.

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the National Sanitary Veterinary and Food Safety Authority of Romania (hereinafter the Romanian side) on inspection and quarantine requirements for dairy products from Romania for export to China, imports of Romanian dairy products that meet the inspection and quarantine requirements are permitted from the date of publication of this Announcement.

I. Inspection and quarantine basis

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations.

(2) The Measures for the Safety and Supervision of Imported Food and the Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Producers of Food Imported into the People's Republic of China.

(3) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the National Sanitary Veterinary and Food Safety Authority of Romania on Inspection and Quarantine Requirements for Dairy Products for Export to China.

(4) GACC Announcement No. 114 of 2021 (on clarifying relevant requirements for inspection and quarantine of imported dairy products).

II. Products permitted for import

Dairy products from Romania permitted for import refer to various types of milk and dairy foods originating in Romania, processed mainly from cow's, sheep's and goat's milk and meeting China's national food safety standards, including pasteurized milk, sterilized milk, formulated milk, fermented milk, cheese and processed cheese, cream, butter, anhydrous butter, condensed milk, milk powder, whey powder, whey protein powder, bovine colostrum powder, casein, milk mineral salts, milk-based infant formula and its premix (or base powder), etc.

III. Requirements for producing enterprises

Enterprises producing dairy products for export to China shall be approved or registered by the Romanian side, produce in Romania under official supervision, and comply with the animal health and public health requirements of both China and Romania.

In accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and the Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Producers of Food Imported into the People's Republic of China, enterprises exporting dairy products to China shall be registered in China, and products produced by unregistered enterprises shall not be exported to China.

IV. Requirements for dairy animals providing raw milk

Dairy animals providing raw milk for dairy products for export to China shall meet the following conditions:

(1) Come from farms meeting the following conditions:

(1) At least one month before the collection of raw milk, there has been no case or suspected case of foot-and-mouth disease on the farm.

(2) At the time of raw milk collection, no clinical symptoms of anthrax have been found on the farm.

(3) The farm is free of Bovine tuberculosis, Paratuberculosis, Rinderpest, Rift Valley fever, Peste des petits ruminants, Sheep pox, Goat pox, and Contagious bovine pleuropneumonia.

(4) The farm is under the supervision of the Romanian side.

(5) The farm and its surrounding areas are not subject to quarantine restrictions due to animal diseases under the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) Code and Romanian animal health regulations.

(2) The animals have not been fed feed or drugs prohibited for animal feeding by Romania and China.

(3) The Romanian national residue monitoring plan is implemented and enterprises are required to test raw milk.

(4) Based on the Romanian national residue monitoring plan and raw milk test results, residues of veterinary drugs, pesticides and other toxic and harmful substances in dairy products for export to China do not exceed the maximum limits set by Chinese standards.

V. Certificate requirements

Dairy products for export to China shall be accompanied by a veterinary health certificate issued by the Romanian side.

VI. Food safety requirements

(1) Dairy products for export to China shall comply with the laws and regulations of Romania and China and China's national food safety standards.

(2) The raw milk for dairy products for export to China shall come from Romania or from countries (regions) approved to export the corresponding dairy raw materials to China.

(3) Dairy products for export to China shall be easily identifiable at all stages of processing and storage and shall not be processed together with dairy products that do not comply with this Announcement.

(4) Raw milk used to process dairy products for export to China does not contain milk from the period of antibiotic application or withdrawal, spoiled milk, or colostrum (except when used to process bovine colostrum powder).

(5) The products adopt one of the following processing procedures:

(1) A sterilization procedure at a minimum temperature of 132ºC for at least 1 second (ultra-high temperature UHT).

(2) If the pH of the milk is below 7.0, a sterilization procedure at a minimum temperature of 72ºC for at least 15 seconds (high temperature short time pasteurization HTST); if the pH is 7.0 or above, the HTST procedure is applied twice.

VII. Packaging and labelling requirements

Dairy products for export to China must be packaged in brand-new materials compliant with Chinese standards. The outer packaging shall bear in Chinese and English the specifications, place of origin (down to state/province/city), destination, product name, weight, name of the producing factory, registration number in China, production batch number, storage conditions, production date and shelf life.

The inner packaging shall comply with relevant Chinese provisions, and the label shall state the country of origin, product name, enterprise registration number, name, address and contact details of the producing enterprise, production date and shelf life, and production batch number.

VIII. Storage and transport requirements

The whole process of packaging, storage and transport of dairy products for export to China shall comply with hygienic conditions and prevent contamination by toxic and harmful substances. After the goods are loaded into containers, seals shall be affixed, and the seal number shall be stated in the veterinary health export certificate. Packaging shall not be opened or changed during transport.
