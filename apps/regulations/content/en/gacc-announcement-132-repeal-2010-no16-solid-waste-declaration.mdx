By Announcement 132 of 2026, the General Administration of Customs repeals Announcement 16 of 2010; from its publication on 1 September 2026, the standardized classification declaration of imported solid wastes is no longer performed under that announcement and its annexes. This is a cleanup of old rules that no longer have any applicable object, not a loosening of solid waste imports.

The repealed Announcement 16 of 2010 once set classification catalogs, declaration elements and customs declaration form examples for certain imported solid wastes such as scrap metal, scrap plastics and waste paper. Since 1 January 2021, China has banned importing solid waste by any means, and these catalogs and examples have lost their applicable basis.

Take a batch of recycled copper raw materials intended for import: first confirm whether the goods are solid waste; if not, classify them under the current standardized declaration catalog and declare product name, material and composition, without copying the 2010 annex examples. If customs identifies the goods as solid waste, the import is prohibited and a return shipment is ordered.

<!-- original -->

In light of actual work conditions, the General Administration of Customs decides to repeal Announcement 16 of 2010 issued by the General Administration of Customs (the announcement on issuing the relevant rules concerning the standardized classification declaration of certain imported solid wastes).

This announcement takes effect from the date of publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

1 September 2026
