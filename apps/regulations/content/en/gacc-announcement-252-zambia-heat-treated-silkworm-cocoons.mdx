From 23 December 2025, heat-treated silkworm cocoons from Zambia may be imported if they meet these requirements. Only cocoons processed in Zambia at 120°C or more for at least 270 minutes, or by an equivalent treatment accepted by China, are covered. Fresh cocoons and untreated pupae are excluded.

Producers must be approved and supervised by Zambia, maintain hygiene and disease controls, and be registered with China. Raw materials must come from disease-free areas. Farms must control pebrine, polyhedrosis, muscardine, aspergillosis, flacherie, bacterial diseases, fly maggot disease and mite infestation; pebrine and white muscardine are monitored twice a year.

At entry, samples are tested for mould, rot and dryness against GB/T 9176-2016. Products must meet Chinese and Zambian safety standards. Packaging must be new, clean, sealed and moisture-proof, with a Chinese-language label giving name, specification, production date, batch and producer; containers need a Zambian-approved disinfectant. Each batch needs an official certificate. Customs checks registration and certificate authenticity, inspects goods and handles nonconformity.

<!-- original -->

Under Chinese laws and regulations and the provisions of the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Fisheries and Livestock of the Republic of Zambia on quarantine and health requirements for heat-treated silkworm cocoons exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol), imports of heat-treated silkworm cocoons from Zambia that meet the following requirements are permitted as of the date of this announcement.

I. Basis for inspection and quarantine

(i) The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) The Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(iii) The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Non-food Animal Products for Entry and Exit;

(iv) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Fisheries and Livestock of the Republic of Zambia on quarantine and health requirements for heat-treated silkworm cocoons exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Scope of imported products

Heat-treated silkworm cocoons in this announcement means cocoons produced in Zambia that have been processed at a maximum temperature of not less than 120°C for not less than 270 minutes, or by other equivalent processing measures.

III. Requirements for producing enterprises

(i) Approved by the Zambian side and producing under its effective supervision;

(ii) Monitoring the hygiene quality of products and establishing and implementing silkworm disease surveillance;

(iii) Recommended by the Zambian side to the Chinese side, and registered after passing Chinese review.

IV. Requirements for raw materials

(i) Coming from areas free of major animal and plant disease outbreaks that can be spread through silkworm cocoons;

(ii) Source mulberry silkworm breeding base farms must adopt control measures and formulate monitoring plans for pebrine, polyhedrosis and viral softening, white muscardine, green muscardine, aspergillosis, flacherie, bacterial gastroenteritis, bacterial septicaemia, fly maggot disease and mite infestation, and effectively control outbreaks; pebrine and white muscardine must be monitored regularly, at least twice a year.

V. Safety and hygiene requirements

(i) Products shall be randomly sampled and tested for mould, rotten cocoons and dryness, and the results shall conform to the Chinese national standard Dried Mulberry Silkworm Cocoons (GB/T 9176-2016);

(ii) Products shall comply with Chinese and Zambian laws and regulations on animal products and safety and hygiene standards, and be permitted for sale and use in Zambia.

VI. Packaging, labelling, storage and transport requirements

(i) Products shall be packed in new, non-toxic and harmless, clean materials with good sealing and moisture-proof performance that are not easily damaged;

(ii) The outer packaging shall bear a Chinese-language label containing the product name, specification, production date, production batch number, storage conditions, producing enterprise and other information;

(iii) Containers carrying the products shall be disinfected with a disinfectant approved by the Zambian side.

VII. Pre-export inspection and certificate requirements

(i) The Zambian side shall be responsible for inspection and quarantine of products exported to China and shall issue a health certificate in accordance with the certificate sample confirmed by both sides, certifying compliance with the Protocol;

(ii) Each batch of products exported to China shall be accompanied by at least one official original health certificate.

VIII. Entry inspection and quarantine requirements

(i) Document verification.

(1) Verify whether the products come from a registered enterprise;

(2) Verify whether the health certificate is authentic and valid.

(ii) Goods inspection.

Chinese customs shall, in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules, and in combination with the requirements of this announcement, conduct inspection and quarantine on heat-treated silkworm cocoons exported from Zambia to China. Those that pass inspection and quarantine shall be permitted to enter.

(iii) Handling of nonconforming cases.

Where nonconformity is found during entry inspection and quarantine, Chinese customs shall handle it in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, the Protocol and other provisions.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

Issued on 23 December 2025
