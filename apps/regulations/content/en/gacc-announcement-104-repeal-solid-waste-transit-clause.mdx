On 23 July 2026 the General Administration of Customs issued Announcement No. 104 of 2026, repealing Article 2 of GACC Announcement 48 of 2017. The transit conditions, designated ports and enclosed-park processing rules for imported solid waste cease to apply, with no transition period.

The repealed Article 2 had let waste paper and waste metals seek customs transit in five cases, including water-to-water multimodal transport, and set duties for the port of entry, the place of destination and supervision facilities. Only that article goes; the rest of Announcement 48 of 2017 on multimodal transport, advance declaration and transit of other goods survives.

In practice, importers no longer apply for transit under Article 2 and should follow current solid waste import administration, port inspection and return shipment rules. Contracts signed before 23 July 2026 and cargo already in transit need an early check against the handling capacity of the port.
