China's State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), through Announcement No. 57 of 2025, changes the certification mode for certain products in the compulsory product certification (CCC) catalogue, moving 16 products, including fuse links, small-power motors and electric drills, from enterprise self-declaration to third-party certification.

The transition has two steps. From 1 July 2026, designated certification bodies begin accepting CCC applications, and valid self-declarations may be recognized and certificates reissued. By 31 December 2026, enterprises must convert certificates and cancel their self-declarations. From 1 January 2027, products without a CCC certificate and mark may not leave the factory, be sold, imported or used.

Importers must confirm the certification status of listed products before arrival and reserve time for sample testing and factory inspection. Products already shipped during the self-declaration's validity and no longer manufactured need no conversion and may continue to be sold; inventory should be managed accordingly.

<!-- original -->

To further strengthen the supervision of product quality and safety, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Certification and Accreditation, the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) has decided to adjust the certification mode for certain products within the compulsory product certification (CCC certification) catalogue and implement the third-party certification evaluation approach. The relevant requirements are hereby announced as follows:

(1) From 1 January 2027, the following 16 products, namely fuse links, small-power motors, electric drills, electric grinders, electric hammers, DC arc welding machines, TIG arc welding machines, MIG/MAG arc welding machines, plasma arc cutting machines, automotive safety glass, automotive seat belts, motor vehicle exterior lighting and light signalling devices, motor vehicle indirect vision devices, vehicle seats and seat head restraints, vehicle travel data recorders, and vehicle body reflective markings, may leave the factory, be sold, imported or used in other business activities only after obtaining a CCC certificate and affixing the CCC certification mark. The self-declarations of the relevant products in the “Compulsory Certification Product Conformity Self-Declaration Information Submission System” (hereinafter referred to as the System) will be cancelled uniformly.

(2) From 1 July 2026, designated certification bodies begin accepting CCC certification applications for the above 16 products (the certification bodies will be designated separately). For products holding valid self-declarations, the designated certification bodies shall, on the premise of ensuring certification quality and keeping risks under control, actively recognize the self-declaration evaluation results and issue CCC certification certificates. The System will no longer accept or generate self-declarations for the relevant products.

(3) Enterprises shall complete the conversion of CCC certificates and promptly and voluntarily cancel the corresponding self-declarations before 31 December 2026. For products that have left the factory during the validity period of the self-declaration and are no longer manufactured, conversion is not required and they may continue to be sold.
