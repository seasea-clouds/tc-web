Filing first. From 1 September 2026, Announcement No. 87 covers fresh and fresh-keeping vegetables supplied to the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region of China: producers file with local customs, filing is valid indefinitely; Guo Jian Shi Han [2009] No. 629 is repealed.

One consignment to Hong Kong, China: keep a growing-base list, verify compliance certificates, self-test, mark packages with name, filing number and batch number, issue a cargo list and seals as required; customs permits supply only after compliant inspection.

Unknown sources, banned pesticides or excess toxins trigger rectification and possible suspension of acceptance; concealment, three notifications in a year or refusal of supervision cancels filing; a Hong Kong, China or Macao, China notice triggers investigation, feedback and notice to local authorities.

To further standardize the inspection, quarantine and supervision of vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China, safeguard their quality, safety and stable supply, and promote the sound development of trade, the following requirements for the inspection, quarantine and supervision of vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China are hereby announced in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; (iii) Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; (iv) Law of the People's Republic of China on Quality and Safety of Agricultural Products; (v) Law on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations; (vi) Special Provisions of the State Council on Strengthening the Supervision and Administration of Food and Other Product Safety; (vii) Opinions of the General Office of the CPC Central Committee and the General Office of the State Council on Further Strengthening Whole-Chain Food Safety Supervision.

II. Scope of Application

This Announcement applies to the inspection, quarantine and supervision of fresh and fresh-keeping vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China.

III. Record-Filing of Production and Processing Enterprises of Vegetables Supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China

For the purposes of this Announcement, a production and processing enterprise of vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China (hereinafter a “production and processing enterprise”) means an enterprise engaged in the purchase and primary processing of fresh and fresh-keeping vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China. Customs implements record-filing administration for production and processing enterprises. A production and processing enterprise shall complete the Record-Filing Form for Production and Processing Enterprises of Vegetables Supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China (Annex 1) and apply for record-filing with the local customs office. Record-filing of production and processing enterprises shall follow the Requirements for the Record-Filing of Production and Processing Enterprises of Vegetables Supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China (Annex 2). Record-filing of a production and processing enterprise is valid for an indefinite period.

IV. Product Quality and Safety Management by Enterprises

(i) A production and processing enterprise shall fulfill its primary responsibility for product quality and safety, conduct production and business activities in accordance with relevant laws, regulations and standards, act with integrity and self-discipline, accept social supervision and bear social responsibility.

(ii) A production and processing enterprise shall ensure that vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China meet the inspection and quarantine requirements of the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region of China. Where the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region of China has no relevant inspection and quarantine requirements, the relevant inspection and quarantine requirements of the mainland shall be met.

(iii) A production and processing enterprise shall establish a product quality and safety management system covering the whole process from intake inspection of vegetable raw materials, through production, processing and packaging, to supply to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China; put in place systems for evaluation of raw-material suppliers, intake inspection, self-testing and self-control of toxic and hazardous substances, ex-factory inspection, product traceability and disposal of non-compliant products; ensure the effective operation of the product quality and safety management system; and be equipped with corresponding quality and safety technical personnel.

(1) A production and processing enterprise shall establish a list of growing bases sourcing vegetable raw materials, including the name, address and contact details of each growing base. Vegetable raw materials sourced from the enterprise's own growing bases, or from bases certified as green, organic, famous-special-excellent-new or geographical indication, are encouraged.

(2) A production and processing enterprise shall conduct intake inspection of vegetable raw materials, verify commitment certificates of compliance with standards, truthfully record the name, quantity, intake date and supplier information of incoming materials, and properly keep the relevant inspection records, purchase vouchers and commitment certificates so as to ensure that the materials can be traced back to the growing base.

(3) A production and processing enterprise shall conduct self-testing and self-control of vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China, and test the products by itself or through a commissioned testing institution in accordance with quality and safety management requirements. Where a product fails testing, control measures shall be taken in a timely manner.

(iv) A production and processing enterprise shall indicate on the markings of transport packaging for vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China such information as the enterprise's name, address and record-filing number, the product name, and the date of production or production batch number. Packaging for vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China shall be clean and hygienic so as to prevent contamination of the products.

(v) A production and processing enterprise shall provide a cargo list as required by the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region of China, and apply seals to road vehicles carrying vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China. The seal number shall be marked on the cargo list or other documents; the sample cargo list is set out in Annex 3.

(vi) Where vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China have a safety problem that may cause or has caused harm to human health and life safety, the production and processing enterprise shall take appropriate measures in accordance with the law and immediately report to the local customs office.

(vii) Records and files relating to product quality and safety management of a production and processing enterprise shall be truthful and kept for not less than 3 years. Production and processing enterprises are encouraged to use information technology to establish such records and files.

V. Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The owner of vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China, or its agent, shall apply for inspection and quarantine to the customs office where the production and processing enterprise is located. Upon acceptance of the application, customs shall conduct inspection and quarantine in accordance with the law. Compliant goods shall be permitted for supply to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China; non-compliant goods may be permitted after passing technical rectification, while goods that cannot undergo technical rectification or remain non-compliant after such rectification shall not be permitted for supply.

(ii) The owner of vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China, or its agent, shall make a declaration to customs in accordance with the law. The port customs office shall conduct port inspection in accordance with the law; compliant goods shall be released, and non-compliant goods shall not be released.

(iii) Vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China declared to customs under the consolidated declaration model shall be handled in accordance with relevant provisions.

VI. Supervision and Administration

(i) Customs shall conduct supervision and administration of production and processing enterprises in accordance with the law, and carry out supervisory inspections of whether such enterprises continue to meet the record-filing conditions and whether their product quality and safety management systems are operating properly.

(ii) Customs shall conduct supervised spot checks and risk monitoring of pollutants, pesticide residues and other toxic and hazardous substances in vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China.

(iii) Where vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China are notified by the competent authority of the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region of China for a product quality and safety problem, the customs office where the production and processing enterprise is located shall organize an investigation, take tightened supervision measures where necessary, and may require the production and processing enterprise to submit test reports.

(iv) Where a production and processing enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, customs shall order rectification. During rectification, if a risk assessment indicates a product quality and safety risk, acceptance of applications for inspection and quarantine of its products for supply to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China may be suspended:

(1) the product quality and safety management system does not operate properly;

(2) facilities and equipment are not commensurate with production capacity;

(3) intake inspection records and ex-factory inspection records are incomplete;

(4) the source of vegetable raw materials is unknown;

(5) packaging and marking do not meet the requirements;

(6) products are detected to contain banned pesticides or excessive toxic and hazardous substances;

(7) the production and processing enterprise fails to apply for a change in accordance with the rules after its information changes;

(8) the production and processing enterprise is notified by the competent authority of the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region of China of product quality and safety non-compliance.

(v) Where a production and processing enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, customs shall cancel its record-filing:

(1) it remains non-compliant after rectification;

(2) it conceals or falsely reports major quality and safety problems;

(3) it is notified by the competent authority of the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region of China of quality and safety non-compliance 3 times within 1 year;

(4) it refuses to accept customs supervision and administration.

(vi) Where a production and processing enterprise, owner or its agent violates this Announcement, customs shall handle the matter in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

VII. Information Notification

Customs shall strengthen coordination with local government authorities and enhance collaborative governance among departments. Where vegetables supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China fail inspection and quarantine, or are notified as non-compliant by the competent authority of the Hong Kong or Macao Special Administrative Region of China, the customs office where the production and processing enterprise is located shall provide feedback to the production and processing enterprise and notify the local government authorities in accordance with relevant provisions.

This Announcement takes effect on 1 September 2026. The Notice of the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on Effectively Carrying Out the Inspection, Quarantine and Supervision of Vegetables Supplied to Hong Kong, China and Macao, China (Guo Jian Shi Han [2009] No. 629) is repealed at the same time.
