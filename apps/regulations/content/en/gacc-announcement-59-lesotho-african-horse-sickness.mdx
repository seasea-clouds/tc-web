Lesotho reported African horse sickness; imports of equine animals and products from Lesotho into China are closed. Announcement No. 59 of 2026 bans direct or indirect import, posting or carriage into China; discovered items are returned or destroyed, effective from publication.

Disposal: waste and swill from incoming Lesotho vehicles must be treated under customs supervision, not discarded at will; equine animals and products intercepted from illegal entry by border inspection and other departments are destroyed under customs supervision. Violators are dealt with by customs under the Customs Law, Biosecurity Law, Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and implementing rules.

The focus is coordination. Customs and agricultural and rural affairs departments at all levels coordinate under the Customs Law, Biosecurity Law, Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and Animal Epidemic Prevention Law for quarantine, prevention and supervision. Ports apply the above bans and disposal rules.

<!-- original -->

Recently, Lesotho reported an outbreak of African horse sickness to the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH). To prevent the disease from being introduced, protect the safety of China's livestock industry and biosecurity, and in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, the following is announced.

Direct or indirect import of equine animals and their related products from Lesotho is prohibited, including products derived from equine animals that are unprocessed or, though processed, may still transmit the disease.

Posting or carrying equine animals and their related products from Lesotho into China is prohibited. Once discovered, they shall all be returned or destroyed.

Animal and plant waste, swill and the like unloaded from incoming transport vehicles from Lesotho shall all be treated under customs supervision and shall not be discarded at will.

Equine animals and their related products from Lesotho that are intercepted from illegal entry by border inspection and other departments shall all be destroyed under customs supervision.

Whoever violates the above provisions shall be dealt with by customs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Animal and Plant Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China and their implementing regulations.

Customs at all levels and agricultural and rural affairs departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Animal and Plant Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China, the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws, work in close coordination to carry out quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision.

This Announcement shall take effect on the date of publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

7 May 2026
