On 13 January 2026 the General Administration of Customs (GACC) published the new Measures for Enterprise Credit Management by Order No. 282, effective on 1 April 2026, with Order No. 251 of 2021 repealed the same day. Registered and recorded enterprises, together with their legal representatives, customs affairs officers and declarants, are subject to credit management under these Measures.

Credit is managed in five levels: advanced certified, certified, regular, discredited and seriously discredited. The first two are China customs AEOs, able to join mutual recognition cooperation and enjoy facilitation measures; the recognition standards cover internal control, financial status, compliance with law and trade security.

Certification decisions are issued within 90 days, extendable by 30 days, and advanced certified enterprises are reviewed every five years. A discredited determination rests on dual thresholds of frequency and amount; after correction, an enterprise may apply for a level change once three months of publication have elapsed for general discredit and one year for serious discredit, with the repair decision made within seven working days.

## Chapter I General Provisions

**Article 1**　These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Customs Audit, the Interim Regulations on Publication of Enterprise Information, the Regulations on Optimizing the Business Environment and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, in order to standardize credit management of customs registered and recorded enterprises, build a new credit-based regulatory mechanism, advance the development of the social credit system and promote trade security and facilitation.

**Article 2**　These Measures apply to the collection and publication of credit information of customs registered and recorded enterprises (hereinafter referred to as enterprises) and of the relevant personnel of enterprises, to the repair of discredited information, and to the recognition and management of enterprise credit status.

**Article 3**　Customs applies credit management to enterprises in accordance with the principles of facilitating the honest and law-abiding, penalizing the discredited and law-breaking, acting in accordance with laws and regulations, and being fair and open.

**Article 4**　Customs enterprise credit levels are divided, on the basis of credit status, into advanced certified enterprises, certified enterprises, regular enterprises, discredited enterprises and seriously discredited enterprises.

**Article 5**　Customs establishes a cooperation mechanism between customs and enterprises and provides enterprises with credit cultivation services, helping enterprises strengthen their awareness of honesty and law-abiding operation and improve their level of honest business operation.

**Article 6**　In accordance with the relevant requirements of the national social credit system, customs implements joint incentives for trustworthy conduct and joint disciplinary action for discredited conduct together with the relevant departments of the State, and establishes a mechanism for sharing enterprise credit information.

**Article 7**　In accordance with the relevant requirements of the national social credit system, customs implements credit repair with respect to discredited information of enterprises.

**Article 8**　Advanced certified enterprises and certified enterprises are China's Authorized Economic Operator (AEO). China customs conducts mutual recognition cooperation with the customs of other countries or regions in accordance with relevant international treaties and agreements and with these Measures, and grants mutual recognition enterprises the relevant facilitation measures.

**Article 9**　Customs establishes an enterprise credit management system and applies information technology to improve the level of customs enterprise credit management.

## Chapter II Collection and Publication of Credit Information

**Article 10**　Customs may collect the following information reflecting the credit status of an enterprise: (1) information on the registration and record-filing of the enterprise and basic information on the relevant personnel of the enterprise; (2) information on the import and export of the enterprise and on business related to import and export; (3) information on administrative licensing of the enterprise; (4) information on administrative penalties and criminal penalties imposed on the enterprise and its relevant personnel; (5) information on joint incentives and joint disciplinary action implemented by customs together with the relevant departments of the State; (6) AEO mutual recognition information; (7) other relevant information reflecting the credit status of the enterprise.

**Article 11**　Enterprises shall submit the annual report on credit information in accordance with the relevant provisions of the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 12**　Customs shall promptly publish the following credit information and announce the means of inquiry: (1) information on the registration and record-filing of enterprises with customs; (2) the results of customs recognition of enterprise credit levels; (3) information on administrative licensing granted to enterprises by customs; (4) information on administrative penalties imposed on enterprises by customs; (5) information on joint incentives and joint disciplinary action implemented by customs together with the relevant departments of the State; (6) other information that shall be published in accordance with law. Where the published credit information involves State secrets, national security, public interests, trade secrets or personal privacy, it shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

**Article 13**　Where a natural person, legal person or unincorporated organization objects to credit information published by customs, it may lodge an appeal with customs and provide the relevant materials or supporting documents. Customs shall reply to the applicant within the prescribed period. Where the grounds for the objection raised by the natural person, legal person or unincorporated organization are established, customs shall adopt them.

## Chapter III Recognition Standards for Enterprise Credit Levels

**Article 14**　The standards for advanced certified enterprises and the standards for certified enterprises cover internal control, financial status, compliance with law and trade security, and the specific standards shall be separately formulated and published by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 15**　Where an enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, customs recognizes it as a discredited enterprise: (1) it violates the relevant laws and administrative regulations on customs and is held criminally liable in accordance with law by a judicial organ; (2) it commits an act of smuggling or an act of intentionally violating the Export Control Law and is subject to an administrative penalty by customs; (3) within one year, the number of times it is subject to an administrative penalty by customs for violating the regulatory provisions of customs exceeds one thousandth of the total number of customs declaration-related documents of the previous year and the cumulative amount of the administrative penalties imposed by customs exceeds 1,000,000 yuan, except where the cumulative penalty amount does not exceed 1% of the total import and export value of the previous year; where the number of declaration-related documents of the previous year cannot be calculated, the cumulative amount of the administrative penalties imposed by customs for violating the regulatory provisions of customs within one year exceeds 1,000,000 yuan; (4) within one year, in engaging in agency declaration business, the number of times it is subject to an administrative penalty by customs for violating the regulatory provisions of customs exceeds one thousandth of the total number of declaration-related documents for the agency declaration business of the previous year and the cumulative amount of the administrative penalties imposed by customs exceeds 500,000 yuan; where the number of declaration-related documents of the previous year cannot be calculated, the cumulative amount of the administrative penalties imposed by customs within one year for violating the regulatory provisions of customs in engaging in agency declaration business exceeds 500,000 yuan; (5) it is subject to an administrative penalty by customs in accordance with law of revoking its license or certificate or prohibiting it from engaging in declaration business; (6) it fails to pay taxes for more than three months from the date of expiry of the payment period; (7) it fails to pay fines, confiscated illegal gains and recovered payment equivalent to the value of smuggled goods and articles for more than six months from the date of expiry of the payment period, and the amount exceeds 10,000 yuan; (8) it resists or obstructs customs officers in the performance of their duties in accordance with law and is punished in accordance with law; (9) it offers bribes to customs officers and is subject to a fine or held criminally liable in accordance with law; (10) other circumstances provided for by laws, administrative regulations and customs rules.

**Article 16**　Where the unlawful act of a discredited enterprise is of a serious nature, customs recognizes it as a seriously discredited enterprise, simultaneously includes it in the list of seriously discredited entities in the field of customs supervision of import and export, and implements joint disciplinary action in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and relevant national policy documents. In determining whether an unlawful act is of a serious nature, customs shall take into comprehensive consideration whether it seriously endangers the health or safety of the people or seriously disrupts the order of fair market competition or the normal social order, or whether there is refusal to perform statutory obligations seriously affecting the credibility of administrative organs, together with factors such as subjective malice, the type of penalty, the amount of fines and confiscations and the social impact.

**Article 17**　Where an enterprise is not recognized by customs as an advanced certified enterprise, a certified enterprise, a discredited enterprise or a seriously discredited enterprise, customs applies management to it as a regular enterprise.

## Chapter IV Recognition Procedures and Management Measures for Enterprise Credit Levels

**Article 18**　Where an enterprise applies to become an advanced certified enterprise or a certified enterprise, it shall submit a written application to customs and submit the relevant materials as required by customs.

**Article 19**　Customs examines the application and relevant materials submitted by the enterprise against the standards for advanced certified enterprises or the standards for certified enterprises, and conducts on-site certification at the enterprise. The certification work shall be carried out by customs officers with the professional qualification for enterprise certification.

**Article 20**　Customs shall complete certification and make a decision within 90 days from the date of receipt of the application. Under special circumstances, the certification time limit of customs may be extended by 30 days. Where an enterprise is subject to customs audit or verification during the certification period, or is placed on file for investigation by customs on suspicion of unlawful acts, the time spent on such audit, verification and investigation may be excluded from the certification time limit.

**Article 21**　After certification, where an enterprise meets the standards for advanced certified enterprises, customs issues an advanced certified enterprise certificate; where an enterprise meets the standards for certified enterprises, customs issues a certified enterprise certificate; where an enterprise does not meet the standards, customs issues a decision on failure to pass certification.

**Article 22**　Customs reviews advanced certified enterprises once every five years, and conducts reviews of certified enterprises on the basis of the results of enterprise credit assessment. Where customs finds any abnormality in the credit status of an advanced certified enterprise or a certified enterprise, it may conduct a review of the enterprise at irregular intervals.

**Article 23**　Before conducting a review, customs shall notify the enterprise in advance and conduct the review by reference to the certification procedure. Where the conditions prescribed by customs are met, the review procedure may be simplified.

**Article 24**　Where, after review, an enterprise meets the standards for its original level, customs shall issue a decision on passing the review; where it does not, customs shall handle the matter in accordance with the following provisions: (1) where no circumstance provided for in Article 15 or Article 16 of these Measures is found, customs shall notify the enterprise in writing that it may apply for rectification as prescribed; (2) where the enterprise does not apply for rectification as prescribed or still fails the review after rectification, customs shall issue a decision on failure to pass the review, adjust the credit level of the enterprise and withdraw the advanced certified enterprise certificate or the certified enterprise certificate; (3) where a circumstance provided for in Article 15 or Article 16 of these Measures is found, customs shall adjust the credit level of the enterprise and withdraw the advanced certified enterprise certificate or the certified enterprise certificate. Where an advanced certified enterprise does not meet the standards for advanced certified enterprises but meets the standards for certified enterprises, it is recognized as a certified enterprise. Where, during the review period, an enterprise applies to give up management as an advanced certified enterprise or a certified enterprise, customs shall issue a decision on failure to pass the review, adjust the credit level of the enterprise and withdraw the advanced certified enterprise certificate or the certified enterprise certificate.

**Article 25**　Where an advanced certified enterprise or a certified enterprise is adjusted by customs to a regular enterprise, it may not submit a certification application within one year. A discredited enterprise or a seriously discredited enterprise may not submit a certification application; where it is adjusted by customs to a regular enterprise, it may not submit a certification application within one year.

**Article 26**　Customs may entrust social intermediary organizations to issue professional conclusions on issues relating to enterprise certification and review. Professional conclusions issued by social intermediary organizations entrusted by an enterprise on issues relating to enterprise certification and review may serve as reference for customs certification and review.

**Article 27**　Before making a decision to recognize an enterprise as a discredited enterprise or a seriously discredited enterprise, customs shall notify the enterprise in writing of the grounds for the proposed decision, its basis and the rights of statement and defense enjoyed in accordance with law. Where an enterprise is recognized as a seriously discredited enterprise and is simultaneously included in the list of seriously discredited entities in the field of customs supervision of import and export, customs shall also notify the enterprise of the reminder of disciplinary measures, the conditions for removal from the list, the removal procedure and the remedies.

**Article 28**　Where an enterprise makes a statement or defense regarding the proposed decision of customs to recognize it as a discredited enterprise or a seriously discredited enterprise or to include it in the list of seriously discredited entities in the field of customs supervision of import and export, it shall submit the statement or defense in writing to customs within five working days from the date of receipt of the written notification; failure to do so within that period is deemed a waiver of the right of statement and defense. Customs shall verify the matter within 20 days, and where the grounds raised by the enterprise are established, customs shall adopt them.

**Article 29**　Where a discredited enterprise corrects its discredited conduct, eliminates the adverse effects and meets the following conditions, it may apply in writing to customs for adjustment of its credit level: (1) six months have elapsed since it was recognized as a discredited enterprise due to a circumstance under item (3) or item (4) of Article 15 of these Measures; (2) three months have elapsed since it was recognized as a discredited enterprise due to a circumstance under item (6) or item (7) of Article 15 of these Measures. Where a discredited enterprise has not committed any circumstance provided for in Article 15 or Article 16 of these Measures within one year, customs applies management to it as a regular enterprise.

**Article 30**　Where a seriously discredited enterprise corrects its seriously discredited conduct, eliminates the adverse effects and one year has elapsed since it was recognized as a seriously discredited enterprise, it may apply in writing to customs for adjustment of its credit level. Where it meets the provisions and customs agrees, it is recognized as a discredited enterprise and may not be adjusted to a regular enterprise within one year. Where three years have elapsed since it was recognized as a seriously discredited enterprise, customs re-determines its credit level on the basis of its credit status. Where customs adjusts the credit level of a seriously discredited enterprise in accordance with the preceding two paragraphs, it shall simultaneously remove the enterprise from the list of seriously discredited entities in the field of customs supervision of import and export.

**Article 31**　Where an enterprise applies for adjustment of its credit level in accordance with Article 29 or Article 30 of these Measures, it shall at the same time submit the relevant supporting materials proving that it has performed its statutory obligations. However, where any of the following circumstances exists and has not been eliminated, customs shall not adjust the credit level: (1) the registration or record-filing information of the enterprise is untrue; (2) the annual report on credit information has not been submitted in accordance with the relevant provisions; (3) the enterprise refuses to cooperate with customs supervision and on-site inspection.

**Article 32**　Customs applies facilitated management measures to advanced certified enterprises and certified enterprises, regular management measures to regular enterprises, and strict management measures to discredited enterprises and seriously discredited enterprises. The enterprise credit management measures shall be separately formulated and published by the General Administration of Customs.

## Chapter V Publication and Repair of Enterprise Discredited Information

**Article 33**　In accordance with the requirements of the national social credit system, customs publishes enterprise discredited information on the Credit China website.

**Article 34**　Enterprise discredited information is divided into three classes: minor, general and serious. Minor discredited information includes warnings and circulated criticism made by customs against enterprises and other administrative penalty information made under the summary procedure. Serious discredited information includes administrative penalty information made for revoking licenses or certificates, prohibiting engagement in declaration business and for acts of smuggling or acts of intentionally violating the Export Control Law, the list of seriously discredited entities in the field of customs supervision of import and export and the administrative penalty information related to inclusion in that list. Apart from minor discredited information and serious discredited information, other administrative penalty information made by customs is general discredited information.

**Article 35**　Customs does not publish minor discredited information, the publication period for general discredited information is one year, and the publication period for serious discredited information is three years. The starting point of the publication period shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the General Administration of Customs. After the publication period of enterprise discredited information expires, customs ceases publication. Where laws, administrative regulations or the national social credit system provide otherwise, those provisions shall prevail.

**Article 36**　Where an enterprise corrects its unlawful act, eliminates the adverse effects and meets the following conditions, it may apply to customs for repair of discredited information: (1) general discredited information has been published for three months or serious discredited information has been published for one year; (2) a credit commitment has been made; (3) other conditions provided for by laws, administrative regulations and the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 37**　Where an enterprise applies for repair of discredited information, it shall submit the following materials in accordance with the provisions of customs: (1) supporting materials proving correction of the unlawful act and elimination of the adverse effects; (2) a letter of credit commitment; (3) other materials provided for by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 38**　Customs shall decide whether to accept an application for repair of discredited information within three working days from the date of receipt of the application; where the application materials meet the requirements, it shall accept the application; where they do not, it shall notify the applicant and explain the reasons. Customs shall make a repair decision within seven working days from the date of acceptance of the application for repair of discredited information; where the circumstances are complicated, the period may be extended by ten working days.

**Article 39**　After making a decision to grant repair of discredited information, customs shall cease publication of the relevant discredited information; where repair is not granted, it shall notify the applicant and explain the reasons. Where an applicant objects to a decision not to accept an application for repair of discredited information or a decision not to grant repair, it may lodge an appeal with customs.

**Article 40**　Where laws, administrative regulations and policy documents of the Party Central Committee and the State Council explicitly provide that repair is not permitted, customs shall not repair the discredited information.

## Chapter VI Supplementary Provisions

**Article 41**　Where customs needs to apply credit management to enterprises that are not registered or recorded but are directly related to import and export business, these Measures shall be applied by reference.

**Article 42**　The credit management of overseas enterprises such as overseas manufacturers of imported food registered with customs and foreign units producing, processing or storing imported animal and plant products shall be separately provided for by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 43**　A criminal offense serving as the basis for recognizing enterprise credit status is determined by the time when the relevant legal document of the judicial organ takes effect. A customs administrative penalty serving as the basis for recognizing enterprise credit status is determined by the time when the customs administrative penalty decision is made. The penalty amount serving as the basis for recognizing enterprise credit status includes the sum of the amounts of fines imposed by customs, illegal gains confiscated and the value of goods and articles confiscated.

**Article 44**　The following circumstances are not recorded by customs in recognizing enterprise credit levels: (1) warnings and circulated criticism made by customs against enterprises; (2) administrative penalties made by customs under the summary procedure; (3) other circumstances provided for by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 45**　Legal documents made by customs in credit management work shall be served on enterprises and take effect from the date of service.

**Article 46**　The following terms used in these Measures have the meanings given below: relevant personnel of an enterprise means the legal representative, the principal person in charge, the person in charge of finance, the customs affairs officer, the declarant and other persons of the enterprise. Customs declaration-related documents means the customs declaration form, the record-filing list for entry and exit, the manifest of inbound and outbound means of transport, the declaration form for transit goods transport and other documents.

**Article 47**　These Measures shall be interpreted by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 48**　These Measures shall come into force on 1 April 2026. The Measures of the People's Republic of China for Credit Management of Customs Registered and Recorded Enterprises promulgated by Order No. 251 of the General Administration of Customs on 13 September 2021 shall be repealed simultaneously.
