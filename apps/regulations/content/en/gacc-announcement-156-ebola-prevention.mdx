Ebola virus disease remains a public health emergency of international concern. Announcement No. 156 takes effect 18 November for 1 year: arrivals declare, contaminated conveyances and goods undergo sanitary treatment, and international travel health advisories may be checked before departure.

A shipment from an affected area: contaminated or possibly contaminated containers, goods and outer packaging get sanitary treatment as required before any opening or transshipment. Personnel in an affected area within 21 days before entry with symptoms such as fever declare to customs on arrival.

Before departure, travellers to affected areas may consult customs and its International Travel Health Care Centres or the General Administration of Customs website for outbreak information and travel health advice. New areas named by the World Health Organization follow this Announcement.

At present, the Ebola virus disease outbreak still constitutes a “public health emergency of international concern”. According to data released by the World Health Organization, from 15 May to 9 November 2026, the Democratic Republic of the Congo reported a cumulative total of 11042 confirmed cases of Ebola virus disease, 5387 deaths, and a crude case fatality rate of 48.8%; from 6 October to 10 November, Kenya reported a cumulative total of 9 confirmed cases of Ebola virus disease and 2 deaths; on 20 October, Nigeria reported 1 imported case of Ebola virus disease. To prevent the introduction of Ebola virus disease into China and to safeguard the health and safety of inbound and outbound persons, pursuant to the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China and its Implementing Rules and other laws and regulations, the following is hereby announced:

I. Persons coming from, or who within 21 days before entry have visited, countries (regions) where Ebola virus disease outbreaks occur shall, if they have been in contact with Ebola virus disease cases or present symptoms such as fever, fatigue, headache, muscle pain, sore throat, nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, diarrhoea, rash and bleeding, declare proactively to customs upon entry, and customs will take medical measures in accordance with prescribed procedures.

II. Means of transport, transport equipment such as containers, goods, luggage, postal parcels and other articles, and outer packaging that come from countries (regions) where Ebola virus disease outbreaks occur and are contaminated or possibly contaminated shall undergo sanitary treatment in accordance with regulations.

III. Persons travelling to countries (regions) where Ebola virus disease outbreaks occur may, before departure, consult customs and its International Travel Health Care Centres, or visit the website of the General Administration of Customs to check relevant outbreak information and international travel health advisories.

The contents of this Announcement take effect from 18 November 2026, with a validity period of 1 year. During this period, countries (regions) newly announced by the World Health Organization as experiencing Ebola virus disease outbreaks shall be handled in accordance with this Announcement.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

12 November 2026
