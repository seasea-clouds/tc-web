Honduran tobacco leaves are now approved for import into China. Announcement No. 179 of 2025 of the General Administration of Customs takes effect upon issuance. The leaves are dried and cured cigar tobacco leaves, scientific name Nicotiana tabacum, whose growing bases and production enterprises are reviewed by the Honduran side and approved and registered by the Chinese side.

Growing bases apply GAP and IPM and monitor four quarantine pests including tobacco blue mold; once oospores of the disease are found, immediate notification and suspension of exports from the affected production area are required. A 2% sample is checked before export, reducible to 1% if no problems occur, and pesticide residues and heavy metals must not exceed the limits.

Each consignment is accompanied by a phytosanitary certificate and the prescribed English statement, and boxes are marked in Chinese or English with export information; if oospores of the disease are detected on entry the consignment is returned or destroyed, while other pests are allowed entry after effective treatment.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions concerning phytosanitary requirements for the export of tobacco leaves from Honduras to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock of the Republic of Honduras (hereinafter referred to as the Honduran side), the import of tobacco leaves from Honduras meeting the following relevant requirements is permitted from the date of issuance.

I. Inspection and Quarantine Basis

(1) Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(3) Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock of the Republic of Honduras on the Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Tobacco Leaves from Honduras to China.

II. Names of Commodities Permitted for Entry

Cigar tobacco leaves that have been dried, cured and graded (hereinafter referred to as tobacco leaves), scientific name Nicotiana tabacum, English name Cigar Tobacco Leaves.

III. Permitted Places of Origin

Tobacco-growing areas of Honduras.

IV. Approved Tobacco Growing Bases and Production Enterprises

Tobacco growing bases and production enterprises exporting tobacco leaves to China shall be reviewed by the Honduran side and approved and registered by the Chinese side. The registration information shall include name, address and registration number so that exports can be accurately traced when they do not comply with the relevant provisions of this Announcement. Before each annual export season, the Honduran side shall provide the Chinese side with the list of registered entities.

V. List of Quarantine Pests of Concern to China

(1) tobacco blue mold Peronospora hyoscyami f.sp. Tabacina

(2) potato zebra chip disease Candidatus Liberibacter solanacearum

(3) johnsongrass Sorghum halepense

(4) Chromolaena odorata

VI. Pre-export Requirements

(1) Field management.

(1) Tobacco growing bases exporting to China shall establish and implement good agricultural practices (GAP) under the supervision of the Honduran side and carry out integrated pest management (IPM) measures. Tobacco leaves for export to China shall comply with China's relevant plant quarantine laws, regulations and safety and hygiene standards, and be free from quarantine pests of concern to China and soil.

(2) The Honduran side or its authorized personnel shall implement preventive measures against tobacco blue mold in seedbeds, and investigate and monitor tobacco blue mold from transplanting to harvest to ensure that tobacco leaves for export to China are free from the pathogen. If tobacco blue mold is confirmed, effective control measures shall be taken to reduce its impact; if oospores of tobacco blue mold are found, the Honduran side shall immediately notify the Chinese side and suspend exports of tobacco leaves from the relevant production area. Before each annual export season, the Honduran side shall provide the Chinese side with the monitoring and investigation report and records of control measures for tobacco blue mold of that year.

(3) Tobacco leaves for export to China shall comply with relevant Chinese provisions and requirements; pesticides banned in China shall not be used. The Honduran side or its authorized personnel shall monitor pesticide use in tobacco-growing areas and guide rational and scientific use, and all pesticides used shall be officially registered.

(4) Monitoring and control of pests in exporting growing bases shall be carried out under the supervision and guidance of professional and technical personnel of the Honduran side. Such personnel shall receive training from the Honduran side or its authorized training institutions. Growing bases shall keep pest monitoring and control records and provide them to the Chinese side when needed. Control records shall include the names of chemical agents used during the growing season, active ingredients, application rates and timing.

(2) Processing management.

Production enterprises shall establish a traceability system to ensure that tobacco leaves exported to China can be traced back to the registered growing bases. The processing, packaging, storage and shipment of tobacco leaves for export to China shall be carried out under the quarantine supervision of the Honduran side or its authorized personnel.

(3) Packaging requirements.

Packaging materials shall be clean, hygienic, unused and comply with relevant Chinese plant quarantine requirements. If wooden packaging is used, it shall comply with the requirements of International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15).

Tobacco leaves exported to China shall be sealed in dedicated packaging boxes to maintain the integrity of the goods. The following information shall be indicated in English on the boxes: type of tobacco leaves, grade, place of origin, harvest year, processing factory, batch number and contract number. Each packaging box and pallet shall be marked in Chinese or English with „Exported to the People’s Republic of China“.

(4) Pre-export inspection and quarantine.

The Honduran side shall conduct sample inspection of tobacco leaves for export to China at a rate of 2%. If no phytosanitary problems have occurred within two years, the sampling rate may be reduced to 1%. If quarantine pests of concern to China are found, the entire consignment shall not be exported to China. The Honduran side shall, as appropriate, suspend the export qualification of the relevant growing bases or production enterprises for the current export season, identify the causes and take improvement measures.

Before the tobacco leaves exported to China leave the country, the Honduran side shall conduct tests for pesticide and heavy metal residues; tobacco leaves with excessive heavy metal or pesticide residues shall not be exported.

(5) Phytosanitary certificate requirements.

Upon passing quarantine, the Honduran side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate in accordance with the International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12 (ISPM 12), indicating the registration numbers of the growing base and the packaging factory, and shall state in the additional declaration: „This consignment complies with the Protocol of the Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Tobacco Leaves from Honduras to China, and is free from any quarantine pests of concern to China and soil.“

The Honduran side shall provide the Chinese side with a specimen of the phytosanitary certificate before commencing trade, for confirmation and record-filing.

VII. Entry Inspection and Quarantine and Non-compliance Handling

Upon arrival of the tobacco leaves at the entry port in China, the Chinese side shall carry out inspection and quarantine in accordance with the following requirements:

(1) Verification of relevant certificates and marks.

(1) Verify whether the phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of item (5) of Article VI.

(2) Verify whether the packaging complies with the provisions of item (3) of Article VI.

(3) Verify whether the goods conform to the certificate.

(2) Entry inspection and quarantine.

In accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and operational guidelines, inspection and quarantine shall be carried out on imported tobacco leaves, and entry shall be permitted only after they qualify.

(3) Non-compliance handling.

(1) If oospores of tobacco blue mold are detected, the consignment of tobacco leaves shall be returned or destroyed, and the Chinese side shall immediately suspend imports of tobacco leaves from the relevant production area.

(2) If other quarantine pests of concern to China or soil are detected, the consignment may be permitted entry after effective treatment qualifies; if no effective treatment method is available, the consignment shall be returned or destroyed.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs

26 August 2025
