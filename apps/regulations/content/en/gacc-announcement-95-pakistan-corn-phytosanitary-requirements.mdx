Pakistan maize can enter China, but only for processing and only from enterprises registered with China after nomination by Pakistan. From 7 July 2026, maize from anywhere in Pakistan is permitted, provided it is not used for planting.

Take one lot end to end: Pakistan monitors 16 pests during the growing season and submits an annual report; enterprises dry, clean and seal the grain for closed transport; bags carry the statement “This product will be exported to the People's Republic of China” and the Chinese registration number; wood packaging follows ISPM 15; pre-export quarantine ends with a certificate whose additional declaration quotes the English sentence verbatim; live insects are fumigated first.

No valid phytosanitary certificate, an unregistered enterprise, soil or unapproved genetically modified material means return or destruction; live quarantine pests are treated where a method exists, otherwise returned or destroyed; China notifies Pakistan and may suspend the enterprise's export access.

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of National Food Security and Research of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan (hereinafter the Pakistani side) on the phytosanitary requirements for the export of maize from Pakistan to China, import of maize from Pakistan that meets the following requirements is permitted from the date of this announcement:

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(iii) the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of National Food Security and Research of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan on Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Maize from Pakistan to China.

II. Names of Commodities Permitted for Entry

Maize (Zea mays L.) in this announcement means maize kernels produced in Pakistan and exported to China for processing only, not for planting.

III. Permitted Places of Origin

The entire territory of Pakistan.

IV. Registration of Enterprises

The Pakistani side shall register and supervise the enterprises producing, processing and storing maize exported to China and require them to take measures such as sieving and impurity removal so that their products meet the relevant quarantine requirements. The Pakistani side shall recommend compliant enterprises to the Chinese side; after review and approval, the Chinese side shall register them and publish and dynamically update the list on its website.

V. Pests of Concern to the Chinese Side

Maize exported to China shall not carry the following pests of concern to the Chinese side:

(1) Barley stripe mosaic virus

(2) Maize dwarf mosaic virus

(3) khapra beetle Trogoderma granarium

(4) stem and bulb nematode Ditylenchus dipsaci

(5) sugarcane white stripe bacterium Xanthomonas albilineans

(6) southern sandbur Cenchrus echinatus

(7) nettle-leaved goosefoot Chenopodiastrum murale

(8) southern three-cornered jack Emex australis

(9) parthenium weed Parthenium hysterophorus

(10) silverleaf nightshade Solanum elaeagnifolium

(11) johnsongrass Sorghum halepense

(12) Monosporascus root rot fungus Monosporascus cannonballus

(13) Philippine downy mildew Peronosclerospora philippinensis

(14) sorghum downy mildew Peronosclerospora sorghi

(15) maize brown stripe downy mildew Sclerophthora rayssiae

(16) Verticillium wilt fungus Verticillium dahliae

VI. Pre-shipment Requirements

(i) Production requirements.

(1) The Pakistani side shall investigate and monitor the pests of concern to the Chinese side during the maize growing season, take prevention and control measures against field pests and reduce pest occurrence. The Pakistani side shall submit annual pest survey and monitoring reports and information on field control measures to the Chinese side.

(2) The Pakistani side shall strengthen supervision over the enterprises producing, processing and storing maize exported to China and supervise their taking measures such as drying, cleaning and impurity removal to prevent the maize from carrying soil, plant residues, weed seeds and other foreign impurities.

(ii) Packaging and transport requirements.

(1) Maize exported to China shall be transported in closed conveyances. Conveyances shall meet hygiene and epidemic prevention requirements and shall be thoroughly inspected before loading to prevent the admixture of weed seeds, live insects, other grain impurities, plant residues and soil; where necessary, they shall be thoroughly cleaned and disinfected.

(2) Where maize exported to China is transported in bags, the packaging materials shall be new, clean and hygienic and comply with China's entry phytosanitary requirements. The packaging shall be marked clearly in Chinese and English with “This product will be exported to the People's Republic of China” and the name and Chinese registration number of the producing, processing and storing enterprise.

Where wood packaging is used, it shall comply with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15).

(iii) Product requirements.

Maize exported to China shall comply with China's entry phytosanitary laws and regulations and food or feed safety laws, regulations and standards, shall be free of the pests of concern to the Chinese side, live insects, soil, branches, leaves and other plant residues, and shall not be deliberately added with or mixed with other grains or foreign impurities.

(iv) Pre-export inspection and quarantine and certificate requirements.

Before export, the Pakistani side shall quarantine maize exported to China and issue a phytosanitary certificate for compliant maize, stating in the additional declaration in English: “This consignment complies with the phytosanitary requirements provided in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for the Maize Exported from Pakistan to China and is free from the pests of concern to China” (the consignment complies with the requirements of the Protocol on Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Maize from Pakistan to China and is free from the pests of concern to China), and stating the name and Chinese registration number of the producing, processing and storing enterprise of the lot, as well as the conveyance number. Where live insects are found, the goods shall be fumigated before export, and the treatment parameters shall be stated on the phytosanitary certificate.

VII. Inspection and Quarantine on Entry

(i) Document verification.

(1) whether the goods come from a registered enterprise;

(2) whether the phytosanitary certificate is authentic and valid.

(ii) Cargo inspection.

In accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules, maize shall be inspected and quarantined against this announcement and shall be permitted to enter after passing inspection and quarantine.

(iii) Treatment of non-conformities.

(1) without a valid phytosanitary certificate, the lot shall be returned or destroyed;

(2) from an unregistered enterprise, the lot shall be returned or destroyed;

(3) where live quarantine pests or other live pests are found, the lot shall be treated if an effective method exists, or returned or destroyed if no effective method exists;

(4) where soil or genetically modified material not approved by the Chinese authorities is found, the lot shall be returned or destroyed;

(5) other cases that do not meet China's entry animal and plant quarantine and food or feed safety laws, regulations and standards shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions.

Where the above violations are found, the Chinese side shall notify the Pakistani side and, depending on the severity, take measures such as suspending the enterprise's export access until the Pakistani side takes effective corrective measures.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

7 July 2026
