Norwegian wild aquatic products are approved for import into China with immediate effect. Announcement No. 159 of 2026 takes effect upon publication. Wild aquatic products means wild aquatic animal products and their processed products for human consumption, as well as marine plant products such as algae and their processed products, excluding amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, live aquatic animals and aquatic animal and plant propagation materials.

Establishments must be approved and supervised by the Norwegian authorities and recommended to the Chinese side for registration; unregistered ones may not export to China. Products must be legally caught or from registered establishments, free of banned drugs and additives, with labels and packaging meeting Chinese standards, and every consignment must carry a health certificate confirmed by both sides in Chinese and English.

The Chinese side destroys, returns or otherwise disposes of non-compliant products and may suspend imports from establishments with serious problems; the announcement is dated 17 November 2026 and signed by the General Administration of Customs.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with Chinese laws and regulations and the provisions on the inspection and quarantine requirements for wild aquatic products exported from Norway to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Fisheries of the Kingdom of Norway (hereinafter referred to as the Norwegian side), wild aquatic products from Norway meeting the following relevant requirements are allowed to be imported with immediate effect:

I. Basis for inspection and quarantine

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Imported and Exported Commodities and its implementing regulations.

(2) The Measures of the People's Republic of China for the Administration of Import and Export Food Safety and the Regulations of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China on the Registration of Overseas Manufacturers of Imported Food.

(3) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Fisheries of the Kingdom of Norway on the inspection and quarantine requirements for wild aquatic products exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Scope of imported products

Wild aquatic products means wild aquatic animal products and their processed products for human consumption, as well as marine plant products such as algae and their processed products, excluding amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, live aquatic animals and aquatic animal and plant propagation materials.

III. Requirements for establishments

Establishments producing wild aquatic products for export to China (including production, processing and storage establishments) must be approved by the Norwegian authorities and remain under their effective supervision. The food safety hygiene management and protection systems of the establishments must meet the requirements of the relevant Chinese and Norwegian food safety laws and regulations.

In accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, establishments producing wild aquatic products for export to China must be recommended by the Norwegian side to the Chinese side for registration. Without registration, export to China is not allowed.

IV. Requirements for imported products

The Norwegian side must ensure that wild aquatic products exported to China meet the following conditions:

(1) They are legally caught in domestic or international waters, or they come from registered aquatic product establishments of countries (regions) approved by the Chinese side.

(2) Neither the raw materials nor the products have any of the following problems:

(a) Wild aquatic products already exported or to be exported from Norway to China violate Chinese food safety laws and regulations, national standards and the Protocol, and have seriously affected the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China.

(b) An aquatic animal disease listed in the Catalogue of Animal Diseases for Entry Quarantine of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Catalogue) or a disease related to the products of the Protocol that must be notified under the rules of the World Organisation for Animal Health has occurred in Norway, and has seriously affected the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China.

(c) A major food safety incident has occurred in Norway, or a major public health incident has occurred at an establishment, or the fishing waters have been affected by pollutants, and the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China has been seriously affected.

(3) There is no direct or indirect use of drugs or additives banned by China and Norway, and drugs or additives restricted or permitted by China are used in accordance with the regulations.

(4) After inspection and quarantine by the competent authorities, no pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or harmful substances or foreign matter listed in Chinese and Norwegian laws and regulations are found, no aquatic animal diseases listed in the Catalogue are found, and no aquatic animal diseases that must be notified under the rules of the World Organisation for Animal Health are found.

(5) The production process (including catching, production and processing, packaging, storage, transport, transit and export) must meet the relevant Chinese and Norwegian safety and hygiene requirements and traceability requirements.

(6) The packaging, labels and markings of the products must comply with Chinese national food safety standards and the relevant provisions on the administration of import food safety.

V. Certificate requirements

The Norwegian side implements inspection and quarantine of wild aquatic products for export to China as required by the Chinese side, and issues a health certificate confirmed by both sides for every consignment of wild aquatic products exported to China, certifying that the consignment complies with the relevant provisions of Chinese and Norwegian food safety laws and regulations and national standards. The certificates must be printed at least in Chinese and English, and English is the mandatory language when completing the certificates.

The health certificate must record the information of the establishment producing the wild aquatic products for export to China in full. The format of the health certificate must be confirmed by the Chinese and Norwegian sides. In case of any change, the Norwegian side must file it with the Chinese side at least one month before the certificate takes effect.

VI. Other requirements

The Chinese side implements inspection and quarantine of wild aquatic products in accordance with Chinese food safety laws and regulations, national standards and the provisions of the Protocol.

For non-compliant products, the Chinese side implements destruction, return or other disposal. For establishments in which serious problems occur or non-compliance occurs repeatedly, the Chinese side may take measures such as strengthening inspection and quarantine or suspending imports.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

17 November 2026
