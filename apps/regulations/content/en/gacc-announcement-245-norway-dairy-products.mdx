From 12 December 2025, Norwegian dairy products meeting inspection, quarantine and food safety requirements may be imported into China; only dairy products originating in Norway and made mainly from cow's, goat's or sheep's milk qualify.

If you arrange the import, check two points first: the manufacturer must be officially approved by Norway and registered in China, or its products may not be exported to China; the raw-milk herds must come from farms where foot-and-mouth disease restrictions have been lifted for at least 2 months, with no clinical Anthrax symptoms in the past 12 months and no Bovine Tuberculosis or other such diseases.

Each consignment carries an official Norwegian veterinary health certificate; according to the WOAH foot-and-mouth disease-free status, pasteurization at 72 ºC for at least 15 seconds or UHT at 132 ºC for at least 1 second applies; registration and batch numbers are marked in Chinese and English on inner and outer packaging, and containers are sealed with the seal number stated on the certificate.

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Food of the Kingdom of Norway on the inspection, quarantine and food safety requirements for Norwegian dairy products exported to China, the import of Norwegian dairy products meeting the inspection, quarantine and food safety requirements is permitted with effect from the date of issuance of this Announcement:

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(a) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations.

(b) The Measures of the People's Republic of China for the Safety Management of Import and Export Food; the Provisions of the People's Republic of China on the Registration of Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food.

(c) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Food of the Kingdom of Norway on the Inspection, Quarantine and Food Safety Requirements for Dairy Products from the Kingdom of Norway Exported to China.

(d) GACC Announcement No. 114 of 2021 (Announcement on Clarifying the Relevant Inspection and Quarantine Requirements for Imported Dairy Products).

II. Products Allowed for Import

The Norwegian dairy products allowed for import refer to all kinds of dairy products originating in Norway, processed with cow's milk, goat's milk and sheep's milk as the main raw materials and meeting the requirements of the national food safety standards of China.

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

Enterprises producing dairy products for export to China shall be approved or registered by the competent Norwegian authority, produce in Norway under official supervision, and comply with the relevant animal health and public health requirements of China and Norway.

In accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and the Provisions of the People's Republic of China on the Registration of Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food, production enterprises exporting dairy products to China shall be registered in China; products manufactured by enterprises that have not obtained registration shall not be exported to China.

IV. Requirements for Dairy Animals Providing Raw Milk

The dairy animals providing raw milk for dairy products exported to China shall meet the following requirements:

(a) They come from farms meeting the following conditions:

(1) The foot-and-mouth disease quarantine restrictions have been lifted for at least 2 months.

(2) At the time of raw milk collection, no clinical symptoms of Anthrax have been detected on the farm in the past 12 months.

(3) The dairy animal farm has been free of Bovine Tuberculosis, Paratuberculosis, Rinderpest, Rift Valley fever, Peste des petits ruminants, Sheep pox and Goat pox, Scrapie and Contagious bovine pleuropneumonia.

(4) The farm is under official Norwegian supervision.

(5) The farm and its surrounding area are not subject to quarantine restrictions imposed on account of animal diseases under the provisions of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) Code and the Norwegian animal health legislation.

(6) Norway has implemented the relevant WOAH requirements and shall continuously monitor and report to the WOAH.

(b) The animals have not been fed feed or drugs prohibited for animal feeding in Norway or China.

(c) The Norwegian authorities have established a national residue monitoring plan and require enterprises to test the raw milk.

(d) Based on the Norwegian national residue monitoring plan and the raw milk test results, residues of veterinary drugs, pesticides and other toxic and harmful substances in dairy products exported to China shall not exceed the maximum limits set by Chinese standards.

V. Certificate Requirements

Dairy products exported to China shall be accompanied by a veterinary health certificate issued by the Norwegian authorities.

VI. Food Safety Requirements

(a) Dairy products exported to China shall comply with the laws and regulations of Norway and China and the requirements of the Chinese national food safety standards.

(b) The raw milk for dairy products exported to China shall come from Norway or from a country (region) approved to export the corresponding dairy raw materials to China.

(c) Dairy products exported to China shall be readily identifiable at every stage of processing and storage, and shall not be processed together with dairy products that do not comply with the provisions of this Announcement.

(d) The raw milk used to process dairy products exported to China shall contain no milk from animals under antibiotic treatment or during the withdrawal period, no deteriorated milk, and no colostrum (except when bovine colostrum powder is being processed).

(e) No colostrum ingredients have been added to milk-based infant and young children formula.

(f) The products shall undergo one of the following processing procedures:

(1) Where Norway is a WOAH-recognized foot-and-mouth disease-free country, a disinfection procedure at a minimum temperature of 72 ºC for at least 15 seconds shall be applied (high-temperature short-time pasteurization, HTST);

(2) Otherwise, a disinfection procedure at a minimum temperature of 132 ºC for at least 1 second shall be applied (ultra-high temperature, UHT), or the disinfection procedure at a minimum temperature of 72 ºC for at least 15 seconds (high-temperature short-time pasteurization, HTST) shall be applied twice.

VII. Packaging and Marking Requirements

Dairy products exported to China shall be packed in brand-new materials complying with Chinese standards. The outer packaging shall state, in Chinese and English, the specifications, place of origin (down to the state/province/city), destination, product name, weight, name of the manufacturer, registration number in China, production batch number, storage conditions, production date and shelf life.

The inner packaging shall comply with the relevant Chinese provisions, and the label shall state the country of origin, product name, registration number in China, name, address and contact details of the manufacturer, production date and shelf life, and production batch number.

VIII. Storage and Transport Requirements

Throughout the whole process of packaging, storage and transport, dairy products exported to China shall meet hygienic conditions and be protected from contamination by toxic and harmful substances. After the goods are loaded into the containers, seals shall be affixed, and the seal number shall be stated on the veterinary health export certificate. The packaging shall not be opened or replaced during transport.

General Administration of Customs
