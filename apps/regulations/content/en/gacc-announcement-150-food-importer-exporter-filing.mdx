On 23 October 2026, GACC issued Announcement No. 150 of 2026. The new Administrative Provisions on Filing of Food Importers and Exporters took effect the same day, and the 2012 version they replace was repealed at the same time. If you export food to China, these provisions govern how you file and how quickly you receive your filing code.

## Who these provisions cover

They cover the exporter or agent layer. If you ship food to China as an overseas exporter or an agent, you file under these provisions. They are separate from the registration of overseas food producers, which runs under GACC Decree No. 280; this document governs the shipping and agent side. Your Chinese buyer, the domestic food importer, is covered as well, but that obligation sits on the buyer's side.

## What you need to do

If you are already on the filing list, your filing stays valid indefinitely and you do not need to file again. When your filing details change, submit the change within 60 days of the change.

If you are filing for the first time, submit the Information Form for Filing of Overseas Food Exporters or Agents to GACC online, through the Single Window or the customs government services portal. Where the form is complete and the supporting documents are in order, customs completes the filing within 3 working days of acceptance and issues a filing code.

## What changed in this revision

Compared with the 2012 rules, the information form for overseas exporters and agents drops from 20 items to 10, customs processing time is set at 3 working days, and the food categories covered now match those for overseas producer registration and domestic importers. The official revision notes state the principles: streamlined procedures, unified standards, shorter timelines, and full-chain supervision.

## The hard requirement at clearance

When food is declared for import, the declaration must truthfully state the overseas exporter or agent filing code, and customs verifies importer and exporter information. The code can only come from a filing. Where filing details do not match reality, customs requires correction within a set period. The provisions carry no dedicated penalty clause; the cancellation cases are listed in Article 8. My advice: confirm your filing code is valid before you ship, and check the declaration against your filing before you submit it, so you are not held at the port for corrections.

## Chapter I General provisions

**Article 1**　For the purposes of safeguarding the safety of imported food and improving the traceability of imported food, these Provisions are formulated in accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its Implementation Regulations, the Special Provisions of the State Council on Strengthening the Supervision and Administration of Food and Other Products, and the Administrative Measures on Food Safety of Imports and Exports, as well as other laws, administrative regulations and rules.

**Article 2**　These Provisions apply to the filing management of overseas exporters or agents that export food into China and of domestic food importers (hereinafter collectively referred to as importers and exporters).

Products other than the food categories listed in the annex to these Provisions, such as food additives and food-related products, shall be handled in accordance with relevant provisions.

**Article 3**　The General Administration of Customs oversees the supervision and administration of importer and exporter filing and publishes filing information.

Customs offices throughout the country accept, file, register changes to, and cancel the filing applications of domestic food importers, and carry out supervision and inspection of filing enterprises.

## Chapter II Filing conditions and procedures

**Article 4**　An overseas exporter or agent that exports food into China shall apply to the General Administration of Customs for filing, submit the Information Form for Filing of Overseas Food Exporters or Agents (Annex 1), and is responsible for the truthfulness and validity of the filing information provided.

**Article 5**　A domestic food importer applying for filing shall have obtained the corresponding domestic business qualification and the consignee and consignor registration for import and export goods with customs, and shall submit the Food Importer Filing Information Form (Annex 2) to the customs office at its domicile, being responsible for the truthfulness and validity of the information provided.

A domestic food importer may complete importer filing together with its consignee and consignor registration for import and export goods.

**Article 6**　Where the filing application form is fully completed and the accompanying documents are complete, customs shall accept the application and complete the filing within 3 working days from the date of acceptance. Where the application documents do not meet requirements, customs shall inform the applicant of everything required at one time within 3 working days.

For an overseas exporter or agent that obtains filing, customs issues a filing code.

Filing information is published through the China Customs Enterprise Credit Information Publicity Platform.

The filing of importers and exporters remains valid indefinitely.

**Article 7**　Where the content of an importer's or exporter's filing changes, the change procedures shall be completed with the filing customs office within 60 days from the date the change occurs.

Where a domestic food importer's information changes, for example on relocation, it shall promptly apply to the customs office at its new domicile for an information change. Where the consignee and consignor registration for import and export goods is affected, a corresponding change application shall be filed at the same time.

**Article 8**　Where an importer or exporter falls under any of the following circumstances, customs cancels its filing in accordance with the law:

(1) It has terminated due to dissolution, bankruptcy declared by a court, or another legal cause;

(2) Its registration has been cancelled or revoked, or its business license revoked, by the market regulation authority;

(3) The importer or exporter applies for cancellation on its own initiative;

(4) Its consignee and consignor registration for import and export goods has been cancelled by customs;

(5) Other circumstances under which cancellation is required by law.

**Article 9**　Importers and exporters may apply to customs for filing, changes, and cancellation through the international trade Single Window or the customs government services portal. A domestic food importer may also submit a paper Food Importer Filing Information Form to the customs office at its domicile to apply for filing, changes, and cancellation.

## Chapter III Supervision and administration

**Article 10**　Customs may supervise the filing status of importers and exporters, including through on-site inspections, lawful document review, and requests that importers and exporters submit materials. Importers and exporters shall cooperate and truthfully provide relevant information and materials.

**Article 11**　When food is declared for import, the name of the importer or exporter, the unified social credit code of the domestic food importer, the overseas exporter or agent filing code, and other information shall be truthfully declared.

Customs verifies importer and exporter information.

**Article 12**　Where customs finds during supervision that filing information does not match actual circumstances, it shall require the importer or exporter to correct and complete the filing information within a specified period. Where the information is not corrected or completed as required, customs orders correction within a prescribed period.

## Chapter IV Supplementary provisions

**Article 13**　These Provisions take effect on the date of issuance. The General Administration of Customs is responsible for their interpretation. The Administrative Provisions on Filing of Food Importers and Exporters, published as Annex 1 to Announcement No. 55 of 2012 of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine, are repealed at the same time.
