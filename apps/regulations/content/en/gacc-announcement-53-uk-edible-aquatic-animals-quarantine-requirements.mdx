Live aquatic animals from the United Kingdom, farmed or wild and intended for human consumption, are approved for import into China, excluding species in CITES appendices or China's protected wildlife and invasive alien species lists. Quarantine approval and an entry quarantine permit are required.

Production enterprises, including farms, packing houses and fishing vessels, must apply HACCP principles and traceability, be approved and supervised by the United Kingdom side, recommended by it and registered by China. Drugs banned by both sides may not be used during farming, capture, holding, packing or transport.

Each consignment needs a British health certificate in one original and two copies, stating scientific names, the farm or fishing waters, the enterprise name and approval number and dispatch details, and declaring protocol compliance. Non-compliant goods are returned, destroyed or otherwise handled, and serious or repeated violations may suspend imports.

Under the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions on quarantine and hygiene requirements for edible aquatic animals from the United Kingdom exported to China agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland (hereinafter referred to as the British side), products meeting the following requirements are approved for import as of today.

I. Quarantine and Inspection Basis

(i) The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) The Animal and Plant Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit and its implementing regulations;

(iii) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(iv) The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Quarantine and Inspection of Entry Aquatic Animals;

(v) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland on Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for Edible Aquatic Animals from the United Kingdom Exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Scope of Products Approved for Import

For the purposes of this announcement, edible aquatic animals mean live aquatic animals, farmed or wild, intended for human consumption, excluding species listed in the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES) appendices, the List of Key Protected Wild Animals in China and the List of Invasive Alien Species under Key Management. The relevant species lists are published on the website of the General Administration of Customs.

III. Quarantine Approval

Importers of edible aquatic animals from the United Kingdom must complete quarantine approval procedures in advance and obtain an Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit.

IV. Requirements for Production Enterprises

Production enterprises exporting edible aquatic animals to China, including farms, packing houses and fishing vessels, must meet the following conditions:

(i) The enterprise must maintain effective food safety management based on hazard analysis and critical control point (HACCP) principles and a traceability system for edible aquatic animals, ensuring that the entire process of farming, capture, packing, storage, transport, transit and export complies with the relevant hygiene and traceability requirements of both sides; its employees must observe infectious disease prevention measures;

(ii) Edible aquatic animals exported to China must comply with the quarantine and food safety and hygiene requirements of both China and the United Kingdom;

(iii) The enterprise must be approved and effectively supervised by the British side and comply with public health procedures issued by the British side;

(iv) The enterprise must be recommended by the British side to the Chinese side and obtain registration with the Chinese side. Without registration, it may not export to China.

V. Requirements for Imported Products

(i) Wild edible aquatic animals exported to China must be legally captured within the United Kingdom or by British fishing vessels in international waters, and farmed edible aquatic animals must be raised in waters within the United Kingdom;

(ii) Drugs banned by both sides may not be used during farming, capture, holding, packing or transport of edible aquatic animals exported to China, and drugs restricted or permitted by both sides must be used as prescribed;

(iii) The British side must carry out effective monitoring of animal diseases, toxic and harmful substances and foodborne microorganisms in edible aquatic animals exported to China, and the monitoring results must meet Chinese food safety and animal health standards;

(iv) Before export, edible aquatic animals must undergo clinical inspection by British official inspection officers, show no clinical signs of infectious animal disease or parasitic disease, and be fit for export.

VI. Packaging and Labelling Requirements

(i) Edible aquatic animals exported to China from different fishing areas or registered enterprises must be packaged separately, and different species must be packaged independently;

(ii) Packaging containers must be new or have been disinfected and must meet the needs of animal survival and welfare;

(iii) Packaging materials, water or ice and bedding materials must meet safety and hygiene requirements and must not contain pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or harmful substances harmful to animals, plants and human health, or aquatic organisms likely to damage the aquatic ecological environment;

(iv) Labels must be affixed to outer packaging, indicating the name and scientific name of the aquatic animal, the fishing area (only for wild-caught aquatic animals), the name of the farm or packing house and the exporter, the registration number and the destination, which must be marked as the People's Republic of China.

VII. Certificate Requirements

Each consignment of edible aquatic animals exported to China must be accompanied by a health certificate issued by the British side, in one original and two copies, with the original accompanying the goods. The health certificate must be printed in Chinese and English or in English and is invalid if handwritten (except for the inspection officer's signature) or altered. It must include at least the following:

(i) The species of the edible aquatic animals (scientific and Latin names), packaging type and quantity or weight;

(ii) For farmed edible aquatic animals, the name of the farm and approval number; for wild-caught edible aquatic animals, details of the fishing waters and the name and approval number of the packing enterprise;

(iii) The names and addresses of the exporter and importer;

(iv) The time of dispatch, the port of departure and the type and number of the means of transport;

(v) A declaration that the edible aquatic animals meet the relevant requirements of the Protocol and are fit for human consumption;

(vi) The time of issuance of the certificate and the signature or seal of the name of the British official veterinarian;

(vii) The official seal of the British competent authority.

VIII. Other Requirements

China Customs conducts import quarantine and inspection on edible aquatic animals exported to China.

Non-compliant products will be returned, destroyed or otherwise handled in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations. For enterprises with serious problems or repeated non-compliance, the Chinese side may intensify quarantine and inspection or suspend imports.

This announcement is hereby issued.
