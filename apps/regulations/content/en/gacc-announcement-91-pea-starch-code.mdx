How should this batch be declared? It comes down to the commodity code. GACC Announcement No. 91 of 2026 takes effect on 1 July: under tariff line 11081900, pea starch is declared as 11081900.10 and other starches as 11081900.90. Tell the two apart and never swap the last two digits, since the correct suffix decides whether your declaration goes through smoothly.

Follow one starch shipment through the whole process: the consignee or its agent first determines whether the goods are pea starch, then fills in 11081900.10 or 11081900.90 accordingly. The code and the product name on the declaration must match the actual goods. A wrong code will affect both classification and the assessment of duty, so check everything before filing.

What if the code was filled in wrongly? Check the product name against the tariff line: pea starch declared as 11081900.90, or other starch declared as 11081900.10, is an inaccurate declaration and must be corrected under customs rules. Classification follows the actual goods and the tariff in force, so verify the real product and fix the record through the proper procedure.

On 30 June 2026, the Ministry of Commerce issued Announcement No. 25 of 2026, deciding to impose provisional anti-dumping measures in the form of deposits on imported pea starch originating in Canada from 1 July 2026, and at the same time specifying the concrete product scope covered by the provisional anti-dumping measures. In order to implement the above provisional anti-dumping measures, the relevant commodity code declaration requirements are hereby announced as follows:

When the consignee of imported goods or its agent declares goods under tariff line 11081900, the commodity code for “pea starch” shall be filled in as 11081900.10, and the commodity code for “other starches” shall be filled in as 11081900.90.

This Announcement shall take effect from 1 July 2026.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China

30 June 2026
