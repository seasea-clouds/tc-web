Import is banned as of today. After the WOAH semi-annual report showed Newcastle disease in Malawi and Morocco, the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs issued Announcement No. 125: poultry and related products from the two countries, whether direct, transshipped via third countries, or posted or carried, are all covered.

Your frozen chicken from Malawi, transshipped via a third country, reaches port: check the origin first; if banned, withdrawal is no longer arranged. Goods already in port are returned or destroyed under customs supervision; waste and kitchen swill unloaded from conveyances are disinfected and not discarded without authorization; illegal border-seized goods are destroyed as well.

The six measures take effect on the date of issuance. Violations are dealt with by customs under the Customs Law, the Biosecurity Law, the Entry-Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and implementing regulations; customs and agriculture departments at all levels perform quarantine, prevention and supervision. The country and consignment are only an illustration; the ban scope and the evidence of origin prevail.

<!-- original -->

The semi-annual report information of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) shows that Newcastle disease is present in Malawi and Morocco. To prevent the introduction of the disease and to protect the safety of China's livestock sector and its biosecurity, in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, this announcement is hereby made as follows:

I. The direct or indirect import from Malawi and Morocco of poultry and related products (products derived from poultry that are unprocessed or, although processed, may still transmit the disease) is prohibited.

II. The posting or carrying of poultry and related products from Malawi and Morocco into the country is prohibited. Once found, such items shall all be returned or destroyed.

III. Animal and plant waste, kitchen swill and other matter unloaded from inbound conveyances coming from Malawi and Morocco shall all undergo disinfection treatment under customs supervision and shall not be discarded without authorization.

IV. Poultry and related products from Malawi and Morocco seized by border inspection and other departments as illegally entered shall all be destroyed under customs supervision.

V. Whoever violates the above provisions shall be dealt with by customs in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and the relevant provisions of their implementing regulations.

VI. Customs offices at all levels and agricultural and rural affairs departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Animal Epidemic Prevention and other relevant provisions, cooperate closely and carry out quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision work well.

This announcement shall take effect on the date of its issuance.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of China and Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

27 August 2026
