Poliomyelitis virus transmission still constitutes a public health emergency of international concern. Announcement No. 129 of the General Administration of Customs takes effect on 3 September 2026 for 1 year: persons entering from affected countries (regions) declare proactively, and contaminated conveyances, cargo and postal parcels undergo sanitary treatment.

A shipment arrives from a country (region) affected by poliomyelitis: contaminated or possibly contaminated containers, goods and outer packaging must undergo sanitary treatment as required. Accompanying personnel who have had contact with a case or show fever, sore throat, diarrhoea or a stiff neck declare proactively on entry, and customs officers take medical measures under prescribed procedures.

The Announcement also covers countries (regions) newly announced by the World Health Organization as affected during the validity period. The publication date and the effective date differ, so arrange declarations and sanitary treatment by the effective date; until the period expires, the official Announcement governs.

On 25 August 2026, the World Health Organization issued the statement of the forty-fifth meeting of the Emergency Committee under the International Health Regulations on poliomyelitis, confirming that the risk of poliovirus transmission still constitutes a “public health emergency of international concern”. To prevent the introduction of poliomyelitis into China, protect people's lives and health, and pursuant to the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China and its Implementing Rules and other laws and administrative regulations, the following is hereby announced:

I. Persons coming from countries (regions) where poliomyelitis outbreaks occur shall, if they have been in contact with poliomyelitis cases or present symptoms such as fever, sore throat, cough, vomiting, diarrhoea, stiff neck, limb pain and acute flaccid paralysis of the lower limbs, declare proactively to customs upon entry, and customs officers will take medical measures in accordance with prescribed procedures.

II. Means of transport, transport equipment such as containers, goods, luggage, postal parcels and other articles, and outer packaging that come from countries (regions) where poliomyelitis outbreaks occur and are contaminated or possibly contaminated shall undergo sanitary treatment in accordance with regulations.

The contents of this Announcement take effect from 3 September 2026, with a validity period of 1 year. During this period, countries (regions) newly announced by the World Health Organization as experiencing poliomyelitis outbreaks shall be handled in accordance with this Announcement.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

2 September 2026
