## Which Table Each of the Nine Interface Families Lands In

The interface table has four columns: which side of your system it is, what the integration item is, who leads the direction, and where the data finally lands. Direction takes three forms, two-way, inbound and you lead; rows with no direction marked are carried on our side. The landing column says which ledger each field belongs to, so whoever takes over can check it against the table.

- Goods and stock: SKU stock and prices, two-way, kept consistent with the warehouse management system and the customs ledger.
- Orders: order data, inbound, lands in the three document fields.
- Member data: promoter relations, you lead, the data is yours and we process it as an entrusted handler.
- Payouts: commission payout, two-way, the China segment is executed by the onshore entity.
- Inventory: the bonded warehouse system, no direction marked, lands in the bonded operations system and shares its source with the ledger.
- Customs: the three-document declaration, no direction marked, we carry the declaration and it stays an item under our control.
- Fulfillment: tracking feed-back, no direction marked, logistics tracking is written back to the order view.
- Returns: return documents, no direction marked, returns re-enter the supervision area through the returns centre.
- Finance: settlement, outbound, including currency and exchange.

Where data flows decides how the interfaces are built. The onshore ledger and the offshore dashboard have to be kept apart, otherwise joint testing will have both sides overwriting the same fields.

- Promotion data collected and settled onshore stays onshore.
- Only de-identified aggregate data goes back to your parent company, and it carries no onshore compensation relations.
- The overseas back office never touches raw China consumer data; cross-border paths open only after the personal information protection impact assessment is passed.
- Onshore promotion and compensation data supports full export and is handed over with the accounts.

The API joint-test monitor lists the health lights in four rows by interface type and counterparty, and every health check and re-push leaves a record. The demo page writes out three items, interface type, counterparty and health light, matching the same set used in the system interface demo; the rows are reviewed daily during joint testing.

- Order push, counterparty storefront, health light normal.
- Payment return, counterparty payment channel, health light normal.
- Waybill return, counterparty courier, health light warning.
- Declaration list, counterparty declaration channel, health light normal.

Set the integration terms around standard APIs first: where a standard API exists it is wired directly, and where none exists middleware maps it. Public-market benchmarks put standard integration at 3 to 7 days and full integration at 8 to 12 weeks; mega-sales are sized with a 10x peak margin. Each side names one technical contact and holds a daily stand-up during joint testing. After launch, run one complete settlement cycle in parallel before switching, so the two systems never diverge on data during the dual-run period.

## How the Mega-Sale Peak Is Judged and How Much Capacity to Reserve

The peak verdict splits into four cases by volume and response needs: low volume with high response needs looks at elasticity, high volume with high response needs looks at lead time, low volume with low response needs looks at baseline capability, and high volume with low response needs looks at steady-state capacity. The capacity curve relative to daily capacity has three tiers: 1x for daily operations, 4 to 5 times for a mega-sale and 10x at peak, with the peak side reserved at a 10x margin. The earlier you give us the pre-filed volumes, the more room there is to plan expansion and staffing; if the volumes arrive a week before the sale, adding staff is about all that is left.

- Low volume and high response needs go to peak elasticity: public cases run at 4 to 5 times daily volume, peaks reach tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands of units, and mega-sale staffing is about 3 times normal; these are public-case benchmarks.
- High volume and high response needs are bounded by lead time: ultra-high peaks need a lead time, SKU growth scales the warehouse and the ledger together, mega-sales reserve at a 10x peak, and the volume inputs come from you.
- Low volume and low response needs are baseline capability: a branded store with licensed payments, documents built in, more than 50 ports and more than 70 ERP systems connected, AEO advanced certification and our own clearance; our side is ready.
- High volume and low response needs are steady-state capacity: sized to daily orders, with early warehouse entry and zoned storage, permanent and temporary staff working together, pre-filed volumes in place, and returns re-entering the supervision area; our side runs it as a process.

The warehouse candidates are four comprehensive bonded zone sites plus a warehouse in Hong Kong, China. Four sites in parallel give multi-port redundancy; you decide the final port, and the candidate list is handed over at the start, so there is no need to wait until the goods reach the port. The Hong Kong, China warehouse sits on the offshore side and is recorded separately from the four onshore sites.

## What to Fix Before Launch on Interfaces and Cutover

Before launch, fix three things: write standard APIs and middleware mapping into the interface table, pin health-light thresholds, failed re-pushes and owners to individual rows, and align pre-filed volumes with capacity tiers on the same set of SKUs. If any of the three is missing, joint testing stalls on re-pushes. The call volumes, success rates, pre-filed volumes and completion dates in the demo interface are illustrative only; peak and staffing figures come from public cases and show magnitude only, with the actual volumes supplied by you. Real declaration practice follows the requirements of the competent authority, and cross-border data paths follow the outcome of the personal information protection impact assessment. Run one complete settlement cycle in parallel before switching, confirm that the two systems have not diverged on data, and only then move the traffic over.
