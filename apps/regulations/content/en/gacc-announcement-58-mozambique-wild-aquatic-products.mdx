Wild aquatic products from Mozambique are cleared for import. Announcement No. 58 of 2026 requires producing enterprises to be approved by Mozambique and registered in China.

They must be legally harvested, free of banned drugs and fully traceable, with packaging and labelling meeting Chinese standards, and enter with a jointly confirmed bilingual health certificate.

On arrival they are inspected under the food safety laws and the Protocol; non-compliant goods are destroyed or returned, and serious or repeated failures may suspend imports.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Environment and Fisheries of the Republic of Mozambique (hereinafter the Mozambican side) on inspection and quarantine requirements for wild aquatic products from Mozambique for export to China, imports of wild aquatic products from Mozambique that meet the following requirements are permitted with effect from today.

I. Inspection and quarantine basis

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations.

(2) The Measures for the Safety and Supervision of Imported Food and the Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Producers of Food Imported into the People's Republic of China.

(3) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Environment and Fisheries of the Republic of Mozambique on Inspection and Quarantine Requirements for Wild Aquatic Products for Export to China (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Scope of imported products

Wild aquatic products refer to wild aquatic animal products and their products for human consumption, and marine plant products such as algae and their products, excluding amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, live aquatic animals and aquatic animal and plant breeding materials.

III. Requirements for producing enterprises

Enterprises producing wild aquatic products for export to China (including production, processing and storage enterprises) shall be approved by the Mozambican side and subject to its effective supervision. The food safety, hygiene management and protection systems of the enterprises shall comply with China's relevant food safety laws and regulations.

In accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, enterprises producing wild aquatic products for export to China shall be recommended by the Mozambican side for registration in China. No enterprise may export to China without registration.

IV. Requirements for imported products

The Mozambican side shall ensure that wild aquatic products for export to China meet the following conditions:

(1) They are legally harvested in national or international waters.

(2) The raw materials and products are free of the following circumstances:

(1) Wild aquatic products from Mozambique already exported or proposed for export to China violate China's food safety laws, regulations, national standards or the Protocol, seriously affecting the safety of the products.

(2) Aquatic animal diseases related to the products of the Protocol that are listed in the List of Quarantine Diseases for Imported Animals of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the List) or notifiable under the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) requirements occur in Mozambique, seriously affecting product safety.

(3) A major food safety incident occurs in Mozambique, a major public health incident occurs in a producing enterprise, or fishing waters are affected by pollutants, seriously affecting product safety.

(3) No drugs or additives banned by China are used, directly or indirectly, and drugs restricted or permitted by the Chinese side are used as prescribed.

(4) Following inspection and quarantine by the competent authority, no pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or harmful substances or foreign matter listed in Chinese laws and regulations are found, and no aquatic animal diseases listed in the List or notifiable under WOAH requirements are found.

(5) The entire production process (including harvesting, production, processing, packaging, storage, transport, transshipment and export) complies with China's relevant safety and hygiene and traceability requirements.

(6) The packaging and labels of the products comply with China's national food safety standards and the relevant provisions on imported food safety administration.

V. Certificate requirements

The Mozambican side shall conduct inspection and quarantine on wild aquatic products for export to China as required by the Chinese side and issue a health certificate confirmed by both sides for each consignment, stating that the products comply with China's food safety laws, regulations and national standards. The certificate shall be printed in at least Chinese and English, with English mandatory for completion.

The health certificate shall be fully completed with the information of the producing enterprise. The Mozambican side shall promptly provide the Chinese side with certificate specimens and official seals for record-filing. In the event of any change, the Mozambican side shall file with the Chinese side at least one month before the change takes effect.

VI. Other requirements

China Customs shall conduct inspection and quarantine on wild aquatic products for export to China in accordance with China's food safety laws, regulations, national standards and the Protocol.

Non-compliant products shall be destroyed, returned or otherwise treated. For enterprises with serious problems or repeated non-compliance, the Chinese side may strengthen inspection and quarantine or suspend imports.

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs
