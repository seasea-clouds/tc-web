Djibouti wild aquatic products are cleared for import. Announcement No. 46 of 2026 requires producers to be approved by Djibouti and recommended for registration in China.

Products must be legally harvested in national or international waters, free of China's prohibited drugs, produced under hygienic and traceable conditions, and packaged and labelled in compliance with Chinese standards.

Each consignment shall be accompanied by a mutually confirmed health certificate in Chinese and English, with English mandatory; non-compliant goods shall be destroyed, returned or otherwise treated.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Water, Fisheries, Livestock and Marine Resources of the Republic of Djibouti (hereinafter the Djibouti side) on inspection and quarantine requirements for wild aquatic products from Djibouti for export to China, imports of Djibouti wild aquatic products that meet the following requirements are permitted with effect from today.

I. Inspection and quarantine basis

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations.

(2) The Measures for the Safety and Supervision of Imported Food and the Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Producers of Food Imported into the People's Republic of China.

(3) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Water, Fisheries, Livestock and Marine Resources of the Republic of Djibouti on Inspection and Quarantine Requirements and Health for Wild Aquatic Products for Export to China (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Scope of products permitted for import

Wild aquatic products refer to wild aquatic animal products and their products for human consumption, algae and other marine plant products and their products, excluding live aquatic animals and aquatic animal and plant breeding materials.

III. Requirements for producing enterprises

Enterprises producing wild aquatic products for export to China (including production, processing and storage enterprises) shall be approved by the Djibouti side and subject to its effective supervision. The food safety, hygiene management and protection systems of the enterprises shall comply with the relevant Chinese laws and regulations on food safety.

In accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, enterprises producing wild aquatic products for export to China shall be recommended by the Djibouti side for registration with the Chinese side. Without registration, they shall not export to China.

IV. Requirements for imported products

The Djibouti side shall ensure that wild aquatic products for export to China meet the following conditions:

(1) Legally harvested in national or international waters.

(2) The raw materials and products are free of the following circumstances:

(1) Wild aquatic products from Djibouti already exported or proposed for export to China violate Chinese food safety laws, regulations, national standards and the Protocol, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products for export to China.

(2) Animal diseases of aquatic animals related to the products of the Protocol, listed in the List of Animal Quarantine Diseases for Entry of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the List) or notifiable under the requirements of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH), occur in Djibouti and have seriously affected the safety of wild aquatic products for export to China.

(3) A major food safety incident occurs in Djibouti, a major public health incident occurs in a producing enterprise, or the harvesting waters are affected by pollutants, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products for export to China.

(3) No drugs or additives prohibited by China are used directly or indirectly, and drugs or additives restricted or permitted by the Chinese side are used in accordance with the provisions.

(4) Upon inspection and quarantine by the competent authorities, no pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or harmful substances and foreign matter listed in Chinese laws and regulations are found, and no aquatic animal diseases listed in the List or notifiable under WOAH requirements are found.

(5) The whole production process (including harvesting, production and processing, packaging, storage, transport, transshipment and export) complies with the relevant Chinese safety and hygiene requirements and traceability requirements.

(6) The packaging and labelling of the products comply with China's national food safety standards and the relevant provisions on the safety supervision of imported food.

V. Certificate requirements

The Djibouti side shall inspect and quarantine wild aquatic products for export to China as required by the Chinese side and issue a health certificate confirmed by both sides for each consignment, certifying that the consignment complies with the relevant provisions of relevant Chinese laws and regulations. The certificate shall be printed in at least Chinese and English, with English mandatory when the certificate is filled in. The health certificate shall contain complete information on the producing enterprise of the wild aquatic products. The Djibouti side shall promptly provide the Chinese side with the certificate specimen and official seal for record-filing. In the event of any change, the Djibouti side shall file with the Chinese side at least one month before it takes effect.

VI. Other requirements

China Customs shall inspect and quarantine wild aquatic products for export to China in accordance with Chinese food safety laws, regulations, national standards and the Protocol. Non-compliant products shall be destroyed, returned or otherwise treated. For enterprises with serious problems or repeated non-compliance, the Chinese side may strengthen inspection and quarantine or suspend imports.
