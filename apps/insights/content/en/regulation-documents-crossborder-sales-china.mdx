The 1210 channel runs on ten regulatory constants, and their sources do not sit at the same level. The consolidated tax of about 9.1%, the 0% tariff rate, and import VAT and consumption tax collected at 70% of the statutory amount are written in Cai Guan Shui [2016] No. 18. The single-transaction limit of 5,000 yuan and the annual limit of 26,000 yuan are written in Cai Guan Shui [2018] No. 49. The order of the three documents is set by GACC Announcement No. 194 of 2018, and the 30-day return application with the 45-day arrival deadline by GACC Announcement No. 45 of 2020. The remaining items, where the quota check actually falls, the 15 days to complete a personal transaction confirmation once an order is risk-targeted, payouts settled in batches by cycle, and the roughly six weeks to open a funds account, have only enforcement practice and working ranges behind them.

The source decides two things: whether the constant can be cited at all, and where you will learn when it moves. The items carrying a document number are published with an announcement, and the issuing agency's own site holds the original text. The items resting on practice alone drift with port enforcement habits and platform rules, and one question to the execution step beats a year in the archives. Sorting the ten constants by source is what tells the access memo which cell may go outside and which cell must be marked as liable to change at any time.

## Three tax items and how the 9.1% comes out

Cai Guan Shui [2016] No. 18 settled three matters for cross-border e-commerce retail imports. The tariff rate is set provisionally at 0%. Import VAT and consumption tax lose the exempt threshold and are collected provisionally at 70% of the statutory amount. The taxpayer is the individual consumer, and the logistics enterprise may withhold and remit on their behalf. The same article fixes the dutiable value at the actual transaction price, covering the retail price of the goods, the freight and the insurance, so all three enter the tax base.

The consolidated tax of about 9.1% is one figure that falls out of those sentences. The VAT rate now in force is 13%; multiply it by the 70% collection ratio and you get 9.1%. That formula holds only for categories with no consumption tax. Milk protein powder and protein powder carry none, so for them 9.1% is the whole burden. Goods in the 9% VAT band come out at 6.3%. Categories carrying consumption tax need that layer added on top, and the arithmetic is not a simple sum, so each one must be worked out separately.

The words “provisionally set” and “provisionally collected” stay in the source text. A 0% tariff and a 70% collection ratio are a policy arrangement, not a statutory setting, which is why the rate layer has remained on the review list ever since. The step that really decides the burden is not on the rate table. Which code the product lands on settles which VAT band applies and whether consumption tax enters at all, and that is a question for classification.

## Two limits, 5,000 yuan per order and 26,000 yuan per year

Cai Guan Shui [2018] No. 49 set the two limits at 5,000 yuan per transaction and 26,000 yuan per year. The two figures did not arrive from nowhere: No. 18 wrote 2,000 yuan per transaction and 20,000 yuan per year in 2016, and the 2018 raise has applied ever since. A limit that has moved once is an adjustable policy parameter, and a long-term plan cannot treat it as a physical constant.

The quota accumulates order by order per purchaser, and the check runs at clearance. Neither the real-name verification nor the receipt step tests it. This item belongs to customs enforcement practice; Announcement No. 194 governs the submission of the three documents and the tax procedures instead. The difference shows up in the consequence: an order above the limit can pass the checkout with no warning, get stopped only when the parcel reaches customs, and then the refund and the warehouse handling each need their own workflow.

Where a single order exceeds the per-transaction limit but contains only one unit, it may still be imported through the cross-border retail channel, with tariff, import VAT and consumption tax charged in full at goods rates and the amount counted toward the annual total. Where an order above the per-transaction limit holds more than one unit, or the annual total passes 26,000 yuan, the retail import regime no longer applies and the shipment is handled as general trade.

Article 3 of No. 49 carries a sentence that is easy to read past. Cross-border e-commerce retail import goods are final goods for the consumer's own use and may not be resold on the domestic market. The stock in the bonded warehouse may be sold to the person who placed the order; selling it on to anyone else leaves the premise this channel rests on. The 5,000 yuan per order is therefore a hard pricing constraint, not a suggestion. Multipacks, promo bundles and gift boxes must be priced to stay within 5,000 yuan per order, or the large orders will stall at clearance.

## Four deadlines, returns, risk targeting, three documents and payouts

GACC Announcement No. 45 of 2020 opened two windows for returns. Apply to customs for the return procedure within 30 days, and once customs approves, get the goods back to the original supervised site within 45 days. A return completed on those terms is not taxed, and the personal annual quota already used is adjusted accordingly. Read the two windows together and you still have to fit the warehouse return and the missing paperwork between them, so the slack you can actually use is tighter than 30 days.

An order pulled into risk targeting carries a different deadline: the purchaser must complete the personal transaction confirmation within 15 days. This one comes from customs enforcement practice, has no document number of its own, and missing it usually means the order is rejected and the workflow starts again. It lands on the same moment as the quota check, both tested by the system at the instant of clearance.

The timing of the three documents comes from Announcement No. 194. Order data and payment data are pushed first, and the logistics data is written back at the release step. Where identity verification has not happened yet, the purchaser and the payer must be the same person. That sequence settles the schedule for system integration, and it settles where you look first when an order throws an exception.

The last two are not in the legal text at all. Payouts follow the platform's settlement rules: batches released on a cycle, not per order, with the payment terms and settlement conditions written on the platform's rule pages. Opening a cross-border funds account takes about six weeks, a working range produced by bank account opening and cross-border receipt and payment stacked together. Both belong in the access memo with their source type marked, so a reader sees at a glance that either can change.

## Three families of document numbers, each kept by a different agency

![Comparison table of the ten regulatory constants and the three families of document numbers, each item marked with its figure and its source](/images/insights/regulation-documents-crossborder-sales-china/regulation-documents-crossborder-sales-china-fig-01.webp)

The cross-border core family sets access and the transaction baseline. Shang Cai Fa [2018] No. 486 gives the treatment of goods supervised as articles for personal use together with the enterprise's disclosure duty. No. 18 governs how tax is computed. No. 49 governs the two limits and the bar on resale. GACC Announcement No. 194 of 2018 governs the three documents and the tax procedures, Announcement No. 45 of 2020 governs returns, Announcement No. 7 of 2022 attaches the list adjustment table, and Announcement No. 108 of 2020 publishes the commodity classification decisions. GACC Decree No. 280 governs registration of overseas producers of imported food entering as goods.

The model and compliance family draws the boundary around selling methods. State Council Decree No. 443 sets the entry conditions for direct selling, and Decree No. 444 lists the pyramid selling conduct that is prohibited. Gong Tong Zi [2013] No. 37, article 5, handles the criminal treatment of remuneration based purely on team performance. The 2016 joint announcement No. 7 of the commerce and industry authorities gives the direct selling product catalogue. Read these together when you design promotion and rebate structures; taking one of them alone can produce the opposite conclusion.

The advertising, food and data family governs what you may say outward and what you hold about individuals. Articles 17, 18, 19, 28, 55 and 58 of the Advertising Law mark out the claim boundary and the liability. GB 2762-2025 sets the limits for contaminants in food. Article 5 of CAC Decree No. 16 lists the cross-border transfer of personal information that is necessary to conclude or perform a contract where the individual is a party as a case exempt from the standard contract. Articles 21, 30 and 39 of the Personal Information Protection Law cover entrusted processing, sensitive personal information and the notice on transfer abroad. Article 927 of the Civil Code requires the trustee to hand over the property acquired in handling the mandate, and article 31 of the E-Commerce Law requires transaction records to be kept for no less than three years.

Exemption from the standard contract is not exemption from every duty. The pre-transfer notice and the separate consent, and the personal information protection impact assessment, still have to be done. Reading Decree No. 16 as meaning data may simply leave the country is the most common misuse in this family. Article 927 of the Civil Code earns its place at exit and handover: what the entrusted party holds, the materials, the accounts and the balances, has to be transferable back.

The three families are maintained by different agencies and revise on different rhythms. The core family moves with announcements from the Ministry of Finance and the General Administration of Customs, the model family with judicial practice and the product catalogue, the advertising, food and data family with market regulation and cyberspace rules. One adjustment usually touches one family only, so subscribing through a single channel means missing the rest, and review has to run family by family.

## How one constant belongs in the working file

The same constant, written into the working file, needs four fields. Leave one out and it cannot be filled in when an inquiry arrives.

- Figure and basis: where you write 9.1%, also write the formula behind it (VAT at 13% times the 70% collection ratio) and the premise (this category carries no consumption tax).
- Document number and article: where Cai Guan Shui [2018] No. 49 applies, cite the number and the article; where the item rests on enforcement practice, write “customs practice” or “platform settlement rule”, and never mix the two source types in one line.
- Quoted wording: for the limits, the single-unit exception and the return windows, copy the key short phrases from the source text instead of a second-hand reading.
- Check date and channel: name the official page or the announcement annex you checked against; that date is the start of the next review.

A jar with a retail price of 38 US dollars and 4 US dollars in freight and insurance has a dutiable value of 42 US dollars, which converts to about 302 yuan at 7.2 yuan per US dollar. VAT in the 13% band with no consumption tax gives a consolidated tax of about 27.5 yuan, roughly 3.8 US dollars. With those four fields filled in, the paragraph can serve directly as the tax basis entry: formula, document number, premise and conversion are all on the page, and the reader does not have to guess backwards.

Three writing defects come up most often. Writing 9.1% without the formula and the premise, which fails as soon as the category changes. Writing “customs rules” without the document number, which leaves no working file to hand over in an inquiry. And putting the roughly six-week account opening in the same field as the 30-day return window, so a working range and a statutory deadline read as one class and the reader assumes both move with the announcements.

## When a constant moves, where do you hear it first

The limits are the case that already moved. No. 18 wrote 2,000 yuan per transaction and 20,000 yuan per year in 2016, and No. 49 changed them to 5,000 and 26,000 yuan in 2018, where they have stayed. The list has moved too: Announcement No. 7 of 2022 adjusted the retail import list to 1,476 codes and added a note to the dairy codes limiting them to bonded e-commerce goods. Classification decisions are added year after year, and a later announcement can rewrite the answer on the same product.

Where to read the changes: the announcement pages of the finance ministry and the customs office, the adjustment table attached to a list announcement, the customs office pages for classification decisions and administrative rulings, and the published replies of the local port customs. The four channels each cover one family, and subscribing to only one means missing the others. We review family by family when building an access memo, enter new announcements and new document numbers in the ledger, and state the check date on the deliverable.

Decree No. 280 is a reminder of another kind. It governs registration of overseas producers of food imported as goods and takes effect on 1 June 2026, while the exemption on the 1210 retail side comes from No. 486. The two lines change on their own schedules. When a new rule appears, ask first which channel it governs and which party it binds, and only then whether your own verdict has to be recalculated.

## Who to ask, and what to have ready before asking

The items resting on practice are best put to the execution step: where the quota check actually occurs, how the 15-day confirmation clock starts after risk targeting, how the platform's settlement batches and payment terms are scheduled. Those answers sit with the local customs office and on the platform's rule pages; published legal text does not carry them. Before asking, have the code, the list line and the product form ready, so the question lands on a specific entry. Otherwise the answer will be one sentence about how the port operates.

Taken together, the ten constants and the three families of document numbers make a file that supports a three-way conversation: document numbers with customs, rules with the platform, figures and dates internally. The file does not replace the verdict. What it decides is whether a verdict can be reviewed, and whether the right cells can be found for recalculation when the next version lands.
