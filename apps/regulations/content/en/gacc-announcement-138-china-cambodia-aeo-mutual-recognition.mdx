The mutual recognition arrangement takes effect 15 September: China and Cambodia Customs mutually recognise AEOs, Cambodia recognises China's advanced certified enterprises and China those certified under the Cambodian AEO system, with one set of facilitation measures for their import clearance.

Declaring an import consignment: lower inspection rates, priority checks when physical inspection is needed, a designated customs liaison for clearance issues, and rapid clearance once infrastructure recovers from trade disruption. All four need declaration data to confirm mutual recognition.

Exports to Cambodia: give the importer your own AEO code to declare. Imports from a Cambodian enterprise: enter the partner code separately in the Overseas Shipper Code field in the import declaration and the Shipper Code field in sea or air manifests. A wrong code voids the facilitation measures.

The “Arrangement between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the General Department of Customs and Excise of Cambodia on Mutual Recognition between the China Customs Enterprise Credit Management System and the Cambodia Customs 'Authorised Economic Operator' System” (hereinafter the “Mutual Recognition Arrangement”) shall be officially implemented from 15 September 2026. The relevant matters are hereby announced as follows:

I. Under the Mutual Recognition Arrangement, China Customs and Cambodia Customs mutually recognise each other's Authorised Economic Operators (AEO). Cambodia Customs recognises China Customs advanced certified enterprises as mutually recognised AEO enterprises, and China Customs recognises AEO enterprises certified under the Cambodia Customs AEO system as mutually recognised AEO enterprises.

II. When clearing import goods, China Customs and Cambodia Customs provide mutually recognised AEO enterprises with the following facilitation measures: a lower import inspection rate; priority inspection for goods requiring a physical check; a designated customs liaison officer responsible for communicating on and handling problems encountered by AEO enterprises during clearance; and, after major infrastructure recovers from a trade disruption, endeavouring to provide, to the greatest extent, rapid clearance for goods imported from the other party's AEO enterprises.

III. When a Chinese advanced certified enterprise exports goods to Cambodia, it must inform the Cambodian importer of its AEO code (AEOCN + the subject identification code at positions 9—17 of the unified social credit code, for example AEOCN123456789) so that the importer declares it in accordance with Cambodia Customs regulations; Cambodia Customs then confirms the status of the China Customs AEO enterprise and grants the relevant facilitation measures.

IV. When a Chinese enterprise imports goods from a Cambodian AEO enterprise, it must fill in the Cambodian AEO enterprise code separately in the “Overseas Shipper Code” field within the “Overseas Shipper” column of the import declaration and in the “Shipper AEO Enterprise Code” field of the maritime and air cargo manifests. The code is entered as “country code (KH) + enterprise code of 7 digits”, for example “KH2025008”. China Customs confirms the status of the Cambodian AEO enterprise and grants the relevant facilitation measures.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

11 September 2026
