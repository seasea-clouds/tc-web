From 1 December 2026, the new measures under GACC Decree No. 284 replace Decree No. 143 of 2011. Consignees of imported cosmetics are no longer required to file a record with the customs, the inspection location moves from the port to the declared destination, and customs clearance arrangements change accordingly.

At import, electronic comparison runs on the registration or filing number; samples, exhibits and non-trial samples for testing, research and development are exempt within reasonable quantities once a written use and disposal statement and a non-sale commitment are submitted. Semi-finished cosmetics skip registration or filing verification and label checks but must declare compliance with the mandatory requirements of national technical specifications.

For exports, manufacturer filing is cancelled and replaced by a quality management system and production-site inspection; where the importing country has no standard and the contract is silent, the GACC may designate the applicable standards. Risk monitoring, credit classification and suspension of imports apply to both the import and export ends.

## Measures of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China for the Inspection, Quarantine, Supervision and Administration of Import and Export Cosmetics

(Adopted at the executive meeting of the General Administration of Customs on 3 April 2026, promulgated by GACC Decree No. 284 of 6 May 2026 and effective from 1 December 2026)

### Chapter I General Provisions

**Article 1**　These Measures are formulated to strengthen the safety supervision and administration of import and export cosmetics and protect consumers' health and rights, in accordance with laws and administrative regulations such as the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations, the Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Cosmetics, and the Special Provisions of the State Council on Strengthening the Safety Supervision and Administration of Food and Other Products.

**Article 2**　These Measures apply to the inspection, quarantine, supervision and administration of the following import and export cosmetics:

(1) Those listed in the catalogue of entry-exit commodities subject to inspection and quarantine by the customs;

(2) Those whose inspection and quarantine shall be implemented by the customs under relevant international treaties, laws or administrative regulations.

**Article 3**　The customs shall conduct inspection, quarantine, supervision and administration of import and export cosmetics in accordance with law.

In accordance with the principles of risk management, the customs shall inspect import and export cosmetics in accordance with the conformity assessment procedures prescribed in the Law of the People's Republic of China on Inspection of Import and Export Commodities.

**Article 4**　Producers and operators of import and export cosmetics shall engage in production and operation activities in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and relevant standards, strengthen management, act in good faith and exercise self-discipline, and ensure the safety of cosmetics.

**Article 5**　The customs shall promote the application of modern science and technology and improve the informatization, digitalization and intelligent level of inspection, quarantine, supervision and administration of import and export cosmetics.

### Chapter II Inspection and Quarantine of Imported Cosmetics

**Article 6**　The consignee of imported cosmetics or its agent shall declare truthfully to the customs in accordance with law and submit relevant supporting materials.

Imported cosmetics shall have completed the registration of special cosmetics or the filing of ordinary cosmetics in accordance with law, and the customs shall verify them through automatic comparison of electronic data.

**Article 7**　The customs shall inspect imported cosmetics in accordance with the mandatory requirements of China's national technical specifications.

Where mandatory requirements of national technical specifications have not yet been formulated in China, inspection may be carried out by reference to relevant foreign standards designated by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 8**　Imported cosmetics shall be inspected at the destination declared by the consignee or its agent. The General Administration of Customs may designate another location for inspection as required for trade facilitation and import inspection work.

**Article 9**　The customs may conduct on-site inspection of imported cosmetics according to the needs of supervision and administration. Where laboratory testing is required, samples shall be drawn in accordance with the provisions, a sampling voucher shall be issued, and the samples shall be sent to an inspection institution for testing.

The inspection institution shall obtain the corresponding qualification accreditation in accordance with law, conduct inspection as required and issue a report within the prescribed time.

**Article 10**　The customs shall examine the labels of imported cosmetics in accordance with law, and the specific requirements shall be prescribed separately by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 11**　Where imported cosmetics pass inspection, the customs shall issue an inspection certificate in accordance with law.

Where imported cosmetics fail customs inspection and the non-conformity involves safety, health or environmental protection items, the customs shall order their destruction or return; where other items fail, technical treatment may be carried out under customs supervision, and the products may be sold or used only after re-inspection confirms compliance with the requirements; where technical treatment is not feasible or the products remain non-conforming after treatment, the customs shall order their destruction or return.

**Article 12**　The importer shall keep truthful records of information on imported cosmetics; the records shall be true and complete and ensure traceability.

The records shall be kept for no less than one year after the expiry of the product's shelf life; where the product's shelf life is less than one year, the records shall be kept for no less than two years.

**Article 13**　For samples used for the registration or filing of imported cosmetics and non-trial samples used by enterprises for testing, research and development and publicity, a statement of the use and disposal of the samples, a written commitment to non-sale and other materials required by the customs shall be provided at declaration; within a reasonable quantity for use, the customs shall exempt them from inspection. The importer shall truthfully record the distribution of the cosmetics, and the records shall be kept for no less than two years.

**Article 14**　Cosmetics declared and imported as official goods by foreign diplomatic and consular missions and by the representative offices in China of international organizations enjoying diplomatic privileges and immunities are exempt from inspection. Where necessary, the customs may conduct examination.

**Article 15**　Where an epidemic or disease occurring abroad may affect the safety of imported cosmetics, the customs shall conduct quarantine on imported cosmetics in accordance with law.

### Chapter III Inspection and Quarantine of Exported Cosmetics

**Article 16**　Exported cosmetics shall conform to the standards of, or contractual requirements in, the importing country or region. Where the importing country or region has no relevant standards and the contract has no requirements, relevant standards may be designated by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 17**　Manufacturing enterprises of exported cosmetics shall establish a quality management system and ensure its continuous and effective operation.

**Article 18**　Exported cosmetics shall be inspected at the place of production. The General Administration of Customs may designate another location for inspection as required for trade facilitation and export inspection work.

**Article 19**　Before export of cosmetics, the manufacturing enterprise, the consignor or its agent shall apply to the customs for inspection in accordance with law and submit relevant supporting materials.

**Article 20**　Where the customs conducts on-site inspection or laboratory testing of exported cosmetics according to the needs of supervision and administration, the provisions of Article 9 of these Measures shall apply.

**Article 21**　When exporting cosmetics, the consignor or its agent shall declare truthfully to the customs in accordance with law and submit relevant supporting materials.

The customs shall conduct examination of exported cosmetics in accordance with law according to the needs of supervision and administration.

**Article 22**　Where exported cosmetics pass customs inspection, they are permitted for export. Where the importing country or region requires an inspection certificate, the customs shall issue one.

Where exported cosmetics fail customs inspection, technical treatment may be carried out under customs supervision, and export shall be permitted only after re-inspection confirms compliance with the requirements; where technical treatment is not feasible or the products remain non-conforming after treatment, export shall not be permitted.

**Article 23**　For cosmetics produced by processing with supplied materials and fully re-exported, where qualifying documents complying with the regulations or standards of the intended re-export country or region can be provided upon import of the supplied materials, inspection against China's standards may be exempt; the processed products shall be inspected against the standards of, or contractual requirements in, the importing country or region.

**Article 24**　Where an epidemic or disease occurring within the territory may affect the safety of exported cosmetics, the customs shall conduct quarantine on exported cosmetics in accordance with law.

### Chapter IV Supervision and Administration

**Article 25**　With respect to import and export cosmetics other than those required to be inspected by the customs under these Measures, the customs shall conduct sampling inspection in accordance with national provisions.

**Article 26**　The customs shall conduct risk monitoring over the safety of import and export cosmetics, and organize the formulation and implementation of safety risk monitoring plans for import and export cosmetics.

**Article 27**　Where a safety problem arises in import and export cosmetics, or a cosmetics safety problem occurring within or outside China may affect the safety of import and export cosmetics, the customs may, on the basis of risk assessment conclusions, take the following disposal measures commensurate with the risk level:

(1) Increase the inspection items and raise the inspection frequency;

(2) Issue a risk warning or notify the relevant departments;

(3) Suspend imports;

(4) Other measures prescribed by the General Administration of Customs.

Where import and export cosmetics pose or may pose a serious risk to human health, the General Administration of Customs may directly take necessary emergency disposal measures.

Where the risk level of import and export cosmetics changes, the customs may adjust the risk disposal measures.

**Article 28**　Where import and export cosmetics have safety problems that have caused or may cause harm to human health and life safety, the producers and operators of such cosmetics shall take corresponding measures in accordance with law and report immediately to the customs.

**Article 29**　The customs shall implement credit management over the consignee of imported cosmetics and the manufacturing enterprises and consignors of exported cosmetics, and take classified management measures.

**Article 30**　Where the consignee of imported cosmetics, or the manufacturing enterprise or consignor of exported cosmetics objects to the customs inspection results, it may apply for re-inspection in accordance with relevant provisions on re-inspection of import and export commodities.

The customs shall not accept a re-inspection application in any of the following circumstances:

(1) Where the inspection results show that microbial indicators exceed the limits;

(2) Where the product has exceeded its shelf life;

(3) Where re-inspection cannot be carried out for other reasons.

### Chapter V Legal Liability

**Article 31**　Where the consignee of imported cosmetics fails to perform the obligations of return or destruction, the customs shall impose a warning or a fine of up to 10,000 yuan.

**Article 32**　Where producers and operators of import and export cosmetics commit other illegal acts, they shall be dealt with in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

### Chapter VI Supplementary Provisions

**Article 33**　The inspection, quarantine, supervision and administration of cosmetics imported through cross-border e-commerce retail import and of cosmetics declared for import as exhibits shall be carried out in accordance with relevant provisions.

**Article 34**　These Measures do not apply to import and export soap, except where the soap is claimed to have the efficacy of special cosmetics.

Import and export toothpaste and semi-finished cosmetics shall be managed by reference to these Measures. At the import stage, the customs exempts semi-finished cosmetics from registration or filing verification and from label examination.

**Article 35**　For the purposes of these Measures, the following terms mean:

(1) Cosmetics mean daily chemical industrial products applied to the skin, hair, nails, lips or other surfaces of the human body by spreading, spraying or other similar methods, for the purposes of cleaning, protecting, beautifying or decorating.

(2) Semi-finished cosmetics mean products that have completed all other production and processing procedures except the final filling or sub-packaging procedure.

**Article 36**　These Measures shall be interpreted by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 37**　These Measures shall take effect on 1 December 2026. The Measures for the Inspection, Quarantine, Supervision and Administration of Import and Export Cosmetics, promulgated by Order No. 143 of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on 10 August 2011 and amended by GACC Decree No. 238 of 28 April 2018, GACC Decree No. 240 of 29 May 2018 and GACC Decree No. 243 of 23 November 2018, are repealed simultaneously.
