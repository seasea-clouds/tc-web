Import is banned as of today. After Lebanon reported foot and mouth disease to the World Organisation for Animal Health, the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs issued Announcement No. 6: cloven hoofed animals and related products from Lebanon, whether direct, transshipped via third countries, or posted or carried, are all covered.

Your frozen pork from Lebanon, transshipped via a third country, reaches port: check the origin first; if banned, withdrawal is no longer arranged. Goods already in port are returned or destroyed under customs supervision; waste and kitchen swill unloaded from conveyances are disinfected and not discarded without authorization; illegal border-seized goods are destroyed as well.

The six measures take effect on the date of issuance. Violations are dealt with by customs under the Customs Law, the Biosecurity Law, the Entry-Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and implementing regulations; customs and agriculture departments at all levels perform quarantine, prevention and supervision. The country and consignment are only an illustration; the ban scope and the evidence of origin prevail.

<!-- original -->

Recently, Lebanon reported foot and mouth disease to the World Organisation for Animal Health. To prevent the introduction of the disease and to protect the safety of China's livestock sector and its biosecurity, the following is announced in accordance with relevant laws and regulations:

I. The direct or indirect import from Lebanon of cloven hoofed animals and related products (products derived from cloven hoofed animals that are unprocessed or, although processed, may still transmit the disease) is prohibited.

II. The posting or carrying of cloven hoofed animals and related products from Lebanon into the country is prohibited. Once found, such items shall all be returned or destroyed.

III. Animal and plant waste, kitchen swill and other matter unloaded from inbound conveyances coming from Lebanon shall all undergo disinfection treatment under customs supervision and shall not be discarded without authorization.

IV. Cloven hoofed animals and related products from Lebanon seized by border inspection and other departments as illegally entered shall all be destroyed under customs supervision.

V. Whoever violates the above provisions shall be dealt with by customs in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and the relevant provisions of their implementing regulations.

VI. Customs offices at all levels and agricultural and rural affairs departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Animal Epidemic Prevention and other relevant provisions, cooperate closely and carry out quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision work well.

This announcement shall take effect on the date of its issuance.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of China and Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

7 January 2026
