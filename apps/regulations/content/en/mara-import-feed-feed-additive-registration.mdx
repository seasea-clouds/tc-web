Overseas enterprises exporting feed and feed additives to China for the first time must apply for import registration and obtain an import registration certificate; without the certificate they may not sell or use the products. The application shall be handled by a domestic agent.

Application materials must be submitted in Chinese and English in duplicate together with samples. Overseas enterprises must also provide proof of approval in the place of production, Chinese label specimens and test reports. The certificate is valid for five years and may be renewed six months before expiry.

Overseas enterprises may not sell directly in China; they must establish a sales organization or appoint an agent and file a record. Packaging must carry Chinese labels, products with safety hazards must be recalled, and unlawful applications will not be accepted for three years.

Ministry of Agriculture Order No. 2 of 2014. The Measures for the Registration and Administration of Imported Feed and Feed Additives were deliberated and adopted at the 11th executive meeting of the Ministry of Agriculture on December 27, 2013, and are hereby promulgated, effective as of July 1, 2014. The Measures for the Registration and Administration of Imported Feed and Feed Additives promulgated by the Ministry of Agriculture on August 17, 2000 and revised on July 1, 2004 are hereby repealed. Minister Han Changfu, January 13, 2014.

Article 1 These Measures are formulated in accordance with the „Regulations on the Administration of Feed and Feed Additives“ to strengthen the supervision and administration of imported feed and feed additives and ensure the quality and safety of animal products.

Article 2 For the purposes of these Measures, „feed“ means products processed and manufactured industrially for animal consumption, including single feed, additive premixed feed, concentrated feed, compound feed and concentrate supplement.
For the purposes of these Measures, „feed additives“ means small or trace substances added in the course of feed processing, manufacturing and use, including nutritional feed additives and general feed additives.

Article 3 Where an overseas enterprise exports feed or feed additives to China for the first time, it shall apply to the Ministry of Agriculture for import registration and obtain an import registration certificate for feed and feed additives; without an import registration certificate, it may not sell or use them within China.

Article 4 An overseas enterprise applying for import registration shall entrust a domestic agent to handle the application.

Article 5 Feed and feed additives for which import registration is applied shall comply with the relevant laws, regulations and technical specifications of the place of production and of China.
Feed and feed additives whose production or use has not been approved, or has been prohibited, in the place of production shall not be registered.

Article 6 To apply for import registration of feed or feed additives, an applicant shall submit truthful, complete and standardized application materials (in Chinese and English, in duplicate) and samples to the Ministry of Agriculture.

Article 7 The application materials shall include:
(1) the application form for import registration of feed and feed additives;
(2) a letter of authorization and proof of the qualifications of the domestic agent: where an overseas enterprise entrusts its resident representative office in China to handle the registration, it shall provide the original letter of authorization and a copy of the „Registration Certificate of Resident Representative Office of Foreign Enterprise in China“; where it entrusts another domestic institution to handle the registration, it shall provide the original letter of authorization and a copy of the corporate business licence of the agent institution;
(3) proof of approval of production and use in the place of production, registration materials in other countries or regions outside the place of production, and the status of product promotion and application;
(4) for imported feed: product name, composition, physicochemical properties, scope of application and method of use; for imported feed additives: product name, main ingredients, physicochemical properties, product source, purpose of use, scope of application and method of use;
(5) production process, quality standards, testing methods and inspection reports;
(6) labels and trademarks used in the place of production and specimens of Chinese labels;
(7) for microbial products or fermentation products, a certificate of strain deposit issued by an authoritative institution shall also be provided.
For feed and feed additives referred to in Article 13 exported to China, the following additional application materials shall be submitted:
(1) a report on the chemical structure identification of the active ingredient, or a taxonomic identification report for animals, plants or microorganisms;
(2) a report on product efficacy evaluation tests and a report on safety evaluation tests (including target animal tolerance evaluation report, toxicological safety evaluation report, metabolism and residue evaluation report, etc.) issued by a testing institution designated by the Ministry of Agriculture; for an application for import registration of a feed additive, an analytical evaluation report on the possible impact of residues of the feed additive in aquaculture products on human health shall also be provided;
(3) a stability test report and an environmental impact report;
(4) where maximum residue limits are set for the feed product, the maximum limit value and the method for detecting the active ingredient in the feed product shall also be provided.

Article 8 Product samples shall meet the following requirements:
(1) for each product, samples from 3 batches, 2 portions per batch, shall be provided, and each portion shall be not less than 5 times the quantity required for testing;
(2) where necessary, relevant reference substances or chemical reference substances shall be provided.

Article 9 The Ministry of Agriculture shall examine the application materials within 10 working days from the date of acceptance of the application; if the examination is passed, the applicant shall be notified to submit the samples to an inspection institution designated by the Ministry of Agriculture for verification testing.

Article 10 Verification testing shall include verification of quality standards and testing of samples. Where national standards or industry standards exist for the testing method, the national standard or industry standard shall be used preferentially; where no national or industry standard exists, the testing method provided by the applicant shall be used; where necessary, the inspection institution may adjust the testing method in light of actual conditions.
The inspection institution shall complete the verification testing within 3 months and submit the verification testing report to the Ministry of Agriculture, with a copy to the applicant.

Article 11 Where an overseas enterprise objects to the result of verification testing, it shall apply for a retest within 15 working days from the date of receipt of the verification testing report.

Article 12 Where verification testing is passed, the Ministry of Agriculture shall issue and announce the import registration certificate for feed and feed additives within 10 working days.

Article 13 Where feed or feed additives for which import registration is applied fall under any of the following circumstances, the Ministry of Agriculture shall organize an evaluation in accordance with the evaluation procedures for new feed and new feed additives:
(1) feed or feed additives exported to China that have not yet been used in China but have been approved for production and use in the place of production;
(2) feed additives whose scope of application is expanded;
(3) feed additives whose content specification is lower than the requirements of the safety use standards for feed additives, except preparations compounded from feed additives and carriers or diluents in a certain proportion;
(4) feed additives whose production process has undergone significant changes;
(5) products for which the Ministry of Agriculture has issued a new feed or new feed additive certificate and which have not been put into production for more than 3 years from the date of certification;
(6) other circumstances involving quality and safety risks.

Article 14 The import registration certificate for feed and feed additives shall be valid for 5 years.
Where it is necessary to continue exporting feed or feed additives to China upon expiry of the import registration certificate, an application for renewal shall be submitted 6 months before the expiry of the validity period.

Article 15 The following materials shall be provided when applying for renewal:
(1) the application form for renewal of registration of imported feed and feed additives;
(2) a copy of the import registration certificate;
(3) a letter of authorization and proof of the qualifications of the domestic agent;
(4) proof of approval of production and use in the place of production;
(5) quality standards, testing methods and inspection reports;
(6) labels and trademarks used in the place of production and specimens of Chinese labels.

Article 16 Under any of the following circumstances, samples shall also be submitted for verification testing when applying for renewal:
(1) where the quality and safety testing items for the product need to be adjusted in accordance with relevant laws, regulations and technical specifications;
(2) where the testing method for the product has changed;
(3) where there is a record of non-conformity in supervisory spot checks.

Article 17 During the validity period of the import registration certificate, if the production site of the imported feed or feed additives is relocated, or if the product quality standard, production process, scope of application, etc. are changed, registration shall be applied for anew.

Article 18 Imported feed or feed additives falling under any of the following circumstances during the validity period of the import registration certificate shall be subject to change registration:
(1) where the Chinese or foreign trade name of the product is changed;
(2) where the name of the applicant enterprise is changed;
(3) where the name of the manufacturer is changed;
(4) where the name of the production address is changed.

Article 19 The following materials shall be provided when applying for change registration:
(1) the application form for change registration of imported feed and feed additives;
(2) a letter of authorization and proof of the qualifications of the domestic agent;
(3) the original import registration certificate;
(4) a statement of the change and relevant supporting documents.
The Ministry of Agriculture shall decide whether to approve the change within 10 working days after accepting the application for change registration.

Article 20 Relevant institutions and personnel engaged in the registration of imported feed and feed additives shall keep confidential the technical materials submitted by applicants that need to be kept confidential.

Article 21 Overseas enterprises shall, in accordance with the law, establish a sales organization in China or entrust a qualified domestic agent to sell imported feed and feed additives.
Overseas enterprises may not sell imported feed and feed additives directly in China.

Article 22 Overseas enterprises shall, within 6 months from the date of obtaining the import registration certificate for feed and feed additives, establish a sales organization in China or entrust a sales agency and report the same to the Ministry of Agriculture for record.
Where the sales organization or sales agency specified in the preceding paragraph is changed, a new record shall be filed with the Ministry of Agriculture within 1 month.

Article 23 Imported feed and feed additives shall be packaged, the packaging shall comply with the relevant safety and hygiene regulations of China, and shall be accompanied by Chinese labels conforming to the regulations.

Article 24 Where imported feed or feed additives are proved to be harmful to farmed animals, human health or the environment during use, the Ministry of Agriculture shall announce a ban and revoke the import registration certificate.
During the validity period of the import registration certificate for feed and feed additives, if the place of production prohibits the use of the product or revokes its production or use licence, the overseas enterprise shall immediately report to the Ministry of Agriculture, and the Ministry of Agriculture shall revoke the import registration certificate and make an announcement.

Article 25 Where an overseas enterprise discovers that feed or feed additives exported by it to China are harmful to farmed animals or human health or present other safety hazards, it shall immediately notify its sales organization or sales agency in China and report to the Ministry of Agriculture.
The sales organization or sales agency of the overseas enterprise in China shall take the initiative to recall the products referred to in the preceding paragraph, keep records of the recall, and report to the feed administration department of the place of sale.
The recalled products shall be subject to harmless treatment or destruction under the supervision of the feed administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level.

Article 26 The Ministry of Agriculture and the feed administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as needed, organize supervisory spot checks of imported feed and feed additives on a regular or irregular basis; the testing work for such spot checks shall be undertaken by an institution with corresponding technical conditions designated by the Ministry of Agriculture or by the feed administration department of a people's government at the provincial, autonomous region or municipality level.
Testing for supervisory spot checks of imported feed and feed additives shall be carried out on the basis of the quality standards determined by the verification testing during import registration.

Article 27 The Ministry of Agriculture and the feed administration departments of people's governments at the provincial level shall promptly publish the results of supervisory spot checks and may publish the names of overseas enterprises with bad records and their sales organizations and sales agencies.

Article 28 Relevant personnel engaged in the registration of imported feed and feed additives who fail to perform the duties prescribed in these Measures, or abuse their power, neglect their duties or engage in malpractices for personal gain, shall be subject to disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated and pursued in accordance with the law.

Article 29 Where an applicant submits false materials or samples or uses other fraudulent means to apply for import registration, the Ministry of Agriculture shall not accept or approve the application, and shall not accept any import registration application from the overseas enterprise and the registration agency for 1 year.
Where an applicant obtains an import registration certificate for feed and feed additives by submitting false materials or samples or by other fraudulent means, the Ministry of Agriculture shall revoke the import registration certificate, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the registration agency, and shall not accept any import registration application from the overseas enterprise and the registration agency for 3 years.

Article 30 Other acts in violation of these Measures shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the „Regulations on the Administration of Feed and Feed Additives“.

Article 31 These Measures shall take effect as of July 1, 2014. The Measures for the Registration and Administration of Imported Feed and Feed Additives promulgated by the Ministry of Agriculture on August 17, 2000 and revised on July 1, 2004 are hereby repealed.
