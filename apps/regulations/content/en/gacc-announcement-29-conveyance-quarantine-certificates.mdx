Ship and aircraft health quarantine certificates are getting new versions, so please note the name changes when you enter or exit. GACC Announcement No. 29 of 2026 merges 10 old certificates into 4 transport health quarantine certificates and splits the ship exemption and sanitary control certificate into 2 separate certificates.

You can download electronic versions yourself via the International Trade “Single Window” or the “Customs Government Service Platform” (online.customs.gov.cn), and paper and electronic certificates have equal legal force. Direct cross-strait routes have two additional dedicated certificates for arrival and departure.

This announcement takes effect on 1 April 2026, and the original 11 certificates are abolished at the same time. However, old ship sanitation certificates issued before implementation and still within their validity period remain valid, so you need not rush to reapply.

<!-- original -->

To implement the decisions and plans of the Party Central Committee and the State Council, further improve the business environment at ports, and enhance cross-border trade facilitation, the General Administration of Customs has decided to optimize and adjust 11 transport health quarantine certificates including the “Ship Entry Health Quarantine Certificate”. The relevant matters are hereby announced as follows:

First, the 10 certificates “Ship Entry Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Ship Entry Quarantine Certificate”, “Means of Transport Exit Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Means of Transport Quarantine Certificate”, “Ship Port-Departure Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Ship Port-Arrival Quarantine Certificate”, “Ship Port-Arrival Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Aircraft Port-Departure Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Aircraft Port-Arrival Quarantine Certificate” and “Aircraft Port-Arrival Health Quarantine Certificate” are consolidated into 4 certificates: “Inbound Means of Transport Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Outbound Means of Transport Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Health Quarantine Certificate for Inbound Means of Transport on Direct Cross-Strait Routes” and “Health Quarantine Certificate for Outbound Means of Transport on Direct Cross-Strait Routes” (collectively referred to as transport health quarantine certificates, models shown in Attachments 1 to 4). The “Ship Exemption from Sanitary Control Measures Certificate/Ship Sanitary Control Measures Certificate” is adjusted into 2 certificates, “Ship Exemption from Sanitary Control Measures Certificate” and “Ship Sanitary Control Measures Certificate” (collectively referred to as ship sanitation certificates, models shown in Attachments 5 to 6).

Second, ship and aircraft operators or agents may download electronic versions of the transport health quarantine certificates themselves via the International Trade “Single Window” or the “Customs Government Service Platform” (online.customs.gov.cn), and may apply for ship sanitation certificates and download electronic versions themselves through these channels.

Third, paper and electronic versions of the transport health quarantine certificates and ship sanitation certificates have equal legal force.

This announcement takes effect on 1 April 2026. The original 11 certificates, namely “Ship Entry Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Ship Entry Quarantine Certificate”, “Means of Transport Exit Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Means of Transport Quarantine Certificate”, “Ship Port-Departure Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Ship Port-Arrival Quarantine Certificate”, “Ship Port-Arrival Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Aircraft Port-Departure Health Quarantine Certificate”, “Aircraft Port-Arrival Quarantine Certificate”, “Aircraft Port-Arrival Health Quarantine Certificate” and “Ship Exemption from Sanitary Control Measures Certificate/Ship Sanitary Control Measures Certificate”, are abolished at the same time. The original “Ship Exemption from Sanitary Control Measures Certificate/Ship Sanitary Control Measures Certificate” issued before this announcement takes effect and still within its validity period remains valid.

This announcement is hereby issued.

Attachments: (1) Inbound Means of Transport Health Quarantine Certificate.

(2) Outbound Means of Transport Health Quarantine Certificate.

(3) Health Quarantine Certificate for Inbound Means of Transport on Direct Cross-Strait Routes.

(4) Health Quarantine Certificate for Outbound Means of Transport on Direct Cross-Strait Routes.

(5) Ship Exemption from Sanitary Control Measures Certificate.

(6) Ship Sanitary Control Measures Certificate.
