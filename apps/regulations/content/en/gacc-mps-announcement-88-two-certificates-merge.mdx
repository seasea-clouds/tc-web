Imported automobile and motorcycle certificates are now combined into one, so watch for the new Certificate of Import of Goods when clearing goods. Announcement No. 88 of 2026 of the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Public Security states that the Certificate and the Accompanying Certificate, formerly issued separately, are replaced by the new combined Certificate after release and inspection.

Apply within three years after release and inspection; if you request only one certificate, the original rules still apply. For imported non-China-specification vehicles, the Specification and Model column of the customs declaration must truthfully state the original destination country vehicle version and type, such as the original European or US specification.

This Announcement takes effect on 1 July 2026, repealing three earlier relevant announcements; where it conflicts with Announcement No. 34 of 2015, this Announcement prevails.

<!-- original -->

To further improve the business environment, enhance the customs clearance efficiency of imported motor vehicles, and promote the sustained and healthy development of China's automobile industry, the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Public Security have decided to comprehensively promote the reform of combining the Certificate of Import of Goods (Automobiles and Motorcycles) (hereinafter referred to as the Certificate) and the Accompanying Inspection Certificate for Imported Motor Vehicles (hereinafter referred to as the Accompanying Certificate) into one. The relevant matters are hereby announced as follows:

For automobiles and motorcycles imported through declaration to the customs in accordance with the law, where the Certificate and the Accompanying Certificate were originally issued separately under Announcement No. 34 of 2015 of the General Administration of Customs (Announcement on Matters Concerning the Certificate of Import of Goods) and the Measures for the Administration of the Inspection of Imported Automobiles (promulgated by Order No. 1 of the former State Administration of Entry-Exit Inspection and Quarantine and amended by Order No. 238 and Order No. 240 of the General Administration of Customs), a Certificate of Import of Goods (Automobiles and Motorcycles) combining the two certificates in one (hereinafter referred to as the new Certificate) shall be issued after the imported vehicle has completed the release procedures and passed inspection.

The consignee of the imported goods shall submit an application to the customs for issuance of the new Certificate within three years from the date on which the imported automobiles or motorcycles are released and pass inspection.

For imported non-China-specification vehicles, the consignee of the imported goods shall declare, in the Specification and Model column of the customs declaration, the vehicle version and type of the original country of destination for sale (such as the original European specification, the original US specification, the original Canadian specification, and the original Middle East specification).

Where the consignee of the imported goods applies for issuance of only one of the Certificate and the Accompanying Certificate, the application procedures shall be handled in accordance with the original provisions.

Where Announcement No. 34 of 2015 of the General Administration of Customs is inconsistent with the provisions of this Announcement, this Announcement shall prevail.

This Announcement shall take effect on 1 July 2026. Announcement No. 43 of 2023 of the General Administration of Customs, Announcement No. 70 of 2024 of the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Public Security, and Announcement No. 89 of 2025 of the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Public Security are repealed at the same time.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs Ministry of Public Security

16 June 2026
