Filing comes before customs clearance. An overseas filer appoints a Chinese enterprise legal person as the domestic responsible person, files with the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA), and assembles the formula, applied standard, label draft, inspection report and safety assessment.

Labels are checked item by item: product name, filer and domestic responsible person, manufacturing enterprise, standard number, all ingredients, net content, use-by date and safety warnings. The name runs trademark, generic and attribute, the attribute always the word toothpaste. Toothpaste claiming suitability for children follows separate children's rules.

Efficacy claims need a scientific basis, and a summary of the supporting literature, data or evaluation goes on the filing platform. New toothpaste ingredients split by function: preservative and colouring types need NMPA registration, others are filed, and both enter a 3-year safety monitoring period. Order No. 71 took effect on 1 December 2023, with the duty on the filer and the domestic responsible person.

Order of the State Administration for Market Regulation

No. 71

The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Toothpaste were adopted at the 2nd executive meeting of the State Administration for Market Regulation on 20 February 2023, are hereby promulgated, and shall come into force on 1 December 2023.

Luo Wen, Director

16 March 2023

Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Toothpaste

**Article 1**　These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Regulation on the Supervision and Administration of Cosmetics to standardize toothpaste production and business activities, strengthen the supervision and administration of toothpaste, ensure the quality and safety of toothpaste, protect consumer health and promote the sound development of the toothpaste industry.

**Article 2**　These Measures apply to toothpaste production and business activities and the supervision and administration thereof within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

**Article 3**　For the purposes of these Measures, toothpaste refers to a paste product applied to the surface of human teeth by friction, with cleaning as its main purpose.

**Article 4**　The National Medical Products Administration is responsible for the supervision and administration of toothpaste nationwide.

The departments responsible for drug supervision and administration of local people's governments at or above the county level are responsible for the supervision and administration of toothpaste within their respective administrative regions.

**Article 5**　Toothpaste is subject to filing management, and the toothpaste filer is responsible for the quality and safety of the toothpaste and for its efficacy claims.

Toothpaste producers and operators shall engage in production and business activities in accordance with laws, regulations, mandatory national standards and technical specifications, strengthen management, exercise integrity and self-discipline, and ensure the quality and safety of toothpaste products.

**Article 6**　An overseas toothpaste filer shall designate an enterprise legal person within the territory of China as the domestic responsible person to handle the filing, assist in toothpaste adverse reaction monitoring, implement product recalls, and cooperate with the supervisory inspections of the drug supervision and administration department.

**Article 7**　Toothpaste industry associations shall strengthen industry self-discipline, urge and guide producers and operators to engage in production and business activities in accordance with the law, and promote integrity in the industry.

**Article 8**　Natural or artificial raw materials used in toothpaste for the first time within the territory of the People's Republic of China are new toothpaste ingredients.

New toothpaste ingredients shall comply with the relevant provisions on the management of new cosmetic ingredients. New toothpaste ingredients with functions such as preservation and colouring may be used only after registration with the National Medical Products Administration; other new toothpaste ingredients are subject to filing management.

A safety monitoring system applies to new toothpaste ingredients that have obtained registration or completed filing, and the safety monitoring period is 3 years. New toothpaste ingredients for which no safety problem arises upon expiry of the safety monitoring period are included in the catalogue of toothpaste raw materials already in use formulated by the National Medical Products Administration.

**Article 9**　The toothpaste filer shall select raw materials that meet the requirements of laws, regulations, mandatory national standards and technical specifications for toothpaste production, and is responsible for the safety of the toothpaste raw materials it uses. When filing, the toothpaste filer shall specify the source of the raw materials and raw material safety related information through the filing information service platform.

**Article 10**　Domestic toothpaste shall be filed, before it is placed on the market for sale, with the drug supervision and administration department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the filer is located.

Imported toothpaste shall be filed with the National Medical Products Administration before importation. The National Medical Products Administration may, in accordance with the law, entrust the drug supervision and administration department of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government with the corresponding capacity to carry out the filing management of imported toothpaste.

**Article 11**　Where the filer or the domestic responsible person files toothpaste, the following materials shall be submitted:

(1) the name, address and contact details of the filer;

(2) the name, address and contact details of the manufacturing enterprise;

(3) the product name;

(4) the product formula;

(5) the standard applied to the product;

(6) the draft product label;

(7) the product inspection report;

(8) the product safety assessment materials.

For the filing of imported toothpaste, documents proving that the product has already been placed on the market for sale in the country or region of production, and materials proving that the overseas manufacturing enterprise complies with the Good Manufacturing Practice for Cosmetics, shall be submitted at the same time; where the product is produced exclusively for export to China and the documents proving that it has been placed on the market for sale in the country or region of production cannot be submitted, the materials of the relevant research and tests conducted for Chinese consumers shall be submitted.

**Article 12**　Before toothpaste is filed, the filer shall conduct a safety assessment itself or entrust a professional institution to do so.

Personnel engaged in safety assessment shall have professional knowledge related to the quality and safety of toothpaste or cosmetics and shall have more than 5 years of professional experience in the relevant field.

**Article 13**　Efficacy claims for toothpaste shall have an adequate scientific basis. The toothpaste filer shall publish on the filing information service platform a summary of the literature, research data or product efficacy evaluation materials on which the efficacy claims are based, and accept public supervision.

The National Medical Products Administration shall formulate, publish and adjust the catalogue of toothpaste classifications on the basis of factors such as the efficacy claims and the user groups of toothpaste. The scope and wording of toothpaste efficacy claims shall comply with laws, regulations, mandatory national standards, technical specifications and the provisions of the National Medical Products Administration.

**Article 14**　Efficacy claim evaluation for toothpaste shall comply with the requirements concerning quality safety and efficacy claim evaluation laid down in laws, regulations, mandatory national standards, technical specifications and the provisions of the National Medical Products Administration, and shall ensure that the results of the efficacy claim evaluation are scientific, accurate and reliable.

**Article 15**　Anyone engaging in toothpaste production activities shall apply, in accordance with the law, to the drug supervision and administration department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located for a production license. The toothpaste filer and the contract manufacturer shall establish a production quality management system and organize production in accordance with the requirements of the Good Manufacturing Practice for Cosmetics.

**Article 16**　Toothpaste adverse reaction reporting follows the principle of reporting on suspicion. Toothpaste producers and operators and medical institutions shall carry out toothpaste adverse reaction monitoring in accordance with the requirements of the cosmetics adverse reaction monitoring system formulated by the National Medical Products Administration.

**Article 17**　Toothpaste labels shall indicate the following content:

(1) the product name;

(2) the names and addresses of the filer and the contract manufacturer; where the filer is overseas, the name and address of the domestic responsible person shall also be indicated;

(3) the name and address of the manufacturing enterprise; for domestic toothpaste, the production license number of the manufacturing enterprise shall also be indicated;

(4) the number of the standard applied to the product;

(5) all ingredients;

(6) net content;

(7) the use-by date;

(8) necessary safety warnings;

(9) other content that shall be indicated under laws, administrative regulations and mandatory national standards.

Where the directions for use need to be indicated specifically in light of the characteristics of the product, they shall be indicated on the visible surface of the sales package.

**Article 18**　The name of a toothpaste product generally consists of three parts: the trademark name, the generic name and the attribute name. The attribute name of toothpaste shall uniformly be expressed with the word toothpaste.

Non-toothpaste products shall not deceive or mislead consumers by means such as indicating the word toothpaste.

**Article 19**　Toothpaste labels are prohibited from indicating the following content:

(1) content that expressly or implicitly indicates a medical effect;

(2) false or misleading content;

(3) content that violates public order and good morals;

(4) other content prohibited from being indicated by laws, administrative regulations, mandatory national standards and technical specifications.

**Article 20**　Toothpaste products claiming suitability for children shall comply with the provisions on children's toothpaste in laws, administrative regulations, mandatory national standards, technical specifications and other rules, and shall be labelled on the product label in accordance with the provisions of the National Medical Products Administration.

**Article 21**　Where toothpaste or the raw materials used therein do not comply with mandatory national standards, technical specifications, the technical requirements stated in the filing materials or the provisions of these Measures, they shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulation on the Supervision and Administration of Cosmetics.

**Article 22**　Where the toothpaste filer, the contract manufacturer, an operator or the domestic responsible person commits any of the following illegal acts, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulation on the Supervision and Administration of Cosmetics:

(1) providing false materials when applying for a toothpaste administrative license or handling filing, or forging, altering, renting, lending or transferring toothpaste license certificates;

(2) engaging in toothpaste production activities without a license, or organizing production not in accordance with the requirements of the Good Manufacturing Practice for Cosmetics;

(3) illegally adding substances that may endanger human health to toothpaste;

(4) changing the use-by date of toothpaste;

(5) failing to publish the summary of the basis for efficacy claims as required by these Measures;

(6) failing to monitor and report toothpaste adverse reactions as required by these Measures;

(7) refusing to implement a decision lawfully made by the drug supervision and administration department ordering a recall or ordering the cessation or suspension of production and business activities;

(8) the domestic responsible person failing to perform its obligations under these Measures, or an overseas toothpaste filer refusing to implement an administrative penalty decision lawfully made.

**Article 23**　For matters concerning the supervision and administration of toothpaste that are not provided for in these Measures, the provisions of the Measures for the Administration of Cosmetic Registration and Filing, the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Cosmetic Production and Business Operations and other rules shall apply by reference.

**Article 24**　After toothpaste or a new toothpaste ingredient has obtained registration or been filed, it shall be numbered in accordance with the following rules:

(1) new toothpaste ingredients: the national toothpaste raw material registration or filing code, plus a four-digit year, plus the sequential number of the toothpaste raw material registered or filed in that year;

(2) domestic toothpaste: the abbreviation of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, plus the national toothpaste online filing code, plus a four-digit year, plus the sequential number of the products filed within that administrative region in that year;

(3) imported toothpaste: the national toothpaste online import filing code, with the abbreviation of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the domestic responsible person is located in parentheses, plus a four-digit year, plus the nationwide sequential number of the products filed in that year;

(4) toothpaste from Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macao of China: the national toothpaste online filing code for toothpaste from these regions, with the abbreviation of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the domestic responsible person is located in parentheses, plus a four-digit year, plus the nationwide sequential number of the products filed in that year.

**Article 25**　These Measures shall come into force on 1 December 2023.
