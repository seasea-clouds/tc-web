Announcement No. 139 of 2026 of the General Administration of Customs, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment and the Ministry of Commerce took effect on the date of publication: to implement the Ecological Environment Code, the three agencies repealed the old Notice No. 172 of 2009 on the handling of offcuts, waste products and defective goods leaving export processing zones.

You have a batch of offcuts in an export processing zone to move out: when you process the exit formalities you no longer cite Notice No. 172 of 2009, and you need not prepare the special disposal documents that notice required; just state the type, quantity and destination of the goods as the current documents require.

The signature date is 5 September 2026, and the announcement takes effect on the date of publication, so publication and effect fall on the same day; only Notice No. 172 of 2009 is repealed, and the four issuing agencies named in that old notice are historical names that must not be confused with the three agencies signing this announcement.

<!-- original -->

To implement the Ecological Environment Code, the General Administration of Customs, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment and the Ministry of Commerce decide to repeal the Notice of the General Administration of Customs, the Ministry of Environmental Protection, the Ministry of Commerce and the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on the Handling of Offcuts, Waste Products and Defective Goods Leaving Export Processing Zones (No. 172 of 2009).

This announcement takes effect on the date of publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs, Ministry of Ecology and Environment, Ministry of Commerce

5 September 2026
