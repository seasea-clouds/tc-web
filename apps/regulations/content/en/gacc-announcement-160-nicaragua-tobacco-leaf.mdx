Nicaraguan cigar tobacco leaves have been approved for export to China. Announcement No. 160 of 2026 of the General Administration of Customs takes effect upon issuance. The leaves are dried, cured and graded cigar tobacco leaves with the scientific name Nicotiana tabacum; the planting bases and enterprises are reviewed by Nicaragua and jointly approved and registered by both sides.

The bases apply Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) and Integrated Pest Management (IPM) and monitor four quarantine pests; if oospores are found, China is notified immediately and exports from the affected area are suspended. Before export, 2% of each batch is sampled, reducible to 1% after two years without problems, and pesticide and heavy metal residues must not exceed the limits.

Each batch is accompanied by a phytosanitary certificate with the required English declaration, and the boxes are marked in Chinese or English with the export information; if oospores of the disease are detected on arrival, the batch is returned or destroyed, while other pests or soil lead to return, destruction or treatment.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions on the phytosanitary requirements for the export of Nicaraguan tobacco leaves to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Nicaraguan Institute of Agricultural Protection and Health (hereinafter referred to as the Nicaraguan side), as of today, the import of Nicaraguan tobacco leaves meeting the following requirements is permitted.

I. Basis of Quarantine

(1) Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) Law of the People's Republic of China on the Quarantine of Animals and Plants Entering and Exiting the Country and its implementing regulations;

(3) Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Nicaraguan Institute of Agricultural Protection and Health on the Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Nicaraguan Tobacco Leaves to China.

II. Names of Commodities Permitted for Import

Cigar tobacco leaves that have been dried, cured and graded (hereinafter referred to as tobacco leaves), with the scientific name Nicotiana tabacum and the English name Cigar Tobacco Leaves.

III. Permitted Origins

The tobacco-producing regions of Nicaragua.

IV. Approved Tobacco Planting Bases and Enterprises

The planting bases and enterprises for tobacco leaves exported to China shall be reviewed by the Nicaraguan side and jointly approved and registered by the Chinese and Nicaraguan sides. The registration information shall include the name, address and registration number so that goods that do not comply with the relevant provisions of this Announcement can be accurately traced. Before each annual export season, the Nicaraguan side shall provide the Chinese side with the registration list.

V. List of Quarantine Pests of Concern to the Chinese Side

(1) Tobacco blue mold Peronospora hyoscyami f.sp. tabacina

(2) Verticillium wilt of alfalfa Verticillium albo-atrum

(3) Potato zebra chip disease Candidatus Liberibacter solanacearum

(4) Johnsongrass Sorghum halepense

VI. Pre-export Requirements

(1) Field Management.

(1) The planting bases for tobacco leaves exported to China shall establish and implement Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) under the supervision of the Nicaraguan side and carry out Integrated Pest Management (IPM) measures, including pest monitoring, chemical or biological control and agronomic operations. Tobacco leaves exported to China shall comply with the relevant plant quarantine laws and regulations and safety and hygiene standards of China and be free from quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side and soil.

(2) The Nicaraguan side or its authorised personnel shall monitor the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side, implement preventive measures against tobacco blue mold in seedbeds, and investigate and monitor tobacco blue mold (including sporangiospores, mycelia and especially oospores) from transplanting to harvest, so as to ensure that tobacco leaves exported to China are free from the pathogen. If tobacco blue mold is confirmed, effective control measures shall be taken to reduce its impact; if oospores of tobacco blue mold are found, the Nicaraguan side shall immediately notify the Chinese side and suspend the export of tobacco leaves from the affected production area. Before each annual export season, the Nicaraguan side shall provide the Chinese side with the monitoring and investigation report and records of control measures for tobacco blue mold for that year.

(3) Tobacco leaves exported to China shall comply with the relevant provisions and requirements of China, and pesticides banned in China shall not be used. The Nicaraguan side or its authorised personnel shall monitor pesticide use in the tobacco-producing regions and guide tobacco growers to use pesticides rationally and scientifically; all pesticides used shall be officially registered.

(4) Monitoring and control of pests in the planting bases for export shall be implemented under the supervision and guidance of professional and technical personnel of the Nicaraguan side. Such personnel shall receive training from the Nicaraguan side or its authorised training institutions. The planting bases shall keep records of pest monitoring and control and provide them to the Chinese side when needed. Control records shall include the names of chemical agents used during the growing season, active ingredients, dosages and application times.

(2) Processing Management.

The enterprises shall establish a traceability system to ensure that tobacco leaves exported to China can be traced back to the registered planting bases. The records shall include the dates of processing and packaging, the name or registration number of the source planting base, the quantity of tobacco leaves received by the packaging plant, the date and quantity of export, the country of destination, the container number and the seal number. The processing, packaging, storage and shipment of tobacco leaves exported to China shall be carried out under the quarantine supervision of the Nicaraguan side or its authorised personnel.

(3) Packaging Requirements.

Packaging materials shall be clean, hygienic, unused and compliant with the relevant plant quarantine requirements of China. If wood packaging is used, it shall comply with the requirements of International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15).

Tobacco leaves exported to China shall be sealed in dedicated packaging boxes to maintain the integrity of the goods. The following information shall be indicated in English on the boxes: type, grade, origin, harvest year, processing plant, batch number and contract number. Each box and pallet shall be marked in Chinese or English to indicate export to the People's Republic of China, using the English string „Exported to the People's Republic of China“.

(4) Pre-export Inspection and Quarantine.

The Nicaraguan side or its authorised personnel shall sample and inspect tobacco leaves exported to China at a rate of 2% of each consignment. If no plant quarantine problem occurs within two years, the sampling rate may be reduced to 1%. If quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side are found, the whole consignment shall not be exported to China. The Nicaraguan side shall, as appropriate, suspend the qualification of the relevant planting bases or enterprises to export to China for the current export season, identify the cause and take improvement measures. Records of the findings shall be kept and provided to the Chinese side upon request.

Before the tobacco leaves exported to China leave the country, the Nicaraguan side shall carry out tests for pesticide and heavy metal residues. Tobacco leaves with excessive heavy metal or pesticide residues shall not be exported.

(5) Phytosanitary Certificate Requirements.

If the quarantine inspection is passed, the Nicaraguan side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate in accordance with the International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12 (ISPM 12), indicating the registration numbers of the planting base and the packaging plant, and shall state in the additional declaration: „This consignment complies with the requirements specified in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of tobacco leaves from Nicaragua to China, and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China and soil.“ (This consignment complies with the requirements of the Protocol on Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Nicaraguan Tobacco Leaves to China and is free from quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side and soil.)

The Nicaraguan side shall provide the Chinese side with a specimen of the phytosanitary certificate before trade commences, for confirmation and record-filing.

VII. Entry Inspection, Quarantine and Non-compliance Treatment

When the tobacco leaves arrive at the entry port in China, the Chinese customs shall conduct inspection and quarantine in accordance with the following requirements:

(1) Verification of Certificates and Markings.

(1) Verify whether the phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of Article VI, clause (5).

(2) Verify whether the packaging complies with the provisions of Article VI, clause (3).

(3) Verify whether the goods conform to the certificate.

(2) Entry Inspection and Quarantine.

Imported tobacco leaves shall be subject to inspection and quarantine in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations, national standards and the requirements of this Announcement.

(3) Non-compliance Treatment.

(1) If oospores of tobacco blue mold are detected, the batch of tobacco leaves shall be returned or destroyed, and the Chinese side shall immediately suspend imports of tobacco leaves from the relevant production area.

(2) If other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side, quarantine pests newly found in Nicaragua, or soil are detected, the batch shall be returned, destroyed or subjected to treatment.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

19 November 2026
