Products listed in the CCC catalogue must obtain certification and bear the CCC mark before leaving the factory, being sold or imported. The importer may act as the certification applicant, or certification may be commissioned by an overseas producer or seller.

Certification normally involves a type test and a factory inspection; once the application is accepted, a certificate is generally issued within ninety days. The certificate is valid for five years and may be renewed within ninety days before expiry. At entry, customs verifies that the certificate, the mark and the goods match.

Changes and exemptions are where errors most easily arise: an application for amendment is required when key components, the production site or the model changes. Nine categories of use, such as scientific research and testing, repair, commercial display and temporary import, may apply for exemption, but the goods must be used for the declared purpose.

## Regulations on Compulsory Product Certification in China

(Promulgated by Decree No. 117 of the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine (AQSIQ) on 3 July 2009 and amended by Decree No. 61 of the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) on 29 September 2022)

### Chapter I General Provisions

**Article 1**　These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Regulations on Certification and Accreditation and other laws, administrative regulations and relevant State provisions, in order to regulate compulsory product certification work, improve the effectiveness of certification, and safeguard the interests of the State, society and the public.

**Article 2**　To protect national security, prevent fraudulent practices, protect human health or safety, protect the life or health of animals and plants, and protect the environment, the relevant products prescribed by the State must undergo certification (hereinafter referred to as compulsory product certification) and bear the certification mark before they may leave the factory, be sold, be imported, or be used in other business activities.

**Article 3**　The State Administration for Market Regulation (hereinafter referred to as SAMR) takes charge of national compulsory product certification work and is responsible for the organization and implementation, supervision and administration, and overall coordination of compulsory product certification throughout the country.

Market supervision and administration departments at or above the county level are responsible for the supervision and administration of compulsory product certification activities within their respective jurisdictions.

**Article 4**　For products subject to compulsory product certification, the State applies a unified product catalogue (hereinafter referred to as the catalogue), unified mandatory requirements of technical specifications, standards and conformity assessment procedures, a unified certification mark, and unified fee standards.

SAMR, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulates and adjusts the catalogue; the catalogue is issued by SAMR and implemented jointly with the relevant parties.

**Article 5**　The State encourages international mutual recognition activities in compulsory product certification on an equal and mutually beneficial basis; such mutual recognition activities shall be carried out within the framework of international mutual recognition agreements signed externally by SAMR or by the relevant departments authorized by it.

**Article 6**　Institutions and personnel engaged in compulsory product certification activities are obliged to keep confidential the trade secrets and technical secrets and information, such as production technologies and processes, that they become aware of in the course of their work.

### Chapter II Implementation of Certification

**Article 7**　The basic norms and certification rules for compulsory product certification are formulated and issued by SAMR.

**Article 8**　Compulsory product certification shall apply the following single certification mode or a combination of several certification modes, including:

(1) design appraisal;
(2) type test;
(3) sampling and testing or inspection of samples drawn at the production site;
(4) sampling and testing or inspection of samples drawn on the market;
(5) inspection of the enterprise's quality assurance capability and product conformity;
(6) follow-up inspection after certification.

The certification mode for a product shall be determined in accordance with principles of scientific soundness and convenience, taking into account comprehensive factors such as the product's performance, the degree of possible hazard to public safety, human health and the environment, the product's life cycle, and the risk profile of produced and imported products.

**Article 9**　The certification rules shall include the following:

(1) the applicable product scope;
(2) the national standards, industry standards and mandatory requirements of national technical specifications applicable to the products;
(3) the certification mode;
(4) the principles or rules for dividing application units;
(5) sampling and sample submission requirements;
(6) confirmation requirements for key components or raw materials (where required);
(7) testing standard requirements (where required);
(8) factory inspection requirements;
(9) follow-up inspection requirements after certification;
(10) requirements for the validity period of the certification certificate;
(11) requirements for affixing the certification mark to certified products;
(12) other provisions.

**Article 10**　Producers or sellers, and importers, of products listed in the catalogue (hereinafter collectively referred to as certification applicants) shall entrust a certification body designated by SAMR (hereinafter referred to as the certification body) to certify the products they produce, sell or import.

Where a product listed in the catalogue is manufactured by another enterprise under entrustment, either the entrusting enterprise or the entrusted enterprise may entrust the certification body with the certification.

**Article 11**　The certification applicant shall provide the relevant technical materials to the certification body in accordance with the provisions of the specific product certification rules.

Where the seller or importer acts as the certification applicant, it shall also provide the certification body with a copy of the relevant contract concluded between the seller and the producer, or between the importer and the producer.

Where a product listed in the catalogue is manufactured by another enterprise under entrustment, the certification applicant shall also provide the certification body with a copy of the relevant contract concluded between the entrusting enterprise and the entrusted enterprise.

**Article 12**　After accepting the certification entrustment, the certification body shall arrange the product type test and factory inspection in accordance with the provisions of the specific product certification rules.

**Article 13**　The certification applicant shall ensure that the samples it provides are consistent with the products actually produced; the certification body shall examine the authenticity of the samples provided by the certification applicant.

In accordance with the requirements of the certification rules, and based on the product characteristics and actual circumstances, the certification body shall, by means such as sample submission by the certification applicant, on-site sampling, or on-site sealing of samples followed by submission by the certification applicant, entrust a laboratory designated by SAMR (hereinafter referred to as the laboratory) to carry out the product type test on the samples.

**Article 14**　When carrying out the product type test on samples, the laboratory shall ensure that the test conclusions are true and accurate, make complete records of the whole testing process, and keep them on file, ensuring that records of the testing process and results are traceable, and cooperate with the certification body in effective follow-up inspection of certified products.

The laboratory and its relevant personnel shall be responsible for the content of the test reports and the test conclusions they issue; where there is doubt about the authenticity of the samples, they shall explain the circumstances to the certification body and take corresponding action.

**Article 15**　Where a factory inspection is required, the certification body shall dispatch compulsory product certification inspectors with national registration qualifications to inspect the product manufacturing enterprise's quality assurance capability, the conformity of the produced products with the type test samples, and other matters, in accordance with the specific product certification rules.

The certification body and its compulsory product certification inspectors shall be responsible for the inspection conclusions.

**Article 16**　After completing the product type test and factory inspection, the certification body shall, for products meeting the certification requirements, generally issue a certification certificate to the certification applicant within ninety days from the date of accepting the certification entrustment.

For products not meeting the certification requirements, the certification body shall notify the certification applicant in writing and state the reasons.

The certification body and its relevant personnel shall be responsible for the certification conclusions they issue.

**Article 17**　The certification body shall, through on-site product testing or inspection, market sampling and testing or inspection of products, quality assurance capability inspection, and other means, implement classified management and effective follow-up inspection of certified products and their manufacturing enterprises, so as to control and verify the conformity of certified products with the type test samples and the continued conformity of the manufacturing enterprise's quality assurance capability with the certification requirements.

**Article 18**　The certification body shall make complete records of the whole follow-up inspection process and keep them on file, ensuring that the certification process and results are traceable.

Where products cannot continue to meet the certification requirements, the certification body shall, according to the circumstances, suspend or revoke the certification certificate and make it public.

**Article 19**　In accordance with the provisions of the certification rules, the certification body shall, taking into account factors such as the safety level of certified products, the stability of product quality, and the good and poor records of the product manufacturing enterprise, apply classified management to follow-up inspection of certified products and their manufacturing enterprises, and determine a reasonable follow-up inspection frequency.

### Chapter III Certification Certificates and Certification Marks

**Article 20**　SAMR prescribes in a unified manner the format and content of the compulsory product certification certificate (hereinafter referred to as the certification certificate), and the design and categories of the compulsory product certification mark (hereinafter referred to as the certification mark).

**Article 21**　The certification certificate shall include the following basic content:

(1) name and address of the certification applicant;
(2) name and address of the product producer (manufacturer);
(3) name and address of the entrusted manufacturing enterprise (where required);
(4) product name and product series, specifications and models;
(5) the certification basis;
(6) the certification mode (where required);
(7) date of issue and validity period;
(8) the issuing body;
(9) the certificate number;
(10) other content that needs to be indicated.

**Article 22**　The certification certificate is valid for five years.

Based on the follow-up inspection of certified products and their manufacturing enterprises, the certification body shall indicate on the certification certificate the website and telephone number for querying the valid status of annual inspections.

Where continued use is required upon expiry of the certification certificate, the certification applicant shall apply within ninety days before the expiry of the certificate.

**Article 23**　Where the content contained in the certification certificate is indicated on a certified product and its sales packaging, it shall be consistent with the content of the certification certificate and comply with the relevant State provisions on product marking and labelling administration.

**Article 24**　In any of the following circumstances, the certification applicant shall apply to the certification body for amendment of the certification certificate, and the certification body shall take corresponding action according to the circumstances:

(1) where the naming method of a certified product changes, resulting in changes to the product name or model, or where the name or address of the producer or manufacturing enterprise of the certified product changes, the certification certificate shall be amended after verification by the certification body;
(2) where the model of a certified product changes without involving changes to internal structure affecting safety performance or electromagnetic compatibility, or where a certified product reduces models of the same product, the certification certificate shall be amended after confirmation by the certification body;
(3) where key components, specifications and models of a certified product, or the design, structure, process and materials affecting the safety or electromagnetic compatibility of the whole product, or the raw material manufacturing enterprises, change, the certification certificate shall be amended after retesting and passing by the certification body;
(4) where the site of the manufacturing enterprise of a certified product, or its quality assurance system or production conditions, change, the certification certificate shall be amended after a renewed factory inspection passes by the certification body;
(5) other circumstances requiring amendment.

**Article 25**　Where the certification applicant needs to expand the coverage of its certified products, it shall apply to the certification body for extension of the certification certificate. The certification body shall verify the conformity of the extended products with the originally certified products and confirm the validity of the original certification result for the extended products. After confirmation and passing, a separate certification certificate may be issued, or a new certification certificate may be issued, as requested by the certification applicant.

The certification body may, in accordance with the requirements of the certification rules, additionally carry out a product type test or factory inspection to address the differences.

**Article 26**　In any of the following circumstances, the certification body shall cancel the certification certificate and make it public:

(1) the validity period of the certification certificate has expired and the certification applicant has not applied for renewal;
(2) the certified product is no longer produced;
(3) the model of the certified product has been included in the catalogue of products explicitly eliminated or prohibited from production by the State;
(4) the certification applicant applies for cancellation;
(5) other circumstances in which cancellation is required by law.

**Article 27**　In any of the following circumstances, the certification body shall suspend the certification certificate for the period prescribed by the certification rules and make it public:

(1) the certification basis or certification rules applicable to the product have changed, and the product does not meet the changed requirements within the prescribed period;
(2) follow-up inspection finds that the certification applicant has violated the certification rules or other provisions;
(3) the certification applicant refuses, without justified reasons, to accept follow-up inspection, or follow-up inspection finds that the product cannot continue to meet the certification requirements;
(4) the certification applicant applies for suspension;
(5) other circumstances in which suspension is required by law.

**Article 28**　In any of the following circumstances, the certification body shall revoke the certification certificate and make it public:

(1) the certified product has defects that lead to a quality and safety accident;
(2) follow-up inspection finds that the certified product is inconsistent with the samples provided by the certification applicant;
(3) during the suspension of the certification certificate, the certification applicant has not taken corrective measures, or still fails to pass after rectification;
(4) the certification applicant obtained the certification certificate by improper means such as deception or bribery;
(5) other circumstances in which revocation is required by law.

**Article 29**　Where the certification certificate of a certified product is cancelled, suspended or revoked, the certification body shall determine the categories and scope of products that do not meet the certification requirements.

From the date of cancellation or revocation of the certification certificate, or during the period of suspension, products that do not meet the certification requirements shall not continue to leave the factory, be sold, be imported, or be used in other business activities.

**Article 30**　The design of the certification mark consists of a basic pattern and a category label; the basic pattern is as follows:

In the basic pattern, “CCC” is the abbreviation of “China Compulsory Certification”, the English name of China's compulsory product certification.

**Article 31**　The certification category is indicated to the right of the basic pattern of the certification mark, consisting of abbreviation letters of the English word representing the certification category of the product.

Based on the needs of compulsory product certification work, SAMR formulates the specific requirements for the category labels of certification.

**Article 32**　The certification applicant shall establish a management system for the use of the certification mark, truthfully record and archive the use of the certification mark, and correctly use and affix the certification mark on products and their packaging, advertisements, product introductions and other promotional materials in accordance with the provisions of the certification rules.

**Article 33**　No unit or individual may forge, alter, illegally use, buy, sell, or transfer certification certificates and certification marks.

### Chapter IV Supervision and Administration

**Article 34**　SAMR conducts annual supervision inspections and irregular special supervision inspections of the certification and testing activities of certification bodies and laboratories.

**Article 35**　The certification body shall notify SAMR and the provincial market supervision and administration departments of the certification applicants of certified products, the certified products and their manufacturing enterprises, and information on certification certificates that have been cancelled, suspended or revoked.

**Article 36**　SAMR makes unified plans and conducts supervision inspections of certified products at regular or irregular intervals.

Producers, sellers, importers and users in business activities of certified products may not refuse supervision inspections.

SAMR establishes a publicity system for certified products and their producers, making supervision inspection results public to society.

**Article 37**　Market supervision and administration departments at or above the county level are responsible for conducting supervision inspections of compulsory product certification activities within their jurisdictions and investigating and handling violations of law.

Where a product listed in the catalogue has not been certified but has not yet left the factory or been sold, the market supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall admonish the product manufacturing enterprise to undergo compulsory product certification in a timely manner.

**Article 38**　When conducting supervision inspections of compulsory product certification, market supervision and administration departments at or above the county level may lawfully enter production and business premises to conduct on-site inspections, consult and copy relevant contracts, bills, account books and other materials, and seal up or seize products that have not been certified or do not meet the certification requirements.

**Article 39**　Where producers or sellers of products listed in the catalogue discover that the products they produce or sell present safety hazards that may cause harm to human health and life safety, they shall make the relevant information public, take the initiative to adopt remedial measures such as product recall, and report to the relevant supervision and administration departments in accordance with the provisions.

Where producers or sellers of products listed in the catalogue fail to perform the obligations prescribed in the preceding paragraph, SAMR shall initiate the product recall procedure, order the producer to recall the products and the seller to stop selling them.

**Article 40**　Entry-exit inspection and quarantine agencies shall implement import conformity verification management over imported products listed in the catalogue, examine certification certificates, certification marks and other supporting documents, and check whether the goods match the documents. Where verification fails, the matter shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, and follow-up supervision shall be applied to the imported products listed in the catalogue.

**Article 41**　In any of the following circumstances, goods entering the country that are listed in the catalogue need not undergo compulsory product certification at entry:

(1) personal effects of foreign embassies and consulates in China, or of resident bodies of international organizations in China, and their diplomatic personnel;
(2) personal effects of official offices of the Hong Kong and Macao Special Administrative Region governments in the mainland and their staff;
(3) personal effects brought into the country in person by entrants from abroad;
(4) goods donated or granted as aid by foreign governments;
(5) other circumstances in which compulsory product certification is not required by law.

**Article 42**　In any of the following circumstances, the producer, importer, seller or their agent of a product listed in the catalogue may apply to the local market supervision and administration department for exemption from compulsory product certification, submit relevant supporting materials, a liability undertaking, a declaration of product conformity (including the type test report) and other documents, and have the product tested as needed; the product may be imported only after approval and issuance of the Certificate for Exemption from Compulsory Product Certification, and shall be used for the declared purpose:

(1) products needed for scientific research and testing;
(2) components needed for assessing an imported technology production line;
(3) products needed directly for repair purposes for end users;
(4) equipment or components needed to support a factory production line or complete production line (excluding office supplies);
(5) products used solely for commercial display and not for sale;
(6) products temporarily imported and required to be shipped back out of the country (including exhibits);
(7) components imported through ordinary trade for the purpose of exporting all completed machines;
(8) components imported through processing with imported materials or processing with supplied materials for the purpose of exporting all completed machines;
(9) other circumstances exempted from compulsory product certification for special purposes.

**Article 43**　Where a certification body or laboratory commits any of the following acts, SAMR shall order it to suspend business for rectification; during the period of suspension, it may not engage in compulsory product certification or testing activities within its designated scope:

(1) adding, reducing, omitting or changing procedures prescribed in the basic certification norms or certification rules;
(2) failing to conduct effective follow-up investigation of the products it certifies, or, upon discovering that the products it certifies cannot continue to meet the certification requirements, failing to suspend or revoke the certification certificate in a timely manner and make it public;
(3) failing to make complete records of the certification or testing process and keep them on file, in serious circumstances;
(4) engaging certification or testing activities with personnel who have not obtained the corresponding qualifications, in serious circumstances;
(5) failing to effectively examine the authenticity of the samples provided by the certification applicant;
(6) obstructing or interfering with certification law enforcement inspections by supervision departments;
(7) conducting compulsory product certification on products not within the catalogue;
(8) other violations of laws and regulations.

**Article 44**　In any of the following circumstances, SAMR may, upon the request of an interested party or ex officio, revoke the designation of the certification body or laboratory:

(1) the designation decision was made by staff abusing their power or derelict in duty;
(2) the designation decision was made beyond statutory authority;
(3) the designation decision was made in violation of statutory procedures;
(4) a certification body or laboratory not qualified for designation was granted designation;
(5) other circumstances in which the designation decision may be revoked by law.

**Article 45**　Where a certification body or laboratory obtained the designation by improper means such as deception or bribery, SAMR shall revoke the designation and make it public.

The certification body or laboratory may not re-apply for designation within three years from the date of revocation of the designation.

**Article 46**　Personnel engaged in compulsory product certification activities who issue false or inaccurate conclusions, or fabricate false or inaccurate documents or records, shall have their practising qualifications revoked; within five years from the date of revocation, the registration body of the China Certification and Accreditation Association (CCAA) shall not accept their registration application.

**Article 47**　Where the certification applicant disputes the certification decision of the certification body, it may lodge an appeal with the certification body.

**Article 48**　Any unit or individual has the right to report violations of law or regulations in compulsory product certification activities to the market supervision and administration department, which shall investigate and handle the matter in a timely manner and keep the reporter confidential.

### Chapter V Penalties

**Article 49**　Where a product listed in the catalogue leaves the factory, is sold, imported, or used in other business activities without certification, the market supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall impose penalties in accordance with Article 66 of the Regulations on Certification and Accreditation.

**Article 50**　Where a product listed in the catalogue, after certification, is produced or sold by engaging in production and business activities that do not comply with statutory conditions or requirements, or where non-compliant products are produced or sold, the market supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall handle the matter in accordance with Article 2 and paragraph 2 of Article 3 of the Special Provisions of the State Council on Strengthening the Supervision and Administration of Product Safety including Food.

**Article 51**　Where, in violation of paragraph 2 of Article 29 of these Regulations, products that do not meet the certification requirements continue to leave the factory, be sold, be imported, or be used in other business activities during the period in which the certification certificate is cancelled, revoked or suspended, the market supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall impose penalties in accordance with Article 66 of the Regulations on Certification and Accreditation.

**Article 52**　Where, in violation of Article 42 of these Regulations, materials are fabricated to obtain by deception the Certificate for Exemption from Compulsory Product Certification, or, after obtaining the certificate, the product is not used for the originally declared purpose, the market supervision and administration department shall order rectification, revoke the Certificate for Exemption from Compulsory Product Certification, and impose penalties in accordance with Article 66 of the Regulations on Certification and Accreditation.

**Article 53**　Where a certification certificate is forged, altered, leased, lent, illegally used, bought, sold or transferred, the market supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall order rectification and impose a fine of 30,000 yuan.

Where the certification mark is transferred or resold, the market supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall order rectification and impose a fine not exceeding 30,000 yuan.

**Article 54**　In any of the following circumstances, the market supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall order rectification and impose a fine not exceeding 30,000 yuan:

(1) where, in violation of paragraph 1 of Article 13 of these Regulations, the samples provided by the certification applicant are inconsistent with the products actually produced;
(2) where, in violation of Article 24 of these Regulations, the product listed in the catalogue leaves the factory, is sold, imported, or used in other business activities without applying to the certification body for amendment of the certification certificate as required;
(3) where, in violation of Article 25 of these Regulations, the product listed in the catalogue leaves the factory, is sold, imported, or used in other business activities without applying to the certification body for extension of the certification certificate as required.

**Article 55**　In any of the following circumstances, the market supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit; where rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine not exceeding 20,000 yuan shall be imposed:

(1) where, in violation of Article 23 of these Regulations, the content contained in the certification certificate indicated on the certified product and its sales packaging is inconsistent with the content of the certification certificate;
(2) where, in violation of Article 32 of these Regulations, the certification mark is not used as required.

**Article 56**　Where a certification body or laboratory issues false conclusions or its conclusions are seriously inaccurate, SAMR shall revoke its designation; the competent persons directly in charge and the persons directly responsible shall have their corresponding practising qualifications revoked; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law; where losses are caused, corresponding compensation liability shall be borne.

**Article 57**　Where a certification body or laboratory commits any of the following acts, SAMR shall order rectification; in serious circumstances, its designation shall be revoked, up to revocation of the certification body's approval document:

(1) engaging in certification of products listed in the catalogue and certification-related testing activities beyond the designated business scope;
(2) transferring designated certification business;
(3) continuing to engage in compulsory product certification or testing activities within the designated scope during the period of suspension for rectification;
(4) after the period of suspension for rectification expires, still failing to meet the rectification requirements upon inspection.

**Article 58**　Where SAMR, market supervision and administration departments at or above the county level, and their staff abuse power, engage in self-seeking misconduct, or derelict in duty, administrative sanctions shall be imposed in accordance with law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

**Article 59**　Other violations of law in compulsory product certification activities shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

### Chapter VI Supplementary Provisions

**Article 60**　Fees for compulsory product certification shall be charged in accordance with the relevant State provisions.

**Article 61**　These Regulations are interpreted by SAMR.

**Article 62**　These Regulations take effect on 1 September 2009. The Regulations on Compulsory Product Certification promulgated by AQSIQ on 3 December 2001 are repealed simultaneously.
