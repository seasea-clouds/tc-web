Dried cassava from Ethiopia is approved for import into China. GACC Announcement No. 258 of 2025 takes effect on publication. It is cassava (Manihot esculenta Crantz) from across Ethiopia, cut and dried into chips and pellets. Enterprises must be approved by Ethiopia and registered after Chinese review.

Dried cassava must not carry the four quarantine pests Rigidoporus microporus, African cassava mosaic virus, Trogoderma granarium and Prostephanus truncatus, nor live insects, soil or impurities. Ethiopia supervises harvesting, processing and storage and fumigates where necessary.

Packages must be marked in Chinese showing that the product is exported to the People's Republic of China. Before export, a phytosanitary certificate and the prescribed English additional declaration are required. On entry, customs inspects and quarantines the goods; noncompliant loads are returned, destroyed or treated, and serious violations may suspend export qualification.

<!-- original -->

Pursuant to the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia (hereinafter referred to as the Ethiopian side) on the phytosanitary requirements for the export of dried cassava from Ethiopia to China, the import of dried cassava from Ethiopia meeting the following requirements is permitted as of today.

I. Basis for inspection and quarantine

(1) The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(3) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations;

(4) The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Entry and Exit Grains;

(5) The Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia for the export of dried cassava from Ethiopia to China.

II. Names of commodities permitted for entry

For the purposes of this Announcement, dried cassava (Manihot esculenta Crantz) refers to cassava grown in Ethiopia that has been cut and dried into cassava chips and cassava pellets.

III. Permitted places of origin

The whole territory of Ethiopia.

IV. Enterprise registration

Enterprises producing, processing and storing dried cassava for export to China shall be assessed and approved by the Ethiopian side, which shall recommend compliant enterprises to the Chinese side. Registration shall be granted after inspection or review by the Chinese side. The Chinese side will publish the relevant enterprise list on the GACC website and update it dynamically.

V. Quarantine pests of concern to China

Dried cassava for export to China must not carry the following quarantine pests:

(1) White root rot fungus Rigidoporus microporus

(2) African cassava mosaic virus

(3) Khapra beetle Trogoderma granarium

(4) Larger grain borer Prostephanus truncatus

VI. Pre-shipment requirements

(1) Production requirements.

(1) The Ethiopian side shall organize relevant growers, processors and exporters to take integrated control measures to minimize the occurrence of pests in the production of dried cassava.

(2) The Ethiopian side shall supervise the harvesting, processing and storage of dried cassava, and urge enterprises to remove soil, clean impurities, sun-dry and oven-dry dried cassava destined for China, ensuring it is free of soil, plant debris and other extraneous matter. Where necessary, fumigation or other treatment shall be carried out to prevent the presence of quarantine pests of concern to China.

(2) Packaging and transport requirements.

Dried cassava for export to China shall be transported in packaging that complies with China's entry plant quarantine requirements. Each package shall be clearly marked in Chinese with the words „This product is exported to the People's Republic of China“, together with the name and registration number of the production, processing and storage enterprise.

(3) Product requirements.

Dried cassava for export to China shall comply with China's entry plant quarantine requirements, be free of live insects, soil and quarantine pests of concern to China, and shall not be deliberately added to or mixed with other extraneous impurities.

(4) Pre-export inspection and certificate requirements.

Before export, the Ethiopian side shall conduct inspection and quarantine of dried cassava for export to China, issue a phytosanitary certificate for dried cassava that meets the requirements, and state in the additional declaration box: “The consignment complies with the requirements of the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for the export of dried cassava from Ethiopia to China and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China.“ At the same time, the name and registration number of the production, processing and storage enterprise of the consignment, the container number and other information shall be indicated. Where quarantine treatment is carried out before export, the name of the chemical, dosage, temperature and duration shall be stated on the phytosanitary certificate.

VII. Entry inspection and quarantine

(1) Supervision requirements.

(1) Dried cassava for export to China shall enter through a port with designated supervision sites for entry grains and be processed and produced at a designated processing plant. It shall not enter the distribution market directly without processing.

(2) The loading, unloading, transport, storage and processing of dried cassava for export to China shall comply with the relevant Chinese sanitary and phytosanitary requirements.

(2) Document verification.

(1) Verify whether an Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit is attached.

(2) Verify whether the goods come from a registered enterprise.

(3) Verify whether the phytosanitary certificate is authentic and valid.

(3) Consignment inspection.

In accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules, and in conjunction with Articles V and VI of this Announcement, inspection and quarantine shall be carried out on entry dried cassava, which shall be allowed to enter only after passing.

VIII. Handling of non-compliance

(1) Consignments without a valid phytosanitary certificate shall be returned or destroyed.

(2) Consignments from unregistered enterprises shall be returned or destroyed.

(3) If live quarantine pests or other live pests are found, the consignment shall be treated, returned or destroyed.

(4) If soil or genetically modified ingredients not approved by the Chinese authorities are found, the consignment shall be returned or destroyed.

(5) Any other failure to comply with China's entry animal and plant quarantine requirements and relevant safety and health standards shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations.

If any of the above non-compliance is found, the Chinese side shall notify the Ethiopian side and suspend the export qualification of the relevant enterprises depending on the severity of the violation until the Chinese side confirms that the problem has been effectively rectified.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (GACC)

December 30, 2025
