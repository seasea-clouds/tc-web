Entry is allowed, but only green unripe limes. Announcement No. 52 takes effect 27/4/2026: fresh pomelos and limes from Viet Nam may be exported to China; orchards and packing houses must be reviewed by Viet Nam and approved and registered by China. Mature or yellow limes may not be exported.

One pomelo lot end to end: bagging in growth; 1 guava fruit fly trap per hectare, at least 3 under 3 hectares, checked every two weeks, mealybugs and three moth species every 14 days; sorting/washing at packhouse, containers sealed; 2% pre-export sampling, at least 30 fruits cut per lot, down to 1% after one problem-free year; Viet Nam issues ISPM 12 certificate, additional declaration quotes the English original verbatim.

If the registration list or monitoring records are missing, do not ship yet; a broken seal, branches, leaves or soil, or a certificate lacking the English additional declaration will hold the consignment at entry. If live organisms are found, return, destruction or disinfestation, and the orchard or packing house may also be suspended from exports to China.

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment of the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam (hereinafter the Vietnamese side) on the phytosanitary requirements for export of fresh pomelos and limes from Viet Nam to China, import of fresh pomelos and limes from Viet Nam that meet the following relevant requirements is permitted from the date of this Announcement.

I. Quarantine Basis

(i) the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; (iii) the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; (iv) the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported Fruits; (v) the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Pomelos and Limes from Viet Nam to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment of the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam.

II. Names of Commodities Permitted for Entry

(1) Fresh pomelos (hereinafter pomelos), scientific name Citrus maxima, English name Pomelo. (2) Fresh limes (hereinafter limes), scientific name Citrus latifolia, Citrus aurantiifolia, English name Limes. Exported limes shall be green and unripe; mature or yellow limes are not permitted for export to China.

III. Permitted Places of Origin

Pomelo and lime producing areas in Viet Nam.

IV. Approved Orchards and Packing Houses

Orchards and packing houses exporting pomelos and limes to China shall be reviewed by the Vietnamese side and approved and registered by the Chinese side. The registration information includes the name, address and registration number, so as to enable accurate traceability when exported goods fail to comply with the relevant provisions of this Announcement. The Vietnamese side shall provide the registration list to the Chinese side; upon review and approval by the Chinese side, it shall be published on the website of the General Administration of Customs and updated regularly.

V. List of Quarantine Pests of Concern to the Chinese Side

(1) guava fruit fly Bactrocera correcta (2) citrus fruit-boring spotted-wing moth Citripestis sagittiferella (3) grey pineapple mealybug Dysmicoccus neobrevipes (4) South Seas striped mealybug Planococcus lilacinus (5) Oceanic striped mealybug Planococcus minor (6) citrus flower nest moth Prays citri (7) citrus fruit nest moth Prays endocarpa (8) a fungal species of the genus Cylindrocarpon, Cylindrocarpon lichenicola

VI. Pre-export Management

(i) Orchard management.

(1) Orchards exporting pomelos and limes to China shall, under the supervision of the Vietnamese side, establish sound quality management and traceability systems, implement Good Agricultural Practices (GAP), maintain orchard hygiene such as no sources of pollution affecting fruit production in the surroundings and timely removal of fallen and rotten fruits, and implement Integrated Pest Management (IPM), including regular pest monitoring and surveys, physical, chemical or biological control, and control measures such as farming operations.

(2) The Vietnamese side shall, in accordance with the requirements of International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 6 (ISPM 6), draw up a management plan for the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side and organize orchard monitoring throughout the year.

For the guava fruit fly, monitoring with traps shall be carried out from the flowering period to the harvest period. The trap density shall be 1 trap per hectare; orchards with an area of less than 3 hectares must have at least 3 traps installed, checked every two weeks. Once fruit flies are detected, effective control shall be carried out.

For mealybugs, orchards exporting to China shall monitor the South Seas striped mealybug, the Oceanic striped mealybug and the grey pineapple mealybug from flowering to harvest, once every 14 days. The survey shall focus on branches, stems, leaves and fruits for the presence of mealybugs. If pests or their corresponding symptoms are found during monitoring, integrated management measures including chemical or biological control shall be taken immediately to control the occurrence of the pests.

For the citrus fruit-boring spotted-wing moth, the citrus flower nest moth and the citrus fruit nest moth, orchards exporting to China shall conduct orchard monitoring from flowering to harvest, once every 14 days. If pests or their corresponding symptoms are found during monitoring, biological or chemical control measures shall be taken in good time.

For a fungal species of the genus Cylindrocarpon, Cylindrocarpon lichenicola, orchards exporting to China shall conduct orchard monitoring from flowering to harvest, once every 14 days. If corresponding symptoms are found during monitoring, samples shall be sent to a laboratory for testing; if the fungus is found, pomelos and limes from that orchard shall not be exported to China for the remainder of the export season.

(3) Pomelos exported to China shall be bagged during the growing cycle; see the Annex for specific operations.

(4) Pest monitoring and control shall be carried out under the guidance of professional technical personnel, and the Vietnamese side or training institutions authorized by the Vietnamese side shall train such personnel.

(5) The Vietnamese side shall keep records of orchard pest monitoring and control and provide them to the Chinese side upon request. The control records shall include information such as the names of chemical agents used during the growing season, active ingredients, application doses and timing.

(ii) Packing house management.

(1) The processing, packaging, storage and shipping of pomelos and limes exported to China shall be carried out under the quarantine supervision of the Vietnamese side or personnel authorized by the Vietnamese side.

(2) Packing houses exporting to China shall be clean and hygienic, with hardened floors, and shall have a raw material yard and a finished product store.

(3) Functional areas for the storage, processing and handling of pomelos and limes exported to China shall be relatively separate and rationally laid out, and shall be isolated from living areas with an appropriate distance.

(4) During packaging, pomelos and limes exported to China shall undergo manual sorting and washing to remove diseased, insect-damaged, rotten and deformed fruits, branches and leaves or other plant residues and soil; post-harvest fungicide treatment may also be applied and, where necessary, the fruit surface may be wiped so as to effectively remove insects, insect eggs and pathogen spores adhering to the fruit surface.

(5) Packed pomelos and limes, if they need to be stored, shall be placed in storage immediately and stored separately to avoid re-infestation by pests.

(6) Containers loaded with pomelos and limes exported to China shall be checked at the time of loading to ensure good hygienic conditions. The containers shall be sealed, and the seal shall be intact upon arrival at the port of entry in China.

(7) Registered packing houses shall establish a traceability system to ensure that pomelos and limes exported to China can be traced back to registered orchards, recording information such as the processing and packaging date, the name of the source orchard or its registration number, quantity, export date, export quantity, country of destination and container number.

(iii) Packaging requirements.

(1) Packaging materials shall be clean, hygienic and unused and shall comply with the relevant phytosanitary requirements of China. If wooden packaging is used, it shall comply with the requirements of International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15).

(2) Each package shall be marked in Chinese or English with information such as the fruit name, exporting country, place of origin (province, city), orchard name or its registration number, and packing house name or its registration number. Each package and pallet shall be marked in Chinese or English with “For export to the People's Republic of China” or “Exported to the People's Republic of China”.

(iv) Pre-export inspection and quarantine.

(1) The Vietnamese side shall carry out sampling inspection of each consignment of pomelos and limes exported to China at a rate of 2% per consignment. If no quarantine problems are found within one year, the sampling rate shall be reduced to 1%. All damaged or suspected damaged fruits shall be cut open for inspection, with no fewer than 30 fruits cut per consignment, to check for infestation by fruit-boring pests.

(2) If quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side, branches and leaves or soil are found, the entire consignment shall not be exported to China, and exports of pomelos and limes from the orchard or packing house concerned may be suspended for the current export season as appropriate. The Vietnamese side shall identify the causes and take improvement measures. At the same time, records of the findings shall be kept and provided to the Chinese side upon request.

(v) Phytosanitary certificate requirements.

(1) For consignments that pass quarantine, the Vietnamese side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate in accordance with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12 (ISPM 12), indicating the names or registration numbers of the orchard and packing house, and shall state in the additional declaration: “This consignment complies with the requirements specified in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Pomelos and Limes from Viet Nam to China, and is free from any quarantine pests of concern to China.” (This consignment complies with the provisions of the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Pomelos and Limes from Viet Nam to China and is free from the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side.)

(2) The Vietnamese side shall provide a sample phytosanitary certificate to the Chinese side before trade begins, for record and verification by the Chinese side.

VII. Entry Inspection, Quarantine and Treatment of Non-compliance

When pomelos and limes exported to China arrive at a port of entry in China, China Customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine in accordance with the following requirements.

(i) Verification of relevant certificates and markings.

(1) Verify whether imported pomelos and limes have obtained the Import Quarantine Permit for Animals and Plants. (2) Verify whether the phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of item (v) of Chapter VI of this Announcement. (3) Verify whether the markings on the packages and pallets comply with the provisions of item (iii) of Chapter VI of this Announcement.

(ii) Entry inspection and quarantine.

(1) Pomelos and limes exported to China shall enter through ports permitted by the Chinese side for the import of fruits. (2) When pomelos and limes arrive at a port of entry in China, China Customs shall check the relevant documents and markings and carry out inspection and quarantine in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations, national standards and the requirements of this Announcement.

(iii) Treatment of non-compliance.

(1) If the consignment is found to come from an orchard or packing house not recognized by the Chinese side, the consignment shall not be admitted. (2) If live quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side or other quarantine pests newly occurring in Viet Nam are found, or if soil, plant residues and the like are found, the consignment shall be returned, destroyed or disinfested. (3) If the consignment is found not to comply with China's national laws, regulations and standards on food safety, it shall be returned or destroyed.

It is hereby announced.
