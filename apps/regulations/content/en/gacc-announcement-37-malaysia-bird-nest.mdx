Effective immediately. GACC Announcement No. 37 admits Malaysian raw and edible bird nests: raw nests are the material for edible nests, and edible nests are cleaned of dirt and feathers; processors of both must register with the Chinese side; only products made after registration may be exported to China.

Your edible nest consignment: check the registration number against the Chinese list first; heat the product to a core temperature not lower than 70°C for at least 3.6 seconds; an original veterinary health certificate travels with the lot and edible nests also carry a Malaysian health certificate; packages state name, weight, registration number and production date in Chinese and English.

At the port, documents and labels are checked: raw nest outer packaging must show the destination as the People's Republic of China with the mark “for deep processing at processing premises only, not for the retail market”; on serious or repeated non-compliance the Chinese side may suspend imports. Lot and certificate counts are illustrative; the registration list and accompanying originals govern.

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China, and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security of Malaysia (hereinafter referred to as the Malaysian side) on the inspection, quarantine and hygiene requirements for the export of raw and edible bird nests from Malaysia to China, raw and edible bird nests from Malaysia that meet the relevant requirements are permitted for import with immediate effect.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) Laws and regulations.

The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations; the Special Provisions of the State Council on Strengthening the Supervision and Administration of the Safety of Food and Other Products; as well as the Measures for the Safety Administration of Import and Export Food of the People's Republic of China and the Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Manufacturers of Imported Food of the People's Republic of China, and others.

(ii) Bilateral protocol.

The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security of Malaysia on the Inspection, Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for the Export of Raw and Edible Bird Nests from Malaysia to China.

II. Products Permitted for Import

Raw bird nests permitted for import refer to products formed from the saliva of swiftlets and similar swift species that have undergone primary processing effectively removing faeces, soil and ordinary impurities, free from mould, with no substances added; they are the raw material for producing edible bird nests.

Edible bird nests permitted for import refer to bird nests formed from the saliva of swiftlets and similar swift species, effectively cleaned of dirt and feathers, and fit for human consumption.

III. Requirements for Manufacturers

The production and processing of raw and edible bird nests exported to China shall comply with the relevant laws and regulations of China and Malaysia on inspection and quarantine and on food safety and hygiene. Raw and edible bird nests exported to China shall undergo effective heat treatment that inactivates avian influenza and Newcastle disease viruses, namely “heating to a core temperature not lower than 70°C for at least 3.6 seconds”.

Primary processing enterprises for raw bird nests and processing enterprises for edible bird nests exported to China shall register with the Chinese side under the Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Manufacturers of Imported Food of the People's Republic of China; only products manufactured after the date of registration may be exported to China, and manufacturers without registration shall not export to China. These enterprises shall possess production and business equipment or facilities commensurate with the varieties and quantities of the products they handle; maintain a rational equipment layout and process flow to avoid cross-contamination; and have effective epidemic-prevention and product safety and hygiene management systems with the corresponding facilities and equipment.

IV. Inspection and Quarantine Requirements

(i) The Malaysian side shall establish swiftlet house (cave) epidemic prevention and hygiene management systems; hygiene control operating procedures for nest harvesting and transport; and annual animal disease surveillance and toxic and harmful substance monitoring plans related to the quarantine, hygiene and food safety of raw and edible bird nests for export, where the annual disease surveillance plan shall include surveillance for avian influenza and Newcastle disease in swiftlets of a reasonable number of swiftlet houses (caves) filed with the Chinese side.

(ii) The Malaysian side shall ensure that primary processing enterprises for raw bird nests and processing enterprises for edible bird nests exported to China establish a nest traceability system from the swiftlet house (cave) to export, so that affected products can be traced and recalled when problems occur, back to the registered swiftlet houses (caves).

(iii) The Malaysian side shall ensure that raw and edible bird nests exported to China are produced and processed under an effective food safety control system, undergo effective heat processing, are free from contamination by any pathogen that may harm poultry or human health, and comply with the laws, regulations and requirements of Malaysia and China. Effective hygiene treatment shall be applied. Outer packaging and transport containers shall be disinfected.

V. Product Certificate Requirements

Each lot of raw and edible bird nests exported to China shall be accompanied by one original veterinary health certificate (edible bird nests shall additionally be accompanied by a health certificate issued by the Malaysian Ministry of Health). The veterinary health certificate and the health certificate shall be written in Chinese and English, and their format and content must be approved in advance by both sides.

(i) The veterinary health certificate issued by the Malaysian side shall state:

(1) the swiftlet house (cave) registration number, the registration number of the primary processing enterprise (processing enterprise), and the source of the raw material;

(2) that highly pathogenic avian influenza and Newcastle disease surveillance has been carried out in the region where the swiftlet house (cave) is located under the relevant rules of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH), with no abnormalities found;

(3) that necessary precautions have been taken to prevent contact between the product and all sources of avian influenza and Newcastle disease viruses;

(4) that the product has undergone effective heat processing, with a processing requirement not lower than “a core temperature not lower than 70°C for at least 3.6 seconds”;

(5) that the product complies with Chinese laws, regulations and relevant standards and requirements;

(6) that it is fit for use in processed food or for human consumption.

(ii) The health certificate issued by the Malaysian side shall state:

(1) the registration number of the processing enterprise;

(2) that the edible bird nests were harvested, processed, stored and transported under hygienic conditions, under the supervision of the Malaysian official competent authority;

(3) that they comply with Chinese laws, regulations and relevant standards and requirements;

(4) that they are fit for human consumption.

VI. Product Packaging and Labelling Requirements

Raw and edible bird nests exported to China must be packed in new materials complying with China's domestic hygiene standards, and the inner and outer packaging shall be sealed.

The outer packaging of raw bird nests exported to China shall clearly state in Chinese and English the product name, weight, swiftlet house (cave) registration number, the name, address and registration number of the primary processing enterprise, the storage conditions, production date and shelf life, and indicate the destination as the People's Republic of China; it shall bear the mark “for deep processing at processing premises only, not for the retail market”.

The inner and outer packaging of edible bird nests exported to China shall state in Chinese and English the product name, weight, swiftlet house (cave) registration number, the name, address and registration number of the processing enterprise, the storage conditions, production date, shelf life and other relevant information, and the labelling of product information shall comply with Chinese laws, regulations and relevant standards and requirements.

VII. Other Requirements

China Customs shall apply inspection and quarantine to raw and edible bird nests exported to China.

Non-compliant products shall be disposed of in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations. For enterprises where serious problems occur or non-compliance occurs repeatedly, the Chinese side may take measures such as intensified inspection and quarantine or suspension of imports.

This Announcement takes effect on the date of issuance; the former AQSIQ Announcement No. 180 of 2013 and GACC Announcement No. 107 of 2018 are repealed concurrently.

It is hereby announced.
