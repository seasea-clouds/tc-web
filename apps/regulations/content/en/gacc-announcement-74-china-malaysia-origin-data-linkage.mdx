Announcement No. 74 of 2026 launches the China-Malaysia origin electronic data exchange system on June 1.

Paperless filers claiming preferential rates need not enter certificate data or upload documents; a transition period applies where data cannot be found.

The transition ends November 30; from December 1 a security is required for early release, refunded once data transmission is confirmed.

<!-- original -->

To further facilitate compliant customs clearance of goods under free trade agreements, the China-Malaysia Origin Electronic Data Exchange System officially goes live on 1 June 2026, transmitting in real time the data of certificates of origin and back-to-back certificates of origin under the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (RCEP) and certificates of origin and movement certificates under the Framework Agreement on Comprehensive Economic Cooperation between China and the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (the China-ASEAN Framework Agreement), collectively referred to as origin documents. The following is hereby announced.

(1) Where an importer or its agent (hereinafter the importer) applies for agreement tariff rates under RCEP or the China-ASEAN Framework Agreement on the basis of an origin document issued by Malaysia, and chooses paperless clearance under GACC Announcement No. 34 of 2021, it need not enter origin document data or the direct consignment undertaking through the preferential trade agreement origin element declaration system, nor upload the origin document electronically.

Where paperless clearance is chosen and the system prompts that origin document data cannot be found, from 1 June to 30 November 2026 the importer may, under GACC Announcement No. 34 of 2021, enter origin document data and the direct consignment undertaking through the preferential trade agreement origin element declaration system and upload the origin document electronically.

From 1 December 2026, where the system prompts that origin document data cannot be found and the importer requests early release of the goods, it shall apply for a customs security in accordance with the rules; after the China Customs origin service platform confirms through the online certificate status query function that the origin document data have been transmitted, the security shall be refunded in accordance with the rules.

(2) An importer choosing paper-based declaration shall submit the paper origin document at the time of import declaration.

This Announcement shall take effect on 1 June 2026.

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

29 May 2026
