GACC Announcement No. 135 of 2026 takes effect on 16 September 2026: RCEP and China-ASEAN preferential certificates of origin for Myanmar and Cambodia, and China-Cambodia FTA certificates for Cambodia, can now be self-printed; all other matters follow Announcement No. 77 of 2019.

Your consignment goes to Cambodia: print the China-ASEAN certificate of origin yourself on the International Trade Single Window, with no visit to a customs counter, and use the printed certificate for clearance in Cambodia; RCEP certificates for Myanmar are printed online through the same entry point.

It was issued on 7 September 2026 but takes effect on 16 September 2026, so the two dates differ. Myanmar is covered by the first two agreements only, Cambodia by all three; pick the certificate for the destination country and do not mix them up.

<!-- original -->

To further improve the port business environment and facilitate cross-border trade, GACC decides that, from 16 September 2026, preferential certificates of origin for Myanmar and Cambodia under the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP) and the Framework Agreement on Comprehensive Economic Cooperation between the People's Republic of China and the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, and those for Cambodia under the Free Trade Agreement between the Government of the People's Republic of China and the Government of the Kingdom of Cambodia, are added to the scope of self-printable certificates.

All other matters follow GACC Announcement No. 77 of 2019 (Announcement on the Comprehensive Promotion of Self-Printing of Certificates of Origin).

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of China

7 September 2026
