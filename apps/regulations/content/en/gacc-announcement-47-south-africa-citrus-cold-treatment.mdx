The cold treatment indicators for South African fresh citrus (except lemons) entering China have been adjusted, so you can follow the new indicators. Announcement No. 47 of 2026 of the General Administration of Customs specifies that it takes effect immediately and that either of the two conditions suffices for release.

For a given consignment: fruit pulp temperature reduced to -0.2 °C or below and maintained continuously for 16 days or more counts as compliant; or fruit pulp temperature of 1.0 °C or below maintained continuously for 19 days or more also counts as compliant. You may choose either one; both do not need to be met.

The other requirements are unchanged: the other conditions for South African fresh citrus entering China still follow Announcement No. 66 of 2021 of the General Administration of Customs. You only need to replace the cold treatment step with the new indicators; everything else stays the same.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions on supplementary phytosanitary requirements for the entry of South African citrus into China agreed upon by the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Republic of South Africa, the cold treatment indicators for South African fresh citrus (except lemons) entering China are adjusted as of today. The relevant requirements are hereby announced as follows:

The cold treatment indicators shall meet any one of the following conditions:

(1) -0.2 °C or below (fruit pulp temperature), treated continuously for 16 days or more.

(2) 1.0 °C or below (fruit pulp temperature), treated continuously for 19 days or more.

Other requirements for South African fresh citrus entering China shall be implemented in accordance with Announcement No. 66 of 2021 of the General Administration of Customs.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

22 April 2026
