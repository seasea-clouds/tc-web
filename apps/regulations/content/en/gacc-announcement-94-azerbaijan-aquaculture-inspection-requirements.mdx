Entry is open. On 3 July 2026, GACC Announcement No. 94 cleared Azerbaijani farmed aquatic products: farmed aquatic animal products and algae for human consumption are admitted, excluding amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, live animals and breeding stock.

Before shipment, check the producer is approved and recommended by Azerbaijan and registered with China, with no export without registration; raw material from Azerbaijani waters or China-approved sources; no drugs prohibited by China; and each consignment carries a health certificate confirmed by both sides, printed in Chinese, Azerbaijani and English, English compulsory.

At the port, customs inspects under the Protocol. Goods mismatching the certificate, or with prohibited drugs or disease detected, face destruction, return or other treatment; serious or repeated non-compliance may suspend imports. Certificate specimen or seal changes must be filed with China one month ahead.

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (GACC), hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side, and the Food Safety Agency of the Republic of Azerbaijan, hereinafter referred to as the Azerbaijani side, on the inspection and quarantine requirements for Azerbaijani farmed aquatic products exported to China, imports of Azerbaijani farmed aquatic products meeting the following requirements are permitted from the date of this Announcement.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations.

(ii) The Administrative Measures on Food Safety of Imports and Exports and the Provisions on the Administration of Registration of Overseas Food Production Enterprises for Imported Food of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China.

(iii) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (GACC) and the Food Safety Agency of the Republic of Azerbaijan on the Inspection and Quarantine Requirements for Farmed Aquatic Products Exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Scope of Products Permitted for Import

For the purposes of this Announcement, farmed aquatic products means artificially farmed aquatic animal products and their products, and algae and other marine plant products and their products, intended for human consumption, excluding amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, live aquatic animals and aquatic animal and plant breeding material.

The list of Azerbaijani farmed aquatic products permitted for export to China is set out in the Annex.

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

Production enterprises exporting farmed aquatic products to China (including production, processing and storage enterprises) shall be officially approved by Azerbaijan and under its effective supervision. The food safety and hygiene management and protection systems of such enterprises shall comply with China's relevant laws and regulations on food safety.

Pursuant to the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, production enterprises exporting farmed aquatic products to China shall be recommended by the Azerbaijani side to the Chinese side for registration. Without registration, they may not export to China.

IV. Requirements for Imported Products

The Azerbaijani side shall ensure that farmed aquatic products exported to China meet the following conditions:

(i) Farmed in the waters of Azerbaijan, or originating from a country or region from which China permits imports.

(ii) Neither the raw material nor the products fall under any of the following circumstances:

(1) Farmed aquatic products from Azerbaijan already exported or intended for export to China violate China's relevant laws and regulations on food safety, national standards or the provisions of the Protocol, seriously affecting the safety of farmed aquatic products exported to China.

(2) An aquatic animal disease related to the products covered by the Protocol that is listed in the List of Quarantine Diseases for Imported Animals of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the List) or is notifiable under the requirements of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) has occurred in Azerbaijan, seriously affecting the safety of farmed aquatic products exported to China.

(3) A major food safety incident has occurred in Azerbaijan, or a major public health incident has occurred in a production enterprise, or a farming area has been affected by pollutants, seriously affecting the safety of farmed aquatic products exported to China.

(iii) No drugs or additives prohibited by China have been used, directly or indirectly, and drugs or additives restricted or permitted by China have been used in accordance with the relevant provisions.

(iv) Upon inspection and quarantine by the competent authority, no pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or harmful substances or foreign matter listed in China's laws and regulations have been found, and no aquatic animal diseases listed in the List or notifiable under WOAH requirements have been found.

(v) The entire production process (including farming, processing, packaging, storage, transport, transshipment and export) complies with China's relevant safety and hygiene requirements and traceability requirements.

(vi) The packaging and labelling of the products comply with China's national food safety standards and the relevant provisions on the administration of imported food safety.

V. Certificate Requirements

The Azerbaijani side shall conduct inspection and quarantine of farmed aquatic products exported to China in accordance with the requirements of the Chinese side and issue a health certificate confirmed by both sides for each consignment, certifying that the consignment complies with China's relevant laws and regulations on food safety and national standards. The certificate shall be printed in at least Chinese, Azerbaijani and English, and English shall be a compulsory language when the certificate is completed.

The health certificate shall be completed in full with the information of the production enterprise of the farmed aquatic products exported to China. The Azerbaijani side shall promptly provide the Chinese side with certificate specimens and official seals for record-filing. In the event of any change, the Azerbaijani side shall file the changed specimens and seals with the Chinese side at least one month before they take effect.

VI. Other Requirements

Chinese customs shall conduct inspection and quarantine of farmed aquatic products exported to China in accordance with China's relevant laws and regulations on food safety, national standards and the provisions of the Protocol.

Non-compliant products shall be subject to destruction, return or other treatment. In the case of production enterprises with serious problems or repeated non-compliance, the Chinese side may take measures such as intensified inspection and quarantine or suspension of imports.
