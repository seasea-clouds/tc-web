Announcement No. 119 [2026] of the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Ecology and Environment takes effect on the date of publication, 12 August 2026: in view of the actual work situation, the two authorities repeal their Announcement No. 79 of 2018 on the ports designated for solid waste import [No. 79 of 2018], and the designated-ports regime it set no longer applies.

If you handle import formalities for solid waste: the formalities no longer cite the designated ports announced by [No. 79 of 2018]; such imports continue to be handled under the current rules on the import of solid waste and on inspection and quarantine, and the announcement is not an endorsement of any irregular import.

Signed on 12 August 2026; the publication date and the effective date are the same day; only [No. 79 of 2018] is repealed and the other current rules remain unaffected; the signatories are the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Ecology and Environment.

<!-- original -->

In view of the actual work situation, the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Ecology and Environment decide to repeal the Announcement No. 79 of 2018 of the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Ecology and Environment. This announcement takes effect on the date of publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs, Ministry of Ecology and Environment

12 August 2026
