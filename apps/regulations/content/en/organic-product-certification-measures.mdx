The Administrative Measures for Organic Product Certification took effect on April 1, 2014 and were second amended by SAMR in 2022, covering domestic certification and the production, processing, import and sale of certified products.

Chapter III sets out importer access: competent authorities may apply for equivalence assessment and sign memoranda; where no memorandum is signed, exports must meet Chinese laws and national standards, and import certification applicants must engage approved bodies and submit Chinese-language versions of materials.

Article 15 provides that processed products with organic ingredient content of 95 percent may be labelled only after certification; Articles 22 and 23 require certificates, sales certificates, certification marks and product labels at entry and verification of goods against documents, otherwise entry is refused.

(Promulgated by Order No. 155 of the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on November 15, 2013; first amended by Order No. 166 of the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on August 25, 2015; second amended by Order No. 61 of the State Administration for Market Regulation on September 29, 2022)

Chapter I. General Provisions

Article 1. These Measures are formulated to safeguard the lawful rights and interests of consumers, producers and sellers, further improve the quality of organic products, strengthen the administration of organic product certification, and promote ecological environmental protection and sustainable development, in accordance with the Product Quality Law of China, the Law of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection, the Regulations of China on Certification and Accreditation, and other laws and administrative regulations.

Article 2. These Measures shall be observed in engaging in organic product certification and in the production, processing, import and sale of certified organic products within the territory of China.

Article 3. For the purposes of these Measures, “organic product” means a product intended for human consumption or animal feed whose production, processing and sale comply with Chinese national standards for organic products. “Organic product certification” means a conformity assessment activity in which a certification body, in accordance with these Measures and the organic product certification rules, assesses whether the production, processing and sale activities of the relevant products comply with Chinese national standards for organic products.

Article 4. The State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) shall be responsible for the unified administration, supervision and overall coordination of organic product certification nationwide. Local market regulation departments shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of organic product certification activities within their respective jurisdictions.

Article 5. The State shall promote a unified organic product certification system, implementing a unified certification catalogue, unified standards and certification implementation rules, and a unified certification mark. SAMR shall be responsible for formulating and adjusting the organic product certification catalogue and certification implementation rules, and shall publish them.

Article 6. SAMR shall organize international cooperation on organic product certification on the basis of equality and mutual benefit. Mutual recognition activities in organic product certification shall be conducted within the framework of international cooperation agreements signed by China.

Chapter II. Certification Implementation

Article 7. An organic product certification body (hereinafter referred to as a “certification body”) shall obtain legal person status in accordance with the law and shall engage in organic product certification activities within the approved scope only after approval by SAMR. The capability of a certification body to conduct certification activities shall meet the requirements of national standards for product certification bodies. Inspectors engaged in organic product certification inspection activities shall be registered by the national certification personnel registration body before conducting such inspection activities.

Article 8. Producers and processors of organic products (hereinafter collectively referred to as “certification applicants”) may voluntarily engage a certification body to conduct organic product certification and shall submit the application materials prescribed in the organic product certification implementation rules. A certification body shall not accept applications from certification applicants whose production site environment does not meet national requirements for organic product production areas, or whose products are outside the organic product certification catalogue.

Article 9. A certification body shall, within 10 days from the date of receipt of a certification applicant's application materials, complete the review of the materials and decide whether to accept the application. If an application is not accepted, the certification applicant shall be notified in writing with reasons given. A certification body shall, within 5 days before conducting an on-site inspection of a certification applicant, submit basic information such as the certification applicant and the certification inspection plan to the information system designated by SAMR.

Article 10. After accepting a certification application, a certification body shall, in accordance with the organic product certification implementation rules, have certification inspectors conduct on-site inspections of the production and processing sites of the organic products, and shall commission an inspection and testing institution with statutory qualifications to inspect and test the products applied for certification. Where, in accordance with the organic product certification implementation rules, environmental monitoring (inspection) of the production area (base) is required, a monitoring (inspection) report shall be issued by a monitoring (inspection) institution with statutory qualifications, or other lawful and valid environmental monitoring (inspection) conclusions provided by the certification applicant may be accepted.

Article 11. Where the requirements for organic product certification are met, the certification body shall promptly issue an organic product certification certificate to the certification applicant and permit the use of the Chinese Organic Product certification mark; where the requirements are not met, it shall notify the certification applicant in writing with reasons given. Certification bodies and certification personnel shall be responsible for the certification conclusions they make.

Article 12. A certification body shall ensure the integrity, objectivity and authenticity of the certification process, shall keep complete records of the certification process and archive them for retention, and shall ensure that the certification process and results are traceable. Product inspection and testing institutions and environmental monitoring (inspection) institutions shall ensure the authenticity and accuracy of their inspection, testing and monitoring conclusions, and shall keep complete records of the inspection, testing and monitoring processes and archive them for retention. Product inspection and testing institutions, environmental monitoring institutions and their relevant personnel shall be responsible for the content and conclusions of the inspection, testing and monitoring reports they issue. The retention period for records prescribed in this Article shall be 5 years.

Article 13. A certification body shall, in accordance with the certification implementation rules, carry out effective follow-up inspections of certified products and their production and processing processes, so as to ensure that the certification conclusions continue to meet the certification requirements.

Article 14. A certification body shall promptly issue an organic product sales certificate to the certification applicant, so as to ensure that the categories, scope and quantity of organic products sold by the certification applicant of the certified products are consistent with those recorded in the certification certificate.

Article 15. Processed products whose organic ingredient content (by weight or liquid volume, excluding water and salt, the same below) is equal to or higher than 95 percent may be labelled with the word “organic” and bear the organic product certification mark on the product or its packaging and labels only after obtaining organic product certification.

Article 16. A certification body shall not conduct organic certification for processed products whose organic ingredient content is lower than 95 percent.

Chapter III. Import of Organic Products

Article 17. The competent authority for organic products of a country or region exporting organic products to China may apply to SAMR for an equivalence assessment of its organic product certification system. SAMR shall accept the application and organize relevant experts to assess the application submitted. The assessment may be conducted by means of document review, on-site inspection, etc.

Article 18. Where the organic product certification system of a country or region exporting organic products to China is equivalent to China's organic product certification system, SAMR may sign a relevant memorandum of understanding with the competent authority of that country or region. Organic products exported from that country or region to China shall be administered in accordance with the provisions of the relevant memorandum of understanding.

Article 19. Where a country or region has not signed a relevant memorandum of understanding with SAMR on the equivalence of organic product certification systems, imported products from that country or region intended for export to China as organic products shall comply with China's relevant laws and regulations on organic products and Chinese national standards for organic products.

Article 20. Producers, sellers, importers or agents of imported products that need to obtain Chinese organic product certification (hereinafter collectively referred to as “import organic product certification applicants”) shall submit certification applications to a certification body approved by SAMR.

Article 21. An import organic product certification applicant shall, in accordance with the organic product certification implementation rules, submit relevant application materials and documents to the certification body, among which the application form, questionnaire, processing flow, product formula and inputs used in production and processing, and other certification application materials and documents shall be submitted in Chinese versions at the same time. If the application materials do not meet the requirements, the certification body shall not accept the certification application. A certification body conducting import organic product certification activities shall comply with these Measures and the organic product certification implementation rules, and certification inspection records and inspection reports shall be available in Chinese versions.

Article 22. When declaring imported organic products for entry inspection, the importer shall submit copies of the Chinese organic product certification certificate obtained, the organic product sales certificate, the certification mark and the product label, and other documents.

Article 23. Within 30 days from the date of issuance of an organic product certification certificate to an import organic product certification applicant, the certification body shall submit the following written materials to SAMR: (1) the categories, scope and quantity of the certified products; (2) the name, address and contact information of the import organic product certification applicant; (3) the name, address and contact information of the producer and importer of the certified products; (4) copies of the certification certificate and inspection report (in Chinese and foreign language versions); (5) other materials prescribed by SAMR.

Chapter IV. Certification Certificates and Certification Marks

Article 24. SAMR shall be responsible for formulating the basic format and numbering rules of organic product certification certificates and the design and numbering rules of certification marks.

Article 25. The validity period of a certification certificate shall be 1 year.

Article 26. A certification certificate shall include the following: (1) the name and address of the certification applicant; (2) the name and address of the producer and processor of the certified products and the production area (base); (3) the quantity of the certified products, the area of the production area (base) and the product categories; (4) the certification category; (5) the national standard or technical specification relied upon; (6) the name of the certification body and the signature of its person in charge, the date of issuance and the validity period.

Article 27. Where, during the validity period of the certification certificate, any of the following circumstances occurs, the certification applicant shall apply to the certification body for a change within 15 days. The certification body shall effect the change to the certification certificate within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application for change: (1) a change occurs in the name or legal person status of the certification applicant or of the organic product production or processing unit; (2) the product categories or quantity are reduced; (3) other circumstances requiring a change to the certification certificate.

Article 28. Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the certification body shall cancel the certification certificate within 30 days and publish it: (1) the validity period of the certification certificate has expired and no application for renewal has been made; (2) the certified products are no longer produced; (3) the certification applicant of the certified products applies for cancellation; (4) other circumstances requiring cancellation of the certification certificate.

Article 29. Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the certification body shall suspend the certification certificate within 15 days and publish it; the suspension period shall be 1 to 3 months: (1) the certification certificate or certification mark is not used in accordance with the provisions; (2) the production, processing, sale or other activities of the certified products, or the management system, do not meet the certification requirements, and the certification body assesses that effective corrections or corrective measures can be taken within the suspension period; (3) other circumstances requiring suspension of the certification certificate.

Article 30. Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the certification body shall revoke the certification certificate within 7 days and publish it: (1) the quality of the certified products fails to meet the mandatory requirements of relevant national laws, regulations and standards, or substances prohibited by national organic product standards are detected; (2) substances prohibited by national organic product standards are used in the production or processing of the certified products, or the products are contaminated by prohibited substances; (3) the certification applicant of the certified products falsely reports or conceals information required for certification; (4) the certification applicant of the certified products uses the certification mark beyond the approved scope; (5) the environmental quality of the production area (base) of the certified products does not meet the certification requirements; (6) the production, processing, sale or other activities of the certified products, or the management system, do not meet the certification requirements, and no effective corrections or corrective measures have been taken during the suspension period of the certification certificate; (7) the certified products have been further processed, repackaged or divided at places other than the production or processing sites specified in the certification certificate; (8) the certification applicant of the certified products fails to take effective measures to handle major complaints from relevant parties where problems are indeed confirmed; (9) the certification applicant of the certified products has been subject to relevant administrative penalties for violations of laws and regulations on agricultural product and food safety in conducting organic product certification activities; (10) the certification applicant of the certified products refuses to accept supervision by market regulation departments or the certification body; (11) other circumstances requiring revocation of the certification certificate.

Article 32. The Chinese Organic Product certification mark shall be used within the product categories, scope and quantity limited by the certification certificate. A certification body shall assign a unique number to each certification mark in accordance with the unified numbering rules of SAMR (hereinafter referred to as the “organic code”), and shall adopt effective anti-counterfeiting and traceability technologies to ensure that each certification mark issued can be traced to its corresponding certification certificate and certified products and their production and processing units.

Article 33. The certification applicant of a certified product shall affix the Chinese Organic Product certification mark, the organic code and the name of the certification body on the certified product or its smallest sales package. The Chinese Organic Product certification mark may be printed on labels, instructions and advertising materials of certified products, and may be enlarged or reduced in proportion, but shall not be distorted or discoloured.

Article 34. Where any of the following circumstances occurs, no unit or individual may mark on a product, its smallest sales package or its label any words or designs containing “organic”, “ORGANIC” or similar wording that may mislead the public into believing that the product is an organic product: (1) the product has not obtained organic product certification; (2) the certified product has been further processed, repackaged or divided at places other than the production or processing sites specified in the certification certificate.

Article 35. During the suspension period of a certification certificate, the certification applicant of the certified product shall suspend the use of the certification certificate and certification mark; after the certification certificate is cancelled or revoked, the certification applicant shall return the certification certificate and unused certification marks to the certification body.

Chapter V. Supervision and Administration

Article 36. SAMR shall organize and carry out supervisory inspections and irregular special supervisory inspections of organic product certification activities.

Article 37. Local market regulation departments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the law, conduct supervisory inspections of organic product certification activities within their jurisdictions and investigate and punish illegal acts in the production, processing and sale of certified organic products.

Article 38. The methods of supervisory inspection by local market regulation departments at or above the county level include: (1) supervisory inspections of whether organic product certification activities comply with these Measures and the organic product certification implementation rules; (2) supervised sampling inspection of certified products; (3) supervisory inspections of units engaged in certification, production, processing, import and sale of certified products; (4) supervisory inspections of organic product certification certificates and certification marks; (5) supervisory inspections of whether organic product certification consulting activities comply with relevant provisions; (6) investigation and handling of reports of organic product certification and certification consulting activities; (7) investigation and punishment of illegal acts in accordance with the law.

Article 39. SAMR shall regularly publish information on developments in organic product certification through the information system. Before issuing a certification certificate, a certification body shall promptly submit relevant organic product certification information to the information system as required and obtain the certification certificate number. Before issuing certification marks, a certification body shall upload information on the certification marks and organic codes to the information system. Local market regulation departments at or above the county level shall, through the information system and on the basis of the certification-related information submitted and uploaded by certification bodies, conduct supervisory inspections of organic product certification activities carried out within their jurisdictions.

Article 40. In the production, processing, packaging, storage, transport and sale of products, the certification applicants of certified products and units and individuals selling organic products shall establish and improve a product quality and safety traceability system and a record filing system for production, processing and sale.

Article 41. Units and individuals selling organic products shall, in purchasing, storing, transporting and selling organic products, comply with the provisions of national organic product standards, ensure that the categories, scope and quantity of organic products sold are consistent with those in the sales certificate, and be able to provide copies of the certification certificate and organic product sales certificate consistent with the originals for inspection by relevant administrative regulatory departments or consumers.

Article 42. Market regulation departments may, on the basis of information such as animal and plant epidemic situations and environmental pollution risk warnings issued by relevant state departments, as well as supervisory inspections, consumer complaints and reports and media reports, promptly issue certification risk warning information on organic product certification regions, certified products and their certification applicants and certification bodies, and take relevant response measures.

Article 43. Where a certification applicant of a certified product provides false information, uses prohibited substances in violation of regulations, uses organic certification marks beyond the approved scope, or a major product quality and safety accident occurs, the certification body shall not accept organic product certification applications from that enterprise and its production base and processing site within 5 years.

Article 44. Where a certification applicant has an objection to a certification conclusion or decision made by a certification body, it may file an appeal with the certification body.

Article 45. Any unit or individual may report illegal acts in organic product certification activities to market regulation departments. Market regulation departments shall promptly investigate and handle the report and keep the informer's identity confidential.

Chapter VI. Penalties

Article 46. Where a person forges, misuses or illegally trades certification marks, the local market regulation department at or above the county level shall impose penalties in accordance with the Product Quality Law of China, the Law of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and their implementing regulations, and other laws and administrative regulations.

Article 47. Where a person forges, alters, misuses, illegally trades, transfers or defaces a certification certificate, the local market regulation department at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of 30000 yuan. Where a certification body, in violation of the second paragraph of Article 39 of these Measures, compiles a certification certificate number by itself on a certification certificate it issues, it shall be deemed to have forged a certification certificate.

Article 48. Where, in violation of Article 34 of these Measures, a person marks on a product or its packaging or label any words or designs containing “organic”, “ORGANIC” or similar wording that may mislead the public into believing that the product is an organic product, the local market regulation department at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of not more than 30000 yuan.

Article 49. Where a certification body commits any of the following acts, SAMR shall order correction, issue a warning and publish it: (1) failing to upload organic product certification marks and organic codes to the information system designated by SAMR in accordance with the third paragraph of Article 39 of these Measures; (2) failing to submit relevant certification information to the information system designated by SAMR in accordance with the second paragraph of Article 9 of these Measures, or submitting false information; (3) failing to submit relevant materials to SAMR for record-filing in accordance with Article 23 of these Measures.

Article 50. Where, in violation of Article 16 of these Measures, a certification body conducts organic certification for processed products whose organic ingredient content is lower than 95 percent, the local market regulation department at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of not more than 30000 yuan.

Article 51. Where a certification body, in violation of Articles 29 and 30 of these Measures, fails to promptly suspend or revoke a certification certificate and publish it, it shall be penalized in accordance with Article 59 of the Regulations of China on Certification and Accreditation.

Article 52. Where a certification body or the certification applicant of a certified product refuses to accept supervisory inspection by SAMR or by a local market regulation department at or above the county level, it shall be ordered to make correction within a time limit; if it fails to make correction within the time limit, a fine of not more than 30000 yuan shall be imposed.

Article 53. Other illegal acts in organic product certification activities shall be penalized in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and departmental rules.

Chapter VII. Supplementary Provisions

Article 54. Fees for organic product certification shall be charged in accordance with relevant state price laws and administrative regulations.

Article 55. Exported organic products shall comply with the requirements of the importing country or region.

Article 56. For the purposes of these Measures, “organic ingredients” means any substance used in and present (including in modified form) in a product in the manufacture or processing of an organic product, including additives.

Article 57. These Measures shall be interpreted by SAMR.

Article 58. These Measures shall take effect on April 1, 2014. The Administrative Measures for Organic Product Certification promulgated by the former AQSIQ on November 5, 2004 (former AQSIQ Order No. 67) shall be abolished at the same time.
