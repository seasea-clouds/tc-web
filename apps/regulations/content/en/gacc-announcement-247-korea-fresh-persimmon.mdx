On December 16, 2025, GACC issued Announcement No. 247: fresh persimmons from Korea that meet the quarantine requirements are allowed for import into China as of the date of issuance. Orchards and packinghouses for persimmons exported to China must be approved and registered by GACC, and are limited to persimmon production areas in Korea.

The announcement sets 7 chapters of rules: persimmons exported to China must be free from 7 quarantine pests of concern to China, such as the San Jose scale; orchards implement IPM with monitoring once every two weeks from flowering to harvest; before export, a 2% sampling rate is applied and suspect fruits are cut open for inspection, and entry requires a phytosanitary certificate bearing the English additional declaration.

The registration list is provided by the Korean side to the Chinese side before each export season and published on the GACC website after review and approval; if a quarantine pest is found, the entire consignment is barred from export; non-compliant consignments are returned, destroyed, or treated according to the circumstances.

In accordance with relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions on the phytosanitary requirements for the export of fresh persimmons from Korea to China agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs of the Republic of Korea (hereinafter referred to as the Korean side), fresh persimmons from Korea that meet the following relevant requirements are allowed for import as of the date of this announcement.

I. Quarantine Basis

(i) Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) Law of the People's Republic of China on the Quarantine of Animals and Plants Entering and Leaving the Territory and its Implementing Regulations;

(iii) Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its Implementing Regulations;

(iv) Measures for the Inspection, Quarantine, Supervision and Administration of Fruits Entering the Territory;

(v) Memorandum of Understanding on Cooperation between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs of the Republic of Korea on the Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Fresh Persimmons from Korea to China.

II. Name of Commodity Permitted for Entry

Fresh persimmons, Diospyros spp. (hereinafter referred to as persimmons).

III. Permitted Place of Origin

Persimmon production areas of Korea.

IV. Approved Orchards and Packinghouses

Persimmon orchards and packinghouses for export to China shall be registered by the Korean side and approved by the Chinese side. The registration information shall include the name, address, and registration number, so as to allow accurate traceability when exported fruits fail to comply with the requirements. The registration list shall be provided by the Korean side to the Chinese side before each export season and published on the GACC website after review and approval by the Chinese side.

V. List of Quarantine Pests of Concern to the Chinese Side

(1) San Jose scale Diaspidiotus perniciosus

(2) Fall webworm Hyphantria cunea

(3) Oystershell scale Lepidosaphes ulmi

(4) Persimmon tube thrips Ponticulothrips diospyrosi

(5) Sooty mold Capnophaeum fuliginodes

(6) Persimmon fruit spot Cercospora kakivora

(7) Persimmon stem blight Macrophoma kaki

VI. Pre-export Management

(i) Orchard management.

(1) All orchards for export to China shall implement Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) or other similar safety systems, maintain orchard hygiene, promptly remove fallen fruits, and conduct post-harvest pruning; they shall implement Integrated Pest Management (IPM), including pest monitoring and surveys, chemical control, biological control, and farming operations.

(2) All orchards for export to China shall carry out pest monitoring and integrated management against the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side under the supervision of the Korean side. Pest monitoring shall be conducted from the flowering stage to harvest, once every two weeks, focusing on surveys of branches, stems, leaves, and fruits for these pests. If any of these pests is found, immediate measures, such as chemical and biological control, shall be taken to control its occurrence.

(3) Phytosanitary measures in all orchards for export to China shall be implemented under the guidance of professional technicians trained by the Korean side or its authorized institutions.

(4) All orchards for export to China shall keep pest monitoring and control records for at least two years and provide them to the Chinese side upon request. Such records shall include detailed information such as the time of monitoring, the names of pests found, the control measures taken, and the names, active ingredients, concentrations, and application dates of the chemicals used.

(ii) Packinghouse management.

(1) The processing, packing, cold storage, and transport of finished products of persimmons exported to China must be carried out under the quarantine supervision of the Korean side or its authorized personnel.

(2) During packing, persimmons for export to China shall be screened and graded, and the fruit surfaces cleaned with high-pressure air or water guns to ensure that the fruits are free from insects, mites, rotten fruits, twigs, leaves, other plant residues, and soil.

(3) Packed persimmons for export to China shall be immediately stored in cold rooms and kept separate from other persimmons that do not meet the quarantine requirements, to prevent secondary pest infestation.

(iii) Packaging requirements.

(1) Packaging materials shall be clean, hygienic, unused, and comply with Chinese phytosanitary and hygiene requirements. If wooden packaging is used, it must comply with International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15).

(2) Each package must be marked with the fruit name, place of origin (province and district), country, orchard or its registration number, packinghouse or its registration number, and other information. Each package and pallet must be labeled in Chinese or English with "Exported to the People's Republic of China".

(iv) Pre-export inspection and quarantine.

(1) Within two years after this announcement takes effect, the Korean side or its authorized personnel shall carry out inspection and quarantine on a 2% sampling basis. If no phytosanitary problems are found within two years, the sampling rate may be reduced to 1%. Suspect fruits found during quarantine shall be cut open for inspection.

(2) If any quarantine pest of concern to the Chinese side is found, the entire consignment of persimmons shall not be exported to China. The Korean side shall investigate the cause, take corrective measures, keep records of the findings, and provide them to the Chinese side upon request.

(v) Phytosanitary certificate requirements.

For persimmons that pass quarantine inspection, the Korean side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate stating the registration numbers of the orchard and the packinghouse, with the following statement in the additional declaration box: "This consignment of persimmons complies with the Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Persimmons from Korea to China, and is free from any quarantine pests of concern to China." (This consignment of persimmons complies with the Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Persimmons from Korea to China and does not carry any quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side.)

VII. Entry Inspection and Quarantine and Handling of Non-compliance

Upon arrival of persimmons exported to China at Chinese ports of entry, Chinese Customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine in accordance with the following requirements.

(i) Verification of relevant certificates and markings.

(1) Verification of whether the imported persimmons have obtained the Permit for the Quarantine of Animals and Plants Entering the Territory.

(2) Verification of whether the phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of Item (v) of Chapter VI of this announcement.

(3) Verification of whether the markings on packages and pallets comply with the provisions of Item (iii) of Chapter VI of this announcement.

(ii) Entry inspection and quarantine.

(1) Persimmons exported to China shall enter through ports approved by the Chinese side for fruit imports.

(2) When persimmons arrive at Chinese ports of entry, Chinese Customs will check the relevant documents and markings, and carry out inspection and quarantine in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations, national standards, and the requirements of this announcement.

(iii) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) If any consignment is found to come from unapproved orchards or packinghouses, it shall not be allowed entry.

(2) If any quarantine pest of concern to the Chinese side, or live quarantine pests not previously recorded in Korea, is found, the consignment shall be returned, destroyed, or treated.

(3) If the consignment is found not to comply with Chinese food safety laws and regulations and national standards, it shall be returned or destroyed.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China

December 16, 2025
