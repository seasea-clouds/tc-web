SAMR Order No. 85 (published in 2023, effective 1 January 2024) governs the registration of foods for special medical purposes, covering both domestically produced and sold products and imported products.

Under Article 7, an overseas manufacturer intending to export to China may be the applicant; Articles 8 to 11 list application materials and review deadlines, while Articles 34 to 36 provide priority review for rare diseases and other cases (30 working days).

Articles 46 and 47 require labels to show the product name, registration number, target population and “please use under the guidance of a physician or clinical nutritionist”, and prohibit claims about disease prevention or treatment; Article 64 repeals the 2016 Order No. 24 measures.

Chapter I. General Provisions

Article 1. These Measures are formulated to regulate the registration of foods for special medical purposes and ensure their quality and safety, in accordance with the “Administrative Licensing Law of China”, the “Food Safety Law of China” (hereinafter the “Food Safety Law”), the “Regulations of China on the Implementation of the Food Safety Law” and other laws and regulations.

Article 2. These Measures apply to the registration administration of foods for special medical purposes produced, sold and imported within China.

Article 3. Registration of foods for special medical purposes means the activity by which the State Administration for Market Regulation examines foods for special medical purposes submitted for registration and decides whether to approve registration, in accordance with the procedures and requirements prescribed in these Measures.

Article 4. The registration administration of foods for special medical purposes shall be guided by clinical nutritional needs, follow the principles of science, openness, fairness and impartiality, and encourage innovation.

Article 5. The State Administration for Market Regulation is responsible for the registration administration of foods for special medical purposes. The food review institution of the State Administration for Market Regulation (Food Review Center, hereinafter the review institution) is responsible for accepting registration applications, technical review, on-site verification, certificate preparation and delivery, and organizes expert demonstrations as needed. Market supervision departments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall cooperate in on-site verification and other work relating to the registration of foods for special medical purposes.

Article 6. An applicant for registration of foods for special medical purposes (hereinafter the applicant) shall be responsible for the authenticity, completeness, legality and traceability of the materials submitted and shall bear legal liability. The applicant shall cooperate with market supervision departments in on-site verification, sample testing and other work relating to registration, and provide necessary working conditions.

Chapter II. Application and Registration

Article 7. The applicant shall be a manufacturer intending to produce and sell foods for special medical purposes within China, or an overseas manufacturer intending to export foods for special medical purposes to China. The applicant shall possess research and development, production and testing capabilities commensurate with the foods for special medical purposes it produces, establish a research and development institution for foods for special medical purposes, set up a production quality management system commensurate with the foods for special medical purposes it produces in accordance with good manufacturing practice requirements, and conduct batch-by-batch inspection of ex-factory products in accordance with the items prescribed by relevant laws and regulations, national food safety standards and technical requirements. The research and development institution shall include personnel with senior professional titles or corresponding professional competence in food-related fields.

Article 8. To apply for registration of foods for special medical purposes, the following materials shall be submitted to the State Administration for Market Regulation: (1) application form for registration of foods for special medical purposes; (2) documents certifying the applicant's status and qualifications; (3) product research and development report; (4) product formula and its design basis; (5) production process materials; (6) product standards and technical requirements; (7) draft product labels and instructions; (8) product test report; (9) materials on research and development, production and testing capabilities; (10) other materials demonstrating product safety, nutritional adequacy and clinical effects for special medical purposes. To apply for registration of specific complete nutritional formulas, a clinical trial report shall generally also be submitted.

Article 9. The applicant shall, in accordance with relevant state provisions, mark the trade secrets, undisclosed information or confidential business information in the application materials and indicate the basis therefor.

Article 10. Registration applications submitted by an applicant shall be handled according to the following circumstances: (1) where the matter does not require registration under the law, the applicant shall be promptly informed that the application will not be accepted; (2) where the matter does not fall within the functions and powers of the State Administration for Market Regulation under the law, a decision not to accept shall be promptly made and the applicant shall be informed to apply to the relevant administrative organ; (3) where there are errors in the application materials that can be corrected on the spot, the applicant shall be allowed to correct them on the spot; (4) where the application materials are incomplete or do not conform to the statutory form, the applicant shall be informed of all the contents to be supplemented and corrected at one time, on the spot or within 5 working days; if no such notice is given within the time limit, the application shall be deemed accepted from the date of receipt of the application materials; (5) where the matter falls within the functions and powers of the State Administration for Market Regulation, the application materials are complete and conform to the statutory form, or the applicant has submitted all supplementary and corrected materials as required, the registration application shall be accepted. For acceptance or non-acceptance of a registration application, a receipt bearing the special administrative licensing seal of the State Administration for Market Regulation and the date shall be issued.

Article 11. The review institution shall examine the product formula, production process, labels and instructions of the product submitted for registration, as well as product safety, nutritional adequacy and clinical effects for special medical purposes, and complete the review within 60 working days from the date of acceptance. Where an extension of the review period is needed under special circumstances, with the consent of the head of the review institution, it may be extended by 30 working days, and the extension decision shall be notified to the applicant in writing.

Article 12. Where, during the review, it is considered necessary for the applicant to supplement and correct materials, the review institution shall notify all the contents to be supplemented and corrected at one time. The applicant shall supplement and correct the materials at one time within 6 months in accordance with the requirements of the notice of supplementation and correction. The time for supplementing and correcting materials shall not be counted in the review period.

Article 13. The review institution may organize experts in nutrition, clinical medicine, food safety, food processing and other fields to demonstrate issues encountered in the review process and form expert opinions.

Article 14. The review institution shall organize on-site production verification and sample testing of the applicant, and on-site verification of clinical trials, based on food safety risks. Where necessary, extended verification may be carried out on manufacturers of food raw materials, food additives, etc.

Article 15. The review institution shall notify the applicant of the verification matters in writing or by electronic means. The applicant shall feedback the date of acceptance of on-site verification within 30 working days. Where feedback cannot be given within the prescribed time limit due to force majeure or other factors, the applicant shall submit a written application for postponement and state the reasons.

Article 16. The review institution shall, within 20 working days from the date of on-site production verification confirmed by the applicant, complete the on-site verification of the applicant's research and development capabilities, production capabilities, testing capabilities and the consistency between the application materials and the actual situation, and issue an on-site production verification report. Where the review institution notifies the market supervision department at the provincial level of the place where the applicant is located to participate in the on-site verification, the provincial market supervision department shall send personnel to participate.

Article 17. The review institution shall draw samples from dynamic production during on-site production verification and entrust a legally qualified food inspection institution to conduct testing. The inspection institution shall complete the sample testing within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the samples in accordance with national food safety standards and technical requirements, and issue a sample test report to the review institution.

Article 18. For on-site verification of clinical trials for an application for registration of specific complete nutritional formulas, the review institution shall, within 30 working days from the date of on-site clinical trial verification confirmed by the applicant, complete the on-site verification of the authenticity, completeness, legality and traceability of the clinical trials, and issue an on-site clinical trial verification report.

Article 19. The review institution shall conduct the review based on the application materials, on-site verification reports, sample test reports and other materials submitted by the applicant, and make a review conclusion.

Article 20. Where the applicant's application meets the statutory conditions and standards, the product is scientific and safe, the production process is reasonable and feasible, product quality is controllable, the technical requirements and testing methods are scientific and reasonable, and the conclusions of the on-site verification report and the sample test report meet the registration requirements, the review institution shall make a review conclusion recommending approval of registration.

Article 21. Under any of the following circumstances, the review institution shall make a review conclusion proposing not to approve registration: (1) the application materials are falsified or untrue; (2) the application materials do not support product safety, nutritional adequacy and clinical effects for special medical purposes; (3) the applicant does not possess research and development, production or testing capabilities commensurate with the product applied for registration; (4) the applicant fails to submit supplementary and corrected materials within the prescribed time limit, or the supplementary and corrected materials submitted do not meet the requirements; (5) the applicant fails to confirm the on-site verification date within the time limit, or refuses or fails to cooperate with on-site verification or sample testing; (6) the conclusion of the on-site verification report or the sample test report does not meet the registration requirements; (7) other circumstances that do not meet registration requirements such as laws, regulations, rules, national food safety standards and technical requirements. Where the review institution makes a review conclusion not to approve registration, it shall issue a notice of proposed non-approval to the applicant and state the reasons. If the applicant objects to the review conclusion, it shall submit a written application for reconsideration to the review institution and state the reasons for reconsideration within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the notice. The content of reconsideration shall be limited to the original application matter and application materials. The review institution shall make a reconsideration decision within 30 working days from the date of acceptance of the reconsideration application and notify the applicant.

Article 22. The time required for on-site verification, sample testing and reconsideration shall not be counted in the review period. The time limits for overseas on-site verification and sample testing shall be determined according to the actual situation.

Article 23. After the review is completed, the State Administration for Market Regulation shall decide whether to approve in accordance with the law. For approved registrations, a registration certificate for foods for special medical purposes shall be issued. For non-approved registrations, a decision of non-approval shall be issued, the reasons shall be stated, and the applicant shall be informed of its right to apply for administrative reconsideration or institute administrative litigation in accordance with the law.

Article 24. The State Administration for Market Regulation shall make a decision within 20 working days from the date of acceptance. The review institution shall, within 10 working days from the date of the decision by the State Administration for Market Regulation, deliver the registration certificate for foods for special medical purposes or the decision of non-approval to the applicant.

Article 25. The registration certificate for foods for special medical purposes and its annexes shall state the following matters: (1) product name; (2) enterprise name and production address; (3) registration number, approval date and validity period; (4) product category; (5) product formula; (6) production process; (7) draft product labels and instructions; (8) other technical requirements of the product. The format of the registration number for foods for special medical purposes is: Guoshi Zhuzi TY + 4-digit year number + 4-digit serial number, where TY denotes foods for special medical purposes. The registration certificate for foods for special medical purposes is valid for 5 years, and the electronic certificate has the same legal effect as the paper certificate.

Article 26. Within the validity period of the registration certificate for foods for special medical purposes, if the applicant needs to change the matters stated in the registration certificate and its annexes, it shall submit an application for change of registration to the State Administration for Market Regulation and submit the following materials: (1) application form for change of registration of foods for special medical purposes; (2) report on the demonstration of the product change; (3) other materials relating to the change.

Article 27. Where an applicant applies for a change in product formula, production process, etc. that may affect product safety, nutritional adequacy or clinical effects for special medical purposes, the review institution shall organize the review in accordance with Article 11 of these Measures and make a review conclusion. Where an applicant applies for a change in enterprise name, production address name, product name, etc. that does not affect product safety, nutritional adequacy or clinical effects for special medical purposes, the review institution shall make a review conclusion within 10 working days from the date of acceptance. Where the applicant's enterprise name is changed, the application shall be made under the changed name.

Article 28. The State Administration for Market Regulation shall make a decision to approve or not approve the change within 10 working days from the date the review conclusion is made. Where the change is approved, a new registration certificate shall be issued to the applicant, indicating the time and matters of change; the date of issuance of the registration certificate shall be subject to the date of approval of the change, the original registration number shall remain unchanged, and the validity period of the certificate shall remain unchanged. Where the change is not approved, a decision of non-approval of the change shall be issued, the reasons shall be stated, and the applicant shall be informed of its right to apply for administrative reconsideration or institute administrative litigation in accordance with the law.

Article 29. Where the types of food raw materials and food additives of the product remain unchanged, the order of the ingredient list remains unchanged and the nutrition facts panel remains unchanged, and the amounts used fluctuate or are adjusted reasonably within a certain range, no application for change is required.

Article 30. Where renewal is required upon expiry of the validity period of the registration certificate for foods for special medical purposes, the applicant shall submit an application for renewal of registration to the State Administration for Market Regulation 6 months before the expiry of the validity period of the registration certificate, and submit the following materials: (1) application form for renewal of registration of foods for special medical purposes; (2) documents certifying the applicant's status and qualifications; (3) information on the enterprise's research and development, production and testing capabilities; (4) self-inspection report on the enterprise's production quality management system; (5) follow-up evaluation of product safety, nutritional adequacy and clinical effects for special medical purposes; (6) written opinion on renewal of registration issued by the market supervision department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the manufacturer is located; (7) other materials relating to renewal of registration.

Article 31. The review institution shall organize the review of the application for renewal of registration in accordance with Article 11 of these Measures and make a review conclusion.

Article 32. The State Administration for Market Regulation shall make a decision to approve or not approve the renewal of registration within 20 working days from the date of acceptance of the application. Where the renewal is approved, a new registration certificate shall be issued to the applicant, the original registration number shall remain unchanged, and the validity period of the certificate shall be recalculated from the date of approval. Where the renewal is not approved, a decision of non-approval of renewal shall be issued, the reasons shall be stated, and the applicant shall be informed of its right to apply for administrative reconsideration or institute administrative litigation in accordance with the law. Where no decision is made within the time limit, the renewal shall be deemed approved.

Article 33. Under any of the following circumstances, renewal of registration shall not be approved: (1) the application for renewal of registration is not submitted within the prescribed time limit; (2) the registered product fails in 3 batches or more in supervisory sampling inspections at or above the provincial level within 12 consecutive months; (3) the applicant fails to maintain the research and development, production and testing capabilities it had at the time of registration; (4) other circumstances that do not comply with relevant provisions.

Article 34. An applicant applying for registration of foods for special medical purposes may apply for application of the priority review and approval procedure under any of the following circumstances: (1) foods for special medical purposes for rare diseases; (2) new types of foods for special medical purposes that are urgently needed clinically and have not been approved before; (3) other circumstances of priority review and approval prescribed by the State Administration for Market Regulation.

Article 35. Before submitting a registration application, the applicant shall communicate with the review institution and, upon confirmation, submit an application for priority review and approval to the review institution at the same time as the registration application. Upon examination, if the circumstances comply with Article 34 of these Measures and there is no objection after public announcement, the review institution shall include the application in the priority review and approval procedure.

Article 36. For foods for special medical purposes included in the priority review and approval procedure, the review period shall be 30 working days; after confirmation through communication, the applicant may supplement and submit technical materials; where on-site verification or sample testing is required, priority shall be given.

Article 37. During the review, if it is found that an application for registration of foods for special medical purposes included in the priority review and approval procedure does not meet the conditions for priority review and approval, the review institution shall terminate the priority review and approval procedure for the product, continue the review under the normal review procedure, and inform the applicant.

Article 38. Matters not prescribed in the procedures for change of registration, renewal of registration and priority review and approval of foods for special medical purposes shall be governed by the relevant provisions on registration of foods for special medical purposes.

Chapter III. Clinical Trials

Article 39. A clinical trial conducted for the registration of specific complete nutritional formulas shall be subject to review and approval by an ethics committee.

Article 40. Clinical trials shall be conducted in accordance with the good clinical practice for clinical trials of foods for special medical purposes. The good clinical practice for clinical trials of foods for special medical purposes shall be issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation.

Article 41. The applicant shall entrust a qualified clinical institution to conduct the clinical trial. The clinical institution entrusted to conduct the clinical trial shall issue a clinical trial report, which shall include a complete statistical analysis report and data.

Article 42. Where the applicant organizes a multi-center clinical trial, the lead institution and the statistical institution shall be specified.

Article 43. The applicant shall be responsible for the quality and safety of the test samples and control samples used in the clinical trial. The test samples used in the clinical trial shall be produced by the applicant in accordance with the product formula and production process submitted for registration, the production conditions shall comply with the good manufacturing practice requirements for foods for special medical purposes, and the products shall comply with the corresponding national food safety standards and technical requirements.

Chapter IV. Labels and Instructions

Article 44. The labels and instructions of foods for special medical purposes shall comply with laws, regulations, rules and national food safety standards, and shall be marked in accordance with the provisions of the State Administration for Market Regulation.

Article 45. The name of a food for special medical purposes shall reflect the true nature of the food and use the category name or equivalent name prescribed by the national food safety standards.

Article 46. The label of a food for special medical purposes shall indicate, on the principal display panel, the product name, registration number, target population and the statement “please use under the guidance of a physician or clinical nutritionist”.

Article 47. The labels and instructions of foods for special medical purposes shall describe the formulation characteristics or nutritional characteristics of the product, and shall indicate, in accordance with the provisions of the national food safety standards, the statements “not suitable for use by non-target populations” and “this product is prohibited for parenteral nutrition support or intravenous injection”.

Article 48. The applicant shall be responsible for the content of the labels and instructions of foods for special medical purposes provided by it. The labels and instructions shall be truthful, accurate, clear and conspicuous; they shall not contain false content, shall not involve the prevention or treatment of diseases, shall not make functional claims about the nutrients and other ingredients in the product, and shall not mislead consumers.

Article 49. The contents of the labels and instructions of foods for special medical purposes shall be consistent; where they involve matters stated in the registration certificate for foods for special medical purposes, they shall be consistent with the contents of the registration certificate. Where the label already covers all the contents of the instructions, no separate instructions need be attached.

Chapter V. Supervision and Administration

Article 50. The institutions and personnel undertaking technical review, on-site verification and sample testing shall be responsible for the review conclusions, on-site verification reports, sample test reports, etc. issued by them; experts participating in demonstrations shall abide by professional ethics when issuing expert opinions. Technical review, on-site verification, sample testing and expert demonstration shall be conducted in accordance with laws, regulations, rules, national food safety standards, technical specifications, etc., so as to ensure that the relevant work is scientific, objective and impartial.

Article 51. Market supervision departments receiving reports from relevant units or individuals about violations of laws or regulations in the registration of foods for special medical purposes shall promptly verify and handle them.

Article 52. Without the consent of the applicant, institutions and personnel participating in the registration of foods for special medical purposes shall not disclose the trade secrets, undisclosed information or confidential business information submitted by the applicant, except where otherwise provided by law or where national security or major social public interests are involved.

Article 53. After an application for registration of foods for special medical purposes has been accepted, if the applicant requests to withdraw the application, it shall submit a written application and state the reasons. If the withdrawal is agreed to, the State Administration for Market Regulation shall terminate its registration procedure. If, during technical review, on-site verification and sample testing, suspected violations such as concealing the true situation or providing false information are found, they shall be handled in accordance with the law, and the applicant may not withdraw the registration application.

Article 54. Under any of the following circumstances, the State Administration for Market Regulation may revoke the registration of foods for special medical purposes upon the request of an interested party or ex officio: (1) a decision approving registration is made by staff members through abuse of power or dereliction of duty; (2) a decision approving registration is made beyond statutory functions and powers; (3) a decision approving registration is made in violation of statutory procedures; (4) registration is approved for an applicant who is not qualified to apply or does not meet statutory conditions; (5) the food production license has been revoked; (6) other circumstances in which registration may be revoked in accordance with the law.

Article 55. Under any of the following circumstances, the State Administration for Market Regulation shall handle the cancellation of registration of foods for special medical purposes in accordance with the law: (1) the enterprise applies for cancellation; (2) the enterprise is terminated in accordance with the law; (3) the validity period of the registration certificate has expired without renewal; (4) the registration certificate has been revoked, withdrawn or nullified in accordance with the law; (5) other circumstances in which registration shall be cancelled as prescribed by laws or regulations.

Chapter VI. Legal Liability

Article 56. Where the Food Safety Law and other laws and regulations already provide for violations relating to the registration of foods for special medical purposes, those provisions shall apply.

Article 57. Where an applicant conceals relevant information or provides false materials when applying for registration of foods for special medical purposes, the State Administration for Market Regulation shall not accept the application or approve the registration, and shall issue a warning to the applicant; the applicant may not apply again for registration of foods for special medical purposes within 1 year; if a crime is suspected, the case shall be transferred to the public security organ in accordance with the law and criminal liability shall be investigated.

Article 58. Where an applicant obtains a registration certificate for foods for special medical purposes by deception, bribery or other improper means, the State Administration for Market Regulation shall revoke it in accordance with the law; the licensee may not apply again for registration of foods for special medical purposes within 3 years; a fine of not less than 10000 yuan but not more than 30000 yuan shall be imposed; if harmful consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 30000 yuan but not more than 200000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is suspected, the case shall be transferred to the public security organ in accordance with the law and criminal liability shall be investigated.

Article 59. Whoever forges, alters, resells, leases, lends or transfers a registration certificate for foods for special medical purposes shall be fined not less than 30000 yuan but not more than 100000 yuan by the market supervision department at or above the county level; if harmful consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100000 yuan but not more than 200000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is suspected, the case shall be transferred to the public security organ in accordance with the law and criminal liability shall be investigated.

Article 60. Where an applicant changes matters that do not affect product safety, nutritional adequacy or clinical effects for special medical purposes without applying for a change in accordance with the law, the market supervision department at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 1000 yuan but not more than 10000 yuan shall be imposed. Where an applicant changes the product formula, production process or other matters that may affect product safety, nutritional adequacy or clinical effects for special medical purposes without applying for a change in accordance with the law, the market supervision department at or above the county level shall impose penalties in accordance with Article 124 of the Food Safety Law.

Article 61. Where a market supervision department or its staff approves registration for an applicant who does not meet the conditions, or approves registration beyond statutory functions and powers, it shall be handled in accordance with Article 144 of the Food Safety Law. Where a market supervision department or its staff abuses power, is derelict in duty or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the course of registration examination and approval, it shall be handled in accordance with Article 145 of the Food Safety Law.

Chapter VII. Supplementary Provisions

Article 62. For the purposes of these Measures, foods for special medical purposes means formula foods specially processed and formulated to meet the special needs of people with restricted food intake, digestion and absorption disorders, metabolic disorders or specific disease states for nutrients or diet, including foods for special medical purposes for infants from 0 to 12 months of age and foods for special medical purposes for people above 1 year of age. Foods for special medical purposes for people above 1 year of age include complete nutritional formulas, specific complete nutritional formulas and incomplete nutritional formulas.

Article 63. These Measures do not apply to nutritional meals prepared by medical institutions and others for patients to eat.

Article 64. These Measures shall take effect from 1 January 2024. The Measures for the Registration Administration of Foods for Special Medical Purposes, published by Order No. 24 of the former State Food and Drug Administration on 7 March 2016, shall be repealed at the same time.
