The General Administration of Customs of China (GACC) published Decree No. 280 on 14 October 2025. It replaces Decree No. 248 and applies from **1 June 2026**.

## Who it applies to

Every overseas company that produces, processes or stores food for export to China must be registered with GACC. Food additives and food-contact materials are outside the scope of this decree.

## What changed in practice

- The five-year registration validity is retained.
- GACC publishes and maintains the catalogue of food categories that require **recommendation by the competent authority** of the exporting country. It currently covers meat and meat products, casings, edible bird's nests, bee products, eggs and egg products, edible oils and fats, stuffed pasta, cereals, cereal flours and malt, dehydrated vegetables, seasoning powders, nuts and seeds, dried fruit, foods for special dietary uses, health foods, dairy products and aquatic products.
- A separate list sets out the categories that are **not eligible for automatic renewal**; companies in those categories must apply again before expiry.
- The scope of overseas storage facilities that must be registered is defined in the same decree and is adjusted dynamically.

## What exporters should do now

1. Check whether your product falls under the official-recommendation catalogue — if so, the application must go through your national competent authority, not directly to GACC.
2. Confirm the registration expiry date of each facility; renewal reminders are the company's responsibility, not the authority's.
3. Keep the registration number and approved product scope consistent with what appears on shipping documents and labels.

**Article 2**　These Provisions apply to the registration of overseas enterprises that produce, process or store food exported to China (hereinafter "overseas producers of imported food"). They do not apply to enterprises that produce, process or store food additives or food-related products.

**Article 4**　An overseas producer of imported food shall be registered with the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 7**　Conditions for registration: (1) the enterprise is established with the approval of, and under the effective supervision of, the competent authority of the country (region) in which it is located; (2) it has an effective food safety, hygiene and protection system, lawfully produces and exports in that country (region), and guarantees that food exported to China complies with Chinese laws, regulations and national food safety standards; (3) it meets the relevant inspection and quarantine requirements agreed between the General Administration of Customs and that competent authority. Producers of food listed in the catalogue must also be recommended by that competent authority.

**Article 14**　When a registered enterprise exports food to China, it shall mark on the food packaging the China registration number or the registration number approved by the competent authority of its country (region).

**Article 15**　Registration is valid for five years. The General Administration of Customs determines the start and end dates of the validity period when granting registration.

**Article 21**　Registration is automatically renewed for a further five years on expiry, except where: (1) the food is included in the list of food not eligible for automatic renewal of registration; (2) the enterprise is in a rectification period for failing to meet the registration requirements; (3) the General Administration of Customs has suspended, in accordance with law, imports of the relevant food from the country (region) concerned. The list referred to in item (1) is published separately by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 22**　Where food is included in the list of food not eligible for automatic renewal, the overseas producer shall apply for renewal through the original application channel between 3 and 12 months before the registration expires, submitting: (1) renewal application information; (2) a declaration that it will continue to meet the registration requirements; (3) for catalogue food, a declaration issued by the competent authority of its country (region) that the enterprise continues to meet the registration requirements. The General Administration of Customs renews registration, extending validity by five years, where the requirements are met.
