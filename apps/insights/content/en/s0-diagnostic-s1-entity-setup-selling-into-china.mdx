Before committing serious money to selling into China, walk two steps first: the S0 diagnostic and the S1 entity setup. The diagnostic is a small outlay that buys the access ruling; the work itself runs one to two weeks to issue all four reports, stretched to two to four weeks when port practice verification is included. The entity setup stands up your onshore entity in four to twelve weeks, depending on conditions. When both steps are done, you hold two things: four signed reports, and an onshore entity that can open accounts in the system. Those two decide whether every later investment stands on a ruling or on a guess.

## The S0 diagnostic buys the access ruling before anything else

The diagnostic asks for three inputs: your SKU list, your product data, and your target ports. The outputs are four reports: access study, compliance basis, execution push, and port practice verification. We issue the four reports one by one and brief you on each, every P0 prerequisite gets a handling action, and the briefing goes far enough that you can take it into your own review meeting. Fees follow a written quotation; the reports are delivered one by one, with nothing hidden behind a package price.

What the reports carry is the basis and the document numbers, not verbal assurances. Category and supervision questions are checked against CF [2018] No. 486, tax figures follow Caiguanshui [2016] No. 18, and the port practice verification walks the real rhythm of declaration, inspection and release at your target port. A supplier saying "you can get in" is worth little; the valuable thing is a report that states its basis, its exceptions, and who to call when something stalls.

## Four questions answered by four reports

The diagnostic buys four answers, and the answers are fixed in four reports, so they do not drift with the conversation.

- Can you get access: the access study report, with a conclusion and its basis for each category.
- Are there blockers: the compliance basis report, laying out prohibitions, restrictions and prerequisites item by item.
- When does access happen: the execution checklist, queueing the prerequisites, each P0 item carrying a handling action.
- How does the port execute: the practice checklist, a field record of the declaration and inspection rhythm at the port.

One outlay asks all four questions. If any one of them stays unanswered, everything behind it (entity setup, store launch, stocking the bonded warehouse) is interest paid on a guess; once all four are answered, every later investment has a target.

## Four steps and two outputs of the S1 entity setup

The entity setup takes one input: the entity path you choose. It produces two outputs: the business licence and customs registration. Four steps are filed by us on your behalf: business registration, customs registration, bank account, tax registration, each step handing back one certificate; only when all four are in place can the system open your account. The range is four to twelve weeks, taken by condition, and the condition is which of the three paths you pick. The registration requirements for cross-border e-commerce enterprises follow General Administration of Customs Announcement No. 194 of 2018.

We do the legwork on this step, but we cannot make the decision for you: the entity path is yours to choose, and what we deliver is the path comparison and the time-cost estimate, also priced by written quotation.

## How to pick among the three entity paths

- A wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE): the most control, with the accounts, the tax number and invoicing in your own hands; the price is pace, and the top of the four to twelve week range usually lands on it.
- A joint venture: trades a share of control for speed on the back of a local partner's existing qualifications; the ledger and the company seal arrangements must be pinned down in the agreement.
- The platform-held path (the one we operate ourselves): fastest, with the duty basis fixed, and the fastest route completes the whole chain in fourteen weeks; the brand steps back into the supplier position and the platform is the selling entity.

One judgement up front: if you are testing one or two SKUs, you do not need a WFOE yet. The entity is a four to twelve week investment, the platform-held path fits a trial stage better, and you can stand up your own entity later once the grading sheet proves the category holds. Conversely, if a full rollout is already decided and you want the membership and the data in your own hands, the slow WFOE is worth waiting for.

## Walk one row: whey protein powder

Take the row SKU20260902003: whey protein powder, protein category, Grade B, documents, declared at 298.00 yuan, submitted on 2026-09-02, status under review. Grade B, documents means access is not in question but the paperwork is incomplete: the filing has been accepted, review resumes once the missing items arrive, so the row sits at under review: it is waiting for documents, not stuck.

The next row shows the boundary: SKU20260903006, collagen peptide drink, functional category, Grade C, priority review, 168.00 yuan, submitted the same day, status returned. A priority-review product coming back means this category wants its claims and paperwork ready before submission; submit first and revise later, and the return stays on the sheet.

Trace both rows back through the first two steps: the grading basis for whey protein powder sits in the access study report of S0; grade B was set at the diagnostic stage by category and document requirements, not handed out at random; and the entity that files these products is the onshore entity stood up in S1, the registration number carried in the filing system. All three statuses appear across the six rows: four filed, one under review, one returned. Every row traces back to an S0 report and an S1 entity, which is exactly what the diagnostic and the setup deliver.
