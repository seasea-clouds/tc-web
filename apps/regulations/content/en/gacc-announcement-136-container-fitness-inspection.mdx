This announcement sets uniform container fitness inspection requirements for shipping export perishable and deteriorating food and frozen products, effective 1 October 2026; earlier inconsistent provisions are superseded.

The commodity codes are published on the GACC portal. Producers and operators must self-check containers and select ones that are clean, sanitary, properly refrigerated and soundly sealed. Before loading, carriers, stuffing operators or their agents must file an application with the customs office where containers are stored via the China International Trade Single Window, uploading the Declaration of Fitness Compliance for Export Containers. Customs reviews the declaration and spot-checks containers; qualified ones may load such goods, and results stay valid for 21 days.

At export declaration customs verifies the results; failed declarations are returned per law. Two annexes cover fitness inspection requirements and the declaration template: body integrity, weather tightness, cleanliness, refrigeration performance and harmful residue criteria.

In order to effectively guard against the risk of export food being contaminated by containers, and in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection, its implementing regulations and other relevant laws and regulations, GACC hereby announces the following on the container fitness inspection work for the shipment of export perishable and deteriorating food and frozen products:

I. Scope of Commodities

The codes of perishable and deteriorating food and frozen products subject to container fitness inspection are published on the GACC portal, at: http://jckspj.customs.gov.cn/spj/zcfg18/index.html.

II. Enterprise Self-Inspection

Producers and operators of export perishable and deteriorating food and frozen products must strictly fulfil their primary responsibility for food safety, carry out self-inspection of containers, and select containers that are clean, sanitary, properly refrigerated and soundly sealed for loading such products. The container fitness inspection requirements are set out in Annex 1.

III. Fitness Inspection

Carriers, container stuffing operators or their agents of export perishable and deteriorating food and frozen products must, before loading, submit an inspection application to the customs office where the containers are stored through the container fitness inspection module for outbound containers of the China International Trade Single Window, and upload the Declaration of Fitness Compliance for Export Containers (hereinafter referred to as the Fitness Compliance Declaration, see Annex 2). Customs reviews the Fitness Compliance Declaration and spot-checks containers; those that pass are permitted to load export perishable and deteriorating food and frozen products. Fitness inspection results are valid for 21 days and expire automatically upon expiry.

IV. Export Verification

At the time of export declaration of perishable and deteriorating food and frozen products, customs will verify the container fitness inspection results, and declarations that fail verification are returned in accordance with the law.

This Announcement comes into force on 1 October 2026. Where previous provisions on container fitness inspection for the shipment of export perishable and deteriorating food and frozen products are inconsistent with this Announcement, this Announcement prevails.

It is hereby announced.

GACC
