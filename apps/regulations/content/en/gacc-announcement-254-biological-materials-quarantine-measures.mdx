The door for inbound biological materials has opened wider. On 24 December 2025, GACC Announcement No. 254 extended the pilot quarantine measures of Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei and Shanghai nationwide, replacing case-by-case handling with risk-tiered supervision for research and production materials.

Level 1 risk still requires special quarantine approval. Level 2 follows ordinary animal and plant quarantine approval, normally within 3 working days. Laboratory animals are isolated for 14 days instead of 30. The designated facility permit is issued once and may be reused during its validity, and experiments may run during isolation with approval. Mice without a health monitoring report or a CNAS-accredited test report remain subject to 30-day isolation.

Outbound materials follow the importing country: no registration requirement there means none here; where required, the producer applies with the form and, if qualified, is registered for 5 years. The announcement takes effect on 30 December 2025 and prevails over the 2017 No. 94 announcement where they differ.

To support life-science research and ensure qualified biological materials for research and production, GACC, in accordance with relevant laws and regulations and after risk assessment, has decided to extend nationwide the pilot quarantine reforms tested in Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei and Shanghai, and to improve risk-tiered quarantine supervision for inbound and outbound biological materials. The relevant matters are announced as follows:

I. Dynamically Adjust the List of Risk Tiers and Corresponding Quarantine Supervision Measures for Inbound Biological Materials

Drawing on industry and research needs, the List of Risk Levels and Corresponding Quarantine Supervision Measures for Inbound Biological Materials (the List, see Annex 1) is adjusted and improved, and is published and dynamically updated on the GACC website. Animal-derived gelatin for research use (pigskin, cowhide and fish skin gelatin) is no longer regulated as biological material; it is regulated as non-edible animal gelatin.

II. Further Improve Quarantine Supervision Measures

(i) Improve quarantine approval for inbound biological materials.

Inbound biological materials of Tier 1 risk shall apply for special animal and plant quarantine approval; the approval procedures and requirements follow GACC announcements on special quarantine approval.

Inbound biological materials of Tier 2 risk shall apply for animal and plant quarantine approval. In principle, the approval time of the competent customs authority shall not exceed 3 working days.

(ii) Improve quarantine supervision measures for inbound laboratory animals.

For laboratory animals in the List, the permit for the use of designated isolation facilities may be issued once and used multiple times within its validity. On the premise of ensuring biosafety, with the approval of the competent customs authority where the isolation site is located, experiments may be conducted while the animals are under isolation.

The isolation and quarantine period for laboratory animals in the List is reduced to 14 days. For imported laboratory mice, a health monitoring report as required by the relevant health certificate shall be provided, or, after entry, a test report issued by a testing institution accredited by the China National Accreditation Service for Conformity Assessment (CNAS) shall be provided. Where the above reports cannot be provided, the isolation and quarantine period remains 30 days.

(iii) Improve registration administration for outbound biological material enterprises.

For units producing, processing or storing outbound biological materials (outbound production units), registration is not required where the importing country or region has no such requirement. Where registration is required, the unit shall apply to customs in advance and submit the application form (see Annex 2) and relevant materials. Customs shall review the application under the Measures for the Administration of Customs Administrative Licensing and grant registration valid for 5 years where conditions are met.

III. Other Provisions

(i) For biological materials subject to access administration as required by the List, the list of products granted access and the list of registered enterprises are published and dynamically updated on the GACC website.

(ii) The consignee of inbound biological materials of Tier 2 risk shall apply to the local customs authority for filing, earnestly perform its primary responsibility for biosafety, strictly implement China's biosafety management requirements, and take effective biosafety measures for the transport, storage, isolation and rearing, use and harmless treatment of inbound biological materials.

(iii) Where inbound and outbound biological materials involve other supervision requirements for special inbound and outbound goods and articles, the provisions of relevant laws and regulations shall apply.

This Announcement takes effect on 30 December 2025. Where the Announcement of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on the pilot expansion (Announcement No. 94 of 2017) is inconsistent with this Announcement, this Announcement shall prevail.

It is hereby announced.
