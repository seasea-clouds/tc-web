It can enter from today. GACC Announcement No. 60 of 2026 was issued on 9 May and took effect the same day, opening fine wheat flour grown and processed in Ukraine for human consumption; seed wheat is outside the scope.

One lot of flour goes through the whole process: raw wheat from growers filed by the Ukrainian side, with ISPM 6 monitoring for dwarf bunt in the growing area; the processing enterprise registered in China; new, breathable packaging marked in Chinese and English for the People's Republic of China with the registration number; a check of the transport means before loading; and entry on a phytosanitary certificate with an English additional declaration plus a non-animal-origin food certificate for each lot.

If the certificate, registration number or package marking does not match, hold this lot for now: no valid certificate, an unregistered enterprise, or detection of dwarf bunt, live insects or soil means return or destruction, or treatment. The country and the lot are process illustrations; the registration list and the certificate govern.

According to the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions on the inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for Ukrainian wheat flour exported to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the State Service of Ukraine for Food Safety and Consumer Protection (hereinafter referred to as the Ukrainian side), Ukrainian wheat flour meeting the following requirements is allowed to be imported from today.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, and the Law on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(2) The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Import and Export Food Safety and the Provisions on the Administration of Overseas Production Enterprises for Imported Food;

(3) The Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for the Export of Ukrainian Wheat Flour to the People's Republic of China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the State Service of Ukraine for Food Safety and Consumer Protection (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Products Allowed for Import

Wheat flour in this Announcement refers to a fine powdered product grown from wheat (Triticum aestivum L.) planted within Ukraine and processed within Ukraine for human consumption.

The Ukrainian side shall ensure that wheat flour exported to China is free from Tilletia controversa J.G. Kühn, live insects, and other impurities deliberately added or mixed in. The whole process of Ukrainian wheat flour exported to China, from raw wheat planting, production, processing, storage, transport to export, shall comply with the relevant safety and sanitary requirements of China and Ukraine, so as to prevent contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

Enterprises that produce, process and store Ukrainian wheat flour for export to China shall ensure that their wheat flour complies with China's food safety and plant quarantine laws, regulations and national food safety standards, and shall be inspected by the Ukrainian side as meeting the requirements of the Chinese side and registered in China.

IV. Requirements for Raw Material

(1) The Ukrainian side shall ensure that the raw material for wheat flour comes from wheat-growing enterprises confirmed by Ukraine as complying with China's food safety and plant quarantine laws, regulations and national food safety standards and the provisions of the Protocol, and filed.

(2) The Ukrainian side shall monitor Tilletia controversa J.G. Kühn in wheat-growing areas in accordance with the International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 6 (ISPM 6) “Monitoring” and internationally recognized methods. If Tilletia controversa J.G. Kühn is found, the Ukrainian side shall immediately notify the Chinese side and suspend the use of wheat produced in the relevant growing areas for the production of wheat flour exported to China.

V. Packaging Requirements

The Ukrainian side shall ensure that wheat flour exported to China is packaged in new, clean, sanitary, breathable materials that meet food safety and plant quarantine requirements, and that spillage is prevented during transport. Each package shall be marked “This product is exported to the People's Republic of China”, together with the product name, place of origin, production enterprise and its registration number in China, exporter name and address, and other traceable information in Chinese or English. The above information may be affixed to the package in the form of a label.

VI. Quarantine Requirements for Transport Means

Before loading, the Ukrainian side shall inspect the transport means carrying wheat flour exported to China. The transport means shall meet the sanitary and epidemic prevention requirements of both sides and shall not be mixed with quarantine pests or other restricted quarantine objects, such as weed seeds, live insects, other grain impurities, plant remnants, soil and other extraneous impurities.

VII. Certificate Requirements

Before export, the Ukrainian side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for wheat flour exported to China that has passed quarantine in accordance with the Protocol. The additional declaration column of the certificate shall state the name of the wheat flour production and processing enterprise, its registration number in China, and “The wheat flour covered by this phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for the Export of Ukrainian Wheat Flour to the People's Republic of China, signed by the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the State Service of Ukraine for Food Safety and Consumer Protection. The consignment is free from Tilletia controversa J.G. Kühn.”

The content and format of the phytosanitary certificate shall be confirmed by both sides in advance.

To ensure the safety of wheat flour exported to China, the Ukrainian side shall issue a non-animal-origin food certificate for each lot of wheat flour exported to China.

VIII. Other Requirements

(1) After Ukrainian wheat flour exported to China arrives at the entry port in China, the Chinese side shall carry out inspection and quarantine supervision and grant entry only after it passes.

(2) If Ukrainian wheat flour exported to China is found to be in violation of China's food safety and plant quarantine laws, regulations and national food safety standards and the provisions of the Protocol, the Chinese customs shall handle it in accordance with China's relevant laws and regulations.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs

9 May 2026
