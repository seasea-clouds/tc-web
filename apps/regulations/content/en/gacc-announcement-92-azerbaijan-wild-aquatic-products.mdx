Yes, they can enter. GACC Announcement No. 92 admits wild aquatic products from Azerbaijan with effect from today: producers must be approved and recommended by the Azerbaijani side and registered by the Chinese side, and unregistered producers shall not export to China.

For a consignment, check the producer's Chinese registration and lawful catch, confirm the raw material carries no banned drugs, pathogenic microorganisms or disease, verify the packaging and label meet Chinese national standards, and attach a health certificate confirmed by both sides, printed in Chinese, Azerbaijani and English with English mandatory, whose specimen and seal were filed 1 month in advance.

At the Chinese port of entry, China Customs carries out inspection and quarantine under food safety laws, national standards and the Protocol. Non-conforming products are destroyed, returned or otherwise handled; for producers with serious or repeated non-conformities, the Chinese side may tighten inspection and quarantine or suspend imports.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions on inspection and quarantine requirements for export of wild aquatic products from Azerbaijan to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Food Safety Agency of the Republic of Azerbaijan (hereinafter the Azerbaijani side), wild aquatic products from Azerbaijan meeting the following requirements are permitted for import into China with effect from today.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(a) Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations.

(b) Measures for the Safety Administration of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China; Regulations of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China on Registration Administration of Overseas Producers of Imported Food.

(c) Protocol of Inspection and Quarantine Requirements for Export of Wild Aquatic Products from Azerbaijan to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Food Safety Agency of the Republic of Azerbaijan (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Scope of Products Permitted for Import

Wild aquatic products means wild aquatic animal products and their products for human consumption, and marine plant products such as algae and their products. It excludes amphibians, reptiles, aquatic mammals, live aquatic animals, and reproductive materials of aquatic animals and plants.

III. Requirements for Producers

Producers exporting wild aquatic products to China (including production, processing and storage establishments) shall be approved by the Azerbaijani authorities and subject to their effective supervision. Their food safety, hygiene management and protection systems shall comply with the relevant Chinese food safety laws and regulations.

Under the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, producers exporting wild aquatic products to China shall be recommended by the Azerbaijani side for registration with the Chinese side. Without registration, they shall not export to China.

IV. Requirements for Imported Products

The Azerbaijani side shall ensure that wild aquatic products exported to China meet the following conditions:

(a) They are lawfully caught in national or international waters.

(b) Neither the raw materials nor the products fall under any of the following circumstances:

(1) Wild aquatic products already exported or intended for export from Azerbaijan to China have violated Chinese food safety laws, regulations, national standards or the Protocol, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China.

(2) Aquatic animal diseases related to the products under the Protocol that are listed in the List of Entry Animal Quarantine Diseases of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the List) or notifiable under the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) have occurred in Azerbaijan, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China.

(3) A major food safety incident has occurred in Azerbaijan, a major public health incident has occurred at a producer, or the catching waters have been affected by pollutants, seriously affecting the safety of wild aquatic products exported to China.

(c) No drugs or additives banned by China have been used directly or indirectly; drugs or additives restricted or permitted by China are used as prescribed.

(d) Inspection and quarantine by the competent authority has found no pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or harmful substances or foreign matter listed in Chinese laws and regulations, and no aquatic animal diseases listed in the List or notifiable under WOAH.

(e) The production process, including catching, production, processing, packaging, storage, transport, transshipment and export, shall comply with the relevant Chinese safety, hygiene and traceability requirements.

(f) The packaging and labelling of the products shall comply with Chinese national food safety standards and the relevant rules on the safety administration of imported food.

V. Certificate Requirements

The Azerbaijani side shall carry out inspection and quarantine of wild aquatic products exported to China as required by the Chinese side, and issue for each consignment a health certificate confirmed by both sides certifying that the consignment complies with the relevant Chinese food safety laws, regulations and national standards. The certificate shall be printed in at least Chinese, Azerbaijani and English, with English the mandatory language when the certificate is filled in.

The health certificate shall state in full the information of the producer exporting wild aquatic products to China. The Azerbaijani side shall provide the Chinese side with the certificate specimen and official seal for record in good time. In the event of any change, the Azerbaijani side shall file it with the Chinese side at least one month before it takes effect.

VI. Other Requirements

China Customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine of wild aquatic products exported to China in accordance with Chinese food safety laws and regulations, national standards and the Protocol.

Non-conforming products shall be destroyed, returned or otherwise handled. For producers with serious problems or repeated non-conformities, the Chinese side may tighten inspection and quarantine or suspend imports.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs
