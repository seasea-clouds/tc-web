Announcement No. 117 [2026] of the General Administration of Customs was published on 12 August 2026 and takes effect on 15 August 2026: as justified by the actual work situation, the General Administration of Customs repeals its Announcement No. 3 of 2001 on the scope of scrap steel [No. 3 of 2001], and the scrap-steel scope it announced no longer serves as the basis for supervision.

If you handle import formalities for scrap steel and other solid waste: the formalities no longer cite the scrap-steel scope announced by [No. 3 of 2001]; such imports continue to be handled under the current rules on solid waste import control and on inspection and quarantine, and the announcement is not an endorsement of any irregular import.

Signed on 12 August 2026; the announcement takes effect on 15 August 2026 and the publication date and the effective date are not the same day; only [No. 3 of 2001] is repealed and the other current rules remain unaffected; the signatory is the General Administration of Customs.

As justified by the actual work situation, the General Administration of Customs decides to repeal its Announcement No. 3 of 2001 on the scope of scrap steel. This announcement takes effect on 15 August 2026.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

12 August 2026
