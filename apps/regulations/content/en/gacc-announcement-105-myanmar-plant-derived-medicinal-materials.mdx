In force, so it can enter. GACC Announcement 105/2026 of 23 July, effective that day, opens 58 medicinal plant products from Myanmar, wild or cultivated, safflower among them, called medicinal materials.

A Myanmar safflower consignment via a third country follows the process: the enterprise is reviewed by the Myanmar side and approved and registered by the Chinese side; wood packaging treated per ISPM 15, boxes and pallets marked “for export to the People's Republic of China” or “Exported to the People's Republic of China”, container sealed; the Myanmar side issues the phytosanitary certificate per ISPM 12 with the additional declaration verbatim in English.

Without the registration list or certificate, hold this consignment for now; if live organisms, soil or plant residues are found, returned, destroyed or treated, and exports to China may be suspended. The country and transit are illustrative; registration list and contract govern.

Under the laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter “the Chinese side”) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Irrigation of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar (hereinafter “the Myanmar side”) on the phytosanitary requirements for export of plant-derived medicinal materials from Myanmar to China, with effect from the date hereof, plant-derived medicinal materials from Myanmar meeting the relevant requirements below are permitted for import.

I. Quarantine Basis

(i) the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; (iii) the Measures for the Quarantine, Supervision and Administration of Entry and Exit Medicinal Materials; (iv) the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Plant-derived Medicinal Materials from Myanmar to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Irrigation of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar.

II. Names of Commodities Permitted for Entry

Plant-derived medicinal materials (hereinafter “medicinal materials”) means 58 kinds of medicinal plant products, wild or cultivated in Myanmar, such as safflower (see the Annex for details).

III. Enterprise Registration

Enterprises producing, processing and storing medicinal materials for export to China (hereinafter collectively “enterprises”) shall be reviewed by the Myanmar side and approved for registration by the Chinese side. Registration information includes the name, address and registration number, so as to allow accurate traceability when exported goods do not comply with the relevant provisions of this Announcement. Before trade begins, the Myanmar side shall provide the Chinese side with the registration list, which is published on the official website after review and approval by the Chinese side.

IV. List of Quarantine Pests of Concern to China

The Myanmar side shall ensure that medicinal materials exported to China are free from quarantine pests of concern to China; the list of quarantine pests is set out in the Annex.

V. Pre-export Management

(i) Integrated pest management.

(1) Medicinal materials for export to China shall, under the supervision and guidance of the Myanmar side, adopt systematic integrated management measures to control pests and diseases, including pest monitoring, integrated control and post-harvest treatment, with relevant records kept. (2) Pest monitoring and control shall be completed under the guidance of professional technical personnel of the Myanmar side, who shall have been trained by the Myanmar side or its authorised institutions. (3) Once a quarantine pest of concern to China is found in monitoring, the enterprise must promptly take corresponding control measures to ensure that medicinal materials exported to China do not carry quarantine pests of concern to China.

(ii) Processing and packaging requirements.

(1) Medicinal materials for export to China shall be cleaned of soil and impurities and dried. (2) Packaging materials shall be clean, hygienic and unused and shall meet the relevant phytosanitary requirements of China; where wooden packaging is used, it shall comply with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15). (3) Each box/bag shall be labelled in Chinese or English with traceability information such as the product name, country, place of origin (province, city or region), enterprise name and its registration number. Each box/bag and pallet shall be labelled in Chinese or English with “for export to the People's Republic of China” or “Exported to the People's Republic of China”. (4) The means of transport or container must be inspected at the time of loading to confirm good hygienic conditions. The means of transport or container shall be sealed, and its seal shall be intact upon arrival at the Chinese port of entry.

(iii) Pre-export inspection and quarantine.

Medicinal materials for export to China are subject to inspection and quarantine supervision by the Myanmar side and, once confirmed as complying with this Announcement, are permitted for export to China. If quarantine pests of concern to China, soil or plant residues are found, the entire consignment shall not be exported to China. The Myanmar side shall immediately suspend the export of medicinal materials to China by the enterprises concerned until the cause is identified and effective corrective measures are taken. Relevant records shall also be kept and provided to the Chinese side upon request.

(iv) Phytosanitary certificate requirements.

For medicinal materials for export to China that pass quarantine, the Myanmar side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate in accordance with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12 (ISPM 12), stating information such as the enterprise name or registration number. The additional declaration of the phytosanitary certificate shall state:

“This consignment complies with the requirements specified in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Plant-derived Medicinal Materials from Myanmar to China, and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China.” (This consignment complies with the phytosanitary requirements of the Protocol for the export of plant-derived medicinal materials from Myanmar to China and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China.)

VI. Entry Quarantine and Non-compliance Handling

When medicinal materials exported to China arrive at the port of entry, China Customs shall verify the relevant documents and labels and carry out inspection and quarantine.

(i) Verification of relevant certificates and labels. (1) Verify whether the phytosanitary certificate complies with Article V (iv) of this Announcement. (2) Verify whether the labels on the boxes/bags and pallets comply with Article V (ii) of this Announcement.

(ii) Entry quarantine. (1) Medicinal materials for export to China shall enter through ports permitted by the Chinese side for import of medicinal materials. (2) Inspection and quarantine shall be carried out in accordance with the laws and regulations of China, national standards and the requirements of this Announcement; those that pass inspection and quarantine are permitted entry.

(iii) Non-compliance handling. (1) If the consignment is found to come from an unregistered enterprise or is not accompanied by a valid phytosanitary certificate, it shall be returned or destroyed. (2) If live quarantine pests of concern to China or other live quarantine pests, or soil, plant residues, etc. are found, the consignment shall be returned, destroyed or treated. (3) If other circumstances not complying with the relevant laws and regulations of China are found, they shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions.
