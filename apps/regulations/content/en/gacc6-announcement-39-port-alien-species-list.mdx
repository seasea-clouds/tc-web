Has the List of Key Controlled Invasive Alien Species at Ports been released? Yes, it takes effect on 1 May 2026. Six Departments' Announcement No. 39 of 2026 states that the List was formulated under the Biosecurity Law and the Invasive Alien Species Measures.

Six departments issued the announcement jointly, namely the General Administration of Customs together with the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, the Ministry of Natural Resources, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, and the National Forestry and Grassland Administration. The List is published as an annex to the announcement. You should check it against the List when conducting inspection and declaration at ports.

Please note that the effective date is 1 May 2026. Before that, please confirm that your inspection and prevention and control measures comply with the new List.

<!-- original -->

Pursuant to the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China and the Measures for the Administration of Invasive Alien Species, the General Administration of Customs, together with the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, the Ministry of Natural Resources, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, and the National Forestry and Grassland Administration, has formulated the List of Key Controlled Invasive Alien Species at Ports (see annex), which is hereby released and shall take effect on 1 May 2026.

This announcement is hereby issued.
