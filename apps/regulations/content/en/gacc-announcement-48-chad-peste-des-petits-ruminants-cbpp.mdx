Chad reports peste des petits ruminants and contagious bovine pleuropneumonia, closing the route for its sheep, goats, cattle and products. Announcement No. 48 of 2026 prohibits direct or indirect import, posting or carriage into China without exception; discovered consignments are returned or destroyed. Effective on issuance.

Waste and swill from Chad must be decontaminated under customs supervision and not discarded without authorization; sheep, goats, cattle and products intercepted as illegal by border inspection and other departments are destroyed under customs supervision. Violators are punished by customs under the Customs Law, Biosecurity Law and Animal and Plant Quarantine Law.

Coordination is key: customs and agriculture and rural affairs authorities at all levels work closely under the Customs Law, Biosecurity Law, Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and Animal Epidemic Prevention Law, conducting inspection, epidemic prevention and supervision. The prohibitions apply at ports.

<!-- original -->

Recently, Chad reported outbreaks of peste des petits ruminants and contagious bovine pleuropneumonia to the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH). To prevent the introduction of the disease and protect China's livestock industry and biosecurity, the following is announced in accordance with relevant laws and regulations:

Direct or indirect import from Chad of sheep, goats, cattle and their related products is prohibited, including products derived from sheep, goats or cattle that are unprocessed or, although processed, may still transmit the disease.

Posting or carrying sheep, goats, cattle and their related products from Chad into China is prohibited. Any such consignment found shall without exception be returned or destroyed.

Animal and plant waste, swill and the like unloaded from incoming transport vehicles from Chad shall without exception undergo treatment to eliminate harmful organisms under customs supervision and shall not be discarded without authorization.

Sheep, goats, cattle and their related products from Chad that are intercepted as illegally entered by border inspection and other departments shall without exception be destroyed under customs supervision.

Anyone violating the above provisions shall be dealt with by customs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations.

Customs authorities at all levels and agriculture and rural affairs departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant provisions, work in close coordination and carry out inspection and quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision.

This announcement takes effect on the date of issuance.

This announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

20 April 2026
