Farmed aquatic products from the United Kingdom are approved for export to China. Before importing, confirm the producer is registered by the Chinese side. GACC Announcement No. 26 of 2026 covers farmed aquatic animals and algae for human consumption, excluding CITES species and live animals.

A shipment must pass three checks. The producer must be approved by the UK side and registered by the Chinese side. Products must be farmed in domestic waters, free of specified diseases and major food safety incidents, with no banned additives. Packaging must carry Chinese and English labels with the product name, dates, batch number and registration number.

On entry, declare with a veterinary health certificate issued by the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, and China Customs carries out inspection and quarantine. Failed products are destroyed or returned. Repeated failures suspend imports.

Under the laws and regulations of China and the provisions on inspection and quarantine requirements for farmed aquatic products exported from the United Kingdom to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland (hereinafter referred to as the UK side), farmed aquatic products from the United Kingdom that meet the following relevant requirements are allowed to be imported from the date of this announcement.

Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations.

(2) The Measures for the Safety Management of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China and the Provisions on the Registration and Administration of Overseas Production Enterprises for Imported Food of the People's Republic of China.

(3) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland on the Inspection, Quarantine and Health Requirements for Farmed Aquatic Products Exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

Scope of Imported Products

Farmed aquatic products means artificially farmed products of aquatic animals for human consumption and their products, and products of marine plants such as algae and their products, excluding species listed in the appendices of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES) and the List of Key Protected Wild Animals in China, live aquatic animals and aquatic animal and plant breeding materials. The List of Approved Farmed Aquatic Products from the United Kingdom for Export to China is set out in the Annex.

Requirements for Production Enterprises

(1) Production enterprises (including processing enterprises and independent cold stores) and farms that export farmed aquatic products to China shall be approved by the UK side and subject to its effective supervision. The hygiene conditions of production enterprises shall comply with the relevant veterinary, health and public health laws and regulations of China and the United Kingdom.

(2) Production enterprises exporting farmed aquatic products to China shall be recommended by the UK side for registration with the Chinese side. Without registration, they may not export to China.

Requirements for Imported Products

The UK side shall ensure that farmed aquatic products exported to China meet the following conditions:

(1) Farmed in domestic waters.

(2) Neither the raw materials nor the products have experienced any of the following problems:

(1) The occurrence in the United Kingdom of aquatic animal diseases related to the products covered by the Protocol that are listed in the List of Quarantine Diseases for Imported Animals of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the List) or are notifiable under the requirements of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH), causing or likely to cause infection of farmed aquatic products exported to China.

(2) Any major food safety incident occurs in the United Kingdom that has affected or may affect farmed aquatic products exported to China.

(3) Farmed aquatic products already exported or intended for export to China from the United Kingdom violate the laws and regulations of China and the United Kingdom and the provisions of the Protocol, seriously endangering the safety of farmed aquatic products exported to China.

(4) A major public health incident occurs at a production enterprise of farmed aquatic products exported to China, such as employees infected with a major infectious disease, which, based on reliable scientific analysis, may contaminate farmed aquatic products exported to China and their packaging or transportation process.

(3) No ingredients or additives banned by both sides have been used, directly or indirectly, such as addition to feed, and ingredients or additives restricted or permitted by the Chinese side have been used in accordance with the regulations.

(4) Upon inspection and quarantine by the competent authorities, no pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or hazardous substances or foreign matter listed in the laws and regulations of China and the United Kingdom have been found, no pathological indications of any infectious or parasitic disease have been found, and no aquatic animal diseases listed in the List or notifiable under the requirements of the WOAH have been found.

(5) The entire process of farming, processing, packaging, storage, transportation, transshipment and export complies with the relevant hygiene and traceability requirements of both sides, and also meets the relevant safety and hygiene requirements for cold-chain food established or recognized by relevant international organizations.

Requirements for Packaging and Labelling

Farmed aquatic products exported to China shall have outer packaging and separate inner packaging. The inner and outer packaging shall be made of completely new materials meeting international hygiene standards and satisfy the requirements for preventing contamination by external factors.

The inner and outer packaging shall bear labels in Chinese and English, indicating: the commercial name and scientific name, specifications, date of production, shelf life, batch number, storage conditions, production method (farmed), country of origin, name and registration number of the production enterprise, production region and address of the production enterprise (down to the state, province and city), and destination (marked as the People's Republic of China).

For farmed aquatic products exported to China in prepackaged form, the Chinese-language labels on the prepackaging shall comply with the labelling requirements for imported prepackaged food in China.

Certificate Requirements

The UK side shall conduct inspection and quarantine of farmed aquatic products exported to China in accordance with the requirements of the Chinese side, and issue a veterinary (health) certificate confirmed by both sides for each container of farmed aquatic products exported to China, certifying that the consignment complies with the relevant provisions of the veterinary and public health laws and regulations of China and the United Kingdom and the Protocol. The UK side shall enter on the veterinary (health) certificate the information of production enterprises involved in the farming, processing, packaging, storage, transportation, transshipment and export processes, including the names and registration numbers of processing enterprises and independent cold stores, and shall not omit information on any enterprise involved in any of the above links.

The certificate shall be printed in Chinese and English (English is mandatory when filling in the certificate). The format and content of the certificate shall be approved by both sides in advance. The UK side shall promptly provide the Chinese side with specimen certificates, the seals of the official issuing authority and the signatures of signing officials for record. If there is any change to the content or format of the specimen certificate, the seal of the official issuing authority or the signature of the signing official, the UK side shall file a record with the Chinese side at least one month before such change takes effect.

Other Requirements

China Customs carries out inspection and quarantine of farmed aquatic products exported to China in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations, national standards and the Protocol.

Non-compliant products shall be destroyed, returned or otherwise disposed of in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations and the Protocol. The Chinese side may take measures such as strengthened inspection and quarantine or suspension of imports against production enterprises with serious problems or repeated non-compliance.

This announcement is hereby made.
