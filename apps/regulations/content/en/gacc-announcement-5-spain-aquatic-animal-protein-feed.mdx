Announcement No. 5 of 2026 permits imports of Spanish aquatic animal protein for feed. Producers must be approved by Spain and registered with China for five years.

Raw material must come from legal harvest, be free of non-aquatic animal ingredients, and undergo heat treatment at no less than 85 degrees Celsius for at least 15 minutes.

Each consignment requires an official health certificate; non-compliant goods are returned or destroyed.

<!-- original -->

Under the laws and regulations of China and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food of the Kingdom of Spain (hereinafter the Spanish side) on quarantine and health requirements for Spanish aquatic animal protein for feed, products meeting the following requirements are permitted for import as of today.

I. Basis for inspection and quarantine

(1) The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(3) The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported and Exported Feed and Feed Additives;

(4) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food of the Kingdom of Spain on Quarantine and Health Requirements for the Export to China of Fishmeal, Fish Oil and Other Aquatic Animal Protein and Oils for Feed (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Scope of products permitted for import

The products permitted under this Announcement include fishmeal, fish oil and other aquatic animal protein and oils for feed, including animal-origin feed made from aquatic animals (excluding aquatic mammals) and by-products generated during their processing.

III. Requirements for producing enterprises

(1) Implement a Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point (HACCP) quality management system or establish a quality management system based on the HACCP concept, and formulate and effectively implement product recall and traceability systems;

(2) Under the effective supervision of the Spanish side, comply with the relevant laws and regulations of Spain;

(3) Be recommended by the Spanish side to the Chinese side and obtain registration with the Chinese side; the registration is valid for five years, and an application for a further five-year extension may be submitted six months before expiry.

IV. Requirements for raw materials

(1) Originate from legally harvested wild aquatic animals, farmed aquatic animals (excluding aquatic mammals), or by-products of plants processing aquatic products for human consumption;

(2) No aquatic animals that died of disease, were culled for disease prevention and control, or died of unknown causes are used;

(3) Contain no ingredients of non-aquatic animal origin.

V. Requirements for production and processing

(1) Undergo heat treatment at a core temperature of no less than 85ºC for no less than 15 minutes, or other equivalent processing methods recognised by the Chinese side;

(2) No ingredients of unknown animal origin, ingredients of non-aquatic animal origin, or prohibited substances banned by the laws and regulations of the Chinese and Spanish sides are added during production;

(3) Effective measures are taken during and after production to avoid contamination.

VI. Safety and hygiene requirements

(1) The products are allowed to be sold freely within Spain;

(2) The products contain no toxic or harmful substances hazardous to animal health and comply with China's feed safety and hygiene standards;

(3) For fishmeal, fish oil and other aquatic animal protein and oils for feed exported to China from countries or regions not recognised by the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) as having negligible bovine spongiform encephalopathy (BSE) risk, testing for ruminant-derived ingredients shall be carried out by a laboratory approved by the competent authority using the polymerase chain reaction (PCR) method or other methods recognised by the Chinese side, with a negative result (the detection limit for ruminant DNA in the exported products is 0.1%);

(4) Before export, the products shall be randomly sampled and tested by the competent authority, with results meeting the following requirements:

(1) Salmonella: not detected in 25 grams of product: n=5, c=0, m=0, M=0;

(2) Enterobacteriaceae: not more than 300 in 1 gram of sample: n=5, c=2, m=10, M=300;

where:

n - the number of samples tested;

m - the minimum bacterial count (threshold); the result is satisfactory if the bacterial count in all samples does not exceed m;

M - the maximum bacterial count; the result is unsatisfactory if the count in one or more samples equals or exceeds M;

c - the number of samples with bacterial counts between m and M; the result is still acceptable if the counts in the other samples are less than or equal to m.

VII. Requirements for packaging, labelling, storage and transport

(1) Packaging is new, clean, well sealed and not easily damaged;

(2) The outer packaging bears a label complying with the relevant requirements of Chinese laws, regulations and national standards for feed labels, and indicating warnings such as “not for human consumption” or “for feed only”;

(3) Effective measures are taken during storage and transport to avoid contamination.

VIII. Requirements for pre-export inspection and certificates

(1) The Spanish side shall inspect and quarantine the products for export to China and issue a health certificate conforming to the agreed specimen, certifying that the products meet the requirements of the Protocol;

(2) Each consignment for export to China shall be accompanied by at least one original official health certificate.

IX. Requirements for inspection and quarantine on entry

(1) Verification of documents.

(1) Verify that the goods come from a registered enterprise;

(2) Verify that the health certificate is authentic and valid.

(2) Inspection of goods.

China Customs shall inspect and quarantine the Spanish products in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and the requirements of this Announcement; goods passing inspection and quarantine are permitted entry.

(3) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) The following shall be returned or destroyed: no valid health certificate; goods from a non-registered Spanish producer; discrepancy between goods and certificate; discovery of soil, animal carcasses, animal excreta or quarantine pests that cannot be effectively treated; labels not meeting standards and cannot be corrected.

(2) If safety and hygiene items are found not to meet China's feed hygiene standards upon testing, the goods shall be treated, returned or destroyed.

(3) In the case of damaged bags or containers, the owner or agent shall make them intact; where damaged packaging poses a risk of spreading animal or plant diseases, the contaminated site, articles and appliances shall undergo quarantine treatment.

(4) Enterprises with serious problems or repeated non-compliance shall be subject to enhanced quarantine or suspension of import by the Chinese side.

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs
