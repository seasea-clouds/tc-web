Animal protein feed from Spain is cleared for import. Announcement No. 51 of 2026 requires producing enterprises to be approved by the Spanish authorities and registered in China.

Raw materials may not come from ruminants and must be heat-treated; blood products must be spray-dried and meet Salmonella and Enterobacteriaceae limits, with packaging marked not for human consumption.

Before export Spain inspects the products and issues a health certificate; on entry the quarantine permit is checked and certificates and goods are verified, with refusal or destruction for invalid papers, mismatch or detected disease.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food of the Kingdom of Spain (hereinafter the Spanish side) on quarantine and health requirements for animal protein feed from Spain, imports of products that meet the following requirements are permitted with effect from today.

I. Inspection and quarantine basis

(1) The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(3) The Measures for the Supervision of Inspection and Quarantine of Feed and Feed Additives for Import and Export;

(4) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food of the Kingdom of Spain on Quarantine and Health Requirements for Animal Protein Feed from Spain for Export to China (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Scope of products permitted for import

Animal protein feed in this Announcement refers to porcine-source protein feed, including intestinal mucosa protein powder, meat meal, bone meal, meat and bone meal, and blood products (such as blood meal, plasma protein powder and haemoglobin powder) derived from pigs.

III. Requirements for producing enterprises

(1) Approved by the relevant Spanish competent authorities and operating legally;

(2) Implementing a hygiene management system based on the principles of hazard analysis and critical control point (HACCP);

(3) Subject to supervision and inspection by the relevant Spanish competent authorities, ensuring continued compliance of production and processing with Spanish safety and hygiene regulations and no restrictions due to safety or hygiene problems;

(4) Recommended by the Spanish side to the Chinese side and registered with the Chinese side.

IV. Raw material requirements

(1) No ruminant material shall be used, and effective measures shall be taken to prevent contamination by ruminant-derived ingredients;

(2) The source animals shall come from areas free of foot-and-mouth disease, classical swine fever, swine vesicular disease and other notifiable swine diseases specified in the Terrestrial Animal Health Code of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH), and shall meet WOAH standards and be recognized by the Chinese side;

(3) The source animals were born and raised in the EU region and slaughtered in slaughterhouses approved by the Spanish authorities, having passed ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection without any symptoms of infectious diseases making them unsuitable as feed material;

(4) The source animals have not been subject to movement restrictions or culled because of an animal epidemic;

(5) Regarding African swine fever, if the raw material comes from pigs raised in Spain, it shall comply with the requirements of the agreement on regionalized management of African swine fever signed by China and Spain;

(6) Pig intestinal mucosa protein uses only pig small intestine as raw material.

V. Production and processing requirements

(1) Heat treatment at a core temperature of no less than 90ºC for at least 15 minutes, or other equivalent processing methods compliant with EU regulations and recognized by the Chinese side; or animal-derived raw materials subjected to heat treatment at no less than 90ºC for at least 15 minutes or other equivalent treatment compliant with EU regulations and recognized by the Chinese side;

(2) Blood products shall be produced by spray drying, with inlet air temperature of no less than 220ºC and outlet air temperature of no less than 80ºC, and the spray-dried material shall be held at above 60ºC for more than 20 minutes;

(3) No unknown animal-derived ingredients, ruminant-derived ingredients or other prohibited substances are added during production and processing;

(4) Effective measures are taken during and after production to avoid contamination; finished blood products shall be stored in a finished-product warehouse (room temperature maintained above 20ºC) for more than 20 days.

VI. Product requirements

Before export, products for export to China shall be randomly sampled by the Spanish competent authorities in accordance with an approved monitoring plan to ensure that the consignments meet the following requirements:

(1) Tests for ruminant-derived ingredients using the polymerase chain reaction (PCR) method or other methods recognized by the Chinese side in laboratories recognized by the Spanish side, with a negative result (no ruminant-derived ingredients). The minimum detection limit of the PCR method for ruminant-derived DNA is 0.1%;

(2) Compliance with Salmonella and Enterobacteriaceae requirements:

(1) Salmonella: not detected in 25 g of product: n=5, c=0, m=0, M=0;

(2) Enterobacteriaceae: per 1 g sample: n=5, c=2, m=10, M=300;

where:

n - number of samples tested;

m - minimum bacterial count (threshold); if the bacterial count in all samples does not exceed m, the result is qualified;

M - maximum bacterial count; if the bacterial count in one or more samples equals or exceeds M, the result is unqualified;

c - number of samples with a bacterial count between m and M; if the bacterial count in the other samples is less than or equal to m, the result is still considered acceptable.

VII. Packaging, labelling, storage and transport requirements

(1) Packaging shall be safe, clean, well-sealed, moisture-proof and not easily damaged;

(2) The outer packaging shall bear a Chinese-language label stating the name of the producing and processing enterprise and its registration number, marked “not for human consumption” or “for feed production only” and complying with Chinese laws, regulations and national standards on feed labelling;

(3) Effective measures shall be taken during transport to avoid contamination.

VIII. Pre-export inspection and certificate requirements

(1) Products for export to China shall be inspected and quarantined by the Spanish side, and a health certificate shall be issued according to the certificate sample confirmed by both sides, certifying that the products comply with the Protocol;

(2) Each consignment shall be accompanied by at least one official original health certificate.

IX. Entry inspection and quarantine requirements

(1) Quarantine approval.

Importers shall apply for the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit in accordance with the relevant provisions.

(2) Document verification.

(1) Verify that the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit is attached;

(2) Verify that the products come from a registered enterprise;

(3) Verify that the health certificate is authentic and valid.

(3) Cargo inspection.

China Customs shall, in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and in light of this Announcement, inspect and quarantine animal protein feed from Spain for export to China. Those that pass inspection and quarantine shall be permitted entry.

(4) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) Consignments shall be refused or destroyed under any of the following circumstances: no valid health certificate; from a non-registered Spanish producing enterprise; inconsistency between the goods and the certificate; detection of soil, animal carcasses, animal excreta or quarantine pests for which effective quarantine treatment cannot be carried out; labels not compliant with standards and cannot be corrected.

(2) If testing shows that safety and hygiene items do not comply with China's feed hygiene standards, the products shall be treated, refused or destroyed.

(3) In the case of torn bags or damaged containers, the owner or agent shall put them in good order; if damaged packaging poses a risk of spreading animal or plant diseases, quarantine treatment shall be applied to the contaminated site, articles and appliances.

(4) For enterprises with serious problems or repeated non-compliance, the Chinese side shall strengthen quarantine or suspend imports.

Hereby announced.
