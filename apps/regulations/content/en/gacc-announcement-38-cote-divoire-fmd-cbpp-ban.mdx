Announcement No. 38 of 2026 bans imports of cloven-hoofed animals and their products from Cote d'Ivoire to prevent foot-and-mouth disease and contagious bovine pleuropneumonia. Imports from Cote d'Ivoire of cloven-hoofed animals and related products are prohibited.

The ban covers postal items and personal carrying; waste from arriving transport must be treated under Customs supervision, and intercepted illegal goods destroyed.

Violators are handled by Customs under the relevant laws; Customs and agriculture authorities shall act respectively under their respective statutes.

<!-- original -->

Cote d'Ivoire has recently reported to the World Organisation for Animal Health the occurrence of foot-and-mouth disease and contagious bovine pleuropneumonia. To prevent the introduction of the diseases and protect the livestock industry and biosecurity of China, the following is hereby announced in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations.

I. Direct or indirect import from Cote d'Ivoire of cloven-hoofed animals and their related products (products derived from cloven-hoofed animals, whether unprocessed or processed but still capable of transmitting the diseases) is prohibited.

II. Postal delivery or carrying into China of cloven-hoofed animals and their related products from Cote d'Ivoire is prohibited. Any such goods discovered shall be returned or destroyed.

III. Animal and plant wastes, swill and the like unloaded from incoming transport from Cote d'Ivoire shall all be treated under the supervision of Customs and shall not be discarded without authorisation.

IV. Cloven-hoofed animals and their related products from Cote d'Ivoire intercepted by border inspection and other authorities as illegal entries shall all be destroyed under the supervision of Customs.

V. Any person violating the above provisions shall be dealt with by Customs in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations.

VI. Customs authorities at all levels and agriculture and rural affairs authorities at all levels shall, respectively in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant provisions, work in close coordination to carry out quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision.

This Announcement shall take effect on the date of publication.

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs, Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

10 April 2026
