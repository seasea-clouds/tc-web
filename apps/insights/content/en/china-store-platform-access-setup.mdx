## How the five steps fix an access ruling for every SKU

S3 gives every SKU one access ruling with no ambiguity left. The output is an access grading sheet, HS classification conclusions grouped by category (for example 0404.9000) and five document sets. The method is five steps in sequence: list check, HS classification, CIQ confirmation, test reports and filing submitted; a ruling is fixed per SKU, documents are matched by category, the two to six weeks depend on conditions, and every SKU's ruling is written into its cell in the grading sheet. The sheet has one row per SKU, recording the SKU code, product name, category, access grade, HS classification conclusion, declaration elements and filing status; the grade decides which channel the product takes.

The difference between two and six weeks comes from the category. SKUs already on the list, with a clear HS classification and a CIQ confirmation that needs no extra documents, move quickly; those needing test reports or filing supplements wait for the supplements to come back. The tax treatment in Cai Guan Shui [2016] No. 18 is also checked by category at this step. The cross-border tax rate note has to appear on the storefront by category, so the category must be fixed first before the line on the product detail page can be filled in.

## Why the independent store comes first and the platform store only supplements it

This step makes a decision for you: open the independent store first, or enter a platform store first. The two paths carry different accounts. The independent store (S) keeps both autonomy and data with you, you set the rules, and who the member is, what they bought and how often they came back all sit in your own system. The platform store (P) has ready traffic and spares you a cold start, but the platform sets the rules and categories, campaigns and data interfaces all follow it. Our advice is to open the independent store first and place the platform store later as a supplementary channel.

The reason, plainly: if you are only trying two or three SKUs, the platform store's ready traffic is convenient, but the account sits with the platform and the data is not exposed to you, so during the trial you never get your own member profile and you give away the very thing you should be accumulating. The other way round, if you have decided to build in China for the long term, member and order data are assets that will follow you for many years, and the independent store is worth standing up first even if it is slower.

Account and data ownership is pinned down in three lines: the account is opened in your company's name and is not locked to any service provider or platform; order and member data stay onshore and belong to you, handled under the requirements of the Personal Information Protection Law; if a platform really must hold the entity during a transition, the two sides' duties are written out item by item in the agreement, who collects, who stores, who exports, with no grey area. The store, the account and the data are all registered in your name; we handle the build, the opening and day-to-day maintenance, while signature authority and ownership stay on your side.

## What to check row by row across the seven storefront fields before launch

Before launch there is one more item-by-item check, using a storefront page field verification sheet: one field per row, with tabs for all pages, store home and product detail, and each row recording the page, the field, whether it is shown, the validation rule, the owner and the status. The two store home rows are the focus: the cross-border operating entity label must match the filing entity, owned by the operations role; the risk disclosure entry must be visible before the order is placed, owned by the compliance role. The sheet checks one and the same thing, that the account is in your name and the data stays in your hands, and that every storefront field is checked item by item before launch.

Of the seven rows, the efficacy claim statement is the one most often only half done. The Chinese electronic label has to contain ingredients and directions for use, and country of origin and specification have to match the filing information; those two line up if you simply copy the filing material. The efficacy claim statement requires that claims do not exceed the filed scope, so whoever writes it must first know what the filing approved. Otherwise one extra line about improving sleep goes onto the detail page, the row is still ticked on the verification sheet, and later there is no basis to cite when a question comes after launch. The cross-border tax rate note is displayed by category, following the tax treatment in Cai Guan Shui [2016] No. 18; the purchase limit and quota notice has to be visible before ordering, the same requirement as the risk disclosure entry. On the right of the table there are also detail and log entries, which show who changed the status to verified and when, so the record can be found afterwards.

## Walking one can of whey protein powder through

Take one 400 g can of concentrated whey protein powder, SKU code 20261015007, category protein powder, HS classification conclusion 0404.9000. First find this category on the cross-border retail e-commerce import goods list, then set the classification conclusion at 0404.9000. CIQ confirmation checks whether the overseas producer and the product are on the access list. Test reports are issued as the category requires, with ingredients and directions for use matching the Chinese electronic label. Finally the filing is submitted under GACC Announcement No. 194 of 2018 and the status lands on this row of the grading sheet. With this can completed, the access ruling is four short statements: the category is on the list, classification 0404.9000, five document sets complete, filing submitted.

Then check the two storefront rows: fill the cross-border operating entity label with the name of your company's filing entity, word for word with the filing receipt, signed off by the operations role; place the risk disclosure entry above the order button, and check the five product detail rows together, Chinese electronic label, country of origin and specification, efficacy claim statement, cross-border tax rate note, and purchase limit and quota notice, confirmed separately by compliance and operations. Once all seven rows pass, run one test order through the payment channel, and this can is done from access ruling to being orderable.
