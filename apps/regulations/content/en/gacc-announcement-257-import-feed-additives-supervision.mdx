From 29 December 2025, GACC has optimized its inspection and quarantine supervision measures for imported feed and feed additives in accordance with the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported and Exported Feed and Feed Additives and the results of risk analysis, and has published the scope of risk-level classification and the corresponding list of measures.

The new list classifies imported feed and feed additives by risk level, with different inspection and quarantine supervision measures applying to each level; importing enterprises should verify declaration, inspection and documentation requirements against the risk level of their products.

The announcement takes effect on the date of publication, and AQSIQ Announcement No. 144 of 2015 is repealed at the same time; matters previously handled under the old announcement should be adjusted in line with the new list.

In accordance with the provisions of the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported and Exported Feed and Feed Additives, and on the basis of the results of risk analysis, GACC has optimized the inspection and quarantine supervision measures for imported feed and feed additives, and hereby publishes the Scope of Risk-Level Classification of Imported Feed and Feed Additives and the Corresponding List of Inspection and Quarantine Supervision Measures (see annex).

This announcement takes effect on the date of publication. AQSIQ Announcement No. 144 of 2015 is repealed at the same time.

It is hereby announced.
