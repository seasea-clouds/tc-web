On 12 August 2026 the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Ecology and Environment repealed the 1996 Notice on Issues Concerning Inspection of Imported Waste (Guojian Jianlian [1996] No. 115) by Announcement 120 of 2026. The announcement took effect on the date of publication, and imported waste inspection is no longer based on that notice.

The repealed notice had set the declaration, inspection and supervision requirements for imported waste. Only that notice is repealed; the laws, licences, port inspection and environmental protection requirements for solid waste imports continue to apply under current rules, and the announcement leaves no transition period.

In practice, companies should obtain permits and go through port inspection under the current solid waste import regime, and check port and carrier requirements in advance for existing contracts and cargo already in transit; this should not be read as a blanket ban on all imported waste.

This announcement takes effect on the date of publication.

It is hereby announced.
