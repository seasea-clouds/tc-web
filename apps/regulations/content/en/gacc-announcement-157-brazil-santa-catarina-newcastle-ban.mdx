From 6 November 2026, the ban covers the entire state of Santa Catarina. Poultry and related products may not be imported directly or indirectly; the scope includes products derived from poultry that are unprocessed or processed but still capable of spreading disease. Poultry from other Brazilian states remains outside this state-level ban and subject to existing quarantine rules.

Postal delivery and baggage carriage are also prohibited; intercepted items are returned or destroyed. Animal and plant waste and swill unloaded from inbound transport means from the state must be treated under customs supervision and may not be discarded without authorization. Illegally imported goods intercepted by border inspection and other departments are destroyed under customs supervision.

Before declaring, check the state of origin, departure date and official quarantine certificate to confirm the goods are outside the ban; for processed products, assess the processing status and raw-material origin. Violations are handled by customs under the Customs Law, Biosecurity Law, and the Entry-Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and its implementing regulations.

<!-- original -->

The Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock of Brazil notified the World Organisation for Animal Health that an outbreak of Newcastle disease has occurred in the state of Santa Catarina, Brazil. To prevent the introduction of the disease and protect China's animal husbandry safety and biosecurity, the following is announced in accordance with relevant laws and regulations:

I. It is prohibited to import poultry and related products directly or indirectly from Santa Catarina, Brazil (products derived from poultry that are unprocessed, or processed but still capable of spreading the disease).

II. It is prohibited to send by post or carry poultry and related products from Santa Catarina, Brazil into China. Any items found shall be returned or destroyed.

III. Animal and plant waste, swill and other materials unloaded from inbound transport means from Santa Catarina, Brazil shall be subjected to harmless treatment under customs supervision and may not be discarded without authorization.

IV. Poultry and related products from Santa Catarina, Brazil that entered China illegally and are intercepted by border inspection and other departments shall be destroyed under customs supervision.

V. Violators shall be dealt with by customs in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations.

VI. Customs at all levels and agriculture and rural affairs departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the Customs Law, the Biosecurity Law, the Entry-Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law, cooperate closely and carry out quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision.

This announcement takes effect on the date of publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs　Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

Issued on 6 November 2026
