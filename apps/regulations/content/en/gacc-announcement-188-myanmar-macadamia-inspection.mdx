Myanmar macadamia nuts have gained access to the Chinese market. Announcement No. 188 of 2025 of the General Administration of Customs takes effect immediately. In-shell edible nuts grown, dehusked, dried and sorted within Myanmar are approved for import.

Two checks before shipment: producers must be inspected and recommended by Myanmar and registered in China, and unregistered producers may not export; raw materials must come from plantations filed with Myanmar. The nuts must not carry four quarantine pests of concern and must be free of live insects and impurities.

Each batch must be accompanied by a phytosanitary certificate with the prescribed English declaration; packaging must be marked in Chinese or English with the China-bound information, registration number and traceability details. On arrival at the port, customs will carry out inspection and quarantine, and non-compliant goods will be handled in accordance with the law.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the relevant provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Irrigation of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar (hereinafter referred to as the Myanmar side) on inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for macadamia nuts exported from Myanmar to China, as of today, the import of macadamia nuts from Myanmar that meet the following requirements is permitted.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(2) Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection and Quarantine of Entry and Exit Animals and Plants and its implementing regulations;

(3) Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations;

(4) Measures for the Safety Administration of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China;

(5) Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Macadamia Nuts to Be Exported from Myanmar to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Irrigation of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Products Approved for Import

Macadamia nuts from Myanmar approved for import refer to in-shell nuts for human consumption made from the fruit of macadamia (Macadamia integrifolia) grown in Myanmar and processed in Myanmar through processes such as dehusking, drying and sorting.

III. Food Safety Requirements

Macadamia nuts exported to China shall comply with China's food safety and plant quarantine laws and regulations, national food safety standards and the provisions of the Protocol.

IV. Phytosanitary Requirements

(1) Macadamia nuts exported to China must not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: Trogoderma granarium Everts, Mikania micrantha Kunth, Cenchrus echinatus L., and Chromolaena odorata L. (Eupatorium odoratum L.).

(2) Macadamia nuts exported to China shall be dehydrated using drying equipment such as ovens or dryers, and must be free of live insects and added or mixed impurities. During processing, insect-damaged nuts, mouldy nuts, husks, weed seeds and the like shall be removed.

V. Requirements for Enterprises

The Myanmar side shall inspect production, processing and storage facilities for macadamia nuts exported to China in accordance with the Chinese side's relevant requirements for the registration of overseas food production enterprises, and recommend qualifying enterprises to the Chinese side. The Chinese side shall register the qualifying enterprises.

Only after an enterprise has obtained registration in China may its products be exported to China. The list of registered Myanmar enterprises exporting macadamia nuts to China can be found on the website of the General Administration of Customs.

VI. Requirements for Raw Materials

Raw materials for macadamia nuts exported to China shall come from planting enterprises that have been confirmed by the Myanmar side as meeting relevant phytosanitary and food safety requirements and have been registered.

VII. Requirements for the Processing

The Myanmar side shall provide guidance and supervision over the planting of raw materials, production, processing, storage, transport, export and other processes of macadamia nuts exported to China, to ensure compliance with the relevant safety and sanitary requirements of both China and Myanmar and to prevent contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

VIII. Requirements for Packaging and Labelling

Macadamia nuts exported to China shall be packaged in clean, sanitary new materials that meet food safety and phytosanitary requirements, and leakage shall be prevented during transport. Each package shall be marked in Chinese or English with “This product is exported to the People's Republic of China”, as well as traceability information such as the place of origin of the macadamia nuts, the name of the production and processing enterprise and its registration number in China, and the name and address of the exporter. The above information may be affixed to the packaging in the form of a label.

IX. Requirements for Transport

Before shipment, the Myanmar side shall inspect the means of transport for macadamia nuts exported to China. The means of transport shall meet sanitary and epidemic prevention requirements and shall not be mixed with quarantine pests or other restricted quarantine objects, such as weed seeds, live insects, plant debris, soil, and other extraneous impurities.

X. Requirements for Certificates

Before export, the Myanmar side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for macadamia nuts exported to China that have passed inspection and quarantine and meet the requirements of the Protocol. The additional declaration column of the certificate shall state the name of the macadamia nut production and processing enterprise, its registration number in China, and “The batch of macadamia nuts certified by this phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Macadamia Nuts to Be Exported from Myanmar to China signed by Myanmar and China on August 30th,2025. The batch of macadamia nuts is free from any quarantine pests of concern to China, such as Trogoderma granarium Everts, Mikania micrantha Kunth, Cenchrus echinatus L., and Chromolaena odorata L.(Eupatorium odoratum L.)”

XI. Entry Inspection and Quarantine and Handling of Non-Compliance

(1) Entry inspection and quarantine. After macadamia nuts exported to China arrive at the entry port in China, China Customs will carry out inspection and quarantine supervision, and entry will be permitted once they pass.

(2) Handling of non-compliance. Should the Chinese side find any violation of China's laws and regulations on food safety and plant quarantine, national food safety standards and the provisions of the Protocol in macadamia nuts exported to China, China Customs will handle the matter in accordance with China's relevant laws and regulations.

This announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs

22 September 2025
