Announcement No. 20 of 2026 publishes customs supervision measures for Hetao Shenzhen Park.

The Measures have five chapters and twenty-five articles governing the supervision zone and movements of goods and persons.

Customs conducts quarantine, inspection, credit management and audits, ensuring data security and inter-agency information sharing.

<!-- original -->

To implement the Development Plan for the Hetao Shenzhen-Hong Kong Science and Technology Innovation Cooperation Zone and support its high-quality development, GACC has formulated the Customs Supervision Measures for the Shenzhen Park, hereby published.

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

9 February 2026

Measures of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China for the Supervision of the Shenzhen Park of the Hetao Shenzhen-Hong Kong Science and Technology Innovation Cooperation Zone

Chapter I General Provisions Article 1 To support the high-quality development of the Shenzhen Park (hereinafter the Park) and regulate customs supervision, these Measures are formulated under the Customs Law, the Law on Import and Export Commodity Inspection, the Law on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law, the Food Safety Law and other laws, administrative regulations and national policies.

Article 2 Customs supervises, under these Measures, the following objects in the customs supervision area subject to specific closed management in the Park (hereinafter the supervised area): (i) means of transport, transport equipment, persons, goods and articles entering or leaving between the supervised area and countries/regions outside the customs territory (hereinafter abroad); (ii) goods subject to customs supervision entering or leaving between the supervised area and other parts of the customs territory (hereinafter outside the zone); (iii) goods in the supervised area with customs formalities not yet completed.

Article 3 The supervised area shall have facilities, equipment and information systems meeting customs supervision requirements. Customs may share government data across departments based on the public information service platform established by the Park's comprehensive management agency, in line with lawful government duties and data security.

Article 4 Customs conducts trade statistics on goods entering or leaving between the supervised area and abroad, except as otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations; conducts single-item customs statistics on goods entering or leaving between the supervised area and outside the zone as needed; and conducts customs business statistics on supervision activities and internal management matters related to the supervised area as needed.

Chapter II Supervision of Movement between the Supervised Area and Abroad Article 5 Customs lawfully quarantines means of transport, transport equipment, persons, goods and articles entering or leaving between the supervised area and abroad.

Article 6 For special goods and articles relating to public health safety, such as human tissues including blood, pathogenic microorganisms and biological products that have obtained Hong Kong biological substance permits (special goods and articles), Customs gives priority to health quarantine approval; for those risk-assessed through the Shenzhen joint supervision mechanism, the approval process is optimised accordingly based on the assessment conclusion. Pathogens with unknown biosecurity risk or special goods and articles that may cause public health emergencies are excluded.

Article 7 Consignors, consignees or their agents of goods entering or leaving between the supervised area and abroad shall declare truthfully to Customs and accept customs supervision.

Article 8 Goods entering or leaving via the dedicated non-freight cross-border passage between the Park and Hong Kong, carried by designated persons with the consent of the competent customs office, shall be declared in writing by the consignor, consignee or agent, and supervised by Customs as import and export goods.

Article 9 Customs supervises means of transport entering or leaving between the supervised area and abroad under the rules on entry and exit means of transport and manifests.

Article 10 Customs supervises articles entering or leaving between the supervised area and abroad under the rules on entry and exit articles.

Chapter III Supervision of Movement between the Supervised Area and Outside the Zone Article 11 Customs does not quarantine goods or articles entering or leaving between the supervised area and outside the zone.

Article 12 Consignors, consignees or their agents of goods entering or leaving between the supervised area and outside the zone shall complete relevant customs formalities as prescribed.

Article 13 Customs applies convenient in-and-out-zone management to goods entering the supervised area from outside the zone after import taxes have been paid or supplemented and licences and relevant documents submitted, and to domestic circulation goods entering the supervised area from outside the zone.

Article 14 For goods transported from the supervised area to outside the zone for testing, repair or display, the consignor, consignee or agent shall complete relevant customs formalities; the period shall not exceed the contract validity and the goods shall be returned to the supervised area on time.

Chapter IV Supervision within the Supervised Area Article 15 For import and export enterprises and units in the supervised area connected to Customs through enterprise resource planning (ERP) and other information systems, Customs may apply corresponding information-based supervision methods.

Article 16 Customs applies credit management to enterprises and units in the supervised area, granting clearance facilitation to trustworthy and law-abiding entities and imposing sanctions and stricter supervision on dishonest or law-breaking entities.

Article 17 Customs conducts audits and verifications of enterprises and units directly related to import and export goods in the supervised area; the entities shall cooperate and provide account books, documents and electronic data.

Chapter V Supplementary Provisions Article 18 For goods and articles entering or leaving between the supervised area and abroad or outside the zone that are subject to national prohibition or restriction measures, Customs supervises them under the relevant rules.

Article 19 Customs lawfully inspects goods entering or leaving between the supervised area and abroad or outside the zone; based on quality and safety risk assessment, Customs may apply differentiated conformity assessment.

Article 20 Tax collection and administration on goods entering or leaving the supervised area and circulating within it follows the relevant rules.

Article 21 For goods or products subject to tariff quota management, trade remedy measures, suspension of tariff concessions, additional duties, or additional duties for retaliatory purposes (excluding those excluded), Customs supervises them and applies the relevant measures under national rules.

Article 22 Customs supervises bonded import and export businesses in the supervised area under bonded supervision rules; damage, loss, destruction, storage periods and disposal of solid waste in the area follow the rules for comprehensive bonded zones.

Article 23 Customs supervision in the supervised area does not affect local governments and other departments performing their duties lawfully.

Article 24 These Measures are interpreted by GACC.

Article 25 These Measures shall take effect on 10 February 2026.
