NMPA Announcement No. 52 of 2026 clarifies the requirements for continuity between registration and filing after changes to the management category or management attribute of medical devices.

The announcement is divided into four parts, covering the validity period of original registration certificates, initial registration, renewal registration, filing changes, and the mutual conversion between product filing and registration.

Those that have not obtained a registration certificate or completed Class I medical device filing by the expiry of the transition period may not produce, distribute, or import the relevant products.

NMPA Announcement on Matters Concerning the Adjustment of Medical Device Classification (No. 52 of 2026)

To strengthen the classified administration of medical devices and, in accordance with the Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Medical Devices, the Measures for the Administration of Medical Device Registration and Filing, the Measures for the Administration of the Registration and Filing of In Vitro Diagnostic Reagents, and other relevant requirements, to further clarify the work relating to registration and filing after the adjustment of the management attribute or management category of medical device products (including in vitro diagnostic reagents, the same below), the following matters are hereby announced:

I. Requirements Concerning Registration and Filing after Adjustment of Medical Device Management Categories

(1) On the validity period of original medical device registration certificates. Where the document adjusting the management category contains special provisions on the validity period of original medical device registration certificates, those provisions shall be followed; where there are no special provisions, this announcement shall apply.

(2) On initial registration. From the date of the management category adjustment, product registration applications for medical devices submitted for the first time shall be accepted in accordance with the adjusted category.

For initial registration applications accepted before the date of the management category adjustment but not yet decided, the drug regulatory department may continue the review and approval in accordance with the original management category; where registration is approved, the adjusted management category shall be noted in the remarks column of the registration certificate, and, where a registration transition period applies to the management category adjustment, the validity period of the medical device registration certificate shall also be limited so as not to exceed the expiry date of the registration transition period.

After the date on which the registrant obtains the medical device registration certificate for the changed category, the registrant shall voluntarily apply for cancellation of the original medical device registration certificate; where the registrant does not do so voluntarily, the original registration department shall carry out the cancellation procedure.

(3) On renewal registration. For renewal registration applications accepted before the date of the management category adjustment but not yet decided, the drug regulatory department may continue the review and approval in accordance with the original management category; where renewal registration is approved, the adjusted product management category shall be noted in the remarks column of the registration certificate, and, where a registration transition period applies to the management category adjustment, the validity period of the medical device registration certificate shall also be limited so as not to exceed the expiry date of the registration transition period specified in the relevant document.

For medical device registration certificates approved before the date of the management category adjustment, where the product management category is adjusted from a higher category to a lower category and renewal is required upon expiry of the validity period, the registrant shall apply for renewal registration or complete filing with the corresponding drug regulatory department in accordance with the adjusted category. Where the drug regulatory department approves the renewal registration, it shall issue a medical device registration certificate in accordance with the relevant medical device classification catalogue and the management category adjustment document, and note the number of the original medical device registration certificate in the remarks column of the registration certificate; where the submitted filing materials comply with the Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Medical Devices and other relevant requirements, the filing shall be completed, and the number of the original medical device registration certificate shall be noted in the remarks column of the filing information form.

For medical device registration certificates approved before the date of the management category adjustment, where the product management category is adjusted from a lower category to a higher category, the registrant shall apply for registration with the corresponding drug regulatory department in accordance with the changed category. For medical device registration certificates approved before the date of the management category adjustment, the original medical device registration certificate shall not exceed the expiry date of the registration transition period specified in the management category adjustment document; where, with technological development, it becomes clear that the product management category should be a Class III medical device, a Class II medical device registration certificate approved before the date of the management category adjustment shall remain valid within its validity period. Where a registration application is submitted in accordance with the adjusted higher category within the validity period of the original medical device registration certificate, and the original medical device registration certificate expires before the expiry date (if any) of the registration transition period for the management category adjustment, the registrant may, on the premise that the product is safe and effective and no serious adverse events or quality accidents have occurred after marketing, apply to the original registration department for renewal registration in accordance with the original management category; where the original registration department, upon review, finds the above conditions met and approves the renewal registration, the adjusted product management category shall be noted in the remarks column of the registration certificate, and the validity period of the medical device registration certificate shall be limited so as not to exceed the expiry date of the registration transition period for the management category adjustment. After the date on which the registrant obtains the medical device registration certificate for the changed category, the registrant shall voluntarily apply for cancellation of the original medical device registration certificate; where the registrant does not do so voluntarily, the original registration department shall carry out the cancellation procedure.

(4) On change registration or filing of registered products. For products that have obtained a medical device registration certificate before the date of the management category adjustment, and products that have obtained renewal registration under the original category after the management category is adjusted in accordance with Article I, paragraph (3) of this announcement, where a change registration or filing is required, the registrant shall apply to the original registration department for change registration or complete filing. Where the drug regulatory department approves the change registration or filing, it shall issue a medical device change registration or change filing document and note the adjusted product management category in the remarks column.

(5) On the adjustment between product filing and registration. For products whose management category is adjusted from a higher category to a Class I medical device, as of the date of the management category adjustment, Class I medical device filing may be completed in accordance with the adjusted category. Among these, for those whose initial registration application was accepted before the date of the management category adjustment but has not yet been decided, the registration applicant may obtain a medical device registration certificate in accordance with Article I, paragraph (2) of this announcement, or may apply to terminate the review, with the relevant registration application materials archived for reference.

For products whose management category is adjusted from a Class I medical device to a higher category, where Class I medical device filing has been completed before the date of the management category adjustment, the filing entity shall apply for registration with the corresponding drug regulatory department in accordance with the changed category and voluntarily cancel the original Class I medical device filing; where the filing has not been voluntarily cancelled as of the expiry date (if any) of the registration transition period for the management category adjustment, the original filing department shall announce the cancellation of the filing.

(6) On changes to product filing. For products for which Class I medical device filing has been completed before the date of the management category adjustment, where, before the expiry date (if any) of the registration transition period for the management category adjustment, the content recorded in the filing information form or the product technical requirements filed has changed and a filing change is required, the filing entity shall make the filing change with the original filing department and submit an explanation of the change and the management category adjustment document, and the drug regulatory department shall record the change in the filing information.

(7) On production, distribution, and import. From the expiry date (if any) of the registration/filing transition period for the management category adjustment, no entity that has not lawfully obtained a medical device registration certificate or completed Class I medical device product filing may produce, distribute, or import.

II. Requirements Concerning Products Adjusted from Not Being Managed as Medical Devices to Being Managed as Medical Devices

For products adjusted from originally not being managed as medical devices to being managed as medical devices, the registration applicant or filing entity shall apply for medical device product registration or complete Class I medical device product filing with the corresponding drug regulatory department; from the expiry date (if any) of the registration/filing transition period, no entity that has not lawfully obtained a medical device registration certificate or completed Class I medical device product filing may produce, distribute, or import.

III. Requirements Concerning Products That, after Adjustment, Are Not Managed as Medical Devices

For products adjusted from being managed as medical devices to not being managed as medical devices, as of the date of the management attribute adjustment, the products shall no longer be regulated as medical devices. The drug regulatory department shall no longer complete medical device product filing or accept medical device registration applications.

For those accepted before the date of the management attribute adjustment for which registration review and approval has not been completed, the drug regulatory department shall terminate the review, and the relevant registration application materials shall be archived for reference.

IV. Other Matters

(1) Where the document adjusting the management category or management attribute contains special provisions on product registration/filing, those provisions shall be followed.

(2) For products for which a registration/filing transition period is established, registrants, filing entities, and relevant enterprises shall actively carry out registration/filing-related work during the transition period, effectively fulfil their primary responsibility for product quality and safety, comprehensively strengthen quality management throughout the product life cycle, and ensure product safety and effectiveness.

(3) For products within their administrative regions whose management category or management attribute has been adjusted, provincial drug regulatory departments shall establish a product management ledger, consolidate enterprises' primary responsibility, strengthen supervision and guidance, and urge and guide registrants, filing entities, and relevant enterprises to carry out their work in accordance with this announcement and the relevant management category or management attribute adjustment documents.

(4) This announcement shall take effect on the date of publication.

Hereby announced.
