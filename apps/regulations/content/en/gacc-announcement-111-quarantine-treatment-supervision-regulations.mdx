From 1 September 2026, GACC Announcement No. 111 of 2026 issues two supervision regulations, on animal and plant quarantine treatment and on hygiene treatment, and repeals No. 115 of 2017 and two other old announcements; where goods require treatment, Customs first reviews the plan, and fumigation and disinfection against pests and diseases must be done by a Customs-approved disinfestation unit.

Live insects are intercepted in a container of imported beans: Customs issues a quarantine treatment notice stating target, reason and method; the implementing unit submits its plan (personnel, technical indicators, chemicals, protective gear), notifies time and place beforehand, keeps at least 2 persons at the fumigation site, then evaluates efficacy and submits the result report form.

Self-implementation or third-party entrustment is allowed; the unit must be capable, with trained personnel, under Customs supervision; mark affixing on export wooden packaging, in-process treatment by manufacturers and return measures do not apply; if work starts before plan review, the results may not be recognized.

<!-- original -->

To standardize and strengthen the supervision and administration of entry and exit quarantine treatment, and in accordance with the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its Implementing Regulations, the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China and its Implementation Rules and other relevant laws and regulations, the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (GACC) has formulated the Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Quarantine Treatment for Entry and Exit Animals and Plants (Annex 1) and the Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Entry and Exit Hygiene Treatment (Annex 2), which are hereby promulgated.

This Announcement shall be implemented as of 1 September 2026, and Announcement No. 115 of 2017 of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine (AQSIQ), Announcement No. 30 of 2018 of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine, and Announcement No. 77 of 2022 of the General Administration of Customs are repealed at the same time.

It is hereby announced.

Annexes: (1) Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Quarantine Treatment for Entry and Exit Animals and Plants

(2) Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Entry and Exit Hygiene Treatment

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China

31 July 2026
