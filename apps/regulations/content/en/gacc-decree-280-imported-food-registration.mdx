The General Administration of Customs of China (GACC) published Decree No. 280 on 14 October 2025. It replaces Decree No. 248 and applies from **1 June 2026**.

## Who it applies to

Every overseas company that produces, processes or stores food for export to China must be registered with GACC. Food additives and food-contact materials are outside the scope of this decree.

## What changed in practice

- The five-year registration validity is retained.
- GACC publishes and maintains the catalogue of food categories that require **recommendation by the competent authority** of the exporting country. It currently covers meat and meat products, casings, edible bird's nests, bee products, eggs and egg products, edible oils and fats, stuffed pasta, cereals, cereal flours and malt, dehydrated vegetables, seasoning powders, nuts and seeds, dried fruit, foods for special dietary uses, health foods, dairy products and aquatic products.
- A separate list sets out the categories that are **not eligible for automatic renewal**; companies in those categories must apply again before expiry.
- The scope of overseas storage facilities that must be registered is defined in the same decree and is adjusted dynamically.

## What exporters should do now

1. Check whether your product falls under the official-recommendation catalogue — if so, the application must go through your national competent authority, not directly to GACC.
2. Confirm the registration expiry date of each facility; renewal reminders are the company's responsibility, not the authority's.
3. Keep the registration number and approved product scope consistent with what appears on shipping documents and labels.
