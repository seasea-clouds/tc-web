Bulgarian saffron has been approved for export to China. Announcement No. 111 of 2025 of the General Administration of Customs permits imports that meet the requirements as of today; saffron means the dried stigmas of Crocus sativus L. from the saffron producing areas of Bulgaria.

The planting bases and processing and packaging plants must be audited by the Bulgarian side and approved and registered by the Chinese side, with names, addresses and registration numbers. The product must not carry the nine quarantine pests of concern to China, packaging must be labelled in Chinese and English, and wood packaging must meet ISPM 15.

Before export the Bulgarian side must inspect the goods and issue a phytosanitary certificate under ISPM 12 with an additional declaration confirming compliance with the protocol. On entry documents and goods are checked; failed consignments are refused, destroyed or treated, and violations may suspend the companies exports to China.

Announcement No. 111 of 2025

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Bulgarian Saffron to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People’s Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Food of the Republic of Bulgaria (hereinafter referred to as the Bulgarian side), imports of Bulgarian saffron that meet the following relevant requirements are permitted as of today.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) The Biosafety Law of the People’s Republic of China;

(2) The Law of the People’s Republic of China on Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(3) The Food Safety Law of the People’s Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(4) The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Quarantine of Chinese Medicinal Materials for Import and Export;

(5) The Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Bulgarian Saffron to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People’s Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Food of the Republic of Bulgaria.

II. Names of Commodities Permitted for Entry

For the purposes of this announcement, saffron means the dried stigmas of Crocus sativus L. of the family Iridaceae, known in English as Saffron.

III. Permitted Places of Origin

The saffron producing areas of Bulgaria.

IV. Enterprise Registration

The planting bases and processing and packaging plants of saffron for export to China must be audited by the Bulgarian side and approved and registered by the Chinese side. Registration information shall include the name, address and registration number, so that accurate traceability can be ensured when exported goods do not comply with the relevant provisions of this announcement. Before trade begins, the Bulgarian side shall provide the Chinese side with the list of registered establishments, which shall be reviewed and approved by the Chinese side and published on the website of the General Administration of Customs.

V. List of Quarantine Pests of Concern to China

(1) Heterobostrychus aequalis

(2) Prostephanus truncates

(3) Trogoderma granarium

(4) Ditylenchus dipsaci

(5) Pratylenchus crenatus

(6) Meloidogyne brevicauda

(7) Myzus persicae

(8) Lepidium draba

(9) Burkholderia gladioli

VI. Pre export Management

(1) Requirements for Planting Bases.

(1) The planting bases of saffron for export to China shall, under the supervision of the Bulgarian side, establish a sound quality management system and traceability management system, implement good agricultural practices (GAP), maintain growing hygiene conditions such as the absence of pollution sources affecting saffron production around the site and the timely removal of diseased plant debris, and implement integrated pest management (IPM), including pest monitoring and surveys, physical, chemical or biological control, and agronomic measures.

(2) The planting bases of saffron for export to China shall, under the supervision of the Bulgarian side, carry out pest monitoring and integrated management targeting the quarantine pests of concern to China. Pest monitoring and control shall be carried out under the guidance of professional and technical personnel, who shall receive training from the Bulgarian side or its authorised bodies.

(3) The planting bases of saffron for export to China shall keep records of pest monitoring and control for at least two years and shall provide them to the Chinese side upon request. Such records shall include at least the monitoring time, the names of pests found, the control measures taken, and detailed information such as the date of use, active ingredients and concentrations of the chemicals used.

(2) Management of Processing and Packaging Plants.

(1) The processing, packaging, storage and shipment of saffron for export to China shall be carried out under the quarantine supervision of the Bulgarian side or its authorised officials.

(2) The processing and packaging plants of saffron for export to China shall be clean and hygienic, with hardened floors, and shall have raw material yards and finished product warehouses.

(3) The functional areas for storage, processing, packaging and other operations of saffron for export to China shall be relatively independent and rationally laid out, and shall be separated from living areas by appropriate isolation measures and at a suitable distance.

(4) During processing, saffron for export to China shall undergo sorting, screening, drying and other processes to ensure that it is free of soil, weed seeds or other pests.

(5) The processing and packaging plants shall establish a traceability system to ensure that saffron for export to China can be traced for information such as processing and packaging date, name of source planting base, export date, export quantity, importing country, and means of transport or container number.

(3) Packaging Requirements.

(1) Packaging materials shall be clean, hygienic, unused and comply with the relevant phytosanitary requirements of China. If wood packaging is used, it shall comply with the requirements of the International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15).

(2) Each packaging box shall be labelled in Chinese or English with traceability information such as product name, country, place of origin, name or registration number of the planting base, and name or registration number of the processing and packaging plant. Each packaging box shall be marked in Chinese and English with „Exported to the People’s Republic of China“.

(3) Means of transport or containers carrying saffron for export to China must be checked at the time of loading to ensure good hygienic conditions. Means of transport or containers shall be sealed, and the seals shall be intact on arrival at the Chinese entry port.

(4) Pre export Inspection and Quarantine.

(1) The Bulgarian side shall carry out pre export inspection of saffron for export to China to ensure that it is free of the quarantine pests of concern to China.

(2) If quarantine pests of concern to China, plant debris or soil are found, the whole consignment shall not be exported to China.

(5) Phytosanitary Certificate Requirements.

(1) For consignments that pass inspection, the Bulgarian side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate in accordance with the International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12 (ISPM 12), indicating the names or registration numbers of the planting base and the processing and packaging plant and the container number, and shall fill in the following additional declaration: „This consignment complies with the requirements specified in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Saffrons from Bulgaria to China, and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China.“ (This consignment complies with the requirements of the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Bulgarian Saffron to China and is free of the quarantine pests of concern to China.)

(2) Before the project starts, the Bulgarian side shall provide the Chinese side with a specimen of the phytosanitary certificate for confirmation and record filing.

VII. Entry Inspection and Quarantine and Handling of Non Compliance

When saffron for export to China arrives at the Chinese entry port, the Chinese customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine in accordance with the following requirements.

(1) Verification of Certificates and Marks.

(1) Verify that the phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of item (5) of Article VI of this announcement.

(2) Verify that the marks on packaging boxes and pallets comply with the provisions of item (3) of Article VI of this announcement.

(2) Entry Inspection and Quarantine.

In accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules, imported saffron shall be subject to inspection and quarantine, and those that pass inspection and quarantine shall be allowed to enter.

(3) Handling of Non Compliance.

(1) If the goods are found to come from unregistered planting bases or processing and packaging plants, the consignment shall not be allowed to enter.

(2) If live quarantine pests of concern to China or other quarantine pests, or soil, plant debris and the like are found, the consignment shall be refused, destroyed or treated.

(3) If the goods are found not to comply with Chinese food and drug safety laws and regulations, national standards and requirements, the consignment shall be refused or destroyed.

(4) If any of the above non compliance is found, the Chinese side shall immediately notify the Bulgarian side and, as appropriate, suspend imports of saffron from the relevant planting bases and processing and packaging plants. The Bulgarian side shall identify the causes of non compliance and take corrective measures to prevent similar cases from recurring. The Chinese side shall decide whether to lift the suspension based on the results of the corrective measures taken by the Bulgarian side.

This announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs

3 June 2025
