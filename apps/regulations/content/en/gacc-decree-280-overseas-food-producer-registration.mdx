On 14 October 2025, GACC published the revised Administrative Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food (GACC Decree No. 280). They take effect on 1 June 2026 and repeal the 2021 provisions issued under Decree No. 248. Overseas manufacturers, processors and storage operators exporting food to China all register under these provisions.

They are separate from the filing regime for importers and exporters (GACC Announcement No. 150 of 2026): these provisions cover overseas food manufacturers, which are recommended by the competent authority of their country or apply on their own.

Key points: registration is valid for 5 years and renews automatically, leaving about 95% of registered firms unaffected; meat and meat products, and bird's nest products on the non-automatic-renewal list must apply for renewal 3 to 12 months before expiry; a list-registration fast track is added; overseas storage cold warehouses are brought into registration; the registration number in China is mandatory at customs declaration.

## GACC Decree No. 280 of the People's Republic of China

The Administrative Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food were deliberated and adopted at the executive meeting of GACC on 22 September 2025, are hereby promulgated and shall take effect on 1 June 2026. The Administrative Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food promulgated by GACC Decree No. 248 on 12 April 2021 are simultaneously repealed.

 Commissioner Sun Meijun

14 October 2025

## Administrative Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food

(Adopted at the executive meeting of GACC on 22 September 2025, promulgated by GACC Decree No. 280 on 14 October 2025, effective from 1 June 2026)

### Chapter I General Provisions

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations, the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Special Provisions of the State Council on Strengthening the Supervision and Administration of Food and Other Products, in order to strengthen the registration administration of overseas food manufacturers of imported food.

**Article 2** These Provisions apply to the registration administration of overseas manufacturers, processors and storage operators (hereinafter collectively referred to as overseas food manufacturers of imported food) that export food into China.

The overseas food manufacturers of imported food under the preceding paragraph do not include manufacturers, processors or storage operators of food additives or food-related products.

**Article 3** GACC uniformly administers the registration of overseas food manufacturers of imported food.

**Article 4** Overseas food manufacturers of imported food shall be registered by GACC.

**Article 5** GACC applies the risk management principle and, based on the assessment and review of the food safety management system and food safety situation of the country (region) where an overseas food manufacturer of imported food is located, combined with the risk level of the relevant food, implements classified administration over such manufacturers, determining the corresponding registration method, application materials, review procedures and other registration administration requirements.

Where risk assessment shows, or there is evidence that, the food safety risk of imported food has changed, GACC may adjust the corresponding registration administration requirements.

### Chapter II Registration Conditions and Procedures

**Article 6** GACC determines, and publishes, the catalogue of imported food subject to registration upon official recommendation (hereinafter the catalogue) based on the analysis of factors such as the raw material sources, production and processing techniques, historical food safety data, consumer groups and consumption methods of the food, in light of international practice.

**Article 7** Conditions for registration of an overseas food manufacturer of imported food:

(1) It is established with the approval of and under the effective supervision of the competent authority of its country (region);

(2) It has established an effective food safety and hygiene management and protection system, produces and exports legally in its country (region), and ensures that food exported to China complies with Chinese laws, regulations and national food safety standards;

(3) It complies with the relevant inspection and quarantine requirements agreed between GACC and the competent authority of its country (region).

An overseas food manufacturer of food listed in the catalogue shall also obtain the recommendation of the competent authority of its country (region).

**Article 8** For an overseas food manufacturer of food listed in the catalogue, the competent authority of its country (region) shall conduct an audit and inspection of the enterprise and, where it meets the registration requirements, issue an audit and inspection report and a letter of recommendation.

**Article 9** An overseas food manufacturer of imported food shall submit the following application materials to GACC on its own or through an entrusted agent:

(1) Enterprise registration application information;

(2) Enterprise identity documents, such as the business licence issued by the competent authority of its country (region) or a certificate issued thereby;

(3) An enterprise statement committing to comply with these Provisions.

An overseas food manufacturer of food listed in the catalogue shall also submit the audit and inspection report and letter of recommendation issued by the competent authority of its country (region).

Where necessary, GACC may require the enterprise to provide materials on its food safety and hygiene and protection system, production type, production capacity, etc.

**Article 10** Enterprise registration application information shall include the enterprise name, the country (region) of location, the address of production premises, the legal representative, the contact person, contact details, the registration number approved by the competent authority of the country (region) of location, the food categories for which registration is applied for, etc.

**Article 11** Registration application materials shall be in Chinese or English.

The competent authority of the country (region) and the overseas food manufacturer of imported food shall be responsible for the authenticity, completeness and legality of the relevant materials.

**Article 12** GACC, based on the risk level of imported food, on its own or by entrusting relevant institutions, conducts assessment and review of overseas food manufacturers of imported food applying for registration through document review, video inspection, on-site inspection or combinations thereof.

Overseas food manufacturers of imported food and the competent authority of the country (region) shall assist in the above assessment and review.

**Article 13** Based on the assessment and review, GACC registers overseas food manufacturers of imported food that meet the requirements, assigns them a registration number in China and notifies them in writing; those that do not meet the requirements are not registered and are notified in writing.

**Article 14** A registered enterprise, when exporting food into China, shall mark on the food packaging its registration number in China or the registration number approved by the competent authority of its country (region).

**Article 15** The registration of an overseas food manufacturer of imported food is valid for 5 years.

When registering an overseas food manufacturer of imported food, GACC shall determine the start and end dates of the validity period.

**Article 16** GACC uniformly publishes the list of registered overseas food manufacturers of imported food.

**Article 17** Where the food safety management system of the country (region) where an overseas food manufacturer of imported food is located has been recognised by GACC, and one of the following circumstances applies, GACC may agree in writing with the competent authority of that country (region) to adopt the list registration method for its enterprises:

(1) An import and export food safety cooperation agreement has been signed with GACC;

(2) Agreements, memorandums of understanding, joint statements or other cooperation documents including food safety cooperation content have been signed with China;

(3) Other circumstances where GACC considers, after risk assessment, that the list registration method may be adopted.

**Article 18** Where the list registration method is adopted, the competent authority of the country (region) shall submit the following materials to GACC:

(1) A list of food manufacturers recommended for registration in China;

(2) The application information listed in Article 10 of these Provisions;

(3) A statement that the recommended enterprises meet the requirements of Article 7, paragraph 1, of these Provisions;

(4) A statement committing to the continuous fulfilment of the responsibilities agreed in bilateral cooperation documents.

Upon review, GACC registers the enterprises on the list that meet the requirements and assigns them registration numbers in China; enterprises on the list that do not meet the requirements are not registered, and the competent authority of the country (region) is notified in writing.

### Chapter III Supervision and Administration

**Article 19** GACC, based on the risk level of imported food, on its own or by entrusting relevant institutions, conducts reviews of whether registered overseas food manufacturers of imported food continue to meet the registration requirements.

Overseas food manufacturers of imported food and the competent authority of the country (region) shall assist in the above reviews.

**Article 20** Where the registration information of a registered overseas food manufacturer of imported food changes during the validity period, the enterprise shall submit a change application to GACC through the registration application channel, together with the following materials:

(1) A comparison table of the changes in registered items;

(2) Supporting materials relating to the changed information.

Where GACC considers, after evaluation, that the change may be made, it approves the change; where circumstances exist that have a major impact on the enterprise's food safety and hygiene management and protection system, such as relocation of production premises, change of legal representative or change of the registration number granted by the country (region) of location, GACC does not approve the change, notifies the enterprise to apply for registration anew, and the registration number in China ceases to be valid from the date of notification.

**Article 21** The registration of an enterprise is automatically renewed upon expiry of the validity period, for a renewal period of 5 years, except in any of the following circumstances:

(1) The imported food is listed in the list of imported food not subject to automatic renewal;

(2) The enterprise is in a rectification period for failure to meet the registration requirements;

(3) GACC has lawfully suspended the import of relevant food from the country (region) where the overseas food manufacturer of imported food is located.

The list of imported food not subject to automatic renewal under item 1 of the preceding paragraph is published separately by GACC.

**Article 22** An overseas food manufacturer of food listed in the list of food not subject to automatic renewal that needs to renew its registration shall submit a renewal application to GACC through the registration application channel within 3 to 12 months before the expiry of the validity period. Renewal application materials include:

(1) Renewal application information;

(2) A statement committing to continuous compliance with the registration requirements;

(3) An overseas food manufacturer of food listed in the catalogue shall also submit a statement of continuous compliance with the registration requirements issued by the competent authority of its country (region).

GACC renews the registration of enterprises that meet the requirements, extending the validity period by 5 years.

**Article 23** Where a registered overseas food manufacturer of imported food falls under any of the following circumstances, GACC cancels its registration, notifies the enterprise, informs the competent authority of its country (region) and publishes the cancellation:

(1) It failed to apply for renewal of registration as required;

(2) The competent authority of its country (region) or the enterprise itself applies for cancellation;

(3) It no longer meets the requirements of item 1, paragraph 1, Article 7 of these Provisions.

**Article 24** The competent authority of the country (region) where an overseas food manufacturer of imported food is located shall fulfil its food safety supervision responsibilities, effectively supervise registered enterprises, urge them to continuously meet the registration requirements, and upon discovering non-compliance immediately take risk prevention and mitigation measures, suspend the relevant enterprises' food exports to China, report to GACC, and supervise rectification until the registration requirements are met.

When an overseas food manufacturer of imported food discovers non-compliance with the registration requirements, it shall proactively suspend food exports to China and immediately take rectification measures until the rectification meets the registration requirements.

**Article 25** Where GACC discovers that a registered overseas food manufacturer of imported food no longer meets the registration requirements, it shall order rectification within a prescribed period, notify the competent authority of its country (region) to urge rectification, and suspend the import of food from the relevant enterprise during the rectification period.

After completing rectification, the enterprise shall submit a supervised rectification report issued by the competent authority of its country (region) and a written statement of compliance with the registration requirements.

GACC shall review the rectification and, where the requirements are met, resume the import of food from the relevant enterprise.

**Article 26** Where GACC suspends or resumes the import of food from registered overseas food manufacturers of imported food pursuant to Articles 24 and 25 of these Provisions, the list of relevant enterprises is published by GACC.

**Article 27** Where a registered overseas food manufacturer of imported food falls under any of the following circumstances, GACC revokes its registration and publishes an announcement:

(1) A major food safety accident of imported food was caused by the enterprise's own causes;

(2) Food exported to China was found to have food safety problems in entry inspection and quarantine, with serious circumstances;

(3) The enterprise's food safety and hygiene management has major problems and cannot ensure that food exported to China meets safety and hygiene requirements;

(4) It still fails to meet the registration requirements after rectification;

(5) It provided false materials or concealed relevant information;

(6) It refuses to cooperate with GACC in reviews and accident investigations;

(7) It rents out, lends, transfers, trades in or fraudulently uses registration numbers;

(8) Registration was granted to an enterprise that does not qualify for application or does not meet the registration conditions;

(9) Other circumstances where the registration may be revoked by law.

### Chapter IV Supplementary Provisions

**Article 28** Where GACC lawfully suspends the import of relevant food from the country (region) where an overseas food manufacturer of imported food is located, GACC does not accept registration applications from relevant food manufacturers of that country (region) during the suspension period.

**Article 29** Where a country (region) concerned has other agreements with China on the registration administration of overseas food manufacturers of imported food, the agreements of both parties prevail.

**Article 30** The scope of overseas storage operators of imported food subject to registration administration under these Provisions is published separately by GACC.

The registration administration of overseas manufacturers of primary edible agricultural products is formulated separately by GACC.

The administration requirements for overseas food manufacturers in cross-border e-commerce retail imports are handled in accordance with relevant provisions.

**Article 31** The competent authority of the country (region) in these Provisions refers to the official department of the country (region) where the overseas food manufacturer of imported food is located that is responsible for the safety and hygiene supervision of food manufacturers.

**Article 32** These Provisions are interpreted by GACC.

**Article 33** These Provisions take effect on 1 June 2026. The Administrative Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food promulgated by GACC Decree No. 248 on 12 April 2021 are simultaneously repealed.
