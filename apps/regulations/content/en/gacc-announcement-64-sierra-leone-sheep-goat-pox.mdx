Sierra Leone has reported sheep pox and goat pox; bringing sheep, goats and products from Sierra Leone into China is closed off. Announcement No. 64 of 2026 by China's customs and agriculture ministries bans direct or indirect import, posting or carriage, requires cases found to be returned or destroyed, and takes effect on publication.

Animal and plant waste and swill from inbound vehicles from Sierra Leone must be treated under customs supervision and not discarded at will; sheep, goats and products intercepted by border and other agencies on illegal entry are likewise destroyed under customs supervision. Violators are handled by customs under the Customs Law, Biosafety Law, Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and implementing rules.

Coordination is key. Customs and agriculture departments at all levels cooperate under the Customs Law, Biosafety Law, Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and Animal Epidemic Prevention Law on quarantine, prevention and supervision. At ports, the above bans apply.

<!-- original -->

Recently, Sierra Leone reported an outbreak of sheep pox and goat pox to the World Organisation for Animal Health. To prevent the introduction of the disease, protect China's livestock industry and biosecurity, and in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, the following is hereby announced:

The direct or indirect import into China of sheep, goats and their related products from Sierra Leone is prohibited (products derived from sheep or goats that are unprocessed or, although processed, may still transmit the disease).

Posting or carrying sheep, goats and their related products from Sierra Leone into China is prohibited. Once discovered, they are without exception returned or destroyed.

Animal and plant waste, swill and the like unloaded from inbound vehicles arriving from Sierra Leone shall without exception be treated under the supervision of customs and must not be discarded at will.

Sheep, goats and their related products from Sierra Leone intercepted on illegal entry by border inspection and other departments shall without exception be destroyed under the supervision of customs.

Whoever violates the above provisions shall be dealt with by customs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China, the Animal and Plant Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations.

Customs authorities and agriculture and rural affairs departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China, the Animal and Plant Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China, the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law of the People's Republic of China and other regulations, work in close coordination and carry out quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision.

This Announcement takes effect from the date of publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

14 May 2026
