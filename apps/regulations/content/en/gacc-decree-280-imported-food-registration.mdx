The General Administration of Customs of China (GACC) published Decree No. 280 on 14 October 2025. It replaces Decree No. 248 and applies from **1 June 2026**.

## Who it applies to

Every overseas company that produces, processes or stores food for export to China must be registered with GACC. Food additives and food-contact materials are outside the scope of this decree.

## What changed in practice

- The five-year registration validity is retained.
- GACC publishes and maintains the catalogue of food categories that require **recommendation by the competent authority** of the exporting country. It currently covers meat and meat products, casings, edible bird's nests, bee products, eggs and egg products, edible oils and fats, stuffed pasta, cereals, cereal flours and malt, dehydrated vegetables, seasoning powders, nuts and seeds, dried fruit, foods for special dietary uses, health foods, dairy products and aquatic products.
- A separate list sets out the categories that are **not eligible for automatic renewal**; companies in those categories must apply again before expiry.
- The scope of overseas storage facilities that must be registered is defined in the same decree and is adjusted dynamically.

## What exporters should do now

1. Check whether your product falls under the official-recommendation catalogue — if so, the application must go through your national competent authority, not directly to GACC.
2. Confirm the registration expiry date of each facility; renewal reminders are the company's responsibility, not the authority's.
3. Keep the registration number and approved product scope consistent with what appears on shipping documents and labels.

## Chapter I — General provisions

**Article 1**　These Provisions are enacted to strengthen the administration of registration of overseas producers of imported food, in accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations, the Law on Entry-Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, the Special Provisions of the State Council on Strengthening the Supervision and Administration of the Safety of Food and Other Products, and other laws and administrative regulations.

**Article 2**　These Provisions apply to the administration of registration of overseas enterprises that produce, process or store food exported to China (collectively "overseas producers of imported food").

Overseas producers of imported food referred to in the preceding paragraph do not include enterprises producing, processing or storing food additives or food-related products.

**Article 3**　The General Administration of Customs is responsible for the administration of registration of overseas producers of imported food in a unified manner.

**Article 4**　An overseas producer of imported food shall be registered with the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 5**　Following the principle of risk management, the General Administration of Customs applies classification-based administration to overseas producers of imported food — on the basis of its assessment and review of the food safety management system and food safety situation of the country (region) in which the producer is located, and taking into account the risk level of the food concerned — and determines the corresponding registration method, application materials, review procedure and other registration administration requirements.

Where risk assessment or evidence shows that the food safety risk of imported food has changed, the General Administration of Customs may adjust the corresponding registration administration requirements.

## Chapter II — Registration conditions and procedure

**Article 6**　On the basis of its analysis of the origin of raw materials, production and processing techniques, historical food safety data, consumer groups, consumption methods and other factors, and in line with international practice, the General Administration of Customs determines and publishes the catalogue of imported food requiring official recommendation for registration (hereinafter "the Catalogue").

**Article 7**　Conditions for registration of an overseas producer of imported food:

(1) it is established with the approval of, and under the effective supervision of, the competent authority of the country (region) in which it is located;

(2) it has established an effective food safety, hygiene management and protection system, lawfully produces and exports in that country (region), and ensures that the food exported to China complies with the relevant Chinese laws and regulations and national food safety standards;

(3) it meets the relevant inspection and quarantine requirements agreed between the General Administration of Customs and that competent authority.

An overseas producer of food listed in the Catalogue shall also obtain the recommendation of the competent authority of the country (region) in which it is located.

**Article 8**　For an overseas producer of food listed in the Catalogue, the competent authority of the country (region) in which it is located shall audit and inspect the enterprise and, where it meets the registration requirements, issue an audit and inspection report and a letter of recommendation.

**Article 9**　An overseas producer of imported food shall submit the following application materials to the General Administration of Customs, either itself or through an agent:

(1) the enterprise's registration application information;

(2) documents proving the enterprise's identity, such as a business licence issued or a certificate produced by the competent authority of the country (region) in which it is located;

(3) a declaration by the enterprise that it meets the requirements of these Provisions.

An overseas producer of food listed in the Catalogue shall also submit the audit and inspection report and the letter of recommendation issued by the competent authority of the country (region) in which it is located.

Where necessary, the General Administration of Customs may require the enterprise to provide information on its food safety, hygiene management and protection system, type of production, production capacity and similar matters.

**Article 10**　The enterprise's registration application information shall include the enterprise's name, country (region), address of the production site, legal representative, contact person, contact details, the registration number approved by the competent authority of the country (region), the types of food applied for registration, and other information.

**Article 11**　Registration application materials shall be in Chinese or English.

The competent authority of the country (region) and the overseas producer of imported food shall be responsible for the truthfulness, completeness and lawfulness of the relevant materials.

**Article 12**　In light of the food safety risk level of imported food, the General Administration of Customs — itself or through a commissioned institution — conducts assessment and review of an overseas producer of imported food applying for registration by means of documentary inspection, video inspection, on-site inspection or any combination of these.

The overseas producer of imported food and the competent authority of the country (region) shall assist in the above assessment and review.

**Article 13**　On the basis of the assessment and review, the General Administration of Customs registers an overseas producer of imported food that meets the requirements and assigns it a registration number in China, notifying the enterprise in writing; it does not register an overseas producer that does not meet the requirements, and notifies the enterprise in writing.

**Article 14**　When a registered enterprise exports food to China, it shall mark on the food packaging the registration number in China or the registration number approved by the competent authority of the country (region) in which it is located.

**Article 15**　The registration of an overseas producer of imported food is valid for five years.

When registering an overseas producer of imported food, the General Administration of Customs shall determine the start and end dates of the period of validity.

**Article 16**　The General Administration of Customs publishes the list of registered overseas producers of imported food in a unified manner.

**Article 17**　Where the food safety management system of the country (region) in which an overseas producer of imported food is located is recognised by the General Administration of Customs and one of the following circumstances applies, the General Administration of Customs may agree in writing with that competent authority to adopt the list-based registration method for its enterprises:

(1) a food safety cooperation agreement on import and export food has been signed with the General Administration of Customs;

(2) an agreement, memorandum of understanding, joint statement or other cooperation document containing food safety cooperation has been signed with China;

(3) any other circumstance in which the General Administration of Customs considers, after risk assessment, that the list-based registration method may be adopted.

**Article 18**　Where the list-based registration method is adopted, the competent authority of the country (region) shall submit the following materials to the General Administration of Customs:

(1) a list of food-producing enterprises recommended for registration in China;

(2) the application information set out in Article 10 of these Provisions;

(3) a declaration that the recommended enterprises meet paragraph 1 of Article 7 of these Provisions;

(4) a declaration undertaking to continuously perform the obligations agreed in the bilateral cooperation documents.

After review, the General Administration of Customs registers the enterprises on the list that meet the requirements and assigns them registration numbers in China; it does not register enterprises on the list that do not meet the requirements, and notifies that competent authority in writing.

## Chapter III — Supervision and administration

**Article 19**　In light of the food safety risk level of imported food, the General Administration of Customs — itself or through a commissioned institution — carries out re-inspection of whether an overseas producer of imported food continues to meet the registration requirements.

The overseas producer of imported food and the competent authority of the country (region) shall assist in the above re-inspection.

**Article 20**　Where the registration information of an overseas producer of imported food changes during the period of validity, the enterprise shall submit an application for modification to the General Administration of Customs through the registration application channel, together with the following materials:

(1) a comparison table of the registration particulars before and after the change;

(2) supporting documents relating to the changed information.

Where the General Administration of Customs considers after assessment that modification may be made, it makes the modification. Where circumstances such as relocation of the production site, change of legal representative or change of the registration number granted by the country (region) have a material impact on the enterprise's food safety, hygiene management and protection system, the General Administration of Customs does not make the modification and notifies the enterprise to apply for registration afresh; the registration number in China becomes invalid from the date of such notification.

**Article 21**　The registration of an enterprise is automatically extended when its period of validity expires, for a further five years, except in any of the following circumstances:

(1) the imported food is included in the list of food not eligible for automatic extension of registration;

(2) the enterprise is in a period of rectification for failing to meet the registration requirements;

(3) the General Administration of Customs has lawfully suspended the import of the relevant food from the country (region) in which the overseas producer of imported food is located.

The list of imported food not eligible for automatic extension of registration referred to in item (1) of the preceding paragraph shall be published separately by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 22**　Where an overseas producer of food included in the list of food not eligible for automatic extension of registration needs to extend its registration, it shall, within the period from 3 to 12 months before the expiry of the period of validity, submit an application for extension of registration to the General Administration of Customs through the registration application channel. The application materials for extension of registration include:

(1) the application information for extension of registration;

(2) a declaration undertaking to continue to meet the registration requirements;

(3) for an overseas producer of food listed in the Catalogue, a declaration issued by the competent authority of the country (region) that the enterprise continues to meet the registration requirements.

The General Administration of Customs extends the registration of enterprises that meet the registration requirements, extending the period of validity by five years.

**Article 23**　Where a registered overseas producer of imported food falls under any of the following circumstances, the General Administration of Customs cancels its registration, notifies the enterprise, informs the competent authority of the country (region) and publishes the cancellation:

(1) it fails to apply for extension of registration as required;

(2) the competent authority of the country (region) or the overseas producer of imported food applies for cancellation on its own initiative;

(3) it no longer meets the requirement in item (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 7 of these Provisions.

**Article 24**　The competent authority of the country (region) in which an overseas producer of imported food is located shall perform its food safety supervision duties, exercise effective supervision over registered enterprises, and urge registered enterprises to continue to meet the registration requirements. Where it finds that an enterprise does not meet the registration requirements, it shall immediately adopt risk prevention, control and mitigation measures, suspend the enterprise's export of food to China, notify the General Administration of Customs, and supervise the enterprise's rectification until it meets the registration requirements.

Where an overseas producer of imported food finds that it does not meet the registration requirements, it shall on its own initiative suspend its export of food to China and immediately adopt rectification measures until the rectification meets the registration requirements.

**Article 25**　Where the General Administration of Customs finds that a registered overseas producer of imported food no longer meets the registration requirements, it shall order the enterprise to rectify within a specified period, notify the competent authority of the country (region) to urge rectification, and suspend imports of food from the enterprise during the rectification period.

After completing rectification, the enterprise shall submit a supervision-and-rectification report issued by the competent authority of the country (region) and a written declaration that it meets the registration requirements.

The General Administration of Customs shall review the enterprise's rectification and, where the requirements are met, resume imports of food from the enterprise.

**Article 26**　Where the General Administration of Customs suspends and resumes imports of food from registered overseas producers of imported food in accordance with Articles 24 and 25 of these Provisions, the list of enterprises concerned shall be published by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 27**　Where a registered overseas producer of imported food falls under any of the following circumstances, the General Administration of Customs revokes its registration and announces the revocation:

(1) a major food safety incident occurs in imported food due to the enterprise's own reasons;

(2) food exported to China is found in entry inspection and quarantine to present food safety problems of a serious nature;

(3) there are major problems in the enterprise's food safety and hygiene management such that it cannot guarantee that the food it exports to China meets safety and hygiene requirements;

(4) it still fails to meet the registration requirements after rectification;

(5) it provides false materials or conceals relevant circumstances;

(6) it refuses to cooperate with the General Administration of Customs in re-inspection and incident investigation;

(7) it rents out, lends, transfers, resells or misappropriates a registration number;

(8) registration was granted to an enterprise that lacks the qualifications to apply or does not meet the registration conditions;

(9) any other circumstance in which registration may lawfully be revoked.

## Chapter IV — Supplementary provisions

**Article 28**　Where the General Administration of Customs lawfully suspends the import of the relevant food from the country (region) in which an overseas producer of imported food is located, it does not accept registration applications from producers of the relevant food in that country (region) during the suspension period.

**Article 29**　Where the relevant country (region) and China have other arrangements on the administration of registration of overseas producers of imported food, those arrangements shall be followed.

**Article 30**　The scope of overseas storage enterprises of imported food that are subject to registration administration under these Provisions shall be published separately by the General Administration of Customs.

The administration of registration of overseas producers of primary edible agricultural products shall be formulated separately by the General Administration of Customs.

The administration requirements for overseas producers of food imported via cross-border e-commerce retail shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions.

**Article 31**　In these Provisions, "competent authority of the country (region)" means the official department responsible for the safety and hygiene supervision of food-producing enterprises in the country (region) in which the overseas producer of imported food is located.

**Article 32**　The General Administration of Customs is responsible for the interpretation of these Provisions.

**Article 33**　These Provisions enter into force on 1 June 2026. The Provisions of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Registration of Overseas Producers of Imported Food, promulgated by GACC Decree No. 248 on 12 April 2021, are repealed on the same date.
