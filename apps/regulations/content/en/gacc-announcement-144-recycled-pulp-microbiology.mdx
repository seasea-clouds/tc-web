GACC Announcement No. 144 takes effect on 1 October: imported recycled pulp gains microbiology key-indicator inspection on top of existing checks, under the revised national standard Recycled Pulp (GB/T 43393-2026), with the limits set out in the single annex. Whether your consignment is released depends on all four annex indicators passing.

You have a batch of imported recycled pulp arriving: first check bacterial and fungal colony counts against the annex limits, then confirm Pseudomonas aeruginosa and Staphylococcus aureus are both absent, and prepare a test report for inspection. Customs inspects against the added indicators; detection of either specified microbe makes the batch non-compliant.

The added requirements apply only to microbiology key indicators for imported recycled pulp and do not replace existing inspection items. Publication and effective dates differ, so prepare the goods for the effective date; the annex Key Indicator Requirements for Recycled Pulp (New) is the sole basis for the determination.

<!-- original -->

Under the technical requirements of the revised national standard Recycled Pulp (GB/T 43393-2026), from 1 October 2026, microbiology key-indicator inspection requirements are added for imported recycled pulp; see the annex for details.

It is hereby announced.

Annex: Key Indicator Requirements for Recycled Pulp (New) .docx

Microbiology indicators: bacterial colony count / (CFU/g) ≤1.0×10⁷; fungal colony count / (CFU/g) ≤1.0×10⁷.

Specified microorganisms: Pseudomonas aeruginosa shall not be detected; Staphylococcus aureus shall not be detected.

General Administration of Customs of China

28 September 2026
