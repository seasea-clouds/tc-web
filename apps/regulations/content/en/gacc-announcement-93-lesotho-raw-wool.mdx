Already in force, it can enter. GACC Announcement 93 of 2026, issued 3 July 2026 and effective that day, opens Lesotho unprocessed greasy wool: raw wool and mohair.

One container lot via a third country, full route: the firm is approved by the Lesotho side and registered by the Chinese side; 6 months before shearing, no anthrax or other outbreaks within 50 km of the farm; infected-zone wool is inactivated at 4℃ for 4 months, 18℃ for 4 weeks, 37℃ for 8 days or by 24-hour formaldehyde fumigation; packs are labelled in English, the container is fumigated and lead-sealed, storage and transit take not less than 28 days, and entry is on the original health certificate and the entry quarantine permit.

Without registration or the certificate, do not ship this lot yet; a broken seal or opening the container in transit stalls entry checks. Country and transit are a process illustration; the registration list and contract prevail.

<!-- original -->

Under the laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Food Security and Nutrition of the Kingdom of Lesotho (hereinafter the Lesotho side) on the quarantine and health requirements for Lesotho wool, with effect from 3 July 2026, Lesotho wool meeting the following requirements is permitted to be imported.

I. Inspection and Quarantine Basis

(i) the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; (iii) the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Entry and Exit Non-edible Animal Products; (iv) the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Food Security and Nutrition of the Kingdom of Lesotho on the Quarantine and Health Requirements for Lesotho Wool Exported to China (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Scope of Imported Products

Wool in this Announcement means unprocessed greasy wool produced in Lesotho, including raw wool and mohair (hereinafter raw wool).

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

The production and processing enterprises and storage facilities exporting raw wool to China shall be approved by and subject to routine supervision of the Lesotho side, and registered by the Chinese side, before they may export raw wool to China.

IV. Requirements for Imported Products

(i) Health requirements for the place of origin of the products.

(1) In accordance with the relevant provisions of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH), the Lesotho side confirms that Lesotho is free of rinderpest, peste des petits ruminants, sheep pox and goat pox; if these diseases occur in Lesotho, the Lesotho side shall immediately stop exports of raw wool to China from the whole territory; (2) within 6 months before shearing of the animals, no anthrax, ovine brucellosis or Rift Valley fever has occurred in the area from which the exported raw wool originates (within a radius of at least 50 km centred on the farm), and there have been no reported human cases of anthrax or ovine brucellosis;

(3) For foot-and-mouth disease: the farm where the source animals of the raw wool are kept has had no foot-and-mouth disease within 12 months before shearing of the animals; or, if that farm is located in a foot-and-mouth disease infected zone, the raw wool is processed by the following methods prescribed in Article 8.8 of the WOAH Terrestrial Animal Health Code to inactivate the foot-and-mouth disease virus:

(1) stored at 4℃ for 4 months, or, (2) stored at 18℃ for 4 weeks, or, (3) stored at 37℃ for 8 days, or, (4) disinfected by formaldehyde fumigation in a sealed chamber for at least 24 hours, and, the above processes shall be carried out at the production and processing enterprises and storage facilities registered by the Chinese side and under the approval and supervision of the Lesotho side.

(ii) Requirements for the source animals.

(1) healthy animals born and raised in Lesotho and kept under the monitoring of relevant animal diseases by the Lesotho side; (2) animals that have not been subject to animal movement restriction measures due to outbreaks of anthrax, ovine brucellosis or other diseases that can be transmitted through wool and endanger human health and animal health.

(iii) Production and processing requirements.

Before baling, raw wool shall be sorted and graded by professionals of the Lesotho side who meet the relevant requirements in accordance with standard procedures, so as to prevent raw wool from carrying animal tissue, blood, faeces, soil and other foreign matter, as well as harmful weeds and weed seeds.

V. Packaging and Labelling Requirements

(i) the packaging site is clean and free of contamination by foreign fibres; (ii) the packaging is brand new or has been disinfected, is sound in appearance and has good wrapping and strapping performance; (iii) the packaging bears clear markings in English, stating at least: country of origin, product name, production batch number, registration number, packaging number and transport mark.

VI. Storage and Transport Requirements

(i) packaging containers and transport containers holding raw wool for export to China shall be clean and sanitary and comply with the relevant veterinary hygiene requirements; (ii) the container is disinfected with an effective disinfectant under the supervision of the Lesotho side before use, is fumigated with formaldehyde or other agents after loading and before closing and sealing, and is fitted with a tamper-proof lead seal bearing a unique numeric code; (iii) effective measures are taken during transport of raw wool to avoid contamination; if transiting a third country or region, the outer packaging such as the container shall not be opened during transit and the seal shall remain intact; (iv) the time from storage in Lesotho to arrival in China after export for each batch of raw wool is not less than 28 days.

VII. Pre-export Inspection and Certificate Requirements

(i) raw wool for export to China is quarantined by the Lesotho side, and a health certificate is issued in accordance with the certificate sample confirmed by both sides to certify that the product meets the requirements of the Protocol; the language of the health certificate shall include English; the health certificate sample and the official seal must be notified to the Chinese side for confirmation before they take effect; (ii) each batch of raw wool for export to China shall be accompanied by at least one original official health certificate.

VIII. Entry Quarantine Requirements

(i) Quarantine approval. The importing enterprise shall apply for the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit in accordance with the relevant provisions.

(ii) Document verification. (1) check whether the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit is attached; (2) check whether the products come from a registered enterprise; (3) check whether the health certificate is authentic and valid.

(iii) Cargo inspection. China Customs shall, in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and in light of the requirements of this Announcement, carry out inspection and quarantine of Lesotho raw wool exported to China. Those that pass inspection and quarantine shall be permitted to enter.

(iv) Handling of non-compliance. For non-compliant products, return, destruction or other treatment shall be carried out in accordance with the laws and regulations of China. For production enterprises with serious problems or repeated non-compliance, the Chinese side shall adopt measures such as strengthened inspection and quarantine or suspension of imports.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

3 July 2026
