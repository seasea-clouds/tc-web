Yes, they can enter. GACC Announcement No. 116 admits industrial animal by-products from Kazakhstan: degreased bone granules, bone oil and peptone made from cattle and sheep bones, provided the producer is recommended by the Kazakh side and registered with China.

For a consignment, check four things before shipment: the producer is on China's registration list and the registration has not lapsed; the source cattle or sheep were born and raised in Kazakhstan for at least 6 months; no anthrax, foot-and-mouth disease or other major animal disease occurred within 50 km of the farms and slaughterhouses in the previous 6 months; and the heat process holds a product centre temperature of at least 120 °C for 30 minutes. Industrial dye must turn the oil dark green, and the outer package carries a Chinese and English label marked not for human consumption.

At the Chinese port, China Customs verifies the import quarantine licence, the registered source and the original health certificate, and releases compliant goods. Non-conforming goods are handled under Chinese law; producers with serious or repeated failures face tighter inspection and quarantine or suspension of imports.

In accordance with China's laws and regulations and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Republic of Kazakhstan (hereinafter referred to as the Kazakh side) on the quarantine and hygiene requirements for animal by-products exported from Kazakhstan to China, imports of Kazakh animal by-products meeting the following requirements are permitted with immediate effect.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its Implementing Regulations;

(3) the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Non-food Animal Products for Entry and Exit;

(4) the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Republic of Kazakhstan on the Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for Animal By-products Exported from Kazakhstan to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Scope of Imported Products

Animal by-products in this Announcement refer to industrial-use products made from the bones of cattle and sheep through crushing, drying, cooking, rendering, spray drying and other processes, including industrial degreased bone granules, bone oil and peptone.

III. Requirements for Producers

Producers and processors of Kazakh animal by-products exported to China (hereinafter referred to as producers) shall be approved by the competent authority of the Kazakh side and recommended by it to the Chinese side. Those that pass the Chinese assessment shall be registered. Producers shall meet the following requirements:

(a) a complete veterinary hygiene and epidemic prevention system and a product traceability system, with production and processing conforming to veterinary hygiene requirements;

(b) facilities that meet the requirements for production, processing, epidemic prevention and disinfection, and for the treatment of wastewater and waste;

(c) separate warehouses for storing products destined for China, with effective measures against cross-contamination, insect breeding and spoilage;

(d) production and processing under effective supervision of the Kazakh side.

Registration of a producer is valid for 5 years. 6 months before the registration expires, the Kazakh side shall submit a renewal application to the Chinese side, which shall process it in accordance with the relevant procedures.

IV. Requirements for Imported Products

(a) Hygiene requirements for the source of raw materials.

(1) The source animals were born and raised in Kazakhstan for at least 6 months and were healthy animals under effective supervision of the Kazakh side;

(2) Within a radius of 50 km centred on the farms and slaughterhouses involved in the source animals, no major animal diseases such as anthrax, foot-and-mouth disease, peste des petits ruminants, rabies, sheep pox and goat pox occurred within 6 months;

(3) The source animals were slaughtered in slaughterhouses approved by the Kazakh side, passed ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection, and showed no clinical signs of animal infectious diseases.

(b) Production and processing requirements.

(1) Heat processing methods such as drying, cooking, rendering and spray drying shall be used, and the heat process shall ensure that the product centre temperature is not lower than 120 °C for at least 30 minutes;

(2) Other production processes shall completely inactivate foot-and-mouth disease and peste des petits ruminants viruses and be assessed and accepted by the Chinese side;

(3) Industrial oil products shall be coloured with industrial dye, added in sufficient quantity and evenly, so that the final product is dark green;

(4) The Kazakh side shall effectively supervise production and processing to ensure that the processing temperature and time and the addition of industrial dye consistently meet the requirements.

V. Packaging and Labelling Requirements

(a) Packaging shall use new materials and meet veterinary hygiene and public health requirements;

(b) A Chinese and English label shall be printed directly on the outer package. The label shall include the product name, the producer's name and registration number, and the production date, and shall be marked prominently as not for human consumption.

VI. Storage and Transport Requirements

After packaging, products shall be stored and transported in clean conditions, with effective measures to prevent contamination during storage and transport.

VII. Pre-export Inspection and Certification Requirements

(a) The Kazakh side shall inspect and quarantine animal by-products and issue a health certificate according to the certificate model confirmed by both sides, certifying that the products meet the requirements of the Protocol;

(b) Each consignment of animal by-products exported to China shall be accompanied by at least one official original health certificate.

VIII. Entry Inspection and Quarantine Requirements

(a) Quarantine approval.

Where quarantine approval is required, the importer shall complete the relevant formalities and obtain the Import Animal and Plant Quarantine Licence.

(b) Document verification.

(1) Where quarantine approval is required, verify that the Import Animal and Plant Quarantine Licence is attached;

(2) Verify that the goods come from a registered producer;

(3) Verify that the health certificate is authentic and valid.

(c) Goods inspection.

China Customs shall inspect and quarantine Kazakh animal by-products exported to China in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and in light of the requirements of this Announcement. Goods that pass inspection and quarantine shall be permitted to enter.

(d) Handling of non-conforming cases.

Non-conforming products shall be handled in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations. For producers with serious problems or repeated non-conformities, the Chinese side will take measures such as tightened inspection and quarantine or suspension of imports.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs
