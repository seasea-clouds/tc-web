On January 1, 2022, the General Administration of Customs of China promulgated the Administrative Measures for the Comprehensive Bonded Zone by Decree No. 256, which took effect on April 1, 2022 and was amended on October 28, 2024 by Decree No. 273. Zone enterprises, means of transport, goods and articles within the zone are all supervised under these Measures.

Article 5 lists research and development, processing, testing and repair, goods storage, logistics distribution, financial leasing and cross-border e-commerce as businesses that zone enterprises may conduct. Goods within the zone have no storage period limit, and goods shipped out of the territory are exempt from export duty, both subject to exceptions where laws and regulations provide otherwise.

Zone enterprises may complete consolidated declaration. Upon the entry into force of these Measures, the Bonded Port Area measures issued by Decree No. 164 of 2007 and the Bonded Logistics Park measures issued by Decree No. 134 of 2005 are repealed simultaneously, and the successive amendment decrees of both former regulations cease to have effect as well.

(Promulgated by GACC Decree No. 256 on January 1, 2022　Effective from April 1, 2022　Amended on October 28, 2024 pursuant to GACC Decree No. 273, the Decision of the General Administration of Customs on Amending Certain Regulations)

## Chapter 1 General Provisions

**Article 1**　These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law on Import and Export Commodity Inspection, the Law on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, the Law on Frontier Health and Quarantine, the Food Safety Law, and other relevant laws, administrative regulations and state provisions, in order to regulate Customs administration of the comprehensive bonded zone and promote its high-standard opening up and high-quality development.

**Article 2**　The Customs shall, in accordance with these Measures, exercise supervision and administration over means of transport entering and leaving the comprehensive bonded zone, goods and their outer packaging and containers, articles, and enterprises within the comprehensive bonded zone (hereinafter the zone).

**Article 3**　The comprehensive bonded zone shall practice closed management.

Except for security personnel, no persons may reside within the zone.

**Article 4**　The infrastructure and supervision facilities of the comprehensive bonded zone shall conform to the standards for establishing such facilities and be accepted after inspection by the Customs jointly with the relevant departments.

**Article 5**　Zone enterprises may, in accordance with the law, conduct the following businesses:

(1) research and development, processing, manufacturing and remanufacturing;

(2) testing and repair;

(3) goods storage;

(4) logistics distribution;

(5) financial leasing;

(6) cross-border e-commerce;

(7) commodity display;

(8) international entrepot trade;

(9) international transshipment;

(10) port operations;

(11) bonded delivery of futures;

(12) other businesses that the state permits to be conducted within the zone.

**Article 6**　The Customs shall implement computerized networked administration of zone enterprises and enhance the information-based and intelligent administration of the comprehensive bonded zone.

## Chapter 2 Administration of Goods Entering and Leaving Between the Comprehensive Bonded Zone and Overseas

**Article 7**　Except where laws and regulations provide otherwise, goods and articles whose import or export is prohibited by the state shall not enter or leave between the comprehensive bonded zone and overseas.

**Article 8**　Goods entering and leaving between the comprehensive bonded zone and overseas shall not be subject to tariff quota or licensing document administration, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or by international treaties and agreements concluded or acceded to by China.

**Article 9**　For goods entering and leaving between the comprehensive bonded zone and overseas, the consignor, consignee or agent shall truthfully declare to the Customs, fill in the record list of inward and outward goods in accordance with Customs provisions, and complete the relevant formalities.

**Article 10**　Goods entering the comprehensive bonded zone from overseas and their outer packaging and containers shall be subject to quarantine by the Customs at the port of entry in accordance with the law. Where port conditions are limited or for other reasons, the Customs may conduct quarantine at qualified sites (premises) within the zone.

Goods shipped from the comprehensive bonded zone to overseas and their outer packaging and containers shall be subject to quarantine by the Customs in accordance with the law.

The means of transport entering and leaving between the comprehensive bonded zone and overseas shall be subject to quarantine by the Customs in accordance with the relevant provisions on inward and outward means of transport.

**Article 11**　Goods entering the comprehensive bonded zone from overseas shall be bonded, except for the circumstances specified in Articles 12 and 14 of these Measures.

**Article 12**　Except where laws and regulations provide otherwise, the Customs shall exempt the following goods entering the comprehensive bonded zone from overseas from import duty and import-related taxes:

(1) machinery and equipment required for productive infrastructure construction projects within the zone, and capital construction materials required for building production plants and storage facilities;

(2) machinery, equipment, moulds and spare parts for their maintenance required by zone enterprises to conduct the businesses listed in Article 5 of these Measures;

(3) office supplies in reasonable quantities for the administrative body of the comprehensive bonded zone and for zone enterprises for their own use.

From the date on which the State Council approves the establishment of the comprehensive bonded zone, machinery and equipment for the own use of zone enterprises entering the comprehensive bonded zone from overseas shall be handled in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

**Article 13**　The supervision period for the goods listed in Article 12 of these Measures shall be administered by reference to imported duty-reduced or duty-exempt goods, and supervision shall be released automatically on expiry; where an enterprise applies for early release before expiry, it shall follow the relevant provisions on supplementary tax for imported duty-reduced or duty-exempt goods, and licensing documents shall be obtained where such administration applies.

**Article 14**　The Customs shall, in accordance with the law, levy import duty and import-related taxes on means of transport and daily consumer goods entering the comprehensive bonded zone from overseas for the own use of zone enterprises and the administrative body.

**Article 15**　Except where laws and regulations provide otherwise, goods shipped from the comprehensive bonded zone to overseas shall be exempt from export duty.

## Chapter 3 Administration of Goods Entering and Leaving Between the Comprehensive Bonded Zone and Areas Outside the Zone

**Article 16**　For goods entering and leaving between the comprehensive bonded zone and other areas within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter areas outside the zone), zone enterprises or consignors and consignees outside the zone shall complete the relevant formalities with the Customs in accordance with the provisions.

Where the goods are subject to tariff quota or licensing document administration, zone enterprises or consignors and consignees outside the zone shall obtain the tariff quota and licensing documents; the Customs shall verify the tariff quota and conduct automatic systematic comparison and verification of the electronic data of the licensing documents.

**Article 17**　Except where laws and regulations provide otherwise, the Customs shall not conduct quarantine on goods entering and leaving between the comprehensive bonded zone and areas outside the zone, or on their outer packaging and containers.

**Article 18**　For goods entering and leaving between the comprehensive bonded zone and areas outside the zone, zone enterprises or consignors and consignees outside the zone shall, in accordance with the law, pay customs duties and import-related taxes based on the actual state of the goods at the time of entering or leaving the zone.

Where goods processed or produced by zone enterprises are sold domestically upon leaving the zone, zone enterprises or consignors and consignees outside the zone may choose to pay customs duties on the corresponding imported materials and pay supplementary interest on deferred customs duties; import-related taxes shall be paid in accordance with the actual state of the goods at the time of leaving the zone.

**Article 19**　Goods under preferential trade agreements shipped to areas outside the zone via the comprehensive bonded zone may, where they comply with the relevant rules of origin administration, be subject to the agreement tariff rate or the preferential tariff rate.

**Article 20**　Goods entering the comprehensive bonded zone by way of export declaration shall be bonded; among them, machinery and equipment purchased by zone enterprises from areas outside the zone shall be administered by reference to the supervision period for imported duty-reduced or duty-exempt goods, and supervision shall be released automatically on expiry without submitting licensing documents; where an enterprise applies for early release before expiry, the relevant formalities shall be completed by reference to the relevant provisions on supplementary tax for imported duty-reduced or duty-exempt goods, without submitting licensing documents.

Export tax refunds for the goods specified in the preceding paragraph shall be handled in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

**Article 21**　Where scrap, defective products, by-products generated from the use of bonded materials in the course of processing or production by zone enterprises, and packaging materials generated in the course of processing, production, storage or transport, are shipped to areas outside the zone for sale, zone enterprises shall pay taxes based on the actual state of the goods at the time of leaving the zone; where defective products or by-products are subject to tariff quota or licensing document administration, zone enterprises or consignors and consignees outside the zone shall obtain the tariff quota and licensing documents; the Customs shall verify the tariff quota and conduct automatic systematic comparison and verification of the electronic data of the licensing documents.

**Article 22**　Solid waste generated by zone enterprises that is not re-exported shall be administered in accordance with the relevant domestic provisions on solid waste. Where it needs to be shipped to areas outside the zone for storage, utilization or disposal, the formalities for leaving the zone shall be completed with the Customs in accordance with the provisions.

**Article 23**　Where a zone enterprise destroys or disposes of goods within the zone in accordance with the law, it shall complete the relevant formalities and bear the costs of destruction or disposal. Solid waste generated by the destruction shall, when leaving the zone, be handled in accordance with Article 22 of these Measures.

**Article 24**　Zone enterprises may complete consolidated declaration formalities in accordance with Customs provisions.

Except where the General Administration of Customs provides otherwise, zone enterprises shall, before the 15th day of the month following each quarter, complete consolidated declaration for that quarter's goods, but not later than the deadline for account book write-off, and shall not carry it over across years.

The consolidated declaration shall be subject to the tax rate and the exchange rate for tax calculation in effect on the date on which the enterprise completes the tax payment formalities.

**Article 25**　Goods circulating between the comprehensive bonded zone and other special customs supervision areas such as comprehensive bonded zones, or bonded supervision premises, shall be bonded.

Goods circulating between the comprehensive bonded zone and other special customs supervision areas such as comprehensive bonded zones, or bonded supervision premises, shall not be subject to customs duties or import-related taxes.

## Chapter 4 Administration of Goods Within the Comprehensive Bonded Zone

**Article 26**　Goods within the comprehensive bonded zone may circulate freely. Where zone enterprises transfer or assign goods, both enterprises shall promptly submit to the Customs electronic data information such as the name, quantity and value of the transferred or assigned goods.

**Article 27**　Zone enterprises may use duty-free equipment within the supervision period to accept entrustment from enterprises outside the zone to conduct processing operations.

Where zone enterprises conduct entrusted processing operations, they shall set up a dedicated electronic account book for entrusted processing. Where the materials for entrusted processing need to use bonded materials, zone enterprises shall report to the Customs for record.

Solid waste generated from entrusted processing shall, when leaving the zone, be handled in accordance with Article 22 of these Measures.

**Article 28**　Where zone enterprises, in accordance with Customs provisions, ship self-use machinery, equipment and their parts, moulds or office supplies to areas outside the zone for testing or repair, such items shall not be used for processing, production or other use outside the zone during the testing or repair period, and shall be shipped back to the comprehensive bonded zone within 60 days from the date of shipment out. Where they cannot be shipped back on schedule for cause, zone enterprises shall, within 7 days before the expiry of the period, apply in writing to the Customs for an extension, and the extended period shall not exceed 30 days.

Where goods referred to in the preceding paragraph cannot, due to special circumstances, finish testing or repair and return to the comprehensive bonded zone within the aforesaid time, they may, with the consent of the Customs, return to the comprehensive bonded zone within the term of the testing or repair contract.

Where spare parts are replaced, the original spare parts shall be shipped back to the comprehensive bonded zone together; where disposal outside the zone is needed, the Customs shall levy tax on the original parts' actual state; where domestically produced spare parts replaced outside the zone are eligible for a tax refund, the enterprise shall complete the formalities under the relevant provisions.

**Article 29**　Where zone enterprises, in accordance with Customs provisions, ship moulds, raw materials, semi-finished products and the like to areas outside the zone for outward processing, the outward processing period shall not exceed the term of validity of the contract, and the processed goods shall be shipped back to the comprehensive bonded zone on schedule.

Where scrap, defective products or by-products generated from outward processing are not shipped back to the comprehensive bonded zone, the Customs shall levy tax based on the actual state of the goods; where defective products or by-products are subject to tariff quota or licensing document administration, zone enterprises or consignors and consignees outside the zone shall obtain the tariff quota and licensing documents; the Customs shall verify the relevant tariff quota and conduct automatic systematic comparison and verification of the electronic data of the licensing documents.

**Article 30**　Where goods within the comprehensive bonded zone are damaged or lost due to force majeure, zone enterprises shall promptly report to the Customs. After verification by the Customs, zone enterprises may handle the matter in accordance with the following provisions:

(1) where goods are lost, or are not lost but have completely lost their use value, the formalities for write-off and tax exemption shall be completed;

(2) where goods entering the comprehensive bonded zone from overseas, or from areas outside the zone with export tax refund formalities completed, are damaged and have partly lost use value, the formalities for domestic sale on leaving the zone or for return shipment shall be completed;

(3) where goods that entered the comprehensive bonded zone from areas outside the zone and for which export tax refund formalities have not been completed are damaged, have partly lost use value, and need to be returned to the exporting enterprise for replacement, the formalities for return shipment shall be completed.

**Article 31**　Where goods within the zone are damaged or lost due to causes other than force majeure, such as improper storage, zone enterprises shall promptly report to the Customs and explain the circumstances. After verification by the Customs, zone enterprises may handle the matter in accordance with the following provisions:

(1) for goods that entered the comprehensive bonded zone from overseas, the relevant formalities shall be completed in accordance with the provisions on goods imported under general trade, and customs duties and import-related taxes shall be paid on the basis of the dutiable value of the goods before damage or loss as determined by the Customs, at the tax rate and exchange rate for tax calculation applicable on the date of damage or loss;

(2) for goods that entered the comprehensive bonded zone from areas outside the zone, the relevant domestic taxes refunded on account of export shall be repaid, and any export duty already paid shall not be refunded.

**Article 32**　Goods that zone enterprises apply to abandon shall, upon approval by the Customs and the relevant competent departments, be taken over and sold by the Customs in accordance with the law, and the proceeds of the sale shall be handled in accordance with the relevant state provisions, except where laws and regulations provide that such goods may not be abandoned.

**Article 33**　Except where laws and regulations provide otherwise, goods within the zone have no storage period limit.

## Chapter 5 Administration of Zone Enterprises

**Article 34**　Zone enterprises and their branches shall obtain market entity status and, in accordance with the law, complete registration or recordation formalities with the Customs.

Enterprises engaged in food production within the zone shall, in accordance with the law, obtain a domestic production license.

**Article 35**　Zone enterprises shall standardize their financial administration in accordance with laws and regulations, and set up Customs electronic account books in accordance with Customs provisions; the recordation, alteration and write-off of the electronic account books shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant Customs provisions.

**Article 36**　The Customs shall apply an audit and verification system to zone enterprises.

Zone enterprises shall cooperate with the audit and verification of the Customs and truthfully provide relevant materials such as account books and documents as well as electronic data.

**Article 37**　Where zone enterprises conduct operations involving Customs security matters, such matters shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions on Customs security.

## Chapter 6 Supplementary Provisions

**Article 38**　Inspection of goods entering and leaving the comprehensive bonded zone shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions.

**Article 39**　The means of transport and persons entering and leaving between the comprehensive bonded zone and areas outside the zone shall pass through designated channels, and the Customs shall conduct inspections as needed.

Where service personnel of means of transport entering and leaving between the comprehensive bonded zone and overseas carry personal articles into or out of the comprehensive bonded zone, the Customs shall exercise supervision in accordance with the relevant provisions on the baggage and articles of inbound and outbound passengers.

**Article 40**　The exercise of supervision by the Customs within the comprehensive bonded zone in accordance with the law shall not affect the performance by local governments and other departments of their respective duties in accordance with the law.

**Article 41**　Except where laws and regulations provide otherwise, the Customs shall compile trade statistics on import and export goods for goods entering and leaving between overseas and the comprehensive bonded zone; for goods entering and leaving between areas outside the zone and the comprehensive bonded zone, it shall compile Customs specific statistics and Customs operational statistics as required by administration needs; and it shall compile Customs operational statistics for Customs supervision and administration activities and internal administration matters related to the comprehensive bonded zone.

**Article 42**　Where a pilot program for general value-added tax taxpayer qualification is conducted within the zone, it shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the policy on the pilot program for general value-added tax taxpayer qualification.

**Article 43**　For goods entering the zone from within the territory that involve no export duty, involve no licensing documents, require no tax refund and are not included in Customs statistics, the Customs shall apply facilitated administration of entry into and exit from the zone.

**Article 44**　Acts in violation of these Measures shall be punished by the Customs in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.

**Article 45**　Requirements concerning the examination and approval of the establishment of the comprehensive bonded zone, acceptance inspection of construction, supervision and administration, and the like shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

**Article 46**　These Measures shall be interpreted by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 47**　These Measures shall take effect on April 1, 2022. The Interim Measures of the Customs of the People's Republic of China for the Administration of Bonded Port Areas, promulgated by GACC Decree No. 164 on September 3, 2007 and amended by GACC Decree No. 191 of March 15, 2010, GACC Decree No. 235 of December 20, 2017, GACC Decree No. 240 of May 29, 2018 and GACC Decree No. 243 of November 23, 2018, and the Measures of the Customs of the People's Republic of China for the Administration of Bonded Logistics Parks, promulgated by GACC Decree No. 134 on November 28, 2005 and amended by GACC Decree No. 190 of March 15, 2010, GACC Decree No. 235 of December 20, 2017, GACC Decree No. 240 of May 29, 2018 and GACC Decree No. 243 of November 23, 2018, shall be repealed simultaneously.
