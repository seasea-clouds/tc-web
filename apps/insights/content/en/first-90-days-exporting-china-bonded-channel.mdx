From the moment you confirm the kickoff to the moment the first product has run the full path from order to doorstep through the bonded channel, what sits in between is 90 days, walked in three segments: the day 0–30 window hands over the access tiering table, the diagnostic report, the port execution checklist and the system integration plan; the day 31–60 window submits the filing dossier and locks the store architecture and the funds settlement path; the day 61–90 window trial-runs one SKU end to end. The first step can be small: one paid diagnostic, priced per the formal quotation, producing four reports; you decide whether to go further after reading them.

Only five decisions truly need your sign-off, at two points in time: two before day 30, whether to launch S0 and which onshore entity route to take; three before day 60, food status positioning, preferred target port, and the sales and stocking plan. Everything past these two gates advances on our side: the port execution ruling is made by us and written into the filing basis, government acceptance time limits and laboratory certifications are tracked by us day by day, and progress is synced every week.

## What Phase 1 does, and what it does not

The scope of Phase 1 is written in three columns: must-do, reserved in the architecture, and explicitly not done. Writing the not-done column out is what forestalls assumed-to-be-included disputes. The regulatory definitions of 1210 bonded online retail and 9610 direct purchase import come from the Notice of the Ministry of Commerce and Five Other Departments on Improving the Supervision of Cross-Border E-Commerce Retail Imports (Shang Cai Fa [2018] No. 486); the whole scheme is designed to the reading of personal-use inbound items, and across Phase 1 the two channels trade places, one advancing and one standing down, with the boundary as follows.

**Four must-do items:**

- 1210 bonded online retail is the main channel of Phase 1.
- The core SKUs are laid out in Phase 1.
- All four launch prerequisites are finalized: entity, customer acquisition, data, qualifications.
- Two-stage split disbursement: onshore commissions and offshore remuneration each run their own channel.

**Reserved in the architecture, switched on later:**

- The connection point for the second supply source is built ahead of time.
- The slot for SKU extension is left ready.
- The mount point for the membership system is left ready.
- The moment conditions arrive they can be switched on, with no rebuilding.

**Phase 1 leaves out:**

- 9610 direct purchase import stays switched off in Phase 1.
- The related switches are all left in place, to open as needed in Phase 2.
- Remuneration for offshore promoters is not booked directly onshore.
- Your global funds pool and offshore payroll are handled by your own company.

## The seven things to have ready before launch

![The seven-item preparation checklist, arranged in three groups: launch day, within 30 days, and verification running in parallel](/images/insights/first-90-days-exporting-china-bonded-channel/seven-item-preparation-checklist-arranged-three.webp)

The seven preparations divide into three groups by suggested timing: the first three decide whether the project can start at all, and you can begin preparing them that same day; the middle three settle scheduling and stocking, and they can be provided within 30 days of launch; the last one guards brand asset security and runs in parallel with the first two groups. This is a working suggestion, with the confirmed execution plan as the governing version; all seven items are listed under your name, and the original sources of the materials sit on your side and the manufacturer's.

**Ready to start immediately, top priority:**

- SKU list, ingredients, specifications, packaging: the starting point for classification and filing; we check each item against the regulatory constants and document numbers.
- CoA and test reports by batch: the same batch within the last 12 months, the governing reference for both the access verdict and the retest scope.
- Confirm the onshore entity route: this decides how S1 proceeds, and it is the longest stretch on the critical path.

**Within 30 days, high priority:**

- Confirm the category and claims strategy: this sets the wording for product pages and content, executed inside the claims boundary.
- Preferred target port: we issue the shortlist together with S0; you simply tick your choices on it.
- Sales and stocking plan: sets the first-leg freight cost and the entry-into-zone schedule, so the goods enter the zone as soon as they arrive.

**Verification in parallel, high priority:**

- Trademark and authorization chain verification: for the handling path when a trademark has been pre-emptively registered by someone else, the risk register already carries ready wording.

While you prepare the materials we run in step: building the S0 diagnostic frame, completing the port execution ruling, issuing the verification checklist. Wherever your preparation has reached, our actions connect right there.

## What gets decided at day 30 and at day 60

Only two real gates sit inside the 90 days. Two things are settled before day 30: whether to launch S0, and which onshore entity route to take. Let these two drag and the critical path as a whole slips back 4–12 weeks. Three things are settled before day 60: food status positioning (conventional food or Blue Hat health food), preferred target port, and the sales and stocking plan. Let these three drag and the schedules for S3 access and S4 warehouse entry slip back after them.

Missing a gate is not a one-day slip. The two day-30 items sit at the very top of the chain; while the diagnostic does not start, everything behind it waits. The three day-60 items sit between filing and stocking; dossier submission, store category and first-leg booking all wait on the output of those three. So these two points are written into the reconciliation and run with you once a month; whether they moved, and where they are stuck, shows on the ledger.

## The first 90 days of a whey protein powder

Let the rhythm land on one concrete product. Suppose your product is a whey protein powder, with a first batch of 2,000 cans.

Day 0: send over the SKU list, the ingredient list, the specifications and the packaging description, and pull the CoA for the same batch from the last 12 months; the onshore entity route is chosen on the spot, for instance starting with the platform-as-entity interim.

Day 0–30: we hand over the access tiering for this SKU, checking which row of the current positive list of cross-border e-commerce retail imported goods it falls on (Announcement No. 7 of 2022 by the Ministry of Finance and seven other departments) and whether the 1210 bonded online retail route is open to it; the four diagnostic reports come in complete, the port execution ruling goes into the record, and the system integration plan sets out interfaces and schedule.

Day 31–60: the filing dossier goes in as conventional food, the store architecture and category are finalized, the funds and settlement path is locked, and the 14-screen demo walkthrough is gone through one screen at a time. In this segment you land the three decisions due before day 60: food status set as conventional food, with zero functional claims; port ticked off the shortlist; first stocking quantity set at 2,000 cans.

Day 61–90: the 2,000 cans make the first-leg move into the zone and into the bonded ledger; the single-SKU full-chain trial runs once, end to end: a consumer places an order, the three documents of order, payment and logistics are matched against each other under the reading of GACC Announcement No. 194 of 2018, clearance releases the goods, and the order leaves the bonded warehouse and reaches the buyer in good order; the operations model calculator passes into your hands, where changing one parameter shows the return at different stocking volumes.

Both gates settle ahead of their deadlines inside these 90 days, and the order of preparation is what makes that possible: the two day-30 items are decided on day 0, and the three day-60 items are landed one by one within the day 31–60 window, with the critical path not spending a single extra day.
