Imports banned immediately. After foot and mouth disease broke out in Namibia, China's customs and agriculture-rural authorities jointly banned cloven hoofed animals and related products from Namibia's whole territory, covering direct imports, third country transshipment, post or carriage.

Frozen beef transshipped via a third country arrives: verify origin first; once it hits the ban, no release. Goods are returned or destroyed under customs supervision; waste and swill unloaded from transport undergo disinfection treatment; illegal inbound goods seized at border checks are destroyed.

The six measures take effect on issue. Violators face the Customs Law, Biosecurity Law, Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and its implementing rules; two prior notices are repealed together. Customs and agriculture-rural departments do quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision.

<!-- original -->

Recently, Namibia reported to the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) an outbreak of foot and mouth disease in its Karas region. To prevent the introduction of the disease and protect China's livestock safety and biosecurity, and in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, the following is hereby announced:

I. It is prohibited to import, directly or indirectly, cloven hoofed animals and related products from the whole territory of Namibia (products derived from cloven hoofed animals that are unprocessed, or that have been processed but may still transmit the disease).

II. It is prohibited to send by post or carry into the country cloven hoofed animals and related products from Namibia. Once found, all such goods shall be subject to return or destruction.

III. Animal and plant waste, swill and the like unloaded from inbound means of transport from Namibia shall, without exception, undergo disinfection or pest-control treatment under customs supervision and shall not be discarded without authorization.

IV. Cloven hoofed animals and related products from Namibia that have entered the country illegally and are seized by border inspection and other departments shall, without exception, be destroyed under customs supervision.

V. Anyone who violates the above provisions shall be dealt with by customs in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations.

VI. Customs offices at all levels and agricultural and rural affairs departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Animal Epidemic Prevention and other relevant provisions, cooperate closely and carry out quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision work well.

This Announcement shall take effect on the date of its publication. The Announcement No. 939 of 2007 of the former Ministry of Agriculture and the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine (Announcement on Preventing the Introduction of Foot and Mouth Disease from Namibia into China), and the Announcement No. 88 of 2014 of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine and the former Ministry of Agriculture (Announcement on Lifting the Ban on Foot and Mouth Disease in Parts of Namibia) are repealed at the same time.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of China and Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

16 October 2026
