Can it get in? Yes, but not nationwide. Announcement No. 70 of 2026 of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs of the People's Republic of China, issued on 21 May 2026, allows only poultry and poultry products from Spain meeting the requirements into China.

How it moves: confirm an eligible regionalised production area or compartment in Spain, then check highly pathogenic avian influenza and Newcastle disease status and inspection and quarantine; compliant goods enter under China Customs inspection. Joint Announcement No. 170 of 2025 is abolished on issue and is no longer an access basis.

How to align: follow this Announcement's regionalisation and compartmentalisation requirements, not the abolished No. 170. Areas or compartments outside the scope, or consignments failing inspection and quarantine, are handled under current rules and not released.

<!-- original -->

Based on the results of the risk analysis, as of the date of issue of this Announcement, poultry and poultry products from Spain that meet the requirements for regionalisation and compartmentalisation for highly pathogenic avian influenza and Newcastle disease and for inspection and quarantine are allowed to enter China.

Joint Announcement No. 170 of 2025 of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs of the People's Republic of China is simultaneously abolished.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs of the People's Republic of China

21 May 2026
