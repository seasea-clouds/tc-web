Yes, they can enter. Announcement No. 124 admits Laos shelled peanuts: the Lao side recommends production, processing and storage enterprises to the Chinese side, which reviews, registers and publishes them; only registered enterprises may ship to China.

For a consignment, check the enterprise's Chinese registration number and the pre-export inspection record; a Lao phytosanitary certificate accompanies the goods, its additional declaration stating the full protocol sentence and the enterprise name and number; each package is marked in both Chinese and English with the destination statement. If live insects are found, fumigate before export and note the fumigant, dose, temperature and duration on the certificate.

At the Chinese port of entry, China Customs checks the certificate and registration, releasing compliant lots. Lots without a valid certificate, from unregistered enterprises, over Chinese food safety limits for aflatoxins, or carrying pests of concern to China with no effective treatment are returned or destroyed; China notifies the Lao side and may, by severity, suspend the enterprise until effective improvements are made.

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment of the Lao People's Democratic Republic (hereinafter the Lao side) on the phytosanitary requirements for the export of Laos peanuts to China, Laos peanuts meeting the following requirements are permitted to be imported into China with immediate effect:

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(a) The Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China;

(b) The Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(c) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(d) The Measures of the People's Republic of China for the Administration of Safety of Imported and Exported Food;

(e) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment of the Lao People's Democratic Republic on Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Peanuts from Laos to China.

II. Permitted Commodity

Peanuts in this Announcement (Arachis hypogaea) are non-seed shelled kernels grown and processed in Laos for human consumption or food processing.

III. Permitted Production Area

The whole territory of Laos.

IV. Enterprise Registration

The Lao side shall ensure that production, processing and storage enterprises handling peanuts exported to China meet Chinese inspection and quarantine requirements, recommend qualified enterprises to the Chinese side, and, after review and approval by the Chinese side, register them, publish the list on the GACC website and update it dynamically.

V. Pests of Concern to the Chinese Side

Peanuts exported to China shall not carry the following pests:

(1) Khapra beetle Trogoderma granarium

(2) Southern sandbur Cenchrus echinatus

(3) Chromolaena Eupatorium odoratum L.

(4) Mile-a-minute weed Mikania micrantha

VI. Pre-shipment Requirements

(a) Production requirements.

(1) The Lao side shall supervise peanut growing and production, processing and storage enterprises in establishing an integrated pest management system to reduce the occurrence of pests of concern to the Chinese side.

(2) The Lao side shall supervise enterprises handling peanuts exported to China to ensure that during collection, storage, processing and transport, or before shipment, screening and other cleaning measures remove plant residues, impurities and dangerous toxic or harmful weed seeds.

(b) Packaging and transport requirements.

Peanuts exported to China shall be packed so as to avoid leakage and contamination in transit. Packaging material shall be clean, hygienic, unused and free of toxic or harmful substances. Each package shall be marked in both Chinese and English with the words "This product is destined for the People's Republic of China/This consignment is exported to the People's Republic of China", together with the product name, origin, the name of the production, processing or storage enterprise and its Chinese registration number.

(c) Pre-export inspection, quarantine and certification requirements.

Before export, the Lao side shall inspect and quarantine peanuts exported to China and issue a phytosanitary certificate for compliant lots. The additional declaration shall state "This consignment complies with the phytosanitary requirements described in the Protocol for Peanut from Laos to China, and is free from pests of concern to China" (the consignment complies with the Protocol on Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Peanuts from Laos to China and is free from pests of concern to China), together with the name of the production, processing or storage enterprise and its Chinese registration number. Where live insects are found, fumigation shall be carried out before export and the fumigant, dose, temperature and duration recorded on the phytosanitary certificate.

VII. Entry Inspection and Quarantine

(a) Document check.

(1) Check whether the lot comes from a registered enterprise;

(2) Check whether the phytosanitary certificate is authentic and valid.

(b) Cargo inspection.

Under the relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules, and in line with this Announcement, the peanuts shall be inspected and quarantined and released if compliant.

(c) Non-compliance handling.

(1) Lots without a valid phytosanitary certificate shall be returned or destroyed;

(2) Lots from unregistered enterprises shall be returned or destroyed;

(3) Lots whose aflatoxins or other safety and hygiene items exceed Chinese national food safety standards shall be returned or destroyed;

(4) Where pests of concern to the Chinese side are found, the lot may be released after effective treatment; where no effective treatment exists, it shall be returned or destroyed;

(5) Other breaches of Chinese entry animal and plant quarantine or food safety laws, regulations and standards shall be handled under the relevant rules.

Where such breaches are found, the Chinese side shall notify the Lao side and, according to severity, suspend the eligibility of the enterprise concerned to export to China until the Lao side takes effective corrective measures.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

19 August 2026
