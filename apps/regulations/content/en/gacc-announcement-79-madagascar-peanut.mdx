It can enter from today. GACC Announcement No. 79 of 2026 was issued on 9 June and took effect the same day, opening shelled, non-seed peanut kernels from Madagascar for food or processing; seed peanuts are excluded, and the whole territory of Madagascar is eligible.

One peanut consignment goes through the whole process: shelling, sieving and hand-sorting to remove mouldy and insect-damaged kernels and foreign matter; pre-export fumigation to kill storage pests; aflatoxin testing of every lot under GB 2761; each package marked in Chinese or English for export to the People's Republic of China with the enterprise name and registration number; and entry on a phytosanitary certificate carrying an English additional declaration.

If the certificate, registration or fumigation details do not match, hold this consignment for now: no valid certificate, an unregistered enterprise or soil found means return or destruction, and live quarantine pests of concern also call for treatment. The country and the batch are process illustrations; the registration list and the certificate govern.

Under the laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter “the Chinese side”) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Sovereignty of the Republic of Madagascar (hereinafter “the Madagascar side”) on the phytosanitary requirements for the export of Madagascar peanuts to China, with effect from today, Madagascar peanuts meeting the following requirements are permitted for import.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; (iii) the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; (iv) the Administrative Measures of the People's Republic of China for the Safety of Imported and Exported Food; (v) the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Sovereignty of the Republic of Madagascar on the Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Madagascar Peanuts to China.

II. Name of the Commodity Permitted for Entry

For the purposes of this Announcement, peanuts (Arachis hypogaea L.) means shelled, non-seed peanut kernels grown and processed in Madagascar for food or processing use.

III. Permitted Places of Origin

The whole territory of Madagascar.

IV. Enterprise Registration

The Madagascar side shall ensure that enterprises producing, processing and storing peanuts for export to China meet China's phytosanitary requirements, and shall recommend compliant enterprises to the Chinese side in advance. Once they pass inspection or review by the Chinese side, they shall be registered, published on the website of the General Administration of Customs of China and updated dynamically.

V. Pests of Concern to the Chinese Side

Peanuts exported to China shall not carry the following pests: (1) peanut mottle virus (Peanut mottle virus); (2) cowpea weevil (Callosobruchus analis); (3) groundnut bruchid (Caryedon serratus); (4) khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium); (5) alfalfa Verticillium wilt pathogen (Verticillium albo-atrum).

VI. Pre-shipment Requirements

(i) Production requirements.

The Madagascar side shall, in line with the relevant international standards for phytosanitary measures, carry out pest surveys and integrated management of the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side during the production, processing and storage of peanuts for export to China. Technical staff of the Plant Protection Directorate or the Regional Directorates of Agriculture and Food Sovereignty shall carry out field monitoring and integrated pest management (IPM). Pesticides prohibited by the Chinese side shall not be used in pest control. Where necessary, the Madagascar side shall provide the Chinese side with survey reports on pest occurrence and integrated management records.

(ii) Processing, packaging and transport requirements.

(1) Peanuts for export to China shall undergo shelling, sieving and hand-sorting to remove mouldy and insect-damaged kernels and foreign matter, and shall be free of live insects, weeds, peanut shells, soil and other impurities. Before export, the Madagascar side shall apply the necessary fumigation to the peanuts to kill storage pests. (2) Once processed, peanuts for export to China shall be stored separately from peanuts destined for other markets to prevent contamination. They shall be packed in clean and hygienic new materials that meet phytosanitary requirements. Each package shall be marked in Chinese or English with “This product is exported to the People’s Republic of China / This product will be exported to the People’s Republic of China” and such information as the product name, the enterprise name and its registration number. (3) Before shipment, the means of transport shall be thoroughly inspected to prevent contamination by pests.

(iii) Pre-export inspection, quarantine and certification requirements.

(1) Before export, the Madagascar side shall carry out inspection and quarantine of peanuts for export to China and test each lot for aflatoxin limits to ensure compliance with the national food safety standard of China (GB 2761). (2) The plant quarantine laboratory of the Madagascar side shall conduct phytosanitary testing and analysis of samples of peanuts for export to China to confirm compliance with China's phytosanitary laws, regulations and standards, and none of the pests listed in Section V of this Announcement shall be detected. (3) For consignments that meet the requirements, the Madagascar side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate stating in the additional declaration: “The consignment complies with the phytosanitary requirements described in the Peanut Protocol from Madagascar to China and is free from the quarantine pests concerned by China” (the consignment complies with the requirements of the Protocol on Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Madagascar Peanuts to China and does not carry the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side), together with the names and registration numbers of the enterprises producing, processing and storing the lot and the container number. Where fumigation has been applied, the phytosanitary certificate shall state the name of the fumigant, the dose, the treatment temperature, the duration and other information.

VII. Entry Inspection and Quarantine

(i) Document verification.

(1) Verify whether the consignment comes from a registered enterprise; (2) verify whether the phytosanitary certificate is authentic and valid.

(ii) Cargo inspection.

In accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules, peanuts shall be inspected and quarantined under this Announcement and permitted entry only once found compliant.

(iii) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) Where no valid phytosanitary certificate is presented, the consignment shall be returned or destroyed; (2) where it comes from an enterprise that has not been registered, it shall be returned or destroyed; (3) where soil is found, it shall be returned or destroyed; (4) where live quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side or other live quarantine pests are found, it shall undergo treatment, return or destruction; (5) where other non-compliance with China's entry animal and plant quarantine and food safety laws, regulations and standards is found, it shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations.

Where any of the above non-compliances is found, the Chinese side shall notify the Madagascar side and, depending on the severity, suspend the eligibility of the enterprises concerned to export to China until the Madagascar side takes effective corrective measures.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs, 9 June 2026
