Import is allowed only for alfalfa pellets or bales grown and processed in Mozambique by high-temperature dehydration and compression. Processors must be assessed and recommended by Mozambique and registered by China after an on-site inspection or document check; the list is updated on the GACC site.

Farms apply integrated pest management, processors run HACCP systems; drying is kept at 170 °C for over 15 minutes, and impurities are removed before compression; clean containers bear the enterprise name, Chinese registration number and bilingual export wording; Mozambique inspects before export and issues phytosanitary and health certificates.

Consignments lacking valid certificates, from unregistered enterprises or containing soil or unauthorized GMOs are returned or destroyed; those with pests of concern to China are released after qualified treatment, otherwise returned or destroyed; China notifies Mozambique and may suspend enterprise and production area qualification until rectified.

In accordance with relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Environment and Fisheries of the Republic of Mozambique (hereinafter referred to as the Mozambican side) on the sanitary and phytosanitary requirements for the export of alfalfa hay from Mozambique to China, the import of alfalfa hay from Mozambique meeting the following requirements is permitted as of the date of this announcement:

I. Basis for inspection and quarantine

(1) Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(3) Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported and Exported Feed and Feed Additives;

(4) Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Environment and Fisheries of the Republic of Mozambique on the Sanitary and Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Alfalfa Hay from Mozambique to China.

II. Name of the commodity permitted for entry

Alfalfa hay (Medicago sativa L.) in this announcement means alfalfa pellets or alfalfa bales grown in Mozambique and processed by high-temperature dehydration and compression.

III. Enterprise registration

Alfalfa hay exported to China shall come from processing enterprises assessed and approved by the Mozambican side. Processing enterprises shall be recommended by the Mozambican side to the Chinese side, registered after passing an on-site inspection or documentary review by the Chinese side, and published and dynamically updated on the website of the General Administration of Customs.

IV. Pests of concern

Alfalfa hay exported to China shall not carry the following pests of concern to China:

(1) Bruchidius atrolineatus

(2) Spodoptera littoralis

(3) Tuta absoluta

(4) Ditylenchus africanus

(5) Meloidogyne ethiopica

(6) Rotylenchulus parvus

(7) Orobanche minor

V. Requirements for production and export of the product

(1) Cultivation, processing, storage and transport.

(1) The Mozambican side shall ensure that farms growing alfalfa hay for export to China implement effective integrated pest management (IPM) measures to prevent and control the occurrence of pests of concern to China. The Mozambican side shall require approved processing enterprises to establish and effectively operate sound hazard analysis and critical control point (HACCP) systems and traceability management systems, or to manage in accordance with their principles.

(2) Processing enterprises and the cultivation, processing and storage sites of raw materials for alfalfa hay exported to China shall be isolated from livestock farms, pastures and other similar sites to ensure that the raw materials are not contaminated by excreta, secretions and other substances of cloven-hoofed animals. During harvesting, drying, storage and transport of alfalfa hay, admixture of stray roots, soil and other foreign matter shall be prevented.

(3) The drying process of processing enterprises shall be maintained at 170 °C for more than 15 minutes to ensure the quality and safety of alfalfa hay. Alfalfa hay processing facilities shall meet the requirements of mechanical drying.

(4) Processing enterprises shall establish an effective quality control system, exercise effective control over raw material procurement, high-temperature dehydration, product testing and other stages, keep the processing area and equipment clean, take effective measures to remove impurities and contaminants before compression and baling, and shall not use metal materials for baling and packaging, so as to ensure that alfalfa hay exported to China meets the following requirements:

(1) it is free from pests of concern to China and other live pests;

(2) it is free from grass roots, plant seeds, other plant residues and foreign matter;

(3) it is free from animal feces, animal carcasses and poultry feathers;

(4) it is free from soil;

(5) it is free from genetically modified material not approved by China;

(6) it complies with the relevant requirements of the national standard of China, the Feed Hygiene Standard (GB13078).

(5) Storage facilities before shipment shall meet the following requirements:

(1) they have a relatively enclosed and independent space;

(2) they take effective measures against rodents, insects and birds to prevent pest contamination.

(6) Clean containers shall be used for transport and, where necessary, inspection and quarantine and quarantine treatment shall be carried out. Each container shall have at least one packaging marking stating the name of the processing enterprise, the Chinese registration number and the wording in Chinese and English: “Alfalfa from Mozambique export to the People's Republic of China”.

The label of each consignment of alfalfa hay exported to China shall meet the requirements of the national standard of China, the Feed Label (GB 10648).

(2) Quarantine and certification requirements before departure.

Before export, the Mozambican side shall carry out inspection and quarantine on alfalfa hay exported to China and, for those meeting the requirements, issue a phytosanitary certificate and a health certificate in accordance with international standards. The phytosanitary certificate and the health certificate shall state the name and registration number of the processing enterprise, the names and addresses of the consignor and the consignee, the container number and other information. The additional declaration of the phytosanitary certificate shall state: “This consignment complies with the Protocol on the sanitary and phytosanitary requirements for the export of alfalfa from Mozambique to China, and is free of any pest of concern for China”; the health certificate shall state: “Does not contain any hazardous substances that pose a risk to public or animal health and complies with China's safety and hygiene standards, does not contain GMOs that are not authorized by China”.

VI. Inspection and quarantine at entry

(1) Verification of documents.

(1) Verify whether the consignment comes from a registered enterprise.

(2) Verify whether the accompanying phytosanitary certificate and health certificate are authentic and valid.

(2) Cargo inspection.

In accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and in light of the requirements of this announcement, inspection and quarantine shall be carried out on imported alfalfa hay, and entry shall be permitted after passing inspection and quarantine.

(3) Handling of non-compliant cases.

(1) Goods found not to meet the requirements of item 6 of paragraph (1) of Article 5 of this announcement shall be subject to correction, return or destruction;

(2) Those without a valid phytosanitary certificate or health certificate shall be returned or destroyed;

(3) Those from unregistered processing enterprises shall be returned or destroyed;

(4) Those in which soil or unauthorized genetically modified material is found shall be returned or destroyed;

(5) Those found not to meet the requirements of item 4 of paragraph (1) of Article 5 of this announcement shall be subject to disinfestation, return or destruction in accordance with relevant laws and regulations. Those passing treatment shall be permitted to enter;

(6) Other cases of non-compliance with the requirements of China's laws and regulations on entry animal and plant quarantine and feed hygiene standards shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

Where the above violations are found, the Chinese side will notify the Mozambican side and, according to the severity of the violations, suspend the qualification of the relevant processing enterprises and production areas to export to China, or even suspend the alfalfa hay trade with China, until the Chinese side confirms that the problems have been effectively rectified.

This is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs
