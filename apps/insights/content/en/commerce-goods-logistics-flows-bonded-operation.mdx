A bonded order takes twelve steps from order to delivery: four commerce steps, four goods steps and four logistics steps. The number of steps is fixed, and the record each step leaves is fixed too, all hanging on one bonded ledger. We have run this process alongside several brands, and below we lay out the twelve steps together with the record each step leaves, then walk a batch of CoQ10 softgels through from the very start.

## Which four gates the order page clears first

The first four steps happen on your store page, while the goods are still in the overseas warehouse. When the buyer clicks order, the page must satisfy four things at once: the goods are for personal use only and a single transaction does not exceed 5000 yuan; the listed price is the tax-inclusive final price, customs duty, consumption tax and VAT are all spread into the listed price and the buyer does not have to estimate the tax; the three risk notices are completed before ordering, notice first and order second, and the sequence cannot be reversed; the buyer ticks and confirms item by item, and only then does the order stand. The limits and the notices rest on Announcement No. 486 of 2018 by the Ministry of Commerce and five other departments, and the tax-inclusive price rests on the consolidated retail import tax regime for cross-border e-commerce established by Cai Guan Shui [2016] No. 18.

The content of the three notices also follows the terms of Announcement No. 486: the goods are produced to the standards of the place of origin and the related risks are borne by the buyer; the goods carry no Chinese-language label and can be checked through the electronic label; personal use only and no resale. The notices must stand as separate items, and on the demo page the three notice lines each carry a tick time and an archive number: risk notice one ticked at 2026-09-22 14:06, archive number DA2609-0301; risk notice two ticked at 14:06, number DA2609-0302; risk notice three ticked at 14:07, number DA2609-0303. Which notice was ticked when and under which number it sits can all be pulled up afterwards.

This line rests on three hard constraints: personal use only, 5000 yuan per transaction, and the three notices before the order. If any one of the three fails, the order stops at the page gate and the goods never enter the remaining eight steps. We have seen stores that turned the notices into a block of collapsed fine print, and after switching to item-by-item ticks with archive numbers, disputes over resale and labels disappeared from the customer complaints.

## How the goods enter the zone, and how the main leg is chosen

Steps five to eight happen on the goods. Your goods first land in the overseas warehouse, are sorted and consolidated by batch, and go onto the main leg once the load is complete; the batch is the key to the later ledger reconciliation and must be kept apart already at consolidation. Step six is main leg transport: sea freight takes 25 to 35 days and suits stocking up ahead; air freight takes 5 to 7 days and suits filling gaps; the two can be combined, and the common pattern is bulk goods by sea with fast movers topped up by air. Whichever main leg is chosen, the pace of every later segment follows it.

Step seven is entering the zone: the customs supervision mode code of this channel is 1210, the full name being bonded cross-border trade e-commerce, and once the goods enter the zone they fall under bonded supervision, with the movement of every shipment recorded in the customs ledger. Step eight is ledger registration: entering the zone, in storage and exiting are registered line by line, one line per shipment, and goods and accounts match. What exactly is written in the registered lines is laid out separately below.

## From exit to delivery, the durations and exceptions of each segment

Steps nine to twelve put the goods into the buyer's hands. Step nine is picking and packing: when the order arrives, the goods are picked in the warehouse, labelled and packed, and the moment packing finishes is the moment of exit registration. Step ten is customs clearance and release: the order, payment and logistics documents are cross-checked one by one, release comes only when they agree, the waybill information syncs at this step, and the comparison basis is Announcement No. 194 of 2018 by the General Administration of Customs. Step eleven is direct express delivery: after release, domestic express delivers straight into the consumer's hands, with the full trajectory trackable.

Direct delivery to the consumer is the base of protection against diversion and counterfeits. The three gates of document cross-checking, the bonded ledger and the resale ban stand together, the goods go from the bonded warehouse straight to the buyer with no link in between where cargo can be intercepted or swapped, and every shipment on the ledger can be traced back. The repair deadline for exceptions is written on the demo page: exceptions are corrected in 24 hours, and no sick record is left overnight.

The practical durations of the segments are given as ranges in these four cells: exit stage 12 to 24 hours, arrival at the warehouse 24 hours, transshipment 3 to 24 hours, inspection 3 to 48 hours. These ranges float with the port of entry and the season; production and restocking plans use them, and no promise is made outward on their basis.

## Walk a batch of CoQ10 softgels through from the very start

Treat the batch of CoQ10 softgels that entered the zone on 09-20 as one thing to watch, and the twelve steps string together like this:

- On 09-20, 1200 bottles of batch PC20260412 enter the zone, registered on ledger line 0101, balance 1200;
- During the days in storage, the store page shows the tax-inclusive final price, the buyer completes the three risk notices item by item before ordering, the three notice lines carry the tick times 14:06 and 14:07 and the archive numbers DA2609-0301 to DA2609-0303, and the order amount stays within 5000 yuan;
- On 09-22, 260 of the bottles exit the zone, registered on ledger line 0103, and the balance rolls to 940;
- After the exit, the order, payment and logistics documents are cross-checked, release comes once they agree, the waybill syncs, express delivers straight to the consumer, and the trajectory is trackable;
- Another batch of fish oil capsules (batch PC20260508) exits 150 bottles on 09-23, the balance rolls from 800 to 650, and the two batches each roll their own account on the same page.

On this run you only need to watch three places: the notice records are generated before the order stands, the ledger lines are generated entry by entry with each entering and exiting, and the result of the document cross-check hangs on the release step. With all three in place, where this cargo came from and where it went can be answered at any moment; missing any one of the three, the missing stretch has to be patched by someone digging through the records.

The ledger thumbnail table embedded in the flow page footer is the quick-reference version of this line: ZC2026-0101 entering the zone 1200, ZC2026-0102 in storage 800, ZC2026-0103 exiting the zone 260, each line carrying two entries for details and log, with a note beside it that one shipment takes one line, registered line by line from entering to exiting. Only with number, date and balance all present does this ledger stand.

## Get this ledger standing before going live

When the twelve steps are done, what remains is a ledger registered entry by entry and a string of numbered notice records. Nail down two things before going live: build the ledger fields to the eight fields in one pass, filling batch number, expiry and balance from the very first entry into the zone, leaving no blank lines; write the notice template into the order page following the terms of Announcement No. 486, with tick times and archive numbers saved into the database automatically. Picking, transshipment and express can be handed to suppliers segment by segment, but the ledger must stay under your own name, because when something goes wrong it is the only original record you can produce.

The dates, batches and balances in the demo interface are demo data, there to show how a line is generated and how it rolls, and in practice the current customs ledger and warehouse records govern; the durations of the segments are practical parameters that float with the port of entry and the season, and the limits and notice terms follow the announcements in force.
