Bovine colostrum powder can enter China through the Mode 1210 bonded route without a Blue Hat and without overseas producer registration: these are the two heaviest gates in the general trade channel. The basis is Article 3 of Announcement No. 486 of 2018 (Shang Caifa [2018] No. 486): goods imported under Mode 1210 are regulated as personal-use inbound items, and first-import licensing, registration, and filing do not apply. Dairy has a third exemption: GACC Announcement No. 114 of 2021 limits quarantine approval to four dairy categories such as raw milk, and bovine colostrum powder is not on that list, so the accompanying official health certificate requirement falls away too.

But a procedure exemption is not a liability exemption. The Advertising Law and the Food Safety Law still govern claims, the personal-use boundary cannot be crossed, and the three-document cross-check, buyer real-name verification, quota deduction, and ledger retention stay. The sections below set out where the exemptions end, where the two channels divide, how conventional food and the Blue Hat compare, and what overseas litigation does and does not change; every key judgment comes with a document number.

## Three exemptions: what you save is time and registration cost

**No overseas producer registration.** GACC Decree No. 280 (effective 1 June 2026) governs overseas producer registration for food imported as cargo, and Announcement No. 27 of 2026 sets the filing scope for the China registration number. Both apply to the cargo channel only. Article 3 of Announcement No. 486 states that personal-use goods under Mode 1210 fall outside the overseas producer registration requirement, so the entire registration process is out of scope for your bonded shipment.

**No Blue Hat first.** Bovine colostrum powder is declared as conventional food, not registered or filed as health food. If you later want health-function claims, that is when the registration route comes in. Listing timing and registration timing are two separate decisions and can be scheduled separately.

**No quarantine approval or official health certificate for dairy.** Announcement No. 114 lists only four dairy categories, including raw milk, for quarantine approval; bovine colostrum powder is not among them, and the official health certificate requirement does not apply.

Stack the three together and you save months of registration time and about 14,000 to 69,000 US dollars in registration spend (originally 100,000 to 500,000 yuan, converted at 7.2 yuan per US dollar, for reference only). For a phase-one pilot, keeping that money and time in the cash flow matters more than anything else.

## Three non-exemptions: liability never moves

**Claims remain bound by two laws.** The Advertising Law and the Food Safety Law restrict efficacy assertions and disease prevention or treatment wording in the same way for bonded goods and general trade goods. The channel exempts access procedures, not content review.

**Personal use only, no resale.** Goods under Mode 1210 are limited to personal use and must not flow back into the domestic market for resale. Cross that line once and the exemption under Announcement No. 486 ends immediately.

**Four musts that cannot be skipped.** Three-document cross-check (order, payment, logistics), buyer real-name verification, annual quota deduction, and ledger retention. GACC Announcement No. 194 of 2018 sets the timing precisely: order and payment data are pushed first, logistics data is written back, and the quota is deducted at clearance.

Once goods are in the zone, they are regulated as domestic conventional food, and the red lines above apply inside the zone as usual.

## The line between the two channels, and where it is most often misread

Decree No. 280 governs food imported as cargo and placed on the domestic market for human consumption; Article 3 of Announcement No. 486 provides the basis for the Mode 1210 exemption, with Article 75 of GACC Decree No. 249 as the reference clause on the boundary for personal-use inbound items. This line is often misread in practice, so our working rule is simple: for the cargo channel, read Decree No. 280; for the bonded channel, read Announcement No. 486 together with Article 75 of Decree No. 249.

There is a second layer to the line: the exemption follows the channel, not the product. The same batch of bovine colostrum powder has all three exemptions under Mode 1210; the day you move it to general trade for domestic supermarkets, overseas producer registration under Decree No. 280 and the China registration number under Announcement No. 27 both come back. The exemption ends the moment the goods shift to general trade or resale.

## Conventional food first, or a Blue Hat first?

Conventional food is the identity we recommend for phase one under Mode 1210:

- No registration and no filing, which removes the whole registration cycle; phase one goes straight through Mode 1210
- Regulated as personal-use inbound items; zone entry and listing are faster
- Function claims are restricted: no health-function claims, no disease prevention or treatment wording
- Labels follow conventional-food rules: ingredient list and warnings as for conventional food, and no function wording on pages or packaging

A Blue Hat health food is the other route:

- Registration or filing is a precondition, and both cycle and cost rise by an order of magnitude
- Zone entry is slower and listing comes much later than conventional food
- In exchange you get claim room within the registered functions, for example the approved wording "helps strengthen immunity"
- The label burden is heavy: you must state that health food is not medicine, and identify the suitable population

When the choice is difficult, the plan offers a quick fork test: look at sugar, dosage form, and claims. A hit on any one points to the Blue Hat route; the closer the product form and claims sit to functional food, the more likely the regulator treats it as health food, and for an uncertain SKU we take the stricter reading.

## Four red lines: never say, never do

- Conventional-food pages and packaging carry no health-function wording at all. Phrases such as "regulates immunity" or "improves sleep" cannot appear even once under a conventional-food identity.
- No disease wording. Words such as "prevent" or "treat" are never used for a food identity; food is not medicine.
- Infant formula must not contain bovine colostrum, or dairy ingredients made from it. The basis is MOH Announcement No. 335 of 2012, and no channel exemption applies.
- Claims are locked before listing. Pages, detail pages, livestream scripts, and customer-service lines all use the same approved claim list.

The first three are content boundaries; the fourth is a process boundary. Break any one and liability falls on your company and the operating entity alike. There is no such thing as "the platform reviewed it, so we are covered".

## Do overseas lawsuits block the way? Two standards, judged separately

The verdict first: an overseas lawsuit of the same kind does not block China market access. China judges by concentration limits; GB 2762-2025 (effective 2 September 2026) sets contaminant limits, and the lead limit for milk and dairy products is 0.2 mg/kg. Some overseas markets judge by exposure thresholds; California Proposition 65 uses MADL (maximum allowable dose level) and NSRL (no significant risk level), which belong to that family. The two systems do not cite each other and cannot be mixed in one calculation.

Put the access-stage risks on one table:

| Risk | Level | Owner | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Port enforcement | High | Ours | Enforcement interpretation filed in advance; alternate port plan |
| Missing CoA | Mid | Both | Request CoA; gaps go to third-party retesting |
| Claim creep | Mid | Both | Content review |
| Mode 9610 misuse | Mid | Ours | Dormant in phase one; flag reserved |
| HS classification dispute | Mid | Ours | Ruling W2020-29 on file |
| Slow first batch | Low to mid | Both | Stock per volume plan; return to zone |

Put the two approaches back on one map: the vertical axis is the overseas exposure threshold, the horizontal axis is China compliance impact. Overseas lawsuits of the same kind and labeling-type cases sit on the high-exposure, low-impact side; access execution such as ports and classification, testing and claim compliance, and a slow first batch sit on the high-impact side. Each system judges on its own, and a conclusion from one side cannot cancel the other.

Against that table, we take two actions: request test reports for the same batches from the last 12 months (heavy metals and microbiology), and prepare the PR contingency plan in advance, with customer-service and media scripts ready and regulatory inquiries answered in 24 hours.

## One batch of bovine colostrum powder, end to end

Put all the rules above into one SKU: a 300-gram jar of bovine colostrum powder, imported through Mode 1210 bonded e-commerce.

- Category: conventional food. HS code 0404.9000 is on the 2022 version of the cross-border e-commerce retail import list, with the note that it is limited to bonded e-commerce goods; confirm the code's eligibility for the bonded route first.
- All three exemptions apply: no overseas producer registration (Article 3 of Announcement No. 486), no Blue Hat, and no quarantine approval or official health certificate (Announcement No. 114).
- Pages and labels: only ingredients, origin, and directions for use; no function wording and no disease wording.
- Channel flow: three documents first, with order and payment data pushed first and logistics written back; buyer real-name verification; annual quota deducted at clearance (26,000 yuan, about 3,600 US dollars); ledger retained.
- Documents: obtain test reports for the same batches from the last 12 months (heavy metals and microbiology); keep the PR contingency plan ready.
- Resale red line: sell only to individual consumers for personal use. Any wholesale or secondary sale to a domestic distributor means the exemption ends and the goods must go through access again as cargo.

After launch, if market feedback calls for function claims, evaluate the Blue Hat registration route then: by that point you will have real sales data, and the registration decision is more grounded.
