Announcement No. 45 of China Customs takes effect on 22 April 2026: mainland customs now issues only electronic inspection and quarantine certificates for animal and plant products sent to Macao, China, and stops paper phytosanitary, animal health and veterinary (health) certificates. Verification moves from scanned paper to the certificate number.

A consignment of fresh fruit bound for Macao, China: the consignor or agent applies for an export electronic certificate through cloud issuance on the China International Trade “Single Window” or “Internet Plus Customs”. Mainland customs issues the certificate, the Municipal Affairs Bureau of Macao, China verifies its number through the official data exchange system, and no paper scan is required.

Network failures follow the bilateral contingency mechanism: a paper certificate from mainland customs still clears the goods. Paperless is not an exemption from declaration; applying, issuing and verifying all remain.

Announcement No. 45 of 2026 of the General Administration of Customs

To advance paperless inspection and quarantine certificates, and pursuant to the electronic certificate cooperation agreement between the General Administration of Customs and the Secretariat for Administration and Justice of the Macao Special Administrative Region Government, the two sides have agreed through consultation to make inspection and quarantine certificates for animal and plant products sent from the mainland to Macao paperless. The following matters are hereby announced:

I. Mainland customs issues electronic inspection and quarantine certificates for animal and plant products sent to Macao, and no longer issues paper phytosanitary certificates, animal health certificates or veterinary (health) certificates.

II. Consignors or consignees of import and export goods, or their agents, may apply for export inspection and quarantine electronic certificates through the cloud issuance mode of the China International Trade “Single Window” (https://www.singlewindow.cn) or “Internet Plus Customs” (http://online.customs.gov.cn).

III. The Municipal Affairs Bureau of Macao recognizes electronic inspection and quarantine certificates transmitted by mainland customs through the official data exchange system, and verifies the certificates by their electronic certificate number.

IV. Where network failures or other special circumstances arise, an enterprise may, under the bilateral contingency mechanism, complete customs clearance with a paper inspection and quarantine certificate issued by mainland customs.

This Announcement takes effect on 22 April 2026.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

20 April 2026
