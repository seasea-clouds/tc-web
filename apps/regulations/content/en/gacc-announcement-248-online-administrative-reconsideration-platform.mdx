From 1 January 2026 customs offices across China use one online platform for administrative reconsideration applications: applicants choosing the internet channel must use it, and the dedicated reconsideration email boxes of the General Administration and its directly affiliated customs offices no longer accept applications.

A company disputing a duty assessment by a directly affiliated customs office registers on 2 January 2026 at https://online.customs.gov.cn/publicService/, files its application, supplements documents online, then tracks progress and receives legal documents there; cases already accepted before the launch keep the old procedure.

The platform is not the only route: applicants may still file offline, or register and switch to the online channel; where system failures prevent online handling, customs handles the case offline under the Administrative Reconsideration Law.

<!-- original -->

To give full play to administrative reconsideration as the main channel for resolving administrative disputes and to make it easier for citizens, legal persons and other organizations to apply for and take part in reconsideration, the General Administration of Customs has decided to launch the online platform for administrative reconsideration applications across all customs offices from 1 January 2026. The relevant matters are announced as follows:

(1) Applicants who choose the internet channel to apply for administrative reconsideration to customs shall use the online platform for administrative reconsideration applications.

(2) The platform is at https://online.customs.gov.cn/publicService/. After registering, applicants may use it to enter, supplement and withdraw applications, check the progress of cases and receive legal documents.

(3) Once the platform is in operation, as the internet channel for submitting applications to customs, the dedicated reconsideration email boxes previously used by the General Administration and its directly affiliated customs offices will no longer accept applications.

(4) Once the platform is in operation, applicants who apply through non-internet channels may choose the offline route, or register on the platform and continue online. Cases accepted before the launch keep the original procedure.

(5) Once the platform is in operation, where system failures or similar situations prevent online handling, customs will handle the cases offline in accordance with the Administrative Reconsideration Law of the People's Republic of China and other provisions.

This announcement takes effect on 1 January 2026.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

16 December 2025
