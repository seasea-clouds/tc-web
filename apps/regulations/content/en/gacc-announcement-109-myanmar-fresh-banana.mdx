From 1 September 2026 imports are allowed. GACC Announcement No. 109 of 2026, issued 28 July 2026, repeals No. 49 of 2022 concurrently; only Myanmar unripe green bananas picked within 10 to 11 weeks of flowering qualify; overripe yellow bananas are barred from export to China.

One Myanmar banana lot goes end to end: orchards and packhouses Myanmar-reviewed, China-registered; mealybugs checked every 15 days from flowering to harvest; TR4 pest-free production site per ISPM 10; boxes and pallets marked "For export to the People's Republic of China"; 1% sampling pre-departure; entry on a phytosanitary certificate with an English additional declaration.

If the registration list or certificate declaration does not match, hold your consignment; overripe bananas barred from entry; TR4 or soil means disinfestation, return or destruction. Country and batch are illustrative; the list, permit and effective date govern.

<!-- original -->

Under the laws and regulations of China and the relevant provisions agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter "the Chinese side") and the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Irrigation of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar (hereinafter "the Myanmar side") on the phytosanitary requirements for the export of fresh bananas from Myanmar to China, fresh bananas from Myanmar that meet the following relevant requirements are permitted for import.

I. Quarantine Basis

(i) the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its Implementing Regulations; (iii) the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its Implementing Regulations; (iv) the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported Fruits; (v) the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Banana Fruits from Myanmar to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Irrigation of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar.

II. Commodity Permitted for Import

Myanmar fresh bananas (scientific name Musa sapientum, English name Banana) (hereinafter referred to as bananas) means unripe green bananas harvested within 10–11 weeks of flowering; any overripe yellow bananas shall not be permitted for export to China.

III. Permitted Production Areas

Banana production areas in Myanmar.

IV. Approved Orchards and Packing Houses

Orchards and packing houses for bananas exported to China shall be reviewed by the Myanmar side and approved and registered by the Chinese side. The registration information includes the name, address and registration number, so as to enable accurate traceability when the exported goods do not comply with the relevant provisions of this Announcement. Before each export season, the Myanmar side shall provide the Chinese side with the list of registered orchards and packing houses; upon review and approval by the Chinese side, the list shall be published on the website of the General Administration of Customs and updated regularly.

V. List of Quarantine Pests of Concern to China

(1) Giant African snail Achatina fulica (2) Spiralling whitefly Aleurodicus dispersus (3) Chilli fruit fly Bactrocera latifrons (4) Grey pineapple mealybug Dysmicoccus neobrevipes (5) Banana Fusarium wilt race 4 Fusarium oxysporum Schlecht f. sp. cubense Race 4 (6) South Seas mealybug Planococcus lilacinus (7) Pacific mealybug Planococcus minor

VI. Pre-export Management

(i) Orchard management.

(1) Banana orchards for export to China shall, under the supervision of the Myanmar side, establish a sound quality management system and traceability system, implement Good Agricultural Practices (GAP), maintain orchard hygiene, such as no sources of pollution affecting fruit production in the vicinity and the timely removal of fallen and rotten fruit, and implement Integrated Pest Management (IPM), including regular pest monitoring and surveys, physical, chemical or biological control, and cultural practices. (2) The Myanmar side shall, in accordance with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 6 (ISPM 6), draw up a management plan for the quarantine pests of concern to China and organise orchard monitoring. If pests or corresponding symptoms are found during monitoring, integrated management measures including chemical, physical or biological control shall be taken to ensure that Myanmar bananas are free from the quarantine pests of concern to China.

(3) Banana orchards for export to China shall, under the supervision and guidance of the Myanmar side, carry out monitoring and integrated control of the quarantine pests of concern to China. Pest monitoring and control shall be implemented under the guidance of technicians trained by the Myanmar side or its authorised institutions.

(4) Banana orchards for export to China shall keep records of pest monitoring and control, which shall be provided by the Myanmar side to the Chinese side upon request. Records of chemical pest control shall specify detailed information, including the names of chemical agents used during the growing season, active ingredients, application doses and dates. (5) For mealybugs such as the Pacific mealybug, the South Seas mealybug and the grey pineapple mealybug, orchards for export to China shall conduct pest monitoring once every 15 days from the flowering stage to harvest, checking fruits, stems, branches and leaves for the presence of scale insects. In addition to visual inspection, multiple physical or chemical methods shall be used at branches and stems to monitor pests; once detected, effective control shall be carried out.

(6) With regard to banana Fusarium wilt race 4 (hereinafter referred to as TR4), banana orchards for export to China shall, in accordance with the guiding principles of International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 10 (ISPM 10), establish a pest-free production site for TR4, the status of which shall be approved by both the Myanmar side and the Chinese side. If TR4 is detected at the production site, the Myanmar side shall immediately suspend banana exports to China from that pest-free production site and notify the Chinese side. Only when no TR4 has been found for 3 consecutive months and the production site is under the supervision of the Myanmar side may exports resume. Orchards shall record pest monitoring and control information and retain it for at least two years, and provide it to the Chinese side upon request. The records shall include information such as inspection dates, the names of pests found, and the active ingredients, doses and application dates of the agricultural chemicals used.

(ii) Packing house management.

(1) The processing, packaging, storage and shipment of bananas for export to China shall be carried out under the supervision of the Myanmar side. (2) The processing and packaging of bananas shall take place at registered packing houses. During processing, bananas shall undergo soaking and washing, manual sorting, grading and other procedures to ensure they are free from insects, mites, snails, rotten fruit, weed seeds, plant residues and soil. (3) If packed bananas need to be stored before export, they shall be placed in storage immediately and stored separately to avoid re-infestation by pests. (4) Packing houses shall establish a traceability system to ensure that bananas for export to China can be traced back to registered orchards, and shall record the processing and packaging date, the name or registration number of the source orchard, the quantity of bananas, the export date, the export quantity, the country of destination, the vehicle or container number, the seal number and other information. If chemicals are used during processing, the names, active ingredients, doses and application dates of the chemicals shall also be recorded. Records shall be kept for at least two years.

(iii) Packaging requirements.

(1) Packaging materials shall be clean, hygienic and unused, and shall comply with the relevant phytosanitary requirements of China. If wooden packaging is used, it shall comply with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15). (2) Each packing box shall be labelled in Chinese or English with the fruit name, country, production area (province or state, city or county), and the names or registration numbers of the orchard and packing house. Each packing box and pallet shall bear, in Chinese or English, the marking "Exported to the People's Republic of China". (3) Containers or enclosed vehicles loaded with bananas for export to China shall be clean and hygienic and sealed; the seal shall remain intact upon arrival at the Chinese port of entry.

(iv) Inspection and quarantine before departure.

(1) The Myanmar side shall conduct sampling inspection of each consignment at a rate of 1%. (2) If quarantine pests of concern to China or soil are found, the entire consignment shall not be permitted for export to China. The Myanmar side shall immediately suspend banana exports to China from the relevant orchard or packing house until the cause is identified and effective corrective measures are taken; relevant records shall also be kept and provided to the Chinese side upon request.

(v) Phytosanitary certificate requirements.

Bananas that pass quarantine inspection shall be issued a phytosanitary certificate by the Myanmar side in accordance with the relevant provisions of International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12 (ISPM 12), indicating the names or registration numbers of the orchard and packing house, and stating in the additional declaration: "This consignment complies with the requirements specified in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Banana Fruits from Myanmar to China, and is free from the quarantine pests of concern to China." (This consignment complies with the requirements of the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Banana Fruits from Myanmar to China and is free from the quarantine pests of concern to China.)

VII. Import Inspection, Quarantine and Handling of Non-compliance

When bananas for export to China arrive at a Chinese port of entry, China Customs shall check the relevant documents and labels and carry out inspection and quarantine.

(i) Verification of relevant certificates and labels.

(1) Verify whether the imported bananas have obtained the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit. (2) Verify whether the phytosanitary certificate complies with Article VI (v) of this Announcement. (3) Verify whether the labels on the packing boxes and pallets comply with Article VI (iii) of this Announcement.

(ii) Import inspection and quarantine.

(1) Bananas for export to China shall enter through ports permitted by the Chinese side for the import of fruits. (2) When bananas arrive at a Chinese port of entry, China Customs shall check the relevant documents and labels and carry out inspection and quarantine in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations, national standards and the requirements of this Announcement; those that pass inspection and quarantine shall be permitted entry.

(iii) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) If the consignment is found to come from an unregistered orchard or packing house, or contains overripe bananas, it shall not be permitted entry. (2) If quarantine pests of concern to China, quarantine pests newly occurring in Myanmar, or soil are found, the consignment shall be subject to disinfestation treatment, return or destruction. (3) If the consignment is found not to comply with Chinese food safety laws, regulations and national standards, it shall be returned or destroyed.

This Announcement shall take effect on 1 September 2026. GACC Announcement No. 49 of 2022 is repealed simultaneously.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China
