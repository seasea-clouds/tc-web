Entry is open. On 22 December 2025, GACC Announcement No. 253 admitted Indonesian aquatic animal protein for feed: fishmeal, fish oil and other aquatic animal protein and fats, with raw material limited to aquatic animals caught in Indonesian waters or on the high seas, or farmed, and marine mammals excluded.

Before one fishmeal consignment ships, check the producer is approved and recommended by Indonesia, registered with China and runs HACCP; the raw material carries no terrestrial animal components; core temperature at least 85 °C for 15 minutes; the Chinese-language label carries the “not for human consumption” warning under GB 10648; official sampling precedes export and the original sanitary certificate travels with the goods.

At the port, Chinese customs verifies the registered source and the certificate, then inspects the goods. No valid certificate, an unregistered producer, mismatched goods, unapproved animal components, soil or animal carcasses mean return or destruction; failing hygiene items mean disinfection, return or destruction. A number quoted by phone does not count: the list and the original certificate govern.

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Quarantine Agency of the Republic of Indonesia (hereinafter referred to as the Indonesian side) on the quarantine and health requirements for Indonesian aquatic animal protein exported to China, imports of products meeting the following requirements are permitted from the date of this Announcement.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(iii) The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported and Exported Feed and Feed Additives;

(iv) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Quarantine Agency of the Republic of Indonesia on the Quarantine and Health Requirements for Aquatic Animal Protein and Fats such as Fishmeal and Fish Oil for Feed Exported from Indonesia to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Scope of Products Permitted for Import

The products permitted for import under this Announcement include aquatic animal protein and fats such as fishmeal and fish oil for feed, and animal-derived feed made from aquatic animals (excluding marine mammals) and by-products generated during their processing.

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

(i) Approved by the Indonesian side, recommended by the Indonesian side to the Chinese side, and registered with the Chinese side;

(ii) Implementing a quality management system based on Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP), or establishing a quality management system following the HACCP concept, and formulating and effectively implementing systems for quality, safety and hygiene control, self-inspection and self-control, product traceability and recall;

(iii) Under the effective supervision of the Indonesian side and in compliance with the relevant laws and regulations of the Indonesian side.

IV. Requirements for Raw Materials

(i) Aquatic animals caught in waters under Indonesian jurisdiction or on the high seas, or farmed aquatic animals (excluding marine mammals);

(ii) Produced from the whole animals referred to above or from by-products of aquatic product processing plants;

(iii) No aquatic animals that died of animal diseases or were culled to control an animal disease outbreak shall be used;

(iv) Free of animal-derived components other than those of aquatic animals, and not contaminated by animal-derived products from third countries.

V. Production and Processing Requirements

(i) Under the effective supervision of the Indonesian side;

(ii) Heat treatment at a core temperature of not less than 85 °C for not less than 15 minutes, or other equivalent processing methods recognised by the Chinese side;

(iii) No animal-derived raw materials of unknown species and no prohibited substances added during processing;

(iv) Effective measures taken during and after production to avoid contamination.

VI. Safety and Hygiene Requirements

(i) The products are permitted to be sold freely within Indonesia;

(ii) The products comply with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and relevant laws, regulations and requirements;

(iii) The products contain no toxic or harmful substances endangering animal health and comply with China's feed safety and hygiene standards;

(iv) Before export, the products are randomly sampled and tested by the competent official authority of the Indonesian side, with results meeting the following requirements:

(1) Salmonella: not detected in 25 g of sample: n=5, c=0, m=0, M=0;

(2) Enterobacteriaceae: 1 g of sample: n=5, c=2, m=10, M=300;

where:

n - number of samples tested;

m - the minimum bacterial count (threshold); if the bacterial count in all samples does not exceed m, the result is acceptable;

M - the maximum bacterial count; if the bacterial count in one or more samples equals or exceeds M, the result is unacceptable;

c - the number of samples with a bacterial count between m and M; if the bacterial count in the other samples is less than or equal to m, the result is still considered acceptable;

(3) In a laboratory recognised by the competent official authority, the polymerase chain reaction (PCR) method is used to test for ruminant-derived components, and the result is negative.

VII. Packaging, Labelling, Storage and Transport Requirements

(i) Packaging is new and clean, with good sealing and moisture-proof performance, and is not easily damaged;

(ii) The outer packaging of the products bears Chinese-language labels, which meet the requirements of the Chinese national standard Feed Labelling (GB 10648) and state warnings such as “not for human consumption” or “for feed use only”;

(iii) Effective measures are taken during storage and transport of the products to avoid contamination.

VIII. Pre-export Inspection and Certificate Requirements

(i) The Indonesian side is responsible for inspection and quarantine of the products exported to China and issues a health certificate according to the certificate model confirmed by both sides, certifying that the products meet the requirements of the Protocol;

(ii) Each consignment exported to China shall be accompanied by at least one original official health certificate.

IX. Inspection and Quarantine at Entry

(i) Document verification.

(1) Verify whether the products come from a registered enterprise;

(2) Verify whether the health certificate is authentic and valid.

(ii) Goods inspection.

Chinese customs carries out inspection and quarantine of the products exported from Indonesia to China in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and in light of the requirements of this Announcement. Products that pass inspection and quarantine are permitted to enter.

(iii) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) The following circumstances shall lead to return or destruction: no valid health certificate; products from a non-registered Indonesian production enterprise; goods inconsistent with the certificate; animal-derived components not approved detected; soil, animal carcasses, animal excreta or quarantine pests found and no effective quarantine treatment possible; labels not meeting the standard and unable to be corrected.

(2) Where testing shows that safety and hygiene items do not meet China's feed hygiene standards, disinfection, return or destruction shall be applied.

(3) Where broken packages or ruptured containers are found, the cargo owner or agent shall sort and repair them; where damaged packaging poses a risk of spreading animal or plant diseases, quarantine treatment shall be applied to the contaminated sites, goods and utensils.

(4) For production enterprises with serious non-compliance, the Chinese side will take relevant measures in accordance with the law and revoke the qualification of the enterprise concerned to export to China.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

22 December 2025
