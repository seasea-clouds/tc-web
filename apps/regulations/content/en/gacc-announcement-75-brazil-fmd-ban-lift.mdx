Brazil is now foot-and-mouth disease free nationwide. Customs and agriculture authorities issued Announcement No. 75 of 2026 on 29 May, effective immediately: based on risk analysis, the ban on foot-and-mouth disease in northern Brazil is lifted and the whole country recognized disease-free. Earlier lifts covered only some regions; this one extends north.

Related products follow the nationwide disease-free status recognized here, not the old northern-region ban. A source is usable if it falls within the recognized scope and meets current inspection and quarantine requirements; otherwise it is handled under current rules and not released.

Do not cite the old basis: the three joint announcements No. 213 of 2002 (12 Brazilian states recognized as vaccination-free zones), No. 565 of 2005 (preventing foot-and-mouth disease introduction into China) and No. 123 of 2009 (lifting the ban on some regions) are simultaneously abolished; this announcement prevails.

<!-- original -->

Based on the results of the risk analysis, from the date of issuance of this announcement, the ban on foot-and-mouth disease in northern Brazil is lifted and Brazil is recognized as free of foot-and-mouth disease throughout its territory.

Joint Announcement No. 213 of 2002 of the former Ministry of Agriculture and the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine (Announcement on Recognizing 12 States of Brazil as Foot-and-Mouth Disease Vaccination-Free Zones), Joint Announcement No. 565 of 2005 of the former Ministry of Agriculture and the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine (Announcement on Preventing the Introduction of Foot-and-Mouth Disease into China), and the Announcement on Lifting the Ban on Foot-and-Mouth Disease in Parts of Brazil (Joint Announcement No. 123 of 2009 of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine and the former Ministry of Agriculture) are simultaneously abolished.

This announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

29 May 2026
