Azerbaijani hazelnuts and almonds are approved for import into China under General Administration of Customs Announcement No. 207 of 2025, effective on publication. Mature fruits of the European hazel Corylus avellana L and the almond tree Prunus dulcis must be selected, dried, shelled and uncooked, and exported only by enterprises recommended by Azerbaijan and registered by China.

Hazelnuts must be free from the fall webworm Hyphantria cunea and the hazelnut weevil Curculio nucum; almonds must be free from the fall webworm Hyphantria cunea and the brown rot fungus Monilinia fructicola. Neither may carry live insects, eggs, soil or impurities, and both require guidance and supervision by Azerbaijan.

Each consignment requires a phytosanitary certificate and an English statement, listed separately for hazelnuts and almonds. Packaging must carry product and traceability information in Chinese and English. Consignments undergo customs inspection and quarantine on entry, and non-compliant goods are handled according to law.

<!-- original -->

Under the laws and regulations of China and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Food Safety Agency of the Republic of Azerbaijan (hereinafter the Azerbaijani side) concerning the inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for Azerbaijani hazelnuts and almonds exported to China, the import of Azerbaijani hazelnuts and almonds meeting the following requirements is permitted as of today.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(2) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(3) The Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations;

(4) The Measures for the Safety Administration of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China;

(5) The Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Hazelnut and Almond Exported from the Republic of Azerbaijan to the People's Republic of China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Food Safety Agency of the Republic of Azerbaijan (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Products Permitted for Import

Hazelnuts and almonds permitted for import refer to nuts for human consumption produced in Azerbaijan from mature fruits of the European hazel (Corylus avellana L) and the almond tree (Prunus dulcis) grown in Azerbaijan, which have been selected, dried, shelled and otherwise processed but not cooked.

III. Food Safety Requirements

Azerbaijani hazelnuts and almonds exported to China shall comply with Chinese laws and regulations on food safety and plant quarantine, national food safety standards and the provisions of the Protocol.

IV. Phytosanitary Requirements

(1) Hazelnuts exported to China must not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: the fall webworm (Hyphantria cunea) and the hazelnut weevil (Curculio nucum).

(2) Almonds exported to China must not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: the fall webworm (Hyphantria cunea) and the brown rot fungus (Monilinia fructicola).

(3) Hazelnuts and almonds exported to China must not carry live insects, insect eggs or soil, and must not be mixed with impurities such as weed seeds, plant debris, metal foreign matter and gravel.

V. Enterprise Requirements

The Azerbaijani side shall inspect the production, processing and storage facilities for hazelnuts and almonds exported to China in accordance with the relevant requirements of the Chinese side for the registration of overseas food production enterprises, and recommend enterprises meeting the Chinese requirements to the Chinese side. The Chinese side shall register enterprises that meet the requirements. An enterprise may export its products to China only after obtaining registration in China. The list of registered Azerbaijani enterprises exporting hazelnuts and almonds to China may be queried on the website of the General Administration of Customs.

VI. Raw Material Requirements

The raw materials of hazelnuts and almonds exported to China shall come from hazel and almond plantations confirmed by the Azerbaijani side as meeting the relevant phytosanitary and food safety requirements.

VII. Processing Requirements

The Azerbaijani side shall guide and supervise processes including the cultivation, production, processing, storage, transport and export of the raw materials of hazelnuts and almonds exported to China, so as to ensure compliance with the relevant food safety and phytosanitary requirements of China and Azerbaijan and prevent contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

VIII. Packaging and Labelling Requirements

Hazelnuts and almonds exported to China shall be packaged in clean, sanitary, breathable, new materials that meet food safety and phytosanitary requirements, and spillage during transport shall be prevented. Each package shall bear in Chinese or English the statement „This product is exported to the People's Republic of China“ as well as traceability information including the product name, shelf life, place of origin, the production and processing enterprise and its registration number in China, and the name and address of the exporter. The above information may be affixed to the packaging in the form of labels.

IX. Transport Requirements

Before shipment, the Azerbaijani side shall inspect the means of transport for hazelnuts and almonds exported to China. The means of transport shall meet sanitary requirements and must not carry quarantine pests or other restricted quarantine objects, such as weed seeds, live insects, plant debris, soil and other impurities.

X. Certificate Requirements

Before export, the Azerbaijani side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for hazelnuts and almonds exported to China that have passed inspection and quarantine and meet the requirements of the Protocol.

The additional declaration column of the phytosanitary certificate for hazelnuts exported to China shall state the name of the hazelnut production and processing enterprise, its registration number in China and the following declaration: „The consignment certified by this phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Hazelnut and Almond Exported from the Republic of Azerbaijan to the People's Republic of China signed by both sides on October 14th, 2025. The consignment is free from Hyphantria cunea, Curculio nucum and other quarantine pests of concern to the People's Republic of China.“ (This phytosanitary certificate certifies that the consignment complies with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Hazelnut and Almond Exported from the Republic of Azerbaijan to the People's Republic of China signed by both sides on 14 October 2025. This consignment is free from the fall webworm (Hyphantria cunea), the hazelnut weevil (Curculio nucum) and other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side.)

The additional declaration column of the phytosanitary certificate for almonds exported to China shall state the name of the almond production and processing enterprise, its registration number in China and the following declaration: „The consignment certified by this phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Hazelnut and Almond Exported from the Republic of Azerbaijan to the People's Republic of China signed by both sides on October 14th, 2025. The consignment is free from Hyphantria cunea, Monilinia fructicola and other quarantine pests of concern to the People's Republic of China.“ (This phytosanitary certificate certifies that the consignment complies with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Hazelnut and Almond Exported from the Republic of Azerbaijan to the People's Republic of China signed by both sides on 14 October 2025. This consignment is free from the fall webworm (Hyphantria cunea), the brown rot fungus (Monilinia fructicola) and other quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side.)

XI. Entry Inspection and Quarantine and Handling of Non-Compliance

(1) Entry inspection and quarantine. Upon arrival of hazelnuts and almonds exported to China at the entry ports in China, the Chinese customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine supervision and grant entry only after they pass inspection.

(2) Handling of non-compliance. If the Chinese side finds that hazelnuts and almonds exported to China violate Chinese laws and regulations on food safety and plant quarantine, national food safety standards or the provisions of the Protocol, the Chinese side shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant Chinese laws and regulations.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

28 October 2025
