Supplements can be sold to consumers in mainland China without health food registration. The workable channel is customs supervision mode 1210, the bonded e-commerce route opened by Announcement No. 486 of 2018 issued by six departments including the Ministry of Commerce. Three conditions have to hold at the same time: the tariff code of your product is on the cross-border e-commerce retail import positive list, the buyer is an individual purchasing for personal use, and the order value stays within the statutory limits. When all three hold, the route is compliant from day one. When any one of them fails, stop at the diagnostic stage and do not force the entry.

This guide covers the whole route: what mode 1210 is, whether your product qualifies, how the 9.1 percent consolidated tax and the consumer quota work, what the fourteen work packages deliver, and how the three-step entry runs with clean exits at every stage.

## What Mode 1210 actually is

China Customs writes every clearance channel into a four digit supervision code. Code 1210 is officially named bonded e-commerce retail import. The word import in the code name describes the movement of goods into a bonded warehouse inside Chinese customs territory; from your side nothing changes in direction, because you ship out of your home market and your buyers order from inside China. Announcement No. 486 of 2018 is the founding document: goods sold through this route are supervised as articles for personal use entering the territory, which is why the first-import licence, registration and filing requirements that ordinary trade imposes do not apply here.

Operationally the goods sit in a bonded warehouse inside a customs special supervision zone while they remain unsold, with no import duties paid at stocking. When a consumer orders, the platform pushes three documents to customs: the order, the payment and the logistics record. Customs matches the three and releases the parcel order by order, and the platform withholds the consumer-side tax at checkout.

## The legal floor in five conclusions

The executive summary of the underlying program gives three judgments and five conclusions, each traceable to a source document. Three judgments:

- Access judgment: the route is open, on the strength of Cai Guan Shui [2016] No. 18, Announcement No. 486 of 2018 and the 2022 positive list notice of eight departments, which carried the list to 1476 eight digit tariff codes.
- Product judgment: health supplements are on the list, and whey and other milk-derived protein powders classify under code 0404.9000, supported by customs classification ruling W2020-29 for colostrum-based products.
- Supervisory judgment: Decree 280, in force from 1 June 2026, governs food imported through goods channels and leaves this consumer route untouched, while under the State Council Decree 443 framework the Direct Selling Regulations read the model as outside direct selling, so no direct-selling licence enters the picture.

Five conclusions:

- Compliance: three exemptions hold. Supervision as personal-use articles waives the formal trade admission documents; the consolidated tax regime waives customs duty; the cross-border mode waives health food registration. Three non-exemptions hold as well: product quality liability, advertising claim boundaries and consumer protection remain fully in force.
- Tax: the consolidated rate is 9.1 percent, withheld by the platform; the buyer can see every deduction in the official customs mobile channels.
- Quota: 5000 yuan per single order and 26000 yuan per person per year, about 700 and 3600 US dollars, checked order by order by the clearance system.
- Funds: settlement runs in three stages with two levels of separation; there is no per-order remittance abroad.
- Cadence: the diagnostic stage spans two to four weeks and a single-SKU pilot six to ten weeks, on the condition that the diagnosis locks the variables.

## The tax math and the two limits

The 9.1 percent consolidated rate is built, not quoted. Customs duty is temporarily set at zero. Value-added tax applies at the statutory rate, 13 percent for milk-derived protein powders, and both VAT and consumption tax are charged at 70 percent of the statutory amount, so 13 percent times 70 percent equals 9.1 percent, under Cai Guan Shui [2016] No. 18. The rate applies only to listed products within the limits; a single indivisible item above the per-order limit is taxed at full goods rates, where the build-up no longer works.

The two limits come from Cai Guan Shui [2018] No. 49: 5000 yuan per single order and 26000 yuan per person per year. The ledger is checked order by order at clearance under GACC Announcement No. 194 of 2018, and the buyer can see each deduction in the official mobile channels.

Work the numbers on a concrete jar. A jar priced at 50 US dollars converts at 7.2 yuan per US dollar to about 360 yuan, inside the limits. The consolidated tax is 360 times 9.1 percent, or 32.8 yuan, about 4.5 US dollars, withheld by the platform for the buyer, and the annual ledger of that buyer records a deduction of 360 yuan. The tax is legally the consumer's liability, yet it lands in your retail price, so pricing takes it into account from day one.

## One batch of 2000 cans, end to end

Put the route on a concrete batch: 2000 cans of whey protein, retail 50 US dollars per can.

The first leg moves the goods into the special supervision zone in bonded status and opens the bonded ledger; no import duty is paid at this point. The entity already holds the licence and customs registration from S1, and the SKU has cleared tiering and classification under 0404.9000 with ruling W2020-29 as reference. When a consumer orders, the platform pushes the order, the payment and the logistics record; customs matches the three, checks the quota order by order and the platform withholds 9.1 percent, about 4.5 US dollars on a 50 US dollars order, and the ledger records 360 yuan. The parcel leaves the bonded warehouse and reaches the buyer. Returns follow GACC Announcement No. 45 of 2020: the buyer may apply within 30 days of confirming receipt, the goods must reach the bonded warehouse within 45 days, and the quota is restored. The whole chain leaves traces layer by layer on the seven flows and one ledger: which order, which declaration, which payment, which flow, all retrievable.

## What this route does not cover

Four boundaries, worth checking before production planning.

- Over-limit orders: a single order above 5000 yuan with an indivisible item, or a buyer above 26000 yuan per year, is stopped by the clearance system.
- B2B volumes: bonded e-commerce retail sells to individuals; distribution, wholesale and corporate purchasing fall outside this route.
- Function claims: health foods must not claim disease prevention or treatment, and the advertising law applies to cross-border goods with full force.
- Port differences: pilot ports differ in operating details; which port of entry and which verification standard apply must be clarified port by port in S0, which is exactly why the port verification list exists as one of the four reports.
