Dried fruits and nuts from Pakistan may enter China. GACC Announcement No. 82 permits four kinds of dried fruit made from fruit grown in Pakistan, washed, dried and sorted. Raw material must be confirmed by Pakistan; producing, processing and storing units may ship only after Pakistani recommendation and Chinese registration.

One consignment end to end: raw material from a grower confirmed by Pakistan, fumigated before export; packed in clean new material; each package marked for the People's Republic of China plus product name, origin, plant name, registration number, exporter name and address; transport means checked before loading; Pakistan issues a phytosanitary certificate before export, with an additional declaration giving plant name, registration number and the English declaration.

Chinese customs inspects on arrival and releases compliant goods; quarantine pests or a breach of the Protocol is handled under Chinese law, with no preset return or destruction. A phone number does not count: the registration list and the original certificate govern; batch and country only illustrate the process.

Under Chinese laws and regulations and the provisions agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter "the Chinese side") and the Ministry of National Food Security and Research of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan (hereinafter "the Pakistani side") on inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for dried fruits and nuts exported from Pakistan to China, imports of Pakistani dried fruits and nuts meeting the following requirements are permitted from the date of this announcement.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations;

(ii) the Administrative Measures for the Safety of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China on the Registration of Overseas Manufacturers of Imported Food;

(iii) the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of National Food Security and Research of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Dried Fruits and Nuts Exported from Pakistan to China (hereinafter "the Protocol").

II. Products Permitted for Import

(i) Pakistani dried fruits and nuts in this Announcement means four kinds of dried fruits and nuts fit for human consumption made from plant fruit grown in Pakistan, washed, dried and sorted within Pakistan: dried prunes (Prunus domestica), dried apricots (Prunus armeniaca), pistachios (Pistacia vera) and almond kernels (Prunu sdulcis).

(ii) The Pakistani side shall ensure that dried fruits and nuts exported to China are free of the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side:

(1) Dried prunes exported to China: the brown peach aphid (Pterochloroides persicae), Heterotermes indicola, Odontotermes lokanandi, Bifiditermes beesoni, Coptotermes heimi, Aspergillus terreus and the codling moth (Cydia pomonella).

(2) Dried apricots exported to China: the peach fruit fly (Bactrocera zonata), Odontotermes lokanandi, Pterochloroides persicae, Indarbela quadrinotata, Microtermes unicolor, Orobanche spp. and the codling moth (Cydia pomonella).

(3) Pistachios exported to China: Anapulvinaria pistaciae, Aspergillus candidus, Lepidium draba and Nattrassia mangiferae.

(4) Almond kernels exported to China: Pterochloroides persicae, Scolytus rugulosus and Phytophthora syringae.

(iii) Dried fruits and nuts exported to China shall carry no live insects, eggs or soil, and shall not be mixed with weed seeds, plant residues, metal foreign matter, grit or other impurities.

(iv) The Pakistani side shall guide and supervise the growing of raw material, production, processing, storage, transport, export and other processes of dried fruits and nuts for China, so as to ensure compliance with the relevant food safety and plant health requirements of China and Pakistan and prevent contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

(v) Dried fruits and nuts exported to China shall be fumigated before export so that no live insects remain.

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

Production enterprises of Pakistani dried fruits and nuts exported to China shall ensure that their products comply with Chinese food safety and plant quarantine laws and regulations, national food safety standards and the Protocol.

The Pakistani side shall, in line with Chinese requirements on the registration of overseas manufacturers of imported food, inspect the units producing, processing and storing dried fruits and nuts for China and recommend qualifying enterprises to the Chinese side for registration. The Chinese side shall register those that qualify. An enterprise may export to China only after it is registered in China. The list of registered Pakistani enterprises exporting dried fruits and nuts to China is available on the GACC website.

IV. Raw Material Requirements

Raw material for dried fruits and nuts exported to China shall come from growers and producers confirmed by the Pakistani side as meeting the relevant plant quarantine and food safety requirements.

V. Packaging and Marking Requirements

Dried fruits and nuts exported to China shall be packed in new, clean, hygienic and breathable material that meets food safety and plant quarantine requirements, and leakage during transport shall be prevented.

Each package shall be marked in Chinese or English with "This product is exported to the People's Republic of China", together with traceability information in Chinese or English such as the product name, origin, the name of the production or processing enterprise and its China registration number, and the name and address of the exporter.

Such information may be affixed to the package as a label.

VI. Quarantine Requirements for Means of Transport

Before loading, the Pakistani side shall inspect the means of transport for dried fruits and nuts exported to China.

The means of transport shall meet hygiene requirements and shall not contain quarantine pests or other regulated quarantine articles such as weed seeds, live insects, plant residues, soil or other impurities.

VII. Certificate Requirements

Before export, the Pakistani side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for dried fruits and nuts that have passed inspection and quarantine in line with the Protocol.

The additional declaration column of the certificate shall state the name of the production or processing enterprise of the dried fruits and nuts, its China registration number, and "The dried fruits and nuts certified by this phytosanitary certificate comply with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Dried Fruits and Nuts to Be Exported from Pakistan to China signed on May 25th, 2026 in Beijing by Pakistan and China. The dried fruits and nuts are free from quarantine pests of concern by the Chinese Party." (The dried fruits and nuts certified by this certificate comply with the Protocol on inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for dried fruits and nuts exported from Pakistan to China signed in Beijing on 25 May 2026 by the two sides. The consignment carries no quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side).

Quarantine treatment methods, such as the agent, temperature, duration and other technical requirements, shall be stated in the phytosanitary certificate.

VIII. Other Requirements

(i) On arrival at a Chinese port of entry, dried fruits and nuts exported to China shall be subject to inspection, quarantine and supervision by Chinese customs and shall be released if compliant.

(ii) Where the Chinese side finds that dried fruits and nuts exported to China breach Chinese food safety or plant quarantine laws and regulations, national food safety standards or the Protocol, the Chinese side shall handle the matter under relevant Chinese laws and regulations.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

11 June 2026
