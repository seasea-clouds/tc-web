China's Announcement No. 128 is the annual clearance framework for the CIIE and replaces eight earlier notices issued since 2018.

The National Exhibition and Convention Center files once and provides unified security, so exhibitors skip per-shipment deposits; items requiring quarantine approval are approved beforehand; consumables are exempt from duty and from import VAT and consumption tax within reasonable limits.

After the show exhibitors may re-export, transfer exhibits to a regional center for write-off, buy them for domestic sale, or apply for an extension; failures to complete customs formalities are handled according to law.

<!-- original -->

To support the China International Import Expo (CIIE) in continuously improving, the General Administration of Customs, on the basis of summarizing the clearance facilitation measures of the previous eight expos, has formulated the Customs Clearance Guide for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026 and the Customs Facilitation Measures in Support of the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026 (see Annexes 1 and 2), which are hereby released.

Upon release of this announcement, General Administration of Customs Announcements No. 55 and No. 76 of 2018, No. 115 of 2019, No. 84 of 2020, No. 54 of 2021, No. 62 of 2022, No. 93 of 2023, No. 78 of 2024 and No. 148 of 2025 are repealed simultaneously.

It is hereby announced.

Annexes: 1. Customs Clearance Guide for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026; 2. Customs Facilitation Measures in Support of the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China

1 September 2026

Annex 1 Customs Clearance Guide for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026

The customs of the People's Republic of China shall, in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Quarantine of Animals and Plants Entering and Leaving the Country, the Frontier Health Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China, the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China and the Tariff Law of the People's Republic of China, supervise the goods entering and leaving the country for the ninth China International Import Expo 2026 (hereinafter the CIIE), and during the CIIE send officers to the integrated service area inside the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai) to provide consulting services.

For exhibits temporarily imported for the CIIE, the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. (hereinafter the National Exhibition and Convention Center) provides security to Shanghai Customs. Overseas exhibitors or their appointed on-site transport service providers holding the Certificate of Goods Entering the Country for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026 (see Schedule 1) and the List of Goods Entering the Country for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026 (see Schedule 2) issued by the National Exhibition and Convention Center are exempt from submitting security to customs for each shipment.

Section 1 CIIE Filing

The National Exhibition and Convention Center completes customs filing of CIIE information in advance through the international trade single window, and handles customs formalities by filing once and submitting lists in batches, so that CIIE goods clear customs quickly after arriving at the port.

Section 2 Approval and Access for Exhibits

Where exhibits such as entry animals and plants and their products, and special goods and articles, require quarantine approval (list see Schedule 3), exhibitors shall apply before entry, and entry is allowed only after approval by customs and by the competent departments for agriculture and rural affairs or for forestry and grassland.

Exhibits shall meet the requirements of the List of Inspection and Quarantine Restrictions for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026 (see Schedule 4) and the List of Inspection and Quarantine Prohibitions for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026 (see Schedule 5).

Section 3 Declaration of Inbound Goods

Goods temporarily imported for the CIIE adopt the integrated clearance model: the National Exhibition and Convention Center or its appointed on-site transport service provider handles declaration formalities at Shanghai Customs, and the customs at the port carries out release. For CIIE goods imported by other trade modes, the overseas exhibitor or its agent handles customs formalities under current provisions.

For CIIE goods temporarily imported under an ATA carnet, the competent customs authority examines and endorses the carnet, and the time limit for re-export is the same as the validity of the carnet.

Section 4 Inspection and Release of Exhibits

CIIE exhibits entering the country are subject to inspection, quarantine and other procedures as required. Where special circumstances require inspection at the exhibition venue, the National Exhibition and Convention Center or its appointed on-site transport service provider applies, and upon customs examination and approval the exhibits may be transported to the venue for inspection. The National Exhibition and Convention Center shall provide the necessary office space and inspection facilities. Exhibits that pass inspection are released by the customs office that carried out the inspection.

Section 5 Supervision During the CIIE

During the CIIE, customs sends officers to conduct on-site supervision in accordance with law. The National Exhibition and Convention Center, on-site transport service providers and exhibitors shall comply with customs rules on the display and sale of exhibits and cooperate with customs supervision.

Section 6 Disposal of Exhibits

(a) Consumption of exhibition articles. The National Exhibition and Convention Center shall urge exhibitors to inform Shanghai Customs in writing in advance of expected consumption, stating the intended use of the articles (trial use, tasting, distribution) and the quantity, which shall be reasonable and match the scale of the event. For articles to be tried, tasted or distributed, the exhibitors concerned shall hold compliant certificates (official certificate of the exhibiting country, third-party test report, self-inspection conformity report of the exhibitor, or declaration of conformity by the exhibitor).

For the following exhibition articles entering the country for the CIIE, customs verifies the quantity and total value according to actual circumstances and, within reasonable limits, exempts them from tariff, import value-added tax and consumption tax under relevant provisions: 1. small samples used in the exhibition (excluding alcoholic beverages, tobacco products and fuels), including samples of food or beverages imported in original packaging or made during the exhibition from imported bulk materials. Such goods shall meet the following conditions: (1) provided free of charge by the exhibitor and used or consumed exclusively for free distribution to visitors during the exhibition; (2) of low unit value and used as advertising samples; (3) not suitable for commercial use, with unit capacity clearly smaller than the smallest retail packaging; (4) samples of food and beverages that are not distributed in the packaging specified in item (3) of this paragraph but are genuinely consumed at the event.

(2) Materials consumed or damaged in operating demonstrations of exhibited machines or devices. (3) Low-value goods consumed in setting up and decorating temporary booths. (4) Publicity materials distributed free to visitors during the exhibition. (5) Archives, forms and other documents used for the exhibition.

Exhibition articles listed in item 1 that are imported beyond the quota shall be taxed on the excess according to law. Exhibition articles listed in items 2, 3 and 4 that are unused or not fully consumed shall be re-exported; those not re-exported shall undergo import formalities as required.

(b) Purchase after the exhibition. For exhibits purchased after the exhibition, the National Exhibition and Convention Center or its appointed on-site transport service provider shall handle import formalities in a unified way under relevant customs provisions. Where licensing is involved, the relevant licences shall be obtained.

Imported exhibits that are on the list of cross-border e-commerce retail imported goods may, after the exhibition and upon entering a special customs supervision area registered with customs or a B-type bonded logistics center (hereinafter a regional center), be sold in the bonded online retail import mode for cross-border e-commerce where they meet the conditions.

(c) Re-export. Temporarily imported CIIE exhibits shall be re-exported within the time limit set by customs.

Temporarily imported CIIE exhibits not under an ATA carnet that are transferred after the CIIE to special customs supervision areas and bonded supervision sites (exhibited vehicles shall be transferred to special customs supervision areas and bonded supervision sites that may handle bonded vehicle storage) are written off and closed after the relevant customs formalities are completed.

(d) Extension of exhibits. Where temporarily imported CIIE exhibits genuinely need an extension of the re-export period, extension formalities shall be handled with Shanghai Customs as required.

(e) Bonded display and trading. Enterprises in regional centers or in other eligible bonded supervision sites approved by customs may, after submitting security for bonded goods, transport the goods to the CIIE venue for display and sale.

Section 7 Equipment of Journalists

For interviewing equipment temporarily imported by overseas journalists for the CIIE, customs handles clearance formalities under General Administration of Customs Announcements No. 104 of 2008, No. 19 of 2009 and No. 31 of 2009.

Where CIIE goods entering or leaving the country are not handled under customs formalities as required, customs deals with them in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

Matters not covered by this guide are handled under relevant laws and regulations. If national policies are adjusted, the adjusted policies apply.

List of Inspection and Quarantine Restrictions for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026; 5. List of Inspection and Quarantine Prohibitions for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026

Schedule 1 Certificate of Goods Entering the Country for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026

Issued by the certifier, National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., stating the country of trade, means of transport, date of entry, bill of lading or waybill number, gross weight, supervision mode (exhibits, bonded goods, transit or other), quantity, domestic consignee or consignor and its ten-digit code, declaring entity and its ten-digit code, and contact person and contact details; the certification states that the shipment is used only for this exhibition and commits to completing the relevant formalities with customs in a timely manner, signed and sealed by the handler and the certifier. The original is kept by the competent customs authority, copy 1 is handed to the customs at the place of exit upon re-export, and copy 2 is kept by the certifier.

Schedule 2 List of Goods Entering the Country for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026

A bilingual Chinese-English form reporting the exhibitor, country or region, hall number, booth number and total number of packages, and, item by item, case number, volume (length, width, height), gross weight, net weight, name and specifications of exhibits, origin, quantity, unit, unit price and total price (USD), commodity code and disposal method of exhibits (sold, returned, abandoned and consumed, given away). Where exhibits are machines, equipment, parts or raw materials, the model and serial numbers must be reported in detail.

Schedule 3 Animals, Plants and Their Products, and Special Goods and Articles Requiring Approval for Entry

(1) Animals and animal products: live animals and animal embryos, semen, fertilized eggs, breeding eggs and other animal genetic material; imported animal serum products, vaccines and biological products; edible animal products (meat products, excluding cooked and cured meat, and fresh eggs); non-edible animal products (see the General Administration of Customs announcement on optimizing risk levels and inspection and quarantine supervision measures for entry non-edible animal products); feed products (see the General Administration of Customs announcement on optimizing inspection and quarantine supervision measures for imported feed and feed additives); animal-derived traditional Chinese medicinal materials (deer antler velvet, deer antler, gecko, earthworm, zaocys, cow bezoar).

(2) Plants and plant products: seeds, seedlings and other plant propagating materials; fruits and vegetables (fresh fruit); grains (wheat, maize, rice, barley, rye, oats, sorghum, rapeseed and other miscellaneous grains); pulses (soybean, mung bean, pea, adzuki bean, broad bean, chickpea and other miscellaneous pulses); tubers (potato, cassava, sweet potato and others); plant-derived foods with a risk of spreading pests or diseases.

(3) Genetically modified animal and plant products: products within the labelling catalogue shall be declared as genetically modified or not; where they contain such components, the Genetically Modified Organism Safety Certificate issued by the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs shall be provided.

(4) Special goods and articles: human tissues such as blood entering the country (whole human blood, plasma, serum, other blood components, cells, cell lines, embryos, organs, tissues, bone marrow, and other body fluids that may transmit infectious diseases such as cerebrospinal fluid, pleural fluid and peritoneal fluid, secretions and excretions), pathogenic microorganisms (microorganisms that can invade the human body and cause infection or even infectious disease, including viruses, bacteria, fungi, actinomycetes, rickettsiae, spirochetes, chlamydiae, mycoplasmas, prions and parasites) and biological products (biologically active preparations from human sources or used in human medical fields, such as proteins, cytokines, enzymes and their preparations, toxins, antigens, antibodies, antigen-antibody complexes, nucleic acids and other biotechnology products) shall obtain the Sanitary Quarantine Approval Form for Special Goods and Articles Entering and Leaving the Country upon approval. They may enter the country only after passing inspection and quarantine, and shall be supervised after entry.

Schedule 4 List of Inspection and Quarantine Restrictions for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026

(1) Animals, non-edible animal products and animal-derived traditional Chinese medicinal materials. For exhibition purposes: before entry of animals, animal-derived feed and animal-derived traditional Chinese medicinal materials, an Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit for Entry shall be obtained, the relevant inspection and quarantine requirements will be stated in the permit, and the official animal quarantine certificate of the exporting country or region shall be attached; where non-edible animal products are of risk level I or II in the List of Risk Levels and Inspection and Quarantine Supervision Measures for Entry Non-edible Animal Products, an Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit for Entry shall be obtained before entry and, except for products of risk level IV, the official quarantine certificate of the exporting country or region shall be attached at entry.

For display and sale purposes: animals must come from the product types and exporting countries or regions published in the List of Countries/Regions and Species of Aquatic Animals Allowed to Enter for Food/Ornamental/Breeding Purposes, an animal and plant quarantine permit for entry shall be obtained before entry and the official animal quarantine certificate of the exporting country or region shall be attached; non-edible animal products must come from the product types and exporting countries or regions published in the List of Countries/Regions and Product Types of Non-edible Animal Products Allowed to Enter, products of risk level I or II shall obtain an animal and plant quarantine permit for entry before entry and, except for products of risk level IV, the official quarantine certificate shall be attached at entry; animal-derived feed must come from the product types and exporting countries or regions published in the List of Countries/Regions and Products of Feed and Feed Additives Allowed to Import, and where it is a product of risk level I or II listed in General Administration of Customs Announcement No. 257 of 2025, an animal and plant quarantine approval permit for entry shall be obtained before entry and the official quarantine certificate shall be attached; animal-derived traditional Chinese medicinal materials must come from the product types and exporting countries or regions published in the Catalogue of Animal-derived Medicinal Materials that Have Obtained Quarantine Access to China and Their Exporting Countries and Regions, an animal and plant quarantine approval permit for entry shall be obtained before entry, and the official quarantine certificate shall be attached at entry.

(2) Plants and products (grain), fresh fruit, plant-derived feed, plant propagating materials and plant-based seasonings. For exhibition purposes: products shall have obtained quarantine access; where quarantine approval is required (see Schedule 3), an Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit for Entry or a quarantine approval form issued by the agriculture and forestry authorities shall be obtained before entry (applicable to plant propagating materials outside the List of Prohibited Entry Plants for Quarantine of the People's Republic of China); the official plant quarantine certificate of the exporting country or region shall be attached. For display and sale purposes: products shall have obtained quarantine access, an Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit for Entry shall be obtained before entry, and the official plant quarantine certificate of the exporting country or region shall be attached. The following exhibits may not be consumed by trial use, tasting, distribution or sale: imported seeds, seedlings and other plant propagating materials approved for exhibition purposes; imported grain, feed grain and feed grass seed.

(3) Animal-derived foods such as meat, bee products, casings, dairy products, aquatic products and bird's nests (excluding bird's nest products), and plant-derived foods such as grain products and dried nuts. For exhibition purposes: quarantine access shall be obtained, and products without access shall come from non-epidemic areas and be approved for exhibition through special approval or risk assessment; where they are listed in Animals, Plants and Their Products, and Special Goods and Articles Requiring Approval for Entry, an Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit for Entry shall be obtained before entry; the official health certificate or animal and plant quarantine certificate of the exporting country or region shall be attached; registration of overseas producers of imported food is exempted. Requirements for sale purposes are the same as for ordinary trade.

(4) Infant formula milk powder. For exhibition purposes: quarantine access shall be obtained; the official health certificate of the exporting country or region shall be attached; registration of overseas producers of imported food is exempted. Requirements for sale purposes are the same as for ordinary trade.

(5) Other prepackaged food. For exhibition purposes: sampling inspection is exempted and Chinese labelling is exempted; for small amounts of trial use or tasting, samples may be taken for testing in light of examination and verification (samples may be provided before the exhibition) and Chinese labelling may be exempted; registration of overseas producers of imported food is exempted. Requirements for sale purposes are the same as for ordinary trade.

(6) Cosmetics. For exhibition purposes: sampling inspection is exempted and Chinese labelling is exempted; for small amounts of trial use or distribution, samples may be taken for testing in light of examination and verification (samples may be provided before the exhibition) and Chinese labelling may be exempted. Requirements for sale purposes are the same as for ordinary trade.

(7) Relevant imported products within the scope of China's licensing and compulsory certification. Exhibition purposes are not restricted; products for sale shall obtain a licence or certification, or be exempted from licence and certification documents.

(8) Products within the scope of energy efficiency and water efficiency labelling. Exhibition purposes are not restricted; products for sale shall bear energy efficiency labels as required by the state.

(9) Special goods and articles of risk levels A and B. Entry requires sanitary quarantine approval; the exhibition site must have the relevant biosafety control conditions.

(10) Special goods and articles of risk level C. Entry requires sanitary quarantine approval.

(11) Biological materials for research and pharmaceutical use. Exhibition purposes follow the List of Risk Levels and Quarantine Supervision Measures for Entry Biological Materials; display and sale purposes shall meet the requirements of the List of Entry Biological Materials Authorized for Animal and Plant Quarantine Approval and follow the List of Risk Levels and Quarantine Supervision Measures for Entry Biological Materials.

(12) Rough diamonds. The original Kimberley Process international certificate issued by the competent authority of the exporting country of the rough diamonds shall be attached.

(13) Hazardous chemicals. Hazardous chemicals listed in the Catalogue of Hazardous Chemicals (2015 edition) shall comply with General Administration of Customs Announcements No. 129 of 2020 and No. 29 of 2023, and may be imported only after passing customs inspection. Goods temporarily imported or exported for exhibition purposes are exempt from inspection according to law, unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.

Schedule 5 List of Inspection and Quarantine Prohibitions for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026

(1) Articles prohibited from entry under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Quarantine of Animals and Plants Entering and Leaving the Country: animal and plant pathogens (including strains and toxins), pests and other harmful organisms; the relevant animals and plants, animal and plant products and other quarantine objects from countries and regions where animal or plant epidemics prevail; animal carcasses; soil. Exhibition and sale are prohibited.

(2) Food, edible agricultural products and feed from Fukushima Prefecture, Gunma Prefecture, Tochigi Prefecture, Ibaraki Prefecture, Miyagi Prefecture, Niigata Prefecture (excluding rice), Nagano Prefecture, Saitama Prefecture, Tokyo Metropolis and Chiba Prefecture in Japan, with the above origins and product scope adjusted dynamically according to risk assessment. Exhibition and sale are prohibited. Under Announcement No. 44 of 2011 of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine and former Food Letter No. 411 of 2011, food, edible agricultural products and feed not produced in the above ten prefectures and metropolis shall be supported by evidence that they originate in areas not listed in the prohibition list; among them, vegetables and their products, milk and dairy products, aquatic products and aquatic animals, tea and its products, fruit and its products, and medicinal plant products shall also be supported by a certificate that radioactive substance testing is qualified.

(3) Used mechanical and electrical products and used clothing within the prohibited import catalogue. Used clothing may not be exhibited or sold; used mechanical and electrical products outside the prohibited import catalogue may be exhibited but may not be sold. The commodity code of used clothing is 6309000000.

Annex 2 Customs Facilitation Measures in Support of the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026

Section 1 Release the clearance guide and provide detailed clearance instructions. Formulate and release the Customs Clearance Guide for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026, the List of Inspection and Quarantine Restrictions for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026 and the List of Inspection and Quarantine Prohibitions for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026, providing detailed instructions to overseas exhibitors.

Section 2 Set up a standing body to respond to needs at any time. Shanghai Convention and Exhibition Center Customs, under Shanghai Customs, serves as the standing body for customs services to the import expo (hereinafter the CIIE) and carries out customs supervision and service support for the CIIE.

Section 3 Deepen the use of technology and build an intelligent supervision and service model. Through the cross-border trade management big data platform and information technology, provide full-process supervision and services for information on CIIE exhibitors and exhibits, and build a digital, intelligent, convenient and intensive full-process supervision and service model for the CIIE.

Section 4 Send officers on site to provide service support. During the CIIE, Shanghai Customs will send officers to the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai) to provide clearance, supervision and consulting services.

Section 5 The organizer provides unified security to ease the burden on overseas exhibitors. For CIIE exhibits temporarily imported, the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. provides security to Shanghai Customs by bank letter of security or tariff security insurance. Overseas exhibitors or their appointed on-site transport service providers holding the Certificate of Goods Entering the Country for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026 and the List of Goods Entering the Country for the Ninth China International Import Expo 2026 issued by the company are exempt from submitting security to customs for each shipment.

Section 6 Carry out verification nearby to improve facilitation for exhibitors. For CIIE entry animals and plants and their products, animal and plant derived foods, traditional Chinese medicinal materials and the like that involve inspection and quarantine administrative approval, Shanghai Customs and the relevant customs offices are entrusted to handle the approval nearby and on the spot, and the approval work is completed within 3 working days; following the principle of being convenient and practicable, Shanghai Customs is entrusted to exercise the powers of acceptance and review over overseas producers of imported aquatic products, dairy products and bird's nests sold in the exhibition area, with on-site overseas assessment exempted, and after review is passed the General Administration of Customs issues a temporary registration approval, which is limited to use during the CIIE and within the exhibition area.

Section 7 Set up dedicated channels and give priority to formalities. At major ports, set up VIP courtesy channels, special windows for declaring entry exhibits and special inspection channels for the CIIE, give priority to customs formalities such as declaration, inspection, sampling and testing, and release goods upon inspection.

Section 8 Consolidate supervision measures and extend the temporary entry period of exhibits under ATA carnets. Consolidate the supervision and service measures of previous CIIE editions, and the re-export period endorsed by customs for goods temporarily imported under ATA carnets is consistent with the validity of the carnet.

Section 9 Advance access negotiations and expand the types of entry exhibits. Advance quarantine access negotiations with participating countries or regions, speed up risk assessment, support the participation and trade with China of agricultural and livestock products with acceptable risk levels, and expand the types of entry exhibits.

Section 10 Shorten approval time and optimize quarantine approval for special goods and articles. Give priority to reviewing sanitary quarantine application materials for special goods and articles brought in by CIIE participants, compress the approval time limit to within 3 working days for special goods and articles used only for entry exhibition, and simplify the quarantine approval application materials for exit of temporarily imported special exhibition goods and articles after the CIIE ends.

Section 11 Simplify entry formalities to facilitate participation of food and cosmetics. Food and cosmetics for exhibition only are exempt from Chinese labelling and sampling inspection, and from verification of the consignee or consignor filing certificate; for appropriate amounts of trial use or tasting, samples may be taken for testing before the exhibition in light of safety risk assessment, with Chinese labelling exempted; for small amounts sold on trial at the exhibition site, Chinese labelling may be exempted.

Section 12 Simplify exit formalities to facilitate post-exhibition disposal of exhibits. CIIE exhibits temporarily imported, other than those under ATA carnets, that are transferred after the CIIE to special customs supervision areas and bonded supervision sites (exhibited vehicles shall be transferred to special customs supervision areas and bonded supervision sites that may handle bonded vehicle storage) are permitted to be written off and closed.

Section 13 Support cross-border e-commerce business and advance online and offline integration. Imported exhibits on the list of cross-border e-commerce retail imported goods are allowed, after the exhibition and upon entering a special customs supervision area registered with customs or a B-type bonded logistics center, to be sold in the bonded online retail import mode for cross-border e-commerce where they meet the conditions.

Section 14 Support cultural relic exhibits and handle post-exhibition purchase formalities. Exhibits recognized as cultural relics by the national cultural relics department may participate as temporarily imported exhibits or in bonded display; cultural relic exhibits that the national cultural relics department allows domestic consumers to buy and that are sold during the CIIE may, where they meet the preferential tax policy for imported exhibits sold during the CIIE, be imported duty free under the policy.

Section 15 Give priority testing and issue relevant certificates for eligible exhibited vehicles. After exhibited vehicles complete single-vehicle certification and obtain the China Compulsory Certification (CCC) certificate, and complete China VI environmental information disclosure as required by the Ecological Environment Code of the People's Republic of China, customs gives priority to commodity inspection according to law and issues the relevant certificates.

Section 16 Expand the scope of participation and allow animal and plant products and food without quarantine access to be exhibited. Animal and plant products and animal and plant derived foods from non-epidemic areas that have not obtained import inspection and quarantine access are allowed to enter for exhibition; where quarantine approval formalities are required, the General Administration of Customs authorizes Shanghai Customs to handle them as special approval.

Section 17 Simplify certificate requirements for some products. For animal and plant derived exhibiting foods whose risk of spreading animal or plant epidemics has been eliminated or is low, the official health certificate or animal and plant quarantine certificate issued by the exporting country or region may be exempted.

Section 18 Support regular bonded display and trading and expand the spillover effect of the expo. Enterprises in special customs supervision areas and B-type bonded logistics centers registered with customs may, after submitting security for bonded goods, transport the goods outside the area for bonded display and trading and other business activities. Where bonded goods in bonded warehouses approved by customs are transported to the CIIE for exhibition, the preceding paragraph applies.
