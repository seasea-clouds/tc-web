Uruguayan bezoar is approved for entry. Before importing, confirm the source farm and producer are on China's registration list. Announcement No. 25 of 2026 of the General Administration of Customs defines bezoar as the dried gallstone from the gallbladder or bile duct of farmed cattle in Uruguay, entered with a Uruguayan sanitary certificate.

A shipment passes three checks. Source animals born and raised on officially supervised farms, free of brucellosis, anthrax and tuberculosis in the 12 months before slaughter, of notifiable diseases in the 6 months before, and of live anthrax vaccine within 14 days; processing requires low-temperature drying, traceability and residues within limits; packaging marks the batch and registration in Chinese, English and Spanish.

On entry, customs verifies the quarantine permit, registration and sanitary certificate and quarantines the cargo; non-compliant goods are handled under the rules. With these three layers ready, clearance is well founded.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions on the quarantine and sanitary requirements for Uruguayan bezoar exported to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Livestock, Agriculture and Fisheries of the Oriental Republic of Uruguay (hereinafter referred to as the Uruguayan side), imports of Uruguayan bezoar meeting the following requirements are permitted as of today:

Inspection and Quarantine Basis

(1) Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(3) Measures for the Supervision and Administration of the Quarantine of Entry and Exit Chinese Medicinal Materials;

(4) Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Livestock, Agriculture and Fisheries of the Oriental Republic of Uruguay on the Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Uruguayan Bezoar Exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

Scope of Products Permitted for Import

For the purposes of this Announcement, bezoar means the dried gallstone from the gallbladder or bile duct of farmed cattle in Uruguay.

Requirements for Source Animals of the Products

(1) Born, raised and slaughtered on officially supervised farms and slaughterhouses in Uruguay, with a unique identity mark and traceable through the official traceability system to the farm of birth and rearing;

(2) In the 12 months before slaughter, no brucellosis, anthrax or tuberculosis occurred at the place of origin;

(3) In the 6 months before slaughter, the place of origin was not subject to isolation restrictions or surveillance due to the occurrence of notifiable bovine diseases (in accordance with the laws and regulations of China and Uruguay and the provisions of the World Organisation for Animal Health, WOAH);

(4) No live anthrax vaccine was administered at least 14 days before slaughter;

(5) No veterinary drugs or feed additives prohibited by China or Uruguay have ever been used, and no ruminant-derived meat and bone meal or tankage has ever been fed;

(6) No clinical symptoms of any infectious disease, no pathological changes in the carcass and organs, and passing of pre- and post-slaughter inspection.

Requirements for Production and Processing Enterprises

(1) Operating under official supervision in Uruguay, complying with the relevant veterinary public health laws and regulations of China and Uruguay, and assessed and registered by the Chinese side;

(2) The production and processing comply with good manufacturing practices and the corresponding quality management system standards and requirements, and an effective product traceability and recall system has been established with detailed records kept for each batch;

(3) Having facilities for low-temperature drying of bezoar, equipped with temperature control equipment;

(4) Having light-proof, dry and hygienic storage facilities for the finished bezoar.

Production and Processing Requirements

(1) Bezoar collection shall be carried out in a certified slaughterhouse with appropriate protective measures to avoid cross-contamination, and the cleaning of the bezoar shall be completed in a separate area within the same premises;

(2) The gallbladder shall be collected in a safe and hygienic manner, and the tools, containers, water and other items used in the operation shall be clean and hygienic to avoid cross-contamination;

(3) Effective measures shall be taken during slaughter and collection to prevent contamination of the gallbladder and the collected bezoar by the brain, skull, eyes, spinal cord, tonsils and terminal ileum;

(4) After collection, the bile, blood and other impurities attached to the surface shall be removed with clean gauze, absorbent paper or the like; the bezoar shall not be rinsed directly with water nor shall impurities be removed by chemical means;

(5) The processing effectively removes moisture from the bezoar and avoids damage from high temperatures;

(6) The drying process shall maintain a clean environment and the drying equipment shall be disinfected regularly;

(7) Included in Uruguay's national residue monitoring programme, with residues of veterinary drugs, pesticides, heavy metals, pollutants and other undesirable substances not exceeding the maximum limit standards of China and Uruguay.

Requirements for Packaging, Labelling, Storage and Transport

(1) Packed in clean, hygienic, brand-new materials, with bezoar of the same production date individually packaged and marked;

(2) The name of the product, quantity, weight, batch number, storage conditions, production date, names, addresses and registration numbers of the officially registered slaughterhouse and production enterprise, and other details shall be indicated on the outside of the package in Chinese, English and Spanish;

(3) Storage and transport shall comply with veterinary hygiene requirements, avoid contamination by toxic and harmful substances, and the packaging shall remain sealed during transport.

Pre-export Inspection and Certificate Requirements

The Uruguayan side is responsible for inspecting and quarantining the bezoar exported to China and issuing a sanitary certificate certifying that the product meets the requirements of the Protocol.

Entry Inspection and Quarantine Requirements

(1) Document verification.

(1) Verify whether the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit has been obtained;

(2) Verify whether the goods come from a registered enterprise;

(3) Verify whether the sanitary certificate is authentic and valid.

(2) Cargo inspection.

China Customs shall quarantine the bezoar exported from Uruguay to China in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules and other provisions, in combination with the requirements of this Announcement. Bezoar that passes quarantine shall be permitted to enter.

(3) Handling of non-compliance.

Where non-compliance is found during entry quarantine, China Customs shall handle it in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, the Protocol and other provisions.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs

12 March 2026
