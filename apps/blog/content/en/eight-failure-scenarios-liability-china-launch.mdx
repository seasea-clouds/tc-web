Who bears a failure, how far compensation reaches, and how fast a response lands: these three should be written down item by item before a launch into China. The liability matrix assigns each of the eight common failure scenarios to a named owner; the compensation mechanism holds exposure in three layers, a liability cap, milestone binding and an insurance backstop; the contingency plan counts from T+0 across four time windows, each with one first action and one output. This page stands on its own, needs no other chapter read first, and only asks whether those boundaries are written into contract clauses.

## The Liability Matrix for Eight Scenarios

The governing rule in one line: whoever's conduct causes it bears it; regulator and platform acts attributable to neither side are listed as shared and disclosed in writing, item by item. The table below lines each scenario up with its owner and handling mechanism, leaving no gray area.

![Liability matrix page: eight failure scenarios with owner and handling mechanism aligned row by row](/images/blog/eight-failure-scenarios-liability-china-launch/liability-matrix-eight-scenarios.webp)

| Scenario | Owner | Handling mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product compliance issue | Provided by you | We provide onshore compliance review |
| Classification and filing errors | Our scope | We bear amendment, back taxes and late-filing costs |
| Warehouse handling error | Our scope | Compensation per agreed ratio and cap |
| Goods damage in transit | Parallel with us | Carrier liability and insurance claims |
| Reasonable inspection delay | Parallel with us | We chase priority; deadlines extend accordingly |
| False-declaration penalty | Our scope | We bear full liability and direct losses |
| Data breach | Parallel with us | Our processor duty; your notice-and-consent duty |
| Platform rule changes | Parallel with us | We appeal and rectify |

The inspection and detention row needs a sentence of its own. Reasonable inspection delays are shared: we chase priority and the deadlines extend, and you do not carry this part. If a false declaration leads to a detention penalty, the liability and the direct losses fall to us in full. Product compliance issues rest with you, with onshore compliance review from us as support; the specific basis is the declaration-element requirements set out in GACC Announcement No. 194 of 2018.

The two likeliest dispute points are these: classification and declaration errors, which are ours, and product compliance, which is yours. Both are agreed item by item in the contract, and the remaining six follow the table above with no further gray areas.

## Compensation: Liability Caps and Protection Mechanisms

The liability matrix settles who pays; this layer settles how much and backed by what. Three layers run top down: the higher the layer, the closer to our liability itself; the lower, the more enforceable the form of security, and each layer lands on one executable backstop.

![Compensation page: liability cap, milestone and insurance backstop as three layers, plus four suggested policies](/images/blog/eight-failure-scenarios-liability-china-launch/compensation-liability-caps-three-layers.webp)

The liability cap narrows exposure to service fees or batch value; anything beyond does not enter this layer. Milestone binding hangs the payout rhythm on delivery nodes agreed in the contract, phase by phase. The insurance backstop is the final security, taken out through performance insurance; we help arrange it, and you may also insure directly. Cap first to narrow exposure, then bind milestones, then close on the policy, and the order cannot be reversed.

Four policies are suggested, one per covered object.

- Warehouse property insurance covers storage property loss, force majeure or inherent causes. Owner: the cargo owner. Per-policy scope and deductible follow the policy.
- Warehouse liability insurance covers the portion our operations are legally payable for. Owner: our scope, cap applies. It pays the liable party's legal debt.
- Cargo transport insurance covers goods damage in transit. Ownership: advanced in parallel with carrier liability. Claims follow the transport documents.
- General liability insurance covers third-party liability in operations. Ownership: terms per the commercial agreement. Coverage and deductible follow the policy.

Liability insurance pays only what the liable party legally owes, and this must be stated. Losses from force majeure or inherent causes remain the cargo owner's own risk, covered by warehouse property insurance. Advance refunds to consumers are borne by the operating entity, and we cooperate in recovery per the liability matrix; the payout process, response deadlines and contacts are all listed item by item in the contract annexes, and this page does not restate the specific sums. The matrix answers who pays, the three layers answer how much and backed by what: cases in our scope are held by the cap and the security, shared cases follow the disclosure and negotiation mechanism. The framework wording here describes structure and mechanism only, and sums insured, deductibles, caps and forms of security are subject to the contract and policy terms.

## Contingency Plans: Severity Tiers and Response Deadlines

The plan counts from T+0. Each of the four time windows has one first action and one output, and the urgent segment is marked in red.

![Contingency page: four time windows from T+0 to T+72 with per-scenario response deadlines](/images/blog/eight-failure-scenarios-liability-china-launch/contingency-plans-severity-tiers-deadlines.webp)

T+0 is contain and classify: once on-site contact is set, halt orders, take the affected pages down, keep evidence, open one case file. T+2 is locate and first actions: within 2 hours find the mismatched field, amend and resubmit, issue the documents. T+24 is documents and one voice: in 24 hours respond and appeal, with one named voice externally. T+72 is close out and review: file and review, one case one file for any inquiry.

Each scenario has its own first action and time target, so nothing is left to interpretation at the moment of an incident.

| Scenario | First action | Time target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three-document cross-check failure | Locate the mismatched field, amend and resubmit | Locate in 2 hours, amend in 24 hours |
| Inspection detention | Issue explanatory documents, cooperate with verification | Submit within 4 hours |
| False-declaration liability | Halt orders immediately, self-check and correct | Same day |
| Consumer complaint | Take affected pages down first, preserve evidence | 4 hours |
| Regulator inquiry | One message, one named respondent | Respond in 24 hours |
| Data security incident | Activate the plan to contain, report within statutory deadlines | Statutory deadline |
| Platform penalty | Appeal, rectification and a fallback plan | Appeal in 24 hours |

The emergency mechanism closes on three things: one emergency contact, one external voice, one case one file. Every exception gets its own file, recording trigger, first action, deadline and outcome, pulled directly for regulator inquiries and audits and never assembled on the fly. A data security incident also carries the notification duty for a breach under Article 57 of the Personal Information Protection Law, with the reporting deadline following the statutory one, not set by this page. The deadlines above execute as agreed in the annex, and this page lists only the structure and the actions.

## One Batch, End to End

Run one batch of infant formula through the full flow to see how the three layers mesh. Suppose a batch triggers a customs classification and declaration error: the tariff code is filed under the formula class, but it contains an undeclared herbal ingredient, so inspection detains it and the declaration is ruled false.

The first step goes back to the liability matrix, where both the classification and filing error and the false-declaration penalty fall in our scope, so amendment, back taxes, late-filing costs and all direct losses are ours. The second step enters the contingency windows: the on-site contact halts orders and pages at T+0 and secures the declaration documents, locates the classification field and resubmits within two hours, issues the explanation and appeal within twenty-four, keeps one external voice, and files the case on its own. The third step lands on compensation: this batch's exposure is first capped by batch value under the liability cap, the payout rhythm hangs on the delivery milestones agreed in the contract, and the policy backstops it; if advance refunds to consumers are involved, the operating entity bears them and we assist recovery against the responsible stage per the matrix. Read the three pages together, and every step of who pays, how far, and how fast closes on a clause.
