Effective immediately. GACC Announcement No. 130 admits bovine bezoars from Brazil, defined only as dried gallstones from the gall bladder or bile duct of farmed Brazilian cattle. Donor animals must be traceable to the source farm, free of anthrax and foot-and-mouth disease for 12 months before slaughter, and processed at plants recommended by Brazil and registered in China.

For one consignment: check farm traceability, the 6-month disease restriction, the 14-day anthrax vaccine interval, residue monitoring and the Chinese registration list. Each lot needs a Brazilian health certificate stating compliance with the Protocol. A number given only by word of mouth does not count; the published list and the original certificate govern.

Collection must not use compressed-air or gas stunning into the cranial cavity, nor spinal puncture; bezoars must not be rinsed with water or cleaned chemically. China Customs checks documents and goods; non-compliant lots are handled under laws, regulations, rules and the Protocol. The country and lot only illustrate the process; the certificate and registration status decide.

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock of the Federative Republic of Brazil (hereinafter the Brazilian side) on the quarantine and hygiene requirements for the export of bovine bezoars from Brazil to China, import of Brazilian bovine bezoars meeting the following requirements is permitted from the date of this announcement.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(iii) Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Quarantine of Entry and Exit Chinese Medicinal Materials;

(iv) Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock of the Federative Republic of Brazil on the Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for Export of Bovine Bezoars from Brazil to China (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Products Permitted for Import

Bovine bezoars in this announcement mean dried gallstones from the gall bladder or bile duct of farmed cattle in Brazil.

III. Requirements for Source Animals

(i) Born, raised and slaughtered at farms and slaughterhouses under effective official supervision in Brazil, with donor cattle traceable to the source farm through an effective traceability system;

(ii) No anthrax or foot-and-mouth disease at the source farm in the 12 months before slaughter;

(iii) The source premises were not subject to quarantine restrictions or monitoring in the 6 months before slaughter on account of other notifiable bovine diseases prescribed by the World Organisation for Animal Health;

(iv) No anthrax live vaccine administered for at least 14 days before slaughter;

(v) No clinical signs or pathological changes of tuberculosis or brucellosis found in ante-mortem or post-mortem inspection; if those diseases are detected in a batch during such inspection, bezoars from the same batch and the same farm shall not be exported to China;

(vi) Never fed ruminant-derived feed (except milk and dairy products, gelatin and collagen made from hides, and dicalcium phosphate with no protein or fat residues), and never given veterinary drugs or feed additives banned in China or Brazil;

(vii) Covered by the Brazilian national residue monitoring programme, with residues of veterinary drugs, pesticides, heavy metals, contaminants and other harmful substances not exceeding the maximum limits of China and Brazil.

IV. Requirements for Production and Processing Enterprises

(i) Approved by the competent Brazilian authority and lawfully operated, subject to its supervision and inspection, with production and processing continuously meeting the safety and hygiene rules of China and Brazil;

(ii) A beef export enterprise to China already approved by the Chinese side;

(iii) Recommended by the Brazilian side to the Chinese side and registered;

(iv) Equipped with low-temperature drying facilities for bezoars and temperature control equipment;

(v) Equipped with light-proof, dry and hygienic storage facilities for finished bezoars.

V. Production and Processing Requirements

(i) Ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection meeting the laws and regulations of China and Brazil;

(ii) Donor cattle confirmed healthy, with no clinical signs of any infectious disease and no disease-related pathological changes in the carcass or organs;

(iii) No stunning method injecting compressed air or gas into the cranial cavity, and no spinal puncture, applied to donor cattle at slaughter;

(iv) Bezoars collected in a safe and hygienic manner, with effective measures preventing contamination by brain, skull, eyes, spinal cord, tonsils and the terminal ileum;

(v) After collection, bile, blood and other impurities on the surface removed with clean gauze or absorbent paper; no direct rinsing with water and no chemical removal of impurities; cleaning carried out in a separate area within the same premises;

(vi) Processing effectively removing moisture from bezoars without heat damage;

(vii) A clean environment maintained during drying, with drying equipment disinfected regularly.

VI. Packaging, Labelling, Storage and Transport Requirements

(i) Packed in clean, hygienic and new materials meeting the requirements, with separate packaging and marking for bezoars of the same production date;

(ii) The outer package stating product name, weight, batch number, storage conditions, production date, and the name, address and registration number of the officially registered slaughterhouse and production enterprise;

(iii) Storage and transport meeting veterinary hygiene requirements, avoiding contamination by toxic or harmful substances, with packages not opened or replaced in transit.

VII. Pre-export Inspection and Certificate Requirements

The Brazilian side is responsible for inspection and quarantine of bovine bezoars exported to China and issues a health certificate stating that the product meets the requirements of the Protocol.

VIII. Entry Quarantine Requirements

(i) Document check.

(1) Check whether an Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit has been obtained;

(2) Check whether the product comes from a registered enterprise;

(3) Check whether the health certificate is authentic and valid.

(ii) Goods inspection.

China Customs carries out quarantine of Brazilian bovine bezoars in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and this announcement. Lots passing quarantine are permitted entry.

(iii) Handling of non-compliance.

Where non-compliance is found in entry quarantine, China Customs handles it in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, the Protocol and other provisions.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

3 September 2026
