Mode 9610 and Mode 1210 are both customs supervision codes, and both were added in 2014. Mode 9610 appears in GACC Announcement No. 12 of 2014, under the full name cross-border trade e-commerce: clearance runs on list verification and consolidated declaration, goods ship direct from overseas, and the channel is commonly known as direct purchase import. Mode 1210 appears in Announcement No. 57 of the same year, under the full name bonded cross-border trade e-commerce: goods enter a customs special supervision zone or a bonded logistics center in bulk and leave against orders for domestic delivery; this is bonded online retail. The two lanes share one positive list and the same consolidated tax and annual personal quota.

The usual questions when picking a lane are "which freight is cheaper" and "which is faster", and that order is reversed. The supervision mode is decided by the tariff code and the list row: the row for colostrum powder's code 0404.9000 on the list carries the remark "bonded online retail goods only", so 9610 direct mail is not among the routes open to this tariff item, and the only lane left to discuss is 1210. This article sets the two lanes side by side and lands on one executable check: verify the list remark SKU by SKU, then fix the lane.

## Where the Two Lanes Differ: Where the Goods Sit

1210 stock enters the zone first. The bulk shipment enters the customs special supervision zone or the bonded logistics center under bonded status, leaves order by order as sales happen, and is delivered domestically on a stable, day-level schedule. 9610 stock sits overseas: once an order is placed, the parcel ships direct from abroad, adding international transport and clearance, and delivery times swing widely. For the same product, whether the goods are in a bonded warehouse in China or in a warehouse overseas is the starting point of every difference between the two lanes.

Stock location shapes everything downstream: the variance in delivery times, the path a return can take, the rhythm of the paperwork, and which chain the system integration has to follow.

## One List, One Set of Taxes

Both lanes apply to the same positive list of cross-border e-commerce retail imported goods. Whether the tariff code is on the list decides eligibility; what the row says in its remark decides which lane you may take. Both lanes have to pass this check, and neither can go around it.

The tax is the same set as well. Within the limits, the tariff rate is provisionally set at 0% and import VAT and consumption tax are collected at 70% of the statutory amount, as written in Cai Guan Shui [2016] No. 18; the single-transaction limit of 5,000 yuan and the annual limit of 26,000 yuan are written in Cai Guan Shui [2018] No. 49; the quota accrues per purchaser, and customs deducts it at clearance. These figures are identical under Mode 1210 and Mode 9610. So the difference between the lanes is not "which side taxes less" but "does the remark on this row let you through".

## How the Remark Column Switches 9610 Off

The remark column of the list is the lane switch. Item 5 of the adjustment table annexed to Announcement No. 7 of 2022 by the Ministry of Finance and seven other departments gives 0404.9000 the remark "bonded online retail goods only". Bonded online retail corresponds to 1210, and this row switches the 9610 direct purchase lane off. The 2019 version of the list had no such remark on this row; the 2022 version added it, and that is a tightening.

The remark does not talk about tax rates; it answers the lane question only. When you meet the claim that this kind of product can also ship direct, there is no need to argue: go back to that row on the list, and a few words end the conversation. The other side of the coin: the remark locks only the row it sits on. In the same store, SKUs under other tariff codes whose list rows carry no such remark are on solid ground with 9610. Lock the row, not the store: that is the key to reading remarks.

## Two Return Paths, and What They Cost

1210 returns have a clear document number and window. GACC Announcement No. 45 of 2020 opens two segments for retail import returns: apply to customs within 30 days from the date the declaration list is released, and within 45 days deliver the goods back to the original site of customs supervision operations, the original customs special supervision zone or the bonded logistics center (Type B). A return completed along this route has the corresponding tax waived, and the personal annual quota it used is adjusted accordingly. The goods are already in the zone, so the return is a short chain.

9610 goods sit overseas, so a return means another cross-border leg: export declaration, international freight and time all multiply. The window is still the window of Announcement No. 45, but delivering the goods to the original supervision site within 45 days is another international journey for a consignment sitting in an overseas warehouse. There is a further layer: for tariff items whose remark locks 1210, 9610 was never a route open to them in the first place, so its return does not even arise.

## The Verdict Move: Check SKU by SKU, Then Fix the Lane

The verdict breaks into three steps. Step one, classify to fix the tariff code: pin the product to a code by means of a classification decision or an advance ruling, with published classification decision texts and customs rules as the basis. Step two, read the list row: find the code in the list in force, copy the remark verbatim, and write down the list version. Step three, let the remark fix the lane: if the remark reads "bonded online retail goods only", go 1210; without that remark, 9610 also stays among the options.

SKU by SKU is a hard requirement. One classification decision does not cover a whole product line: reformulations and specification changes can drop the next SKU onto a different code and a different row. Dating the verdict materials and noting the list version is exactly what makes "when to re-verdict" traceable.

## Pick Once in Phase One, and Write It Where It Counts

The lane choice is not a switch operations can flip at any time. In phase one, write the chosen supervision mode into the filings and the store architecture: store, SKU, fulfillment and declaration chains are built to that standard, and the three-document cross-check integration is built on it too. Choosing means building the architecture; that is what "pick once" means.

This is not a lock. If a second lane really needs to open later, the changes land in the fulfillment and declaration chains; the storefront and product pages can stay untouched. But migrating mid-course costs far more than getting the first choice right. Get it right first, then talk about expansion.

## How the Two Routes Coexist: A and B

The two recommendations do not conflict. A: the remaining overseas supply also goes 1210, one unified path with the ledger and the three-document cross-check reused as a set. B: SKUs under non-0404 tariff codes, skincare products for example, whose list rows have been checked and carry no remark restriction, go 9610. One store may run different SKUs on different lanes, on the condition that every row has been checked and category experience is not used as a substitute for row-level verification.

Coexistence carries one more premise: the paperwork and the declaration standards of the two routes each follow their own code. Mixing the flows is fine; mixing the declarations is not.

## What the Page Handed to Customs and the Platform Says

The verdict has to land on paper, and that one page has to answer questions from customs and the platform on its own:

- Tariff code and classification basis per SKU: classification decision number or advance ruling decision, and the effective date.
- The list version in force and the row the SKU sits on: remark copied verbatim, sourced to item 5 of the adjustment table annexed to Announcement No. 7 of 2022.
- The supervision mode chosen: 1210 or 9610, and the date it was written into the filings and the store architecture.
- Returns and quota terms: the two windows of Announcement No. 45 of 2020, and the texts in force of Cai Guan Shui [2016] No. 18 and [2018] No. 49.

Platform review, questions from customs and internal project approval all read the same page, and the numbers and document numbers must agree from front to back. Every one of these bases comes from publicly available government documents, and each number can be traced back to its source text, and that is why a lane verdict stands up to questioning.
