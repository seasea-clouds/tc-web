On December 2, 2025, GACC issued Announcement No. 234: fresh bananas from Peru meeting the phytosanitary requirements are permitted for import. Only unripe green bananas with uncracked peel (Musa spp.) are allowed; orchards and packinghouses exporting to China shall be approved and registered by GACC.

The Announcement sets out rules in seven chapters: bananas shall be free from five quarantine pests of concern to China; orchards shall apply integrated pest management, establish a pest free production place for bacterial wilt race 2, and monitor every 15 days; pre-export sampling is at 2%, and entry requires a phytosanitary certificate with an English additional declaration.

The Peruvian side shall provide the registration list to the Chinese side before each export season; if quarantine pests or soil are found, the consignment shall not be exported; non-compliant consignments shall be returned, destroyed or treated.

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions on the phytosanitary requirements for the export of fresh bananas from Peru to China agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side, GACC) and the National Agrarian Health Service of the Ministry of Agrarian Development and Irrigation of the Republic of Peru (hereinafter referred to as the Peruvian side, SENASA), effective immediately, fresh bananas from Peru that meet the following requirements are permitted for import.

I. Legal Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(iii) Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(iv) Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported Fruits;

(v) Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Banana Fruits from Peru to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agrarian Development and Irrigation of the Republic of Peru.

II. Name of Commodity Permitted for Import

Fresh bananas (hereinafter referred to as bananas), scientific name Musa spp., English name Banana. Only green bananas that are unripe and have uncracked peel are permitted.

III. Permitted Places of Origin

Banana producing areas in Peru.

IV. Approved Orchards and Packinghouses

Orchards and packinghouses for bananas exported to China shall be reviewed by the Peruvian side and approved and registered by the Chinese side. The registration information shall include the name, address and identification number, so as to enable accurate traceability when exported consignments do not comply with the relevant provisions of this Announcement. Before each export season, the Peruvian side shall provide the registration list to the Chinese side.

V. List of Quarantine Pests of Concern to China

(1) Spiralling whitefly Aleurodicus dispersus

(2) Grey pineapple mealybug Dysmicoccus neobrevipes

(3) South American palm weevil Rhynchophorus palmarum

(4) West Indian red scale Selenaspidus articulatus

(5) Banana bacterial wilt race 2 Ralstonia solanacearum race 2

VI. Pre-export Management

(i) Orchard management.

(1) Orchards exporting to China shall establish and implement Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) to ensure the traceability of the production process; maintain orchard sanitary conditions, remove rotten, diseased and cracked fruits at harvest, and implement Integrated Pest Management (IPM), including pest monitoring, chemical or biological control and cultural practices and other control measures.

(2) Orchards exporting to China shall, under the supervision of the Peruvian side, adopt effective pest monitoring and integrated control measures to prevent the occurrence of quarantine pests of concern to China.

(3) Phytosanitary measures in orchards exporting to China must be implemented under the guidance of technical personnel with phytosanitary knowledge of pest prevention, control and monitoring, and such technical personnel shall receive training from the Peruvian side or its authorised institutions.

(4) Orchards exporting to China shall retain records of pest monitoring and control and provide them to the Chinese side upon request. The control records shall include detailed information such as the names, active ingredients, application dates and concentrations of all chemical agents used during the growing season.

(5) With respect to banana bacterial wilt race 2, orchards exporting to China shall establish a pest free production place (PFPP) in accordance with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 10 (ISPM 10). Once symptoms related to banana bacterial wilt race 2 are found in an orchard, integrated pest management measures must be implemented to eradicate the pest, and the pest free status may be restored only if there is no record of the pest for 6 months.

(6) For other quarantine pests of concern to China such as whiteflies and scale insects, orchard monitoring must be conducted once every 15 days from the flowering stage to the harvest stage of bananas. If pests or their corresponding symptoms are detected during monitoring, integrated management measures including chemical, physical or biological control shall be adopted to ensure that bananas exported to China are free from the aforementioned quarantine pests. Monitoring and control activities shall be carried out by producers and supervised by the Peruvian side.

(ii) Packinghouse management.

(1) The processing, packaging, storage and shipment of bananas exported to China shall be carried out under the supervision of the Peruvian side.

(2) During processing and packaging, bananas shall undergo procedures such as soaking and washing, manual sorting and grading, so as to ensure that they are free from insects, mites, snails, rotten fruits, weed seeds, plant debris and soil.

(3) Packaged bananas that need to be stored shall be placed in storage immediately and stored separately to avoid re-infestation by pests.

(4) Packinghouses shall establish a traceability system to ensure that bananas exported to China can be traced back to registered orchards, and shall record such information as the processing and packaging date, the name or registration number of the source orchard, the quantity of bananas, the export date, the export quantity, the country of destination, the vehicle or container number and the seal number. If chemicals are used during processing, the names, active ingredients, dosages and application dates of the chemicals shall also be recorded. The records shall be retained for at least two years.

(iii) Packaging requirements.

(1) Packaging materials shall be clean, hygienic and unused, and shall comply with the relevant phytosanitary and sanitary requirements of China. If wooden packaging is used, it shall comply with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15).

(2) Each box shall be labelled in Chinese or English with such information as the name of the fruit, the exporting country, the place of origin (province and city), and the names and registration numbers of the orchard and packinghouse. Each box and pallet shall be marked in Chinese or English with "To be exported to the People's Republic of China" or "Exported to the People's Republic of China".

(3) Containers or enclosed vehicles carrying bananas exported to China shall be clean, hygienic and sealed, and their seals shall remain intact upon arrival at the Chinese port of entry.

(iv) Pre-export inspection and quarantine.

(1) During the first two years from the start of trade, the Peruvian side shall conduct sampling inspection of bananas exported to China at a rate of 2% of each consignment. If no phytosanitary problems occur within the two years, the sampling rate may be reduced to 1%.

(2) If quarantine pests of concern to China or soil are found, the entire consignment shall not be exported to China. The Peruvian side shall investigate the causes and take improvement measures. Relevant records shall also be kept and provided to the Chinese side upon request.

(v) Phytosanitary certificate requirements.

For bananas that pass quarantine inspection, the Peruvian side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate in accordance with the relevant provisions of International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12 (ISPM 12), indicating the names or registration numbers of the orchard and packinghouse, and shall state in the additional declaration: "This consignment complies with the requirements specified in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Banana Fruits from Peru to China, and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China." (That is, this batch of goods complies with the requirements of the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Fresh Banana Fruits from Peru to China and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China.)

VII. Import Inspection and Quarantine and Handling of Non-compliance

When bananas exported to China arrive at a Chinese port of entry, China Customs shall conduct inspection and quarantine in accordance with the following requirements.

(i) Verification of relevant certificates and labels.

(1) Verify whether the imported bananas have obtained the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit.

(2) Verify whether the phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of (v) of Chapter VI of this Announcement.

(3) Verify whether the labels on boxes and pallets comply with the provisions of (iii) of Chapter VI of this Announcement.

(ii) Import inspection and quarantine.

(1) Bananas exported to China shall enter through ports permitted by the Chinese side for the import of fruits.

(2) Inspection and quarantine shall be conducted in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations, national standards and other provisions, and those that pass inspection and quarantine shall be permitted to enter.

(iii) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) If the bananas are found to come from orchards or packinghouses not approved by the Chinese side, the consignment shall not be permitted to enter.

(2) If ripe bananas or bananas with cracked peel are found, such bananas shall be returned or destroyed.

(3) If banana bacterial wilt race 2 is found, the consignment shall be returned or destroyed.

(4) If other quarantine pests of concern to China, or live quarantine pests newly occurring in Peru, as well as branches, leaves and soil, are found, the consignment shall be returned, destroyed or treated to eliminate the pests.

(5) If the bananas are found not to comply with Chinese food safety laws, regulations and national standards, the consignment shall be returned or destroyed.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs
