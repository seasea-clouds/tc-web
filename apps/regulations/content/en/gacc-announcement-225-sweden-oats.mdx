Swedish oats have been approved for export to China. Announcement No. 225 of 2025 requires that producing, processing and storage enterprises be registered by the Chinese side, with the list published after review. The oats come from throughout Sweden and are non-seed grain for food or food processing.

When exporting, first check the enterprise's Chinese registration number; apply integrated pest management at source and sieve and clean before shipment. The Swedish side inspects and issues a phytosanitary certificate, with an additional declaration of Protocol compliance and freedom from pests concerned by China. Bags carry the marking “Exported to the People’s Republic of China”; prepackaged labels follow GB 7718.

On arrival, customs checks registration and the certificate and carries out inspection and quarantine. Consignments without a valid certificate, from unregistered enterprises, or with unapproved GM material are returned or destroyed; treatable live pests are treated, and other cases are handled per the rules. The Chinese side notifies the Swedish side and suspends the relevant enterprises' qualifications.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry for Rural Affairs and Infrastructure of Sweden (hereinafter referred to as the Swedish side) concerning the phytosanitary requirements for the export of Swedish oats to China, imports of Swedish oats meeting the following requirements are permitted with effect from the date of this announcement:

Basis for inspection and quarantine

(i) the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(iii) the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(iv) the Administrative Measures for the Safety of Import and Export Food of the People's Republic of China;

(v) the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry for Rural Affairs and Infrastructure of Sweden on phytosanitary requirements for the export of oats from Sweden to China.

Name of goods permitted for entry

In this announcement, oats (Avena sativa) means oat grain produced in Sweden, exported to China for food use or food processing, and not used for seed.

Permitted place of origin

The whole territory of Sweden.

Enterprise registration

Enterprises producing, processing and storing oats for export to China shall be registered by the Chinese side to ensure compliance with Chinese inspection and quarantine requirements. The Swedish side shall submit in advance to the Chinese side the list of producing, processing and storage enterprises; after review and approval by the Chinese side they are registered, and the Chinese side publishes the relevant enterprise list on the GACC website.

Pests concerned by the Chinese side

Oats for export to China shall not carry the following pests:

(1) Tobacco ringspot virus

(2) Tomato black ring virus

(3) Barley stripe mosaic virus

(4) Ambrosia artemisiifolia

(5) Lolium temulentum

(6) Sorghum halepense

(7) Avena ludoviciana

(8) Claviceps purpurea

(9) Oculimacula yallundae

(10) Cephus pygmeus

(11) Mayetiola destructor

(12) Oscinella frit

Pre-shipment requirements

(i) Production requirements.

(1) The Swedish side shall supervise enterprises in establishing an integrated pest management (IPM) system to reduce the prevalence of pests concerned by the Chinese side. When pests concerned by the Chinese side or other harmful pests break out in Sweden, the Swedish side shall immediately suspend oat exports from the relevant areas, conduct investigations and adopt control measures. Exports to China may resume only after review and approval by the Chinese side.

(2) The Swedish side shall supervise the planting, harvesting, storage and transport of oats for export to China. Before shipment, enterprises shall adopt cleaning measures such as sieving to ensure that no soil, plant residues, weed seeds or other foreign impurities are carried.

(ii) Packaging and transport requirements.

(1) Oats for export to China shall be transported in packaging that is clean, hygienic, breathable and new, made of materials meeting Chinese phytosanitary and food safety requirements. Each bag shall be clearly marked with “Exported to the People’s Republic of China”, together with the product name, the names of the producing, processing and storage enterprises, their Chinese registration number and other traceability information. The packaging and labels of prepackaged oats for direct sale shall comply with Chinese regulations on the packaging and labelling of prepackaged food (GB 7718).

(2) Means of transport for oats for export to China shall meet hygiene and epidemic prevention requirements, and shall be thoroughly inspected before shipment to prevent the mixing of weed seeds, live insects, other grain impurities, plant residues, soil and so on.

(iii) Product requirements.

Oats for export to China shall comply with Chinese entry plant quarantine and food safety laws, regulations and standards, shall be free from pests concerned by the Chinese side, live insects, mites, molluscs, soil, feathers, animal excrement, weed seeds, branches and leaves and other animal or plant residues, and shall not deliberately have other grains or foreign impurities added or mixed in.

(iv) Pre-export inspection and quarantine and certificate requirements.

Before export, the Swedish side shall carry out inspection and quarantine of oats for export to China and issue a phytosanitary certificate for oats meeting the requirements; in the additional declaration field it shall state in English: “The consignment is in compliance with the requirements described in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of oats from Sweden to China, and is free from any pests concerned by China” (the consignment complies with the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of oats from Sweden to China and is free from pests concerned by China), together with the names of the producing, processing and storage enterprises of the consignment, their Chinese registration number, the means of transport number and other traceability information. Consignments in which live insects are found shall be fumigated before export, and the treatment indicators shall be stated on the phytosanitary certificate.

Inspection and quarantine on entry

(i) Document verification.

(1) verify whether the goods come from a registered enterprise;

(2) verify whether the phytosanitary certificate is authentic and valid.

(ii) Goods inspection.

In accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules, and in conjunction with this announcement, inspection and quarantine are carried out on the oats; entry is permitted after the goods pass inspection and quarantine.

(iii) Handling of non-compliant cases.

(1) consignments without a valid phytosanitary certificate are returned or destroyed;

(2) consignments from unregistered enterprises are returned or destroyed;

(3) consignments found to contain genetically modified components not approved by the Chinese authorities are returned or destroyed;

(4) where live quarantine pests or other live pests are found, pest treatment is applied if an effective treatment method exists; otherwise the consignment is returned or destroyed;

(5) other cases found not to comply with Chinese entry animal and plant quarantine and food safety laws, regulations and standards are handled in accordance with the relevant provisions.

Where the above non-compliances are found, the Chinese side shall notify the Swedish side and, according to the severity, take measures such as suspending the relevant enterprises' qualification for export to China, until the Swedish side adopts effective corrective measures.

This announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs

20 November 2025
