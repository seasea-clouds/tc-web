GACC Announcement No. 141 of 2026, effective 25 September 2026, issues 7 industry standards: SN/T 5939-2026, Rules for Inspection of Imported Recycled Pulp, takes effect on 1 October 2026, and the other 6 on 1 April 2027; 2 older standards are repealed when the new ones take effect.

You have a batch of imported recycled pulp arriving for inspection: prepare the test items and the report under SN/T 5939-2026, since the older SN/T 5939-2025 no longer applies; the full texts are in the Trade Navigation section of the China Technical Trade Measures website, so there is no need to wait for printed copies.

The publication date is not the implementation date: the announcement takes effect on 25 September 2026, but the 7 standards start on two different dates, so switch according to each row of the annex. Only 2 rows carry a replaced standard number, matching the 2 repealed standards in the text; the other 5 must not be read as replacing an older rule.

<!-- original -->

7 industry standards including the Rules for Inspection of Imported Recycled Pulp are hereby issued (see the annex for the catalogue). 2 industry standards including the Rules for Inspection of Imported Recycled Pulp (SN/T 5939-2025) are repealed with effect from the date on which the new standards take effect.

The texts of the standards issued here are available in the Trade Navigation section of the China Technical Trade Measures website (http://www.tbtsps.cn).

It is hereby announced.
