Plant quarantine requirements for imported plant-origin fertilizers have been revised. GACC Announcement No. 185 of 2025 sets out the revision, effective March 30, 2026. Such fertilizers come from plants, may still spread pests and diseases despite processing, and contain no animal-origin material. The rules cover all countries and regions.

Overseas producers, processors and storage operators must register with their local plant quarantine authority and be recommended to GACC, which reviews and registers them for 5 years. Products must carry no quarantine pests, soil or animal-origin components, and all materials must be non-reproductive. Packaging needs Chinese and English labels; wood packaging must meet ISPM 15.

Pre-export quarantine and a phytosanitary certificate under ISPM 12 are required, with an additional declaration that the consignment meets China's quarantine requirements. The customs declaration „purpose“ column must read „fertilizer“. At entry, documents and goods are checked; failed consignments are refused, destroyed or treated.

GACC Announcement No. 185 of 2025

To prevent the introduction of plant epidemics and alien invasive species, and to safeguard China's agricultural production and ecological security, in accordance with relevant laws, regulations and International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures, GACC has revised the plant quarantine requirements for plant-origin fertilizers entering China, formulated the „Plant Quarantine Requirements for Imported Plant-Origin Fertilizers (2025 Revision)“ and hereby promulgates it.

This Announcement shall take effect on March 30, 2026. The former „Plant Quarantine Requirements for Imported Plant-Origin Fertilizers“ (issued under Guozhi Jian Dong Han [2011] No. 674) shall be abolished at the same time.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of China

September 22, 2025

Plant Quarantine Requirements for Imported Plant-Origin Fertilizers

(2025 Revision)

I. Legal and Regulatory Basis

(1) „Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China“;

(2) „Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine“ and its implementing regulations.

II. Definition of Plant-Origin Fertilizers

For the purposes of these Requirements, plant-origin fertilizers refer to fertilizer products derived from plants that, though processed, may still spread pests and diseases and contain no animal-origin components, such as fertilizers made from processed crop straw.

III. Applicable Countries and Regions

All countries and regions.

IV. Enterprise Registration Requirements

Plant-origin fertilizers must be produced and stored under conditions that prevent contamination or infection. Producers, processors and storage operators of plant-origin fertilizers must be registered with the plant quarantine authority of the country or region where they are located and recommended to GACC. The recommendation information must include the enterprise name and the registration number assigned by the competent authority of the country or region, address, product name and composition, production and processing techniques and treatment specifications, epidemic prevention management measures, and packaging labels (in Chinese and English). Where fertilizer product registration is required by law, the registration information issued by the relevant Chinese competent authority must also be provided; where registration is exempted, relevant supporting documents must be provided.

GACC shall conduct a review; upon approval, the enterprise shall be registered and published on the website, with a registration validity period of 5 years. Within 3 months before expiry, the plant quarantine authority of the country or region shall recommend the enterprise again to GACC; upon GACC approval, the validity period shall be extended by 5 years.

V. Product Requirements

Plant-origin fertilizers exported to China must comply with China's laws, regulations and standards on fertilizer safety, hygiene and environmental protection, and meet the following requirements:

(1) They must not carry pests listed in the „List of Quarantine Pests for Imported Plants of the People's Republic of China“ or other pests and alien species that cause serious harm to agricultural and forestry production and the ecological environment;

(2) They must not carry soil, animal carcasses or other articles prohibited from entry by laws and regulations, or other biosecurity risk substances;

(3) They must not carry animal excreta, feathers or other animal-origin components;

(4) All materials in the plant-origin fertilizers must be incapable of reproduction.

VI. Requirements for Packaging, Storage and Transportation

(1) Where transported in packaging, the packaging materials must be unused and clean. Products must be sealed in packaging, and each package must be labelled in Chinese and English with information such as the name of the plant-origin fertilizer, place of origin, name of the producer, processor or storage operator and its registration number in China.

(2) The storage premises for plant-origin fertilizers prior to export must be relatively independent, clean and sanitary, with effective measures taken to prevent pest infestation.

(3) Means of transport must be clean and sanitary and free of pests and other foreign matter. Packaging must not be opened or replaced during transportation.

(4) Where wood packaging is used, it must comply with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15.

VII. Pre-Export Quarantine Requirements

The plant quarantine authority of the exporting country or region shall quarantine plant-origin fertilizers before they are exported to China. For plant-origin fertilizers that meet these Requirements, a phytosanitary certificate shall be issued in accordance with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12, with an additional declaration stating „the consignment complies with China's plant quarantine requirements for imported plant-origin fertilizers“ and the names of the producer, processor and storage operators and their registration numbers in China. For plant-origin fertilizers that have undergone treatment, the treatment specifications, including temperature, humidity, chemical agent and concentration, shall be indicated in the phytosanitary certificate.

VIII. Import Declaration Requirements

The domestic consignee or its agent shall make a declaration to the customs in accordance with the law, fill in „fertilizer“ in the „purpose“ column of the customs declaration, and provide the phytosanitary certificate and other documents as required.

IX. Entry Quarantine and Handling of Non-Compliance

(1) Document verification.

Verify whether the phytosanitary certificate is authentic and valid and whether it is consistent with the declaration and goods information.

(2) Goods inspection.

Quarantine inspection shall be carried out on the plant-origin fertilizers, their packaging and means of transport. Where necessary, samples shall be taken and sent to a laboratory for quarantine. Consignments that pass quarantine shall be allowed entry.

(3) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) Any consignment without a valid quarantine certificate issued by the plant quarantine authority of the exporting country or region shall be refused entry or destroyed.

(2) Where soil, animal carcasses or other articles prohibited from entry by laws and regulations are found, the consignment shall be refused entry or destroyed.

(3) Where live quarantine pests or other live pests that cause serious harm to agricultural and forestry production and the ecological environment are found, entry shall be allowed after treatment qualifies; where no effective treatment method is available, the consignment shall be refused entry or destroyed.

(4) Where other cases do not comply with China's laws, regulations and national standards on plant quarantine for entry, they shall be handled in accordance with relevant provisions.

With regard to the relevant violations discovered, GACC shall notify the plant quarantine authority of the exporting country or region and, as appropriate, take measures such as suspending the entry of the relevant enterprises or the relevant types of plant-origin fertilizers in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

X. Other Requirements

GACC shall conduct further risk assessments of imported plant-origin fertilizers based on pest interception and other circumstances, and dynamically adjust the list of permitted plant-origin fertilizers and the list of exporting countries or regions.

Samples of imported plant-origin fertilizers used for fertilizer registration may, upon presentation of relevant supporting documents, be exempted from the requirements for registration of producers, processors and storage operators, and their registration numbers in China may be omitted from the packaging and phytosanitary certificate.
