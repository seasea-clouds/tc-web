Opening paragraph. Two to four sentences that state the problem and promise a concrete answer.

## First Section Heading

Body paragraph. Keep sections short and specific.

- One point per line
- No blank line between list items
- Three to six items per list

Amounts: always spell the currency out in words, never with a currency sign or an ISO code. Write "50 US dollars" and "5000 yuan", not "$50", "¥5000" or "360 USD". Only these two currencies are allowed.
