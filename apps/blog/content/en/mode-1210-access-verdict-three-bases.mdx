Whether a product can enter China through the Mode 1210 bonded route is not a matter of experience or a service provider's quote. It rests on three citable bases: classification, the positive list, and tax. All three must hold for the route to hold; if any one fails, tax and logistics no longer matter. Bovine colostrum powder is the worked example that runs through this guide.

The order is fixed: classify the product to an HS code, check the list for eligibility and the line note, then calculate tax and quota against that code. The code is the root; the list line and the tax rate hang from it. Get the code wrong and the other two bases fall with it. This method applies to any cross-border retail product headed for Mode 1210; food is one category.

## What Mode 1210 is, and why the verdict revolves around it

Mode 1210 is the customs supervision code for cross-border e-commerce bonded retail import: goods enter a bonded warehouse in bulk, leave in batches against orders, and are declared by the buyer for personal use. Mode 9610 is direct purchase import: goods ship from overseas straight to the buyer and never enter a bonded warehouse. The difference is where the goods sit, not which courier carries them.

The access verdict revolves around Mode 1210 because the HS code and the list line can limit the supervision mode. When a list line carries the note "bonded e-commerce goods only", that code can only use Mode 1210; 9610 direct purchase is not an option. The verdict first names the supervision mode, then the tax, documents, and quota. The mode follows the code and the list, not a freight quote.

## Basis one, classification: fix the product to an HS code first

Classification is where every verdict starts. Customs determines the code by heading, subheading, composition, and processing method. Where a classification decision or administrative ruling exists, identical goods follow it. Decisions are published by the General Administration of Customs and bind classification; of the three bases, this is the hardest one.

The colostrum example: classification decision W2020-29 describes powder made from bovine colostrum collected in the first 24 hours after calving and classifies it under 0404.9000 as a natural-milk product not elsewhere specified. It is not classed as bovine milk under 0401, and a powder does not take the liquid-milk code. Headings 0401 and 0404 are different, with different regulatory conditions; declaring colostrum powder under 0401 puts the product into the milk regime by choice.

A classification decision covers a specific form. Add vitamins, minerals, or other ingredients and the code may move to another heading; change the process or the packaging form and the code must be rechecked. Judge each SKU on its own. One classification decision cannot cover a whole product line.

## Basis two, the list: is the code on it, and what does the note say?

The cross-border e-commerce retail import list is managed by HS code. The version in force since 1 March 2022 contains 1,476 codes. If the code is not on the list, Mode 1210 retail import is not available, however clean the tax math looks. If it is on the list, read the note on that line; the note can close the route.

The colostrum example: 0404.9000 is on the list, and its line note reads "bonded e-commerce goods only". Bonded e-commerce means Mode 1210; direct purchase means Mode 9610. The note separates the two. When the market says this kind of product can ship via 9610, go back to the exact wording of the list note; the answer is plain.

The list changes. The 2022 version refined the 2019 version, and later versions will follow. Check the current version and the exact note before each stocking run; do not build a long-term plan on an old list.

## Basis three, tax: where the rate comes from and how the quota is deducted

Within the limits, cross-border e-commerce retail imports pay duty at a provisional 0%, and import VAT at 70% of the statutory amount. For a product with a 13% VAT rate, 13% times 70% gives 9.1%, which is the consolidated tax; the tax payable is the dutiable value times 9.1%. For goods at another VAT rate, substitute that rate into the same formula. The buyer is the taxpayer; the platform or logistics provider may collect and remit, and the tax settles with the order.

The single-transaction limit is 5,000 yuan and the annual limit is 26,000 yuan, about 700 and 3,600 US dollars at 7.2 yuan per dollar, for reference. Quota accrues per buyer and is deducted by Customs at clearance. Orders within the limits pay the consolidated tax above. An order above the single limit that contains only one unit may still be imported after paying full goods tax, with the amount counted toward the annual total; an order above the single limit with more than one unit, or any total above the annual limit, leaves the cross-border retail regime and is managed as general trade.

The colostrum calculation: a 50-dollar jar is about 360 yuan at 7.2 yuan per dollar, and the consolidated tax is about 32.8 yuan, or about 4.5 US dollars. The tax is modest, but it rests on a correct code and a correct list line; a wrong code cannot be rescued by 9.1%.

## When the three bases conflict, which one wins?

In descending order of authority: classification decision, list note, platform and market practice. A classification decision is published by the General Administration of Customs and binds classification; a list note comes from an eight-department announcement and decides whether the goods may use this route; platform pages, peer practice, and customs habits are references only and cannot overturn the first two.

Three conflicts are common. A platform system accepts a wrong code: the classification decision wins. A provider says this kind of product has always shipped via 9610: the list note wins. The market splits orders to stay under the limits: the limit and tax rules win. Reference information tells you how customs operates, not what is lawful.

## What goes into the access memo

Put the verdict on paper, with four items that cannot be omitted:

- HS code and classification basis: the code, the classification decision number relied on, the product form, and the verdict date. For colostrum powder: 0404.9000, under W2020-29.
- List basis: the current list version, the line holding the code, and the exact note.
- Tax basis: the applicable rate and formula, the limit treatment, and how the buyer's quota is deducted.
- Customs execution: declaration practice, document requirements, and an escalation contact, set by us in the memo and delivered with the first declaration.

The memo is not only an internal record. Platform review, customs inquiries, and internal approval use the same document, so figures and document numbers must match. Conventional food or Blue Hat is a separate decision that shapes claims and testing; if you choose Blue Hat, the product identity and listing materials must be updated, while the code and the list line are still checked against the product itself.

## A verdict expires; when must it be redone?

A new list version, a new or revised classification decision, or a change in formula or process means the verdict must be redone. Watch announcements for the first two and your own product files for the third. The date on the memo exists to tell you when to review.

One trigger is often missed: a change of pack size, say from a 300-gram jar to stick packs. Classification usually stays, but the dutiable value and the single-order limit math must be recalculated. A verdict has a shelf life; review it when it comes due, and do not treat it as a permanent certificate.
