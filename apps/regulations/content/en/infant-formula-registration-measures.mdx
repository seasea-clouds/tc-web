SAMR Order No. 80 (revised in 2025) establishes the registration system for infant formula milk powder product formulas: formulas for domestic sale and imported formulas must both be reviewed and approved by SAMR.

Under Article 8, overseas manufacturers intending to export to China may act as applicants, provided they possess research and development, production and inspection capabilities and conduct batch-by-batch inspection; in principle, each enterprise may not exceed three formula series and nine formulas.

Applicants submit nine categories of materials under Article 9, with review taking sixty working days; Chapter III separately sets labeling and instruction requirements, and Article 37 lists nine categories of prohibited content, which also apply to imported products.

(Promulgated as State Administration for Market Regulation Order No. 80 on 26 June 2023, and revised in accordance with State Administration for Market Regulation Order No. 109 of 1 December 2025.)

Chapter I. General Provisions

Article 1. These Measures are formulated in order to strictly regulate the registration management of infant formula milk powder product formulas, ensure the quality and safety of infant formula milk powder, and pursuant to the Administrative Licensing Law of the People's Republic of China, the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, the Regulations for the Implementation of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws and regulations.

Article 2. These Measures shall apply to the registration management of infant formula milk powder product formulas produced, sold and imported within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

Article 3. Registration of infant formula milk powder product formulas means the activity whereby the State Administration for Market Regulation, in accordance with the procedures and requirements prescribed in these Measures, reviews the infant formula milk powder product formulas submitted for registration and decides whether to approve registration.

Article 4. The registration management of infant formula milk powder product formulas shall follow the principles of being scientific, strict, open, fair and impartial.

Article 5. The State Administration for Market Regulation shall be responsible for the registration management of infant formula milk powder product formulas. The food review institution of the State Administration for Market Regulation (the Food Review Centre, hereinafter referred to as the review institution) shall be responsible for the acceptance, technical review, on-site verification, certificate preparation and delivery, and other work relating to applications for registration of infant formula milk powder product formulas, and shall organise expert demonstrations as needed. Market regulation departments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall cooperate in the on-site verification and other work relating to the registration of infant formula milk powder product formulas.

Article 6. An applicant for registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula (hereinafter referred to as the applicant) shall be responsible for the authenticity, completeness and legality of the materials submitted and shall bear legal liability. The applicant shall cooperate with market regulation departments in on-site verification, sampling inspection and other work relating to registration, and provide the necessary working conditions.

Article 7. Research and development and innovation in infant formula milk powder product formulas are encouraged, and formulas shall be optimised in light of research findings on breast milk to improve the quality of infant formula milk powder.

Chapter II. Application and Registration

Article 8. An applicant shall be a production enterprise intending to produce and sell infant formula milk powder within the territory of the People's Republic of China, or an overseas production enterprise intending to export infant formula milk powder to the People's Republic of China. The applicant shall possess research and development capabilities, production capabilities and inspection capabilities commensurate with the infant formula milk powder produced, comply with the good manufacturing practice requirements for powdered infant formula foods, implement a hazard analysis and critical control point system, and conduct batch-by-batch inspection of ex-factory products in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations and the items prescribed in the national food safety standards for infant formula milk powder. Where an enterprise group has an independent research and development institution, a holding subsidiary, as the applicant, may share part of the group's research and development capabilities. Where an applicant uses a compound ingredient that already complies with the nutrient composition requirements of the national food safety standards for infant formula foods as a raw material to apply for formula registration, no registration shall be granted.

Article 9. An application for product formula registration shall comply with the relevant laws, regulations and national food safety standards, and shall provide research and demonstration reports and sufficient evidence supporting the scientific basis and safety of the product formula. To apply for registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula, the following materials shall be submitted to the State Administration for Market Regulation: (1) an application form for registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula; (2) the applicant's entity qualification documents; (3) the quality and safety standards of raw and auxiliary materials; (4) the product formula; (5) a research and development and demonstration report on the product formula; (6) a description of the production process; (7) a product inspection report; (8) materials evidencing research and development capabilities, production capabilities and inspection capabilities; (9) other materials demonstrating the scientific basis and safety of the formula. The applicant shall, in accordance with the relevant state provisions, mark and state the basis for any trade secrets, undisclosed information or confidential business information in the application materials.

Article 10. Where the same enterprise applies for registration of two or more product formulas for the same age group, there shall be clear differences between the product formulas, which shall be scientifically substantiated. In principle, each enterprise may not exceed three formula series and nine product formulas, and each formula series shall comprise infant formula milk powder (0–6 months of age, Stage 1), follow-on infant formula milk powder (6–12 months of age, Stage 2), and young child formula milk powder (12–36 months of age, Stage 3).

Article 11. The parent company of an enterprise group, or its holding subsidiary, that has obtained a registration certificate for an infant formula milk powder product formula and a production licence may use an infant formula milk powder product formula already registered by another holding subsidiary within the same enterprise group or by the parent company of the enterprise group. Before organising production, the parent company of the enterprise group shall fully assess the feasibility of using the formula, ensure product quality and safety, and submit a written report to the State Administration for Market Regulation.

Article 12. Applications for registration of infant formula milk powder product formulas submitted by applicants shall be dealt with in accordance with the following circumstances respectively: (1) where the matter applied for does not require registration under the law, the applicant shall be informed immediately that the application will not be accepted; (2) where the matter applied for does not fall within the functions and powers of the State Administration for Market Regulation under the law, a decision not to accept shall be made immediately and the applicant shall be informed to apply to the relevant administrative organ; (3) where there are errors in the application materials that can be corrected on the spot, the applicant shall be permitted to correct them on the spot; (4) where the application materials are incomplete or do not conform to the statutory form, the applicant shall be informed once, on the spot or within five working days, of all the content that needs to be supplemented and corrected; if no such notice is given within the time limit, the application shall be deemed accepted from the date of receipt of the application materials; (5) where the matter applied for falls within the functions and powers of the State Administration for Market Regulation and the application materials are complete and conform to the statutory form, or the applicant has submitted all supplementary and corrected application materials as required, the registration application shall be accepted. For acceptance or non-acceptance of a registration application, a certificate bearing the special administrative licensing seal of the State Administration for Market Regulation and the date shall be issued.

Article 13. The review institution shall examine the scientific basis and safety of the applied formula and the consistency between the claims made for the product formula and the registered content of the product formula, and shall complete the review within sixty working days from the date of acceptance. Where an extension of the review period is required under special circumstances, an extension of twenty working days may be granted with the consent of the head of the review institution, and the decision to extend shall be notified to the applicant in writing.

Article 14. Where, in the course of review, it is considered necessary for the applicant to supplement or correct materials, the review institution shall notify the applicant once of all the content that needs to be supplemented or corrected. The applicant shall supplement and correct the materials once, within three months, in accordance with the requirements of the supplement notice. The time taken to supplement and correct materials shall not be counted towards the review period.

Article 15. The review institution shall organise on-site verification and sampling inspection according to actual needs, and, where necessary, carry out extended verification of raw material production enterprises and others. On-site verification shall verify the applicant's research and development capabilities, production capabilities and inspection capabilities, as well as the consistency between the application materials and the actual situation, and shall draw samples from dynamic production for inspection. The varieties of dynamic production samples for sampling inspection shall be determined on the basis of risk.

Article 16. On-site verification shall be carried out in any of the following circumstances: (1) the three formula series and nine product formulas applied for registration by the applicant for the first time; (2) where there is a major change in the composition of the product formula; (3) where the type of production process changes and the formula already registered by the applicant and still within its validity period does not include such a process type; (4) where the production address undergoes an actual change; (5) where problems requiring verification through on-site verification are discovered during the technical review; (6) where there was concealment of the true situation or provision of false materials in a previous registration application; (7) other circumstances requiring on-site verification. Where major changes occur in the national food safety standards for infant formula foods and the applicant applies for product formula registration or amendment, the review institution shall carry out on-site verification. However, where all three product formulas of the same series of the applicant have obtained administrative licences after the change in standards, on-site verification may not be carried out again for product formulas of other series with the same production process type.

Article 17. Where on-site verification is required, the review institution shall notify the applicant of the verification matters in writing or by electronic means, and the applicant shall feedback the date on which it accepts on-site verification within thirty working days. Where feedback cannot be given within the prescribed time limit due to force majeure or other reasons, the applicant shall submit a written application for extension and state the reasons. The review institution shall complete the on-site verification within twenty working days from the date of on-site verification confirmed by the applicant. Where the review institution notifies the market regulation department at the provincial level of the place where the applicant is located to participate in the on-site verification, that department shall send personnel to participate.

Article 18. The review institution shall entrust a legally qualified food inspection institution to carry out the inspection. The inspection institution shall, within twenty working days from the date of receipt of the samples, complete the inspection in accordance with the national food safety standards and the determination methods submitted by the applicant, and issue a sample inspection report to the review institution.

Article 19. The review institution shall carry out the review on the basis of the application materials, on-site verification reports and sample inspection reports submitted by the applicant, and shall draw review conclusions. In the course of technical review, on-site verification and product inspection, opinions may be sought from experts in the fields of food safety, food processing, nutrition and clinical medicine on major and complex issues.

Article 20. Where the applicant's application satisfies the statutory conditions and standards, the product formula is scientific and safe, and the conclusions of the on-site verification report and the inspection report are in compliance with the registration requirements, the review institution shall draw a review conclusion recommending that registration be granted.

Article 21. The review institution shall draw a review conclusion proposing that registration be refused in any of the following circumstances: (1) the application materials are falsified or untrue; (2) the scientific basis and safety evidence for the product formula are insufficient; (3) the applicant does not possess the research and development capabilities, production capabilities or inspection capabilities commensurate with the product formula applied for registration; (4) the applicant fails to submit supplementary and corrected materials within the prescribed time limit, or the materials submitted do not meet the requirements; (5) the applicant fails to confirm the date of on-site verification within the time limit, or refuses to cooperate or does not cooperate with on-site verification or sampling inspection; (6) the conclusion of the on-site verification report or the inspection report is not in compliance with the registration requirements; (7) there is no clear difference between the product formula applied for registration by the same enterprise and its product formulas already applied for in the same age group; (8) other circumstances that do not meet the registration requirements prescribed by laws, regulations, rules, national food safety standards, etc. Where the review institution draws a conclusion refusing registration, it shall issue a notice of proposed refusal to the applicant and state the reasons. If the applicant objects to the review conclusion, it shall, within twenty working days from the date of receipt of the notice, submit a written application for re-examination to the review institution and state the reasons for re-examination. The re-examination shall be limited to the original application matters and application materials. The review institution shall make a re-examination decision within thirty working days from the date of acceptance of the re-examination application and notify the applicant.

Article 22. After the review is concluded, the State Administration for Market Regulation shall, in accordance with the law, decide whether to approve. For approved registrations, a registration certificate for the infant formula milk powder product formula shall be issued. For refused registrations, a decision of refusal shall be issued, the reasons shall be stated, and the applicant shall be informed of its right to apply for administrative reconsideration or institute administrative litigation in accordance with the law.

Article 23. The State Administration for Market Regulation shall make a decision within twenty working days from the date of acceptance. The review institution shall, within ten working days from the date on which the State Administration for Market Regulation makes its decision, deliver to the applicant the registration certificate for the infant formula milk powder product formula or the decision of refusal.

Article 24. The time required for on-site verification, sampling inspection and re-examination shall not be counted towards the review period. The time limits for on-site verification and sampling inspection of overseas production enterprises shall be determined according to the actual circumstances.

Article 25. The registration certificate for an infant formula milk powder product formula and its annexes shall specify the following matters: (1) the product name; (2) the enterprise name and production address; (3) the registration number, approval date and validity period; (4) the type of production process; (5) the product formula. The format of the registration number for infant formula milk powder product formulas shall be: Guoshi Zhuzi YP plus a four-digit year number plus a four-digit serial number, where YP stands for infant formula milk powder product formula. The registration certificate for an infant formula milk powder product formula shall be valid for five years, and an electronic certificate shall have the same legal effect as a paper certificate.

Article 26. During the validity period of the registration certificate for an infant formula milk powder product formula, if the certificate is lost or damaged, the applicant shall apply to the State Administration for Market Regulation for reissue and state the reasons. Where reissue is applied for on the ground of loss, a loss statement shall be submitted; where reissue is applied for on the ground of damage, the original registration certificate shall be returned. The State Administration for Market Regulation shall reissue the certificate within ten working days from the date of acceptance. The reissued registration certificate shall indicate the original approval date and bear the words “reissued”.

Article 27. During the validity period of the registration certificate for an infant formula milk powder product formula, where the applicant needs to amend the certificate or the matters specified in the annexes, it shall apply to the State Administration for Market Regulation for amendment of registration and submit the following materials: (1) an application form for amendment of registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula; (2) a demonstration report on the amendment of the product formula; (3) other materials relating to the matters to be amended.

Article 28. Where an applicant applies for an amendment to a product formula or other matters that may affect the scientific basis or safety of the product formula, the review institution shall organise a review in accordance with Article 13 of these Measures and draw a review conclusion. Where an applicant applies for a change in enterprise name, production address name, product name or other matters that do not affect the scientific basis or safety of the product formula, the review institution shall verify the matter and draw a review conclusion within ten working days from the date of acceptance. Where an applicant changes its enterprise name, it shall apply under the changed name. The State Administration for Market Regulation shall decide whether to approve the amendment within ten working days from the date on which the review conclusion is made. For those satisfying the conditions, the amendment procedures shall be completed in accordance with the law, the date of issuance of the registration certificate shall be the date of approval of the amendment, the original registration number shall remain unchanged, and the validity period of the certificate shall remain unchanged; where the amendment is not approved, a decision of refusal to amend the registration shall be issued, the reasons shall be stated, and the applicant shall be informed of its right to apply for administrative reconsideration or institute administrative litigation in accordance with the law.

Article 29. Where the varieties of raw materials (including food additives) of the product formula remain unchanged, the order of the ingredient list remains unchanged, and the nutrition fact table remains unchanged, and the amounts used fluctuate or are adjusted reasonably within a certain range, no application for amendment is required. Where the varieties of raw materials (including food additives) of the product formula and the nutrition fact table are adjusted at the same time, thus essentially constituting a new product formula, a new application for product formula registration shall be made.

Article 30. Where the validity period of a registration certificate for an infant formula milk powder product formula expires and renewal is required, the applicant shall apply to the State Administration for Market Regulation for renewal of registration six months before the expiry of the validity period of the certificate, and submit the following materials: (1) an application form for renewal of registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula; (2) the applicant's entity qualification documents; (3) information on the enterprise's research and development capabilities, production capabilities and inspection capabilities; (4) a self-inspection report on the enterprise's production quality management system; (5) follow-up evaluation information on the nutrition and safety of the product; (6) a written opinion on renewal of registration issued by the market regulation department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the production enterprise is located. The review institution shall organise a review of the renewal application in accordance with Article 13 of these Measures and draw a review conclusion. The State Administration for Market Regulation shall decide whether to approve the renewal within twenty working days from the date of acceptance of the application. Where the renewal is approved, a new registration certificate shall be issued to the applicant, the original registration number shall remain unchanged, and the validity period of the certificate shall be recalculated from the date of approval; where the renewal is not approved, a decision of refusal to renew the registration shall be issued, the reasons shall be stated, and the applicant shall be informed of its right to apply for administrative reconsideration or institute administrative litigation in accordance with the law. Where no decision is made within the time limit, the renewal shall be deemed to have been approved.

Article 31. Registration shall not be renewed in any of the following circumstances: (1) failure to apply for renewal within the prescribed time limit; (2) the applicant has not organised production in accordance with the registered formula within five years after the product formula was registered; (3) the enterprise has failed to maintain its research and development capabilities, production capabilities and inspection capabilities as at the time of registration; (4) other circumstances that do not comply with the relevant provisions.

Article 32. Where the procedures for amendment of registration and renewal of registration of infant formula milk powder product formulas are not prescribed, the relevant provisions of these Measures on registration of infant formula milk powder product formulas shall apply.

Chapter III. Labels and Instructions

Article 33. The labels and instructions for infant formula milk powder shall comply with laws, regulations, rules and national food safety standards, and shall be marked in accordance with the provisions of the State Administration for Market Regulation. When applying for registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula, the applicant shall submit label drafts and explanatory materials for any claims; where instructions are submitted at the same time, the instructions shall be consistent with the label content. Where the labels and instructions relate to the product formula of infant formula milk powder, they shall be consistent with the registered content of the product formula and shall bear the registration number.

Article 34. Where the product name contains words indicating an animal source, the sources of milk protein such as raw milk, milk powder and whey powder shall all come from that species. The ingredient list shall indicate the specific names of the edible vegetable oils in descending order of the amount added. The nutrition fact table shall be listed in the order of nutrients prescribed in the national food safety standards for infant formula milk powder, and shall be listed by categories such as energy, protein, fat, carbohydrate, vitamins, minerals and optional ingredients.

Article 35. Where claims are made about the source of raw materials such as raw milk or raw milk powder, the country of origin or the specific place of origin shall be truthfully indicated.

Article 36. The label shall indicate the applicable months of age for the infant formula milk powder, and may use the expressions “Stage 1”, “Stage 2” and “Stage 3” at the same time.

Article 37. Labels shall not contain any of the following: (1) content relating to the prevention or treatment of diseases; (2) express or implied claims of health effects such as enhancing immunity or regulating intestinal flora; (3) express or implied functional statements such as improving intelligence, increasing resistance or protecting the intestines; (4) the use of words such as “not added”, “not contained” or “zero added” to emphasise that substances which should not be contained in or used in the product formula under laws, regulations and national food safety standards have not been used or are not contained; (5) false, exaggerated, unscientific or absolute content; (6) the use of vague information such as “imported milk sources”, “from foreign pastures”, “ecological pastures”, “imported ingredients”, “original ecological milk sources” or “pollution-free milk sources”; (7) claims inconsistent with the registered content of the product formula; (8) the use of images of infants and women, or expressions such as “humanised”, “maternalised” or similar terms; (9) other content that does not comply with laws, regulations, rules and national food safety standards.

Chapter IV. Supervision and Administration

Article 38. The institutions and personnel undertaking technical review, on-site verification and sampling inspection shall be responsible for the review conclusions, on-site verification reports and product inspection reports they issue; experts participating in demonstrations shall abide by professional ethics when issuing expert opinions. Technical review, on-site verification, sampling inspection and expert demonstrations shall be carried out in accordance with laws, regulations, rules, national food safety standards and technical specifications, so as to ensure that the relevant work is scientific, objective and impartial.

Article 39. Where market regulation departments receive reports from relevant units or individuals concerning violations of laws or regulations in the registration of infant formula milk powder product formulas, they shall verify and handle them in a timely manner.

Article 40. The State Administration for Market Regulation shall publish the registration information of infant formula milk powder product formulas within twenty working days from the date of approval.

Article 41. Without the consent of the applicant, institutions and personnel participating in the registration of infant formula milk powder product formulas shall not disclose trade secrets, undisclosed information or confidential business information submitted by the applicant, except as otherwise provided by law or where national security or major public interests are involved.

Article 42. After an application for registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula has been accepted, if the applicant requests to withdraw the application, it shall submit a written application and state the reasons. If the withdrawal is agreed to, the State Administration for Market Regulation shall terminate its registration procedure. If, in the course of technical review, on-site verification and sampling inspection, suspected violations such as concealment of the true situation or provision of false information are discovered, they shall be handled in accordance with the law, and the applicant may not withdraw the registration application.

Article 43. The State Administration for Market Regulation may, upon the request of an interested party or ex officio, revoke the registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula in any of the following circumstances: (1) where the decision to approve registration was made by staff abusing power or neglecting duty; (2) where the decision to approve registration was made in excess of statutory functions and powers; (3) where the decision to approve registration was made in violation of statutory procedures; (4) where registration was approved for an applicant who was not qualified to apply or did not meet the statutory conditions; (5) other circumstances in which registration may be revoked in accordance with the law.

Article 44. The State Administration for Market Regulation shall cancel the registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula in any of the following circumstances: (1) where the enterprise applies for cancellation; (2) where the enterprise is terminated in accordance with the law; (3) where the validity period of the registration certificate expires without renewal; (4) where the registration certificate is revoked, withdrawn or has its registration cancelled in accordance with the law; (5) other circumstances in which cancellation is required by laws or regulations.

Chapter V. Legal Liability

Article 45. Where laws and regulations such as the Food Safety Law already provide for violations relating to the registration of infant formula milk powder product formulas, those provisions shall apply.

Article 46. Where an applicant conceals relevant information or provides false materials when applying for registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula, the State Administration for Market Regulation shall refuse to accept the application or refuse registration, and shall issue a warning to the applicant; the applicant may not apply again for registration of an infant formula milk powder product formula within one year; if a crime is suspected, the matter shall be transferred to the public security organ for criminal liability in accordance with the law. Where an applicant obtains a registration certificate for an infant formula milk powder product formula by fraud, bribery or other improper means, the State Administration for Market Regulation shall revoke it in accordance with the law, and the licensee may not apply for registration again within three years; a fine of not less than ten thousand yuan and not more than thirty thousand yuan shall be imposed; if harmful consequences are caused, a fine of not less than thirty thousand yuan and not more than two hundred thousand yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is suspected, the matter shall be transferred to the public security organ for criminal liability in accordance with the law.

Article 47. Where an applicant changes matters that do not affect the scientific basis or safety of the product formula without applying for amendment in accordance with the law, the market regulation department at or above the county level shall order rectification within a prescribed period; if rectification is not made within the period, a fine of not less than one thousand yuan and not more than ten thousand yuan shall be imposed. Where an applicant changes matters that may affect the scientific basis or safety of the product formula without applying for amendment in accordance with the law, the market regulation department at or above the county level shall impose a penalty in accordance with Article 124 of the Food Safety Law.

Article 48. Whoever forges, alters, resells, rents, lends or transfers a registration certificate for an infant formula milk powder product formula shall be fined not less than thirty thousand yuan and not more than one hundred thousand yuan by the market regulation department at or above the county level; if harmful consequences are caused, a fine of not less than one hundred thousand yuan and not more than two hundred thousand yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is suspected, the matter shall be transferred to the public security organ for criminal liability in accordance with the law.

Article 49. Where producers or sellers of infant formula milk powder violate Articles 33 to 37 of these Measures, the local market regulation department at or above the county level shall order rectification within a prescribed period and impose a fine of not less than ten thousand yuan and not more than thirty thousand yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than thirty thousand yuan and not more than one hundred thousand yuan shall be imposed; if harmful consequences are caused, a fine of not less than one hundred thousand yuan and not more than two hundred thousand yuan shall be imposed.

Article 50. Where market regulation departments and their staff approve registration for applicants who do not meet the conditions, or approve registration in excess of statutory functions and powers, they shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 144 of the Food Safety Law. Where market regulation departments and their staff abuse power, neglect duty or engage in malpractices for personal gain in the course of registration review, they shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 145 of the Food Safety Law.

Chapter VI. Supplementary Provisions

Article 51. For the purposes of these Measures, the term “product formula of infant formula milk powder” means the food raw materials and food additives used in the production of infant formula milk powder and the amounts thereof, as well as the content of nutrients in the product.

Article 52. The registration management of product formulas of infant formula liquid milk shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of these Measures concerning product formulas of infant formula milk powder.

Article 53. These Measures shall come into force on 1 October 2023, and the Measures for the Registration of Product Formulas of Infant Formula Milk Powder promulgated as Order No. 26 of the former State Food and Drug Administration on 6 June 2016 shall be abolished at the same time.
