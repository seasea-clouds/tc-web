Import is banned as of today. After Botswana reported foot and mouth disease to the World Organisation for Animal Health, the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs issued Announcement No. 67: cloven hoofed animals and related products from Botswana, whether direct, via third countries, or posted or carried, all covered.

Frozen beef transshipped via a third country arrives: verify origin first; once it hits the ban, no release. Goods are returned or destroyed under customs supervision; waste and swill unloaded from transport undergo disinfection treatment; illegal inbound goods seized at border checks are destroyed.

The six measures take effect on issue. Violators face the Customs Law, Biosecurity Law, Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and its implementing rules; two prior notices are repealed together. Customs and agriculture-rural departments do quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision.

<!-- original -->

Recently, Botswana reported foot and mouth disease to the World Organisation for Animal Health. To prevent the introduction of the disease and to protect the safety of China's livestock sector and its biosecurity, the following is announced in accordance with relevant laws and regulations:

I. It is prohibited to import, directly or indirectly, cloven hoofed animals and related products from Botswana (products derived from cloven hoofed animals that are unprocessed, or that have been processed but may still transmit the disease).

II. It is prohibited to send by post or carry into the country cloven hoofed animals and related products from Botswana. Once found, all such goods shall be subject to return or destruction.

III. Animal and plant waste, swill and the like unloaded from inbound means of transport from Botswana shall, without exception, undergo disinfection or pest-control treatment under customs supervision and shall not be discarded without authorization.

IV. Cloven hoofed animals and related products from Botswana that have entered the country illegally and are seized by border inspection and other departments shall, without exception, be destroyed under customs supervision.

V. Anyone who violates the above provisions shall be dealt with by customs in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations.

VI. Customs offices at all levels and agricultural and rural affairs departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Animal Epidemic Prevention and other relevant provisions, cooperate closely and carry out quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision work well.

This Announcement shall take effect on the date of its publication. The Announcement No. 27 of 2020 of the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs (Announcement on Lifting the Ban on Foot and Mouth Disease in Parts of Botswana), and the Announcement No. 90 of 2022 of the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs (Announcement on Revoking the Status of the Foot and Mouth Disease-Free Zone without Vaccination in Parts of Botswana) are repealed at the same time.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of China and Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

14 May 2026
