Imports stop today. After the World Organisation for Animal Health reported Newcastle disease and foot-and-mouth disease in The Gambia, the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs jointly banned poultry, cloven-hoofed animals and related products from there, covering direct imports, indirect transshipment, postal items and carried baggage.

Take frozen poultry meat reaching a port via a third country. Its origin is checked, and once the ban applies no shipment is arranged. Arrived goods are returned or destroyed under customs supervision, unloaded waste and swill from the transport are all treated, and illegal goods seized by border control are destroyed too.

The six measures apply from publication, and violators are dealt with under the Customs Law, Biosecurity Law, Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and its implementing rules. Customs and agriculture and rural affairs bodies at all levels separately conduct quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision. The example batch and disposal follow customs findings and outbreak reports.

<!-- original -->

Recently, the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) released information that outbreaks of Newcastle disease and foot-and-mouth disease have occurred in The Gambia. To prevent the introduction of the diseases and to protect the safety of the livestock industry and the biosecurity of China, and in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, the following is hereby announced:

I. It is prohibited to import, directly or indirectly, from The Gambia poultry, cloven-hoofed animals and related products (products derived from poultry or cloven-hoofed animals that are unprocessed, or that have been processed but may still transmit disease).

II. It is prohibited to send by post or to carry into the country poultry, cloven-hoofed animals and related products from The Gambia. Once discovered, they shall all be returned or destroyed.

III. Animal and plant waste, swill and the like unloaded from incoming means of transport from The Gambia shall all be given treatment under customs supervision and shall not be discarded without authorisation.

IV. Poultry, cloven-hoofed animals and related products from The Gambia that have entered the country illegally and are intercepted by border control authorities and other departments shall all be destroyed under customs supervision.

V. Anyone who violates the above provisions shall be dealt with by customs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations.

VI. Customs authorities and agriculture and rural affairs departments at all levels shall each, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Animal Epidemics and other relevant laws, work in close coordination and properly carry out quarantine, epidemic prevention and supervision.

This Announcement shall take effect from the date of its publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

28 October 2026
