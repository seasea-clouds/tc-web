Yes, effective immediately: GACC Announcement No. 250 opens China to Russian medicinal dried deer antlers, limited to TCM raw material dried and heat-treated. Producers must be recommended by Russia and registered by China; check the official online list before ordering.

Per batch: donor deer from officially registered Russian farms in FMD-free zones, clinical checks by official Russian veterinarians before collection; processing: at least 10 minutes in 100 °C boiling water, then heat drying for at least 30 minutes, interior not below 80 °C; labels in Chinese, Russian and English, with a Russian veterinary certificate.

At the port, customs check registered producers, the Quarantine Permit for Inbound Animals and Plants and sanitary certificates; failures are handled under laws and the Protocol, and producers with serious or repeated failures may face intensified inspection and quarantine or an import ban; the Measures for the Administration of Imported Medicinal Materials also apply.

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Rosselkhoznadzor (Federal Service for Veterinary and Phytosanitary Surveillance, hereinafter referred to as the Russian side) on the quarantine and hygiene requirements for dried deer antlers for medicinal use exported from Russia to China, the import of Russian dried deer antlers for medicinal use that meets the following requirements is permitted as of the date of promulgation.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(a) The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(b) The Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its Implementation Regulations;

(c) The Measures for the Quarantine Supervision and Administration of Inbound and Outbound TCM Materials;

(d) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Rosselkhoznadzor (Federal Service for Veterinary and Phytosanitary Surveillance) on the Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for Dried Deer Antlers for Medicinal Use Exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the “Protocol”).

II. Scope of Imported Products

For the purposes of this Announcement, dried deer antlers for medicinal use are antlers that are dried and heat-treated after fresh collection and used as raw materials for traditional Chinese medicine (TCM).

III. Requirements for Production Establishments

(a) The deer antler establishments (including collection, processing, packaging and storage establishments) have established a sound product traceability system to ensure that the products can be traced back to the farms or herds of origin of the antlers;

(b) The Russian side supervises the production and processing of the deer antlers to ensure that the deer antler establishments meet the requirements of the Protocol;

(c) The deer antler establishments shall be recommended by the Russian side to the Chinese side and, after passing the review of the Chinese side, be registered; establishments that have completed registration may export deer antlers to China, and establishments not registered with the Chinese side may not export deer antlers to China.

IV. Requirements for Imported Products

(a) Requirements for donor deer providing antlers for export to China.

(1) Born and raised on farms officially registered in Russia;

(2) Marked in accordance with Russian laws and regulations so as to be traceable to the farm of origin;

(3) Inspected under the annual monitoring plan and the veterinary epidemic prevention measures;

(4) From foot-and-mouth disease (FMD) free zones recognized by the Chinese side;

(5) The farm has had no cases of foot-and-mouth disease, chronic wasting disease or anthrax within the 12 months before antler collection, and has not been subject to quarantine or movement restrictions due to animal epidemic diseases;

(6) Not fed with feed containing ruminant-derived ingredients (milk and dairy products excluded);

(7) The breed complies with the relevant provisions of the Pharmacopoeia of the People's Republic of China (Chinese Pharmacopoeia).

(b) Requirements for collection of antlers for export to China.

(1) The antlers are collected at establishments approved by the Russian side, and the antler collection environment and storage conditions meet the relevant hygiene requirements of both countries;

(2) Before collection, official Russian veterinarians perform clinical examinations to confirm that the deer providing the antlers are healthy;

(3) The antler collection process complies with the requirements of Russian veterinary laws and regulations.

V. Requirements for Production and Processing

(a) The antlers are processed at deer antler establishments registered with the Chinese side, and the processing complies with the laws and regulations of both sides;

(b) After collection, the antlers are first treated in boiling water at 100 °C for at least 10 minutes and then heat-dried for at least 30 minutes, ensuring that the temperature inside the antlers is not lower than 80 °C;

(c) The dried antlers are stored in dedicated warehouses to prevent contamination.

VI. Requirements for Packaging, Storage and Transport

(a) The antlers shall be packed in brand-new (unused), non-toxic and safe materials;

(b) A cargo label shall be applied to the package, indicating in Chinese, Russian and English the product name, weight, storage conditions, date of production, and the name, address and registration number of the producing establishment and other relevant information;

(c) The packaging, storage and transport shall meet the relevant hygiene requirements of both countries; the antlers for export to China shall be stored separately from products not for export to China after packing.

VII. Requirements for Pre-export Inspection and Certificates

The Russian side ensures that the antlers for export to China meet the relevant provisions of the Protocol and issues veterinary (sanitary) certificates.

VIII. Requirements for Inspection and Quarantine upon Entry

(a) Verification of documents and certificates.

(1) Whether the antlers come from a registered establishment;

(2) Whether the Quarantine Permit for Inbound Animals and Plants has been obtained;

(3) Whether the sanitary certificate is authentic and valid.

(b) Cargo inspection.

China Customs conducts inspection and quarantine over the antlers for export to China in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and in combination with the requirements of this Announcement. Those that pass inspection and quarantine are allowed to enter.

(c) Handling of non-conformities.

Where non-conformities are found in entry inspection and quarantine, China Customs shall dispose of them in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, the Protocol and other provisions. For deer antler establishments where serious problems occur or non-conformities occur repeatedly, the Chinese side may take measures to strengthen inspection and quarantine or to prohibit imports.

IX. Other Requirements

The antlers for export to China shall also comply with the relevant provisions and requirements of laws and regulations such as the Measures for the Administration of Imported Medicinal Materials.

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs
