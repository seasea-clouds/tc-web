Effective immediately, GACC Announcement No. 142 of 2026 admits dried laurel leaves from Türkiye, the dried leaves of the laurel (family Lauraceae) grown there. Plantations and processing and packing plants must be reviewed by the Turkish side and approved and registered by the Chinese side; the list is on the GACC website.

Each consignment: boxes are labelled for export to China, wood packaging per ISPM 15; for 2 years after the Protocol takes effect sampling is 2%, cut to 1% if no phytosanitary problems occur in those two years; the Turkish side issues a certificate under ISPM 12 with an added declaration in English.

At the port, documents and marks are checked; lots from unregistered plantations are not permitted entry; live quarantine pests, plant residues or soil are returned, destroyed or treated. Six quarantine pests are on the concern list; batch and sampling figures are illustrative, the registration list and accompanying certificate controlling.

In accordance with China's relevant laws and regulations and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry of the Republic of Türkiye (hereinafter referred to as the Turkish side) on the phytosanitary requirements for the export of laurel leaves from Türkiye to China, laurel leaves from Türkiye that comply with the following requirements are permitted for import into China with immediate effect.

I. Quarantine Basis

(i) Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its Implementing Regulations; (iii) Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its Implementing Regulations; (iv) Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry of the Republic of Türkiye on Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Laurel Leaves from Türkiye to China.

II. Name of Commodity Permitted for Entry

For the purposes of this Announcement, laurel leaves means the dried leaves of the laurel (Laurus nobilis), a plant of the family Lauraceae, grown in Türkiye.

III. Enterprise Registration

Laurel leaf plantations for export to China (hereinafter referred to as plantations) and processing and packing plants shall be reviewed by the Turkish side and approved and registered by the Chinese side. Registration information includes the name, address and registration number, so as to allow accurate traceability when exported goods do not comply with the relevant provisions of this Announcement. Before trade begins, the Turkish side shall provide the Chinese side with the registration list which, after review and approval by the Chinese side, shall be published on the GACC website and updated regularly.

IV. Quarantine Pests of Concern to the Chinese Side

(1) Laurel scale Aonidia lauri

(2) Pine-needle golden scale Chrysomphalus pinnulifer

(3) Grape leaf-roller (proposed name) Ditula angustiorana

(4) Laurel psyllid Lauritrioza alacris

(5) Khapra beetle Trogoderma granarium

(6) Sudden oak death pathogen Phytophthora ramorum

V. Pre-Export Management

(i) Plantation management.

(1) Plantations shall, under the supervision of the Turkish side, establish sound quality management and traceability management systems, apply Good Agricultural Practices (GAP), maintain plantation hygiene conditions, such as no sources of pollution affecting laurel leaf production in the vicinity and timely removal of diseased plant residues, and implement Integrated Pest Management (IPM), including pest monitoring and surveys, physical, chemical or biological control, and other control measures such as cultural practices.

(2) Plantations shall, under the supervision of the Turkish side, carry out pest monitoring and integrated management targeting the quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side. Pest monitoring and control shall be implemented under the guidance of professional technical personnel. Such personnel shall receive training from the Turkish side or its authorized institutions.

(3) The Turkish side shall retain records of pest monitoring and control during the growing period, as well as monitoring plans and records for toxic and harmful substances such as pesticide residues and heavy metal contaminants, and shall provide them to the Chinese side upon request. Control records shall include information such as the names of chemical agents used during the growing season, active ingredients, application doses and timing.

(ii) Management of processing and packing plants.

(1) The processing, packaging, storage and shipment of laurel leaves exported to China shall be carried out under the quarantine supervision of the Turkish side or its authorized officials.

(2) Processing and packing plants for laurel leaves exported to China shall be clean and hygienic, with hardened floors and with a raw material area and a finished product warehouse.

(3) Functional areas for the holding, processing, packaging and storage of laurel leaves exported to China shall be relatively independent and rationally laid out, and shall be separated from living areas by isolation measures with an appropriate distance.

(4) During sorting, screening, cleaning and other processes, laurel leaves exported to China shall be ensured free from insects, mites, snails, diseased leaves, rotten leaves, weed seeds, stems and leaves and other plant residues, and soil.

(5) Registered packing plants shall establish a traceability system to ensure that laurel leaves exported to China can be traced back to registered plantations, recording information such as the processing and packing date, the name or registration number of the source plantation, quantity, export date, export quantity, importing country, and the number of the means of transport or container.

(iii) Packaging requirements.

(1) Packaging materials shall be clean, hygienic and not previously used, and shall comply with the relevant phytosanitary requirements of China. Where wood packaging is used, it shall comply with International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15).

(2) Each packing box shall be labelled in Chinese or English with traceability information such as the product name, country, place of origin, and the name or registration number of the plantation and the processing and packing plant. Each packing box and pallet shall be marked in Chinese or English with the words "for export to the People's Republic of China" or "Exported to the People’s Republic of China".

(3) The means of transport or container used for laurel leaves exported to China shall be inspected at the time of loading to ensure good hygienic conditions. The means of transport or container shall be sealed, and the seal shall be intact and undamaged on arrival at the Chinese port of entry.

(iv) Pre-export inspection and quarantine.

(1) Prior to export, the Turkish side shall conduct inspection and quarantine of laurel leaves exported to China. Within 2 years after the Protocol takes effect, officials of the Turkish side or institutions authorized by the Turkish side shall sample and inspect the packaged laurel leaves at a rate of 2%. If no phytosanitary problems occur within those two years, the sampling rate shall be reduced to 1%.

(2) If quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side, plant residues or soil are found, the entire consignment shall not be exported to China. The Turkish side shall investigate the cause and take improvement measures. At the same time, detection records shall be kept and provided to the Chinese side upon request.

(v) Phytosanitary certificate requirements.

(1) For laurel leaves that pass quarantine, the Turkish side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate under International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 12 (ISPM 12), indicating the name or registration number of the plantation and the processing and packing plant, and shall include the following added declaration: "This consignment complies with the requirements specified in the Protocol of Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Laurel leaves from Türkiye to China, and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China." (The consignment complies with the phytosanitary requirements set out in the Protocol for the export of laurel leaves from Türkiye to China and is free from quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side.)

(2) Before trade begins, the Turkish side shall provide the Chinese side with a sample phytosanitary certificate for confirmation and record by the Chinese side.

VI. Entry Inspection, Quarantine and Handling of Non-compliance

When laurel leaves exported to China arrive at a Chinese port of entry, China Customs shall conduct inspection and quarantine in accordance with the following requirements.

(i) Verification of relevant certificates and marks.

(1) Verify whether the phytosanitary certificate complies with the provisions of Section V, item (v) of this Announcement.

(2) Verify whether the marks on the packing boxes or wood packaging comply with the provisions of Section V, item (iii) of this Announcement.

(ii) Entry inspection and quarantine.

When laurel leaves arrive at a Chinese port of entry, Customs shall verify the relevant documents and marks and conduct inspection and quarantine in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations, national standards and the requirements of this Announcement. Those that pass inspection and quarantine shall be permitted entry.

(iii) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) If a consignment is found to come from an unregistered plantation or packing plant, that consignment of laurel leaves shall not be permitted entry.

(2) If live quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side or other quarantine pests, or plant residues, soil and the like, are found, the consignment shall be returned, destroyed or treated.

(3) If a consignment is found not to comply with Chinese food safety laws, regulations and national standards, it shall be returned or destroyed.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China

28 September 2026.
