Canadian pet food is approved for China. Before importing, confirm it is industrially processed for dogs and cats and the producer is registered in China. Announcement No. 16 of 2026 requires dry, canned, snacks, chews to meet quarantine and sanitary requirements.

Three checks apply. Raw materials must not be from ruminants (except milk and dairy products), illegal hunting or disease deaths, and animal-origin materials must be from disease-free areas with no unapproved genetically modified ingredients. HACCP must be applied; commercially sterile food needs an F0 value of not less than 3.0; extruded dried and other foods need a core temperature above 90ºC to eliminate avian influenza and Newcastle disease risks.

Entry requires a sanitary certificate (one original, two copies) and an import quarantine permit, plus document and cargo checks. Shipments without a valid certificate, mismatched documents or bad labels are returned or destroyed. Repeat offenders lose export-to-China status.

Pursuant to the laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the Arrangement between the General Administration of Customs of the People‘s Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Canadian Food Inspection Agency (hereinafter the Canadian side) on the quarantine and sanitary requirements for Canadian pet food exported to China, Canadian pet food meeting the following requirements is permitted for import as of today.

Inspection and Quarantine Basis

(1) Biosecurity Law of the People‘s Republic of China.

(2) Law of the People‘s Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations.

(3) Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported and Exported Feed and Feed Additives.

(4) Arrangement between the General Administration of Customs of the People‘s Republic of China and the Canadian Food Inspection Agency on the Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Canadian Pet Food Exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the “Arrangement”).

Scope of Products Allowed for Import

Pet food in this Announcement means products that are industrially processed and manufactured for direct consumption by pets (dogs and cats), including dry food, canned food, snacks and chews.

Requirements for Source Animals of Products

The source animals of raw materials for pet food exported to China shall meet the following requirements:

(1) They must not be ruminants (except milk and dairy products) or aquatic mammals.

(2) They must not be animals from illegal hunting or illegal fishing.

(3) They must not be animals that died of animal disease.

(4) They must not be animals subject to movement restrictions or culled for the control or eradication of an animal epidemic.

(5) Terrestrial animals must be slaughtered in slaughterhouses approved by the relevant Canadian competent authorities and undergo pre-slaughter and post-slaughter inspection, showing no clinical signs of animal infectious diseases transmissible to animals or humans through the products.

Raw Material Requirements

(1) They must come from enterprises under the effective supervision of the relevant Canadian competent authorities, comply with Canadian laws, regulations and safety and hygiene requirements, and be suitable for pet food production.

(2) Animal-origin raw materials shall comply with the following:

(1) They must not contain ruminant-origin ingredients and must not be contaminated by them (except milk and dairy products);

(2) They must come from relevant disease-free zones that comply with the provisions of the World Organisation for Animal Health and are recognized by the Chinese side;

(3) Aquatic raw materials must come from aquatic animals (except aquatic mammals) and by-products generated in the production of products for human consumption, and the source animals must show no signs of infectious disease.

(3) Animal-origin raw materials from countries other than Canada must be legally imported, pass inspection and quarantine by the Canadian side, and be lawfully used in pet food production.

(4) Plant-origin raw materials must not contain genetically modified ingredients not approved by the Chinese authorities.

Requirements for Production and Processing Enterprises

(1) They must be approved by the relevant Canadian competent authorities and operate lawfully.

(2) They must implement a hygiene management system based on the principles of Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP).

(3) They must undergo supervision and inspection by the relevant Canadian competent authorities to ensure continuous compliance of their production and processing with Canadian safety and hygiene regulations and that they have not been restricted for safety or hygiene reasons.

(4) They must be recommended by the Canadian side and obtain registration with the Chinese side.

Production and Processing Requirements

The production and processing of pet food exported to China shall comply with the following requirements:

(1) Production shall be organized in accordance with the hygiene management system approved or recognized by the relevant Canadian competent authorities, with effective control of critical control points in production.

(2) The Canadian side shall conduct daily supervision of production and processing in accordance with relevant laws and regulations to ensure continuous compliance with Canadian safety and hygiene regulations.

(3) Commercially sterile pet food shall be processed by a heat treatment method with an F0 value of not less than 3.0.

(4) Extruded and dried pet food shall undergo extrusion and drying processing at a core temperature above 90ºC, or be processed by other equivalent means approved by the Canadian side and recognized by the Chinese side, to ensure the elimination of epidemic risks such as highly pathogenic avian influenza and Newcastle disease.

(5) Other processed pet food shall undergo heat treatment at a core temperature above 90ºC, or be treated by other equivalent means approved by the Canadian side and recognized by the Chinese side, to ensure the elimination of epidemic risks such as highly pathogenic avian influenza and Newcastle disease.

(6) Effective measures shall be taken during production in accordance with the hygiene management system to avoid contamination by unapproved raw material ingredients, foreign matter and the like.

Product Requirements

(1) It may be freely sold on the Canadian domestic market.

(2) It has complete traceability.

(3) It does not contain any prohibited substance prescribed by Chinese laws and regulations or Canadian food and feed safety legislation.

(4) It has not been contaminated by unapproved production raw materials or ingredients.

(5) It contains no toxic or harmful substances endangering animal health and complies with relevant Chinese and Canadian safety and hygiene standards.

(6) It complies with the Chinese hygienic standards for pet feed and the requirements of the Provisions on the Labeling of Pet Feed.

Packaging, Labeling, Storage and Transportation Requirements

(1) Packaging materials shall comply with relevant Chinese safety and hygiene standards.

(2) Chinese-language labels shall be affixed to the packaging, and the labels shall comply with the requirements of the Provisions on the Labeling of Pet Feed.

(3) The outer packaging shall indicate the name and registration number of the production and processing enterprise and bear the Chinese words “Exported to the People‘s Republic of China”, “Not for Human Consumption” or “For Pet Consumption Only”.

(4) Effective measures shall be taken during storage and transportation of pet food to avoid contamination.

Pre-export Inspection and Certificate Requirements

Pet food exported to China shall be inspected and quarantined by the Canadian side, which shall issue a sanitary certificate attesting that the products meet the requirements of the Arrangement. Each shipment of pet food shall be accompanied by a sanitary certificate, one original and two copies.

Entry Inspection and Quarantine Requirements

(1) Quarantine approval.

Import enterprises shall apply for the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit in accordance with the relevant provisions.

(2) Document verification.

(1) Verify whether the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit is attached as required.

(2) Verify whether the goods come from a registered enterprise.

(3) Verify whether the sanitary certificate is authentic and valid.

(3) Cargo inspection.

China Customs shall inspect and quarantine pet food exported from Canada to China in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules, in combination with the requirements of this Announcement. Products that pass inspection and quarantine shall be allowed to enter.

(4) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) Any of the following shall result in return or destruction: the absence of a valid sanitary certificate; goods from a non-registered Canadian production enterprise; a mismatch between the goods and the certificate; the discovery of soil, animal carcasses, animal excrement or quarantine pests for which effective quarantine treatment cannot be carried out; or labels that do not meet the standards and cannot be corrected.

(2) If testing shows that safety and hygiene items do not comply with the Chinese hygienic standards for pet feed, the goods shall be treated, returned or destroyed.

(3) If loose packaging or damaged containers are found, the consignee or its agent shall be responsible for making them intact; if packaging is damaged and poses a risk of spreading animal or plant disease, quarantine treatment shall be carried out on the contaminated premises, articles and appliances.

(4) The Chinese side may suspend the export-to-China qualification of a production enterprise with serious problems or repeated non-compliance.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs

2 February 2026
