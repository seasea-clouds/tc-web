Russian compound feed may now enter China. GACC Announcement No. 86 permits compound feed prepared by mixing feed materials and feed additives in certain proportions; producers must be approved by Russia, run an HACCP-based hygiene system and be registered with China.

One consignment end to end: animal-derived material from WOAH-recognised disease-free areas or Russian aquaculture, plant-derived material free of prohibited substances and viable seeds; raw material heated to at least 90℃ for 30 minutes and finished feed to at least 70℃ for 30 minutes; new sealed moisture-proof packing with Chinese, Russian and English labels and the warning "for feed use only"; Russia issues the original sanitary certificate before export; China checks the import permit and the registration list at entry.

Compliant goods are released after inspection; non-compliance is handled under the laws, regulations and the Protocol, with no preset return or destruction. A number quoted by phone does not count: the registration list and the original certificate govern. Read all four statistics n, c, m and M; "not detected" alone will mislead.

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Federal Service for Veterinary and Phytosanitary Surveillance of the Russian Federation (hereinafter referred to as the Russian side) on the quarantine and health requirements for Russian compound feed exported to China, imports of Russian compound feed meeting the following requirements are permitted from the date of this Announcement:

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) The Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(iii) The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported and Exported Feed and Feed Additives;

(iv) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Federal Service for Veterinary and Phytosanitary Surveillance of the Russian Federation on the Quarantine and Health Requirements for Russian Compound Feed Exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Scope of Products Permitted for Import

For the purposes of this Announcement, compound feed means feed prepared by mixing various feed materials and feed additives in certain proportions.

III. Requirements for Production and Processing Enterprises

(i) Approved by the relevant competent authority of Russia and lawfully operating;

(ii) Implementing a hygiene management system based on Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP);

(iii) Recommended by the Russian side and registered with the Chinese side.

IV. Requirements for Raw Materials

(i) Animal-derived raw materials.

(1) Terrestrial animal-derived raw materials shall come from areas free from the relevant animal diseases as specified by the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) and recognised by China;

(2) Aquatic animal-derived raw materials shall come from aquaculture farms in Russia or from legally caught aquatic animals;

(3) They shall not come from terrestrial animals culled to control an animal disease outbreak or from animals of unknown cause of death;

(4) Ruminant feed shall not contain animal-derived ingredients other than milk and dairy products.

(ii) Plant-derived raw materials.

(1) They shall not contain any substance harmful to animal health that is prohibited by the laws and regulations of China, the Russian Federation and the Eurasian Economic Union, including genetically modified components not approved by the Chinese competent authority, and the content of toxic substances shall not exceed the limits set by the laws and regulations of China, the Russian Federation and the Eurasian Economic Union;

(2) Viable seeds shall not be used as plant-derived raw materials.

(iii) Raw materials from a third country shall be legally imported and traceable to the country of origin.

V. Production and Processing Requirements

(i) Raw materials for feed production shall undergo heat treatment at a temperature of not less than 90℃ for not less than 30 minutes, or other equivalent treatment recognised by the Chinese side;

(ii) Finished feed shall undergo heat treatment at a temperature of not less than 70℃ for not less than 30 minutes, or other equivalent treatment recognised by the Chinese side.

VI. Product Requirements

Before export to China, processed compound feed shall be randomly sampled and tested by the Russian competent authority and shall meet the following requirements: Salmonella: not detected in 25 g of sample: n=5,c=0,m=0,M=0; Enterobacteriaceae: 1 g of sample: n=5,c=2,m=10,M=300; where: n - number of samples tested; m - the minimum bacterial count (threshold); if the bacterial count in all samples does not exceed m, the result is acceptable; M - the maximum bacterial count; if the bacterial count in one or more samples equals or exceeds M, the result is unacceptable; c - the number of samples with a bacterial count between m and M; if the bacterial count in the other samples is less than or equal to m, the result is still considered acceptable.

VII. Packaging, Labelling, Storage and Transport Requirements

(i) Packaging materials shall be new and clean, with good sealing and moisture-proof performance, and not easily damaged;

(ii) Packages shall bear labels in Chinese, Russian and English, and the labels shall meet the requirements of the Chinese national standard Feed Labelling (GB 10648);

(iii) The outer package shall indicate the production and processing enterprise and its registration information, and bear warnings such as "for feed use only";

(iv) Effective measures shall be taken during production, storage and transport to avoid contamination.

VIII. Pre-export Inspection and Certificate Requirements

(i) Products exported to China shall be inspected and quarantined by the Russian side, and a sanitary certificate shall be issued according to the certificate model confirmed by both sides, certifying that the products meet the requirements of the Protocol;

(ii) Each consignment exported to China shall be accompanied by at least one original official sanitary certificate.

IX. Inspection and Quarantine at Entry

(i) Quarantine approval. Import enterprises shall apply for the Import Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit in accordance with relevant provisions.

(ii) Document verification. (1) Verify whether the Import Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit is attached; (2) verify whether the goods come from a registered enterprise; (3) verify whether the sanitary certificate is authentic and valid.

(iii) Goods inspection. Chinese customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine of Russian compound feed exported to China in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and in light of the requirements of this Announcement. Goods that pass inspection and quarantine shall be permitted to enter.

(iv) Handling of non-compliance. Where non-compliance is found during inspection and quarantine at entry, Chinese customs shall handle the matter in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, the Protocol and other provisions.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs
