A regulator inquiry gives no notice. It can be a phone call, an email or an on-site visit, and it circles back to five scenarios: team-based pay being recognized, data export being questioned, a claim that breaks the rules, a port reading that differs, and promoters stockpiling and reselling. The scenarios differ; the answer follows one line: classify, show, pull.

That line is not improvised wording. It rests on three document sets kept ready and on a log with one row per session, which turns answering consistently into a daily routine. Below, the five scenarios are each pinned to one answer, then the three principles and the log are laid out.

## Five scenarios, five triggers

Team-based pay is found when the hierarchy runs past two levels or head-count-based pay appears. The unified answer: promoter levels are hard-capped at two, commission is calculated only on attributed transaction value, never per head; cap rules and attribution detail are filed order by order and retrievable. This is no multi-level under the Four Prohibitions, and the pay-basis limit under the Two Limits.

Data export is questioned when the compliance path is skipped. The unified answer: outbound data follows that path, separate consent and in-country storage in place; consent copies, export log and assessment records are retrievable, and the path basis maps to specific data. The Personal Information Protection Law requires notice, consent and in-country storage; the path is not explained later.

A claim breaks the rules when it asserts an effect. The unified answer: creative passes content review and legal review together, effect claims do not appear; review records and creative versions are archived and traceable to the exact page and time of publication.

A port reading gap appears at the execution stage. The unified answer: the port execution reading is set and archived by our team, a backup port plan is in place; when a gap appears, the archived reading governs, with the basis and filing record on file.

Promoters stockpiling and reselling is an individual violation. The unified answer: the agreement expressly bans stockpiling and resale, backed by periodic spot checks and breach handling; check records and outcomes are archived and mapped clause by clause to the agreement.

## Three reply principles, in an order that cannot change

First, classify: are these goods supervised as personal-use inbound articles, or is this money a promotion service fee? The classification decides which document number to show and which log to pull.

Second, show: the document numbers and the agreement. The Shang Cai Fa [2018] No. 486 notice, State Council Decree No. 444, the Regulations on the Prohibition of Pyramid Schemes, the Gong Tong Zi [2013] No. 37 opinion, plus the agreement clauses signed with promoters.

Third, pull: the log and the audit trail. Attribution detail, cap records, consent copies and spot-check records, pulled from the book that matches the classification.

One contact, one line, one consistent answer. Reverse the order, explain first and supply documents later, and the other side will hear more and more versions.

## Before the inquiry arrives, three document sets stand ready

The three sets map to the three principles: the classification basis, covering the application note for supervision mode 1210 and the goods list; the document numbers and agreement, covering the three legal texts and the clause index; the log and audit trail, covering order-by-order attribution, caps, consent and spot checks.

The response mechanism is written into delivery governance, the project charter and the contract annex: who receives an inquiry, how fast the first reply comes, when it goes to the compliance contact, all with names and actions. When an inquiry arrives, answer straight from the agreed line, without improvising.

## Walking one compliance inquiry through

Take the third row in the log. HS260924-008, channel store online chat, type marked compliance referral, handler the compliance contact, first response 2 minutes, status in progress. The customer asked: will this commission structure be recognized as team-based pay?

Answer by the three principles. Classify: goods are supervised as personal-use inbound articles; the promoter receives a promotion service fee, not a goods margin. Show: the Shang Cai Fa [2018] No. 486 reading, the Article 5 criminal safe harbor for team-based pay in Gong Tong Zi [2013] No. 37, and the two-level cap and attributed transaction value clauses. Pull: this order's attribution and cap record.

After the reply goes out, the session is marked done and the log keeps three things: who answered, how long the first response took, whether it closed. HS260923-002 on the same page is an email qualification query with a first response of 22 minutes, because qualification files must be pulled across departments; first-response time counts the first substantive answer, not "received".

The demo data only shows the log structure; session numbers and durations are not real service commitments. Before going live, check every field against your own handling roles and system fields.

## What this mechanism does not cover

It does not cover case-by-case discretion in administrative penalties, nor customs, tax or market supervision findings on a specific transaction; it does not replace formal legal advice either.

The log is an internal trail, not a hookup to a regulator system; it proves you answered consistently and promptly, not that the content is automatically correct. The three document sets are the floor; when a new scenario appears, fill it in the same order: classify, show, pull.

When an inquiry comes, five people giving five versions costs more than one wrong answer. Write the line down, fix the order, log every session, and the same answer is there whenever it is needed.
