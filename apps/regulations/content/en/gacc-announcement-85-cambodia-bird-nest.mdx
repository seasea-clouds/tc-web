Cambodian raw and edible bird nests may enter China from today under GACC Announcement No. 85 of 2025 and the China Cambodia protocol; Cambodia is China's 5th bird nest country, raw nests are the material for edible nests, which are cleaned of dirt and feathers, fit to eat.

One batch, whole route: swiftlet houses registered with Cambodia's authority and filed with China, processing enterprises registered in China, exporting once the list is out, heating to a core temperature of at least 70°C for at least 3.6 seconds to kill avian influenza and Newcastle disease viruses, an original Chinese English veterinary health certificate per lot.

At the port, papers and labels: inner and outer packaging sealed, marked in Chinese and English with registration number and production date; raw nest outer packaging states destination the People's Republic of China, deep processing on premises only, no retail; in force from release; the old one is not repealed.

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China, and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries of the Kingdom of Cambodia (hereinafter referred to as the Cambodian side) on the inspection, quarantine and hygiene requirements for the export of raw bird nests and edible bird nests from Cambodia to China, raw bird nests and edible bird nests from Cambodia that meet the relevant requirements are permitted for import with immediate effect.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) Laws and regulations.

The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations; the Special Provisions of the State Council on Strengthening the Supervision and Administration of the Safety of Food and Other Products; as well as the Measures for the Safety Administration of Import and Export Food of the People's Republic of China and the Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Manufacturers of Imported Food of the People's Republic of China, and others.

(ii) Bilateral protocol.

The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries of the Kingdom of Cambodia on the Inspection, Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for the Export of Raw Bird Nests and Edible Bird Nests from Cambodia to China.

II. Scope of Products Permitted for Import

The “raw bird nests” permitted for import refer to products formed from the saliva of swiftlets and similar swift species that have undergone primary processing effectively removing faeces, soil and ordinary impurities, free from mould, with no substances added; they are the raw material for producing edible bird nests.

The “edible bird nests” permitted for import refer to bird nests formed from the saliva of swiftlets and similar swift species, effectively cleaned of dirt and feathers, and fit for human consumption.

III. Requirements for Manufacturers

The production and processing of raw bird nests and edible bird nests exported to China shall comply with the relevant provisions of China and Cambodia on inspection and quarantine and on food safety and hygiene. Raw bird nests and edible bird nests exported to China shall undergo effective heat treatment that inactivates avian influenza and Newcastle disease viruses, namely “a core temperature of at least 70°C for at least 3.6 seconds”.

The swiftlet houses (caves) from which raw bird nests and edible bird nests exported to China originate shall be registered with the Cambodian competent authority and filed with the Chinese side. Primary processing enterprises for raw bird nests and processing enterprises for edible bird nests exported to China shall register with the Chinese side under the Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Manufacturers of Imported Food of the People's Republic of China; only products manufactured after the date of registration may be exported to China, and manufacturers without registration shall not export to the Chinese side. Primary processing enterprises for raw bird nests shall have a certain scale of production and establish effective epidemic prevention and safety and hygiene systems; their layout shall be scientifically designed and rational and free from cross-contamination; and they shall be capable of the primary processing of raw bird nests to remove faeces, soil and ordinary impurities, of disinfecting outer packaging and of other such capacities. Primary processing enterprises for raw bird nests and processing enterprises for edible bird nests shall be capable of applying effective hygiene treatment to raw bird nests and edible bird nests.

IV. Inspection and Quarantine Requirements

(i) The Cambodian side shall establish swiftlet house (cave) epidemic prevention and hygiene management systems; hygiene control operating procedures for nest harvesting and transport; and annual animal disease surveillance and toxic and harmful substance monitoring plans related to the quarantine, hygiene and food safety of raw bird nests and edible bird nests for export, where the annual disease surveillance plan shall include surveillance for avian influenza and Newcastle disease in swiftlets of a reasonable number of swiftlet houses (caves) filed with the Chinese side.

(ii) The Cambodian side shall ensure that primary processing enterprises for raw bird nests and processing enterprises for edible bird nests exported to China establish a nest traceability system from the swiftlet house (cave) to export, so that affected products can be traced and recalled when problems occur, back to the registered swiftlet houses (caves).

(iii) Raw bird nests and edible bird nests exported to China are produced and processed under an effectively operating quality system and have undergone effective heat processing, are free from contamination by any pathogen that may harm poultry or human health, and comply with the requirements of the laws and regulations of the Cambodian side and the Chinese side. Effective hygiene treatment shall be applied. Outer packaging and transport containers shall be disinfected.

V. Product Certificate Requirements

Each lot of raw bird nests and edible bird nests exported to China shall be accompanied by one original veterinary health certificate. The veterinary health certificate shall be written in Chinese and English, and its format and content must be approved in advance by both sides.

The veterinary health certificate issued by the Cambodian side shall state:

(i) the swiftlet house (cave) registration number, the registration number of the primary processing enterprise (processing enterprise), and the source of the raw material;

(ii) that highly pathogenic avian influenza and Newcastle disease surveillance has been carried out in the region where the swiftlet house (cave) is located under the relevant rules of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH), with no abnormalities found;

(iii) that necessary precautions have been taken to prevent contact between the product and all sources of avian influenza and Newcastle disease viruses;

(iv) that the product has undergone effective heat processing, with a processing requirement not lower than “a core temperature of at least 70°C for at least 3.6 seconds”;

(v) that the product complies with Chinese laws, regulations and relevant standards and requirements;

(vi) that it is fit for use in processed food or for human consumption.

VI. Product Packaging and Labelling Requirements

Raw bird nests and edible bird nests exported to China must be packed in new materials complying with international hygiene standards, and the inner and outer packaging shall be sealed.

The outer packaging of raw bird nests exported to China shall clearly state in Chinese and English the product name, weight, swiftlet house (cave) registration number, the name, address and registration number of the primary processing enterprise, the storage conditions, production date and shelf life, and indicate the destination as the People's Republic of China; it shall bear the mark “deep processing on premises only, no retail” and other relevant information.

The inner and outer packaging of edible bird nests exported to China shall state in Chinese and English the product name, weight, swiftlet house (cave) registration number, the name, address and registration number of the processing enterprise, the storage conditions and production date and other relevant information, and the labelling of product information shall comply with Chinese laws, regulations and relevant standards and requirements.

This Announcement takes effect on the date of issuance.

It is hereby announced.
