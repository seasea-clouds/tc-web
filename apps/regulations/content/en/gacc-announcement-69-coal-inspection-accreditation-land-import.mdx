Announcement No. 69 of 2026 applies accreditation-based inspection to coal imported by rail and road. Enterprises may commission accredited bodies and Customs accepts their results.

The importing consignee or its agent may commission an accredited body to inspect coal imported by land; Customs accepts the inspection results in accordance with the Measures.

A report is valid only for its inspection batch, no more than 5,000 tonnes of the same coal from the same mine, valid for six months from issue; accredited bodies must be registered overseas and internationally accredited.

<!-- original -->

Under the Import and Export Commodity Inspection Law of the People's Republic of China and the Measures of the General Administration of Customs for the Accreditation of Import and Export Commodity Inspection (hereinafter the Measures), GACC has decided, in light of pilot experience and quality and safety risk assessment, to apply accreditation-based inspection to coal imported by rail and road (hereinafter land transport). The relevant matters are hereby announced as follows.

I. Scope of goods subject to accreditation

Where an importing consignee or its agent imports by land the coal listed in the List of Commodity Codes of Coal for Import Subject to Accreditation (see Annex 1), it may commission an accredited body to carry out the inspection, and Customs shall accept the inspection results of the accredited body in accordance with the Measures.

II. Inspection items, applicable technical specifications and inspection methods

The accredited body shall inspect coal imported by land in accordance with the Inspection Items, Applicable Technical Specifications and Inspection Methods for Accreditation of Coal Inspection (see Annex 2).

III. Content of the inspection report

The accredited body commissioned by the importing consignee or its agent shall inspect the coal imported by land and issue an inspection report. The report shall comply with Article 13 of the Measures, be prepared and issued in accordance with the template (see Annex 3), and be accompanied by sampling and supervision-of-loading photographs.

IV. Validity of the inspection report

The accredited body shall take and prepare samples by inspection lot for coal of the same mine and the same variety, with no more than 5,000 tonnes per inspection lot. The inspection report issued by the accredited body is valid only for that lot of coal, for six months from the date of issue.

V. Requirements for accredited bodies

(1) Application conditions.

An inspection body applying for inclusion in the catalogue of accredited bodies for coal import inspection (hereinafter the applicant), in addition to satisfying the provisions of Articles 7(1), (2), (4), (5) and (6) of the Measures, shall also meet the following requirements: it shall be registered and actually operating in the country of origin of the imported coal, and have obtained ISO/IEC 17025 and ISO/IEC 17020 accreditation from an accreditation body signatory to the International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation Mutual Recognition Arrangement (ILAC-MRA), with its scope of inspection capability covering the inspection items, applicable technical specifications and inspection methods listed in Annex 2 to this Announcement.

(2) Application method.

The applicant shall submit application materials to Customs in accordance with Article 8 of the Measures. For the application procedure, see the Service Guide for the Matter of Catalogue Management of Accredited Bodies for Import and Export Commodity Inspection (see Annex 4).

VI. Declaration requirements

Where an importing consignee or its agent wishes to apply for acceptance of inspection results for the imported goods, at declaration it shall select the “inspection results to be accredited” type in the “goods attribute” field, enter the “accredited body code/inspection report number” in the “product qualification” field of the corresponding goods item, and upload the inspection report and the Quality and Safety Compliance Statement (see Annex 5) in the “accompanying documents” field; Customs shall apply accreditation in accordance with the Measures.

This Announcement shall come into force on the date of publication.
