On 18 March 2026, the General Administration of Customs of China issued Announcement No. 27 to implement Decree 280, the new registration rules for overseas food manufacturers effective on 1 June 2026: which foods need official recommendation from the competent authority of the producing country, how to fill in the registration number on the customs declaration form and which system to use are all set out here.

Three lists to remember: 17 food categories, including meat products, dairy products, aquatic products and health food, must be registered upon official recommendation; meat and meat products, and bird's nest, two categories, are not automatically renewed; and overseas cold warehouses storing food of terrestrial animal origin and aquatic products must also register.

When declaring, fill in the registration number in China for the country of origin in the 519 certificate column under "Product Qualification" on the customs declaration form, and "edible" in the Use column; customs do not accept wrongly filled declarations. Applications and queries go through the registration system cifER (cifer.singlewindow.cn), free of charge.

The Administrative Provisions on the Registration of Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food of the People's Republic of China (GACC Decree No. 280, hereinafter referred to as the Provisions) were published on 14 October 2025 and will take effect from 1 June 2026. The relevant implementing matters are hereby announced as follows:

**I. Catalogue and List Supporting the Provisions**

Based on the analysis of factors such as the raw material sources, production and processing techniques, historical food safety data, consumer groups and consumption methods of the food, in light of international practice, GACC determines the catalogue of imported food subject to registration upon official recommendation, the list of imported food not subject to automatic renewal of registration, and the scope of overseas storage operators of imported food subject to registration administration under the Provisions. The relevant catalogue, list and scope are subject to dynamic management.

**(1) Catalogue of Imported Food Subject to Registration upon Official Recommendation.**

In accordance with Article 6 of the Provisions, the catalogue of imported food subject to registration upon official recommendation includes: meat and meat products, casings, bird's nest and bird's nest products, bee products, eggs and egg products, edible fats and oils, filled flour products, edible grains, grain milling products and malt, dehydrated vegetables, seasoning powders, nuts and seeds, dried fruits, foods for special dietary uses, health food, dairy products, aquatic products.

**(2) List of Imported Food Not Subject to Automatic Renewal of Registration.**

In accordance with Article 21 of the Provisions, the list of imported food not subject to automatic renewal of registration includes: meat and meat products, bird's nest and bird's nest products.

**(3) Scope of Overseas Storage Operators of Imported Food Subject to Registration Administration under the Provisions.**

In accordance with Article 30, paragraph 1, of the Provisions, the scope of overseas storage operators of imported food subject to registration administration under the Provisions includes: cold warehouses used to store food of terrestrial animal origin and aquatic products.

**II. Declaration Requirements for Imported Food Relating to Registration**

**(1) Requirements for Filling in Information.**

For imported food imported as goods and placed on the market in China for human consumption or as raw material for food processing, at the time of the import declaration, the registration number in China of the overseas food manufacturer corresponding to the "country (region) of origin" of the imported food shall be properly filled in under the "Product Qualification" item of the customs declaration form, in the certificate column for "Registration of Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food" (licence category code "519"); in the "Use" column under the "Commodity Description" item of the customs declaration form, "edible" shall be properly filled in.

Where the information is not filled in properly as required, the declaration is not accepted by customs. Where false information is filled in to fraudulently obtain customs documents, customs will investigate and deal with the case in accordance with the law.

**(2) Declaration Requirements for Imported Food.**

For overseas food manufacturers subject to registration upon official recommendation, products produced within the validity period of the registration may be declared for import (including food produced within the validity period of the registration and within its shelf life by enterprises that did not renew their registration after its expiry); for overseas food manufacturers that apply for registration on their own, the enterprise registration shall be within the validity period at the time of the import declaration; for overseas food manufacturers whose registration has been suspended, cancelled or revoked, the declaration for import of food shipped before the date of suspension, cancellation or revocation is not affected.

Where GACC has special requirements, they are implemented accordingly.

**III. Channels for Registration Application and Information Query**

**(1) Registration Management System for Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food.**

To support the implementation of the Provisions, facilitate enterprises' registration applications and improve service efficiency, overseas food manufacturers of imported food may query and handle applications, changes, renewals, suspensions, resumptions and other services through the Registration Management System for Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food of GACC (hereinafter referred to as the registration system); the access address of the registration system is: https://cifer.singlewindow.cn. GACC reminds all relevant enterprises to identify the system carefully and avoid losses from logging into fake websites.

**(2) Information Query Channels.**

During the application for registration, an overseas food manufacturer of imported food can check in the registration system the progress of its registration application, as well as the review opinions and feedback of GACC.

Information such as the registration number in China and the validity period of a registered overseas food manufacturer of imported food can be queried through the official website of GACC or the "List of Registered Overseas Food Manufacturers of Imported Food" function of the registration system.

Materials such as the service guide for the registration of overseas food manufacturers of imported food, the user manual of the registration system and the consultation hotline for registration questions can be found through the "Service Guide" function of the registration system or in the service guide published on the website of GACC.

The product categories involved in the registration of overseas food manufacturers of imported food, together with the corresponding customs commodity numbers and regulatory identification codes, can be queried through the "Product Category Query" function of the registration system.

**IV. Other Explanations**

**(1)** The catalogue of primary edible agricultural products entering China that require the registration of overseas enterprises, the import declaration requirements and the query methods are implemented in accordance with GACC Announcement No. 219 of 2025.

**(2)** In implementing the registration of overseas enterprises for imported food and primary edible agricultural products, GACC does not charge any fees.

It is hereby announced.
