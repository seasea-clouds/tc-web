Announcement No. 133 of 2026 of the General Administration of Customs of China, effective from 8 September 2026: goods under tariff heading 28539090 that are dichlorosilane originating in Japan shall be declared under commodity code 28539090.61, pursuant to the provisional anti-dumping measure imposed as a deposit by Ministry of Commerce Announcement No. 37 of 2026.

Your consignment of dichlorosilane from Japan has arrived: classify it under tariff heading 28539090 and enter 28539090.61 in the commodity code field; customs then collects the provisional anti-dumping measure in the form of a deposit. Entering only the tariff heading, without the dedicated code, leaves the goods outside the measure.

The issuance date is 7 September 2026 and the implementation date is 8 September 2026; they differ. This announcement only sets the commodity code declaration requirements and does not change the measure or product scope determined by Ministry of Commerce Announcement No. 37 of 2026.

<!-- original -->

Pursuant to Ministry of Commerce Announcement No. 37 of 2026, provisional anti-dumping measures in the form of a deposit have been imposed, as of 8 September 2026, on imports of dichlorosilane originating in Japan. In accordance with the specific product scope of the provisional anti-dumping measures defined in the above announcement, the requirements for declaring the commodity code are hereby announced as follows:

Where the consignee of imported goods or its agent declares imports of goods under tariff heading 28539090, the commodity code for “dichlorosilane” shall be declared as 28539090.61.

This announcement shall be implemented from 8 September 2026.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China

7 September 2026
