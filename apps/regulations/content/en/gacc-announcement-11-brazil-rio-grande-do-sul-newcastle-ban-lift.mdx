The lifting took effect on publication, and the poultry ban on Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, ended with it. All six measures of Joint Announcement No. 100 of 2024 were repealed, so the bans on imports, on postal and hand-carried goods, on transport waste and on illegally entered cargo stopped applying.

Announcement No. 100 met the Newcastle disease outbreak that a farm in that state reported on 19 July 2024: no direct or indirect imports of poultry or related products, poultry meat shipped before publication released only after each batch passed laboratory testing, no entry by post or hand carriage, and plant and animal waste and swill unloaded from means of transport treated under customs supervision.

In practice you follow the inspection and quarantine rules in force for imported poultry products, not the state-level ban of Announcement No. 100: check the overseas producer's registration, the official quarantine certificate and traceability, and declare once documents and goods match; customs and agriculture authorities then link quarantine and disease control under their own duties.

<!-- original -->

Based on the results of the risk analysis, the ban imposed on Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, for Newcastle disease is lifted as of the date of publication of this announcement.

Joint Announcement No. 100 of 2024 of the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs is repealed at the same time.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs, Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs

16 January 2026
