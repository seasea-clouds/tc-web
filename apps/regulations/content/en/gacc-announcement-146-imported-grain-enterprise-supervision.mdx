Imported grain may only go to designated processing enterprises. Announcement No. 146, issued on 13 October, takes effect on 15 November 2026. An enterprise first self-assesses against the government service guide, files a declaration of conformity with the customs office in its locality, and may start work only after Customs approves it.

When handling the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit, first establish which designated enterprise will receive the grain: the "Receiving Customs" field of the quarantine approval form names the customs office where that enterprise is located, not the customs office at the port of entry. Obtaining the Preliminary Review Contact Sheet for Imported Grain in advance removes the need for the Article III supporting materials.

Loading and unloading, transshipment and reloading, processing and residue handling all require records and spill prevention. If a designated enterprise commits any of the last four of the seven listed violations, Customs will not approve further quarantine approval applications until rectification is complete; if it refuses, unprocessed grain is disposed of as Customs requires.

To improve the port business environment and prevent the introduction of plant pests and invasive alien species, and in accordance with the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Entry and Exit Grain and other relevant provisions, the following matters concerning the optimisation of supervision of enterprises handling imported grain are hereby announced:

I. Imported grain shall be processed and used at designated processing enterprises that meet the requirements for pest prevention and treatment (hereinafter "designated enterprises").

II. An enterprise intending to process imported grain shall conduct a self-assessment against the pest prevention and treatment conditions published by the General Administration of Customs in its government service guide. Once it confirms compliance, it shall submit a declaration of conformity and supporting materials through the Single Window for International Trade or the Customs Government Service Platform to the customs office in its locality, and may commence imported grain processing after the customs review is passed. The enterprise shall ensure that the materials submitted are authentic, complete and accurate. If information in the declaration of conformity changes, it shall submit an application for amendment to Customs. Customs shall exercise quarantine supervision over designated enterprises.

III. When applying for the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter "the Permit"), the cargo owner or its agent shall identify the designated enterprise to which the grain is to be delivered after entry and provide the corresponding supporting materials, and shall enter the customs office where the designated enterprise is located in the "Receiving Customs" field of the quarantine approval application form. Customs shall issue the Permit in accordance with the relevant provisions.

IV. Before applying for the Permit, the cargo owner or its agent may apply to the customs office where the designated enterprise is located for a Preliminary Review Contact Sheet for Imported Grain. Where the Preliminary Review Contact Sheet for Imported Grain is provided when applying for the Permit, the supporting materials referred to in Article III need not be submitted.

V. The operators of designated supervision sites for imported grain and of sites for transshipment and reloading during transport (hereinafter "site operators"), the cargo owner or its agent, designated enterprises and transport enterprises shall bear responsibility for preventing and controlling plant pest risks in the loading and unloading, transport, transshipment and reloading, processing and handling of processing residues, shall take measures to prevent spillage and to ensure sealing, and shall keep records of the transfer, storage, processing and handling of residues of imported grain as required by Customs. Imported grain shall be transshipped and reloaded at sites that meet the conditions for preventing and controlling plant pest risks.

VI. Where Customs finds that an enterprise has committed any of the following violations, it shall, in addition to pursuing the liability of that enterprise in accordance with the law, require rectification within a specified period:

(i) failing to implement plant pest monitoring and control measures as required; (ii) failing to record information on the unloading, transport, storage, processing, handling of processing residues and dispatch destinations of imported grain as required; (iii) imported grain not being processed or stored at the designated enterprise as required; (iv) failing to take measures to prevent spillage and to ensure sealing in the loading, unloading and transport of imported grain as required; (v) failing to apply effective pest control treatment or processing treatment to imported grain as required; (vi) failing to render processing residues and packaging materials harmless as required; (vii) refusing to accept Customs quarantine supervision.

If a designated enterprise is found to have committed any of the circumstances in (iv), (v), (vi) or (vii) above, Customs shall not approve subsequent quarantine approval applications for grain intended for that designated enterprise until the rectification is completed.

If a designated enterprise refuses to rectify, fails the rectification or cannot rectify, the grain that has not yet been processed shall be disposed of as required by Customs.

VII. The cargo owner or its agent shall inform the designated enterprise and the site operator of the declared use of the imported grain. The cargo owner or its agent and the designated enterprise shall use transport means that comply with the relevant national safety and hygiene provisions to load imported grain, and shall urge the transport enterprise to take effective measures to prevent grain spillage during transport and at intermediate transshipment and reloading.

VIII. For the purposes of this Announcement, grain means the seeds of cereal, legume and oilseed crops and the roots or tubers of tuber crops used for processing and not for propagation, as specified in the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Entry and Exit Grain.

This Announcement shall take effect on 15 November 2026.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

13 October 2026
