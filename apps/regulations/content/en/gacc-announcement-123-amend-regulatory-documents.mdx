Announcement 123 does not change market access; it rewires the citations. The solid waste disposal and zone-exit clauses in three earlier announcements now read that the goods are managed under domestic solid waste provisions. It took effect on publication, 17 August 2026, and touches bonded repair, bonded research and development in comprehensive bonded zones, and contract processing in zones.

Take a zone enterprise doing bonded repair on inbound equipment. Parts stripped during repair count as solid waste. The old text made the enterprise cite the Measures for the Administration of Solid Waste Imports and hand the waste to a qualified enterprise for disposal; the new text says only that domestic solid waste provisions apply. The enterprise must change its internal work instruction and the basis cited in the zone-exit declaration.

The three edits land in Article 10 of the 2018 No. 203 announcement, Article 7 of the 2019 No. 27 announcement and Article 10 of the 2019 No. 28 announcement. Only the solid waste sentence changes; the rest stands. The Ecological Environment Code drove the clean-up, not a green light for solid waste imports, and no transition period is set.

GACC Announcement No. 123 of 2026

To complete the clean-up of regulatory documents in connection with the Ecological Environment Code, the General Administration of Customs has decided to amend three documents, including GACC Announcement No. 203 of 2018. The amendments are as follows.

I. GACC Announcement No. 203 of 2018 (Announcement on Issues Concerning Supervision of Bonded Repair Business) is amended as follows.

In Article 10, the text “where the goods are solid waste, the enterprise shall, in accordance with the Measures for the Administration of Solid Waste Imports (Order No. 12 of the Ministry of Environmental Protection, the Ministry of Commerce, the National Development and Reform Commission, the General Administration of Customs and the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine) and the relevant requirements of the competent department of ecology and environment, hand them to a qualified enterprise for disposal” is replaced by “where the goods are solid waste, they shall be managed under the relevant domestic solid waste provisions”.

II. GACC Announcement No. 27 of 2019 (Announcement on Supporting Bonded Research and Development Business in Comprehensive Bonded Zones) is amended as follows.

In Article 7, the text “where the goods are solid waste, the enterprise in the zone shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Measures for the Administration of Solid Waste Imports (Joint Order No. 12 of the Ministry of Environmental Protection, the Ministry of Commerce, the National Development and Reform Commission, the General Administration of Customs and the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine), complete the formalities for leaving the zone” is replaced by “where the goods are solid waste, they shall be managed under the relevant domestic solid waste provisions”.

III. GACC Announcement No. 28 of 2019 (Announcement on Supporting Enterprises in Comprehensive Bonded Zones in Undertaking Processing Orders from Enterprises Within China but Outside the Zone) is amended as follows.

In Article 10, the text “where the scraps, defective products, waste products and by-products generated from bonded materials are solid waste, the formalities for leaving the zone shall be completed in accordance with the Measures for the Administration of Solid Waste Imports (Joint Order No. 12 of the Ministry of Environmental Protection, the Ministry of Commerce, the National Development and Reform Commission, the General Administration of Customs and the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine)” is replaced by “where the scraps, defective products, waste products and by-products generated from bonded materials are solid waste, they shall be managed under the relevant domestic solid waste provisions”.

This announcement takes effect on the date of publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

17 August 2026
