On January 6, 2026, the General Administration of Customs of China promulgated these Provisions as Order No. 281, effective on August 1, 2026, when the 2015 Order No. 160 of the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on health quarantine of special articles entering and leaving the country is repealed.

It applies to human tissues such as blood, pathogenic microorganisms and biological products, except those managed as medicines, veterinary drugs or medical devices. A directly subordinate customs handles the approval and makes a decision within 20 working days, extendable by 10 working days; an approval certificate is valid for up to 12 months and is subject to write-off management.

Goods must be declared against the approval certificate, and any inconsistency between documents and goods bars entry or exit. A person carrying such goods without a certificate must obtain one within 7 days, and release follows if inspection passes. Unauthorized sale or use, or failure to return or destroy as required, draws a fine of up to 100,000 yuan, and production, sale and use records must be kept for at least 3 years.

## Chapter 1 General Provisions

**Article 1**　These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the Frontier Health Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing rules, the Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Regulations on the Prevention and Treatment of AIDS, the Regulations on the Biosafety Management of Pathogenic Microorganism Laboratories and other laws and administrative regulations, in order to standardize the supervision and administration of health quarantine of inbound and outbound special goods and articles, prevent the cross-border transmission of infectious diseases, safeguard public safety and health, and prevent and defuse public health risks.

**Article 2**　These Provisions apply to the supervision and administration of health quarantine of inbound and outbound special goods and articles that bear on public health safety, such as human tissues such as blood, pathogenic microorganisms and biological products, excluding those managed as medicines, veterinary drugs or medical devices.

**Article 3**　The General Administration of Customs shall exercise unified administration over the supervision and administration of health quarantine of inbound and outbound special goods and articles nationwide; competent customs shall be responsible for such work within the areas under their jurisdiction.

**Article 4**　The supervision and administration of health quarantine of inbound and outbound special goods and articles follows the principle of risk management, and health quarantine approval, quarantine inspection and supervision are carried out on the basis of risk assessment according to risk levels.

The risk levels of inbound and outbound special goods and articles and the corresponding health quarantine supervision measures shall be dynamically adjusted and published by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 5**　The General Administration of Customs implements health quarantine access management for high-risk special goods and articles that enter the country for the first time or resume entry after suspension, and the specific requirements shall be separately prescribed by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 6**　Units of inbound and outbound special goods and articles, and the consignor and consignee, sender and recipient, the person carrying them, carrier, or their agent of such goods and articles, shall, in accordance with laws, regulations and relevant standards, import, export, produce, sell and use special goods and articles, be responsible to society and the public, ensure the safety of special goods and articles, accept social supervision and bear social responsibility.

## Chapter 2 Health Quarantine Approval

**Article 7**　Directly subordinate customs shall be responsible for health quarantine approval of inbound and outbound special goods and articles within their jurisdiction, hereinafter referred to as approval of special goods and articles.

**Article 8**　An application for special goods and articles approval shall meet the following conditions:

(1) where laws and regulations require approval documents from relevant departments, the corresponding approval documents shall be obtained;

(2) the applicant shall have biosafety control capacity appropriate to the inbound and outbound special goods and articles.

**Article 9**　A unit of inbound and outbound special goods and articles or a natural person, hereinafter collectively referred to as the applicant, shall apply to the directly subordinate customs at the place where the applicant is located for approval before the special goods and articles are shipped.

**Article 10**　An applicant for special goods and articles approval shall provide the following materials as required:

(1) an application form for special goods and articles approval;

(2) descriptive materials on the inbound and outbound special goods and articles, including names in Chinese and English, category, composition, source, production process, use, distribution, countries or regions of export and import, and manufacturer, proof materials on product safety, and a self-assessment of biosafety risks;

(3) where the inbound and outbound special goods and articles contain or may contain pathogenic microorganisms, an explanatory document on the scientific names in Chinese, English or Latin and the biological characteristics of the pathogenic microorganisms in Chinese and English, and documents proving the corresponding level of biosafety prevention and control;

(4) where a unit using inbound and outbound special goods and articles that contain or may contain pathogenic microorganisms conducts pathogenic microorganism experiments in level 1 or level 2 pathogenic microorganism laboratories, the laboratory filing materials filed with the health authority of the people's government at the districted city level; where such experiments are conducted in level 3 or level 4 pathogenic microorganism laboratories, the documents on highly pathogenic microorganism experimental activities approved by the health authority of the people's government at or above the provincial level;

(5) where highly pathogenic microorganism strains, bacterial or viral, or samples are imported or exported, the transport-related proof materials approved by the health authority of the people's government at or above the provincial level.

**Article 11**　Where the applicant is a unit, at the time of its first application for special goods and articles approval, in addition to the materials listed in Article 10 of these Provisions, it shall also provide the following materials as required by customs:

(1) basic information on the unit, such as management system certification, address, production site, laboratory setup, storage facilities and equipment, product processing, production process or technical process, and floor plans;

(2) proof materials on biosafety control capacity.

Where the applicant is a natural person, identity documents shall be provided.

For inbound and outbound pathogenic microorganisms or special goods and articles that may contain pathogenic microorganisms, the applicant shall not be a natural person.

**Article 12**　A directly subordinate customs shall handle an application for special goods and articles approval filed by an applicant according to the following circumstances:

(1) where the matter does not legally require special goods and articles approval, the applicant shall be informed immediately that the application is not accepted;

(2) where the matter falls outside the authority of that customs according to law, a decision not to accept shall be made immediately, and the applicant shall be informed to apply to another directly subordinate customs or a relevant administrative organ;

(3) where the application materials contain errors that can be corrected on the spot, the applicant shall be allowed to correct them on the spot;

(4) where the application materials are incomplete or do not conform to the statutory form, the applicant shall be informed at once, on the spot or within 5 working days, of all contents to be supplemented or corrected; if no such notice is given within that period, the application shall be deemed accepted from the date the materials are received;

(5) where the matter falls within the authority of that customs and the application materials are complete and conform to the statutory form, or the applicant has submitted all supplemented or corrected materials as required by that customs, the application for administrative license shall be accepted.

**Article 13**　A directly subordinate customs shall examine the application materials and may verify them by means such as expert review of documents and on-site assessment, as the circumstances require.

**Article 14**　Where the application meets the statutory conditions and standards, the directly subordinate customs shall issue the special goods and articles approval certificate within 20 working days from the date of acceptance.

Where the application does not meet the statutory conditions and standards, the directly subordinate customs shall, within 20 working days from the date of acceptance, make a written decision not to grant approval, state the reasons, and inform the applicant of the right to apply for administrative reconsideration or bring an administrative lawsuit according to law.

Where the directly subordinate customs cannot make a decision to grant or not to grant approval within 20 working days, it may, with the approval of the head of that customs, extend the period by 10 working days and shall inform the applicant of the reasons for the extension.

**Article 15**　The validity period of a special goods and articles approval certificate shall be determined according to the risk situation and shall not exceed 12 months.

Customs implements write-off management for special goods and articles approval certificates.

## Chapter 3 Quarantine Inspection

**Article 16**　The consignor and consignee, sender and recipient, the person carrying them, or their agent of inbound and outbound special goods and articles shall declare to customs against the special goods and articles approval certificate and other materials. Customs shall compare and verify the approval certificate and other documents. Where the declaration materials are incomplete or do not conform to the statutory form, customs shall not permit entry or exit.

For outbound special goods and articles, quarantine shall be applied for to the customs at the place of production or the place where the applicant is located.

**Article 17**　Customs shall conduct quarantine inspection of inbound and outbound special goods and articles according to the following requirements and fill in quarantine inspection records:

(1) check whether the name, composition, batch number, specifications, quantity, validity period, transport and storage conditions, exporting or importing country and producing unit of the inbound and outbound special goods and articles conform to the contents of the special goods and articles approval certificate;

(2) check whether the packaging of the inbound and outbound special goods and articles is safe and undamaged, does not seep or leak, and whether goods with biosafety risks bear biosafety marks that meet relevant requirements.

Where the inspection site at the port of entry lacks the safety protection conditions required for quarantine inspection of special goods and articles, the special goods and articles shall be transported to a designated place that meets the conditions for quarantine inspection.

For inbound special goods and articles containing highly pathogenic microorganisms, the directly subordinate customs at the destination shall promptly notify the health authority at the place where the unit using the special goods and articles is located.

**Article 18**　Where inbound or outbound special goods and articles are carried without an approval certificate, customs shall temporarily withhold release and issue the relevant document.

After the person carrying them obtains the special goods and articles approval certificate within 7 days, customs shall conduct quarantine inspection in accordance with Article 17 of these Provisions and grant release if the inspection passes.

**Article 19**　Customs shall release inbound and outbound special goods and articles that pass quarantine inspection. Under any of the following circumstances, outbound special goods and articles shall not be permitted to leave the country, and inbound special goods and articles shall be returned or destroyed according to law under customs supervision:

(1) the name, batch number, biologically active composition, specifications or producing unit does not conform to the contents of the special goods and articles approval certificate;

(2) no special goods and articles approval certificate has been obtained, or the quantity exceeds the approved scope;

(3) the packaging does not meet the safety management requirements for special goods and articles;

(4) other circumstances in which quarantine inspection fails.

For inbound special goods and articles whose return shipment may pose public health risks due to damaged packaging, leakage or the like, destruction shall be carried out according to law under customs supervision.

## Chapter 4 Supervision and Administration

**Article 20**　A unit of inbound and outbound special goods and articles shall establish a biosafety management system for special goods and articles and shall produce, sell and use special goods and articles strictly for the approved purposes.

A unit of inbound and outbound special goods and articles shall keep records of the production, sale and use of special goods and articles. The records shall be truthful and shall be kept for not less than 3 years.

**Article 21**　Customs shall conduct biosafety risk monitoring of inbound and outbound special goods and articles that may cause harm to public health safety within the territory.

Where samples need to be collected from inbound and outbound special goods and articles for testing in the course of risk monitoring, the unit of such goods and articles shall cooperate in sample collection as required by customs.

**Article 22**　Customs shall supervise and administer the production, sale and use of special goods and articles by units of inbound and outbound special goods and articles on the basis of the special goods and articles approval certificate.

Where any of the following circumstances is found in the course of supervision and administration, return shipment or destruction shall be carried out according to law under customs supervision:

(1) the laboratory using the special goods and articles is inconsistent with the special goods and articles approval certificate, unless approved by customs;

(2) the inbound special goods and articles do not conform to the special goods and articles approval certificate in terms of goods and documents.

**Article 23**　Customs officers shall enforce the law impartially and be loyal to their duties, and shall keep confidential the commercial secrets, personal privacy and personal information they learn in the performance of their duties.

## Chapter 5 Legal Liability

**Article 24**　Anyone who violates these Provisions and commits any of the following acts shall be fined not more than 100,000 yuan by customs:

(1) obtaining a special goods and articles approval certificate by fraud, bribery or other improper means;

(2) selling or using special goods and articles without the consent of customs;

(3) failing to return or destroy special goods and articles as required by these Provisions;

(4) carrying out operations on special goods and articles in laboratories not of the corresponding biosafety level;

(5) failing to establish records of the production, sale and use of special goods and articles, or keeping records inconsistent with the facts.

**Article 25**　Anyone who violates these Provisions by refusing or obstructing customs and its officers in the lawful performance of their duties shall be referred to the relevant departments for handling according to law.

**Article 26**　Where customs officers engage in favoritism, abuse their powers or neglect their duties in violation of relevant laws and regulations, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law; where the circumstances are serious and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be pursued according to law.

**Article 27**　Where a violation of these Provisions constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be pursued according to law.

## Chapter 6 Supplementary Provisions

**Article 28**　For the purposes of these Provisions, the following terms have the meanings given below:

Human tissues such as blood refer to human whole blood, plasma, serum, other blood components, cells, cell lines, embryos, organs, tissues, bone marrow and the like, as well as other secretions, excretions and body fluids that may transmit infectious diseases, such as cerebrospinal fluid, pleural fluid and peritoneal fluid.

Pathogenic microorganisms refer to microorganisms that can invade the human body and cause infection or even infectious diseases, including viruses, bacteria, fungi, actinomycetes, rickettsiae, spirochetes, chlamydiae, mycoplasmas, prions and parasites.

Biological products refer to bioactive preparations derived from humans or used in human medicine-related fields, such as proteins, cytokines, enzymes and their preparations, toxins, antigens, antibodies, antigen-antibody complexes, nucleic acids and other biotechnology products.

A unit of inbound and outbound special goods and articles refers to a legal person or other organization engaged in the production, sale or use of inbound and outbound special goods and articles, including scientific research, medical treatment, inspection and testing, and pharmaceutical research and development outsourcing.

**Article 29**　Where inbound and outbound special goods and articles involve other regulatory requirements such as animal and plant quarantine, the provisions of relevant laws and regulations shall apply.

**Article 30**　These Provisions shall be interpreted by the General Administration of Customs.

**Article 31**　These Provisions shall come into force on August 1, 2026. The Provisions on the Administration of Health Quarantine of Special Articles Entering and Leaving the Country, promulgated on January 21, 2015 by Order No. 160 of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine and amended by Order No. 184 of the former General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine on October 18, 2016, Order No. 238 of the General Administration of Customs on April 28, 2018, Order No. 240 of the General Administration of Customs on May 29, 2018 and Order No. 243 of the General Administration of Customs on November 23, 2018, shall be repealed simultaneously.
