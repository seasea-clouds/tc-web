Live animals only, and species must clear three exclusion lists. Announcement No. 118 admits Thai edible aquatic animals to China from 9 August, except species on the CITES appendices, China's protected wildlife list and invasive alien species list. A spoken registration number is not enough; hold your booking.

One lot of Thai farmed live shrimp: get the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit; the farm is recommended by Thailand and registered with China; pack lots from different areas or establishments separately; a Chinese outer label gives species, scientific name, registration number and destination as the PRC; each lot carries a Thai health certificate, original with the goods, printed in English or Chinese, handwriting (bar the inspector's signature) or alterations invalid.

Wild-caught lots add the fishing area and packing house number. Missing certificate items stall the port: noncompliant goods are returned, destroyed or otherwise disposed of; serious or repeated failures let China tighten checks or suspend that establishment. Country and lot are a walkthrough; the registered list and certificate govern.

Pursuant to the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter "the Chinese side") and the Ministry of Agriculture and Cooperatives of the Kingdom of Thailand (hereinafter "the Thai side") on the inspection, quarantine and hygiene requirements for Thai edible aquatic animals exported to China, import of Thai edible aquatic animals meeting the following requirements is permitted from today.

I. Inspection and Quarantine Basis

(i) Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; (iii) Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; (iv) Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Entry Aquatic Animals; (v) Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Cooperatives of the Kingdom of Thailand on Inspection, Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for Thai Edible Aquatic Animals Exported to China (hereinafter "the Protocol").

II. Scope of Imported Products

Edible aquatic animals in this Announcement means live aquatic animals, farmed or wild, for human consumption, excluding species listed in the appendices to the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES), the National Key Protected Wildlife List of China and the Key Managed Invasive Alien Species List; the relevant species list is published on the GACC website.

III. Quarantine Approval

Import of Thai edible aquatic animals requires prior quarantine approval and an Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit.

IV. Requirements for Production Establishments

Production establishments (including farms and transit packing houses) exporting edible aquatic animals to China shall satisfy the following conditions:

(i) comply with the relevant laws and regulations of Thailand and be under the effective supervision of the Thai competent authority; (ii) be approved by the Thai competent authority, recommended to the Chinese side for registration and obtain Chinese registration; (iii) registered establishments shall establish effective safety and hygiene self-management and traceability systems to ensure that the whole process of farming, capture, packaging, storage, transport, transit and export of edible aquatic animals complies with the hygiene and traceability requirements of both sides.

V. Requirements for Imported Products

(i) Wild edible aquatic animals exported to China shall originate in Thai territory or international waters, and farmed edible aquatic animals shall originate in Thai territorial waters; (ii) drugs banned by both China and Thailand shall not be used during farming, capture, temporary holding, packaging and transport, and drugs restricted or permitted by both sides shall be used scientifically as prescribed; (iii) the Thai competent authority shall effectively monitor diseases, toxic and harmful substances and foodborne pathogenic microorganisms of edible aquatic animals exported to China, and the monitoring results shall comply with Chinese food safety and animal health standards; (iv) before export, edible aquatic animals shall undergo clinical inspection by Thai official inspectors and be confirmed healthy, free of clinical signs of infectious diseases and parasitic diseases, and fit for export.

VI. Packaging and Labelling Requirements

(i) Edible aquatic animals exported to China from different capture areas or registered establishments shall be packed separately, and different species of edible aquatic animals shall be packed independently; (ii) packaging containers shall be brand new or disinfected and able to meet animal survival and welfare needs; (iii) packaging materials, water or ice and bedding materials shall comply with safety and hygiene requirements and shall not contain pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances endangering animals, plants and human health; (iv) outer packages shall bear a Chinese label stating the species name and scientific name of the aquatic animals, the capture area (applicable only to wild-caught aquatic animals), the names of the farm/packing house and the exporting establishment, the registration number and the destination (which shall be marked as the People's Republic of China), among other information.

VII. Certificate Requirements

Each lot of edible aquatic animals exported to China shall be accompanied by a health certificate issued by the Thai competent authority, and the original of the health certificate shall travel with the goods. The health certificate shall be printed in English or Chinese; handwriting (except the inspector's signature) or alterations render it invalid. The health certificate shall include at least the following:

(i) the scientific name, packaging type and number/weight of the edible aquatic animals; (ii) for farmed edible aquatic animals, the name and registration number of the farm; for wild-caught edible aquatic animals, specific information on the capture area and the name and registration number of the packing house; (iii) the names and addresses of the exporter and importer; (iv) the date of departure, the port of exit and the type and number of the means of transport; (v) a statement that the edible aquatic animals comply with the relevant requirements of the Protocol and are fit for human consumption; (vi) the date of issuance and the signature or seal of the Thai official veterinarian; (vii) the official seal of the Thai competent authority.

VIII. Other Requirements

China Customs shall, in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and in combination with this Announcement, conduct inspection and quarantine of edible aquatic animals exported to China.

For noncompliant products, return, destruction or other disposal shall be carried out in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations. For production establishments with serious problems or repeated noncompliance, the Chinese side may tighten inspection and quarantine or suspend imports.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

9 August 2026
