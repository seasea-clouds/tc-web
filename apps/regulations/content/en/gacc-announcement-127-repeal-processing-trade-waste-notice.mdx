Announcement No. 127 [2026] of the General Administration of Customs takes effect on the date of publication, 31 August 2026: to implement the Ecological Environment Code, the General Administration of Customs repeals the 2000 General Office notice on customs supervision of waste arising from imported materials for processing [No. 102 of 2000], and the waste-supervision regime it set no longer applies.

If you handle formalities for the supervision of waste arising from imported processing-trade materials: the formalities no longer cite [No. 102 of 2000]; such waste continues to be handled under the current rules on processing by-products and defective products, and the announcement is not an endorsement of any irregular disposal.

Signed on 31 August 2026; the publication date and the effective date are the same day; only [No. 102 of 2000] is repealed and the other current rules remain unaffected; the signatory is the General Administration of Customs.

To implement the Ecological Environment Code, the General Administration of Customs decides to repeal the Notice of the General Office of the General Administration of Customs on Customs Supervision of Waste Arising from Imported Materials for Processing [No. 102 of 2000]. This announcement takes effect on the date of publication.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

31 August 2026
