The same dairy-protein powder can sit on a shelf at 58 yuan per kilogram or at 396 yuan per kilogram, and the difference is not only the formula. Split the public price ladder into four bands, then compare the cross-border consolidated tax with the general-trade tax burden, and you can see which band your product belongs in and what the price space is made of. That band cannot be picked by feel; it has to hold up when read both per kilogram and per serving.

## The price ladder has four bands

The public price ladder for dairy-protein products has four bands: commodity, mainstream brands, professional, and ultra-premium. In US dollars per kilogram, the four bands fall at 8–14, 14–22, 22–35, and 35–55. In yuan, that is about 58–101, 101–158, 158–252, and 252–396 per kilogram, converted at about 1 US dollar to 7.2 yuan. The first three bands also carry a per-serving price: 0.7–1.2, 1.5–2.5, and 3.0–4.0 US dollars. The suggested position is professional band and up.

| Band | US dollars per kg | yuan per kg | US dollars per serving (first three bands) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commodity | 8–14 | about 58–101 | 0.7–1.2 |
| Mainstream brands | 14–22 | about 101–158 | 1.5–2.5 |
| Professional | 22–35 | about 158–252 | 3.0–4.0 |
| Ultra-premium | 35–55 | about 252–396 | not listed |

## Where the cross-border gap comes from

The cross-border consolidated tax is priced into cost at 9.1%. Public modeling puts the tax burden on comparable protein products under general trade at about 27.5%. The gap between the two figures is the ceiling of pricing headroom that the cross-border route carries. It is a ceiling, not profit: channel costs still come off one by one when you position.

This waterfall from the neighboring page sets the cost basis: consolidated tax is charged on the dutiable value, while platform fee, fulfillment, first-leg freight, commission, and settlement are deducted layer by layer, each at the rate on the actual quotation. However the price space is calculated, it lands on the same deduction path.

## Headroom is not price

Headroom does not mean you can label any price you like. Functional health food belongs at the professional band and up, where a formula plus testing endorsement supports the price, not a low-price volume play. A low-price play also weakens the credibility of the professional endorsement: the price signal and the endorsement signal contradict each other, and buyers remember only cheap.

## Scan on two bases

Price scanning has to read two bases at once: per kilogram and per serving. Public data gives ranges, not single prices, so our assumptions never set your price. The per-serving basis depends on the serving weight in particular; the same tin, scooped differently, can move into a different band. Ranges update with data, and the plan locks in no unit price.

## Six price variables

A band becomes a quotation through six variables: ingredients, first-leg freight, FX, platform commission, payment fees, and promotion commission rate. The public category range for platform commission is 0.5%–5%. Move any one variable and the final price moves with it.

| Variable | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Ingredients | Formula and ingredient grade set the cost base |
| First-leg freight | Charged by weight and volume; sea and air differ widely |
| FX | The US dollar reference uses about 1 US dollar to 7.2 yuan; the rate on the quotation day prevails |
| Platform commission | Public category range 0.5%–5% |
| Payment fees | A share of the amount collected |
| Promotion commission rate | Attributed by GMV; the rate is yours to set |

## Walk one dairy-protein product through it

Suppose a dairy-protein powder is priced at 200 yuan per kilogram, about 28 US dollars, inside the professional band of 158–252 yuan. The cross-border consolidated tax at 9.1% is about 18.2 yuan. At the same price, the public general-trade burden at 27.5% is about 55 yuan. The difference is about 36.8 yuan per kilogram, and that is the ceiling of price space available to this product.

Then deduct channel costs: platform commission at the top of the public range, 5%, is 10 yuan; payment fees at an assumed 1% are 2 yuan; first-leg freight is charged separately by weight and volume; the promotion commission rate is yours to set. These items eat into the 36.8 yuan one by one, and what remains is the room that brand and formula endorsement can hold. The example uses assumed parameters and demonstrates the calculation path only, not a quotation.

Read the same product per serving: with a different serving weight, the per-serving price moves between bands. Check per kilogram and per serving together, and the price stands only when both readings stand.
