When the bonded lane to China reaches market access execution, judgment is no longer the open question: bovine colostrum powder classifies as HS 0404.9000, the 1210 lane is open, licence, registration and filing are all waived, and the ordinary food positioning waives the blue hat registration; all four conclusions come from public government documents with their numbers attached. What remains is division of work and progression: five types of materials are provided by you, five execution lines are handled by us, and the customs execution arrangement is issued together with the S0 deliverable. This article lays out who prepares which materials, who makes which judgments, and which decisions still sit with you.

This division of work comes from the way we actually run products like bovine colostrum powder. The classification basis chain and the consolidated document-number table were established in other articles of this series; this article cites the conclusions and does not re-derive them. The scope sits on the execution side: what you need to produce, what we carry out on our side, and which decisions remain yours at the end of the access push.

## Market access division: five types you provide, five lines we execute

The starting point of the division is where each material originates. Statutory materials can only be issued by their holders: a CoA comes from the production batch at the factory, the trademark registration certificate sits with the rights holder, and the authorization letter has to be collected along the authorization chain; verification and classification actions, by contrast, are carried out on the customs side under public regulations and supplement rather than replace the issuing of materials. The alignment is laid out line by line below: materials you issue on the left, actions that land on our side on the right.

- You provide the full SKU list, ingredient tables, specifications and packaging images; on that basis we complete the classification and the list comparison and confirm the inspection and quarantine framing for entry.
- You provide CoAs for the same batches within the last 12 months, covering heavy metals and microorganisms; we consolidate the CoAs, arrange supplementary testing for the gaps, consolidate the certificates of origin and review the Chinese electronic labels.
- You provide the factory qualifications and the endorsement reports from third-party testing institutions; we verify the qualifications and the endorsements.
- You provide the target sales volume and the stocking scale; we reconcile the scales and schedule the first leg and the stocking.
- You provide the trademark registration certificate and the brand authorization letter; we run the China trademark status screening, the customs IP recordal and the authorization-chain completeness check.

Five lines do not mean we do everything. In the status column of the division table, all five types of materials are marked "pending from your company": the origin of every material sits with you, the factory and the rights holder, and that is exactly where the division draws its line. The supporting actions (trademark status screening, customs IP recordal, supplementary testing for detection gaps) are executed by us, on the condition that the existing materials and the authorization letter are in place first.

## Where each of the five types of materials gets held up

The SKU list and the ingredient tables are the raw material of the classification comparison. Which row the tariff code lands on, and whether the list remark lets it through, all start from this table; bovine colostrum powder landing on 0404.9000 is one example. When the list lacks specifications or packaging images, classification can point to a direction but not to a row, and every comparison downstream has no footing.

The key for a CoA is the same batch within the last 12 months. Heavy metals and microorganisms are priority items in customs sampling; when the batch does not match, the fullest report still is not counted toward this batch, and the gap is closed by supplementary testing, which needs scheduling; that is the weight behind the "gap testing" status in the materials table. The same CoA also feeds two places: the certificate-of-origin consolidation and the Chinese electronic label review, one against the origin, one against the consistency between label information and test data.

Factory qualifications and testing endorsements verify authenticity and coverage. Registration of the overseas producer is waived under the 1210 framing (Article 3 of Announcement No. 486 regulates goods under Mode 1210 as personal-use inbound items, and first-import licensing, registration and filing do not apply), yet the qualifications and endorsements still need to be on file: both customs inquiries and platform reviews can see this item.

The volume scale sets the first leg and the stocking schedule. Mode 1210 stocks goods in the zone before orders arrive. Only when the target sales volume and the stocking scale are reconciled can the first-leg batches be scheduled; when the scale is off by one step, the schedule shifts by one step, so this reconciliation starts the scheduling.

The trademark registration certificate and the brand authorization letter correspond to three actions: China trademark status screening, customs IP recordal, and the authorization-chain completeness check. The institutional basis for the recordal is the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Customs Protection of Intellectual Property Rights; with the recordal done in advance, customs-side intellectual property protection has its ex officio basis. The authorization-chain check asks whether the authorization letter connects back to the rights holder; when a general agent holds only first-tier authorization and the rights holder's authorization is missing, the recordal waits at that step until the chain is complete.

## Three lists: how far the judgments have gone

The status of market access can be summarized in three lists: settled, handled by us, and awaiting your decision. This structure answers three questions: how far the judgments have gone, which actions need no hand of yours, and what still needs your decision. The four settled items follow.

- Bovine colostrum powder classifies as HS 0404.9000, pinned by classification decision W2020-29 (published in GACC Announcement No. 108 of 2020).
- The 1210 lane is open and 9610 is not among the routes open to this tariff line: item 5 of the Adjustment Table annexed to Announcement No. 7 of 2022 gives 0404.9000 the remark "bonded online retail goods only".
- The three exemptions apply: no licence, no registration, no filing, under Article 3 of Shang Caifa [2018] No. 486.
- Under the ordinary food positioning, blue hat registration and filing are waived, consistent with the framing of the same announcement.

The nature of these four items needs to be stated plainly: they are not estimates, they are settled judgments, each from a public government document with its number attached, traceable to the original text item by item. Once the judgment side converges into four items, the weight of the access push lands on the execution side.

Only one item is handled by us: the customs execution arrangement for 0404.9000, where we complete the judgment under public regulations and work it into the filing framing. This item breaks down into four tasks:

- Inspection and quarantine arrangements for the zone-entry step: bovine colostrum powder is outside the four dairy categories listed in GACC Announcement No. 114 of 2021 as requiring entry quarantine approval (raw milk, raw milk products, pasteurized milk, and adjusted milk produced by pasteurization), and its entry inspection and quarantine number is 0404900000201.
- Declaration route: declared under the ordinary food positioning, carrying no health food registration number, consistent with the settled positioning conclusion.
- Accompanying documents: the CoA, the certificate of origin and the Chinese label information are consolidated with the declaration, sourced from the consolidation results of the five types of materials above.
- Processing time frames: the statutory time frames at each customs step are listed with the verification checklist as the baseline for progression, not as a delivery promise.

The judgment basis is taken from the original regulation texts, listed item by item with the verification checklist and checkable line by line. The verification checklist is part of the S0 deliverable and is issued by stage; S0 is the first of the fourteen work packages (S0 to S13) in the service framework, covering the starting diagnosis and entity setup.

## Three items awaiting your decision

The decision list carries three items. All three were established in the "three items to settle at the start"; here we only state what each one holds up:

- Target category and claim strategy: once the category is set, the choice of list row and the declaration framing follow; once the claim strategy is set, the copy is written within the ordinary food boundary or within the health function boundary.
- Whether to start the blue hat route: the ordinary food positioning and blue hat registration are two routes, each with its own time cost and material volume; we produce the two-case comparison and the cost comparison, and the choice is yours. The differences between the routes already have their own article in this series, so they are not repeated here.
- The trademark registration certificate and the authorization letter: this one is a materials action. The certificate and the authorization letter sit with the rights holder; the trademark status screening and the customs IP recordal start counting from the day you hand them over.

These three items need your side to decide, and the reason is authority, not workload: category and route are business decisions, and the choice sits with the brand owner; the certificate and the authorization letter are holder materials, and the issuing authority sits with the rights holder. Judgments and verifications can be handled for you; decisions and certified materials come from you.

## Completeness and the progression of S0

As the five types of materials arrive one by one, completeness is confirmed item by item; once the materials are in place, S0 starts and the rough schedule comes out with it. The progression logic is simple, and the sticking points usually appear in two types of materials.

CoA batches that do not line up are the most common one: the materials at hand are from older batches, they do not match the same-batch-within-12-months requirement, supplementary testing needs scheduling, and completeness stays at that line. A broken authorization chain is the other one: the general agent holds only first-tier authorization and the rights holder's authorization is missing, so the trademark screening and the recordal have nothing to start from. Both sticking points have supporting actions (a supplementary testing schedule, a verification path for completing the authorization chain), but the starting point is always the materials, and the supporting actions follow only after the materials are in place.
