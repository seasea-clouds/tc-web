The quarantine certificate for plant products exported from Chile to China is going paperless; from 20 April 2026 you just follow the electronic certificate. GACC Announcement No. 41 of 2026 makes it clear: Chilean authorities issue electronic certificates and no longer issue paper ones.

For a specific consignment, here is how to declare: where Chile has already issued an electronic certificate, you only need to enter the certificate number when declaring and are exempt from submitting a paper scan of the phytosanitary certificate; customs now verifies through online checking of the electronic certificate.

In special cases such as a network failure, under the emergency mechanism agreed by both sides, you may use a paper phytosanitary certificate issued by Chile to complete customs clearance. The electronic certificate is the norm; the paper certificate is an emergency backup.

<!-- original -->

To advance the paperless process of inspection and quarantine certificates between China and Chile, and through consultation and agreement by both sides, it has been decided to implement paperless quarantine certificates for plant products exported from Chile to China. The relevant matters are announced as follows:

From 20 April 2026, Chilean authorities issue electronic certificates for plant products exported to China and no longer issue paper certificates.

When an enterprise declares plant products exported from Chile to China, if Chile has already issued an electronic certificate, it only needs to enter the certificate number and is exempt from submitting a paper scan of the phytosanitary certificate.

Customs verifies the phytosanitary certificate through online checking of the electronic certificate.

If, for special reasons such as a network failure, an enterprise may use a paper phytosanitary certificate issued by Chile to complete customs clearance under the emergency mechanism agreed by both sides.

This announcement is hereby made.

General Administration of Customs

15 April 2026
