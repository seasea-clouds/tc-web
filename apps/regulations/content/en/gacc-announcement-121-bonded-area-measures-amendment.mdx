The General Administration of Customs of China amended the Measures for the Supervision of the Yangshan Special Comprehensive Bonded Zone and the Yangpu Bonded Port Area in Announcement No. 121 of 2026, removing two instances of “solid waste that can be used as raw materials”. The announcement takes effect on 17th August 2026.

This amendment is part of the review of the supporting documents for the Ecological and Environmental Code of the People's Republic of China. China has banned the import of solid waste, and the original provisions no longer have any objects of regulation, which also makes it easy for enterprises to misinterpret the scope of access.

Enterprises within the zone still maintain their books, go through customs clearance, and undergo follow-up supervision in accordance with the current regulatory measures. Raw materials are imported based on their actual characteristics, and the previously deleted term “solid waste” is no longer used.

<!-- original -->

In order to effectively review the administrative regulatory documents related to the Ecological and Environmental Code of the People's Republic of China, it has been decided to amend certain provisions of Announcement No. 170 of 2019 of the General Administration of Customs (Announcement on Issuing the Measures of the Customs of the People's Republic of China for the Supervision and Administration of the Yangshan Special Comprehensive Bonded Zone) and Announcement No. 73 of 2020 of the General Administration of Customs (Announcement on Issuing the Measures of the Customs of the People's Republic of China for the Supervision and Administration of the Yangpu Bonded Port Area) as follows:

Delete “, solid waste that can be used as raw materials” from Article 11 of Announcement No. 170 [2019] of the General Administration of Customs.

Delete “, solid waste that can be used as raw materials” from Article 8 of Announcement No. 73 [2020] of the General Administration of Customs.

This announcement shall take effect from the date of its issuance.

This announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs

17 August 2026
