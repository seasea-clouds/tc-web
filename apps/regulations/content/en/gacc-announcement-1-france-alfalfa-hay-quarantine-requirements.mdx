French alfalfa hay enters now. GACC Announcement No. 1 of 2026 opens imports of French alfalfa hay, but only alfalfa pellets or bales grown in France and processed by heat dehydration and compression; processing plants must be assessed and approved by the French side and registered with the Chinese side.

One lot's whole route: a registered plant keeps raw alfalfa away from livestock farms and ranches, mechanical drying holds 170 °C for at least 15 minutes, and impurities and contamination are removed before compression and baling, with no metal parts used; before departure the French side inspects and issues a phytosanitary certificate and a health certificate, both stating the plant name and registration number, the consignor and consignee and the container number.

If the certificate, the registration or the label does not match, hold this lot: no valid certificate, an unregistered plant, soil or unapproved GM material means return or destruction; other failures are handled by disinfestation, correction, return or destruction under the law, and serious cases can suspend the plant, the production area or even the alfalfa hay trade with China.

Pursuant to the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Agrifood and Food Sovereignty of the French Republic (hereinafter the French side) on the sanitary and phytosanitary requirements for the export of alfalfa hay from France to China, effective immediately, French alfalfa hay complying with the following requirements is permitted for import into China:

I. Legal Basis for Quarantine

(i) Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China;

(ii) Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(iii) Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Imported and Exported Feed and Feed Additives;

(iv) Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Agrifood and Food Sovereignty of the French Republic on the Sanitary and Phytosanitary Requirements for the Export of Alfalfa Hay from France to China.

II. Names of Commodities Permitted for Import

Alfalfa hay (Medicago sativa L.) in this Announcement means alfalfa pellets or alfalfa bales grown in France and processed by heat dehydration and compression.

III. Registration of Enterprises

Alfalfa hay exported to China shall come from processing enterprises assessed and approved by the French side. Processing enterprises shall be recommended by the French side to the Chinese side and registered after passing an on-site inspection or document review by the Chinese side, and the list shall be published and dynamically updated on the GACC website.

IV. Pests of Concern

Alfalfa hay exported to China shall not carry the following pests of concern to the Chinese side:

(1) Verticillium wilt Verticillium albo-atrum

(2) Darnel ryegrass Lolium temulentum

(3) Australian dodder Cuscuta australis

(4) Field dodder Cuscuta campestris

(5) Flax dodder Cuscuta epilinum

(6) Thyme dodder Cuscuta epithymum

(7) Diffuse knapweed Centaurea diffusa

(8) Horsenettle Solanum carolinense

(9) Silverleaf nightshade Solanum elaeagnifolium

(10) Johnsongrass Sorghum halepense

(11) Yellow starthistle Centaurea solstitialis

(12) Spotted knapweed Centaurea stoebe subsp. australis

(13) Quackgrass Elymus repens

(14) Common fumitory Fumaria officinalis

(15) Small broomrape Orobanche minor

(16) Prostrate pigweed Amaranthus blitoides

(17) Nettle-leaved goosefoot Chenopodiastrum murale

(18) African feather grass Pennisetum macrourum

V. Production and Export Requirements

(i) Cultivation, processing, storage and transport.

(1) The French side shall strengthen official control over growing farms and processing enterprises and exercise day-to-day supervision. Growing farms shall implement effective integrated pest management (IPM) measures to avoid and control the occurrence of pests of concern to the Chinese side. Processing enterprises shall establish and effectively operate a good hazard analysis and critical control point (HACCP) system and a traceability management system, or manage in accordance with their principles.

(2) Processing enterprises and the cultivation, processing and storage sites of raw alfalfa shall be isolated from animal farms and ranches to avoid contamination by excreta, secretions and other substances of cloven-hoofed animals. During the harvesting, drying, storage and transport of alfalfa hay, grass roots, soil and other foreign matter shall be prevented from mixing in.

(3) Processing enterprises shall have mechanical drying facilities, and the drying process shall be maintained at 170 °C for at least 15 minutes.

(4) Processing enterprises shall exercise effective management over raw material procurement, high-temperature dehydration, product testing and other links, keep the processing area and equipment clean, take effective impurity and contamination removal measures before compression and baling, and shall not use metal materials for baling and packaging, so as to ensure that alfalfa hay exported to China meets the following requirements: (1) free from pests of concern to the Chinese side and other live pests; (2) free from grass roots, plant seeds and other plant residues and foreign matter; (3) free from animal manure, animal carcasses and poultry feathers; (4) free from soil; (5) free from genetically modified material not approved by the Chinese side; (6) in compliance with the relevant requirements of China's national standard Feed Hygiene Standard (GB 13078).

(5) Storage facilities before shipment shall meet the following requirements: (1) a relatively enclosed and independent space; (2) effective rodent, insect and bird control measures to prevent pest contamination.

(6) Clean containers shall be used for transport; where necessary, inspection and quarantine and quarantine treatment shall be carried out. Each container shall have at least one packaging label stating the name and registration number of the processing enterprise and the words in Chinese and English "Alfalfa from France export to the People's Republic of China".

Product labels shall comply with the requirements of China's national standard Feed Labelling (GB 10648).

(ii) Pre-departure quarantine and certificate requirements.

Before export, the French side shall carry out inspection and quarantine on alfalfa hay exported to China and, for those that meet the requirements, issue a phytosanitary certificate and a health certificate that comply with international standards. The phytosanitary certificate and the health certificate shall state the name and registration number of the processing enterprise, the names and addresses of the consignor and consignee, the container number and other information. The additional declaration of the phytosanitary certificate shall state "This consignment complies with the Protocol on the sanitary and phytosanitary requirements for the export of alfalfa from France to China, and is free of any pest of concern for China." (the consignment complies with the requirements of the Protocol on the sanitary and phytosanitary requirements for the export of alfalfa hay from France to China and is free of pests of concern to the Chinese side), and the health certificate shall state "Does not contain any hazardous substances that pose a risk to public or animal health and complies with China's safety and hygiene standards, does not contain GMOs that are not authorized by China." (it does not contain any hazardous substances that pose a risk to public or animal health, complies with China's feed safety and hygiene standards, and does not contain genetically modified material not approved by China).

VI. Entry Inspection and Quarantine

(i) Verification of documents and certificates.

(1) Verify whether the alfalfa hay comes from registered processing enterprises.

(2) Verify whether the accompanying phytosanitary certificate and health certificate are authentic and valid.

(ii) Cargo inspection.

In accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules and other provisions, and in combination with Article IV and Article V of this Announcement, inspection and quarantine shall be carried out on alfalfa hay exported to China, and those that pass shall be permitted entry.

(iii) Handling of non-compliance.

(1) Where there is no valid phytosanitary certificate or health certificate, the consignment shall be returned or destroyed.

(2) Where the consignment comes from an unregistered enterprise, it shall be returned or destroyed.

(3) Where soil or genetically modified material not approved by the Chinese official authorities is found, the consignment shall be returned or destroyed.

(4) Where non-compliance with item 4 of paragraph (i) of Article V of this Announcement is found, disinfestation, return or destruction shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations. Those that pass treatment shall be permitted entry.

(5) Where non-compliance with item 6 of paragraph (i) of Article V of this Announcement is found, correction, return or destruction shall be carried out.

(6) Where other non-compliance with China's entry animal and plant quarantine requirements or China's feed safety and hygiene standards is found, it shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations.

Where the above non-compliance is found, the Chinese side shall notify the French side and, depending on the severity of the violation, take measures such as suspending the qualification of the relevant processing enterprise or production area to export to China, or even suspending the alfalfa hay trade with China, until the Chinese side confirms that the problem has been effectively rectified.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

4 January 2026
