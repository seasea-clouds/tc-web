Announcement No. 36 of 2026 prevents the introduction of dengue fever into China: travellers from affected countries or regions with fever or rash must declare on arrival.

Conveyances, containers, goods, luggage and postal items are subject to quarantine inspection; mosquitos found trigger sanitary treatment; ports must remove breeding sites.

Outbound travellers may consult Customs before departure; the announcement takes effect on 10 April 2026 and is valid for one year.

<!-- original -->

According to data published by the World Health Organization, from January to February 2026 a total of 423000 dengue cases were reported worldwide, of which 352000 were reported in the Americas. To prevent the introduction of dengue fever and protect the health and safety of persons entering and leaving the country, the following is hereby announced in accordance with the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing rules and other laws and regulations.

I. Persons arriving from countries or regions where dengue fever is endemic who show symptoms such as fever, headache, muscle and joint pain, rash, and flushing of the face, neck and chest shall voluntarily declare to Customs on entry, and Customs shall take medical measures in accordance with prescribed procedures.

II. Means of transport from countries or regions where dengue fever is endemic, together with containers and other transport equipment, goods, luggage, postal parcels and other articles and their outer packaging, shall undergo quarantine inspection by Customs in accordance with the law; where mosquitos are found during inspection, sanitary treatment shall be carried out as prescribed.

III. Port operators shall take effective measures to eliminate mosquito breeding sites, monitor and control mosquito density at ports, and submit to port health supervision by Customs.

IV. Persons travelling to countries or regions where dengue fever is endemic may, before departure, consult Customs and its International Travel Healthcare Centres, or visit the GACC website for information on the epidemic and international travel health advice.

This Announcement shall take effect on 10 April 2026 and shall be valid for one year. During that period, any country or region newly reported by the World Health Organization as having a dengue epidemic shall be subject to this Announcement.

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

8 April 2026
