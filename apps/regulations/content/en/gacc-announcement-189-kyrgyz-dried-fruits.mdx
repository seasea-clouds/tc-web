Dried fruits from the Kyrgyz Republic are approved for import into China. Announcement No. 189 of 2025 of the General Administration of Customs takes effect upon publication. Nine dried fruits including prunes and dried apricots are allowed for import; production, processing and storage facilities may export only after inspection and recommendation by the Kyrgyz side and registration by the Chinese side.

Each of the nine dried fruits shall not contain quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side, such as the grain moth and the codling moth, shall not carry live insects, insect eggs or soil, and shall be fumigated to eliminate insects before export.

Each consignment shall be accompanied by a phytosanitary certificate and the required English statement; packaging shall bear product and traceability information in Chinese and English; upon entry, customs shall conduct inspection and quarantine, and non-compliant goods shall be disposed of in accordance with the law.

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions concerning the inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for dried fruits exported from the Kyrgyz Republic to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Water Resources, Agriculture and Processing Industry of the Kyrgyz Republic (hereinafter referred to as the Kyrgyz side), as from today, dried fruits from the Kyrgyz Republic that meet the following requirements are permitted for import.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(2) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Quarantine of Entry and Exit Animals and Plants and its implementing regulations;

(3) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations;

(4) The Measures for the Safety Administration of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China;

(5) The Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Dried Fruits to Be Exported from Kyrgyzstan to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Water Resources, Agriculture and Processing Industry of the Kyrgyz Republic (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Products Permitted for Import

The dried fruits permitted for import refer to fruits of plants grown in the Kyrgyz Republic that are processed for human consumption through cleaning, drying, sorting and other technical processes: prunes (Prunus domestica), dried apricots (Prunus armeniaca), dried apples (Malus domestica), dried pears (Pyrus communis), dried cherries (Prunus cerasus), raisins (Vitis L.), dried melon (Cucumis melo), dried oleaster (Elaeagnus orientalis) and dried rose hips (Rosa L. species), nine dried fruits in total.

III. Food Safety Requirements

Dried fruits exported to China shall comply with China's laws and regulations on food safety and plant quarantine, national food safety standards and the provisions of the Protocol.

IV. Phytosanitary Requirements

(1) Dried plums exported to China shall not contain the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: grain moth (Nemapogon granella), codling moth (Cydia pomonella), European apple weevil (Rhynchites bacchus), almond wasp (Eurytoma amygdali), fall webworm (Hyphantria cunea), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), dahlia Verticillium wilt fungus (Verticillium dahliae), fire blight bacterium (Erwinia amylovora).

(2) Dried apricots exported to China shall not contain the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: grain moth (Nemapogon granella), codling moth (Cydia pomonella), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), Verticillium wilt fungus (Verticillium dahliae).

(3) Dried apples exported to China shall not contain the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: grain moth (Nemapogon granella), codling moth (Cydia pomonella), European apple weevil (Rhynchites bacchus), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), Verticillium wilt fungus (Verticillium dahliae), tobacco ringspot virus (Tobacco ringspot virus, TRSV).

(4) Dried pears exported to China shall not contain the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: grain moth (Nemapogon granella), codling moth (Cydia pomonella), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae).

(5) Dried cherries exported to China shall not contain the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: grain moth (Nemapogon granella), cherry fruit fly (Rhagoletis cerasi), codling moth (Cydia pomonella), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), Verticillium wilt fungus (Verticillium dahliae), plum pox virus (Plum pox virus), tobacco ringspot virus (Tobacco ringspot virus, TRSV).

(6) Raisins exported to China shall not contain the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: grain moth (Nemapogon granella), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), Verticillium wilt fungus (Verticillium dahliae), tobacco ringspot virus (Tobacco ringspot virus, TRSV).

(7) Dried melon exported to China shall not contain the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: grain moth (Nemapogon granella), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), Verticillium wilt fungus (Verticillium dahliae), alfalfa Verticillium wilt fungus (Verticillium albo-atrum), tobacco ringspot virus (Tobacco ringspot virus, TRSV).

(8) Dried oleaster exported to China shall not contain the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: grain moth (Nemapogon granella), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), Verticillium wilt fungus (Verticillium dahliae).

(9) Dried rose hips exported to China shall not contain the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: grain moth (Nemapogon granella), San Jose scale (Diaspidiotus perniciosus), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae).

(10) Dried fruits exported to China shall not contain live insects, insect eggs or soil, shall not be mixed with weed seeds, plant debris, metal foreign matter, gravel and other impurities.

(11) Dried fruits exported to China shall be fumigated before export to ensure that they do not contain live insects.

V. Enterprise Requirements

The Kyrgyz side shall inspect the production, processing and storage facilities of dried fruits exported to China in accordance with the relevant requirements of the Chinese side for the registration of overseas food production enterprises, and recommend compliant enterprises to the Chinese side. The Chinese side shall register the enterprises that meet the requirements.

Only after an enterprise has obtained registration may its products be exported to China. The list of registered Kyrgyz dried fruit enterprises exporting to China may be queried on the website of the General Administration of Customs.

VI. Raw Material Requirements

The raw materials for dried fruits exported to China shall come from fresh fruit growing enterprises confirmed by the Kyrgyz side as meeting the relevant phytosanitary and food safety requirements.

VII. Processing Requirements

The Kyrgyz side shall provide guidance and supervision over the processes of raw material cultivation, production, processing, storage, transport and export of dried fruits exported to China, so as to ensure compliance with the relevant food safety and phytosanitary requirements of China and the Kyrgyz Republic and to prevent contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

VIII. Packaging and Labeling Requirements

Dried fruits exported to China shall be packaged in clean, hygienic new materials that meet food safety and phytosanitary requirements, and leakage shall be prevented during transport. Each package shall bear in Chinese or English the statement „This product is exported to the People's Republic of China“ as well as traceability information including the product name, place of origin, name of the production and processing enterprise and its registration number in China, and the name and address of the exporter. The above information may be affixed to the package in the form of a label.

IX. Transport Requirements

Before shipment, the Kyrgyz side shall inspect the means of transport for dried fruits exported to China. The means of transport shall meet sanitary and epidemic prevention requirements and shall not contain quarantine pests or other regulated quarantine articles, such as weed seeds, live insects, plant debris, soil and other extraneous impurities.

X. Certificate Requirements

Before export, the Kyrgyz side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for dried fruits exported to China that have passed inspection and quarantine and meet the requirements of the Protocol. The additional declaration column of the certificate shall state the name of the dried fruit production and processing enterprise, its registration number in China, and the following: „The dried fruits certified by this phytosanitary certificate comply with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Dried Fruits to Be Exported from Kyrgyzstan to China signed by Kyrgyzstan and China on August 30th, 2025. This consignment is free from quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side.“ (The above means that the dried fruits certified by this phytosanitary certificate comply with the provisions of the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Dried Fruits to Be Exported from Kyrgyzstan to China signed by the two sides on August 31, 2025, and that this consignment is free from quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side.)

The quarantine treatment methods (such as chemicals, temperature, duration and other technical requirements) shall be stated in the phytosanitary certificate.

XI. Entry Inspection and Quarantine and Treatment of Non-Compliance

(1) Entry inspection and quarantine. Upon arrival of dried fruits exported to China at the entry port in China, China Customs shall carry out inspection and quarantine supervision, and entry shall be permitted only upon compliance.

(2) Treatment of non-compliance. If the Chinese side finds that dried fruits exported to China violate China's laws and regulations on food safety and plant quarantine, national food safety standards or the provisions of the Protocol, China Customs shall dispose of them in accordance with the relevant Chinese laws and regulations.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

22 September 2025
