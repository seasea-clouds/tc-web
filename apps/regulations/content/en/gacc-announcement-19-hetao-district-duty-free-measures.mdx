Announcement No. 19 of 2026 publishes duty-free measures for imports into Hetao Shenzhen Park.

Customs manages duty-free goods through electronic ledgers per eligible entity; imports must use prescribed declaration codes and remain under customs supervision.

The Measures set out tax depreciation formulas, supervision periods (three years for equipment, none for consumables) and treatment of goods diverted to other uses.

<!-- original -->

To implement the Development Plan for the Hetao Shenzhen-Hong Kong Science and Technology Innovation Cooperation Zone and the tax policies on goods entering and leaving the Shenzhen Park, GACC has formulated the Duty-Free Measures for Imported Goods of the Shenzhen Park, hereby published.

Hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

9 February 2026

Measures of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China for the Administration of Duty-Free Imported Goods of the Shenzhen Park of the Hetao Shenzhen-Hong Kong Science and Technology Innovation Cooperation Zone

Article 1 These Measures are formulated to regulate customs administration of duty-free goods in the customs supervision area (hereinafter the supervised area) subject to specific closed management in the Shenzhen Park, under the Customs Law, the Tariff Law and the Notice of the Ministry of Finance, GACC and the State Taxation Administration on Tax Policies for Goods Entering and Leaving the Shenzhen Park (Caiguanshui [2026] No. 1, the Notice).

Article 2 These Measures apply to duty-free research goods entering the supervised area via the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, to such goods and their R&D products entering other parts of the customs territory or going abroad from the supervised area, and to their management within the supervised area.

Article 3 Eligible entities importing for their own use research goods listed in the Duty-Free Research Goods List annexed to the Notice, except goods prohibited from import, goods expressly excluded from duty exemption, and goods in the Catalogue of Major Technology Equipment and Products Not Eligible for Duty Exemption, are exempt from import tariff, import-stage VAT and consumption tax (import taxes). The goods must meet the criteria for scientific research, technology development and pilot-scale transformation set by the Shenzhen municipal government. Eligible entities are enterprises with independent legal person status registered in the supervised area, institutions engaged in research there, and science and technology private non-enterprise units registered there, meeting the recognition criteria; the recognised list is communicated by Shenzhen authorities to Shenzhen Customs and filed with GACC.

Article 4 Customs manages duty-free research goods through electronic ledgers per eligible entity. On first declaration, the customs information system establishes a ledger based on the declaration, recording the entity's name and unified social credit code. Electronic data of goods entering via Hong Kong are included in the ledger after the declaration is closed and released. Customs may audit and verify the use of goods under the Customs Law and the Customs Audit Regulations.

Article 5 For duty-free research goods entering via Hong Kong, the declaration customs and port of entry are filled in as Hetao Shenzhen Park (code 5321); the consuming unit is the eligible entity. For equipment and its repair parts, appliances, documents and specimens, the levy-exemption nature is filled in as equipment for research use in Hetao Shenzhen Park (code 508, Hetao research equipment); for consumables, animals and their products and other parts, it is filled in as consumables for research use in Hetao Shenzhen Park (code 509, Hetao research consumables). The duty-levy-exemption method is filled in as no tariff, VAT or consumption tax levied (code A). Where an entity voluntarily applies to pay import taxes, the method is filled in as taxation according to regulations (code 1). Such entity may not reapply for duty-free import of the same goods within 36 months; same goods means goods with the same 8-digit tariff heading. Goods on which all import taxes have been paid are managed under domestic circulation rules.

Article 6 Where duty-free research goods entering via Hong Kong are subject to tariff quota management, trade remedy measures, suspension of tariff concessions, additional duties, or additional duties for retaliatory purposes (excluding those excluded) (four categories of measures), the four categories shall be applied under unified national rules.

Article 7 Where supervised duty-free research goods and their R&D products are transported abroad from the supervised area, customs supervision is released from the date of departure, the corresponding ledger is written off, and no import taxes need be paid; export duties are levied where applicable.

Article 8 Duty-free research goods shall be under customs supervision. Equipment and its repair parts, appliances, documents and specimens entering via Hong Kong are subject to customs supervision for 3 years from release; supervision ends upon expiry. Consumables, animals and their products and other parts have no supervision period, but R&D products formed from them remain under supervision.

Article 9 Repair parts may only be used to repair duty-free equipment under supervision. Where consumables are used in research and form R&D products, the entity shall file the correspondence between imported materials and R&D products with the competent customs and the goods are included in the ledger; where no R&D product is formed, the entity may apply to end supervision and write off the ledger without paying import taxes. The entity shall self-report the previous month's inventory, use and disposal to the competent customs by the fifth working day of each month and bears legal responsibility for accuracy.

Article 10 To circulate supervised equipment and related items to other units or individuals in the supervised area, into other parts of the customs territory, or to end supervision early, the entity shall apply to pay import taxes. The dutiable value is based on the value at import declaration from Hong Kong, depreciated by the ratio of time imported to the supervision period, using the formula: dutiable value = value at import declaration × [1 − time imported / (supervision period × 12)]. Time imported is counted by month from release: less than one month but more than 15 days counts as one month; 15 days or less is not counted.

Article 11 To divert supervised equipment and related items to other uses, the entity shall apply in advance and, upon approval, pay the corresponding import taxes; where the time cannot be determined, a security not exceeding the tax amount shall be provided. The dutiable value is depreciated by the ratio of time of diversion to the supervision period: dutiable value = value at import declaration × [time of tax payment / (supervision period × 365)]. The time is counted by day from actual diversion, with less than or more than 8 hours of use in a day both counted as one day.

Article 12 To circulate, divert to other uses or end supervision of duty-free consumables, animals and their products or other parts, the entity shall apply in advance and pay import taxes as imported materials. For R&D products in the same circumstances, the entity shall pay import taxes based on the corresponding imported materials.

Article 13 Where repair parts are used to repair goods other than duty-free equipment, the entity shall pay import taxes as imported materials. Where goods are found by Shenzhen authorities to exceed research self-use or not used for research, the entity shall, within 10 working days of confirmation, apply to pay import taxes under Articles 11 and 12.

Article 14 Where duty-free research goods are subject to the four categories of measures, the entity paying taxes under Articles 10 to 13 shall also apply the four categories under unified national rules; taxes already paid at entry via Hong Kong need not be paid again. Where the goods are not subject to the four categories but their R&D products are, taxes shall be paid based on the actual declared state of the R&D products and the four categories applied.

Article 15 When applying to pay taxes, the declaration customs and port of entry are filled in as Hetao Shenzhen Park (code 5321), levy-exemption nature as at original import, consuming unit as the entity, supervision mode as subsequent tax payment (code 9700), and duty-levy-exemption method as special case (code 4).

Article 16 On payment of all import taxes, customs writes off the corresponding ledger and ends supervision; the goods are then managed under domestic circulation rules.

Article 17 Where an entity transfers duty-free goods to another eligible entity due to bankruptcy or deregistration, the transferor and transferee shall each declare a ledger note list to customs, without paying import taxes.

Article 18 The declaration customs and port of entry may be adjusted according to the actual situation of the supervised area and announced by Shenzhen Customs.

Article 19 These Measures are interpreted by GACC.

Article 20 These Measures shall take effect on 10 February 2026.
