Export remains permitted, but the declaration must be accurate. From 30 June 2026, Announcement No. 78 regulates export declaration of drones and related items: unmanned aircraft, airships, parts and civil counter-drone systems. Exporters must declare truthfully and are liable.

For drone exports: if controlled, note “subject to export control” in “Remarks” and list the dual-use control code; if uncontrolled but similar, also note “not subject to export control”. Declare the identification code, full tariff code rather than only its first 4 digits, the overseas consignee's full name, and the domestic maker as producer and seller, not a platform.

Attach the contract, invoice and technical data; Class C express may use paper or CD, e-commerce lists are exempt, and entries must match documents and logistics. Complete elements speed clearance; if authenticity is in doubt, customs challenges and withholds release.

To regulate the export declaration of drones and related items, the following declaration requirements are hereby announced in accordance with the Export Control Law of the PRC, the Customs Law of the PRC and other relevant provisions.

I. Scope of Goods

For the purposes of this Announcement, drones and related items refer to unmanned aircraft, unmanned airships and related equipment and parts, civil counter-unmanned-aircraft systems, etc.

II. Filling Requirements

Exporters shall declare truthfully to customs in accordance with the law and are responsible for the authenticity, completeness and compliance of the declared information. In addition to filling in the form in accordance with the Specifications for Filling in the Customs Declaration Form of the People's Republic of China, exporters shall, for the goods covered by this Announcement, also fill in the form in accordance with the following requirements:

(i) Strengthen identification of export items. Where an item is subject to export control, “subject to export control” shall be noted in the “Remarks” field of the Customs Declaration Form of the People's Republic of China for Export Goods (hereinafter the Customs Declaration Form), and the corresponding dual-use item export control code shall be listed. Where an item is not subject to control but its characteristics and other indicators are close to those of a controlled item, or where its intended use does not meet the control requirements but its characteristics and other indicators do, “not subject to export control” shall be noted in the “Remarks” field of the Customs Declaration Form, the Customs Declaration Form for Outbound Class C Express Consignments of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Class C Express Consignment Declaration Form), and the Customs Cross-Border E-Commerce Retail Export Declaration List of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Cross-Border E-Commerce Declaration List).

(ii) The corresponding content shall be truthfully filled in the “Restricted Control Identification Code” and “Restricted Control Declaration Elements” fields of the Customs Declaration Form, in accordance with the restricted control declaration elements (which may be consulted in the “Parameter Query” field of the “China International Trade Single Window”) and against the “Standard Declaration Category” to which the goods belong. In the “Other” section of the “Specification and Model” field of the Class C Express Consignment Declaration Form and the Cross-Border E-Commerce Declaration List, the item name and description (including but not limited to characteristics and intended use) shall be truthfully filled in. For the Cross-Border E-Commerce Declaration List, the simplified declaration mode (that is, declaring only the first 4 digits of the tariff code) shall not be used; the full tariff code shall be declared.

(iii) The full Chinese or English name of the overseas consignee shall be filled in the “Overseas Consignor and Consignee” field of the Customs Declaration Form, the “Consignee” field of the Class C Express Consignment Declaration Form, and the “Remarks” field of the Cross-Border E-Commerce Declaration List. In the “Producer and Seller Unit” field of the Cross-Border E-Commerce Declaration List, the information on the domestic manufacturer or seller of the goods shall be filled in; the name of an e-commerce platform or agent shall not be filled in as a substitute.

(iv) When declaring on the Customs Declaration Form, the contract, invoice, technical data and other relevant documents shall be attached. Supporting documents for the Class C Express Consignment Declaration Form may be submitted in paper or CD form. The Cross-Border E-Commerce Declaration List does not require the above materials to be attached.

(v) The above information shall be true, complete and accurate, and consistent with the attached documents such as the contract, invoice and technical data as well as the logistics information. Customs shall speed up clearance for compliant goods with complete declaration elements; where the authenticity of the filled-in information is in doubt, customs shall raise a lawful challenge, and the export goods shall not be released during the challenge.

Within the territory of the People's Republic of China, goods moving between customs special supervision zones and bonded supervision places, or entering customs special supervision zones and bonded supervision places from outside them, shall be declared in accordance with the relevant provisions.

This Announcement shall take effect on 30 June 2026.

This Announcement is hereby issued.

General Administration of Customs

9 June 2026
