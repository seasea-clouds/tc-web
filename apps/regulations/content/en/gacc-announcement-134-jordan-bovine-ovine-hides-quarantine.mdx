China Customs admits Jordanian bovine and ovine hides from today under Announcement No. 134 of 2026. Only hides from Australian cattle and sheep processed at registered Jordanian plants qualify; plants need Jordanian recommendation and Chinese registration, and each consignment needs an original official health certificate.

One lot of wet-salted sheep hides goes through the whole route: Australian-born animals lawfully enter Jordan and pass quarantine; a registered plant slaughters them and cures the hides for at least 7 days in sea salt with 2% sodium carbonate; the container is disinfected, made leak-proof and sealed, and the hides are stacked and wrapped; the Jordanian side issues the health certificate, and China Customs checks the permit, the registration list and the certificate before release.

If the certificate, registration or treatment record does not match, hold this lot: no valid certificate, an unregistered plant or non-compliance means disposal under Chinese law, and serious or repeated failures can suspend imports. Immediate effect is not an exemption from quarantine: permit approval, document checks and cargo inspection all remain.

Under the laws and regulations of China and the provisions between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter “the Chinese side”) and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan (hereinafter “the Jordanian side”) on the quarantine and health requirements for the export of Jordanian bovine and ovine hides (hereinafter “hides”) to China, with effect from today, Jordanian hides meeting the following requirements are permitted for import.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; (iii) the Administrative Measures for Inspection, Quarantine and Supervision of Non-edible Animal Products Entering or Leaving the Country; (iv) the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan on the Quarantine and Health Requirements for the Export of Jordanian Bovine and Ovine Hides to China (hereinafter “the Protocol”).

II. Scope of Products Permitted for Import

For the purposes of this Announcement, hides means skins produced from cattle and sheep of Australian origin slaughtered and processed at registered Jordanian establishments in accordance with the quarantine requirements of the Protocol.

III. Requirements for Production Establishments

Establishments producing, processing and storing Jordanian hides (hereinafter “establishments”) must be approved by the Jordanian side and recommended by it to the Chinese side. Those that pass assessment by the Chinese side are registered. Establishments must meet the following requirements:

(i) clean and tidy premises, with a rational layout for raw material, production and finished product areas, level and hardened ground, walls that do not retain water, and surfaces easy to clean and disinfect; (ii) facilities and equipment adequate for production, processing and storage, with effective insect and rodent control; (iii) a complete traceability system covering records of raw material intake, processing, storage and dispatch; (iv) documented production procedures or operating instructions, with production, processing and storage meeting veterinary hygiene requirements and no mixing with other animal products; (v) continuing approval and day-to-day supervision by the Jordanian side and continued compliance with the Protocol and its veterinary hygiene requirements.

Registration of an establishment is valid for 5 years. 6 months before expiry the Jordanian side must apply to the Chinese side for renewal, which the Chinese side processes in due course under the relevant rules.

IV. Product Requirements

(i) Health requirements for the source animals. (1) Born and raised in Australia, lawfully imported from Australia, with an animal health certificate issued by the Australian official animal quarantine authority; (2) no occurrence of anthrax or lumpy skin disease within 6 months in a 50 km radius around the Jordanian entry quarantine station, slaughterhouse, hide processing plant and those sites; (3) healthy animals that passed quarantine at an approved Jordanian isolation station and remain under Jordanian animal disease surveillance; (4) not subject to movement restrictions because of foot-and-mouth disease, anthrax or other diseases transmissible by hides that harm human or animal health; (5) ante-mortem inspection by an official or approved Jordanian veterinarian showing no clinical signs of infectious animal disease.

(ii) Hygiene requirements for slaughterhouses handling the source animals. (1) A rational layout, good environmental hygiene, facilities for ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection and hide storage, and effective insect and rodent control; (2) documented slaughter and hide handling procedures or operating instructions, no slaughter of other animals on the same line, and slaughter in line with the relevant animal welfare provisions of the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH); (3) a complete traceability system covering records of animal intake, slaughter, hide storage and dispatch; (4) continuing approval and day-to-day supervision by the Jordanian side and continued compliance with the Protocol and its veterinary hygiene requirements.

(iii) Processing requirements. Hides exported to China must undergo one of the following treatments: (1) drying at a temperature of 20 °C or above for at least 42 days, or (2) curing for at least 7 days in sea salt containing 2% sodium carbonate, or (3) wet-salting or dry-salting for at least 14 days.

V. Packaging and Container Requirements

(i) Containers must be disinfected with an effective disinfectant under Jordanian supervision before use; (ii) container floors must be made leak-proof or lined with plastic sheeting or similar material; (iii) hides must be stacked in separate lots inside the container and wrapped in clean, leak-proof packaging material; (iv) the Jordanian side applies or supervises the application of tamper-proof seals or marks to the container.

VI. Storage and Transport Requirements

(i) Necessary measures must be taken during storage and transport to prevent cross-contamination and maggot growth; (ii) if hides are wet-salted, they must be naturally air-dried for at least a further 7 days after treatment to avoid bloody effluent during transport.

VII. Pre-Export Inspection and Certificate Requirements

(i) The Jordanian side is responsible for inspection and quarantine of Jordanian hides exported to China and issues a health certificate confirming that the products meet the Protocol; (ii) every consignment of hides exported to China must be accompanied by at least one original official health certificate.

VIII. Entry Inspection and Quarantine Requirements

(i) Quarantine approval. Where quarantine approval is required, the importer must obtain the Permit for Entry Quarantine of Animals and Plants under the relevant rules.

(ii) Document checks. (1) For consignments requiring quarantine approval, check that the Permit for Entry Quarantine of Animals and Plants is attached; (2) check whether the hides come from a registered establishment; (3) check that the health certificate is authentic and valid.

(iii) Cargo inspection. China Customs inspects and quarantines Jordanian hides exported to China under the relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and in line with this Announcement. Hides that pass inspection and quarantine are admitted.

(iv) Handling of non-compliance. Non-compliant products are dealt with under Chinese laws and regulations. For establishments with serious problems or repeated non-compliance, the Chinese side may apply tighter inspection and quarantine or suspend imports.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China 7 September 2026
