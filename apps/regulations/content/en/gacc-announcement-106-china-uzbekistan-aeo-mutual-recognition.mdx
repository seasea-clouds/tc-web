Four facilities require the counterpart to list you as recognised. From 1 August 2026, China Customs and Uzbekistan Customs recognise each other's AEOs: Uzbekistan recognises Chinese Customs advanced certified enterprises; China recognises enterprises certified under the Uzbek AEO system that also hold the customs business and security certificate. Imports get a lower inspection rate, priority physical inspection, a designated liaison officer and priority clearance after disruption.

Your shipment enters from Tashkent and the Uzbek shipper is an AEO: enter UZB/123456789/III in the overseas shipper code field of the declaration and the same code in the shipper AEO code field of the sea or air manifest. The importer need not be an AEO unless it is a dishonest or seriously dishonest enterprise.

Exports work the other way: give the Uzbek importer AEOCN plus positions 9 to 17 of your unified social credit code, for example AEOCN123456789. One wrong field or a code given only by word leaves the identity unconfirmed and the facilities unavailable.

The Arrangement between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Customs Committee of the Ministry of Economy and Finance of the Republic of Uzbekistan on Mutual Recognition of the 'Authorised Economic Operator' System (hereinafter the 'Mutual Recognition Arrangement') will be officially implemented from 1 August 2026. Relevant matters are hereby announced as follows:

I. In accordance with the Mutual Recognition Arrangement, China Customs and Uzbekistan Customs mutually recognise each other's authorised economic operators (AEOs). Uzbekistan Customs recognises advanced certified enterprises of China Customs as mutually recognised AEO enterprises, and China Customs recognises enterprises that are certified under the Uzbekistan Customs AEO system and at the same time hold a customs business and security certificate as mutually recognised AEO enterprises.

II. When imported goods are cleared, China Customs and Uzbekistan Customs provide the following facilitation measures to mutually recognised AEO enterprises: applying a lower inspection rate to imported goods; giving priority inspection to goods requiring physical inspection; designating customs liaison officers responsible for communicating and handling problems encountered by programme members during clearance; and giving priority clearance after international trade is disrupted and resumes.

III. When an advanced certified enterprise of China exports goods to Uzbekistan, it shall inform the Uzbek importer of its AEO code (AEOCN + the subject identification code at positions 9 to 17 of the unified social credit code, for example AEOCN123456789), and the importer shall declare it in accordance with Uzbekistan Customs regulations; Uzbekistan Customs shall confirm the enterprise's status as a China Customs AEO enterprise and grant the relevant facilitation measures.

IV. When a Chinese enterprise imports goods from an Uzbekistan AEO enterprise, it shall enter the Uzbekistan AEO enterprise code separately in the 'overseas shipper code' field under the 'overseas shipper' column of the import declaration and in the 'shipper AEO enterprise code' field of the sea and air cargo manifests. The code is entered as 'country code (UZB)/9-digit enterprise code/III', for example 'UZB/123456789/III'. China Customs shall confirm the enterprise's status as an Uzbekistan AEO enterprise and grant the relevant facilitation measures.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

23 July 2026
