Cashew nuts from African countries with diplomatic ties may enter China. Announcement No. 80 permits in-shell or shelled nuts from cashews grown, peeled, dried and sorted in the exporting country. Raw material must be confirmed by its competent authority; processing and storage units ship only after its recommendation and Chinese registration.

One consignment end to end: raw material from a confirmed grower; sorting, screening, cleaning and impurity removal, drying with temperature and humidity controlled; clean breathable new packing, each package marked for the People's Republic of China plus name, origin, enterprise, registration number and exporter; transport means checked before loading; a phytosanitary certificate with an additional declaration giving enterprise, registration number and the English declaration; live pests are fumigated.

Chinese customs releases compliant goods after inspection; breaches of food safety or quarantine rules are handled under Chinese law, with no preset return or destruction. Nuts from the three approved countries follow the earlier announcements; the registration list and the original certificate govern.

In accordance with relevant Chinese laws and regulations and upon risk assessment, imports of cashew nuts from African countries having diplomatic relations with China that meet the following requirements are permitted from the date of this announcement.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations;

(ii) the Administrative Measures for the Safety of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China and the Provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China on the Registration and Administration of Overseas Producers of Imported Food.

II. Products Permitted for Import

Cashew nuts in this Announcement means in-shell or shelled nuts fit for human consumption, made by taking cashew nuts (Anacardium occidentale) grown within the territory of the exporting country as raw material and processing them within that territory by such processes as shelling, drying and sorting.

Cashew nuts exported to China shall not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: the khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium), the warehouse beetle (Trogoderma inclusum), the cowpea weevil (Callosobruchus maculatus) and Silvanolomus pullus.

Throughout the whole process of cashew nuts exported to China, from raw material growing, production, processing, storage and transport to export, they shall comply with the relevant safety and hygiene requirements of the competent authorities of China and the exporting country, and shall be prevented from contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

Enterprises producing, processing and storing cashew nuts exported to China shall ensure that they comply with Chinese food safety and plant quarantine laws and regulations as well as Chinese national food safety standards. The competent authority of the exporting country shall, in line with Chinese requirements on the registration of overseas producers of imported food, inspect the units producing, processing and storing cashew nuts exported to China and recommend qualifying enterprises to the Chinese side for registration. The Chinese side shall register those that qualify. An enterprise may export its products to China only after obtaining registration in China. The list of registered cashew nut enterprises is available on the GACC website.

IV. Raw Material Requirements

Raw material for cashew nuts exported to China shall come from growers confirmed by the competent authority of the exporting country as meeting the relevant plant quarantine and food safety requirements.

V. Processing Requirements

(i) In the processing of cashew nuts exported to China, such steps as sorting, screening, cleaning and removal of impurities shall be carried out; the nuts shall carry no other live insects, eggs or soil and shall not be mixed with weed seeds, plant residues, metal foreign matter, grit or other impurities. During drying, temperature and humidity shall be controlled to ensure that the moisture content of the product meets the requirements and to inhibit the reproduction of storage pests.

(ii) The competent authority of the exporting country shall guide and supervise the growing of raw material, production, processing, storage, transport, export and other processes of cashew nuts exported to China, so as to ensure compliance with the relevant safety and hygiene requirements of China and the exporting country and prevent contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

VI. Packaging and Marking Requirements

Cashew nuts exported to China shall be packed in clean, hygienic, breathable and new material that meets food safety and plant quarantine requirements, and leakage during transport shall be prevented.

Each package shall be marked in Chinese or English with “This product is exported to the People's Republic of China”, together with traceability information such as the product name, origin, the name of the production or processing enterprise and its China registration number, and the name and address of the exporter.

Such information may be affixed to the package in the form of a label.

VII. Transport Requirements

Before loading, the competent authority of the exporting country shall inspect the means of transport for cashew nuts exported to China. The means of transport shall meet hygiene requirements and shall not carry quarantine pests or other regulated quarantine articles such as weed seeds, live insects, plant residues, soil or other impurities.

VIII. Certificate Requirements

Before export, the competent authority of the exporting country shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for cashew nuts exported to China that have passed inspection and quarantine in compliance with Chinese requirements.

The additional declaration column of the certificate shall state the name of the cashew nut production or processing enterprise and its China registration number, and the following additional declaration shall be filled in: “This consignment complies with Phytosanitary Requirements for Export of Nuts from Africa to China, and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China.” (This consignment complies with the phytosanitary requirements for the export of nuts from Africa to China and carries no quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side).

Where live pests are found, the goods shall be fumigated before export to ensure that no live pests remain, and the quarantine treatment method, such as the agent, temperature, duration and other technical requirements, shall be stated in the phytosanitary certificate.

The content and format of the phytosanitary certificate shall be confirmed by both sides in advance.

IX. Inspection and Quarantine at Entry and Handling of Non-compliance

(i) After cashew nuts exported to China arrive at a Chinese port of entry, Chinese customs shall carry out inspection, quarantine and supervision and shall release them if they pass.

(ii) Where the Chinese side finds that cashew nuts exported to China violate Chinese food safety and plant quarantine laws and regulations or national food safety standards, Chinese customs shall handle the matter in accordance with relevant Chinese laws and regulations.

X. Other Matters

Cashew nuts from Guinea-Bissau, Mozambique and The Gambia have already obtained quarantine access to China, and the relevant import inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements shall be implemented in accordance with GACC Announcement No. 85 of 2024, No. 123 of 2024 and No. 135 of 2025.

It is hereby announced.
