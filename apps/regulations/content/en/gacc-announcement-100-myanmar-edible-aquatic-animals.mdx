Entry is allowed, but only for live animals, and the species must clear a list check first. Announcement No. 100 opens China to Myanmar edible aquatic animals from 14 July 2026, excluding species on the CITES appendices, China's protected wildlife list and the invasive alien species list. A registration number quoted verbally by the supplier is not enough to book space.

Follow one lot of farmed live shrimp end to end: obtain the Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit, let Myanmar monitor drug residues and run the clinical check. Farms and species are packed separately, and the label gives the name, scientific name, registration number and the destination marked as the People's Republic of China. The health certificate is one original plus two copies, the original travels with the goods, listing the Latin name, weight, port of departure and vehicle plate, signed by an official veterinarian.

Wild-caught lots also need the capture area and the packing establishment number. A missing certificate or label item holds the consignment at the border; serious or repeated non-compliance can bring tighter inspection or suspension of that establishment's imports.

In accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Irrigation of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar (hereinafter the Myanmar side) on the inspection, quarantine and hygiene requirements for the export of Myanmar edible aquatic animals to China, import of Myanmar edible aquatic animals meeting the following requirements is permitted from the date of this Announcement.

I. Quarantine and Inspection Basis

(i) the Biosafety Law of the People's Republic of China; (ii) the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations; (iii) the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations; (iv) the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Inspection and Quarantine of Entry Aquatic Animals; (v) the Protocol on Inspection, Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for the Export of Edible Aquatic Animals from Myanmar to China between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Irrigation of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar (hereinafter the Protocol).

II. Scope of Imported Products

For the purposes of this Announcement, edible aquatic animals means live aquatic animals, farmed or wild, for human consumption, excluding species listed in the Appendices to the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES), China's National List of Key Protected Wildlife and the List of Key Managed Invasive Alien Species. The list of relevant species is published on the website of the General Administration of Customs.

III. Quarantine Approval

Import of Myanmar edible aquatic animals requires prior quarantine approval and an Entry Animal and Plant Quarantine Permit.

IV. Requirements for Production Establishments

Production establishments in Myanmar exporting edible aquatic animals to China, including farms and packing establishments, shall meet the following conditions:

(i) comply with Myanmar laws and regulations and be subject to effective supervision by the competent Myanmar authority; (ii) be registered by the competent Myanmar authority, recommended by the Myanmar side and granted registration by the Chinese side; (iii) maintain effective safety and hygiene self-management and traceability systems ensuring that farming, capture, holding, packaging, storage, transport, transhipment and export of edible aquatic animals meet the hygiene and traceability requirements of both sides.

V. Requirements for Imported Products

(i) wild-caught edible aquatic animals exported to China shall be lawfully captured in Myanmar waters or by Myanmar fishing vessels in international waters, while farmed edible aquatic animals shall be farmed in Myanmar waters; (ii) drugs prohibited by either side shall not be used during farming, capture, holding, packaging or transport, and drugs restricted or permitted by both sides shall be used as prescribed; (iii) the Myanmar side shall effectively monitor diseases, toxic and harmful substances and foodborne pathogenic microorganisms in edible aquatic animals exported to China, and the results shall meet Chinese food safety and animal health standards; (iv) edible aquatic animals shall undergo clinical inspection by Myanmar official inspectors before export, show no clinical signs of infectious animal or parasitic diseases, and be fit for export.

VI. Packaging and Labelling Requirements

(i) edible aquatic animals exported to China from different capture areas or different registered establishments shall be packed separately, and different species shall be packed independently; (ii) packaging containers shall be new or disinfected and able to meet animal survival and welfare needs; (iii) packaging materials, water or ice and bedding materials shall meet safety and hygiene requirements and shall not contain pathogenic microorganisms, toxic or harmful substances, or aquatic organisms that could harm animal or human health or damage the aquatic ecological environment; (iv) outer packaging shall bear a label stating the common and scientific names of the aquatic animals, the capture area (wild-caught animals only), the names and registration numbers of the farm or packing establishment and the exporter, and the destination, which shall be marked as the People's Republic of China.

VII. Certificate Requirements

Each consignment of edible aquatic animals exported to China shall be accompanied by a health certificate issued by the Myanmar side. The health certificate shall consist of one original and two copies, and the original shall travel with the consignment. The health certificate shall be printed in Chinese and English or in English and shall include at least the following:

(i) the species of the edible aquatic animals (scientific name and Latin name), packaging type, quantity and weight; (ii) for farmed animals, the name and registration number of the farm, and for wild-caught animals, the specific capture area and the name and registration number of the packing establishment; (iii) the names and addresses of the exporter and the importer; (iv) the date of departure, port of departure, means of transport and vehicle plate number; (v) a statement that the edible aquatic animals comply with the relevant requirements of the Protocol and are fit for human consumption; (vi) the date of issue and the signature or stamp of an official veterinarian of the Myanmar side; (vii) the official stamp of the competent authority of the Myanmar side.

VIII. Other Requirements

China Customs shall inspect and quarantine edible aquatic animals exported to China in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules and with the requirements of this Announcement.

Non-compliant products shall be returned, destroyed or otherwise handled in accordance with Chinese laws and regulations. For production establishments with serious problems or repeated non-compliance, the Chinese side may apply measures such as intensified inspection and quarantine or suspension of imports.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

14 July 2026
