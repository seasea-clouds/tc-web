Dried longan from Cambodia may now enter China. GACC Announcement No. 84 permits the dried fruit of mature longan, sorted, dried, shelled or unshelled, uncooked. Raw material must be confirmed by Cambodia; producing, processing and storing units may ship only after Cambodian recommendation and Chinese registration.

One consignment end to end: raw material from a grower confirmed by Cambodia; packing in new, clean material; each package marked in Chinese or English for the People's Republic of China plus product name, shelf life, origin, plant name, China registration number, exporter name and address; transport means checked before loading; Cambodia issues a phytosanitary certificate before export, with an additional declaration giving plant name, registration number and the English declaration in full.

Chinese customs inspects on arrival and releases compliant goods; larger grain borer, khapra beetle or a breach of the Protocol is handled under Chinese law, with no preset return or destruction. A number quoted by phone does not count: the registration list and the original certificate govern; batch and country here only illustrate the process.

Under Chinese laws and regulations and the provisions agreed between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter "the Chinese side") and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries of the Kingdom of Cambodia (hereinafter "the Cambodian side") on inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for dried longan exported from Cambodia to China, imports of Cambodian dried longan meeting the following requirements are permitted from the date of this announcement.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(i) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection and its implementing regulations;

(ii) the Administrative Measures for the Safety of Imported and Exported Food of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China on the Registration of Overseas Manufacturers of Imported Food;

(iii) the Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries of the Kingdom of Cambodia on Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Dried Longan Exported from Cambodia to China (hereinafter "the Protocol").

II. Products Permitted for Import

(i) Cambodian dried longan in this Announcement means the dried fruit fit for human consumption made from mature fruit of longan (Dimocarpus longan) grown in Cambodia, sorted, dried, and shelled or unshelled within Cambodia, and not cooked.

(ii) The Cambodian side shall ensure that dried longan exported to China is free of the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: larger grain borer (Prostephanus truncatus) and khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium).

(iii) Dried longan exported to China shall carry no live insects, eggs or soil, and shall not be mixed with weed seeds, plant residues, metal foreign matter, grit or other impurities.

(iv) The Cambodian side shall guide and supervise the growing of raw material, production, processing, storage, transport, export and other processes of dried longan for China, so as to ensure compliance with the relevant food safety and plant health requirements of China and Cambodia and prevent contamination by pathogenic microorganisms or toxic and harmful substances.

III. Requirements for Production Enterprises

Production enterprises of Cambodian dried longan exported to China shall ensure that their products comply with Chinese food safety and plant quarantine laws and regulations, national food safety standards and the Protocol.

The Cambodian side shall, in line with Chinese requirements on the registration of overseas manufacturers of imported food, inspect the units producing, processing and storing dried longan for China and recommend qualifying enterprises to the Chinese side for registration. The Chinese side shall register those that qualify. An enterprise may export to China only after it is registered in China. The list of registered Cambodian enterprises exporting dried longan to China is available on the GACC website.

IV. Raw Material Requirements

Raw material for dried longan exported to China shall come from growers and producers confirmed by the Cambodian side as meeting the relevant plant quarantine and food safety requirements.

V. Packaging and Marking Requirements

Dried longan exported to China shall be packed in clean, hygienic, breathable and new material that meets food safety and plant quarantine requirements, and leakage during transport shall be prevented.

Each package shall be marked in Chinese or English with "This product is exported to the People's Republic of China", together with traceability information in Chinese or English such as the product name, shelf life, origin, the name of the production or processing enterprise and its China registration number, and the name and address of the exporter.

Such information may be affixed to the package as a label.

VI. Requirements for Means of Transport

Before loading, the Cambodian side shall inspect the means of transport for dried longan exported to China.

The means of transport shall meet hygiene requirements and shall not contain quarantine pests or other regulated quarantine articles such as weed seeds, live insects, plant residues, soil or other impurities.

VII. Certificate Requirements

Before export, the Cambodian side shall issue a phytosanitary certificate for dried longan that has passed inspection and quarantine in line with the Protocol.

The additional declaration column of the certificate shall state the name of the production or processing enterprise of the dried longan, its China registration number, and "The consignment complies with the requirement specified in the Protocol of Inspection, Quarantine and Sanitary Requirements for Dried Longan Exported from Cambodia to China, and is free from quarantine pests of concern to China." (The consignment complies with the Protocol on inspection, quarantine and sanitary requirements for dried longan exported from Cambodia to China and carries no quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side).

VIII. Other Requirements

(i) On arrival at a Chinese port of entry, dried longan exported to China shall be subject to inspection, quarantine and supervision by Chinese customs and shall be released if compliant.

(ii) Where the Chinese side finds that dried longan exported to China breaches Chinese food safety or plant quarantine laws and regulations, national food safety standards or the Protocol, Chinese customs shall handle the matter under relevant Chinese laws and regulations.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

16 June 2026
