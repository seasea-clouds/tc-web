On 5 June 2025, the General Administration of Customs of China issued Announcement No. 117: as of that day, dried nuts from Rwanda that meet the inspection and quarantine and hygiene requirements may be imported, covering 4 products, namely macadamia, dried pineapple, dried mango and dried banana.

The announcement lays down 12 sections of rules: 4 lists of quarantine pests name the pests product by product, fumigation and vacuum packing are required before export, production and processing enterprises must be recommended by the Rwandan side to obtain registration in China, and the additional declaration on the plant quarantine certificate must be completed in full product by product.

You can check the list of registered enterprises on the website of the General Administration of Customs; as for the basis of inspection and quarantine, packaging and marking, certificate requirements and the rest, the original text of this announcement prevails.

<!-- original -->

In accordance with the laws and regulations of China and the provisions of the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese side) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Animal Resources of the Republic of Rwanda (hereinafter referred to as the Rwandan side) on the inspection and quarantine and hygiene requirements for dried nuts from Rwanda exported to China, dried nuts from Rwanda that meet the following requirements are allowed for import with effect from the date of this announcement.

I. Basis for Inspection and Quarantine

(1) The Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations;

(2) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and its implementing regulations;

(3) The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities and its implementing regulations;

(4) The Measures of the People's Republic of China for the Safety Management of Import and Export Food;

(5) The Protocol between the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Agriculture and Animal Resources of the Republic of Rwanda on the Inspection and Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for Dried Nuts from Rwanda Exported to China (hereinafter referred to as the Protocol).

II. Products Allowed for Import

The dried nuts from Rwanda allowed for import refer to 4 kinds of dried nuts suitable for human consumption, namely macadamia (Macadamia ternifolia), dried pineapple (Ananas comosus), dried mango (Mangifera indica) and dried banana (Musa nana), which are made from ripe fruits of plants grown within the territory of Rwanda as raw materials and are processed within the territory of Rwanda through techniques such as sorting, shelling (peeling and pitting) and dehydration drying.

III. Food Safety Requirements

Dried nuts from Rwanda exported to China shall comply with the relevant laws and regulations of China on food safety and plant quarantine, the national food safety standards, and the provisions of the Protocol.

IV. Plant Quarantine Requirements

(1) Macadamia from Rwanda exported to China shall not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: false codling moth (Thaumatotibia leucotrata), cowpea weevil (Callosobruchus maculatus), larger grain borer (Prostephanus truncatus) and khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium).

(2) Dried pineapple from Rwanda exported to China shall not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: cowpea weevil (Callosobruchus maculatus), larger grain borer (Prostephanus truncatus), khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium), almond moth (Cadra cautella), carob moth (Ectomyelois ceratoniae), tropical meal moth (Pyralis manihotalis), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), dark flour beetle (Tribolium destructor) and kidney-spotted skin beetle (Trogoderma inclusum).

(3) Dried mango from Rwanda exported to China shall not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium), Verticillium wilt fungus (Verticillium dahliae), spiralling whitefly (Aleurodicus dispersus), dried fruit moth (Cadra calidella), dark flour beetle (Tribolium destructor) and mango bacterial black spot pathogen (Xanthomonas campestris pv. mangiferaeindicae).

(4) Dried banana from Rwanda exported to China shall not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium), cowpea weevil (Callosobruchus maculatus), larger grain borer (Prostephanus truncatus), almond moth (Cadra cautella), carob moth (Ectomyelois ceratoniae), tropical meal moth (Pyralis manihotalis), clubbed spider beetle (Ptinus clavipes), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), dark flour beetle (Tribolium destructor), kidney-spotted skin beetle (Trogoderma inclusum) and cacao dieback fungus (Lasiodiplodia theobromae).

(5) Dried nuts from Rwanda exported to China shall not carry live insects, insect eggs or soil, and shall not be mixed with impurities such as weed seeds, plant residues, metal foreign objects and grit.

(6) Before export, dried nuts from Rwanda exported to China shall be fumigated and vacuum packed, to ensure that the dried nuts carry no live insects, especially stored-product pests.

V. Enterprise Requirements

The Rwandan side shall inspect the units producing, processing and storing dried nuts for export to China in accordance with the relevant requirements of the Chinese side for the registration of overseas food manufacturers of imported food, and recommend the enterprises that meet the requirements to the Chinese side for registration. Only after an enterprise has obtained registration from the Chinese side may its products be exported to China.

VI. Raw Material Requirements

The raw materials of dried nuts for export to China shall come from dried nut growing and production enterprises confirmed by the Rwandan side to comply with the relevant plant quarantine and food safety requirements.

VII. Processing Requirements

The Rwandan side shall provide guidance for and supervision over the growing of raw materials, production, processing, storage, transport, export and other processes of dried nuts for export to China, to ensure that they comply with the relevant food safety and plant health requirements of China and Rwanda and are not contaminated by pathogenic micro-organisms or toxic and harmful substances.

VIII. Packaging and Marking Requirements

Dried nuts for export to China shall be packed in new, clean, hygienic and breathable materials that meet food safety and plant quarantine requirements, and leakage shall be prevented during transport. Each package shall be marked in Chinese or English with the words "This product is exported to the People's Republic of China", together with traceable information in Chinese or English such as the product name of the dried nuts, the place of origin, the name of the production and processing enterprise and its registration number in China, and the name and address of the exporter. The above information may be affixed to the package in the form of a label.

IX. Transport Requirements

Before loading, the Rwandan side shall inspect the means of transport for dried nuts for export to China. The means of transport shall meet the hygiene requirements and shall not carry quarantine pests or other regulated quarantine articles, such as weed seeds, live insects, plant residues, soil and other impurities.

X. Certificate Requirements

Before export, the Rwandan side shall issue a plant quarantine certificate for dried nuts for export to China that have passed inspection and quarantine and meet the requirements of the Protocol.

The additional declaration box of the plant quarantine certificate for macadamia for export to China shall state the name of the production and processing enterprise of the macadamia, the registration number in China, and the words "The macadamia certified by this plant quarantine certificate complies with the Protocol on the Inspection and Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for Dried Nuts from Rwanda Exported to China signed between the Chinese side and the Rwandan side on 20 May 2025. This consignment does not carry quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side such as false codling moth (Thaumatotibia leucotrata), cowpea weevil (Callosobruchus maculatus), larger grain borer (Prostephanus truncatus) and khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium)."

The additional declaration box of the plant quarantine certificate for dried pineapple for export to China shall state the name of the production and processing enterprise of the dried pineapple, the registration number in China, and the words "The dried pineapple certified by this plant quarantine certificate complies with the Protocol on the Inspection and Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for Dried Nuts from Rwanda Exported to China signed between the Chinese side and the Rwandan side on 20 May 2025. This consignment does not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: cowpea weevil (Callosobruchus maculatus), larger grain borer (Prostephanus truncatus), khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium), almond moth (Cadra cautella), carob moth (Ectomyelois ceratoniae), tropical meal moth (Pyralis manihotalis), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), dark flour beetle (Tribolium destructor) and kidney-spotted skin beetle (Trogoderma inclusum)."

The additional declaration box of the plant quarantine certificate for dried mango for export to China shall state the name of the production and processing enterprise of the dried mango, the registration number in China, and the words "The dried mango certified by this plant quarantine certificate complies with the Protocol on the Inspection and Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for Dried Nuts from Rwanda Exported to China signed between the Chinese side and the Rwandan side on 20 May 2025. This consignment does not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium), Verticillium wilt fungus (Verticillium dahliae), spiralling whitefly (Aleurodicus dispersus), dried fruit moth (Cadra calidella), dark flour beetle (Tribolium destructor) and mango bacterial black spot pathogen (Xanthomonas campestris pv. mangiferaeindicae)."

The additional declaration box of the plant quarantine certificate for dried banana for export to China shall state the name of the production and processing enterprise of the dried banana, the registration number in China, and the words "The dried banana certified by this plant quarantine certificate complies with the Protocol on the Inspection and Quarantine and Hygiene Requirements for Dried Nuts from Rwanda Exported to China signed between the Chinese side and the Rwandan side on 20 May 2025. This consignment does not carry the following quarantine pests of concern to the Chinese side: khapra beetle (Trogoderma granarium), cowpea weevil (Callosobruchus maculatus), larger grain borer (Prostephanus truncatus), almond moth (Cadra cautella), carob moth (Ectomyelois ceratoniae), tropical meal moth (Pyralis manihotalis), clubbed spider beetle (Ptinus clavipes), broad-nosed grain weevil (Caulophilus oryzae), dark flour beetle (Tribolium destructor), kidney-spotted skin beetle (Trogoderma inclusum) and cacao dieback fungus (Lasiodiplodia theobromae)."

The quarantine treatment methods (such as chemicals, temperature, duration and other technical requirements) shall be indicated in the plant quarantine certificate.

XI. Entry Inspection and Quarantine and Handling of Non-Conformities

(1) Entry inspection and quarantine.

After the dried nuts for export to China arrive at a Chinese port of entry, China Customs will carry out inspection and quarantine supervision, and entry is allowed once they pass.

(2) Handling of non-conformities.

Where the Chinese side finds, in dried nuts from Rwanda exported to China, any violation of the relevant laws and regulations of China on food safety and plant quarantine, the national food safety standards or the provisions of the Protocol, the Chinese side will handle the case in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China and notify the Rwandan side of the violations and the measures taken.

XII. Other Requirements

Dried nuts from Rwanda exported to China shall come from enterprises registered by the Chinese side. The list of registered enterprises exporting dried nuts from Rwanda to China can be queried on the website of the General Administration of Customs.

It is hereby announced.

General Administration of Customs

5 June 2025
